Cryptography¶

The signature and encryption schemes, and whether the mathematics is done (real) or only charged for (modelled). Committees, where t-of-n servers share one authority's key, are a research option and not the US system.

recommended ieee1609.2 (US).		 advanced wired The envelope profile: ieee1609.2 or etsi103097. The secured-message format: IEEE 1609.2 (the US) or ETSI TS 103 097 (Europe).
security.signature string ecdsa-p256 ecdsa-p256, ecdsa-brainpoolp256r1, ecdsa-p384, hybrid-falcon512-ecdsa-p256, hybrid-mldsa44-ecdsa-p256, hybrid-mldsa65-ecdsa-p256, ml-dsa-44, ml-dsa-65, ml-dsa-87, falcon-512, falcon-1024, slh-dsa-sha2-128s, slh-dsa-sha2-128f

recommended ECDSA P-256, or a hybrid to study the cost of post-quantum security.		 basic partial The signature scheme, e.g. ecdsa-p256, hybrid-mldsa44-ecdsa-p256, ml-dsa-65 — any id in v2xw_sec::scheme::SIGNATURE_IDS. The digital signature on every message. ECDSA is what is deployed today; post-quantum signatures resist a quantum computer but are many times larger. Status: Any scheme in the registry (v2xw_sec::scheme::SIGNATURE_IDS; adding one is one module): classical ecdsa-p256, ecdsa-brainpoolp256r1, ecdsa-p384; hybrids (ECDSA P-256 with Falcon-512, ML-DSA-44 or ML-DSA-65 concatenated); pure ML-DSA-44/65/87, Falcon-512/1024 and SLH-DSA-SHA2-128s/128f. Each changes the octets of every signed message and attached certificate on the air by its parts' published sizes (FIPS 204, FIPS 205, Falcon, SEC 1; a pure scheme replaces the ECDSA fields); an SPDU above the network MTU is refused and counted unless a fragmenter is selected. Each is charged its time: every signature and verification costs the sum of its parts on the hardware profile, a profile with no figure for a part falls back to the catalogue anchor it names (crypto_anchors; derived by cycle ratio where nothing is published, and flagged), and a profile that prices none is refused by name (the reference OBU publishes no post-quantum figure). brainpool and P-384 are charged the P-256 time. Only ecdsa-p256 runs in real crypto mode. A post-quantum scheme is the credential system's too (SCMS and CCMS): every certificate carries the post-quantum key and issuer signature, every signed backend message the post-quantum signature, authorities pay at the Raspberry Pi 5 liboqs figure (an upper bound for a server) and devices at the Cohda MK6's; there is no post-quantum butterfly, so a device generates one post-quantum key per pseudonym certificate or ticket and uploads each encrypted to the PCA or AA (v2xw_proto::hybrid).

Deployment¶

Which certificate system runs and how it is laid out: the providers on the trust list, the electors who sign that list, and the parts of the design the US does not deploy. Leave it on "US system (2025)" for the real deployment. Any change the US does not make is marked not US and recorded in the run's manifest.
