messages¶

Group: Messages. Which V2X messages are generated and how they are encoded.

15 settings. The status column says whether editing a setting changes a run; see how to read these tables. Paths use [] for a list element and * for a map key.

Status Meaning
wired
partial

General¶

Setting Type Unit Default Range or choices Level Status Description
messages.sets[] string bsm, cam, denm, spat, map, srm, ssm, psm, vam, cpm advanced partial Sets[] Status: Which message sets are generated, each by the station that sends it in a deployment. bsm and cam: every equipped vehicle; a BSM carries J2945/1 path history and path prediction, and its event flags and lights when set; a CAM its low-frequency container every 500 ms. denm (needs gn-btp), at the C2C-CC triggering conditions: a vehicle whose emergency stop signal comes on (6 m/s2, UN R48) raises an emergency-brake-light DENM updated every 100 ms while it stays on, and a broken-down vehicle with its hazards on, once it has stood 10 s, a stationary-vehicle DENM repeated every second, updated every 15 s and cancelled (the cancellation repeated for 15 s) when the hazards go out. spat and map: roadside units with that role, from the signal plan the drivers obey (min, max and likely end times) and the junction's own lanes, at 10 Hz and 1 Hz. srm and ssm (need codec_tier size-model): emergency vehicles ask the junction whose MAP they heard for priority, its unit answers, and its controller extends or ends greens for it (NTCIP 1211, signal.priority). cpm (needs gn-btp): each vehicle's radar and camera perception, shared at the TS 103 324 rules. psm and vam: VRU devices.
messages.codec_tier string uper uper, size-model advanced wired Which codec tier: real uper bytes or the validated size-model.
messages.rate_hz number Hz [1, 50]
recommended Unset; 10 Hz to match ns-3 and Veins; 1 to 20 Hz to study load.		 basic wired A fixed awareness-message rate, messages per second: every BSM and every CAM goes out on this period instead of J2945/1's 10 Hz and the ETSI CAM triggers (heading 4°, position 4 m, speed 0.5 m/s, EN 302 637-2 §6.1.3), the way ns-3's BsmApplication and Veins' beaconInterval send. Congestion control may still stretch it. Unset keeps the standards' generation rules. How many awareness messages each vehicle sends per second, at a fixed rate. Unset, vehicles follow the standards: 10 per second in the US, and in Europe as often as their motion changes.
messages.spat_interval_ms integer ms [100, 10000]
recommended 100 ms (10 Hz, CTI 4501).		 advanced wired The SPaT broadcast interval of a roadside unit running spat, ms: CTI 4501's 10 Hz (100 ms) unless set. How often a traffic light's roadside unit broadcasts its signal phase and timing.
messages.map_interval_ms integer ms [100, 60000]
recommended 1,000 ms (1 Hz, CTI 4501).		 advanced wired The MAP broadcast interval of a roadside unit running map, ms: CTI 4501's 1 Hz (1 000 ms) unless set. How often a roadside unit broadcasts the intersection's lane map.
messages.cpm_interval_ms integer ms [100, 1000]
recommended 100 ms (ETSI TS 103 324) to 1,000 ms.		 advanced wired T_GenCpm, ms: how often a vehicle running cpm checks whether a CPM is due (ETSI TS 103 324 §6.1.2.3, 100 ms unless set; at most T_GenCpmMax, 1 000 ms). How often a vehicle checks whether to share what its sensors see; a longer interval means fewer, larger collective perception messages.
messages.cpm_dcc_limited boolean recommended Off (today); on for a realistic CPM load in dense traffic (ETSI TS 103 324). advanced wired Whether T_GenCpm follows the congestion control: the DCC's minimum gap between transmissions, clamped to [T_GenCpmMin, T_GenCpmMax] = [100, 1 000] ms (ETSI TS 103 324 V2.1.1 §6.1.2.1, Annex D; Off unless set. Whether vehicles send collective perception messages less often when the channel is congested, following the congestion control as the standard says, instead of at their own rate whatever the load.
messages.cpm_quality_threshold integer rating (0-15) [0, 15]
recommended Empty (not applied, today); 3 as ETSI TS 103 324 recommends.		 advanced wired ObjectPerceptionQualityThreshold, 0 to 15: a CPM carries only objects whose §7.1.8.6 quality exceeds it, and states that quality (TS 103 324 §6.1.2.3, Table F.1 recommends 3; Not applied unless set. The lowest perception quality (0 to 15) an object must exceed to be shared in a collective perception message; tracks the sensors have lost for a while fall below it.
messages.cpm_max_objects integer objects [1, 255]
recommended Empty (255, the format's limit); lower to study a size budget.		 advanced wired The most perceived objects one CPM carries, 1 to 255: the selection is ordered by the §6.1.3.2 object utility and the lowest-utility objects beyond it wait for the next event. The ASN.1 bound 255, in perception order, unless set. The most objects one collective perception message carries; when more are due, the most informative go first and the rest wait for the next message.
messages.denm_traffic_condition boolean recommended Off (today); on for highway studies (C2C-CC triggering conditions). advanced wired Whether a vehicle running denm raises the C2C-CC traffic-condition DENMs (sudden speed drop / dangerous end of queue, local slow down / traffic jam ahead; RS 2007 R1.6.10; Off unless set. Whether vehicles warn the traffic behind them of the end of a queue and of slow or stopped traffic ahead, on non-urban roads, as deployed C-ITS cars do.
messages.denm_traction_loss boolean recommended Off (today); on for winter-weather studies (C2C-CC triggering conditions). advanced wired Whether a vehicle running denm raises the C2C-CC traction-loss DENM (adverse weather, RS 2002 R1.6.10 §3.3; Off unless set. Whether a vehicle whose anti-lock brakes find a slippery road (ice, snow) warns the vehicles around it.
messages.path_history_points integer points [0, 23]
recommended 15 (SAE J2945/1); 0 to 23 (the J2735 limit).		 advanced wired The most path-history points a BSM's Part II (and a CAM's low-frequency container) carries: J2945/1's 15 unless set, at most J2735's 23; 0 sends none. The points are the Part II's bulk, about 8 to 9 bytes each. How many points of its recent track a vehicle puts in each safety message; more points let receivers follow its path but make every message bigger.

generator¶

Setting Type Unit Default Range or choices Level Status Description
messages.generator.id string generator/bsm-j2945-1, generator/cam-en302637-2 (or another value) advanced wired The model's registry id.
messages.generator.params json (serde_json::Value) advanced wired Overrides for its card's defaults. Anything not named here keeps the card's value, which is what makes the parameter set content-addressable.

bsm¶

Setting Type Unit Default Range or choices Level Status Description
messages.bsm.path_history_on_change string keep keep, clear
recommended keep (SAE J2945/1, the default for a vehicle sending BSMs; a CAM-only ETSI station clears unless this is set); clear to study what clearing buys for privacy.		 advanced wired What a pseudonym change does to the vehicle's path history. Unset follows the node's standard: keep on a node that sends BSMs (SAE J2945/1; J5), and clear on a station without the BSM service (an ETSI CAM station, as before J5). A set value holds for every node. Whether a vehicle keeps its recent track in its safety messages when it changes pseudonym, or starts it again so the new pseudonym's messages do not show where the old one drove.

Choices¶

The labelled values of every closed or model setting above.

messages.sets[]
Value Label Meaning
bsm BSM: Basic Safety Message Every equipped vehicle's position, speed and heading, ten times a second (SAE J2735/J2945/1).
cam CAM: Cooperative Awareness Message The European counterpart of the BSM, sent on change of position, speed or heading (ETSI EN 302 637-2).
denm DENM: hazard warning An event message raised by a hazard such as hard braking or a breakdown (ETSI EN 302 637-3).
spat SPaT: signal phase and timing Roadside units broadcast each signal's state and time to change (needs units with the spat role).
map MAP: intersection geometry Roadside units broadcast the lanes and stop lines of their junction (needs the map role).
srm SRM: signal request (emergency priority) Emergency vehicles ask the junction for a green; needs SPaT and MAP and the size-model codec.
ssm SSM: signal status The junction's answer to a signal request.
psm PSM: Personal Safety Message Pedestrians' and cyclists' devices send it (SAE J2735) when actors.vru.device_fraction is above 0; it is not a set to list here.
vam VAM: vulnerable road user awareness The European PSM (ETSI TS 103 300-3): VRU devices send it on the ETSI stack when actors.vru.device_fraction is above 0; it is not a set to list here.
cpm CPM: collective perception Vehicles share what their sensors detect (ETSI TS 103 324).

messages.bsm.path_history_on_change
Value Label Meaning
keep Keep (SAE J2945/1 6.3.6.16) The path history carries on under the new pseudonym: J2945/1 has no clearing rule and asks for 200 m of history (DATAACC-039).
clear Clear at each pseudonym change (privacy research) The history starts again at each change, so the new pseudonym's first messages do not show the old one's track; not a J2945/1 rule.
