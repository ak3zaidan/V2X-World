### 1.3 The TLS pair where TLS acts (`uu-tls13` → `uu-tls12`)

`security.scms.tls` acts only on the packet-level Uu (validate.rs key note: "every
device-to-RA session over a packet-level cellular link"); the realism base's
`cell-capacity-mm1` Uu carries no TCP or TLS, so `tls12` cannot move anything there. Two new
scenarios put M0 on `cellular/uu/packet-pf` (LTE, ISD 500 m, 80 % modems) and change only
the TLS version. Shortened to 0–60 s live and 43,185–43,230 s live (ends at 43,230 s): the
reports (attack from 20 s) and the rollover top-ups are all a TLS check needs.

| Change | (a) metrics | (b) performance | (c) SCMS records | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TLS 1.3 → 1.2 on packet Uu | report upload and top-up exchange latency up (one more RTT at each session's first message) | equal (a few more packets) | same TLS sessions; handshake bytes ×1.0–1.15 (payload 1,251 vs 1,322 B, two more flights' headers); same certificates issued | RFC 8446 §2, RFC 5246 §7.3; constants.md TLS; `scms_knobs_runs::tls_1_2_costs_more_handshake_and_a_slower_topup` |

Rows `uutls-sessions`, `uutls-same-sessions`, `uutls-bytes`, `uutls-report-latency`,

carrier counters (`access.carrier.*`) and the per-exchange latency
