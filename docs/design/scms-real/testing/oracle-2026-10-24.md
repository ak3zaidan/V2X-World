Before OF-3 was added, conf passed 88,981 / 88,981 checks over 19,758 PDUs, every rule green
and 30 / 30 rows; mbr passed every pre-existing rule (330,244 checks, 55,080 PDUs). The t0
CRLs are now sent (`never_sent` 0, was 2), because the engine logs the pre-run fetch.

**OF-3** (`tools/oracle/scms_oracle/records.py`, rule ids `OF-3` and `OF-3-JOIN`). Written from
the sources, before the results were read, with four expectation rows (`pass`):

* *Fetch*: before its first `node.security` row a CRL Store sends each device, per CRACA that
  has published, a SecuredCrl that is one of that CRACA's issued pseudonym (series 1) lists.
* *Held*: every row lists, per CRACA, a list issued by then, no older than the one fetched,
  and no more than a week behind the newest ("as often as possible but no later than once a
  week", [CAMP122] §3 item 10; revocation is checked against the current list, 1609.2 5.1.3.4).
* *OF-3-JOIN*: the list fetched at join is the CRACA's newest at that instant (a device fetches
  on every RA connection, [PRIMER] pp. 5, 8).

The node-to-device map comes from the records alone: the pseudonym a row names, found in a
device's `node.credentials` certificates.

Faults (`--mutate`): `crl-prerun-skipped` (no pre-run fetch), `crl-held-dropped` (a device stops
listing one CRACA), `crl-held-future` (a list no CRACA issued), `crl-join-old` (a joiner fetches
a superseded list). All four red on both runs. The engine fault was also injected: a CLI built
with `run.note_prerun_crl(..)` skipped, conf re-run, OF-3 red with 130 checks, each "no
SecuredCrl of CRACA .. from a CRL Store by its first row".

## 3. R-JOINCRL, found by OF-3-JOIN and fixed

On `81a12183` a vehicle that joins after a provider published a newer list was given the
`t0` lists (`Phase2::prerun_crl` returned a snapshot taken at build). conf: 35 joiners at
196,218 s fetched provider-b's t0 list although its revocation list (issueDate 719,762,405,
191,400 s) was current; they trusted the revoked liars for the whole radio window. mbr: 108
such fetches, and 1,044 rows of joiners at 1,300,013 s holding lists more than a week out of
date. This is §9 item 4 ("a joiner holds the newest list each CRACA published before it
joined").

Fix (`phase2.rs`): a joiner holds the composite list its RA serves at the join instant
(`published_crl_for`) and is marked with that serial, so its first poll is answered 412; the
`t0` snapshot is still used while nothing newer is served, so a fleet on the road from the start
is unchanged. Test: `phase2::join_crl_tests::a_device_joining_after_a_publication_holds_the_newest_list`.
Fault (the old snapshot for every joiner): red, "the composite version its RA serves: 0 vs 1".

Measured effect (same seed): conf revoked receptions 22,355 -> 23,053 (+698, the joiners now
refuse the revoked liars), honest revocations 0 -> 0, attackers revoked 5 -> 5, reports
3,891 -> 3,897; mbr unchanged in every phase2 count (its joiners arrive after the lists they
miss stopped mattering), records 13,697,740 -> 13,697,794. Oracle: rows behind the newest
list 1,499 -> 0 (conf) and 2,554 -> 0 (mbr).

Goldens: `i-s1-phase2-60s` (scms_scale, bsm_timing) unchanged. `phase2-path` moves (§7).
