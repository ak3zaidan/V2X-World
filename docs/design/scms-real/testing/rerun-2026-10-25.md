carried `77cffb5b` values (rerun-2026-10-24-a §3, §5: the 20 variants `on-demand` …

`--record-level metrics --no-verify` (full-bench.md method).

Two findings for the analyst: the frozen-span windows carry their sample counts (`cbr` n 308
and 327 with mean 0), so a run-wide n-weighted CBR mean over `metrics.json` is diluted by
them; and the 129,585 s frozen window holds 121 reception trials and 14 verifications (frames
handled at fast-forward stops). Both follow the R-FFWIN design (06 §6 item 2); a reader should
drop windows whose span is frozen.
