**Why no case.** The corroborated MA revokes on ≥ 3 corroborated events inside 60 s from
≥ 3 distinct trusted reporters (`ma_corroborate.rs`). From `ma.report`: the most-heard
attacker had 107 reports from **2** vehicles, the second 19 from 1. The RSUs' reports (105,
trusted infrastructure) used to make the quorum. This is the US reading (no roadside
reporter, 06 §8 item 6), not an engine defect; but the realism base exists "so the
misbehaviour flow has something real to do", and with 40 s of attack at a fifteenth of rush
hour it no longer did: T6, T7, Z5, A3, L1–L6 and every case-flow variant row lost their subject.

**Fix (fixture, `b645dd99`).** `midtown-scms-realism.yaml` runs its first live window to
300 s (fast-forward 300–43,185 s), as `scms_wiring` needed for a vehicle-only quorum (06 §6
item 9). Expected before the run: ≥ 1 vehicle attacker revoked, 0 honest, wall ×4–6, more
devices at the rollover. Measured: 43 and 23 reporters, **2 of 3 revoked, 0 honest**, decision
115,220 s and CRL 147,600 s on the calendar as before, wall 33 → 185 s, 128 → 201 MB,
105 devices at the rollover (2,100 certificates). No engine test or digest uses the file.
