## 2. `sensitivity_security::the_revocations_move_the_crl_size`

It asserts that a revoked device is reported again under its next pseudonym and that the
linkage authorities tie the verdict to the decided device (`decisions_linked > 0`). Same
fleet at 30 % attackers, by CRL poll interval:

| `crl_fetch_interval_s` | revoked receptions | MA decisions | linked verdicts | reports at the MA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 (the test's value) | 35,094 | 7 | **0** | 2,227 |
| 30 | 16,086 | 8 | 7 | 2,866 |
| 120 | 2,876 | 8 | 7 | 3,378 |

With a 5 s poll every receiver holds the linkage-seed CRL before the revoked device's next
pseudonym is reported, so that pseudonym is refused (35,094 times), not reported: nothing is
left to link. The linkage path works (7 linked at 30 s and 120 s). With
`modelled_reports: 1` the 5 s run is unchanged (7 decisions, 0 linked), so the REPORTS
wave's filing rule is not the cause. The test now polls every 30 s, with the table in its
comment; the CRL-size assertions are unchanged. The original value is the fault: at 5 s the
assertion is RED (the main-branch failure).

## 3. `fragmentation::a_padded_message_goes_out_in_pieces_and_reaches_the_node_only_whole`

M-RX1 failed 2,603 times. A diagnostic run printed each mismatch piece by piece: the
receiver's `node.rx` fate is `reassembly-failed` at the end of the SDU's **first** fragment
(`net.frag` outcome `Failed` with no set held), and the PHY lost the **second** fragment to a
collision about 1.4 ms later.

| `max_open_sdus` | SDUs | sets complete / lost | refused at a fragment | fates with a fragment lost after them |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 64 (default) | 1,884 | 20,697 / 32,099 | 11,607 | 2,603 |
| 4,096 | 1,884 | 29,690 / 23,095 | 0 | 0 |

So the generic reassembler's 64-set buffer is full in this dense padded run, it refuses new
sets, and that refusal is the SDU's fate. The engine's cause is the right one: a loss that
happened later cannot have caused it. M-RX1 (`v2xw-metrics` `check_m_rx1`) now takes a
fragment's PHY loss as the cause only when the fragment ended no later than the fate; a
whole frame's loss is always its cause, as before. New unit test
`m_rx1_does_not_blame_a_fate_on_a_fragment_lost_after_it` (holds for the refusal case; fails
for a fate stamped after the loss and for a whole frame). With the filter removed
(`tools/mutate/mutate.py`) it is RED. The full-buffer refusal itself is a real effect of the
fragmenter's default and is visible in `net.frag`.

## 8. ISS-form certificate sizes against the published ISS chain

`tools/realworld/component_certs.py <run-dir>` decodes a run's provisioning SPDUs from
`pki.flow` with the oracle's COER codec (not the Rust), takes every certificate in them, and
compares each component certificate with the published certificate of its role:
provider-a (ISS form) with the ISS pre-production chain in the 2025-07-17 test chain file,
provider-b (SAESOL form) with the production chain. A role passes when the field sets are
equal, every field but the name has the same encoded size, and the engine's size is the
published size plus the name-length difference. On the M0 run: **10/10 roles pass**, and
every SPDU re-encodes byte for byte.

| Role | Engine ISS | Published ISS | Engine SAESOL | Published SAESOL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PCA/ACA | 203 B (name 16) | `pca.preprod.v2x.isscms.com` 215 B (name 28) | 156 B | `aca.prod.s2x.io` 157 B |
| RA | 204 B | `ra.preprod…` 216 B | 192 B | `ra.prod.s2x.io` 193 B |
| ECA | 218 B | `dot1.eca.preprod…` 235 B | 171 B | `eca.prod.s2x.io` 172 B |
| ICA | 191 B | `ica.preprod…` 199 B | 179 B | `ica.prod.s2x.io` 176 B |
| root | 206 B | `v2xrootca-test.ghsiss.com` 210 B | 206 B | `rootca.prod.s2x.io` 203 B |

So the ISS SPDUs' extra bytes (matrix.md open item 5) are exactly the published ISS
certificates' own fields: `identifiedRegion` {124, 484, 840} (12 B) on the ICA and every
component, and an `encryptionKey` (35 B) on the PCA and the ECA, which the SAESOL ones lack.
RaEeCertAck 292 → 304 B is the RA's region (+12); AcaEeCertResponsePrivate 454 → 501 B is the
PCA's region and key (+47); EcaEeCertResponse 958 → 1,064 B is the ECA twice (+47 each) and the
ICA once (+12). The production chain holds two `aca.prod.s2x.io` certificates (157 and 162 B);
the engine matches the 157 B one. `--mutate` drops the ISS region from the engine's RA and the
key from its PCA: 2 roles RED. Realism rows Z1/Z3/Z4 now take the ISS form's sizes
(`runtime_expectations.yaml`, corrected with this evidence; injected sizes outside the bands
are RED).

**Found on the way (not fixed):** every enrolment certificate is named
`ee-<device>.provider-a`, also the 46 that provider-b's ECA issues
(`v2xw-proto/src/scms/run/enrol.rs` `device_name` hard-codes the label; its comment says
"unique per device and provider"). The size is unchanged, but the name is wrong for provider-b
devices; fixing it moves the certificate bytes and the regression digests.

## 9. Open findings from this package (not fixed)

1. **R-JOINCRL** (§7): a mid-run joiner holds every provider's t0 CRL, so late in a long run
   it refuses the home provider's messages until its first download: 27 joiners in MWP
   (nodes 45-74, W3-W5) and 4 in MWC's W5. Fix: give it the newest list each CRACA published before its join.
2. **MWP.off-no-backend reopened** (§7): a switched-off device records three CRL downloads
   at the first live step after a fast-forward.
3. **An overdue-CRL refusal is labelled `signature-invalid` on `node.rx`.** In MWC's W4,
   `receiver_validation.refused` counts 97,679 `crl-overdue` and `node.rx` has exactly
   97,679 `signature-invalid` fates. An analyst reading `node.rx` would see a forgery storm
   where there is a stale list. `node.rx` needs a `crl-overdue` cause.
4. **Enrolment certificate names** (§8): every one is `ee-<device>.provider-a`, the 46
   provider-b issues included (`v2xw-proto/src/scms/run/enrol.rs` `device_name`). Fixing it
   moves certificate bytes and the regression digests.
5. **The dense saturated-queue regime** (§1) is not reachable in `dsrc-dense.yaml` with the
   real certificate: the row now states that the queue does not bind.
