# Re-runs on `81a12183` and the benchmark variants (2026-10-24, package b)

Package: re-run the post-quantum, full-fleet and stress rows on current code; the R-FS40
window and `gnss-raw` (06 §9, "testing still owed"); correct `FB2.privacy`; bring
`pq-profile.md` up to date; run the LTE-V2X, Portland and 2×-density variants owed in
`docs/benchmark/scms-real-benchmark.md` §10, at quarter fleet and a 60 s window.

**Method.** Every expectation was written and committed with `measured: pending` before its
run (`39d22ab8`, `b5f9799c`); the rows are in
`crates/v2xw-engine/tests/expectations/scms-variants.yaml`. Binary: the integrator's
release build of `81a12183`, copied to the scratchpad. Seed `0xBE7C_0001` (the benchmark's)
or the scenario's own; 2 rayon threads; `--no-verify`; one run per run-lock call with the
2,500 MB cap; `/usr/bin/time -l` for wall, CPU and peak. Records: `--record-level events`
for the variant windows, R-FS40 and the realism pair; `metrics` for the PQ, relay and
multi-week runs (as the runs they repeat). Reading and judging:

```
python3 tools/realworld/matrix.py extract <runs>/<id>
python3 tools/realworld/matrix.py check crates/v2xw-engine/tests/expectations/scms-variants.yaml <runs> --fill
python3 tools/realworld/variants_faults.py crates/v2xw-engine/tests/expectations/scms-variants.yaml <runs>
```

`matrix.py extract` now also writes the detector verdicts (`p2.verdicts.<detector>`), the
device count (`p2.devices`) and the record digest (`digest.record`), and `check` has an
`identical` expectation (a digest). The variant windows set `security.scms.pki_view: true`
(the benchmark turns it off for its two-day run) so that `pki.flow` gives the per-entity
loads. Shown to record only: the first Q60 run without it and the second with it differ
in no measurement but cost, record count, digest and the PKI-view keys.

## 1. Fault proof of the rows

`variants_faults.py` takes every row that passes, pushes the variant's value just outside
its band (a bound for `eq`/`le`/`ge`, the base's value for `up`/`down`, a changed digest for
`identical`) and judges it again. A row that still passes cannot fail.

**60 rows: 51 pass, 5 wrong expectations (corrected with evidence), 4 open.** All 51
passing rows go red under their fault, 0 silent. The 9 rows that do not pass were not
injected. Four rows (`VB.LTE.frames-twice`, `VB.Q60.requests`, `VB.Q60X2.requests`,
`VB.Q60P.requests`) are the corrected expectations of wrong rows, written after their runs
from the standard and the records, and say so in their `source`.

### 3.3 The SCMS architecture: per-entity loads (`pki.flow`, 60 s)

| Entity pair (messages) | Q60 | LTE | 2× | Portland | Per device | CAMP / design |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EE → DCM (enrolment) | 313 | 313 | 602 | 96 | 1 | one enrolment per device |
| ECA → DCM, DCM → EE | 313 | 313 | 602 | 96 | 1 | one enrolment certificate |
| EE → RA | 975 | 990 | 1,851 | 300 | ~3.1 | 1 request (2 for an RSU), ~2 downloads, plus reports |
| RA → ACA, ACA → RA | 11,380 | 11,380 | 22,940 | 3,384 | 40 per OBU, 2 per RSU | one ACA issuance per certificate, 2 weeks × 20 (CAMP 1.2.2 Table 67) |
| LA1 → RA, LA2 → RA | 11,320 | 11,320 | 22,880 | 3,360 | 40 per OBU/handset | one pre-linkage value per certificate per LA |
| RA → EE | 11,723 | 11,723 | 23,572 | 3,492 | ~37 | batch files and acks |
| CRL store → EE | 566 | 566 | 1,144 | 168 | 2 per OBU/handset | both CRACAs' t0 lists (OF-3) |
| Misbehaviour reports (EE → RA) | 6 | 21 | 15 | 0 | — | — |

Every load is linear in the devices and unchanged by the radio. The LA1 and LA2 loads are
exactly 40 × the non-RSU devices (283, 572, 84), and the ACA's are those plus 2 × the RSUs.
Against CAMP's sizing (about 0.5 requests/s at the RA for 17 M vehicles a year, 04 §4.1),
the 343 to 632 requests here all arrive in the first simulated second: a scenario start
provisions its whole fleet at once, which no real RA sees (bench §5). The RA's steady load
(1 top-up a week per device and the hourly CRL polls: 2 to 8 answered 412 in 60 s) is not
exercised by a 60 s window.
