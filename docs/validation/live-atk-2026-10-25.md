# Live attack-and-detection tests, 2026-10-25

Track: live engine testing, attacks and detection. Base `486c8cba`, the integrator's
release `v2xw` copied to the scratchpad. Rows:
`crates/v2xw-engine/tests/expectations/live-atk.yaml`, each written and committed before the
run it judges. Checker: `tools/realworld/live_atk.py`. It reads only the run's files
(`run-report.json` plus the records `gt.attack.action`, `gt.subject`, `ma.report`,
`ma.decision`, `app.outcome` and `proto.revocation`). Each row names a fault applied to those
raw inputs, and `selftest` shows the row goes red under that fault.

## 1. Method

- **Family.** `scenarios/live-atk/`: the SCMS lifecycle (`credential-lifecycle.yaml`, every
  US SCMS entity, compressed calendar, CRL every 30 s, MA review 0 s). The fleet is held at
  40 vehicles from t = 0 for 150 s, the seed is `0xC0FFEE_5EED`, attackers act from 20 s,
  and records are kept at `events` level.
- **Suites.**
  - **L**: `detect/legacy-12` filing only what SM-MBR v1.1 can encode. This is the default.
  - **M**: L with `modelled_reports: 1`, so every verdict reaches the MA.
  - **U**: `detect/us-sm-mbr`.
  - TS 103 759 cannot be selected in `detection.local` (06 §6 item 19).
- **MA rules.** `threat/ma/corroborated` (the default, with `min_events` and
  `event_min_reporters` swept) and `threat/ma/legacy-window`.
- **Catalogue.** All 23 runnable attack types, one attacker each, in three groups (`cat1-3`)
  of 7 or 8 types. The attackers are on actors present from the start (picked from B0L by
  how long they stay), and each group runs under L, M and U.
- **Run size.** Each run takes 60 to 175 s of wall time and 63 to 90 MB peak (the cap is
  1,200 MB). There is one run per run-lock call. Disk stayed above 14 GB free.

## 2. Headline results

- **Attack-free gives no revocation.** This holds under L, M and U with the deployed
  receiver model. B0L and B0L2 are byte-identical (record digest `ade2f953…`).
- **The research suite (L).** On the attacks it can file it detects every attacker within
  1 to 6 s of onset, because of the 1 Hz check rate and the two-check streak gate. It then
  revokes 30 to 35 s after onset; the RA's 30 s shuffle is most of that time.
- **The US profile (U).** It detects within one BSM interval (10 to 14 ms) and revokes at
  about 30 s.
- **Shares.** ConstPos at 5, 10 and 20 % gives 2, 5 and 10 attackers. All are reported, and
  2, 5 and 9 are revoked. No honest vehicle is revoked, and the event count stays within
  3 %.
- **Never reported (DataReplay, DelayedMessages, OutOfOrder).** The 2 s `generationTime`
  window refuses these BSMs before any check runs, so no suite reports them (confirms 06 §6
  item 17).
- **InvalidSignature.** The research suite reports it (`signatureVerification`), but the MA
  sets aside evidence whose only content is a bad signature, so it is never revoked.
