### model/string-stability

**Platoons amplify or damp a disturbance exactly where linear string-stability theory says**

For `a = f(s, v, Δv)` (Δv = v − v_leader) a platoon in equilibrium is string stable iff `f_v²/2 + f_v·f_Δv − f_s ≥ 0` (Wilson & Ward 2011, Transp. Res. C 19; Treiber & Kesting 2013 §15.5: |G(iω)| ≤ 1 for all ω). The partial derivatives are taken numerically from the engine's IDM at each density's equilibrium, and give the linearised per-car gain |G(iω)|. A 16-car platoon at that equilibrium is then simulated with the model while the leader's speed oscillates 0.05 m/s at ω = √|criterion| (inside the unstable band when there is one); the per-car amplitude gain, read by projecting the speeds onto sin and cos at ω, is held to |G(iω)| and must agree on growth. (A single dip is not used: its spectrum is mostly outside the band, so a weakly unstable platoon damps it.) Densities where the criterion is within 0.01 s⁻² of zero are marginal and skipped.
