## 1. Families and what was run

- **Application family (G\*)**: `glosa-energy` cut to 150 s. Five SPaT/MAP units, 23 dBm,
  2,400 veh/h, every driver following GLOSA. One-factor variants: compliance 0, 0.5,
  GLOSA off; RSU power 0, 10, 30 dBm; no MAP; one unit; the unit at site 10 down from
  60 s; plus compliance x power and seeds 2 and 3.
- **SCMS family (S\*)**: the Midtown realism base cut to a 120 s live window. 30 units
  (report-forward, crl, provisioning-proxy), every vehicle relay-only (penetration 0),
  a CRL every 10 s, a 5 s report shuffle. Variants: DSL, microwave, cellular, no backhaul;
  50 ms and 2,000 ms latency; 10 % and 30 % loss; 5 and 0 units; a 1, 3 and 20 s CRL
  carousel; unit 5 down from 30 s; 2,000 ms x 3 s carousel.

The run slots were shared and badly contended: each run waited 7 to 21 minutes for the
lock, so 9 of 33 runs finished in the window (G0, G0b, Gc0, Gp10, S0, Slat2000,
Slat2000bc3, FSlat2000bc3; plus the S0 pilot). Run cost: G runs 127-188 s wall, 81-89 MB;
S runs 57-92 s, 111-118 MB. The other 54 rows stay `pending` with their predictions
written; the runs are listed in §5.

**Wrong prediction, corrected with evidence (power).** The 50 % SPaT edge did not move
from 23 to 10 dBm. The edge is the block geometry, not the link budget: under TR 37.885
urban LOS (PL = 38.77 + 16.7 log10 d + 18.2 log10 f) with 3 dBi each end and about
-92 dBm sensitivity, the LOS reach is about 2 km at 10 dBm and 510 m at 0 dBm, beyond
the 300 m blocks; the fall at 200-400 m is receivers behind the 30 m buildings (urban
NLOS, 30 log10 d: 77 m at 10 dBm). Power moved exactly those: 23 % fewer SPaT receptions,
300-400 m PRR 0.283 -> 0.205. The first GLOSA advice comes as a vehicle clears the
previous junction (277 m in every run), so it cannot move either. The p30 and p0 rows
were rewritten before their runs (`d5ccda49`).

## 3. Engine defects

**Fixed: a roadside unit's CRL ignored its backhaul, and a unit that was down kept
broadcasting** (`4399f8f8`, `run.rs`). `install_rsu_crls` dropped the arrival time
`rsu_send` returned and installed every list at its publication step; the carousel let
every connected unit broadcast whatever its own download; an outage event did not stop
a unit's fetches or CRL frames (8 frames from unit 5 in the base, where it is up, is
what `LR.S.out.crl-frames` reads; the `Sout` run did not finish). Now each unit's
download stays in flight until it lands, installed at the first mobility step it has
landed by; the carousel skips a unit with a download in flight or switched off; an off
unit starts no fetch. On the fixed binary with a 2 s line: RSU installs 90 -> 60 (the
last list still on the line at the horizon), CRL frames 300 -> 290, vehicle CRL lag
2.88 -> 3.67 s. Goldens: none by design (a download landing inside the current step
installs in that step, which covers fibre 0.3 ms, DSL 8.8 ms and cellular 29 ms; no
shipped scenario switches a unit off). `LR.S.fix.neutral` (S0 vs FS0 byte identity)
was queued and not run. Test: `tests/live_rsu.rs` (ignored, 90 s Midtown). It checks
the 2 s download with a 3 s carousel and unit 5 down. Written and compiled, not yet run.

Why `LR.S.lat2000.crl-lag` passed on the base binary: the first list arms the carousel
at the earliest unit arrival (2 s), and with a 10 s cadence and a 5 s carousel every later
list lands 2 s before a tick. With a 3 s carousel only one revocation list fell in the
window, so the unaligned case was not exercised live (`LR.S.lat2000bc3.crl-lag` open).

**Open, not fixed: every CRL publication is booked twice on each unit's line.** Step 4 of
the backend step sends the list down every unit's line to time the carousel start, on
top of the unit's own fetch: S0 pilot `backhaul_messages` 151 = 1 report + 90 installs +
60 (2 publications x 30 units). Removing it shifts the carousel start by microseconds and
moves every SCMS record digest, so it is left for the integrator with a re-bless.

**Open: GLOSA's CO2 and fuel disagree in sign** (`LR.G.c1.co2`). In one class, CO2 per km
rose 1.4 % while fuel per km fell 0.5 %. Under HBEFA, CO2 is fuel times a per-fuel
factor, so this needs the fuel-split check. The seed-2 and seed-3 pairs are owed.

**Found while setting up, not fixed:** `glosa-energy.yaml`'s own sweep fails validation
on a variant whose `apps` block equals the default ("`apps.glosa_compliance` does not name
a field"): the default is not serialised, so the swept path is absent.

## 4. Not modelled (domain gaps)

TIM (no traveller-information message exists in the engine); SPaT/MAP rates and contents
are constants (10 Hz, 1 Hz, the size model's nominal elements), not scenario knobs;
wired backhaul has no jitter and microwave outage is a per-packet loss, not a timed
outage; a backhaul outage mid-run exists only as the whole unit going down.

### 6.2 Corrections (wrong predictions, with the evidence)

- **GLOSA fuel and CO2 (LR.G.c1.fuel, LR.G.c1.co2, open on 10-25).** The energy layer has
  no accounting issue. Two separate things were wrong:
  1. The energy metrics are cumulative ("run so far"). The reader summed the running ratio
     of every window, which is not the run's ratio. The final sample is (`g.final_*`).
  2. `co2_per_km` divides by every km, EVs included. `fuel_per_100km` divides by
     combustion km only (`v2xw_energy` provider.rs:922).

  In the seed-1 pair the EV share of the km fell from 28.5 % to 27.4 %, which raised
  CO2/km while fuel/100 km fell. CO2 per combustion km fell 1.2 % with fuel. CO2 per litre
  held at 2,407.4 g/L, inside the 2,350-2,500 band a third-diesel fleet gives. Seed 2:
  fuel -2.3 %, CO2 per combustion km -2.3 %, stops -16 %. Seed 3 is congested (1.6
  stops/km, 7.8 m/s): stops -3.2 % and fuel +0.9 %, so it stays open. Over the 3 seeds
  fuel moved -0.9 % on average. A 150 s window cannot separate an effect that size from
  trajectory noise, so the pairs need 600 s windows.
- **Cellular backhaul one way (LR.S.cell.hop).** The engine takes a cellular unit's line
  from the scenario's own Uu (`cell-capacity-mm1`: radio/2 + 2.355 ms transport + 0.34 ms
  server, about 4 ms) plus the 14.4 ms internet leg, so 18.41 ms. The prediction's 29.3 ms
  used the Rsu card's LTE half round trip, which is only the fallback. All 186 reports took
  exactly 18.414 ms, so there is no jitter (§4 gap).
- **Loss and delay (LR.S.loss30.delay; LR.S.loss10.delay passed at 53 ms against a 22.5 ms
  prediction).** The line delivers in order, so a report queued behind another report's
  retransmission waits for it (TCP head-of-line blocking, RFC 793). In Sloss10k (Sloss10
  with its recording kept), 35 reports were delayed. 17 were delayed by their own loss (18
  expected at p = 0.1) and 18 by an earlier report on the same unit. The mean is about
  twice the own-loss term: 2 x 85 ms = 170 ms at p = 0.3, against 163.7 ms measured. The
  retransmission shares are what theory gives: 0.0997 against 0.111, and 0.393 against
  0.425.
- **Carousel period and vehicle lag (LR.S.bc20.veh-lag).** The measure is a mean over
  vehicles that downloaded the list. With a 20 s period, the vehicles that would have heard
  a later repetition are not counted. The downloads fell as predicted (102 to 96), but the
  mean of the vehicles counted fell too (1.23 to 0.98 s). A share-by-deadline measure (the
  vehicles holding the list 10 s after publication) is owed.
- **0 dBm and GLOSA advice (LR.G.p0.advice).** The LOS budget at 0 dBm still reaches about
  510 m (the §2 link budget), beyond the 300 m advice range. A 10 Hz SPaT is still fresh
  within 1 s even at PRR 0.2, so advice moved -0.2 %. Range50 fell from 300 to 280 m, and
  SPaT receptions fell 49 % (LR.G.p0.heard, pass).

### 6.3 The 10-25 fix checked live, and a defect it leaves

- **FS0 (LR.S.fix.neutral): byte-identical.** S0 on `486c8cba` and on `4399f8f8` have the
  same record digest `bea8a0f6...` (2 Rayon threads on 10-25, 1 thread now). The fix
  moves nothing on a line faster than a mobility step.
- **Outage (LR.S.out.*, fixed).** The 10-25 Sout was not a one-change variant:
  `scms-out.yaml`'s `events` list replaced the base's 4x demand event, so the run had a
  third of the vehicles and published no list. The scenario now repeats that event. On the
  release, the down unit kept broadcasting (8 frames after 30 s, 30 units). On the fix it
  sends 0 frames and 29 units broadcast. The 938fb16a Sout is still queued.
- **The 240 s pair with 3 s ticks proved nothing (correction rows LR.S.l240.bc4.*).** The
  three lists came 30 s apart, on the 10 s cadence grid, and 30 s is a multiple of 3 s. So
  every later list met a tick 2.146 s after it on both binaries, and the l240 rows passed
  without exercising the unaligned case. With 4 s ticks a later list meets a tick 0.146 s
  after publication:
  - On the release (S240bc4), the list is on the air at 0.146 s, before any 2 s download.
