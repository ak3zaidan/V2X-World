## 5. Fix wave (2026-10-25, on `38ad00cf`), work in progress

2.5 veh/s, 300 s; mobility harness, not the CLI scenario):

1. **Open item 1 has two causes.** (a) The permitted-left gap test counted claimants that
   were not coming: an opposing car standing at its line behind a full exit was "arriving" in
   0.7 s, and every car queued behind a standing one in 3-5 s (its free launch). The two
   opposing heads waited on each other's followers for minutes on an empty junction. (b) A
   turner facing a continuous opposing stream had no capacity at all: it waited at the line,
   where a US driver pulls into the junction and clears at the change (HCM sneakers).
   Measured on the harness: head-of-line holds over 60 s 37 -> 29 and green-time holds over
   50 s 19 -> 9 with (a) fixed; the rest are against moving opposing traffic, cause (b).
   A first try at (b), turning from the line on the amber, never fired (the opposing
