**The Falcon AoI check.** In both Falcon runs `delivery_ratio` is 0, while 20,826 frames
decoded at the PHY. The cause is `net_mtu_refusals` = 80 (40 vehicles × 2 s × 1 Hz). A
Falcon-512 SPDU that carries its certificate (an 897-octet key and 666-octet signatures)
does not fit the WSMP MTU, so every certificate frame is refused. No receiver ever learns a
signer, nothing is delivered, and AoI is undefined. This is correct behaviour: a real
stack also needs fragmentation for a post-quantum certificate. The check now sets
`net.fragmenter: generic-sdu` in its `base_set`, and the PRR and CBR checks keep their
setup. Re-run result: see §4.

sign, which reads as a small bias and not as noise.

## 4. Continued 2026-10-25: the NR selection window, and the rows judged by tests

### 4.1 A defect: the sidelink access wait was U-shaped in T2

Written before the runs (`8adb2281`): on NR-V2X mode 2 (Ali/Todisco profile, µ = 1,
0.5 ms slots, T1 = 2 slots) the resource is picked uniformly in [n + T1, n + T2] and a
periodic message keeps that offset, so the mean access wait is about (T1 + T2) / 2
(TS 38.214 8.1.4; TR 37.885 A.1.1 periodic traffic). Each run: 40 vehicles, 4 s, 10 Hz,
2.3 s wall, 40 MB.

| T2 (ms) | theory (ms) | measured, 486c8cba | measured, no hand-off jitter | after the fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 41.88 | 1.20 | AFTER1 |
| 5 | 3 | 29.64 | 3.37 | AFTER5 |
| 10 | 5.5 | 18.86 | 6.10 | AFTER10 |
| 16.5 (profile) | 8.75 | 15.53 | 9.56 | AFTERU |
| 50 | 25.5 | 27.18 | 26.27 | AFTER50 |
| 100 | 50.5 | 49.85 | 47.29 | AFTER100 |

**Cause.** Every generated message waited a fresh hand-off jitter `U[0, 10 ms]` before
the access layer (ns-3's WAVE `TxMaxDelay`; `v2xw_msg::GenerationTiming` documents it as
a simulator convention, there to stop two CSMA stations of one phase colliding every
period). On semi-persistent scheduling the reservation is made for one message. When the
next message's draw exceeds the reserved offset it arrives after its slot, and the grant
is still inside the 100 ms budget, so no reselection triggers (TS 36.321 5.14.1.1). The
message then waits almost a whole period. The smaller T2, the likelier the miss: 0.40 at
T2 = 1 ms. That is the radio.yaml `selection_window_ms` failure (+6.3 %).

**Fix.** `wiring::generation_timing` gives a PC5 sidelink (LTE-V2X and NR-V2X) no
hand-off jitter. Each node keeps its uniform phase: TR 37.885's random start per UE and
then the exact period. A scenario's own `max_jitter_ms` still wins. Test:
`sensitivity_radio::nr_access_wait_follows_the_selection_window` (T2 = 1 ms under 3 ms,
50 ms in [18, 33] ms). TESTRED

## 5. Open items

- **Sidelink candidate set (X %).** NR only (LTE fixes 20 %). At 40 vehicles a larger X
  raises the exclusion threshold more often and keeps resources that neighbours reserved,
  so collisions rising (0.0002 → 0.0097) at low load is physically possible. The row's
  "down" holds only where concurrent reselections dominate. Settling it needs a dense NR
  base. Not changed without that evidence.
- **Sidelink NF row.** PRR at 600 to 1,000 m is 0.96 on the LTE grid, so the edge is past
  1 km and 3 dB of SNR moves the band by 0.02 %. The row needs a band at the sidelink
  edge.
- **J2945/1 power response (SUPRA)** is not reached: CBR peaked at 0.31 (160 vehicles,
  10 Hz) against the 50 % vMinChanUtil, and the sensitivity recorder has no transmit-power
  metric. The SUPRA step is covered by `v2xw-radio` dcc unit tests only.
