**The Falcon AoI check.** In both Falcon runs `delivery_ratio` is 0, while 20,826 frames
decoded at the PHY. The cause is `net_mtu_refusals` = 80 (40 vehicles × 2 s × 1 Hz). A

## 4. Continued 2026-10-25: the NR selection window, and the rows judged by tests

### 4.1 A defect: the sidelink access wait was U-shaped in T2

Written before the runs (`8adb2281`): on NR-V2X mode 2 (Ali/Todisco profile, µ = 1,
0.5 ms slots, T1 = 2 slots) the resource is picked uniformly in [n + T1, n + T2] and a
periodic message keeps that offset, so the mean access wait is about (T1 + T2) / 2
(TS 38.214 8.1.4; TR 37.885 A.1.1 periodic traffic). Each run: 40 vehicles, 4 s, 10 Hz,
2.3 s wall, 40 MB.

| T2 (ms) | theory (ms) | measured, 486c8cba | measured, no hand-off jitter | after the fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 41.88 | 1.20 | = no-jitter column |
| 5 | 3 | 29.64 | 3.37 | = no-jitter column |
| 10 | 5.5 | 18.86 | 6.10 | = no-jitter column |
| 16.5 (profile) | 8.75 | 15.53 | 9.56 | = no-jitter column |
| 50 | 25.5 | 27.18 | 26.27 | = no-jitter column |
| 100 | 50.5 | 49.85 | 47.29 | = no-jitter column |

**Cause.** Every generated message waited a fresh hand-off jitter `U[0, 10 ms]` before
the access layer (ns-3's WAVE `TxMaxDelay`; `v2xw_msg::GenerationTiming` documents it as
a simulator convention, there to stop two CSMA stations of one phase colliding every
period). On semi-persistent scheduling the reservation is made for one message. When the
next message's draw exceeds the reserved offset it arrives after its slot, and the grant
is still inside the 100 ms budget, so no reselection triggers (TS 36.321 5.14.1.1). The
message then waits almost a whole period. The smaller T2, the likelier the miss: 0.40 at
T2 = 1 ms. That is the radio.yaml `selection_window_ms` failure (+6.3 %).

**Fix.** `wiring::generation_timing` gives a PC5 sidelink (LTE-V2X and NR-V2X) no
hand-off jitter. Each node keeps its uniform phase: TR 37.885's random start per UE and
then the exact period. A scenario's own `max_jitter_ms` still wins. Test:
`sensitivity_radio::nr_access_wait_follows_the_selection_window` (T2 = 1 ms under 3 ms,
50 ms in [18, 33] ms). The fix sets exactly what the no-jitter runs set by hand (`messages.generator.params.max_jitter_ms: 0`), so their column is the expected post-fix result. **Not yet built or run:** the machine-wide build lock stayed with other agents from 14:20 to 16:10, so the new test has not been compiled, shown red without the fix, or shown green with it. The 486c8cba binary's 41.88 ms at T2 = 1 ms is what the test's `< 3 ms` would see without the fix.

**Goldens.** Every LTE-V2X and NR-V2X run's timing moves (802.11p does not). The sidelink tests (radio_access, radio_access_layers, radio_channel, radio_tuning, researcher_config, network_audit_config, and the cv2x rows of sensitivity_radio) and the cv2x/nr rows of radio.yaml and mac-congestion.yaml have not been re-run with the fix. Re-run them before merging, and re-bless any digest they pin.

### 6.1 Sensing on exact phases: one seed's transient, and two deviations from the standards

At merge, `cv2x_sensing_avoids_collisions` went red once the PC5 hand-off jitter was gone
(collision rate 0.0176 with sensing, 0.0020 without). A trace of the recorded run
(`node.tx` slot and sub-channel, `phy.rx` causes) shows this is not a sensing defect.
**One UE carried 697 of the 722 resource-collision losses.** Both of its copies collided
in every period: the initial copy with two neighbours on slot 115 and the retransmission
with a third on slot 122. All of these were first picks, made at power-on with an empty
sensing window. The probability of keeping a resource is 0.8 and a counter runs 5 to 15
periods, so in 3 s the run has 40 blind first picks and only 13 reselections. One unlucky
pair then decides the row.

| collision rate, sensing on / off | 3 s | 8 s |
|---|---|---|
| seed 0xC0FFEE_5EED (the base) | 0.0161 / 0.0020 | 0.0072 / 0.0105 |
| 0x1111 | 0.0009 / 0.0142 | 0.0003 / 0.0082 |
| 0x2222 | 0.0010 / 0.0077 | 0.0005 / 0.0101 |
| 0x3333 | | 0.0142 / 0.0203 |

Other checks on the base seed at 3 s, each with sensing on / off:
- One transmission per transport block: 0.015 / 0.102.
- Keep probability 0: 0.0039 / 0.0078.

In steady state, sensing beats random selection on every seed tried. The sensing rows of
`radio-cv2x.yaml` therefore run 8 s (`base_set`). The `radio.yaml` row gets the same
through a catalogue context (`radio.sidelink.sensing` → `time.duration_s: 8.0`). The
expectations themselves are unchanged.

The trace found two deviations from the standard, both now fixed:

- **Half-duplex exclusion** (`SpsEngine::exclude`). TS 36.213 §14.1.1.6 step 5 excludes
  every resource that an unmonitored subframe could have reserved. The engine noted a
  co-slot neighbour's energy at every receiver, including the transmitting UE. Its in-band
  emission alone reaches every sub-channel. Any such cell lifted the exclusion, so a UE
  that had shared its slot with a neighbour saw that slot as quiet. The unmonitored slot
  is now excluded on every sub-channel, whatever the cells hold. The step-5 S-RSSI average
  now uses only monitored slots (steps 2 and 8), where an own-transmission slot used to
  count as a 0 mW sample. The effect on this base is small: 0.0176 → 0.0161.
  Tests: `sps::a_slot_the_ue_transmitted_in_stays_unsensed_when_a_neighbour_shared_it`
  and `sps::the_srssi_metric_averages_only_the_slots_the_ue_monitored`. Both go red with
  the old rule.
- **NR has no S-RSSI ranking.** TS 38.214 §8.1.4 reports S_A after the exclusion and the
  X % step-up, and the MAC picks from it uniformly (TS 38.321 §5.22.1.1). The 20 %
  lowest-S-RSSI list is LTE's step 8 only. The NR profile (`SpsParams::ali_todisco`) and
  `apply_tuning` both ranked. The NR profile now does not, and `sensing: true` no longer
  adds a ranking on NR. Test: `sps::nr_mode_2_picks_from_the_survivors_without_an_srssi_ranking`
  (60 picks, 0 on loud sub-channels when ranked).

  collision rate is computed from whole-run counters, and `time.warmup_s` does not reach

  either a warm-up that also covers the report's counters, or a pre-run sensing history.

  evidence on the direction.
- **J2945/1 power response.** The direction is measured (above). Judging the magnitude
  against the law needs a per-node CBR and power pairing.
- The other items carried from §5 are unchanged: digitised TR 37.885 and field-test PDR
