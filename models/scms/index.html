SCMS and credential systems¶

Code: crates/v2xw-proto (entities, flows, protocols), with the cryptographic core in crates/v2xw-sec and the run-time wiring in crates/v2xw-engine/src/phase2.rs, scms_topology.rs, credentials.rs, pseudonym_policy.rs and ma_policy.rs. Selected by security.protocol.id (and the same id in actors.backend.protocol).

Two protocols are selectable: the US CAMP SCMS (protocol/scms/camp) and the ETSI ITS PKI (protocol/etsi/ts102941). Settings: security, actors.backend; every default with its source: SCMS constants.

CAMP SCMS (protocol/scms/camp)¶

Implements the US Security Credential Management System as specified by CAMP (SCMS PoC EE Requirements and Specifications, Release 1.2.2), IEEE 1609.2.1 and the SCMS Manager profiles, with Brecht et al., A Security Credential Management System for V2X Communications (IEEE T-ITS 2018) for the design rationale. Modules: crates/v2xw-proto/src/scms/ (run/ holds the behaviour of each flow).

Components¶

Component Role in the engine
End entity (EE) an OBU, RSU or VRU device: holds its enrolment certificate, butterfly keys and pseudonym pool
Device Configuration Manager (DCM) proxies the device's enrolment to the ECA
Enrolment CA (ECA) issues the enrolment certificate (US profile: crlSeries 4, region USA/Canada/Mexico)
Registration Authority (RA) accepts requests, expands cocoon keys, shuffles, routes to the PCA, serves batch downloads, keeps blocklists
Pseudonym / Authorization CA (PCA, the 1609.2.1 ACA) issues pseudonym certificates and encrypts each response to the device
Linkage Authorities LA1, LA2 each hold a linkage-seed chain and produce pre-linkage values the RA cannot open
Location Obscurer Proxy (LOP) hides the device's network identity from the RA
Misbehaviour Authority (MA) receives reports, decides, runs linkage resolution and revocation
CRL Generator (CRLG), CRL store and broadcast signs CRLs on their cadence; distribution by download through the RA and by RSU broadcast
SCMS Manager, five electors, Root CA, Intermediate CA, Policy Generator governance: the elector-signed CTL (any 3 of 5), offline roots, the Global and Local Policy Files

scms/deployment.rs builds the topology from a preset: the default (us-production-2025) models two production providers on one CTL, each with its own roots and online components; a single-SCMS reference topology is kept for comparison. Provider regions and fleet shares are labelled assumptions.

The role separations of Brecht et al. are enforced on the placement: a scenario that co-hosts PCA with RA, PCA with either LA, LA1 with LA2, LOP with RA or MA, or MA with RA, either LA or PCA is refused unless it sets security.protocol.params.relax_separation.

How the layer measures cost¶

The purpose of this layer is to measure what credential management costs (round trips, queueing delay, bytes) and what each party learns. Every backend role is therefore a node with a service queue on modelled links, never a function call:

  • every message between entities crosses a v2xw_proto::net::Link and pays for its bytes; Kernel::dispatch refuses delivery between nodes without a link;
  • every compute step is charged to the entity's queue (an entity fills an Outbox and the kernel decides when its work finished);
  • every flow declares which credential signs what and the stage timestamps it emits (spec::FlowSpec::stages); tests require the emitted stages to equal the declaration;
  • end entities read no backend state; answers reach them only as messages;
  • every byte count on a link has a provenance (sizes::SizeProvenance): the real encoder, a cited constant, or a model-card parameter with a calibration plan.

Butterfly key expansion (v2xw_sec::butterfly, CAMP SCP1)¶

The device uploads two caterpillar public keys \(A = aG\) and \(P = pG\) and two AES-128 expansion keys \(ck\) and \(ek\). With \(\iota = (i, j)\) packed into a 128-bit block \(x = \text{prefix}^{32} \| i^{32} \| j^{32} \| 0^{32}\) (prefix \(0^{32}\) for signing, \(1^{32}\) for encryption):

\[ f(k, \iota) = \big(\mathrm{AES}_k(x{+}1) \oplus (x{+}1) \,\|\, \mathrm{AES}_k(x{+}2) \oplus (x{+}2) \,\|\, \mathrm{AES}_k(x{+}3) \oplus (x{+}3)\big) \bmod n . \]
Party Computes
RA cocoon signing key \(B_{ij} = A + f_1(ck, \iota)\,G\); cocoon encryption key \(J_{ij} = P + f_2(ek, \iota)\,G\)
PCA, explicit certificate certified key \(B_{ij} + cG\), with \(c\) secret to the PCA
PCA, implicit (ECQV) reconstruction value \(P_U = B_{ij} + kG\), \(r = e k + d_{CA}\)
device, explicit \(d = a + f_1(ck, \iota) + c \pmod n\), checked \(dG\) = certified key
device, implicit \(d = e\,b_{ij} + r\) with \(b_{ij} = a + f_1(ck, \iota)\), checked \(dG = eP_U + Q_{CA}\)
device, response opened with \(h_{ij} = p + f_2(ek, \iota)\) (IEEE 1609.2.1-2022 §9.3.4, original variant)

So the RA never learns a certified key, the PCA cannot link two certificates to one device (the cocoon keys are pseudorandom without \(ck\), and the RA shuffles), and the device re-derives every private key itself. Test vectors: CAMP bfkeyexp.txt with every intermediate and the OpenSCMS (Apache-2.0) signing and encryption vectors, plus cocoon keys, explicit and implicit issuance and response encryption per IEEE 1609.2.1-2022 §9.3 (crates/v2xw-sec/tests/official_vectors.rs). Only the original butterfly variant is accepted; the unified and compact unified variants are refused.

Linkage values (v2xw_sec::linkage, CAMP SCP2)¶

Per Linkage Authority \(x \in \{1, 2\}\) with a 16-bit identifier \(la\_id_x\):

\[ \begin{aligned} ls_x(i) &= \operatorname{trunc}_{128}\big(\operatorname{SHA\text{-}256}(la\_id_x \| ls_x(i{-}1) \| 0^{112})\big)\\ plv_x(i, j) &= \operatorname{trunc}_{72}\big(\mathrm{AES}_{ls_x(i)}(la\_id_x \| j \| 0^{80}) \oplus (la\_id_x \| j \| 0^{80})\big)\\ lv(i, j) &= plv_1(i, j) \oplus plv_2(i, j) \end{aligned} \]

Only the PCA ever computes \(lv\); the 9-byte value is the LinkageValue in each certificate. Revocation publishes \(ls_x(i)\) for the current period, never \(ls_x(0)\), and a CRL entry refuses to match an earlier period, so revocation is forward-only; tests check that certificates from before the revocation period stay unlinkable. Two seeds revoke every future certificate of a device, about 40 bytes per entry (Brecht 2018 §VI-F). Vectors: CAMP lv.txt and glv.txt. Group linkage exists and is off by default.

Revocation¶

Active (a linkage-based series-1 CRL is produced, distributed, installed and enforced) and passive (issuance stops and the device runs out of certificates). Revocation latency is decomposed into the stages of the path, from the detector firing to the receivers that install the CRL (revocation_latency_stage). Hash-based CRLs (series 2, 3, 4, 256) cover non-pseudonym certificates. CRLs are real Ieee1609Dot2Crl encodings with their sizes from the encoder.

Calendar¶

time.calendar: real (default) runs one-week i-periods counted from the US SCMS epoch, 2015-01-06 04:00 US Eastern, with US Eastern daylight-saving time, US federal holidays and business days (scms/calendar.rs) for rules such as CRL publication within one business day. compressed allows a shorter i_period_s; every output labels it. time.fast_forward windows let a run cover weeks of credential activity.

ETSI ITS PKI (protocol/etsi/ts102941)¶

crates/v2xw-proto/src/etsi/. Enrolment with the Enrolment Authority, standard and butterfly authorization (TS 102 941 §6.2.3.5) with the Authorization Authority, authorization-ticket batch download, ECTL issuance by the Trust List Manager and publication at the CPOC, CA-only CRLs signed by the Root CA, fetches from the Distribution Centre that return only newer list versions (§6.3, §6.3.3), and TS 103 759 misbehaviour reporting with an EA blocklist. Certificates and cryptographic components are sized by the real COER encoder and cited constants; the TS 102 941 message framing that no clause fixes is a card parameter with a calibration plan.

Threshold protocols (crates/v2xw-proto/src/threshold/)¶

A t-of-n committee replaces one server's private-key operation (security.threshold names which roles). Shipped: FROST (RFC 9591), threshold ECDSA (CGGMP21), a t-of-n umbrella authority, and t-of-n linkage-seed release. The group arithmetic is not executed; the rounds, per-round message sizes, per-round compute from cited anchors, what each party learns, and slow, offline and malicious members (identifiable abort and restart) are. Flows: distributed key generation, signing, proactive refresh.

Post-quantum provisioning¶

v2xw_proto::hybrid applies the signature and KEM schemes of security.md to the credential system: what entities pay and carry when certificates are hybrid or post-quantum, and provisioning without butterfly expansion.

Pseudonym change¶

How often a device switches certificates (security.pseudonym_change: SAE J2945/1 CERTCHG, the NYC pilot rule, C2C-CC, mix zones), and the eavesdropper that measures the result, are on Privacy.
