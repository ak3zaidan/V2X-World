## Screening

Unique records across all queries: 5958. Kept after the inclusion screen: 1899.
Screen (automatic, on title + abstract + venue): a vehicular term (V2X, VANET, C-ITS, vehicular, connected vehicle, automotive, RSU/OBU, DSRC, ...) plus a PKI/credential term. high = a core term (SCMS, CCMS, butterfly key, linkage value, pseudonym certificate, VPKI, PKI, revocation/CRL, misbehaviour authority/report, 1609.2, trust list, ECQV/implicit certificate, enrolment/authorisation authority, certificate provisioning/distribution); medium = vehicular security with pseudonym/certificate/credential/misbehaviour elements; low = vehicular authentication/signature work without a PKI element, or non-vehicular PKI/revocation background. Records without abstracts were enriched from Semantic Scholar (batch) and Crossref before screening.
