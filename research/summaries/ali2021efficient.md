# An Efficient and Provably Secure ECC-Based Conditional Privacy-Preserving Authentication for Vehicle-to-Vehicle Communication in VANETs
Ikram Ali, Yong Chen, N. Ullah, Rajesh Kumar et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2021.3050399 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs), safety messages are exchanged among vehicles and between vehicles and infrastructure to ensure passengers’ safety and efficiency in traffic. The source authentication as well as integrity checking of these messages are very necessary for a receiver.
**Approach:** In this paper, a provably secure and efficient certificateless short signature-based conditional privacy-preserving authentication (CLSS-CPPA) scheme for V2V communication is designed.
**Tools and scale:** The CLSS-CPPA scheme ensures security against type-I and type-II attackers with respect to existential unforgeability against adaptively chosen message attacks (EUF-CMA) under a hardness assumption of the elliptic curve discrete logarithm problem (ECDLP) in the random oracle model (ROM).
**Results:** The proposed scheme significantly improves performance in terms of computational and communication costs in comparison with state-of-the-art schemes.
