# Pseudonym change strategy based on the reputation of the neighbouring vehicles in VANETs
Luz Marina Santos Jaimes, Edson dos Santos Moreira · DYNA 2019 · https://doi.org/10.15446/dyna.v86n211.75602 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Unnecessary pseudonym changes when no attackers present.
**Approach:** Synchronous change conditioned on neighbours' reputation status in beacons.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation on a real scenario.
**Results:** Fewer pseudonyms used with similar anonymity.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
