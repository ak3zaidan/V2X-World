# A novel transformer-based explainable AI approach using SHAP for intrusion detection in vehicular ad hoc networks
Waqar Khan, Waqar Khan, Jawad Elsayed Ahmad, Nada Abdulaziz Alasbali, Alanoud Al Mazroa, Mohammed S. Alshehri et al. · Computer Networks 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.comnet.2025.111575 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** False positional information in VANETs.
**Approach:** Transformer-based IDS with SHAP explanations.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi extension.
**Results:** 96.15% multi-class and 98.28% binary accuracy; MCC and Kappa reported.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — offline ML.
**Novelty implications:** Saturated.
