# Hierarchical Hybrid Trust Management Scheme in SDN-Enabled VANETs
Ming Mao, Peng Yi, Tao Hu, Zhen Zhang, Xiangyu Lǚ, Jingwei Lei · Mobile Information Systems 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2021/7611619 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Trust management delay and low malicious node recognition.
**Approach:** Hierarchical hybrid trust in SDN-VANET combining V2V trust and RSU auxiliary trust.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Better resistance to simple, selective and time-dependent attacks; higher accuracy.
