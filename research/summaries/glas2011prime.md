# Prime Field ECDSA Signature Processing for Reconfigurable Embedded Systems
B. Glas, O. Sander, Vitali Stuckert, K. Müller-Glaser et al. · International Journal of Reconfigurable Computing 2011 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2011/836460 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Growing ubiquity and safety relevance of embedded systems strengthen the need to protect their functionality against malicious attacks. Communication and system authentication by digital signature schemes is a major issue in securing such systems.
**Approach:** This contribution presents a complete ECDSA signature processing system over prime fields for bit lengths of up to 256 on reconfigurable hardware.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** By using dedicated hardware implementation, the performance can be improved by up to two orders of magnitude compared to microcontroller implementations.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — Bespoke VANET/IoV authentication or privacy scheme (CPPA/certificateless/batch/blockchain style), evaluated by analysis or small simulation; not IEEE 1609.2/SCMS and no closed-loop safety evaluation.
**Novelty implications:** Adds to the large body of standalone authentication schemes; does not touch end-to-end SCMS simulation or safety impact of security.
