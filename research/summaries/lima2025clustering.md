# Clustering-based Spoofing Detection and Mitigation in Autonomous Vehicles
D. V. de Lima, Kevin Herman Muraro Gularte, João Paulo Javidi da Costa, Danielli Nogueira Alves da Silva, G. A. Santos, K. Kastell · International Conference on Transparent Optical Networks 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icton67126.2025.11125342 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs) enable a collective perception of traffic to achieve safety and efficiency.
**Approach:** Spoofers can compromise this system by injecting misleading data, undermining trust and reliability. This paper extends the concept of trust and reliability in VANETs by integrating clustering techniques to mitigate spoofer activity in a simulated traffic scenario.
