# A Comparative Study of PKI and Blockchain-Based Security Architectures in V2X Communication Systems
Shahul H. Abubakkar · 2025 International Conference on Vehicular Technology and Transportation Systems (ICVTTS) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icvtts67119.2025.11296537 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Which of PKI and blockchain suits V2X security?
**Approach:** Synthesis of standards and studies with order-of-magnitude metrics for latency, revocation timeliness, overhead, scalability. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Analytical comparison.
**Results:** PKI gives millisecond authentication and mature deployment; permissioned blockchain improves transparency and near-real-time consensus-driven misbehaviour handling and revocation.
