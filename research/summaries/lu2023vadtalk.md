# VADtalk: An Internet of Vehicles Platform Facilitating Anomaly Detection Modeling and Deployment for Self-Driving Vehicles
Yi-Chen Lu, Shun-Ren Yang, P. Lin, Chih-Wei Huang · International Conference on Wireless Communications and Mobile Computing 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iwcmc58020.2023.10182671 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In recent years, self-driving vehicles have gradually been appearing on the road, but society has also begun to worry about the possibility of accidents caused by the anomaly self-driving system.
**Approach:** Many researchers have begun to study the anomaly detection of self-driving vehicles, and each has proposed different detection algorithms. However, since self-driving vehicles are not yet popular, how to collect data, simulate attacks, and verify and compare multiple algorithms is a major obstacle to research.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** When developers complete model uploading and setting through the GUI, the platform will automatically collect self-driving data, train the model, and even verify the operation of the model using a self-driving simulator, and then provide the results to the developer.
