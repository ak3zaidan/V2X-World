# Environment-Aware Channel Prediction for Vehicular Communications: A Multimodal Visual Feature Fusion Framework
Xuejian Zhang, Ruisi He, Minseok Kim, Inocent Calist et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02396 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The deep integration of communication with intelligence and sensing, as a defining vision of 6G, renders environment-aware channel prediction a key enabling technology. As a representative 6G application, vehicular communications require accurate and forward-looking channel prediction under stringent reliability, latency, and adaptability demands.
**Approach:** This paper proposes an environment-aware channel prediction framework based on multimodal visual feature fusion.
**Tools and scale:** Experiments on a synchronized urban V2I measurement dataset yield the best root mean square error (RMSE) of 3.26 dB for PL, RMSEs of 37.66 ns, 5.05 degrees, and 5.08 degrees for DS, ASA, and ASD, respectively, and mean/median APS cosine similarities of 0.9342/0.9571, demonstrating strong accuracy, ...
**Results:** As a result, joint prediction of path loss (PL), delay spread (DS), azimuth spread of arrival (ASA), azimuth spread of departure (ASD), and APS is achieved.
