# A Multilayer Perceptron-Based Distributed Intrusion Detection System for Internet of Vehicles
Ayesha Anzer, Mourad Elhadef · IEEE International Conference on Collaboration and Internet Computing (CIC) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cic.2018.00066 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Security of Internet of vehicles (IoV) is critical as it promises to provide with safer and secure driving. IoV relies on VANETs which is based on V2V (Vehicle to Vehicle) communication.
**Approach:** In this paper, we concentrate on intrusion detection in IoV and propose a multilayer perceptron (MLP) neural network to detect intruders or attackers on an IoV network.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** A thorough simulation study demonstrates the effectiveness of the new MLP-based intrusion detection system.
