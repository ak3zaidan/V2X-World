# A Co-Simulation Platform for Mixed Traffic Flow of Vehicular Networks
Yi Zhi, Baiyi Li, Li-Na Zhu, J. Ben Othman · ICC 2026 - IEEE International Conference on Communications 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc59461.2026.11588115 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The development of Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) and autonomous driving technologies are accelerating the transition of vehicular networks toward mixed traffic scenario, where human-driven, assisted-driving, and fully autonomous vehicles coexist. In such scenarios, traffic dynamics significantly influence communication performance, while the safety and efficiency of transportation systems rely on timely and reliable V2X communication.
**Approach:** To address this issue, we propose a cross-domain co-simulation platform tailored for mixed traffic vehicular networks.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: SUMO, OMNeT++, Veins, 5G. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** The platform integrates SUMO, OMNeT++, and Veins into a unified architecture that tightly couples vehicular mobility with cellular V2X communications. On the other hand, the OMNeT++ module implements a standardized 5G protocol stack along with multiple service models, significantly improving the system’s fidelity, scalability, and adaptability to diverse service requirements. Simulation results demonstrate that our platform accurately reflects performance dynamics across diverse traffic conditions, provides dependable traffic load forecasting, and achieves efficient resource allocation.
