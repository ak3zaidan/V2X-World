# Beyond PKI: A DNSSEC Delegation Approach for Scalable Dynamic Credential Management in IoT
Daniel Díaz-Sánchez, F. Almenárez, Celeste Campo et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2025.3600371 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Internet of Things (IoT) systems that manage data across cloud, fog, and edge environments—and the devices that consume those services—face substantial challenges in confidentiality, privacy, and authentication.
**Approach:** In this article, we present a collision-based DNSSEC signature-delegation mechanism designed to overcome these scalability limitations.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** By allowing a central DNS-A (DNS) authority to delegate signing responsibilities to local DNS servers, our approach reduces certificate-management overhead and enables a dynamic, hierarchical trust model.
