# Congestion-based certificate omission in VANETs
Michael Feiri, Jonathan Petit, Frank Kargl · VANET@MOBICOM 2012 · https://doi.org/10.1145/2307888.2307915 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Telematic awareness of nearby vehicles is a basic foundation of electronic safety applications in Vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANETs). This awareness is achieved by frequently broadcasting beacon messages to nearby vehicles that announce a vehicle's location and other data like heading and speed.
**Approach:** Such safety-related beacons require strong integrity protection and high availability, two properties that are hard to combine because the communication and computation overhead introduced by security mechanisms affects reliability.
