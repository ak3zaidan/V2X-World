# Deep Learning-based QoS Prediction with Innate Knowledge of the Radio Access Network
Jose Perdomo, Apostolos Kousaridas, Chan Zhou, Jose F. Monserrat · 2021 IEEE Global Communications Conference (GLOBECOM) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/globecom46510.2021.9685405 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** QoS prediction with limited training data.
**Approach:** Wireless-knowledge penalties (cell load, channel, UL interference) in DNN cost function.
**Tools and scale:** System-level simulations, teleoperated driving.
**Results:** Improved UL data-rate prediction with reduced training data.
