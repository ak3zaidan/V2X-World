**Problem:** Connected vehicle (CV) technologies are emerging as a crucial technology for improving safety on future roadways.
**Approach:** Wireless vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communication allows CVs to exchange information that provides enhanced awareness and supports better driving decisions, especially in high-risk or non-line-of-sight scenarios.
