# Demand Response Management via Real-Time Pricing for Microgrid with Electric Vehicles under Cyber-Attack
Hong-Bo Zhu, Hui Yin, Xue Feng, Xinxin Zhang, Zongyao Wang · Electronics 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics12061321 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The initiative of users to participate in power grid operation is a key factor in realizing the optimal allocation of power.
**Approach:** Demand response (DR) management mechanisms based on real-time pricing (RTP) can effectively promote the enthusiasm of users, stimulate the efficiency of microgrids for power dispatch, and achieve the goasl of power peak shifting and valley filling. In this paper, we consider a microgrid composed of several energy providers (EPs) and multiple users, and each user is equipped with several electric vehicles (EVs).
