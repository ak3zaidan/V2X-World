# An ECC-Diffie Hellman Authentication Scheme with PK-based Revocation for VANETs
Laxmi Chandolia, Om Pal · Research Square 2026 · https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-10228660/v1 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Costly or leaky VANET authentication.
**Approach:** ECDSA, ECDH, HMAC with dynamic pseudonyms and a public-key-based revocation list; GPS time for replay protection.
**Tools and scale:** ROM proof; cost analysis; no PHY or latency modelling.
**Results:** Lower computation and communication cost than compared schemes.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Excludes physical layer, end-to-end latency and transmission delay from analysis.
- Extend to large-scale IoV and edge/5G integration.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — revocation list design, analysed without simulation.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
