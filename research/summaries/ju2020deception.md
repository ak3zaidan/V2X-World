# Deception Attack Detection and Estimation for a Local Vehicle in Vehicle Platooning Based on a Modified UFIR Estimator
Zhiyang Ju, Hui Zhang, Ying Tan · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2020.2966672 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this article, the position sensor deception attack detection and estimation problem is investigated for a local vehicle in a vehicle platoon.
**Approach:** In a platoon system, the position measurement is critical as the distances between neighboring vehicles are relatively small. However, the position measurement of vehicles is usually vulnerable to deception attacks as it relies on external information, such as GPS and environment information from cameras.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: platoon/CACC attacks).
