# A survey of V2V channel modeling for VANET simulations
Herve Boeglen, Benoi Hilt, P Lorenz, J Ledy et al. · 2011 Eighth International Conference on Wireless On-Demand Network Systems and Services 2011 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wons.2011.5720179 · relevance: medium · basis: full text

**Problem:** V2V channel modelling for VANET simulations.
**Approach:** Survey of wideband TDL structures and three simulation-ready V2V channel models; two VANET environments.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the extracted sections.
**Results:** The V2V channel strongly impacts VANET real-time communications though many simulations underestimate it.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Few V2V measurements for statistically significant statements
- regional variation needs more measurements
- multi-antenna systems must be considered.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — none.
**Novelty implications:** Nothing of our candidate directions is ruled out.
