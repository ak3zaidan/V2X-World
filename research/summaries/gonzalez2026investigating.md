# Investigating the Impact of Traffic Demand, Fleet Electrification, and Driving Behavior on Urban Vehicle Emissions Using a SUMO-Based Simulation
Cesar González, Juan Sánchez, Helbert Espitia · Vehicles 2026 · https://doi.org/10.3390/vehicles8080196 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Urban transport emissions are a major contributor to climate change and urban air pollution.
**Approach:** Although previous studies have demonstrated that traffic demand, fleet electrification, and driving behavior individually influence vehicular emissions, their combined effects under different congestion conditions remain insufficiently understood. This study investigates the interactions among these factors using the microscopic traffic simulator SUMO (Simulation of Urban MObility).
