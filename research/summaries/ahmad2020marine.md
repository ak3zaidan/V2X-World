# MARINE: Man-in-the-Middle Attack Resistant Trust Model in Connected Vehicles
Farhan Ahmad, Fatih Kurugollu, Asma Adnane, Rasheed Hussain et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2020.2967568 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc network (VANET), a novel technology, holds a paramount importance within the transportation domain due to its abilities to increase traffic efficiency and safety. Connected vehicles propagate sensitive information which must be shared with the neighbors in a secure environment.
**Approach:** In this article, we propose a novel trust model, namely, MiTM attack resistance trust model in connected vehicles (MARINE), which identifies dishonest nodes performing MiTM attacks in an efficient way as well as revokes their credentials.
**Tools and scale:** Extensive simulations are carried out to evaluate the performance and accuracy of MARINE rigorously across three MiTM attacker models and the benchmarked trust model.
**Results:** The simulation results show that for a network containing 35% of MiTM attackers, MARINE outperforms the state-of-the-art trust model by 15%, 18%, and 17% improvements in precision, recall, and $F$ -score, respectively.
