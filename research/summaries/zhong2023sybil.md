# Sybil Attack Detection in VANETs: An LSTM-Based BiGAN Approach
Yongchao Zhong, Haonan Yang, Ying Li, Bo Yang, Xiaoqi Li, Qiuling Yue et al. · 2023 International Conference on Data Security and Privacy Protection (DSPP) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/dspp58763.2023.10404993 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Real-time Sybil detection.
**Approach:** LSTM-based BiGAN with multivariate Gaussian input and abnormal score.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi.
**Results:** 93% average accuracy over five attacks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — offline.
**Novelty implications:** Saturated.
