# A Novel Mechanism for Misbehavior Detection in Vehicular Networks
Edivaldo Pastori Valentini, Geraldo Pereira Rocha Filho, Robson Eduardo De Grande, Caetano Mazzoni Ranieri, Lourenço Alves Pereira Júnior, Rodolfo Ipolito Meneguette · IEEE Access 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2023.3292055 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** DoS in V2V communication.
**Approach:** DAMASCO IDS using Median Absolute Deviation of per-node MAC packet counts to flag outliers, block, and keep a reputation list.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation with realistic urban mobility.
**Results:** 3% false positive rate and no false negatives.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — MAC-layer DoS detection.
**Novelty implications:** Adjacent.
