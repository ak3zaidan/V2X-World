# A Replay Attack Against ISAC Based on OFDM
Georgios Chrysanidis, Yanwei Liu, Antonios Argyriou · IEEE Access 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3359680 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper presents a replay attack that can compromise the functionality of joint radar communication (JRC) systems that use orthogonal frequency division multiplexing (OFDM) by creating ghost target signatures. The attack is particularly dangerous in connected autonomous vehicles that deploy JRC.
**Approach:** To evaluate the impact of this attack we calculate the Range-Doppler response for different system configurations of an 802.11-based OFDM JRC system and different spoofing strategies in a representative vehicular system.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** Our results show that JRC based on OFDM can easily be spoofed and it may lead to inability of a autonomous vehicular system to operate safely.
