# Design a Process to Detect and Mitigate the Malicious Attacks in VANET Using Ml Approach
Prakash Sontakke, Nilkanth B. Chopade · International Journal of Intelligent Systems and Applications in Engineering 2024 · https://ijisae.org/index.php/IJISAE/article/view/5050 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Detecting and blocking malicious vehicles, including certified insiders.
**Approach:** HCMNDA hybrid cooperative malicious node detection with cluster-head accusation and fuzzy/ML prediction.
**Tools and scale:** Network simulation; CA-validated addresses assumed.
**Results:** Fewer false positives than non-FGA schemes, which treat every packet loss as an attack.
