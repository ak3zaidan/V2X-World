# Avoiding SDN Application Conflicts With Digital Twins: Design, Models and Proof of Concept
Marco Polverini, Andrés García-López, Juan Luis Herrera, Santiago García-Gil, Francesco G. Lavacca, Antonio Cianfrani, Jaime Galan-Jimenez · IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnsm.2026.3652800 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Software-Defined Networking (SDN) enables flexible and programmable control over network behavior through the deployment of multiple control applications.
**Approach:** However, when these applications operate simultaneously, each pursuing different and potentially conflicting objectives, unexpected interactions may arise, leading to policy violations, performance degradation, or inefficient resource usage. This paper presents a Digital Twin (DT)-based framework for the early detection of such application-level conflicts.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: emulated, simulation.
**Results:** The performance evaluation shows that three out of four DT models achieve a precision above 90%, while the minimum recall across all models exceeds 84%. These results demonstrate that the framework can accurately detect conflicts and deliver feedback fast enough to support timely network adaptation.
