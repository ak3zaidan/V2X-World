# Intelligent Digital Twin-Based Software-Defined Vehicular Networks
Liang Zhao, Guangjie Han, Zhuhui Li, Lei Shu · IEEE Network 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mnet.011.1900587 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Software-defined vehicular networks lack data collection, prediction, verification and validation of networking schemes before deployment.
**Approach:** IDT-SDVN architecture: an intelligent digital twin of the vehicular network that learns and iteratively updates networking schemes; challenges and open issues discussed; case study.
**Tools and scale:** Not specified in abstract.
**Results:** Case study reports significant performance improvement (no numbers in abstract).

- Presents challenges and open issues of IDT-SDVN (not enumerated in abstract).
