# Design, Modeling, and Control of a Hardware-in-the-Loop Testbed for Off-Road Vehicles
Gaonan Zhao, Jie Yao, Connor P. Edson, Zongxuan Sun · ASME Letters in Dynamic Systems and Control 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1115/1.4066465 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract This paper presents the design, modeling, and control of a hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) testbed for off-road vehicles.
**Approach:** The proposed HIL testbed employs a transient hydrostatic dynamometer to load a diesel engine to emulate any loading cycles of a wheel loader, which is a representative off-road vehicle. A fully validated wheel loader model is used to calculate the engine load, including both the drive and work functions.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting. The available text does not describe a V2X simulator, radio stack, traffic model or SCMS-style PKI in enough detail to report; scale not stated.
**Results:** The developed HIL testbed is used to demonstrate more than 26% energy benefits of automated wheel loaders through systematic optimization compared with human-operated wheel loaders.
