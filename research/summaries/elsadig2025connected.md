# Connected Vehicles Security: A Lightweight Machine Learning Model to Detect VANET Attacks
Muawia Abdelmagid Elsadig, Abdelrahman Altigani, Yasir Abdelgadir Mohamed, Abdul Hakim H. M. Mohamed et al. · World Electric Vehicle Journal 2025 · https://doi.org/10.3390/wevj16060324 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) aim to manage traffic, prevent accidents, and regulate various parts of traffic. However, owing to their nature, the security of VANETs remains a significant concern.
**Approach:** This study provides insightful information regarding VANET vulnerabilities and attacks. It investigates a number of security models that have recently been introduced to counter VANET security attacks with a focus on machine learning detection methods.
**Tools and scale:** CICIDS2017 (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** It achieved an accuracy rate of 99.8%, outperforming all benchmark classifiers, including AdaBoost, decision tree (DT), K-nearest neighbors (KNNs), and multi-layer perceptron (MLP). In terms of processing cost, it consumes the least processing time, requiring only 69%, 59%, 35%, and 1.4% of the AdaBoost, DT, KNN, and MLP processing times, respectively.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- This confirms that several challenges remain unsolved.
