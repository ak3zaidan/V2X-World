# Data Manipulation Attacks in Electricity Market with Generative Adversarial Network for Electric Vehicle Aggregator
Poornachandratejasvi Laxman Bhattar, Naran M Pindoriya, Anurag Sharma · 2024 IEEE 4th International Conference on Sustainable Energy and Future Electric Transportation (SEFET) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/sefet61574.2024.10718193 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The electrification of the transportation sector is reducing its dependency on fossil fuels and promoting sustainability.
**Approach:** Electric vehicles (EVs) play a substantial role in transportation sectors. The growing number of EVs is increasing the opportunity for their participation in the electricity market in an aggregated form.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** An FDI attack with an application of GAN is showcased in this work for transactive energy management. The results indicate the susceptibility of EVs, EVAs, and DSO in transactive energy management.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, the dependence on ICTs can make the EVAs and EVs vulnerable to cyber-attacks and cyber-threats.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
