# Privacy-Preserving and Zero-Trust Vehicle Renting in VANETs: Secure Authentication, Driver Accountability, and Sybil Resistance
Madhab Chandra Jena, Tamal Paul, Om Krishna Jena · International Journal of Creative and Open Research in Engineering and Management 2025 · https://doi.org/10.55041/ijcope.v2i9.221 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Vehicle renting in VANETs needs temporary authorisation, renter privacy, Sybil resistance and accountability.
**Approach:** PP-ZTVR conceptual framework: pseudonymous authentication, controlled pseudonym issuance, rental-session binding, zero-trust authorisation, conditional accountability and revocation.
**Tools and scale:** Conceptual; evaluation metrics defined but not run.
**Results:** No experimental results.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Requires experimental evaluation; future work: implement in SUMO with Veins or NS-3 and compare quantitatively.
- Future work: formal verification against impersonation, replay, MITM, Sybil.
- Future work: post-quantum lattice extensions and optional blockchain audit/revocation.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — conceptual framework.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
