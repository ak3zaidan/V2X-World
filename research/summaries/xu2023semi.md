# Semi-Automatic Geographic Information System Framework for Creating Photo-Realistic Digital Twin Cities to Support Autonomous Driving Research
Haowen Xu, Yunli Shao, Jianfei Chen, Chieh (Ross) Wang et al. · Transportation Research Record: Journal of the Transportation Research Board 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1177/03611981231205884 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Digital twin cities are frequently used in vehicle and traffic simulations to render realistic on-road driving scenarios under various traffic and environmental conditions.
**Approach:** This paper presents a semi-automated spatial framework to construct realistic 3D digital twin cities to support autonomous driving research using readily available geographic information system (GIS) data and 3D prefabricated (prefab) models.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: carla, simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** The paper closes with a showcase to demonstrate the quality and usability of a digital twin city created to replicate the Shallowford Road corridor in Chattanooga in both Unity and Unreal engine-based virtual environment.
