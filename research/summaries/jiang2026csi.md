# CSI-MAE: A Masked Autoencoder-based Channel Foundation Model
Jun Jiang, Xiaolong Ruan, Shu-Gong Xu · venue unknown 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03789 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Self-Supervised Learning (SSL) has emerged as a key technique in machine learning, tackling challenges such as limited labeled data, high annotation costs, and variable wireless channel conditions. It is essential for developing Channel Foundation Models (CFMs), which extract latent features from channel state information (CSI) and adapt to different wireless settings.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose CSI-MAE, a generalized CFM leveraging masked autoencoder for cross-scenario generalization.
