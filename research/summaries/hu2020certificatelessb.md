# Certificateless Aggregate Signature schemes for Privacy Protection of Security Anlysis and Improvement
Xiao-Ming Hu, Wen-An Tan, Chuang Ma · 2020 International Conference on Computer Science and Management Technology (ICCSMT) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccsmt51754.2020.00071 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-Hoc Network (VANET) is an open environment. In VANET, it needs protect some privacy information of the vehicle, such as the real identity of the vehicle, the number of the vehicle.
**Approach:** In this paper, we present two CLAS schemes proposed recently and also give a security analysis on the two CLAS schemes. At the same time, we also show that second CLAS scheme does not satisfy the tractability property.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** Certificateless aggregate signature (CLAS) scheme can reduce the calculation cost and communication cost due to the aggregation property, which is important part of cryptography. By some security analysis and efficiency analysis, it can find that the improved CLAS schemes both not only can overcome the security problem but also are still efficient.
