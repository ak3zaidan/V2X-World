# A Maneuver Coordination Analysis Using Artery V2X Simulation Framework
João Oliveira, Emanuel Vieira, João Almeida, Joaquim Ferreira, Paulo C. Bartolomeu · Electronics 2024 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics13234813 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Using ETSI Maneuver Coordination Messages for cooperative path planning.
**Approach:** Implements MCM and MCS with a cooperative driving system, trajectory approach and collision handling in Artery + SUMO.
**Tools and scale:** Artery, SUMO.
**Results:** V2X ensures safety and efficiency (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Preliminary work.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — ETSI facilities-layer service in Artery; no security.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
