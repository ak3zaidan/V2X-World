# Enhanced EAADE: a quantum-resilient and privacy-preserving authentication protocol for secure data exchange in vehicular social networks
Reem Alrashdi, J. Altmemi, Mahmood A. Al-Shareeda, A. Al-Hchaimi et al. · Scientific Reports 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-34201-1 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** VSN authentication relies on quantum-vulnerable ECC/pairings.
**Approach:** Enhanced EAADE: lattice authentication with ephemeral pseudonyms, spatial cloaking and federated learning.
**Tools and scale:** AVISPA; experimental comparisons.
**Results:** 44.96% less computation, 22.16% lower communication, 17.65% less authentication delay, 23.64% lower packet loss than baselines.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Lattice-based PQC imposes overhead limitations (stated as a limitation for future research)
**Overlap with our engine:** none — Bespoke.
**Novelty implications:** None.
