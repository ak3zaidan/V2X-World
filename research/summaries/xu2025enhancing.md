# Enhancing 5G-based Localization in Dynamic Environments through Network Digital Twins
Zheng-Cheng Xu, Silvia Mura, F. Linsalata, Lorenzo Cazzella et al. · Global Communications Conference 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/globecom59602.2025.11431735 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** 5G localisation in dynamic urban V2X environments.
**Approach:** NDT combining ray tracing with real vehicular traffic data; NLOS-exploiting precoding.
**Tools and scale:** Ray tracing plus real traffic data.
**Results:** Position error bound reduced about 35%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — couples real traffic with radio propagation in a twin; no security.
**Novelty implications:** No impact on our directions.
