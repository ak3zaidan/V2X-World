# Extending OMNeT++ Simulator to Secure Vehicular Communication under Blackhole Attack
Gerardo Marotta, Floriano De Rango · Proceedings of the 11th International Conference on Simulation and Modeling Methodologies, Technologies and Applications 2021 · https://doi.org/10.5220/0010568700002995 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** VANETs need security against blackhole attacks.
**Approach:** Security module in OMNeT++ adding cryptography and authentication; attack scenarios and mitigations.
**Tools and scale:** OMNeT++.
**Results:** Small overhead increase while maintaining performance under attack (dropped packets, sent packets, delay).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — attacks and crypto countermeasures simulated in OMNeT++, but routing-layer blackhole, no SCMS.
**Novelty implications:** Simulated crypto against routing attacks exists; not PKI lifecycle.
