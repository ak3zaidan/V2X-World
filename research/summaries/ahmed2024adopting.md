# Adopting Long-Short Term Memory to predict reliability in different use cases in autonomous vehicles in 6G
Arshee Ahmed, Haroon Rasheed, Muslim Jameel Syed · 2024 International Conference on Emerging Trends in Smart Technologies (ICETST) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icetst62952.2024.10737988 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Predicting vehicular network reliability.
**Approach:** LSTM on simulation-generated data.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation parameters from prior work.
**Results:** LSTM beats other ML; excluding driver behaviour reduces reliability by 10%, excluding PRF/coding 3% more.
