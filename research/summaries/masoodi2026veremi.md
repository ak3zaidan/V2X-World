# VeReMi-Graph v1.0: A Temporal Attributed Graph Dataset for Vehicular Misbehavior Detection
Faheem Masoodi · Mendeley Data 2026 · https://doi.org/10.17632/s6vvgcnsvz · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Lack of standardised graph datasets for MBD.
**Approach:** VeReMi-Graph: temporal attributed graphs from VeReMi with leakage-aware features and masks.
**Tools and scale:** 225 archives, 22,500 snapshots, about 2.0 million nodes, 39.1 million edges.
**Results:** Dataset release.
