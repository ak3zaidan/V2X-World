# A Security Evaluation Framework for Intelligent Connected Vehicles Based on Attack Chains
Qi Li, Jinxin Zuo, Ruohan Cao, Yibo Zhang, Jianrui Chen, Qiang Liu et al. · IEEE Network 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mnet.2023.3333545 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the rapid development of intelligent connected vehicles (ICVs), their intelligent, remote, and high-speed mobility characteristics have led to an expansion of their attack surface, and thus an increase in security requirements.
**Approach:** Consequently, security research for ICVs has become a current research focus. However, existing security risk monitoring measures lack linkage with attack behavior, and security defense strategies cannot fully and promptly respond to security risk levels and system vulnerabilities.
