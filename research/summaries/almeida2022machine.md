# Machine Learning Based Propagation Loss Module for Enabling Digital Twins of Wireless Networks in ns-3
Eduardo Nuno Almeida, Mohammed Rushad, Sumanth Reddy Kota, Akshat Nambiar, Hardik L. Harti, Chinmay Gupta et al. · Proceedings of the 2022 Workshop on ns-3 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3532577.3532607 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Trace-based ns-3 simulation reproduces testbed conditions only for identical topology, mobility and node count.
**Approach:** MLPL module for ns-3: ML-learned deterministic path loss plus stochastic fast fading from testbed traces.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3; SIMBED testbed traces; compared with Friis and log-distance.
**Results:** Accurately predicts real propagation loss and reproduces testbed conditions (numbers not extracted).
