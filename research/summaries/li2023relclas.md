# RelCLAS: A Reliable Malicious KGC-Resistant Certificateless Aggregate Signature Protocol for Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Xincheng Li, Xinchun Yin, Jian-Ting Ning · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2023.3285402 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc network (VANET) enables a more efficient and secure traffic environment by integrating several emerging technologies. The technology of certificateless aggregate signature (CLAS) is widely utilized to guarantee secure communications in VANET.
**Approach:** In this article, a reliable malicious KGC-resistant CLAS protocol, called RelCLAS, is proposed to eradicate this problem.
**Tools and scale:** Extensive simulations illustrate that RelCLAS is efficient, reliable, and feasible.
**Results:** Formal security proof demonstrates that RelCLAS satisfies the fundamental security requirements of VANET.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.

**Novelty implications:** Adds to the large body of standalone authentication schemes; does not touch end-to-end SCMS simulation or safety impact of security.
