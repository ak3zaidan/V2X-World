# Physical Invariant Based Attack Detection for Autonomous Vehicles: Survey, Vision, and Challenges
Francis Akowuah, Fanxin Kong · 2021 Fourth International Conference on Connected and Autonomous Driving (MetroCAD) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/metrocad51599.2021.00014 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Automobiles continue to become more autonomous and connected as increasingly integrating with information technology. Meanwhile, this advance also comes with a higher risk of various security violations on vehicles.
**Approach:** In this paper, we study how to detect attacks on autonomous vehicles, and specially focus on physical invariant-based attack detection. We first discuss existing research efforts of PI-based attack detection and classify them according to the knowledge of physical invariants and sensor redundancy.
