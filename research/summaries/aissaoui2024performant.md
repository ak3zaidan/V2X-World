# A Performant Quantum-Resistant KEM for Constrained Hardware: Optimized HQC
Ridwane Aissaoui, Jean-Christophe Deneuville, Christophe Guerber, Alain Pirovano · Proceedings of the 21st International Conference on Security and Cryptography 2024 · https://doi.org/10.5220/0012757800003767 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Running PQ KEM HQC on constrained hardware (IoT, small UAVs).
**Approach:** Algorithmic optimisation of HQC on an RTOS testbed.
**Tools and scale:** RTOS-based constrained hardware test bed.
**Results:** Key generation, encapsulation and decapsulation about 10x faster; significantly lower RAM.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none: PQ KEM performance; engine focuses on PQ signatures.
**Novelty implications:** Background for PQ cost on embedded devices.
