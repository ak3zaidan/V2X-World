# Digital Twin-Enabled Federated Misbehavior Detection Model for Internet of Vehicles Networks
Burcu Bolat-Akça, Nimet Merve Telçeken, Elif Bozkaya-Aras · IEEE International Symposium on Personal, Indoor and Mobile Radio Communications 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/pimrc62392.2025.11275116 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Malicious vehicles may poison federated learning in IoV.
**Approach:** DT-enabled federated misbehaviour detection with XGBoost.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation with real vehicle behaviour.
**Results:** Up to 99% detection accuracy.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - misbehaviour detection, but for FL poisoning, not V2X message MBD with SCMS reporting.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on closed-loop revocation claims.
