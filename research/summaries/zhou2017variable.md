# Variable Periodic Pseudonym-based Location Privacy Preserving for ICV
Xinling Zhou, Lei Peng, Huiyun Li, Mingsong Chen, Hongwei Zhu, Shi Dong · unknown venue 2017 · https://doi.org/10.2991/itim-17.2017.19 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Fixed pseudonym change periods leak location privacy.
**Approach:** Variable periodic pseudonym change, privacy leak modelled as gamma distribution convolved with period.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Effective under different leak parameters.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Pseudonym change strategy; our engine has configurable pseudonym change but not adaptive VPP.
**Novelty implications:** Same topic area; does not provide the closed-loop, SCMS-complete, cross-layer evaluation our engine targets, so our claims stand but this work must be cited as related.
