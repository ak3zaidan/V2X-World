# NR-V2X Quality of Service Prediction Through Machine Learning with Nested Cross-Validation Scheme
Ibrahim Yazici, Emre Gures · 2024 6th International Conference on Communications, Signal Processing, and their Applications (ICCSPA) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccspa61559.2024.10794205 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Robust NR-V2X QoS prediction.
**Approach:** Nested cross-validation with RF, GBR, CBR, LGBM, ANN, SVR predicting PDR and throughput.
**Tools and scale:** NR-V2X datasets (described as real-world).
**Results:** RF best: MAE 0.183 for throughput and 3.873 for PDR.
