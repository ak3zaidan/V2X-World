# Using Real-World Car Traffic Dataset in Vehicular Ad Hoc Network Performance Evaluation
Lucas Rivoirard, Martine Wahl, Patrick Sondi, Marion Berbineau, Dominique Gruyer · International Journal of Advanced Computer Science and Applications 2016 · https://doi.org/10.14569/ijacsa.2016.071251 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Mobility models used for VANET evaluation miss real traffic characteristics.
**Approach:** Builds mobility traces from the MOCoPo real trajectory dataset (619 vehicles, 60 min, 0.1 s sampling) and evaluates 802.11p communication.
**Tools and scale:** Real dataset (video-derived trajectories), 802.11p simulation.
**Results:** Real dataset contains information not obtainable from analytical/simulated models and impacts performance results.
