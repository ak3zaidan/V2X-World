# GenNet: A Generative AI-Driven Mobile Network Simulator for Multi-Objective Network Optimization With RL
Wei-Kang Su, Tong Li, Wenzhen Huang, Zi-Han Huang et al. · IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tmc.2025.3637214 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Simulation-based optimization has emerged as a crucial methodology in the field of mobile network optimization, addressing the need for dynamic and predictive network management. To address the scarcity of open-source mobile network simulators for advanced research, we developed GenNet—a generative AI-driven mobile network simulator.
**Approach:** Through this innovative tool, we aim to empower researchers and practitioners to identify and implement the most effective approaches for network optimization, paving the way for future advancements in mobile network management.
