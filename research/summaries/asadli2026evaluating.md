# Evaluating Agentic Configuration Repair for Computer Networks
Rufat Asadli, Benjamin Hoffman, Ioannis Protogeros, Laurent Vanbever · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06212 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** LLMs fail at large-scale configuration repair.
**Approach:** Agentic LLMs with formal verification, context retrieval and iterative editing on Cornetto.
**Tools and scale:** Open and closed LLMs.
**Results:** Agentic setups improve repair efficacy by 12% and safety by 17% on average.
