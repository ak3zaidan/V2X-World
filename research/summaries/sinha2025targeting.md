# Targeting and Automating Recoveries from Cybersecurity Vulnerabilities Using Large Language Models
Nishchay Sinha, Raghav Trivedi, Sanjit Mittapalli, Anuj Parihar, Ramani Selvanambi · 2025 International Conference on Cognitive Computing in Engineering, Communications, Sciences and Biomedical Health Informatics (IC3ECSBHI) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ic3ecsbhi63591.2025.10990905 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** There is a growing need to develop more robust, automated methods to check for vulnerabilities in code and software.
**Approach:** Large language models, due to their ability to engage with a wide variety of scenarios, provide a personalized framework for users to safeguard their devices. This paper focuses on using a LLaMa3.2 3B model as its base, pretrained on the DiverseVul dataset consisting of vulnerable code snippets in programming languages such as C, C++, and Python.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: dataset.
**Results:** Using QLoRa for PEFT-based fine-tuning, the model achieved an Accuracy of 93.75%, outperforming other models. Despite its promising results, the study identifies limitations, including challenges with obfuscated code, computational resource constraints, and generalization issues.
