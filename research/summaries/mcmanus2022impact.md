# The Impact of Cyberattacks on Efficient Operations of CAVs
Ian McManus, Kevin P. Heaslip · Frontiers in Future Transportation 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3389/ffutr.2022.792649 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The implementation of connected and automated vehicles promises increased safety and efficiency by leveraging advances in technology.
**Approach:** With this new technology, some vulnerabilities could lead to cyberattacks. Without a focus on cybersecurity, vehicles may be attacked, reducing the efficiency and safety advantages promised through technological advancement.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: Veins
**Results:** Without a focus on cybersecurity, vehicles may be attacked, reducing the efficiency and safety advantages promised through technological advancement. Each attack’s worst-case scenario resulted in an over 20% increase in travel time delay per vehicle as the attack severity increased.
