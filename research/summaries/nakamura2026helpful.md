# How Helpful Is LLM Assistance in Network Operations? a Case Study at a Large Demonstration Network
Ryo Nakamura, Koshi Eguchi · NOMS 2026-2026 IEEE Network Operations and Management Symposium 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/noms69089.2026.11668182 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper reports on a real-world case study in which over 100 network engineers assessed how a Large Language Model (LLM) can assist in building and operating a network. The versatility of LLMs has accelerated their adoption across a wide range of domains, and assisting network operations is one such promising application.
**Approach:** This paper reports on a real-world case study in which over 100 network engineers assessed how a Large Language Model (LLM) can assist in building and operating a network.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Analysis of the chat histories shows that 68.1% of the evaluations were positive, indicating a quantitative baseline of the LLM's helpfulness in network operations. Our results also demonstrate that understanding the capabilities of the chatbot is important for eliciting better responses.
