# Digital Twin-Driven Intelligent Task Offloading for Collaborative Mobile Edge Computing
Yongchao Zhang, Jia Hu, Ge-Yong Min · IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jsac.2023.3310058 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Collaborative mobile edge computing (MEC) is a new paradigm that allows cooperative peer offloading among distributed MEC servers to balance their computing workloads.
**Approach:** However, the highly dynamic workloads and wireless network conditions pose great challenges to achieving efficient task offloading in collaborative MEC. To address this challenge, digital twin (DT) has emerged as one promising solution by building a high-fidelity virtual mirror of the physical MEC to simulate its behaviors and help make optimal operational decisions.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: simulate.
**Results:** Experimental results show that our proposed approach can effectively adapt the task offloading decisions according to the dynamic environment, and significantly improve the MEC system’s income through extensive comparison with three state-of-the-art algorithms.
