# Detecting Sybil Attacks in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Salam Hamdan, Amjad Hudaib, Arafat Awajan · International Journal of Parallel Emergent and Distributed Systems 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1080/17445760.2019.1617865 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Sybil attacks in pseudonymous VANETs.
**Approach:** Hybrid of P2DAP and Footprint selected by speed threshold.
**Tools and scale:** ns-2.35, MOVE/SUMO, 10 scenarios, 300 m street, 900 s, AODV.
**Results:** Higher detection rate than either alone.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Speed threshold not fixed; depends on street nature, nodes and lanes.
- Implemented as a TCL script; future work to reimplement in an object-oriented language.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Sybil MBD.
**Novelty implications:** Minor.
