# TACKing Together Efficient Authentication, Revocation, and Privacy in VANETs (CMU-CyLab-08-011)
Ahren M. Studer, Elaine Shi, Fan Bai, Adrian Perrig · KiltHub Repository 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1184/r1/6468077 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Fixed keys enable tracking; revocation must be timely.
**Approach:** TACK: temporary anonymous certified keys with timely revocation at 1609.2-level overhead.
**Tools and scale:** CMU CyLab report.
**Results:** Same or less overhead than IEEE 1609.2.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — revocation timeliness (direction 1).
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
