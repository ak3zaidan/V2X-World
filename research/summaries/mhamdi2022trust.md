# Trust-Based Smart Contract for Automated Agent to Agent Communication
Halima Mhamdi, Ben Othman Soufiene, Ahmed Zouinkhi, Obaid Ali, Hedi Sakli · Computational Intelligence and Neuroscience 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2022/5136865 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Blockchain technology is now regarded as one of the most interesting and possibly innovative technologies.
**Approach:** It enables information to be stored and exchanged securely and transparently without the need for a centralized authority to regulate it. Some of the primary benefits of this technology are the atomicity of the stored data.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: blockchain
**Results:** This solution removes the central authority (CA) to reduce maintenance costs and eliminate legacy threats from centralized systems. The results, obtained from consumption costs, show that the developed platform is characterized by security, availability, and privacy.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Given its features, this technology has the potential to provide answers to challenges encountered in a very sensitive sector, namely, Internet of Vehicles (IoV).
- To overcome these challenges, the integration of blockchain technology and multi-agent systems is a key solution.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
