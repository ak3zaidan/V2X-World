# Quantum Entanglement-Assisted Maritime Satellite Communication Framework Over Free Space
Parth Shah, Leena Shah, Rajesh Gupta, Neha Agrawal et al. · 2026 12th International Conference on Web Research (ICWR) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icwr69602.2026.11513342 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Global satellite infrastructure has transformed maritime data links, allowing ships to share real-time details—such as location, speed, and environmental readings—with orbiting platforms. Yet conventional encryption methods can be compromised, threatening the confidentiality of these exchanges.
**Approach:** We evaluate performance over free-space optical paths up to 25 km under realistic noise and weather scenarios.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** Findings show that MDIQKD achieves higher raw key generation rates but experiences rising quantum bit error rates at extended ranges, whereas BBM92 delivers lower error rates and steadier secure key throughput at moderate distances.
