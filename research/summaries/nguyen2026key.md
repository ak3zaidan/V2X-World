# Key Reconciliation with RC-LDPC/Error Estimation for Satellite-based FSO/QKD Systems
Cuong T. Nguyen, Hoang D. Le, Anshul Jaiswal, Swaminathan R. et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, 2026 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2026.3725740 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Satellite-based free-space optics (FSO) quantum key distribution (QKD) systems have recently attracted significant research interest due to their potential to enable globally secured applications. However, the inherent uncertainty of FSO channels, caused by weather conditions and satellite mobility, induces severe fluctuations in quantum bit-error rate (QBER) between legitimate users.
**Approach:** In this work, we propose a key reconciliation scheme that combines protograph rate-compatible (RC) low-density parity-check (LDPC) codes with a syndrome-based error estimation method. Finally, we validate the proposed framework through a case study that incorporates data from a Starlink low-Earth orbit (LEO) satellite and moving ground vehicles.

**Results:** The proposed error estimation method reduces the number of communication rounds without requiring additional information disclosure. Numerical results demonstrate that the proposed scheme consistently outperforms conventional blind reconciliation under diverse FSO channel conditions and provide practical guidelines for system parameter selection.
