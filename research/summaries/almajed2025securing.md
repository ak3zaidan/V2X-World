# Securing vehicular ad-hoc networks against DDoS and Sybil attacks via machine learning techniques
Hussain Almajed, Munam Ali Shah · IET conference proceedings. 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1049/icp.2025.2977 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ML detection of DDoS and Sybil.
**Approach:** PRISMA systematic review.
**Tools and scale:** Review.
**Results:** RF 99.7% on VDDD for DDoS; ELM best for Sybil on simulated data.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Training data imbalance; few real-world applications; need datasets reflecting real situations.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial.
**Novelty implications:** Supports the open need for realistic datasets.
