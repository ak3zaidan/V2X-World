# PQCMC: Post-Quantum Cryptography McEliece-Chen Implicit Certificate Scheme
Abel C. H. Chen · IACR Cryptology ePrint Archive 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13691 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Lattice implicit certificates are hard; SCMS needs PQ pseudonym issuance.
**Approach:** PQCMC McEliece-based implicit certificate scheme with efficient random invertible matrix generation; key expansion models.
**Tools and scale:** Experiments on certificate length and computation time.
**Results:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Reconstruction value is long for V2X
- suggest preloading implicit certificate and embedding digest in SPDU
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — PQ implicit certificates with key expansion for SCMS pseudonyms.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first PQ implicit-certificate design for SCMS; open: channel/system impact.
