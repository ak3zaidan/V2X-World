# Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment (TARA) Analysis of an Autonomous Emergency Braking (AEB) System
Umberto Della Monica, Kimberly-Annalena Munjal, Mark Paul Tamas, Biagio Boi et al. · Applied Sciences 2025 · https://doi.org/10.3390/app15031400 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** To ensure security and stable quality, deeper cybersecurity evaluations are essential for the development of safety features and functionalities in vehicles.
**Approach:** Among these, the AEB system is the most relevant. This research presents a comprehensive TARA of the AEB system, emphasizing the identification, validation, and mitigation of major cybersecurity threats and risks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: ISO/SAE 21434 / TARA).
**Results:** The assessment aligns with ISO/SAE 21434, which offers a robust framework for risk management in automotive cybersecurity and IT security, ensuring a thorough evaluation of a system’s architecture. The results of this analysis highlight critical flaws within the AEB framework and propose corrective measures to enhance cybersecurity resilience.
