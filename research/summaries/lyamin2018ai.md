# AI-Based Malicious Network Traffic Detection in VANETs
Nikita V. Lyamin, Denis V. Kleyko, Quentin Delooz, Alexey V. Vinel · IEEE Network 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mnet.2018.1800074 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Inherent unreliability of wireless communications may have crucial consequences when safety-critical C-ITS applications enabled by VANETs are concerned. Although natural sources of packet losses in VANETs such as network traffic congestion are handled by decentralized congestion control (DCC), losses caused by malicious interference need to be controlled too.
**Approach:** Our second goal is to present and evaluate our own recent results on how to address the real-time jamming detection problem in V2X safety-critical scenarios with the use of AI.
