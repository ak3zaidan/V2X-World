# How Does Distributed Denial of Service Affect the Connected Cars Environment?
Ayoub Wehby, S. Zeadally, Rida Khatoun, M. Bouchouia, Ahmad Fadlallah · International Conference on Control, Decision and Information Technologies 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/codit62066.2024.10708325 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) attacks are among the most insidious cyberattacks targeting any networking system.
**Approach:** In the Internet domain, these attacks have demonstrated the capability of bringing down most systems for extended periods. In recent years, researchers have been exploring and simulating DDoS attacks against connected car systems.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The results obtained reveal that our attack exerts a significant, silent, and stealthy impact on connected cars.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, these simulated attacks have primarily employed messages from the Internet domain rather than leveraging the messages exchanged between connected vehicles.
- While the system remains operational, the quality of the services it provides (e.g., data collection and vehicle cooperation) is severely compromised.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
