Constantinos Challoumis · Digital Twin Approaches in Autonomous Vehicles 2026 · https://doi.org/10.4018/979-8-3373-7785-8.ch009 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The increasing deployment of autonomous vehicle systems has intensified the need for technologies that improve efficiency, reduce costs, and enhance safety.
**Approach:** Although Digital Twin technology has gained growing attention, its economic impact within autonomous vehicle ecosystems remains insufficiently quantified, particularly regarding capital expenditure, operating costs, and supply chain resilience. This study proposes an economic framework for evaluating the cost–benefit implications of adopting Digital Twin technology in key operational domains, including fleet management and regulatory compliance.
