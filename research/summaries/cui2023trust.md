# Trust Evaluation of Topological Nodes in Intelligent Connected Vehicles Communication Network under Zero-Trust Environment
Jinhu Cui, Yuhong Na, Darong Huang · 2023 CAA Symposium on Fault Detection, Supervision and Safety for Technical Processes (SAFEPROCESS) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/safeprocess58597.2023.10295910 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This article examines the evaluation of trust in communication topology nodes within intelligent connected vehicles operating under a zero-trust environment.
**Approach:** We present a trust model specifically designed for the zero-trust environment. Initially, we determine the comprehensive trust function of communication topology nodes in ICVs under the zero-trust environment by employing a weighted calculation of the node importance function and the node trust function.
