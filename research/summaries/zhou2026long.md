# Long Distance Daylight Drone-based Quantum Key Distribution under Relative Motion
Chun Zhou, Yan-Yang Zhou, Ping Wang, Tan Li et al. ·  2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18934 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Drone-based QKD under motion.
**Approach:** Polarization adaptive correction; 1.2 km drone QKD.
**Tools and scale:** Field experiment.
**Results:** 1.2 km free-space link with 2.76 kbps secure key rate.
