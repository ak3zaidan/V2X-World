# Providing k-anonymity and revocation in ubiquitous VANETs
Cándido Caballero‐Gil, Jezabel Molina‐Gil, Juan Hernández Serrano, Olga León, Miguel Soriano · Ad Hoc Networks 2015 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adhoc.2015.05.016 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Anonymity in VANETs prevents authorities from revoking malicious users; full identifiability violates privacy.
**Approach:** Revocation scheme guaranteeing k-anonymity that lets the CA track and revoke a specific malicious user only after a threshold number of complaints.
**Tools and scale:** NS-2 simulation plus an analytical model validated with scripts.
**Results:** Increases privacy protection while allowing revocation with little extra cost (no figures in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — complaint-threshold revocation vs anonymity, analogous to MA decision thresholds; no SCMS linkage values or safety outcome.
**Novelty implications:** Threshold-of-reports-before-revocation design exists; its safety-time cost under a real SCMS is not measured.
