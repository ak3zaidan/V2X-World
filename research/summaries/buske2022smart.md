# Smart GPS spoofing to countermeasure autonomously approaching agile micro UAVs
I. Buske, A. Walther, Daniel Fitz, Juan S. Acosta, Andiy Konovaltsev, L. Kurz · Security + Defence 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2636236 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Small unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) are increasingly becoming a challenge for both civilian and military security.
**Approach:** Even simple modifications of these inexpensive and widely available systems can create a serious threat which potentially causes major damage. Using signals from global navigation satellite system (GNSS), UAVs are able to operate over long distances and to find their target zone without remote control of a pilot.
