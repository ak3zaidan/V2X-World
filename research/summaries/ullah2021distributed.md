# A Distributed Mix-Context-Based Method for Location Privacy in Road Networks
Ikram Ullah, Munam Ali Shah, Abid Khan, Carsten Maple et al. · Sustainability 2021 · https://doi.org/10.3390/su132212513 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Mix zones fail at low density.
**Approach:** Crowd-based Mix Context (CMC) using speed, direction and density.
**Tools and scale:** NS2 with SUMO on OpenStreetMap; 200 vehicles, 400 s, about 5.2 km x 5.1 km.
**Results:** Better anonymisation than IndMZ and TAPCS.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- More experiments at high speed and low density.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
