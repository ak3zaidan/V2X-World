# ALPHA: LLM-Enabled Active Learning for Human-Free Network Anomaly Detection
Xuanhao Luo, Shivesh Madan Nath Jha, Akruti Sinha, Zhizhen Li et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.05936 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Log anomaly detection needs labels.
**Approach:** ALPHA: embeddings, cluster sampling and LLM few-shot annotation with refinement.
**Tools and scale:** Real-world log datasets.
**Results:** Accuracy comparable to fully supervised.
