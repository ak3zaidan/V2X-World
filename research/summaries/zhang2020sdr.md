# An SDR-in-the-Loop Carla Simulator for C-V2X-Based Autonomous Driving
Wei Zhang, Siyu Fu, Zixu Cao, Zhiyuan Jiang, Shunqing Zhang, Shugong Xu · IEEE INFOCOM 2020 - IEEE Conference on Computer Communications Workshops (INFOCOM WKSHPS) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/infocomwkshps50562.2020.9162743 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Evaluating collaborative autonomous driving with real C-V2X radios.
**Approach:** Demo: SUMO-driven vehicles communicating through SDRs running C-V2X Mode 4 with a parallel communication scheme; platooning.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO (vehicle control), SDRs, C-V2X Mode 4.
**Results:** Platooning demonstrated.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Extend to more advanced collaborative driving applications.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
