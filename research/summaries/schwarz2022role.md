# The Role of Digital Twins in Connected and Automated Vehicles
Chris Schwarz, Ziran Wang · IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Magazine 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mits.2021.3129524 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Digital twins found their genesis in the halls of NASA and the methods of product lifecycle management. Rapidly evolving trends around the proliferation of sensors, the Internet of Things, Industry 4.0, and cyber-physical systems have spurred the growth of digital twins.
**Approach:** This paper reviews digital twins and their use in connected and automated vehicles (CAVs). We note some developing areas as traffic management centers, digital maps, onboard diagnostics, and logistics.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Strictly speaking, digital twins must have communication between a physical system and its model, as opposed to similar methodologies that achieve indirect communication through iteration, or that substitute different parts of a system simulation with bits of hardware or software for testing.
