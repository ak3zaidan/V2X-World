# From Prompts to Protection: Large Language Model-Enabled In-Context Learning for Smart Public Safety UAV
Yousef Emami, Hao Zhou, Miguel Gutierrez Gaitan et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.02649 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A public safety Uncrewed Aerial Vehicle (UAV) enhances situational awareness during emergency response.
**Approach:** We present a case study on data collection scheduling, demonstrating that the LLM-assisted ICL framework can significantly reduce packet loss compared to conventional approaches while also mitigating potential jailbreaking vulnerabilities. The ICL framework enables adaptive, context-aware decision-making for public safety UAVs, offering a lightweight and efficient solution to enhance UAV autonomy and responsiveness in emergencies.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Deploying LLMs at the network edge, rather than in the cloud, further reduces latency and preserves data privacy, making them suitable for real-time, mission-critical public safety UAVs.
