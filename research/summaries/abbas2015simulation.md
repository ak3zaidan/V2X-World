# Simulation and Measurement-Based Vehicle-to-Vehicle Channel Characterization: Accuracy and Constraint Analysis
Taimoor Abbas, Jorg Nuckelt, Thomas Kurner, Thomas Zemen et al. · IEEE Transactions on Antennas and Propagation 2015 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tap.2015.2428280 · relevance: medium · basis: full text

**Problem:** Accuracy of deterministic ray-tracing V2V channel models vs. measurements.
**Approach:** Compares ray-tracing channel gain, delay and Doppler spreads, eigenvalues and antenna correlation against channel sounder measurements.
**Tools and scale:** Urban four-way intersection in Lund, Sweden; LOS and NLOS; ~10 m/s.
**Results:** Comparison of metrics for LOS/NLOS (details in paper).
