# Existence of Trust-field in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks: Empirical Evidence
Md Mahmudul Islam, Shaurya Agarwal · arXiv (Cornell University) 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.13923 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) play a crucial role in enhancing road safety and traffic efficiency by enabling communication between vehicles (V2V) and between vehicles and infrastructure (V2I).
**Approach:** Robust trust management is necessary to ensure the reliability of information in decentralized systems. This paper presents the notion of a ``Trust Field" in VANETs, conceptualized as the behavior of the nodes that represents trust levels evolving in both spatial and temporal dimensions.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The results of our study demonstrate that the trust domain can adjust to fluctuations in network conditions, thereby offering a comprehensive metric for assessing the reliability of nodes. This innovative method improves the dependability of VANET applications by efficiently detecting and mitigating malicious actions.
