# TUMTraf V2X Cooperative Perception Dataset
Walter Zimmer, Gerhard Arya Wardana, Suren Sritharan, Xingcheng Zhou, Rui Song, Alois C. Knoll · 2024 IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cvpr52733.2024.02139 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cooperative perception offers several benefits for enhancing the capabilities of autonomous vehicles and improving road safety.
**Approach:** Using roadside sensors in addition to onboard sensors increases reliability and extends the sensor range. External sensors offer higher situational awareness for automated vehicles and prevent occlusions.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: dataset; scale not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Through multiple experiments, we show that our CoopDet3D camera-LiDAR fusion model achieves an increase of +14.36 3D mAP compared to a vehicle camera-LiDAR fusion model.
