# Quantum safe drone security: Authenticated key exchange and privacy preserving intrusion detection for low altitude intelligent transportation systems
Shafiq Ahmed, M. Anisi, Ayesha Iqbal, A. Suresh et al. · Computers & electrical engineering 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compeleceng.2026.111429 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** PQ authentication and privacy-preserving IDS for low-altitude drone ITS.
**Approach:** PQ-LITS: ML-KEM-768 + ML-DSA-65 three-message handshake, zone handover token, federated DP anomaly detection.
**Tools and scale:** Analysis and experiments.
**Results:** Handshake 0.82 ms, 15,656 bytes.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future work: ProVerif/Tamarin, tighter QROM reductions, adaptive DP budgets.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — drones.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
