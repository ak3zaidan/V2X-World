# Edge Computing-enabled Intrusion Detection for C-V2X Networks using Federated Learning
Aymene Selamnia, Bouziane Brik, Sidi Mohammed Senouci, Abdelwahab Boualouache et al. · GLOBECOM 2022 - 2022 IEEE Global Communications Conference 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/globecom48099.2022.10001675 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Intrusion detection systems (IDS) have already demonstrated their effectiveness in detecting various attacks in cellular vehicle-to-everything (C-V2X) networks, especially when using machine learning (ML) techniques. However, it has been shown that generating ML-based models in a centralized way consumes a massive quantity of network resources, such as CPU/memory and bandwidth, which may represent a critical issue in such networks.
**Approach:** This paper designs a new IDS for C-V2X networks based on FL. Moreover, we build our FL-based IDS on top of well-know CIC-IDS2018 dataset, that includes the main network attacks.
**Tools and scale:** C-V2X/LTE-V2X/5G, federated learning (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** Simulation results show the efficiency of our system compared to the existing solutions in terms of attack detection accuracy while reducing the network resource consumption.
