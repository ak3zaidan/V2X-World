# Cybersecurity in Automotive: An Intrusion Detection System in Connected Vehicles
Francesco Pascale, Ennio Andrea Adinolfi, Simone Coppola, Emanuele Santonicola · Electronics 2021 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics10151765 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Today’s modern vehicles are connected to a network and are considered smart objects of IoT, thanks to the capability to send and receive data from the network. One of the greatest challenges in the automotive sector is to make the vehicle secure and reliable.
**Approach:** So, in this paper, an embedded Intrusion Detection System (IDS) for the automotive sector is introduced.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The results show that our method has good correspondence in the presence of the most common cyber-attacks (DDoS, Fuzzy, Impersonating), obtaining a good score relative to the classic evaluation parameters for a test’s accuracy.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- One of the greatest challenges in the automotive sector is to make the vehicle secure and reliable.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — In-vehicle network / vehicle-internal security; outside our engine scope (V2X radio, PKI, traffic).
**Novelty implications:** No bearing on our novelty claims beyond background.
