# Artificial Intelligence in Vehicular Wireless Networks: A Case Study Using ns-3
Matteo Drago, Tommaso Zugno, Federico Mason, Marco Giordani, Mate Boban, Michele Zorzi · Proceedings of the 2022 Workshop on ns-3 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3532577.3532605 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** How to simulate AI techniques and their impact on the V2X stack is open.
**Approach:** ns-3 framework with a geometry-based V2X channel, full 5G-NR stack (ns3-mmwave), V2X application and RAN-AI entity.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3, 5G NR; RL-based PQoS scenario.
**Results:** RL-driven RAN-AI satisfies QoS/QoE better than non-AI baselines.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Simulating AI and its impact on the communication stack was 'still an open question'.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - V2X simulator with AI-in-the-loop control; no security.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on PKI claims.
