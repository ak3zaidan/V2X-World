# Enhancing Vehicle Platooning Safety via Control Node Placement and Sizing under State and Input Bounds
Yifei She, Shen Wang, Ahmad Taha, Xiaofeng Tao · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08089 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle platooning with Cooperative Adaptive Cruise Control improves traffic efficiency, reduces energy consumption, and enhances safety but remains vulnerable to cyber-attacks that disrupt communication and cause unsafe actions.
**Approach:** To address these risks, this paper investigates control node placement and input bound optimization to balance safety and defense efficiency under various conditions. We propose a two-stage actuator placement and actuator saturation approach, which focuses on identifying key actuators that maximize the system's controllability while operating under state and input constraints.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Simulation results show that our method effectively mitigates the impact of attacks while preserving defense efficiency, offering a robust solution to vehicle platooning safety challenges.
