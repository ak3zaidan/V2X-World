# Review and Perspectives on Driver Digital Twin and Its Enabling Technologies for Intelligent Vehicles
Zhongxu Hu, Shanhe Lou, Yang Xing, Xiao Wang, Dongpu Cao, Chen Lv · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Vehicles 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tiv.2022.3195635 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Digital Twin (DT) is an emerging technology and has been introduced into intelligent driving and transportation systems to digitize and synergize connected automated vehicles.
**Approach:** However, existing studies focus on the design of the automated vehicle, whereas the digitization of the human driver, who plays an important role in driving, is largely ignored. Furthermore, previous driver-related tasks are limited to specific scenarios and have limited applicability.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The related technologies are comprehensively reviewed and discussed with a view to improving them by leveraging the DDT.
