# Mutual influence of certificate distribution and pseudonym change strategies in vehicular ad-hoc networks
Sebastian Bittl, Karsten Roscher · International Journal of Vehicle Information and Communication Systems 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1504/ijvics.2017.10007555 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Interaction of certificate distribution and pseudonym change strategies not studied.
**Approach:** Analysis of cross-influence between certificate dissemination (attach certificate vs digest) and pseudonym change.
**Tools and scale:** IJVICS; analysis/simulation.
**Results:** Identifies mutual influence (details in paper).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
