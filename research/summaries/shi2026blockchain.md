# A Blockchain-driven Revocable Data Sharing Scheme Supporting Policy Hiding for VANETs
Yun-Yu Shi, Li Wang, Shuo Jiang, Zhen Yang et al. · Proceedings of the 2026 7th International Conference on Computer Information and Big Data Applications 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3813822.3813873 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle ad hoc networks (VANETs) need to share data over open environments in a secure and precise manner. In the context of semi-trusted clouds, existing ciphertext-policy attribute-based encryption (CP-ABE) systems have limited verifiability, ineffective revocation, and policy leakage.
**Approach:** In this paper, we present a blockchain-assisted traceable and revocable data sharing scheme with policy hiding. We prove that the proposed scheme is secure.
