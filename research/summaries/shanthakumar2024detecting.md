# Detecting Location Spoofing Attacks using Multiple Angle of Signal Arrivals in MM-Wave Vehicular Networks
Chandana Sai Thondebhavi Shanthakumar, Aishwarya Chawariya, Yuan Cheng, Mohammed E. Eltayeb · IEEE Vehicular Technology Conference 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2024-spring62846.2024.10683294 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Location spoofing in mm-wave vehicular networks.
**Approach:** Physical-layer detection comparing clusters of estimated angles of arrival from hybrid antenna arrays with reported positions.
**Tools and scale:** Numerical mm-wave channel simulation.
**Results:** Highly accurate detection with a few packets and no extra sensing overhead.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — PHY-layer position verification; our radio stacks are sub-6 GHz.
**Novelty implications:** PHY-based position verification is established; not claimable by us.
