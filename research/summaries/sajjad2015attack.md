# Attack Mitigation in Adversarial Platooning Using Detection-Based Sliding Mode Control
Imran Sajjad, Daniel D. Dunn, Rajnikant Sharma, Ryan Gerdes · Proceedings of the First ACM Workshop on Cyber-Physical Systems-Security and/or PrivaCy 2015 · https://doi.org/10.1145/2808705.2808713 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we consider a mitigation strategy to prevent a vehicle controlled by an attacker from causing collisions in a vehicular platoon.
