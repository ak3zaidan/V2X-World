# Attack-Resilient Design for Connected and Automated Vehicles
Tianci Yang, Carlos Murguia, Dragan Nesic et al. · arXiv 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.10925 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** By sharing local sensor information via Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) wireless communication networks, Cooperative Adaptive Cruise Control (CACC) is a technology that enables Connected and Automated Vehicles (CAVs) to drive autonomously on the highway in closely-coupled platoons.
**Approach:** In this manuscript, we propose a co-design methodology (built around a series convex programs) to synthesize distributed attack monitors and $H_{\infty}$ CACC controllers that minimize the joint effect of stealthy FDI attacks and system disturbances on the platoon dynamics while guaranteeing a prescribed platooning performance (in terms of tracking and string stability).
**Tools and scale:** Computer simulations are provided to illustrate the performance of out tools.
