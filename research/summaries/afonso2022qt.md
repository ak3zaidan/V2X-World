# QT-Routenet: Improved GNN generalization to larger 5G networks by fine-tuning predictions from queueing theory
Bruno Klaus de Aquino Afonso, Lilian Berton · ITU Journal on Future and Evolving Technologies 2022 · https://doi.org/10.52953/fbrb3688 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** GNNs fail to generalise to longer paths and larger capacities.
**Approach:** Extract queueing-theory features and fine-tune with a modified RouteNet.
**Tools and scale:** GNNet Challenge 2021 dataset.
**Results:** MAPE reduced from 10.42 (analytical) to 1.45 (1.27 ensemble).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: combine approach with other methods.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - hybrid analytical/learned surrogate.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
