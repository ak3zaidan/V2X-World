# FedSecureFormer: A Fast, Federated and Secure Transformer Framework for Lightweight Intrusion Detection in Connected and Autonomous Vehicles
Devika Sathyan, Vishnu Hari, Pratik Narang, Tejasvi Alladi, F. Yu · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2026.3681531 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The era of Connected and Autonomous Vehicles (CAVs) reflects a significant milestone in transportation, improving safety, efficiency, and intelligent navigation.
**Approach:** However, the growing reliance on real-time communication, constant connectivity, and autonomous decision-making raises serious cybersecurity concerns, particularly in environments with limited resources. This highlights the need for security solutions that are efficient, lightweight, and suitable for deployment in real-world vehicular environments.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The model is designed for efficient and accurate cyber attack detection, achieving 93.69% classification accuracy across 19 attack types and improving performance on 10 attack classes, outperforming several state-of-the-art (SOTA) methods. For testing its generalization to unseen attacks, we used a histogram-guided GAN with LSTM and attention modules to generate unseen data, achieving 88% detection accuracy.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, the growing reliance on real-time communication, constant connectivity, and autonomous decision-making raises serious cybersecurity concerns, particularly in environments with limited resources.
- These results position FedSecureFormer as a fast, scalable, and privacy-preserving solution for securing future intelligent transport systems.
