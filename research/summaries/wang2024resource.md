# Resource Allocation for Dynamic Platoon Digital Twin Networks: A Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning Method
Lei Wang, Hongbin Liang, Guotao Mao, Dongmei Zhao et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2024.3414447 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle driving in a platoon is an efficient and ecological driving solution.
**Approach:** In this paper, we investigate the resource allocation problem in a PDTN. By comprehensively considering the effects of high mobility of platooning vehicles, real-time nature of the DTs, and multi-vehicle cooperation, we propose a PDT utility optimization model for bandwidth and computation resource allocation.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Introducing the concept of digital twin (DT) into the platoon to establish platoon digital twin (PDT) can improve the management efficiency and driving safety of the platoon. To improve the learning efficiency, a decentralized multi-agent deep deterministic policy gradient (DDPG)-based learning framework is proposed.
