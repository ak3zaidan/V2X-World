# A Trust-Aware POMDP Framework for Thwarting Data Falsification Attacks in Cooperative Driving
Ziqi Xu, Jingcheng Li, Loukas Lazos, Ming Li · 2025 IEEE Conference on Communications and Network Security (CNS) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cns66487.2025.11195017 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Data falsification attacks in cooperative driving.
**Approach:** Trust-aware POMDP fusing sensing and V2V data at different rates; switches between sensors-only and sensor+V2V modes by trust.
**Tools and scale:** Simulations with real traffic data.
**Results:** Maintains true state, prevents collisions, keeps comfort and efficiency (no numbers in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — vehicle-level response to falsification with safety outcome.
**Novelty implications:** Local trust-driven fallback is known; system-level revocation timing is not.
