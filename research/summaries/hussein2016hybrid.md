# A new hybrid jammer and its impact on DSRC Safety Application reliability
Sherif Hussein, Mohamed S. Mohamed, Axel Krings · 2016 IEEE 7th Annual Information Technology, Electronics and Mobile Communication Conference (IEMCON) 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iemcon.2016.7746090 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Jamming against DSRC safety applications.
**Approach:** Hybrid jammer (constant, deceptive, random traits) that makes safety apps fail and innocent nodes appear misbehaving without destroying messages; detection algorithm.
**Tools and scale:** Lab experiments with commercial DSRC equipment and field experiments.
**Results:** Jammer causes application failure and false misbehaviour attribution.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — jamming causing false MBD accusations, relevant to MA false-positive risk.
**Novelty implications:** Shows PHY attacks can frame innocents; cross-layer framing relevant to direction 1.
