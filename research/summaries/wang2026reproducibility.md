# Reproducibility Data and Numerical Evidence for Security-Conditioned Viability for Post-Quantum V2X Control
Yong Wang, wyong9677, Xiao Ma, Ying Zhou · Zenodo (CERN European Organization for Nuclear Research) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23175460 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Reproducibility artefact for a study on security-conditioned viability of post-quantum V2X control.
**Approach:** Frozen configurations, numerical results and a Bellman common-projection evidence chain comparing ML-DSA-65 and SLH-DSA-SHA2-192s real profiles; SHA-256 manifests. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Fixed-point solves over 94,325 physical nodes.
**Results:** Terminal common projected required-spacing vectors identical on all 94,325 nodes (max absolute difference zero) across four matched solves; internal Bellman states differ.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Does not claim complete Bellman-trajectory equality or continuous maximal-kernel equality (stated).
