# Edge Computing-Based Privacy-Preserving Authentication Framework and Protocol for 5G-Enabled Vehicular Networks
Jing Zhang, Hong Zhong, Jie Cui et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2020.2994144 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Based on the functional characteristics of inter-vehicle communication networks under the current framework, a privacy-preserving authentication framework that combines fifth-generation communication technology (5G) and edge computing technology is proposed.
**Approach:** The proposed framework is different from that used in the previous 802.11p-based inter-vehicle communication network architecture, and we use device-to-device technology to achieve communication between vehicles. One difference between a 5G-enabled model and a traditional model for vehicle ad hoc networks is that secure communication between vehicles in a 5G-enabled model is challenging.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: 5G.
**Results:** Performance evaluation results demonstrate that our proposed scheme has relatively lower computational and communication overhead than existing schemes.
