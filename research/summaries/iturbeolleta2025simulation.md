# Simulation-Based Framework of V2X-Enabled Digital Twin Application for Urban Environments
Nagore Iturbe-Olleta, Hafsa Iqbal, Joshué Pérez, Abdulla Al-Kaff, Fernando García · International Conference on Vehicular Electronics and Safety 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icves65691.2025.11375922 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** V2X-enabled urban DT with sensing.
**Approach:** LiDAR/camera detections shared via ETSI CAM/CPM to a cloud DT; CARLA for visualisation, Veins (SUMO+OMNeT++) for scalability.
**Tools and scale:** Veins, SUMO, OMNeT++, CARLA; Leganés, Spain; varying densities.
**Results:** CPM effective; packet loss and bandwidth insights under load.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - ETSI message co-simulation in a real city; no security.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
