# An Agentic Framework for Intent Co-Creation in 6G NaaS: Architecture and Open-Source Model Evaluation
Kostis Trantzas, Besiana Agko, Christos Tranoris, Irene Denazi · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23288 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Ambiguous intents in 6G NaaS.
**Approach:** Agentic intent co-creation with domain expert agents on OpenSlice and MCP.
**Tools and scale:** Open-source LLMs.
**Results:** High instruction compliance but gap in translating detailed intents.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Gap in translating high-resolution intents.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
