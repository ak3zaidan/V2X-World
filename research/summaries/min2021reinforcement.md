# Reinforcement learning-based sensitive semantic location privacy protection for VANETs
Minghui Min, Weihang Wang, Liang Xiao, Yilin Xiao et al. · China Communications 2021 · https://doi.org/10.23919/jcc.2021.06.019 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Location-based services (LBS) in vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) must protect users' privacy and address the threat of the exposure of sensitive locations during LBS requests.
**Approach:** Users release not only their geographical but also semantic information of the visited places (e.g., hospital). This sensitive information enables the inference attacker to exploit the users' preferences and life patterns.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: location privacy).
**Results:** Simulations demonstrate the DSLP-based scheme outperforms the benchmark schemes, which increases the privacy, reduces the QoS loss, and increases the utility of the vehicle.
