# Q-CARE-VLC: Quantum-Secured Vehicular Visible-Light Communication with Key-Continuity-Aware Handover for Smart-Ambulance Healthcare
Vivek Kumar, Keshav Kumar, Mohit Kumar Srivastava, Abhishek Rathour et al. · International journal of computer information systems and industrial management applications 2026 · https://doi.org/10.70917/ijcisim-2026-4915 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Ambulance telemetry over vehicular VLC needs long-term confidentiality; QKD under mobility is fragile.
**Approach:** Q-CARE-VLC: classical VLC data plane with decoy-state BB84 key plane, roadside quantum-VLC units, make-before-break handover and key buffer.
**Tools and scale:** Time-slotted simulation with published parameters; not hardware.
**Results:** Relative comparisons only (no absolute numbers extracted).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Simulation only; acquisition/switching latencies are architectural assumptions; asymptotic SKR.
- A hardware-in-the-loop study would need to replace stated assumptions.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — QKD/VLC.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
