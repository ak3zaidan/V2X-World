# Simutack - An Attack Simulation Framework for Connected and Autonomous Vehicles
Andreas Finkenzeller, Anshu Mathur, Jan Lauinger, Mohammad Hamad, Sebastian Steinhorst · 2023 IEEE 97th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2023-Spring) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2023-spring57618.2023.10200555 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Testing IDS/IRS needs realistic attack scenarios that are hard to produce physically.
**Approach:** Simutack open-source attack simulation framework (sensor, V2X and in-vehicle network attacks) feeding attack effects back into simulation, with a custom autopilot.
**Tools and scale:** Driving simulator based (CARLA per related work); three scenarios.
**Results:** Demonstrated three attack scenarios and impact on autonomous driving.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — closed-loop attack injection in simulation; no PKI/SCMS.
**Novelty implications:** Attack-in-simulation frameworks exist; closed-loop with PKI revocation does not.
