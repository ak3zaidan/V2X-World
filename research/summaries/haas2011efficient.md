# Efficient Certificate Revocation List Organization and Distribution
Jason J. Haas, Yih‐Chun Hu, Kenneth P. Laberteaux · IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications 2011 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jsac.2011.110309 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Fast, wide CRL distribution under bandwidth limits and partial deployment.
**Approach:** Compact CRL organisation (linking revoked vehicle's certificates via a key) and V2V epidemic CRL exchange.
**Tools and scale:** IEEE JSAC; simulation with partial penetration.
**Results:** V2V exchange distributes CRLs faster than RSUs alone.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — CRL distribution speed and partial deployment directly relate to our CRL broadcast and time-to-neutralise.
**Novelty implications:** V2V CRL dissemination speed is studied; coupling dissemination with detection and safety outcome remains open.
