# An Evaluation of Sybil Attack’s Detection Approaches in Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks (VANETs)
Muhammad Iqbal Younis, Rana M. Amir Latif, Izharul Haq, N. Z. Jhanjhi et al. · International Journal of Intelligent Systems and Applications in Engineering 2022 · https://ijisae.org/index.php/IJISAE/article/view/2372 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Comparing Sybil attack detection approaches for VANETs.
**Approach:** Review classifying Sybil detection into resource-testing, localisation and authentication-based approaches, plus a dataset-based simulation.
**Tools and scale:** Survey with a small dataset experiment; no named V2X simulator stack.
**Results:** Qualitative: resource testing insufficient; localisation suits position verification; authentication essential for identity legitimacy.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Detection should avoid extra overhead; accuracy of localisation techniques needs continued improvement.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Sybil detection taxonomy; no simulation of PKI-based Sybil.
**Novelty implications:** No claim impact; PKI-backed Sybil resistance under pseudonyms remains open in this paper.
