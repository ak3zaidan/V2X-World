# RouteNet-TGNN-A Temporal Graph Neural Network for Delay and Loss Forecasting in Dynamic Communication Networks
Sultan Ahmad, Gadu Srinivasa Rao · Journal of Communications 2026 · https://doi.org/10.12720/jcm.21.2.211-225 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Temporal dynamics in network modelling.
**Approach:** RouteNet-TGNN with GRUs in path message passing for multi-step forecasting.
**Tools and scale:** Synthetic scenarios.
**Results:** Delay error -27.5%, jitter -21.3%, loss -18.9% vs static GNNs.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Designed for short horizons t+1 to t+3.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - surrogate.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
