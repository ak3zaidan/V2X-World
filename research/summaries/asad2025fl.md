# FL-SATS: Federated Learning for Sybil Attack Detection in Transportation System
Muhammad Asad, Safa Otoum · ICC 2025 - IEEE International Conference on Communications 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc52391.2025.11161123 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil detection in ITS.
**Approach:** Three-tier federated learning (RSU, RSC, SDN) with fuzzy vehicle selection.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** 98.7% accuracy baseline, 98.5% dense; 20 ms latency; 33% less overhead.
