# Privacy Impact Assessment of Cyber Attacks on Connected and Autonomous Vehicles
Sakshyam Panda, Emmanouil Panaousis, George Loukas, Konstantinos Kentrotis · Proceedings of the 18th International Conference on Availability, Reliability and Security 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3600160.3605073 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Connected and autonomous vehicles (CAVs) are vulnerable to security gaps that can result in serious consequences, including cyber-physical and privacy risks.
**Approach:** For example, an attacker can reconstruct a vehicle’s location trajectory by knowing the speed and steering wheel position of the vehicle. Such inferences not only lead to safety issues but also significantly threaten privacy.
