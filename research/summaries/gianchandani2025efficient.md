# An Efficient Machine Learning Technique of Intrusion Detection System for Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Deepa Gianchandani, Anamika Singh · 2025 4th OPJU International Technology Conference (OTCON) on Smart Computing for Innovation and Advancement in Industry 5.0 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/otcon65728.2025.11070905 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle ad hoc networks function as the core element of smart transportation systems (ITS) when referred to as VANETs.
**Approach:** The proposed research develops an effective SVM-based intrusion detection system (IDS) to detect and stop harmful activities within VANETs. The proposed testing method employs a detailed dataset to assess its performance capabilities that outshine existing intrusion detection system methods through accuracy improvements and higher detection rates and lower false alarm rates.
