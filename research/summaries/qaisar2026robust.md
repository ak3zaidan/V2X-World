# A Robust Trust Management System for V2X Networks Integrating ISAC With Blockchain Smart Contracts
Muhammad Umar Farooq Qaisar, Wei-Jie Yuan, Lin Zhang, S. A. Chaudhry, Guang-Jie Han, Yun-Yang Zhang · IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnsm.2026.3658589 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle-to-everything (V2X) networks face critical security challenges due to their dynamic nature, stringent latency requirements, and susceptibility to malicious attacks.
**Approach:** Traditional trust management approaches often rely on centralized authorities or historical data, creating vulnerabilities and scalability limitations. This paper presents a new trust management system that leverages integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) technology and blockchain-based smart contracts to provide secure and decentralized trust evaluation in V2X networks.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G, blockchain
**Results:** Numerical results demonstrate that the proposed protocol achieves faster trust convergence, higher communication reliability, significant reduction in false positive rates, improved detection accuracy, acceptable end-to-end latency, and lower computational overhead compared to state-of-the-art approaches.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Vehicle-to-everything (V2X) networks face critical security challenges due to their dynamic nature, stringent latency requirements, and susceptibility to malicious attacks.
- Traditional trust management approaches often rely on centralized authorities or historical data, creating vulnerabilities and scalability limitations.
