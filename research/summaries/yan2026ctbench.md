# CTBench: Evaluating Troubleshooting Capabilities of AI Agents in Realistic Telecom Network Operations
Xingyu Yan, Tingting Dai, Antonio De Domenico, Mohamed Sana, Nicola Piovesan, Changchang Li et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12002 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Evaluations do not capture real telecom troubleshooting.
**Approach:** CTBench: 234 expert-curated RCA and path-restoration tasks with golden evidence steps.
**Tools and scale:** Telecom network environments; multiple agent harnesses.
**Results:** Agents good at endpoints, weak at RCA; often lack evidence-grounded diagnoses.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Partial observability and heterogeneity expose agent limitations.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - evidence-grounded RCA benchmark (runner-up LLM RCA).
**Novelty implications:** Evidence-grounded RCA benchmarks exist for telecom; V2X with simulator ground truth remains open.
