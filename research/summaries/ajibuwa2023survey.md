# A Survey on AI/ML-Driven Intrusion and Misbehavior Detection in Networked Autonomous Systems: Techniques, Challenges and Opportunities
Opeyemi Ajibuwa, Bechir Hamdaoui, Attila A. Yavuz · arXiv (Cornell University) 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.05040 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** AI/ML IDS/MDS see little real deployment partly due to lack of objective evaluation and feasibility metrics.
**Approach:** Survey establishing baseline feasibility metrics (FPR, FNR, accuracy, threat model, unseen attacks, latency, placement, validation) and guidelines.
**Tools and scale:** Survey with tabulated metrics.
**Results:** Many papers omit FNR, latency or placement; key findings and guidelines.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Lack of objective evaluation and feasibility metrics
- vulnerability to adversarial ML
- limited practical deployment.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — argues for latency- and deployment-aware MBD evaluation, which our closed loop provides.
**Novelty implications:** Supports that end-to-end feasibility metrics are an open gap.
