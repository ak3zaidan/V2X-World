# Toward 6G Sidelink Reliability: MAC PRR Modeling for NR Mode 2 SPS and ns-3 Validation
Liu Cao, Zhaoyu Liu, Lyutianyang Zhang · arXiv 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16680 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Analytical models omit NR-specific SPS features affecting PRR.
**Approach:** Closed-form MAC collision and PRR model for NR SL Mode 2 SPS including reselection collisions, persistent collisions, duplicate transmissions and minimum availability requirement.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3 5G-LENA validation.
**Results:** Close agreement under under-saturation, deviation near saturation; multiple transmissions help only under-saturated; X > 0.2 gives no benefit.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Model deviates as system approaches saturation.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - NR SL Mode 2 MAC modelling, which our NR-V2X stack implements.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on security claims.
