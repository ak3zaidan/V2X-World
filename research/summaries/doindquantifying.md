# Quantifying Ghost Object Detectability in V2X Networks via Data-Driven Traffic Generation
Doi, G. Perrone, Francesca Bassi, M. Rapelli et al. · PORTO@Iris None · https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/394f43b267726b7473f56ca84a7cc38ab7610946 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** How often real traffic lets honest sensor-equipped vehicles expose ghost objects.
**Approach:** Learned generative traffic model synthesises 1,000 realistic scenes per condition over highway and intersection layouts with explicit sensor modelling to quantify ghost detectability.
**Tools and scale:** Data-driven traffic generation (learned model).
**Results:** Quantified detectability conditions (truncated abstract).
