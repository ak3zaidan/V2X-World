# ANAA-Fog: A Novel Anonymous Authentication Scheme for 5G-Enabled Vehicular Fog Computing
B. Mohammed, Mahmood A. Al-Shareeda, S. Manickam, Z. Al-Mekhlafi et al. · Mathematics 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/math11061446 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular fog computing enabled by the Fifth Generation (5G) has been on the rise recently, providing real-time services among automobiles in the field of smart transportation by improving road traffic safety and enhancing driver comfort. Due to the public nature of wireless communication channels, in which communications are conveyed in plain text, ...
**Approach:** To face this problem, we propose a novel anonymous authentication scheme named ANAA-Fog for 5G-enabled vehicular fog computing.
**Tools and scale:** The signing step of the ANAA-Fog scheme is analyzed and proven secure with the use of the ProfVerif simulator.
**Results:** Finally, the result of the proposed ANAA-Fog scheme in terms of communication cost and single signature verification is 108 bytes and 2.0185 ms, respectively. Hence, the assessment metrics section demonstrates that our work incurs a little more cost in terms of communication and computing performance when compared to similar studies.
