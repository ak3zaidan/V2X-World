# Deep Learning-Based Intrusion System for Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Li Fei, Zhang Jiayan, Edward Szczerbicki, Song Jiaqi, Li Ruxiang, Diao Renhong · Computers, Materials &amp; Continua 2020 · https://doi.org/10.32604/cmc.2020.011264 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The increasing use of the Internet with vehicles has made travel more convenient.
**Approach:** However, hackers can attack intelligent vehicles through various technical loopholes, resulting in a range of security issues. Due to these security issues, the safety protection technology of the in-vehicle system has become a focus of research.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The experimental results show that the combination of the two technologies can effectively and accurately identify abnormal boundary behavior. We verified that the model proposed in this study can reach a nearly 96% accurate detection rate.
