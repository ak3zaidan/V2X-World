# A Bio-Inspired Cluster Optimization Schema for Efficient Routing in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs)
Ghassan Husnain, Shahzad Anwar, Gulbadan Sikander, Armughan Ali, Sang-Kug Lim · Energies 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/en16031456 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Unstable routes in VANET clustering.
**Approach:** Probability-based whale optimisation for cluster-head selection.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation; 1x1 and 4x4 km grids.
**Results:** 45 and 53 clusters for the grids; better PDR, throughput, latency vs ALO and GWO.
