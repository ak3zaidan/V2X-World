# Machine Learning for CSI Recreation in the Digital Twin Based on Prior Knowledge
Brenda Vilas Boas, W. Zirwas, M. Haardt · IEEE Open Journal of the Communications Society 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ojcoms.2022.3208323 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Knowledge of channel state information (CSI) is fundamental to many functionalities for mobile communication systems. With the advance of machine learning (ML) and digital maps, i.e., digital twins, we have a big opportunity to learn the propagation environment and design novel methods to derive and report CSI.
**Approach:** In this work, we propose to combine untrained neural networks (UNNs) and conditional generative adversarial networks (cGANs) for MIMO channel recreation based on prior knowledge. This combined approach can be used for low overhead CSI reporting as, after training, we only need to report the desired location.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Our results show that our CSI recreation method is successful in modelling the wireless channel under different configurations of prior-CSI spatial sampling. The signal to noise ratio (SNR) achieved by our CSI recreation is better than the SNR reported by the measured campaign providers.
