# Network Digital Twins for 6G: Defining Data Taxonomy and Data Models
Ayat Zaki-Hindi, Jean-Sébastien Sottet, Ion Turcanu, Sébastien Faye · IEEE Conference on Standards for Communications and Networking 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cscn63874.2024.10849705 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As the telecommunications industry moves towards 6G, Network Digital Twins (NDTs) have emerged as a useful means for real-time monitoring, automation, and performance optimization in next-generation networks.
**Approach:** This paper contributes to the ongoing research on NDTs within the framework of the 6G-TWIN project by presenting a comprehensive vision for NDT architecture and establishing baseline definitions for NDT data models, i.e., basic and functional models. We propose a structured taxonomy for the data required by these models to ensure a shared semantics consistent with existing standards.
