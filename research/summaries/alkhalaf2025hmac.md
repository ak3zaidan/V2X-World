# A HMAC-Based Token Authentication Protocol with Bloom Filter Revocation for Fog-Assisted Vehicular Networks
Hussein Asaad Shakir Al-Khalaf, Ali Aliedani, M. Jawad, Huda Mohammed Alsayednoor et al. · Informatica 2025 · https://doi.org/10.31449/inf.v49i35.12009 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Lightweight fog vehicular authentication with revocation.
**Approach:** Five-round HMAC token protocol with Bloom-filter revocation.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** 2.10 ms processing, 512 bytes communication, 1.8 KB storage; over 35% lower computation and 42.5% lower communication.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Include post-quantum ciphers in future
**Overlap with our engine:** none — Bespoke.
**Novelty implications:** None.
