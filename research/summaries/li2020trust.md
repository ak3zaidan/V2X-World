# Trust Based Secure Content Delivery in Vehicular Networks: A Bargaining Game Theoretical Approach
Jiliang Li, Rui Xing, Zhou Su, Ning Zhang, Yilong Hui, Tom H. Luan, Hangguan Shan · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2020.2964685 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular networks expect to promote the road safety for moving vehicles and improve the driving experience of drivers. In the vehicular networks, secure content delivery becomes significant yet very challenging to protect the vehicles from various threats (i.e., outside attacks and inside attacks).
**Approach:** To this end, this paper proposes a security-aware content delivery scheme in vehicular networks when the vehicles request contents from roadside units (RSUs). Next, to achieve the secure content delivery and optimize the players’ utilities, we apply the backward induction method to analyze the proposed model in a two-round game and an $M$-round game, respectively.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Finally, simulation results are provided to evaluate the efficiency of the proposed scheme by comparing with several conventional schemes.
