# Automatic Root Cause Analysis via Large Language Models for Cloud Incidents
Yinfang Chen, Huaibing Xie, Ming-Hua Ma, Yu Kang et al. · European Conference on Computer Systems 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3627703.3629553 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Ensuring the reliability and availability of cloud services necessitates efficient root cause analysis (RCA) for cloud incidents. Traditional RCA methods, which rely on manual investigations of data sources such as logs and traces, are often laborious, error-prone, and challenging for on-call engineers.
**Approach:** In this paper, we introduce RCACopilot, an innovative on-call system empowered by the large language model for automating RCA of cloud incidents. We evaluate RCACopilot using a real-world dataset consisting of a year's worth of incidents from Microsoft.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: real-world. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Our evaluation demonstrates that RCACopilot achieves RCA accuracy up to 0.766.
