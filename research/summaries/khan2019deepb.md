# Deep Learning-aided Application Scheduler for Vehicular Safety Communication
Mohammad Irfan Khan, François-Xavier Aubet, Marc-Oliver Pahl, Jérôme Härri · arXiv 2019 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.08872 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** 802.11p stochastic MAC cannot prevent broadcast collisions at high load.
**Approach:** DNN learns transmit patterns to predict channel activity and schedule transmissions.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation with multiple heterogeneous V2X safety services.
**Results:** Reduced collisions and improved communication performance.
