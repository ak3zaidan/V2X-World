# Benchmarking the Performance of 5G CV2X for Connected Vehicles based Adaptive Traffic Signal
Mahbubul Alam Palash, Duminda Wijesekera · 2024 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iv55156.2024.10588485 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Can 5G C-V2X support connected-vehicle adaptive signals?
**Approach:** Benchmarks 5G C-V2X at 5.9 GHz and 24 GHz mmWave for ATSC and signal status.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation, 5G CV2X.
**Results:** Average delay about 4.25 ms V2I, 2.03 ms I2V, 42.71 ms V2V at 5.9 GHz; sub-ms at 24 GHz but needs more infrastructure.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- mmWave requires additional infrastructure.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
