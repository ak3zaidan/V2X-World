# MAGNNETO: A Graph Neural Network-Based Multi-Agent System for Traffic Engineering
Guillermo Bernárdez, José Suárez-Varela, Albert López, Xiang Shi, Shihan Xiao, Xiangle Cheng, Pere Barlet-Ros, Albert Cabellos-Aparicio · IEEE Transactions on Cognitive Communications and Networking 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tccn.2023.3235719 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Current trends in networking propose the use of Machine Learning (ML) for a wide variety of network optimization tasks.
**Approach:** As such, many efforts have been made to produce ML-based solutions for Traffic Engineering (TE), which is a fundamental problem in Internet Service Provider (ISP) networks. Nowadays, state-of-the-art TE optimizers rely on traditional optimization techniques, such as Local search, Constraint Programming, or Linear programming.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Our experimental results show that, thanks to its distributed nature, MAGNNETO achieves comparable performance to state-of-the-art TE optimizers with significantly lower execution times.
