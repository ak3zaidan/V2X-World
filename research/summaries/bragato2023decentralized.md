# Towards Decentralized Predictive Quality of Service in Next-Generation Vehicular Networks
Filippo Bragato, Tommaso Lotta, Gianmaria Ventura, Matteo Drago, Federico Mason, Marco Giordani et al. · arXiv 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.11268 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** PQoS for teleoperated driving with strict latency/reliability.
**Approach:** RL agent choosing compression level; centralised, distributed and federated learning compared.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3 simulations.
**Results:** Centralised best; federated offers good convergence/reliability trade-off.
