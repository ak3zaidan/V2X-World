# Evaluation of a Reputation Management Technique for Autonomous Vehicles
Darius Kianersi, S. Uppalapati, Anirudh Bansal, J. Straub · Future Internet 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/fi14020031 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Future autonomous vehicles will rely heavily on sharing and communicating information with other vehicles to maximize their efficiency. These interactions, which will likely include details about the positions of surrounding vehicles and obstacles on the road, are essential to their decision-making and the prevention of accidents.
**Approach:** This paper presents and evaluates a reputation management system, capable of identifying malicious actors, to mitigate their effects on the vehicle network. The viability of multiple report weighting schemes to calculate reputation is evaluated through a simulation, and a blockchain-based backend for the reputation management system to securely maintain and communicate reputation data is proposed.
