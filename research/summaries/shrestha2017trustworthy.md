# Trustworthy Event-Information Dissemination in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Rakesh Shrestha, Seung Yeob Nam · Mobile Information Systems 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2017/9050787 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In vehicular networks, trustworthiness of exchanged messages is very important since a fake message might incur catastrophic accidents on the road. In this paper, we propose a new scheme to disseminate trustworthy event information while mitigating message modification attack and fake message generation attack.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a new scheme to disseminate trustworthy event information while mitigating message modification attack and fake message generation attack.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** We compare our scheme with other schemes using simulation for several scenarios. The simulation results show that our proposed scheme has a low false decision probability and can efficiently disseminate trustworthy event information to neighboring vehicles in VANET.
