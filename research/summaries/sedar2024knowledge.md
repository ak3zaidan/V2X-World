# Knowledge Transfer for Collaborative Misbehavior Detection in Untrusted Vehicular Environments
Roshan Sedar, Charalampos Kalalas, Paolo Dini, Francisco Vazquez-Gallego, Jesus Alonso-Zarate, Luis Alonso · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2024.3461837 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Collaborative RSU MBD vulnerable to label-flipping and policy induction.
**Approach:** Deep RL with selective transfer learning from trustworthy source RSUs.
**Tools and scale:** Open-source dataset scenarios.
**Results:** Reduced training time and superior detection vs tabula rasa; detects unseen attacks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Existing MBD suffers high false alarms and poor robustness.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
