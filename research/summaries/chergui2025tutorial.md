# A Tutorial on Cognitive Biases in Agentic AI-Driven 6G Autonomous Networks
Hatim Chergui, Farhad Rezazadeh, Merouane Debbah et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.19973 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The path to higher network autonomy in 6G lies beyond the mere optimization of key performance indicators (KPIs), requiring systems that perceive and reason over the network environment as it is.
**Approach:** This can be achieved through agentic AI, where large language model (LLM)-powered agents utilize multimodal telemetry, memory, and cross-domain negotiation to achieve multi-objective goals. To overcome the prohibitive execution delays of cloud-based LLMs, this use-case deploys a locally hosted 1B-parameter model on an RTX A4000 GPU, successfully achieving sub-second inference latencies compatible with near-real-time operations.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Grounding decisions in this richer, debiased historical context yields highly robust agreements, achieving a $\times 5$ latency reduction and roughly 40\% higher energy savings compared to memoryless baselines.
