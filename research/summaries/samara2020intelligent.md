# Intelligent Reputation System for Safety Messages in VANET
Ghassan Samara · IAES International Journal of Artificial Intelligence (IJ-AI) Vol. 9, No. 3, September 2020, pp. 439~447, ISSN: 2252-8938, DOI: 10.11591/ijai.v9.i3.pp439-447 2020 · https://doi.org/10.11591/ijai.v9.i3.pp439-447 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Identify vehicles sending false safety messages.
**Approach:** Intelligent Reputation System using opinions, trust values, traffic analysis, position data and multi-parameter Greedy Best First decision making.
**Tools and scale:** MATLAB R2018a simulation, compared with CDPD and RSMA.
**Results:** Better in messages received, execution time and number of victims affected (no numbers extracted).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — reputation-based MBD.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
