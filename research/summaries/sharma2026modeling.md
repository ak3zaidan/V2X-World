# Modeling and simulation of VANET routing protocols under realistic mobility patterns
S. Sharma, S. Kour, Himali Sarangal · Scientific Reports 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-36039-7 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Routing performance depends on mobility model choice.
**Approach:** Evaluates AODV, DSR, DSDV, OLSR, GRP across fourteen mobility models (IDM, CFM, LWR, ...) with eight metrics.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO, NS-3, Veins.
**Results:** AODV+CFM best: 93% PDR, 79.4 ms delay, 332 kbps, 3.4 ms jitter, 3.48 J, 7% PLR, 0.3 NRL, 5 link breakages.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Multi-speed experiments not included
- future work to evaluate across speed ranges.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
