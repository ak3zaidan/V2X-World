# Towards Secure Communication in CR-VANETs Through a Trust-Based Routing Protocol
Sharmin Akter, Mohammad Shahriar Rahman, Md Zakirul Alam Bhuiyan, Nafees Mansoor · IEEE INFOCOM 2021 - IEEE Conference on Computer Communications Workshops (INFOCOM WKSHPS) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/infocomwkshps51825.2021.9484515 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cognitive Radio Networks (CRNs) promise efficient spectrum utilization by operating over the unused frequencies where Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs) facilitate information exchanging among vehicles to avoid accidents, collisions, congestion, etc.
**Approach:** Thus, CR enabled vehicular networks (CR-VANETs), a thriving area in wireless communication research, can be the enabler of Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) and autonomous driver-less vehicles. Similar to others, efficient and reliable communication in CR-VANETs is vital.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Simulation is conducted for performance evaluation that shows the proposed routing selects the efficient routing path by discarding malicious nodes from the network and outperforms the existing routing protocols.
