# The Role of Large Language Models in Designing Reliable Networks for Internet of Things: A Short Review of Most Recent Developments
Melchizedek Alipio, Miroslav Bures · IEEE Access 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2025.3614246 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The rapid growth of Internet of Things (IoT) networks has increased the need for intelligent, flexible, and scalable networking solutions.
**Approach:** This paper reviews the use of Large Language Models (LLMs) to improving network protocols, automate decision-making, and strengthen security in IoT networks. A detailed analysis was conducted to classify the existing research based on applications, network types, methodologies, and performance metrics.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** LLMs have been used in network configuration, security monitoring, cyber threat detection, federated learning, and for improving network performance.
