# Enhanced Management of Certificate Caching and Revocation Lists in VANET
Sadiq H. Abdulhussain · International Journal of Computer Applications 2013 · https://doi.org/10.5120/14503-2393 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Certificate exchange and revocation with many moving vehicles.
**Approach:** CRL enhancement for revoking all certificates of one party; RSUs as certificate caches.
**Tools and scale:** Analysis.
**Results:** Cooperation between caching servers improves distribution.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Requires a vehicle movement simulator for results; cooperation protocol needs development and testing.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
