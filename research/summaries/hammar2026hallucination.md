# Hallucination-Resistant Security Planning with a Large Language Model
Kim Hammar, Tansu Alpcan, Emil C. Lupu · NOMS 2026-2026 IEEE Network Operations and Management Symposium 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/noms69089.2026.11668410 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Large language models (LLMs) are promising tools for supporting security management tasks, such as incident response planning.
**Approach:** In this paper, we address these challenges by introducing a principled framework for using an LLM as decision support in security management. Our framework integrates the LLM in an iterative loop where it generates candidate actions that are checked for consistency with system constraints and lookahead predictions.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Experiments on four public datasets show that our framework reduces recovery times by up to 30% compared to frontier LLMs.
