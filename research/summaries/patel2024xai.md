# XAI-based RF Jamming Detection Framework for Vehicular Networks in Battlefield Environment
Kavya Patel, Dhruv A. Thakkar, Rajesh Gupta, Nilesh Kumar Jadav, Sudeep Tanwar, Deepak Garg · International Conference for Emerging Technology (INCET) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/incet61516.2024.10593294 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Modern military operations require communication among vehicles and drones are of utmost significance for mission success and troop safety.
**Approach:** However, this communication is susceptible to Radio Frequency (RF) jamming attacks, which lead to channel disruption and compromise situational awareness. To address this challenge, this paper proposes an AI-based solution for predictive analysis of RF jamming attacks in Vehicular Adhoc Networks (VANETs).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Evaluation results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, achieving an accuracy of 93.00% on the test dataset.
