# BOTVEILLANCE: A Vehicular Botnet Surveillance Attack against Pseudonymous Systems in VANETs
Mevlut Turker Garip, Peter L. Reiher, Mário Gerla · IFIP Wireless and Mobile Networking Conference (WMNC) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.23919/wmnc.2018.8480909 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) use inter-vehicle communications to improve traffic safety by broadcasting information such as vehicle speed, location and heading to other vehicles. This approach depends on each vehicle advertising its location information.
**Approach:** We present BOTVEILLANCE, an adaptive cooperative surveillance attack by vehicular botnets-effective even against the best existing pseudonym changing scheme. Since we use our vehicular bots, our surveillance attack is not confined to a specific area.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** We show via simulation that our attack can keep a vehicle under surveillance up to 85 percent of its route, and identify its destination address 90 percent of the time.
