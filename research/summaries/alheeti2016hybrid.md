# Hybrid intrusion detection in connected self-driving vehicles
Khattab M. Ali Alheeti, Klaus Dieter McDonald-Maier · International Conference on Automation and Computing (ICAC) 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iconac.2016.7604962 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Emerging self-driving vehicles are vulnerable to different attacks due to the principle and the type of communication systems that are used in these vehicles. These vehicles are increasingly relying on external communication via vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs).
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose an intelligent security system designed to secure external communications for self-driving and semi self-driving cars.
**Tools and scale:** The proposed scheme is based on Proportional Overlapping Score (POS) to decrease the number of features found in the Kyoto benchmark dataset.
**Results:** The experimental results show that the proposed BP-IDS is capable of identifying malicious vehicles in self-driving and semi self-driving vehicles.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- VANETs add new threats to self-driving vehicles that contribute to substantial challenges in autonomous systems.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — In-vehicle network / vehicle-internal security; outside our engine scope (V2X radio, PKI, traffic).
**Novelty implications:** No bearing on our novelty claims beyond background.
