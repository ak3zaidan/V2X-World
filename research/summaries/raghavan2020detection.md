# Detection of Denial of Service (DoS) Attacks in VANET using Filters
Uppili Srinivasa Raghavan · NORMA 2020 · https://norma.ncirl.ie/4156/1/uppilisrinivasaraghavan.pdf · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks (VANET) are considered as a subset of Mobile Ad-Hoc Networks (MANET).
**Approach:** Once the attack is detected it generates a broadcast message to all the other vehicles that are present in the network.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** VANET can be used to coordinate the traffic, improve safety measures, support the drivers for hassle-free driving.
