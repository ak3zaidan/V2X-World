# Digital Twin-Assisted Space-Air-Ground Integrated Networks for Vehicular Edge Computing
Anal Paul, Keshav Singh, Minh-Hien T. Nguyen, Cunhua Pan et al. · IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Signal Processing 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jstsp.2023.3340107 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we present a framework that integrates digital twin (DT) technology into space-air-ground integrated networks (SAGINs) to enhance vehicular edge computing (VEC). Our objective is to efficiently offload tasks in ultra-reliable low-latency communications (URLLC)-enabled vehicular networks, focusing on minimizing overall latency for requested tasks by reducing transmission time for task offloading and edge processing requirements.
**Approach:** We formulate a non-convex optimization problem considering various network constraints to achieve the system objective. To solve this optimization problem, we develop a novel multi-agent deep reinforcement learning (DRL) algorithm, enabling intelligent decision-making by individual agents.
