# A Data Synchronization Incentive Scheme in Vehicular Digital Twin Network with Stackelberg Game
Jingru Tan, Xiaoqing Yang, Jinkai Zheng, Tom H. Luan et al. · 2024 IEEE 99th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2024-Spring) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2024-spring62846.2024.10683329 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The evolving digital twin technology translates physical entities into the digital realm, allowing the exploration of abundant digital resources to optimize the task execution of these physical entities. Real-time data synchronization between physical entities and their digital twins is essential for the effective functioning of digital twin systems.
**Approach:** In this paper, we investigate the challenge of data synchronization in vehicular digital twin networks operating in open street scenarios, where multiple vehicles rely on cellular networks for continuous data synchronization with their digital twins. Through extensive simulations, we demonstrate that the proposed scheme successfully motivates vehicles to follow the center's guidance, leading to efficient data synchronization and mutual benefit maximization.
