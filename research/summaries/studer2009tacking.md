# TACKing Together Efficient Authentication, Revocation, and Privacy in VANETs
Ahren M. Studer, Elaine Shi, Fan Bai, Adrian Perrig · Annual IEEE Communications Society Conference on Sensor, Mesh and Ad Hoc Communications and Networks (SECON) 2009 · https://doi.org/10.1109/sahcn.2009.5168976 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Fixed keys allow tracking; VANETs need authentication, revocation and privacy together.
**Approach:** TACKs: temporary anonymous certified keys issued by regional authorities with timely revocation of misbehaving participants. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Scheme design and overhead analysis.
**Results:** Same or less V2V overhead than IEEE 1609.2.
