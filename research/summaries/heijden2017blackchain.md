# Blackchain
Rens W. van der Heijden, Felix Engelmann, David Mödinger, Franziska Schönig et al. · arXiv 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3152824.3152828 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** SCMS revocation relies on a single trusted MA.
**Approach:** Blackchain: distributed ledger acting as distributed event data recorder; hierarchical consensus (vehicle clusters, RSUs, set of MAs) for accountable revocation.
**Tools and scale:** SERIAL 2017; conceptual design.
**Results:** Conceptual foundations; no quantitative evaluation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Many open questions before practical feasibility.
- Whether vehicle clusters are stable enough for consensus.
- Guarantees of hierarchical versus full consensus.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — directly addresses SCMS MA revocation decision design (direction 1).
**Novelty implications:** Alternative MA decision architectures are proposed but unevaluated; quantitative evaluation in a faithful SCMS is open.
