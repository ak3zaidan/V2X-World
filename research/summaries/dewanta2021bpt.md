# BPT Scheme: Establishing Trusted Vehicular Fog Computing Service for Rural Area Based on Blockchain Approach
Favian Dewanta, M. Mambo · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2021.3051258 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Passing through a rural area with a limited network infrastructure may disrupt fog computing support for vehicles. As a result, some applications on vehicles may turn off and bother the performance of the vehicular systems.
**Approach:** To deal with this situation, this paper proposes a method called Bidding-Price-based Transaction (BPT) for vehicular fog computing service in rural areas.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The simulation results and feasibility analysis then validate the performance of the BPT scheme in rural areas.
