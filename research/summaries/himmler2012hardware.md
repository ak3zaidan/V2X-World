# Hardware-in-the-Loop Testing in the Context of ISO 26262
Andreas Himmler, Klaus Lamberg, Michael Beine · SAE Technical Paper Series 2012 · https://doi.org/10.4271/2012-01-0035 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** <div class="section abstract"><div class="htmlview paragraph">Hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) simulation is now a standard component in the vehicle development process as a method for testing electronic control unit (ECU) software.
**Approach:** The ISO 26262 standard is mandatory for passenger car development worldwide.</div><div class="htmlview paragraph">ISO 26262 explicitly names HIL as a suitable test environment for software unit tests and integration tests, even recommends HIL for the verification of safety requirements at component level, and also names it as a suitable method for testing single ECUs/components and for testing ECU networks up to an entire virtual vehicle.</div><div class="htmlview paragraph">This paper describes the role of HIL simulation in the development of safety-relevant systems and the relationships between test methods proposed by ISO 26262 and HIL testing.
