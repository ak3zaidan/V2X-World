# Black hole attack detection in vehicular ad-hoc network using secure AODV routing algorithm
Ankit Kumar, V. Varadarajan, Abhishek Kumar, Pankaj Dadheech, S. Choudhary, Visvam Devadoss Ambeth Kumar et al. · Microprocessors and microsystems 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.micpro.2020.103352 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Black hole attacks via spoofed routing in VANETs.
**Approach:** Secure AODV with modified RREQ/RREP and encryption.
**Tools and scale:** NS-2.33.
**Results:** Improved drop, delay and PDR (no numbers).
