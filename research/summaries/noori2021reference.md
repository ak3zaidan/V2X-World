# A Reference Design for a Spectrum Misbehavior Authority for Connected Vehicle Environments
Hamed Noori, Akhil Prabhu, David G. Michelson, Mohsen M. Razlighi · 2021 IEEE 94th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2021-Fall) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2021-fall52928.2021.9625268 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** No authority exists to handle spectrum misbehaviour reports.
**Approach:** Reference design for a Spectrum Misbehavior Authority receiving reports through SCMS; interference source localisation PoC.
**Tools and scale:** DSRC, ITS-G5, C-V2X; PoC localisation.
**Results:** Implemented and validated localisation scheme.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Extends SCMS MA-style backend; our engine has MA and reporting.
**Novelty implications:** None.
