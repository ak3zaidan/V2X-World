# Reliable Routing for V2X Networks: A Joint Perspective of Trust Prediction and Attack Resistance
Ye Wang, Honghao Gao, Zhengzhe Xiang, A. Al-Dulaimi · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2024.3468472 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In intelligent transportation systems, data routing in vehicle-to-everything (V2X) networks is key to ensuring efficient information transfer among vehicles, pedestrians, and infrastructure.
**Approach:** The quality of data routing directly affects communication efficiency and system performance. However, data routing in V2X networks often faces potential security threats, which may lead to communication interruption, data delay, or information loss.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The experimental results show that, compared with the three baseline schemes, this scheme improves the accuracy and false alarm rate on the UNSW-NB15 dataset by 2.94% and 6.31%, respectively, and this scheme also performs better in terms of the data reception rate and transmission delay rate in actual application scenarios.
