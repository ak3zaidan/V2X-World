# Digital Twin Based Network Latency Prediction in Vehicular Networks
Yanfang Fu, Dengdeng Guo, Qiang Li, Liangxin Liu, Shaochun Qu, Wei Xiang · Electronics 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics11142217 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Latency irregularity in vehicular networks.
**Approach:** DT-collected latency data, autocorrelation check, RBF and Elman networks for prediction.
**Tools and scale:** MATLAB; measured latency data.
**Results:** Average relative error 7.6% (RBF) vs 14.2% (Elman).
