# Design and Implementation of a Co-Simulation Framework for Testing of Automated Driving Systems
Demin Nalic, Aleksa Pandurevic, Arno Eichberger, Branko Rogic · Sustainability 2020 · https://doi.org/10.20944/preprints202011.0252.v1 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The increasingly used approach of combining different simulation softwares in testing of automated driving systems (ADSs) increases the need for potential and convenient software designs.
**Approach:** In this paper, we present a software design for a CSP based on the Model–View–Controller (MVC) design pattern as well as an implementation of a complex CSP for virtual testing of ADSs.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: vissim, simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** The complexity levels of the vehicle, object, sensors, and environment models used are essential for valid and representative simulation results. The results showed that the presented CSP design and the implementation of the co-simulation can be used to generate relevant scenarios for testing of ADSs.
