# Secure Source Identification Scheme for Revocable Instruction Sharing in Vehicle Platoon
Yanan Zhao, Haiyang Yu, Yuhao Liang, Alessandro Brighente et al. · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2023.3329488 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The secure transmission of instructions among vehicles in a platoon is one of the most essential needs for a vehicle platoon.
**Approach:** Despite the existence of cryptographic methods to securely share instructions, instruction sharing is still subject to forgery, tampering, and denial-of-service attacks. Therefore, it is urgent to find a solution to perform data source identification to filter out irrelevant information (not instructions) while ensuring the authenticity of encrypted instructions is urgent to address.
