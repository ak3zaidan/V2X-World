# Causal Digital Twins for Cyber-Physical Security: A Framework for Robust Anomaly Detection in Industrial Control Systems
Mohammadhossein Homaei, Mehran Tarif, Pablo Garcia Rodriguez et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mlwa.2025.100824 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Industrial Control Systems (ICS) in water distribution and treatment face cyber-physical attacks exploiting network and physical vulnerabilities.
**Approach:** We propose a Causal Digital Twin (CDT) framework for water infrastructures, combining causal inference with digital twin modeling.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** CDT reduces false positives by 74\%, achieves 78.4\% root cause accuracy, and enables counterfactual defenses reducing attack success by 73.2\%. Real-time performance at 3.2 ms latency ensures safe and interpretable operation for medium-scale water systems.
