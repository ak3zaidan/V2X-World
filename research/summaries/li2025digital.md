# Digital Twin Online Channel Modeling: Challenges, Principles, and Applications
Junling Li, Cheng-Xiang Wang, Chen Huang, Tianrun Qi, Tong Wu · IEEE Vehicular Technology Magazine 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mvt.2025.3527729 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Different from traditional offline channel modeling, digital twin (DT) online channel modeling can sense and accurately characterize dynamic wireless channels in real time and can therefore greatly assist 6G network optimization.
**Approach:** This article proposes a novel promising framework and a step-by-step design procedure for DT online channel models (DTOCMs). By enabling continuous visualization and accurate prediction of dynamic channel variations, the DTOCM can synchronize the performance between simulated and real networks.
