# An Anonymous Communication Scheme based on Ring Signature in VANETs
Yichen Jiang, Yi Ji, Tianhua Liu · arXiv.org 2014 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.1639 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc networks allow vehicles to connect themselves as networks so that cars could communicate with each other.
**Approach:** This paper introduces an anonymous communication scheme providing integrity protection, multi-level privacy and auditability. The scheme is based on a certificateless ring signature proposed in this paper, which is contributed to reduce the length of the signature and simplify the key management.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** The computation overhead is close to a normal signature scheme, so it is efficient in most application scenarios.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
