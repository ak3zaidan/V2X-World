# A survey of security, privacy and trust issues in vehicular computation offloading and their solutions using blockchain.
Sharifah Yaqoub Fayi, Zhengguo Sheng · Open Research Europe 2023 · https://doi.org/10.12688/openreseurope.16189.2 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Continuous improvement in transportation systems and smart vehicles' appearance make new highly intensive applications.
**Approach:** Complex applications need high-performance capabilities, real-time responses, and generate massive amounts of data to process and exchange. This presents the idea of vehicular edge computing (VEC), which is proposed to handle complex applications and satisfy smart vehicle processing requirements.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: blockchain
**Results:** VEC enables computation offloading to an edge server to reduce communication latency, execution cost and energy consumption greatly. Moreover, trust issues in such an untrustworthy environment need an effective trust management solution and incentive mechanisms to improve overall security.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, offloading to another node opens up new vulnerabilities regarding security and privacy.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
