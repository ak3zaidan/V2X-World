# Analyzing the Impact of Adversarial Attacks on C-V2X-Enabled Road Safety: An Age of Information Perspective
Mahmudul Hassan Ashik, Moinul Hossain · ICC 2026 - IEEE International Conference on Communications 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc59461.2026.11587579 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** NR-V2X MAC vulnerabilities threaten road safety but evaluations rely on packet reception.
**Approach:** Novel resource-starvation attack on NR-V2X SPS; DTMC model of Age of Information under attack validated by simulation.
**Tools and scale:** WiLabV2Xsim; NR-V2X Mode 2 SPS.
**Results:** Attack reduces SLR of time-sensitive applications by up to about 15% and increases AoI; benign vehicles behave selfishly under scarcity.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- PRR cannot capture MAC-layer resource exhaustion (motivating AoI).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - NR sidelink attack with safety-oriented metric (runner-up direction on NR sidelink attacks); no PKI/verification load or traffic closed loop.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first NR-V2X SPS starvation attack or AoI-based safety framing. Open: combining such attacks with signature-verification load and SCMS revocation.
