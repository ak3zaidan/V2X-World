# DynamicRouteGPT: A Real-Time Multi-Vehicle Dynamic Navigation Framework Based on Large Language Models
Ziai Zhou, Bin Zhou, Hao Liu · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14185 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Real-time dynamic path planning in complex traffic environments presents challenges, such as varying traffic volumes and signal wait times.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a novel approach based on causal inference for real-time dynamic path planning, balancing global and local optimality. Experimental results show that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance in real-time dynamic path planning for multiple vehicles while providing explainable path selections, offering a novel and efficient solution for complex traffic environments.
