# One Versus All Binary Tree Method to Classify Misbehaviors in Imbalanced VeReMi Dataset
Omessaad Slama, Mounira Tarhouni, Salah Zidi, Bechir Alaya · IEEE Access 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2023.3337378 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Imbalanced multi-class VeReMi.
**Approach:** One-vs-All Binary Tree with six ML models and ensemble, five-fold CV.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi-derived dataset.
**Results:** OVA-BT improves accuracy over single multi-class model; ensemble beats best single.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Real data may not fit OVA assumptions.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — offline.
**Novelty implications:** Saturated.
