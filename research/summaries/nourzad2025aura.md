# AURA: Adaptive Unified Reasoning and Automation with LLM-Guided MARL for NextG Cellular Networks
Narjes Nourzad, Mingyu Zong, Bhaskar Krishnamachari · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.17506 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Next-generation (NextG) cellular networks are expected to manage dynamic traffic while sustaining high performance.
**Approach:** We present AURA, a framework that integrates cloud-based LLMs for high-level planning with base stations modeled as MARL agents for local decision-making.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: reinforcement learning.
**Results:** To reduce latency, AURA employs batched communication so that agents update the LLM's view of the environment and receive improved feedback. In a simulated 6G scenario, AURA improves resilience, reducing dropped handoff requests by more than half under normal and high traffic and lowering system failures.
