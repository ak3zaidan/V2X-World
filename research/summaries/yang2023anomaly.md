# Anomaly Detection Against GPS Spoofing Attacks on Connected and Autonomous Vehicles Using Learning From Demonstration
Zhen Yang, Jun Ying, Junjie Shen, Yi-Heng Feng, Qi Alfred Chen, Z. Mao, Henry X. Liu · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2023.3269029 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** GPS spoofing attacks pose great challenges to connected vehicle (CVs) safety applications and localization of autonomous vehicles (AVs). In this paper, we propose to utilize transportation and vehicle engineering domain knowledge to detect GPS spoofing attacks towards CVs and AVs.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose to utilize transportation and vehicle engineering domain knowledge to detect GPS spoofing attacks towards CVs and AVs. We validate the proposed method using two threat models (i.e., attacks targeting the multi-sensor fusion system of AVs and attacks targeting the intersection movement assist application of CVs) on two real-world datasets (i.e., KAIST and Michigan roundabout dataset).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Results show that the proposed model is able to detect almost all of the attacks in time with low false positive and false negative rates.
