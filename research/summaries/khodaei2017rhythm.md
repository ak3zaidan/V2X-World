# RHyTHM: A randomized hybrid scheme to hide in the mobile crowd
M. Khodaei, Andreas Messing, P. Papadimitratos · IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc.2017.8275642 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicles that run out of pseudonyms without connectivity become trackable when generating pseudonyms on the fly.
**Approach:** RHyTHM: vehicles with valid pseudonyms randomly join in using on-the-fly self-certified pseudonyms with aligned lifetimes.
**Tools and scale:** Evaluation of privacy and computational overhead (details not in abstract).
**Results:** Enhanced privacy for disconnected users with reasonable overhead.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial: engine models pseudonym pools and top-ups over cellular; pool depletion is a shared concern.
**Novelty implications:** Pool depletion privacy is studied; coupling top-up connectivity to safety in a full SCMS simulation remains open.
