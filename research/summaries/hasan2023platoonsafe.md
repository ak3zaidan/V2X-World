# PlatoonSAFE: An Integrated Simulation Tool for Evaluating Platoon Safety
Shahriar Hasan, Joseba Gorospe, Svetlana Girs, Arrate Alonso Gómez, Elisabeth Uhlemann · IEEE Open Journal of Intelligent Transportation Systems 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ojits.2023.3271608 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Evaluating platoon safety under transient communication outages.
**Approach:** PlatoonSAFE open-source tool: runtime manager assigning performance levels by communication quality, emergency braking strategies, two onboard ML predictors.
**Tools and scale:** Bidirectionally coupled network and traffic simulation (Plexe/Veins-based per paper); proof-of-concept use cases.
**Results:** Transient outages significantly impact platoon safety and performance.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Potential extensions listed in Section VII (not extracted in detail).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - links communication degradation to safety outcomes in simulation; no security/PKI.
**Novelty implications:** Communication-to-safety coupling is studied for platoons; security-to-safety cost (direction 2) is not.
