# MSR-DoS: Modular Square Root-Based Scheme to Resist Denial of Service (DoS) Attacks in 5G-Enabled Vehicular Networks
‪Mahmood A. Al-Shareeda‬‏, Selvakumar Manickam · IEEE Access 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2022.3222488 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Traffic safety and efficiency are extremely significant in both private and public transportation. The fifth-generation (5G)-enabled vehicular networks works wirelessly to share information among vehicles for helping drivers and passengers.
**Approach:** To address this, this paper proposes modular square root-based to resist denial of service (DoS) attacks (MSR-DoS) scheme in 5G-enabled vehicular networks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Meanwhile, the proposed MSR-DoS scheme reduces the computation overhead of signing the message and verifying the message by 99.80% and 98.55%, respectively.
