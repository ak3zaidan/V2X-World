# An Efficient Vehicle-Assisted Aggregate Authentication Scheme for Infrastructure-Less Vehicular Networks
Hao-Wen Tan, Wenying Zheng, P. Vijayakumar, K. Sakurai et al. · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2022.3176406 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** However, the existing schemes all focus on the common vehicular communication scenarios where facilities are deployed, whereas the secure data exchange in the abnormal infrastructure-less vehicular environment has not been properly investigated.
**Approach:** To deal with unpredictable abnormal situations caused by artificial or natural disasters such as earthquakes and floods, a distinctive vehicle-assisted aggregate authentication mechanism for infrastructure-less vehicular networks is presented in this paper. The security proofs and discussions regarding vital security properties are presented, while the performance analysis follows.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** Compared with the state-of-the-art, advantages in terms of security and performance properties can be proved.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "With assistance from the neighboring vehicles, the homomorphic signature involving all requesting vehicles is generated and forwarded to the remaining functional RSUs."
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
