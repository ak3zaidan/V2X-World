# Demystifying Network Foundation Models
Sylee Beltiukov, Satyandra Guthula, Wenbo Guo, Walter Willinger, Arpit Gupta · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23089 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Network foundation models are judged only on downstream performance.
**Approach:** Three-part intrinsic evaluation: embedding geometry, metric alignment, causal sensitivity to protocol perturbations.
**Tools and scale:** Five datasets; four NFMs.
**Results:** All show anisotropy, inconsistent sensitivity, payload dependency; fixes improve F1 by up to 0.35.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Scope and metrics far from exhaustive.
- Cosine similarity may mislead for some models.
- Public datasets may be flawed or biased.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
