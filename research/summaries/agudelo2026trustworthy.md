# Towards Trustworthy 6G Network Digital Twins: A Framework for Validating Counterfactual What-If Analysis in Edge Computing Resources
Julian Jimenez Agudelo, Paola Soto, Ayat Zaki-Hindi, Jean-Sébastien Sottet, Sébastien Faye, Nina Slamnik-Kriještorac et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14787 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** NDT counterfactual what-if analysis needs validation.
**Approach:** Data-driven NDT on 6G-TWIN with telemetry pipeline and validation via Sign Agreement and Directional Sensitivity.
**Tools and scale:** Kubernetes cluster.
**Results:** R^2 > 0.99 for DNN and XGBoost; XGBoost Sa > 0.90, DNN about 0.5.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Aggregate error metrics hide biases under what-if scenarios.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - validating counterfactual what-if outputs of learned twins (direction 3 evaluation methodology).
**Novelty implications:** Directional counterfactual validation metrics exist; exact per-unit counterfactual ground truth is stronger.
