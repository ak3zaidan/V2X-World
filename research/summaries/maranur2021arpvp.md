# ARPVP: Attack Resilient Position-Based VANET Protocol Using Ant Colony Optimization
Jyoti Maranur, Basavaraj Mathapati · unknown venue 2021 · https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-718374/v1 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Position-based VANET routing vulnerable to attacks.
**Approach:** ARPVP: trust-based detection (speed, link quality, congestion) plus trust-based route formation.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Throughput +13%, PDR +12.5%, delay -11%, overhead -14%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Research other attack types
- apply other AI methods for route formation
**Overlap with our engine:** none — Routing.
**Novelty implications:** None.
