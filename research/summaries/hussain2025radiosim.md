# RadioSim Agent: Combining Large Language Models and Deterministic EM Simulators for Interactive Radio Map Analysis
Sajjad Hussain, Conor Brennan · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.05912 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** EM simulators need expert configuration.
**Approach:** RadioSim Agent: LLM orchestrates ray-tracing tools and visually analyses radio maps.
**Tools and scale:** Urban UAV scenarios.
**Results:** Agent selects propagation mechanisms and summarises pathloss.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Demonstrations only.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — LLM agent configuring and interpreting a deterministic simulator, as ours does (radio only).
**Novelty implications:** LLM-plus-deterministic-simulator agents exist; cross-layer V2X/PKI version remains open.
