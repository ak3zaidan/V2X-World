# Consensus-based Distributed Quantum Kernel Learning for Speech Recognition
Kuan-Cheng Chen, Wenxuan Ma, Xiao-Tian Xu · 2025 IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech, and Signal Processing Workshops (ICASSPW) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icasspw65056.2025.11011036 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper presents a Consensus-based Distributed Quantum Kernel Learning (CDQKL) framework aimed at improving speech recognition through distributed quantum computing. CDQKL addresses the challenges of scalability and data privacy in centralized quantum kernel learning.
**Approach:** This paper presents a Consensus-based Distributed Quantum Kernel Learning (CDQKL) framework aimed at improving speech recognition through distributed quantum computing.
**Tools and scale:** Experimental evaluations on benchmark speech emotion recognition datasets demonstrate that CDQKL achieves competitive classification accuracy and scalability compared to centralized and local quantum kernel learning models.
**Results:** Experimental evaluations on benchmark speech emotion recognition datasets demonstrate that CDQKL achieves competitive classification accuracy and scalability compared to centralized and local quantum kernel learning models.
