# BiRep: A Reputation Scheme to Mitigate the Effects of Black-Hole Nodes in Delay-Tolerant Internet of Vehicles
C. Nabais, P. Pereira, Naércio Magaia · Italian National Conference on Sensors 2021 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s21030835 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Black-hole nodes in delay-tolerant IoV.
**Approach:** Decentralized BiRep reputation on Prophet routing.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation (50 cars scenario).
**Results:** Detection around 70-80% with low false positives; increased delivery ratio.
