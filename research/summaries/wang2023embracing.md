# Embracing Uncertainty: A Diffusion Generative Model of Spectrum Efficiency in 5G Networks
Hao Wang, Zhiwei Tang, Shutao Zhang, Chao Shen et al. · 2023 International Conference on Wireless Communications and Signal Processing (WCSP) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wcsp58612.2023.10404983 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Spectrum efficiency modeling is a crucial enabling technology for optimizing 5G networks. Neural networks have been employed to model the complex and nonlinear relationship between spectrum efficiency and other 5G network features, including reference signal received power (RSRP), modulation and coding scheme (MCS), and rank indicator (RI).
**Approach:** To address this limitation and embrace the uncertainty, we propose a diffusion generative model that can learn the conditional distribution of spectrum efficiency given the information of RSRP, MCS, and RI, providing a more accurate representation of the inherent variability in wireless networks. Additionally, we introduce a novel neural network architecture that incorporates a U-Net structure with Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) as building blocks, facilitating efficient learning of the denoising function within the diffusion generative model.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G, real-world. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed diffusion generative model accurately learns the statistical distribution of spectrum efficiency.
