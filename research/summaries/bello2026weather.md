# Weather Data Spoofing Attacks on Rain-Adaptive Millimeter-Wave Frequency Selection in V2X Communication Networks
Rasheed Bello, Idreez Yusuf, Justice Adjei Owusu, Oluwatobiloba Aiyewunmi et al. · arXiv (Cornell University) 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20601 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Weather-aware mmWave band selection as attack surface.
**Approach:** Spoof rainfall input to force carrier band and reduce range without transmitting.
**Tools and scale:** MilliCar ns-3 with 3GPP NR V2X carrier, per-band propagation, beamforming, blockage; eight-vehicle platoon.
**Results:** Forcing 73 GHz holds reliable range at 38 m versus 82 m honest.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — attack-to-communication impact in NR V2X simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Cross-layer non-RF input attacks shown; safety impact via traffic not stated.
