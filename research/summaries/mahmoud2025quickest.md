# Quickest Attempt Attack (QuAck) Against Randomized KF Innovations-based Detection in AVs with Integrated GPS-IMU
Marim Mahmoud, Wesam Al Amiri, Terry N. Guo et al. · 2025 IEEE 68th International Midwest Symposium on Circuits and Systems (MWSCAS) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mwscas53549.2025.11244424 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Advancements in GPS technology have significantly enhanced navigational accuracy, yet they remain vulnerable to spoofing attacks.
**Approach:** This paper presents a spoofing attack capable of manipulating the trajectory of an autonomous vehicle (AV) equipped with a global positioning system and inertial measurement unit system. However, our proposed algorithm, quickest attempt attack (QuAck), identifies that an attacker can bypass randomized NIS in a realistic threat model.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Advancements in GPS technology have significantly enhanced navigational accuracy, yet they remain vulnerable to spoofing attacks. This is achieved without triggering detection mechanisms that rely on Kalman Filter's innovationsbased detection.
