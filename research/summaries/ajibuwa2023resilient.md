# Resilient and Privacy-Preserving Threshold Vehicular Public Key Infrastructure (VPKI)
Opeyemi Ajibuwa, Samson Damilola Fabiyi, Emmanuel Gbenga Dada · arXiv.org 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.02711 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** VPKI single points of failure, Sybil misuse, CRL size.
**Approach:** Threshold VPKI with Schnorr threshold signatures across CAs, time-restrictive pseudonyms, region-specific CRLs.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Improved resiliency, reduced handover rates, better scalability.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — Threshold signing of certificates by multiple CAs, overlapping our threshold authorities (FROST-like Schnorr).
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first threshold-signing VPKI; open: FROST/CGGMP21 in a full SCMS with traffic/radio and outage analysis.
