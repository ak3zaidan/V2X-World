# CT2‐MDS: Cooperative trust‐aware tolerant misbehaviour detection system for connected and automated vehicles
Ying Liu, Hongwei Xue, Weichao Zhuang, Faan Wang, Liwei Xu, Guodong Yin · IET Intelligent Transport Systems 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1049/itr2.12139 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Securing exchanged CAV messages.
**Approach:** CT2-MDS: V2X network model in complex scenarios with simulated attacks; cooperative trust factor per message; sensor measurement uncertainty; multi-model fusion with cross-validation into misbehaviour classes.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** Outperforms single classifiers with good accuracy across attack densities.

- None stated in abstract.
