# Misbehavior Reporting Protocol for C-ITS
Joseph Kamel, I. Ben Jemaa, Arnaud Kaiser, P. Urien · IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc.2018.8628407 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** How a detecting entity reports misbehaviour to the Misbehaviour Authority with usable evidence.
**Approach:** Functional requirements for misbehaviour reporting and a report message format specifying which evidence data to include for the MA.
**Tools and scale:** Protocol/format design (basis for ETSI TS 103 759 work).
**Results:** Report format and evidence content defined.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — misbehaviour report format to an MA, which our engine implements in the closed MBD-to-MA loop.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim novel report formats; the open part is how report content and timing drive MA decisions and safety outcomes.
