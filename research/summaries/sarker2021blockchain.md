# Blockchain-based root of trust management in security credential management system for vehicular communications
Arijet Sarker, SangHyun Byun, Wenjun Fan, Sang‐Yoon Chang · ACM Symposium on Applied Computing 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3412841.3441905 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** SCMS elector-based root management could be more decentralised.
**Approach:** Blockchain-Based Root Management on Hyperledger Fabric replacing RCA with electors.
**Tools and scale:** Hyperledger Fabric experiments.
**Results:** Lightweight processing, efficient ledger size, multiple tx/s.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — SCMS root of trust / elector management, which our engine models via trust lists.
**Novelty implications:** Elector alternatives exist; not our directions.
