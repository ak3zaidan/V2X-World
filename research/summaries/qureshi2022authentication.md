# Authentication scheme for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles based Internet of Vehicles networks
Kashif Naseer Qureshi, Muhammad Arslan Saleem Sandila, Ibrahim Tariq Javed, Tiziana Margaria et al. · Egyptian Informatics Journal 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eij.2021.07.001 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Due to the open nature of these networks, security and user privacy is always a serious concern.
**Approach:** The existing solutions have been designed to resolve and tackle security issues such as authenticity, insurance of data integrity, provision of correct message authentication, and removal of redundant authentication. This paper proposes an Efficient Authentication Scheme for Safety Applications for Internet of Vehicles (EASSAIV) for message authentication.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** No quantitative results stated in the abstract.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "However, existing schemes have some limitations in terms of computational costs and efficiency and do not provide security at all levels."
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
