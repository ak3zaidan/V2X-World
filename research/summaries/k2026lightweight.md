# Lightweight Explicitly Certifed ECC-based Mutual Authentication Scheme for Secure Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
A. K, S. Jayanthy, ArunSekar Rajasekaran, Hakshaya Shree R et al. · 2026 7th International Conference on Inventive Research in Computing Applications (ICIRCA) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icirca69024.2026.11570384 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Providing an efficient, anonymous authentication scheme with low computational cost in Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks (VANETs) remains a significant challenge. Existing schemes often incur high computational overhead during certificate and signature verification, leads to increased message loss and a failure to meet the requirement of verifying hundreds of messages per second.
**Approach:** We propose the protocol, an efficient anonymous authentication solution designed to prevent malicious entities from infiltrating the VANET.
