# Leveraging Large Language Models for Multi-Objective and Adaptive SFC Deployment: Techniques, Case Study, and Promising Directions

**Problem:** Network function virtualization enables flexible network services through Service Function Chain (SFC) deployment.
**Approach:** Existing SFC deployment solutions rely on mixed integer linear programming (MILP) solvers or heuristics. However, they fail to support multi-objective deployment and adaptive service operation.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Experimental results demonstrate that compared to existing solutions, MoED reduces the power consumption by up to 68.4% while achieving near-optimal performance in terms of throughput, latency, and resource consumption, and reduces deployment strategy solving time by several orders of magnitude.
