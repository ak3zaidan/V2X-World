# Secure Emergency Services Protocol over Vehicular Cloud Computing
Lewis Nkenyereye, K. Rhee · Research Briefs on Information & Communication Technology Evolution 2016 · https://doi.org/10.56801/rebicte.v2i.31 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Natural disasters such as typhoon or earthquake requires an efficient disaster rescue system in orderto minimize the losses. Vehicular cloud Computing (VCC) which integrates cloud computing withinthe vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) offers a suitable platform especially for disaster assistanceand rescue teams (DART) which use vehicles to reach the disaster locations.
**Approach:** However, some disasters such as earthquakes and tsunamis can repeatedly happen within ashort interval, requiring the disaster documents to be updated continuously for better rescue services.In this paper we present a secure protocol for emergency services over vehicular cloud computingwhich enables the DART vehicles to access disaster scenes and have updated information of disasterscenes during their activities.
