# Toward Digital Network Twins: Integrating Sionna RT in ns-3 for 6G Multi-Rat Networks Simulations
Roberto Pegurri, Francesco Linsalata, Eugenio Moro, Jakob Hoydis et al. · IEEE INFOCOM 2025 - IEEE Conference on Computer Communications Workshops (INFOCOM WKSHPS) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/infocomwkshps65812.2025.11152946 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The increasing complexity of 6G systems demands innovative tools for network management, simulation, and optimization. This work introduces the integration of ns-3 with Sionna RT, establishing the foundation for the first open source full-stack Digital Network Twin (DNT) capable of supporting multi-RAT.
**Approach:** With up to 65% observed differences in application-layer performance compared to stochastic models, this work highlights the transformative potential of ray-traced simulations for 6G research, training, and network management.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: ns-3, NS-3, Sionna, 6G, ray trac. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Tested in a challenging vehicular urban scenario, the proposed solution demonstrates significant improvements in accurately modeling wireless channels and their cascading effects on higher network layers.
