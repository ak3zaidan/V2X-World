# Simuhack : A Realistic Attack Simulation Framework for Enhancing Cybersecurity in Autonomous Vehicles
D. Kiruthika, K.A. Kameshwari, K.S. Sanjay Kumar et al. · 2024 First International Conference on Pioneering Developments in Computer Science &amp;amp; Digital Technologies (IC2SDT) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ic2sdt62152.2024.10695991 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As autonomous driving technology advances, modern vehicles are evolving into sophisticated digital systems.
**Approach:** This evolution introduces new challenges for ensuring safety and security in vehicle design, particularly concerning smart sensors, Electronic Control Units (ECUs), and in-vehicle communications. To overcome this hurdle, proposed the Simuhack framework within the CARLA software, which simulates attack datasets for comprehensive security analysis in vehicle design.
**Tools and scale:** To overcome this hurdle, proposed the Simuhack framework within the CARLA software, which simulates attack datasets for comprehensive security analysis in vehicle design. We demonstrate the utility and effectiveness of our framework by evaluating five cyber attack scenarios using the LGSVL simulation software.
**Results:** We demonstrate the utility and effectiveness of our framework by evaluating five cyber attack scenarios using the LGSVL simulation software.
