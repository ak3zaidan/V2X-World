# A threshold group signature scheme suitable for the Internet of Things
Bei Gong, Xinyi Zhang, Yang Cao, Zheng Li et al. · Concurrency and Computation: Practice and Experience 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1002/cpe.6243 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Group manager as a single risk in threshold group signatures.
**Approach:** EC threshold group signature with mutual authentication of members and manager.
**Tools and scale:** Security and performance analysis.
**Results:** Shorter signatures and lower computation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial: threshold signing, but not FROST/CGGMP21 authorities.
**Novelty implications:** Background for threshold authorities.
