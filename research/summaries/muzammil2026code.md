**Approach:** Replication package for a measurement audit of VeReMi and VeReMi Extension: leakage-controlled loader, 14 experiments, 21 verifiers, claim checker, container.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi, VeReMi Extension; 92-paper corpus match matrix.
