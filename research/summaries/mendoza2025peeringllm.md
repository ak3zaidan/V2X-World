# PeeringLLM-Bench: Evaluating LLMs for BGP Configuration Tasks
John Robert Mendoza, Roel Ocampo · Proceedings of the 20th Asian Internet Engineering Conference 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3763400.3763451 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Quality of LLM-generated BGP configurations is underexplored.
**Approach:** PeeringLLM-Bench framework measuring syntax, semantics and reliability, verified by network simulation.
**Tools and scale:** Open-source LLMs; multiple vendor formats.
**Results:** LLMs can produce valid BGP configs; effectiveness depends on OS and complexity.

- Not stated in the abstract.
