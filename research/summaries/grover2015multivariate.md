# Multivariate verification for sybil attack detection in VANET
Jyoti Grover, Manoj Singh Gaur, Vijay Laxmi · Open Computer Science 2015 · https://doi.org/10.1515/comp-2015-0006 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil attack consequences and detection.
**Approach:** RSUs passively overhear RSS, distance and angle to detect Sybils.
**Tools and scale:** Realistic traces from a multi-agent microscopic traffic simulator.
**Results:** Effective localisation of Sybil nodes.
