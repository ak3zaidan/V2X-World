# Proxy Vehicle-Based Message Authentication Protocol for Secure Transportation Communication over VANET
H. Byeon, Manoj Meghrajani, A. Zunjur, Santosh Yerasuri et al. · 2023 International Conference on New Frontiers in Communication, Automation, Management and Security (ICCAMS) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccams60113.2023.10526120 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Due to their special characteristics, vehicle-based ad hoc networks (VANET) are susceptible to a number of security issues.
**Approach:** Particularly when many vehicles are present within the RSU’s coverage area, existing authentication methods struggle to ensure that the roadside unit (RSU) rapidly authenticates each vehicle’s signature. A novel agent-based message verification method is consequently proposed.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** The RSU verifies the output results from the proxy vehicle to reduce computational overhead. Additionally, simulation results show that the suggested authentication method requires 76% less time to validate 3,000 messages than the conventional authentication method and can more effectively meet the VANET’s requirements for message authentication.
