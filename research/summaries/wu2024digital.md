# A Digital Twin Framework for Physical-Virtual Integration in V2X-Enabled Connected Vehicle Corridors
Keshu Wu, Pei Li, Yang Cheng, Steven T. Parker, B. Ran, D. Noyce et al. · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2025.3564351 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Real-world traffic digital twins spanning infrastructure, vehicles, signals and communications are rare.
**Approach:** Traffic DT of a real C-V2X connected-vehicle corridor with data pipeline, synchronisation, scalable redundant architecture; feedback to the physical side (signal timing, advisories, incident notices).
**Tools and scale:** Real C-V2X corridor infrastructure plus traffic simulation.
**Results:** Improved data integration, synchronisation and simulation fidelity (numbers not extracted).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Discusses potential improvements of the DT (section V); real-world DTs limited by infrastructure access, heterogeneous data and real-time sync.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - joint traffic, signal and C-V2X communication replica; no PKI/security.
**Novelty implications:** Traffic+comm DTs with real corridors exist; security/PKI ground truth is not covered.
