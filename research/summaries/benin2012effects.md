# The effects of quality of service on vehicular network pseudonym distribution in congested urban environments
Joseph Benin, Michael Nowatkowski, Henry Owen · 2012 IEEE Wireless Communications and Networking Conference Workshops (WCNCW) 2012 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wcncw.2012.6215523 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Pseudonym distribution under congestion in urban settings.
**Approach:** QoS priority and stochastic delays for pseudonym transmission in downtown grid and urban highway.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3, heavily congested urban scenarios.
**Results:** QoS or a simple imitation significantly improves background throughput and pseudonym distribution.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Over-the-air pseudonym distribution under load relates to our pseudonym provisioning/top-ups and RSU relay; we use cellular TCP/TLS and SCMS.
**Novelty implications:** Same topic area; does not provide the closed-loop, SCMS-complete, cross-layer evaluation our engine targets, so our claims stand but this work must be cited as related.
