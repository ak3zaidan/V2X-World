# Assessment of Cyberattack Detection-Isolation Algorithm for CAV Platoons Using SUMO
Sanchita Ghosh, Tanushree Roy · 2025 American Control Conference (ACC) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.23919/acc63710.2025.11107994 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** Cyberattacks on CAV platoons in V2V and V2I.
**Approach:** Residual-generator detection and V2I isolation in SUMO-TraCI with Matlab.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO, NGSIM, UDDS; DDoS, FDI, replay.
**Results:** Detected all V2V and V2I attacks at 20 Hz sampling.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- First step toward practicable resilience.
- test-bed in future.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — In-simulation attack detection and isolation with traffic simulator.
**Novelty implications:** None.
