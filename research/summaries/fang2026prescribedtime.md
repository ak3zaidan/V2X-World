# Prescribed‐Time Fuzzy Adaptive Performance‐Constraint Control for Nonlinear Vehicle Platoon Under DoS and FDI Attacks
Yi-Xian Fang, Ge Lu, Shuai Cheng, Jun Hu, Ke-Wen Li · International Journal of Adaptive Control and Signal Processing 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1002/acs.70067 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ABSTRACT This article focuses on the issue of predefined‐time performance constrains fuzzy adaptive secure control for nonlinear vehicle platoon system under DoS and FDI attacks.
**Approach:** First, fuzzy logic systems (FLSs) are utilized to estimate unknown nonlinear dynamics. A switching state observer is designed to estimate the state information of the leader vehicle, which enables handling of the DoS attacks occurring in the inter‐vehicle communication links.
