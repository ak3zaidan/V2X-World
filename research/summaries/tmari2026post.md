# A Post-Quantum Certificateless Aggregate Signature Scheme for VANETs Resilient to Rogue-Key Attacks
Zainab Y. Al Tmari, Mohanad Ahmed Abdulrazzaq Diwan Alzamili, Jalal M. H. Altmemi, Karrar Ali Abdullah et al. · Journal of Communications 2026 · https://doi.org/10.12720/jcm.21.2.233-244 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Classical certificateless aggregate signatures are quantum-vulnerable and rogue-key prone.
**Approach:** PQ-CLAS with double verification against rogue keys and malicious KGC. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Experimental timing.
**Results:** Sign 1.25 ms, verify 2.85 ms, aggregate verification of 100 vehicles 302 ms, 1440 bits per message, about 18% better than 2022 work.
