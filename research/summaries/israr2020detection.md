# Detection and Minimization of Jamming Attacks to Enhance String Stability in VANETs
Abid Israr, M. Ashraf, Sadeeq Jan, F. Khan · Journal of Information Communication Technologies and Robotic Applications 2020 · https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/84488fd36417dabec47a1b87ccee9a90be26ec70 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Adhoc Networks (VANETs) is an emerging area and has achieved popularity in the last decade.
**Approach:** Due to its increased use, attacks on such networks have also increased. In this paper, we have evaluated an approach for Jamming attack’s detection and also to minimize such attacks for the string stability of vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Satisfying results in terms of maintaining the string stability among the nodes are achieved.
