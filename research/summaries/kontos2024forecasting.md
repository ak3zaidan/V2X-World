# Forecasting Roll Rate for Sensor Plausibility Check in Off-Road Vehicle Conditions
János Kontos, László Bódis, Ágnes Vathy-Fogarassy · 2024 IEEE 3rd Conference on Information Technology and Data Science (CITDS) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/citds62610.2024.10791355 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cheap roll-rate plausibility checks off-road.
**Approach:** LSTM predicting roll rate 0.04 s ahead from vehicle sensor signals.
**Tools and scale:** Five hours of off-road test-track data.
**Results:** Reliable prediction compared with literature.
