# Comparing the Use of Simu5G and 5G‐Sim‐V2I/N Modules When Analysing the Edge Computing Resource Management Efficiency
Cristina Bernad, Katja Gilly, Sonja Filiposka, Nigel Thomas, Pedro Juan Roig, Salvador Alcaraz · Modelling and Simulation in Engineering 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1155/mse/7283539 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Does the choice of 5G simulation framework affect edge orchestration results?
**Approach:** Two identical studies differing only in the 5G framework (Simu5G vs 5G-Sim-V2I/N).
**Tools and scale:** OMNeT++, Simu5G, 5G-Sim-V2I/N; smart city vehicular scenario.
**Results:** Average end-to-end edge latency differs by more than 21 times; over 25% more migrations with 5G-Sim-V2I/N.

- Not stated.

**Novelty implications:** Strengthens the need for cross-validated stacks; one-change comparisons of simulators are prior art.
