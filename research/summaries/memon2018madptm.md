# MADPTM: Mix zones and dynamic pseudonym trust management system for location privacy
Imran Memon, Hamid Turab Mirza · International Journal of Communication Systems 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1002/dac.3795 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Event trust management with pseudonym expiry and mix zones.
**Approach:** MADPTM mix zones plus dynamic pseudonym trust management with a reporting server to shorten pseudonym expiry.
**Tools and scale:** Simulations under trust attack models.
**Results:** Resilient; outperforms existing methods.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — pseudonym change interacting with trust.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
