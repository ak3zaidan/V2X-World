# SecureGuard: A Certificate Validation System in Public Key Infrastructure
Arwa Alrawais, Abdulrahman Alhothaily, Xiuzhen Cheng, Chunqiang Hu et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2018.2805700 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Certificate validation is slow and costly for TLS clients.
**Approach:** SecureGuard: ISP proxy-cache servers deliver validation information.
**Tools and scale:** Alexa Top 1M TLS handshake analysis; evaluation.
**Results:** Current systems fail to deliver validation info timely; SecureGuard is efficient.
