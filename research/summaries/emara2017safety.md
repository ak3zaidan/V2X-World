# Safety-Aware Location Privacy in VANET: Evaluation and Comparison
Karim Emara · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2017.2736885 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** VANET safety applications broadcast cooperative awareness messages (CAM) periodically to provide vehicles with continuous updates about the surrounding traffic.
**Approach:** The periodicity and the spatiotemporal information contained in these messages allow a global adversary to track vehicle movements. Many privacy schemes have been proposed for VANET, but only few schemes consider their impact on safety applications.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: location privacy).
**Results:** A distortion-based privacy metric is initially proposed and compared with other popular privacy metrics showing its effectiveness in measuring privacy.
