# The research of Random Forest Intrusion Detection Model based on Optimization in Internet of Vehicles
Yalin Li, Fei Li, Jiaqi Song · Journal of Physics: Conference Series 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/1757/1/012149 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Security model is the main means to protect the network information security of vehicle.
**Approach:** Due to the rapid development of artificial intelligence in recent years, machine learning technology is also emerging in the field of Internet of vehicles security. The random forest model is a strong classifier and can prevent overfitting better than the decision tree model.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** In this paper, it is optimized in a lightweight way to reduce the time consumption and improve the accuracy of intrusion detection in the vehicle networking intrusion detection model.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, only using the traditional random forest invasion detection model has some problems, such as: the model detection time is long, the false alarm rate is high, the ability of using platform transplantation is poor, etc.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
