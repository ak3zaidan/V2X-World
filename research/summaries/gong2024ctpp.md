# CTPP: A Conditional Trajectory Privacy Preservation Scheme Using Blockchain in V2X
Yujing Gong, Bin-Jie Hu · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2024.3380619 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X), the periodic broadcasting and reception of safety beacons by vehicles are pivotal for ensuring security.
**Approach:** Nevertheless, the transmission of these messages in unencrypted formats over public channels presents substantial security risks. Adversaries with prior knowledge can potentially reconstruct the trajectories of targeted vehicles, thereby compromising sensitive information such as the behavioral patterns of drivers.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: location privacy).
**Results:** Extensive experimental results demonstrate that our CTPP significantly enhances trajectory privacy while incurring a moderate utility loss compared to the existing methods.
