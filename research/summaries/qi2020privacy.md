# A Privacy-Preserving Authentication and Pseudonym Revocation Scheme for VANETs
Jiayu Qi, Tianhan Gao · IEEE Access 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2020.3027718 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Pseudonym revocation and CRL size.
**Approach:** Identity-based anonymous authentication with pseudonyms and Bloom-filter-compressed CRLs; batch pseudonym revocation preserving unlinkability.
**Tools and scale:** Security and cost analysis.
**Results:** Meets requirements; cost formulas given.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future work: pseudonym generation not dependent on RSU/TA (certificateless).
- Future work: replace CRL with non-interactive zero-knowledge for revocation performance.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — compressed CRLs for pseudonym revocation.
**Novelty implications:** Minor.
