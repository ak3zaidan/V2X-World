# Digital Twin for Safety Validation and Crash Scenario Simulations
Aarthi Selvakumar, Ravikumar Natarajan, Dilmurod Turaev, Anorgul Ashirova, Khayrulla Urozboev, N. Naveena · Digital Twin Approaches in Autonomous Vehicles 2026 · https://doi.org/10.4018/979-8-3373-7785-8.ch004 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Ensuring safety and reliability is paramount in the development of Autonomous Vehicles (AVs).
**Approach:** Digital Twin (DT) technology provides a dynamic virtual counterpart to real vehicles, enabling scenario-based safety validation and crash simulations without physical risk. This chapter explores how DTs integrate real-time sensor data, AI-driven analytics, and multi-physics modeling to simulate complex collision dynamics and evaluate vehicle responses.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting. The available text does not describe a V2X simulator, radio stack, traffic model or SCMS-style PKI in enough detail to report; scale not stated.
**Results:** Emphasis is placed on aligning DT testing with international standards such as ISO 26262 and SOTIF.
