# How LLM Saved Me from Struggling with Experiment Reproduction: LEO Networking as A Case Study
Yibo Wang, Yunan Hou, Zeqi Lai, Hewu Li et al. · Proceedings of the 2025 3rd Workshop on LEO Networking and Communication 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3748749.3749084 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Reproducing network experiments is laborious.
**Approach:** LASER: semi-automated LLM tool integrated with LEO satellite network simulation to reproduce papers.
**Tools and scale:** LEO network simulation case studies.
**Results:** Preliminary evidence of reproduced results consistent with originals.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Preliminary evidence only.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — LLM driving a network simulator.
**Novelty implications:** LLM-driven simulator use exists; V2X cross-layer analysis remains open.
