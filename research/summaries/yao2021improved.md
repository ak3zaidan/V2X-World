# An Improved and Privacy-Preserving Mutual Authentication Scheme with Forward Secrecy in VANETs
Meng-meng Yao, Xiaoming Wang, Qing-Qing Gan, Yijian Lin et al. · Secur. Commun. Networks 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2021/6698099 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc network (VANETs) plays a major part in intelligent transportation to enhance traffic efficiency and safety. Security and privacy are the essential matters needed to be tackled due to the open communication channel.
**Approach:** In this article, we propose a privacy-preserving mutual authentication scheme with batch verification for VANETs which support both message authentication and identity authentication.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** More importantly, the proposed scheme achieves forward secrecy, which means the exposure of the shared key will not compromise the previous interaction. The experiment analysis results based on communication and computation cost demonstrate that our scheme is more efficient compared with the related schemes.
