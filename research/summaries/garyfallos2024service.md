# Service Level Prediction in Non-Markovian Nonstationary Queues: A Simulation-Based Deep Learning Approach
Spyros Garyfallos, Yunan Liu, Pere Barlet-Ros, Alberto Cabellos-Aparicio · 2024 Winter Simulation Conference (WSC) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wsc63780.2024.10838828 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Service-level prediction in non-Markovian, nonstationary queues is hard.
**Approach:** Simulation-trained autoregressive RNN (SADLA).
**Tools and scale:** Synthetic queueing simulation data.
**Results:** High accuracy with six orders of magnitude less computation than simulation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — conceptually like our surrogate direction (learned replacement for simulation), but for queues.
**Novelty implications:** Simulation-trained surrogates with large speed-ups are established; a V2X cross-layer surrogate remains open.
