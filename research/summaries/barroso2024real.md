# Real-time Hazard Prediction in Connected Autonomous Vehicles: A Digital Twin Approach
Sergio Barroso, Noé Zapata, Gerardo Pérez, Pablo Bustos, Pedro Núñez · 2024 IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iros58592.2024.10802333 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ADAS reacts only in real time; DT could simulate hazards in advance.
**Approach:** DT with internal CARLA simulator inside CORTEX architecture; particle-filter pedestrian trajectory prediction from external cameras.
**Tools and scale:** CARLA, CORTEX; real autonomous EV on campus.
**Results:** Validated on campus tests (no numbers in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Initial model.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — though running a simulator inside the loop to evaluate what-if futures is conceptually related to counterfactual rollouts.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
