# A Quantum-Resilient Sharded Blockchain Framework for Secure V2X and Federated Learning in Intelligent Transportation Systems
Tariq Qayyum, Zouheir Trabelsi, Asadullah Tariq, Mohamed Adel Serhani et al. · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2025.3614888 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** PQ credential management at scale without overloading a single chain.
**Approach:** Sharded blockchain with PQ credentials registered/revoked via PoS; RSUs verify signatures; FL case study.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO/TraCI with 2,000 vehicles and 10 shards.
**Results:** Near real-time delays and high throughput despite PQ overhead.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — PQ credential issuance/revocation simulated with SUMO at 2,000 vehicles; non-SCMS blockchain.
**Novelty implications:** Large-scale PQ credential simulation exists in blockchain form; SCMS-conformant PQ remains.
