# A Counterfactual Safety Margin Perspective on the Scoring of Autonomous Vehicles' Riskiness
A. Zanardi, A. Censi, Margherita Atzei, Luigi Di Lillo, E. Frazzoli · arXiv.org 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.01050 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Autonomous Vehicles (AVs) promise a range of societal advantages, including broader access to mobility, reduced road accidents, and enhanced transportation efficiency.
**Approach:** However, evaluating the risks linked to AVs is complex due to limited historical data and the swift progression of technology. This paper presents a data-driven framework for assessing the risk of different AVs' behaviors in various operational design domains (ODDs), based on counterfactual simulations of"misbehaving"road users.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: simulations.
**Results:** Importantly, we show that our approach is applicable even when the AV's behavioral policy remains undisclosed, through worst- and best-case analyses, benefiting external entities like regulators and risk evaluators.
