# AI-Empowered Trajectory Anomaly Detection and Classification in 6G-V2X
Gunasekaran Raja, Mubeena Begum, Sugeerthi Gurumoorthy, Deepak Suresh Rajendran, Ponnada Srividya, Kapal Dev, Nawab Muhammad Faseeh Qureshi · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2022.3197446 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The immense growth of Autonomous Vehicles (AVs) and networking technologies have paved the way for advanced Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS). AVs increase data demands from in-vehicle users, which pose a significant risk to the vehicular trajectory data and are extremely vulnerable to security threats.
**Approach:** This paper proposes Efficient Trajectory Anomaly Detection and Classification (ETADC) framework in a 6G-V2X environment.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The result analysis shows that the proposed ETADC technique outperforms the existing systems by 97% accuracy.
