# Datanet: Deep Learning Based Encrypted Network Traffic Classification in SDN Home Gateway
Pan Wang, Feng Ye, Xuejiao Chen, Y. Qian · IEEE Access 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2018.2872430 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A smart home network will support various smart devices and applications, e.g., home automation devices, E-health devices, regular computing devices, and so on.
**Approach:** Most devices in a smart home access the Internet through a home gateway (HGW). In this paper, we propose a software-defined-network (SDN)-HGW framework to better manage distributed smart home networks and support the SDN controller of the core network.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Specifically, the proposed SDN-HGW can achieve distributed application awareness by classifying data traffic in a smart home network. The experimental results show that the developed DataNets can be applied to enable distributed application-aware SDN-HGW in future smart home networks.
