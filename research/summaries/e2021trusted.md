# Trusted Authentication using hybrid security algorithm in VANET
Prasanna Venkatesan E, Kristen Titus W · arXiv 2021 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.06105 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) improves traffic management and reduce the amount of road accidents by providing safety applications.
**Approach:** We propose the utilization of Elliptic Curve Cryptography in the design of an efficient data encryption/decryption system for sensor nodes in a wireless network. Sensors will benefit from having to store relatively smaller keys coupled with increased computational capability and this will be a stronger design as the bit-level security is improved.
**Tools and scale:** The implementation of this work is done with NS2 software.
**Results:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) improves traffic management and reduce the amount of road accidents by providing safety applications. The communication between nodes is enhanced, this can reduce 50% of road accidents.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — addresses misbehaviour/attack detection (direction 1 area), but per the abstract without an integrated SCMS backend, closed-loop revocation, per-message joint ground truth or counterfactual reruns.
**Novelty implications:** Does not rule out any candidate direction on the evidence of the abstract. Leaves open quantifying this in a closed loop with real SCMS revocation and traffic safety outcomes.
