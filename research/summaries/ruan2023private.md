# Private Status Retrieval for Blockchain-Based Certificate Revocation System
Zhichao Ruan, Wei Ye, Yankai Xie et al. · ICC 2023 - IEEE International Conference on Communications 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc45041.2023.10278733 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Blockchain is the most promising technology to tackle the security challenges of certificate revocation schemes, such as vulnerability to the single point of failure and lack of accountability systems.
**Approach:** However, current blockchain-based certificate revocation systems suffer from privacy problems as the blockchain nodes can learn which website the client is going to connect with and infer the end-user's private information, such as identity, location, and health condition.
