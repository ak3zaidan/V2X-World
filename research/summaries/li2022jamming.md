# Jamming Detection and Classification in OFDM-Based UAVs via Feature- and Spectrogram-Tailored Machine Learning
Yuchen Li, Jered Pawlak, Joshua Price, Khair A. Al Shamaileh, Quamar Niyaz, Sidike Paheding, Vijay Devabhaktuni · IEEE Access 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2022.3150020 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, a machine learning (ML) approach is proposed to detect and classify jamming attacks against orthogonal frequency division multiplexing (OFDM) receivers with applications to unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs). Using software-defined radio (SDR), four types of jamming attacks; namely, barrage, protocol-aware, single-tone, and successive-pulse are launched and investigated.
**Approach:** In this paper, a machine learning (ML) approach is proposed to detect and classify jamming attacks against orthogonal frequency division multiplexing (OFDM) receivers with applications to unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs).
**Tools and scale:** Using software-defined radio (SDR), four types of jamming attacks; namely, barrage, protocol-aware, single-tone, and successive-pulse are launched and investigated. Then, a systematic testing procedure is established by placing an SDR in the vicinity of a UAV (i.e., drone) to extract radiometric features before and after a jamming attack is launched.
**Results:** Results show that the spectrogram-based model classifies jamming with an accuracy of 99.79% and a false-alarm of 0.03%, in comparison to 92.20% and 1.35%, respectively, with the feature-based counterpart.
