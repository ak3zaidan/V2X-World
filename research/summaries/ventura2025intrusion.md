# Intrusion detection in vehicular networks using machine learning
Heitor Tonel Ventura, Raian de Almeida Moretti, Ana Cristina B. Kochem Vendramin, Daniel Fernando Pigatto · Journal of Internet Services and Applications 2025 · https://doi.org/10.5753/jisa.2025.5017 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular networks and intelligent transport systems play a critical role in modern urban mobility.
**Approach:** In order to improve urban transportation in smart cities, vehicles and fixed stations exchange information about traffic, road conditions, and accidents, allowing better decision-making and ensuring greater safety for the population. However, to provide security, a vehicular network must be resilient to attacks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Anomaly detection models are a potential solution to the reduced effectiveness of signature-based intrusion detection systems, which struggle to detect new attacks due to the absence of previous signatures. However, many models proposed for anomaly detection based on machine learning lack validation and application in vehicular networks, thus lacking evidence of promising results in these specific contexts.
