# ZebraCPA: Decentralized, Postquantum Conditional Privacy-Preserving Authentication for VANETs via Traceable ZK Ring Signatures
Long-Bo Han, Xiaoting Li, Lin You, Gengran Hu et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2026.3667814 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** VANET CPPA struggles to balance unlinkability, tracing, quantum security and fast revocation.
**Approach:** ZebraCPA: lattice traceable ring signatures with ZK proofs, auditor-only tracing trapdoor, threshold tracing, homomorphic key updates, hierarchical blockchain status sync. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Experiments and network-level simulations.
**Results:** 1.7x-7.0x faster signing/verification with anonymity set N=10.

- Not stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — PQ alternative to SCMS with revocation and multi-region sync.
