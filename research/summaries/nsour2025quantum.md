# Quantum-Resilient Secure Onboard Communication (QRSecOC): Integrating Post-Quantum Cryptography for Robust Automotive Network Security
Amjad Nsour · International Journal of Computer Networks and Applications 2025 · https://doi.org/10.22247/ijcna/2025/03 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** In-vehicle CAN security against quantum.
**Approach:** QRSecOC hybrid classical/PQC adaptive protocol.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** 47.3% lower encryption latency than RSA, 23.2% better energy efficiency, 9.8% higher computational overhead, 88.9% throughput improvement.
