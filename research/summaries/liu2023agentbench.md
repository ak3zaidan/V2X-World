# AgentBench: Evaluating LLMs as Agents
Xiao Liu, Hao Yu, Hanchen Zhang, Yi-Fan Xu, Xuanyu Lei, Hanyu Lai et al. · International Conference on Learning Representations 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03688 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The potential of Large Language Model (LLM) as agents has been widely acknowledged recently.
**Approach:** Thus, there is an urgent need to quantitatively \textit{evaluate LLMs as agents} on challenging tasks in interactive environments. We present AgentBench, a multi-dimensional benchmark that consists of 8 distinct environments to assess LLM-as-Agent's reasoning and decision-making abilities.

**Results:** Improving instruction following and training on high quality multi-round alignment data could improve agent performance.
