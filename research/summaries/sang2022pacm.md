# PACM: Privacy-Preserving Authentication Scheme With on-Chain Certificate Management for VANETs
Guohuai Sang, Jingwei Chen, Yiliang Liu, Haiqin Wu, Yong Zheng Zhou, Shunrong Jiang · IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnsm.2022.3201551 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Opaque certificate issuance/revocation and high overhead.
**Approach:** PACM: ECC/XOR mutual authentication, blockchain certificate log, counting garbled Bloom filter.
**Tools and scale:** Security analysis and experiments.
**Results:** Stronger security, less overhead.
