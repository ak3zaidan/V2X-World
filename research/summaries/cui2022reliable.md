# Reliable and Efficient Content Sharing for 5G-Enabled Vehicular Networks
Jie Cui, Jia-Yin Chen, Hong Zhong, Jing Zhang et al. · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2020.3023797 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Conditional privacy preservation and message authentication serve as the primary research issues in terms of security in vehicular networks. With the arrival of 5G era, the downloading speed of network services and the message transmission speed have significantly improved.
**Approach:** To solve this problem, we propose a reliable and efficient content sharing scheme in 5G-enabled vehicular networks.
