# Differentiable and Learnable Wireless Simulation with Geometric Transformers
Thomas Hehn, Markus Peschl, Tribhuvanesh Orekondy, Arash Behboodi et al. · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.14995 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Ray tracers are limited by assumptions and parameter accuracy.
**Approach:** Wi-GATr: equivariant geometric algebra transformer neural surrogate for wireless simulation.
**Tools and scale:** Signal strength, delay spread, localisation, geometry reconstruction tasks; real-world transfer.
**Results:** Accurate, fast, sample-efficient, symmetry-robust.

- Not stated in the abstract.

**Novelty implications:** PHY surrogates established; cross-layer surrogate open.
