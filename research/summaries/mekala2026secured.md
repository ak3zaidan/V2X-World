# A Secured Data Delivery and Validation Model for Information-Centric Vehicular Cloud Network
Sanjeev Kumar Mekala, Satish Anamalamudi, Anil Carie Chettupally, M. Enduri · International journal of mathematical, engineering and management sciences 2026 · https://doi.org/10.33889/ijmems.2026.11.1.020 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Latency and PDR problems in information-centric vehicular cloud data validation.
**Approach:** Caching vehicles, probabilistic verification of transient packets, provider signatures with metadata and an edge Bloom-filter randomised verification protocol (RIVP).
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Not quantified in extracted text.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Scalability and caching costs in highly dynamic settings.
- Future work: large-scale real-world urban/highway tests; integration with 5G/6G edge; multi-cloud.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — probabilistic verification of messages is a verification-policy idea related to direction 2.
**Novelty implications:** Probabilistic verification is known; its safety cost in V2X safety apps remains to be measured.
