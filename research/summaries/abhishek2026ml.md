# ML-ALERTCHAIN: AN ENSEMBLE MACHINE LEARNING AND BLOCKCHAIN-ENABLED FRAMEWORK FOR PREDICTIVE ACCIDENT ALERTING AND TRUSTWORTHY DISSEMINATION IN IOV
Abhishek, Lalit Johari, Sudhanshu Ballabh · ShodhKosh Journal of Visual and Performing Arts 2026 · https://doi.org/10.29121/shodhkosh.v7.i12s.2026.8336 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Internet of Vehicles (IoV) is an important part of smart transportation, but it relies on data supplied by vehicles which may be maliciously manipulated and falsely alarmed for safety. Existing reputation based frameworks like BRAVE-IOV are mainly reactive and concentrate on the past behavior of nodes rather than proactively predicting hazards.
**Approach:** Existing reputation based frameworks like BRAVE-IOV are mainly reactive and concentrate on the past behavior of nodes rather than proactively predicting hazards.
**Tools and scale:** Experimental assessments with SUMO and Python TraCI show that the proposed system obtains a prediction accuracy of 94.2% which is much better than single model baselines.
**Results:** Experimental assessments with SUMO and Python TraCI show that the proposed system obtains a prediction accuracy of 94.2% which is much better than single model baselines. Moreover, the framework achieves a low dissemination latency of less than 45ms and filters out 98% of false alarm injections even when 30% of the network is hostile.
