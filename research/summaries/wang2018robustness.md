# Towards Robustness: Machine Learning for MmWave V2X with Situational Awareness
Yuyang Wang, Murali Narasimha, Robert W. Heath · 2018 52nd Asilomar Conference on Signals, Systems, and Computers 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/acssc.2018.8645370 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** High mobility poses challenges to millimeter wave (mmWave) vehicular beam tracking.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose to recommend a set of candidate beam pairs using machine learning classification and ranking, based on the target vehicle’s situational awareness. We observe that the optimal beam directions can be captured by the location of the receiver and the sizes and locations of the neighboring vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** To further reduce overhead, we leverage a new beam search mechanism, where the search can automatically terminate when the current beam measurement exceeds a specific target power. The numerical results show that the beam search overhead can be reduced by 70% when seven beams are recommended, at a comparable beam alignment probability.
