# Data Mining Intrusion Detection in Vehicular Ad Hoc Network
Xiaoyun LIU, Gongjun YAN, Danda B. RAWAT, Shugang DENG · IEICE Transactions on Information and Systems 2014 · https://doi.org/10.1587/transinf.e97.d.1719 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The past decade has witnessed a growing interest in vehicular networking. Initially motivated by traffic safety, vehicles equipped with computing, communication and sensing capabilities will be organized into ubiquitous and pervasive networks with a significant Internet presence while on the move.
**Approach:** We are the first to attempt to adapt data mining method for intrusion detection in vehicular networks. The main contributions include: 1) specially design a decentralized vehicle networks that provide scalable communication and data availability about network status; 2) applying two data mining models to show feasibility of automated intrusion detection system in vehicular networks; 3) find the detection patterns of unknown intrusions.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset (mentioned in abstract)
