# Consensus-based Attack Detection and Cooperative Perception of Connected Vehicles
Muzaffer Citir, Sangyoung Park · 2024 IEEE 100th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2024-Fall) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2024-fall63153.2024.10757806 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Manipulated CPM data.
**Approach:** Consensus-based detection using state covariances.
**Tools and scale:** Three-vehicle scenario.
**Results:** 67.57% of 37 attacks; 13.5% higher TPR than baseline.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Only multi-observed objects.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — CPM MBD.
**Novelty implications:** None.
