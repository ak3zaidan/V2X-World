# Building Trust in Autonomous Vehicles: Role of Virtual Reality Driving Simulators in HMI Design
Lia Morra, Fabrizio Lamberti, F. Gabriele Pratticó, Salvatore La Rosa, Paolo Montuschi · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, 68(10), pp.9438-9450, 2019 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2019.2933601 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Trust in AVs depends on HMI.
**Approach:** VR driving simulation with physiological signals (GSR) to evaluate HUD interfaces.
**Tools and scale:** VR simulator.
**Results:** Complete surrounding picture leads to less stress despite higher cognitive load; users more willing to try real AV.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- GSR measures arousal not valence
- add HR sensors
- extend to whole-experience features
- may not apply to performance phase.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
