# Enhancing Security in 5G Vehicular Networks Through Machine Learning-Based Attack Detection
Aarzoo Verma, S. G. Bhol, P. K. Pattnaik, Bishnupriya Panda · 2024 IEEE 4th International Conference on Applied Electromagnetics, Signal Processing, & Communication (AESPC) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/aespc63931.2024.10872181 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The vehicular networks along with 5G technology is promising and has a potential to revolutionize transportation sector, making it both smarter and safer.
**Approach:** By analyzing data from connected vehicles, ML can improve traffic flow, predict accidents, and optimize route planning. However, challenges such as accurately predicting vehicle movements, managing computational and network resources, and ensuring data security in the cloud persist.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G
**Results:** Edge computing improves the performance , as data is being processed closer to its source, enabling real-time decision-making. This integrated strategy aims to refine classification algorithms used in traffic management, resulting in more accurate predictions and efficient resource use.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, challenges such as accurately predicting vehicle movements, managing computational and network resources, and ensuring data security in the cloud persist.
- The ultimate goal is to develop a system that not only improves traffic flow and reduces accidents but also ensures data security and optimal resource management, paving the way for the future of smart mobility.
