# Federated Learning for Misbehaviour Detection with Variational Autoencoders and Gaussian Mixture Models
Enrique Mármol Campos, Aurora González Vidal, José Luis Hernández Ramos, Antonio Skarmeta · International Journal of Information Security 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s10207-025-01000-8 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Supervised FL MBD needs labels and misses unknown threats.
**Approach:** Unsupervised FL with GMM and VAE, RBM pre-training, Fed+ aggregation for non-IID data.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi.
**Results:** More than 80% performance, better than recent supervised proposals.
