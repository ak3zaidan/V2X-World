# AI-Driven Multi-Dimensional Trust Framework for Securing VANETs against Blackhole and Greyhole Attacks
C. Balakumar, N. Shanmugapriya · International Conference on Computing, Communication and Automation 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccca66364.2025.11325559 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks (VANETs) constitute the foundation of intelligent transportation systems, facilitating the sensing, transmission, and sharing of real-time information among cars and between vehicles and roadside infrastructure. **Approach:** This study introduces TRIDENT, a trust-based intelligent detection system aimed at addressing blackhole and greyhole assaults in VANETs. This research validates that TRIDENT is an intelligent WSN-based framework designed to mitigate routing attacks in VANET, demonstrating effective scalability.

**Results:** The simulation findings indicate that TRIDENT surpasses earlier trust-based and cryptographic methods in precision, accuracy, recall, F1-score, and PDR, demonstrating significant differences.
