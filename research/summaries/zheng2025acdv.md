# ACDV: Adaptive Content Delivery for Vehicular Digital Twin Networks
Jinkai Zheng, Tom H. Luan, Guanjie Li, Zhisheng Yin, Yuan Wu, Mianxiong Dong · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2024.3517657 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Digital Twins (DTs), serving personalized cloud-based digital assistants, hold great promise for supporting infotainment applications of the Internet of Vehicles (IoVs), in which personalized content such as high-definition maps, system updates, and social video streaming can be delivered from individualized DTs to vehicular users (VUEs) to enhance the intelligence of IoV services and improve their driving experience.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a novel content caching framework tailored to DT-enabled IoVs, where DTs selectively cache on-demand contents on edge devices (i.e., cellular base stations and roadside units) managed by the edge service manager (ESM) along the VUE's trip to avoid backbone congestion yet save download time. However, ESMs are inherently selfish and reluctant to contribute resources to cache contents without benefits.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Through extensive simulations, we show that our proposal can effectively find the optimal strategy and achieve a fast convergence speed and high-level performance compared to the baselines.
