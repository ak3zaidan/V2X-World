# DUEL: Dempster Uncertainty-Based Enhanced- Trust Level Scheme for VANET
Arpita Bhargava, Shekhar Verma · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2021.3136548 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This work presents an uncertainty-based Trust Model (TM). It uses Dempster Shafer Theory (DST) to cater to uncertainty arising due to information scarcity in Vehicular Adhoc Networks (VANETs).
**Approach:** This work presents an uncertainty-based Trust Model (TM). Additionally, we present trust contexts based on types of messages altered or dropped by them.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Results show that DUEL can capture both false message attacks and message suppression attacks even in rapidly changing neighbourhoods and performs well in terms of high values of TPR and low values of FPR.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Trust establishment assumes that vehicle behaviour is predictable, and its past transactions are useful for anticipating its future response.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Trust/reputation management for detecting malicious senders; an alternative to the SCMS misbehaviour-report -> MA -> CRL pipeline our engine models, with no linkage-based revocation or time-to-neutralise analysis.
**Novelty implications:** Does not rule out any candidate direction; it does not model SCMS certificates, linkage-based revocation, per-message joint ground truth or counterfactual reruns.
