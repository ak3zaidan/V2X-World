# Privacy-Aware Secure Routing through Elliptical Curve Cryptography with Optimal RSU Distribution in VANETs
Ghadeer Ghazi Shayea, Dheyaa Abdulameer Mohammed, A. Abbas, N. F. Abdulsattar · Designs 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/designs6060121 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Overhead and security in VANET routing.
**Approach:** ECC-GA-PASR: GA-optimised RSU placement plus ECC authentication.
**Tools and scale:** NS-2.35, SUMO; 500 vehicles, 50 malicious; random mobility.
**Results:** Efficiency up to 95% vs vehicle count; some results moderate.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Some results 'moderate and need improvement'.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
