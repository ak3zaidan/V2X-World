# Efficient Group Authentication Protocol for Secure 5G Enabled Vehicular Communications
V. Nyangaresi, A. Rodrigues, Silvance O. Abeka · International Computer Engineering Conference 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icenco49778.2020.9357372 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Although a lot of research efforts have been directed towards addressing 5G technical issues for the support of V2X communications, these researches only concentrate on synchronization, physical layer structure or resource allocation.
**Approach:** To address these challenges, this paper sought to develop an efficient and secure protocol that was shown to have average communication and computational costs and also robust against impersonation, MitM, replay and eavesdropping attacks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** Very little research has been carried out on how to uphold message security as well as user privacy in V2X networks and as such, these networks still face a number challenges relating to privacy and secure payload exchanges. Moreover, although novel access validation and key agreement schemes have been suggested, secure and efficient mobility management still faces many challenges owing to the high number of recurrent handovers and massive vehicular communications.

- None stated in the abstract.
