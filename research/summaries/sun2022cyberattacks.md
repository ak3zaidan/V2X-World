# Cyberattacks on connected automated vehicles: A traffic impact analysis
Zhanbo Sun, Runzhe Liu, Haitao Hu, Dengyue Liu, Zhiqi Yan · IET Intelligent Transport Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1049/itr2.12259 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Prior work focuses on detection; traffic impact of CAV cyberattacks is unquantified.
**Approach:** Microsimulation of time-delay and disturbance attacks on freeway segments and unsignalised intersections, evaluated with TTC, DRAC, speed and flow-density.
**Tools and scale:** Traffic microsimulation (no network stack described).
**Results:** Both attacks raise collision risk and severity; location-based delay causes deceleration, congestion and throughput loss; disturbance causes uneven density; worse in heavy traffic; increased right-angle collision risk at intersections.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — safety surrogate measures under attack; no radio, PKI or detection.
**Novelty implications:** Attack-to-SSM quantification exists; adding detection/revocation timelines is open.
