# Fed-IoVIDS: Intrusion Detection based on Attack Behavior Analysis with Temporal Model on IoV Considering Privacy Protection
Rui Chen, Jing Zhao · 2024 International Joint Conference on Neural Networks (IJCNN) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ijcnn60899.2024.10650781 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With advancements in distributed communications for the IoV, security threats expose significant challenges.
**Approach:** While current IoV intrusion detection systems demonstrate high accuracy, they rely heavily on private or easily forged data. Moreover, the training process incurs increased network communication costs, fails to protect user privacy, and distorted data degrades detection performance.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Finally, we use a federated learning algorithm for data processing and training of the model, effectively reduce communication burden and protect privacy training data on the vehicle-side. Extensive experiments on two datasets validate the proposed model, achieving F1 scores of 91.66% and 90.25% respectively, outperforming state-of-theart methods.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- With advancements in distributed communications for the IoV, security threats expose significant challenges.
- To address these limitations, we proposes a distributed federated learning-based intrusion detection model for IoV using non-private behavior features.
