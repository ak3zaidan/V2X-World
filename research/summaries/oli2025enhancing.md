# Enhancing Data Integrity and Confidentiality Preservation in VANET using a Trusted Authority Void Scheme
Jayashree M. Oli, N. Neelima · 2025 IEEE 2nd International Conference on Information Technology, Electronics and Intelligent Communication Systems (ICITEICS) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iciteics64870.2025.11341665 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-Hoc Network (VANET) has become an important area of research due to diverse field of applications it can provide.
**Approach:** In this paper we concentrate mainly on the security challenges posed to a VANE T system and a model that could help the system from accepting wrong messages, that is messages that are altered or messages that are not sent by an authentic node. The proposed model address two major issues through a three-phase algorithm.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The first phase deals with the issue of reduction of overhead communication bits to help in the reduction of the bandwidth used.
