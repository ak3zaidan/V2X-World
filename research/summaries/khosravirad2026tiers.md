# A Tiers-of-Knowledge Framework for 6G Digital Twinning
Saeed R. Khosravirad, I. Van de Voorde, H. Viswanathan et al. · IEEE Open Journal of the Communications Society 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ojcoms.2026.3735905 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Radio access networks (RAN) are facing a major transformation driven by the need for site-specific automation and customization in the 6G era.
**Approach:** We study the operational and planning impact of this transformation on RAN and propose a novel Tiers of Knowledge framework, classifying network prior knowledge from Tier 0 (no prior knowledge) to Tier 4 (site-specific predictive knowledge) and a theoretical Oracle bound. Within this framework, we study the benefits of digital twins in critical use cases such as handover optimization, beam management, and positioning.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** We complement this taxonomy with a mathematical grounding that casts each tier as a Bayesian estimation problem with progressively richer side information, and with system-level quantification of digital twin-enabled improvements to example RAN functions.
