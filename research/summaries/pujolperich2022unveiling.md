# Unveiling the potential of Graph Neural Networks for robust Intrusion Detection
David Pujol-Perich, Jose Suarez-Varela, Albert Cabellos-Aparicio, Pere Barlet-Ros · ACM SIGMETRICS Performance Evaluation Review 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3543146.3543171 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The last few years have seen an increasing wave of attacks with serious economic and privacy damages, which evinces the need for accurate Network Intrusion Detection Systems (NIDS).
**Approach:** Recent works propose the use of Machine Learning (ML) techniques for building such systems (e.g., decision trees, neural networks). However, existing ML-based NIDS are barely robust to common adversarial attacks, which limits their applicability to real networks.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: dataset; scale not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** In our evaluation, we first show that the proposed GNN model achieves state-of-the-art results in the well-known CIC-IDS2017 dataset. The results show that our model is able to maintain the same level of accuracy as in previous experiments, while state-of-the-art ML techniques degrade up to 50% their accuracy (F1-score) under these attacks.
