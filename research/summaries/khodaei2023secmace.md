# SECMACE+: Upscaling Pseudonymous Authentication for Large Mobile Systems
Mohammad Khodaei, Hamid Noroozi, Panos Papadimitratos · IEEE Transactions on Cloud Computing 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tcc.2023.3250584 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** VPKI must be highly available and scalable for large multi-domain systems while preventing Sybil misuse.
**Approach:** SECMACE+ cloud-based, dynamically scalable, resilient VPKI.
**Tools and scale:** Full implementation on Google Cloud Platform.
**Results:** About 56 ms to issue 100 pseudonyms; cost-effective large-scale deployment.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — Full VPKI backend implementation with pseudonym issuance at scale, overlapping our SCMS backend (though C-ITS/SECMACE design, not US SCMS, and no traffic/radio coupling).
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first scalable full VPKI implementation; can claim coupling with traffic/radio and US SCMS fidelity if no other paper does.
