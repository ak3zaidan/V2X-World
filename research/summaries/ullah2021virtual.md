# Virtual Pseudonym-Changing and Dynamic Grouping Policy for Privacy Preservation in VANETs
Ikram Ullah, Munam Ali Shah, Abid Khan, Carsten Maple et al. · Sensors 2021 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s21093077 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Short silent periods and visible neighbours enable tracking on highways.
**Approach:** Virtual pseudonym changing with dynamic grouping (DGVP).
**Tools and scale:** SUMO on OpenStreetMap; entropy metrics.
**Results:** Higher mean maximum entropy than CPS and CLPS.
