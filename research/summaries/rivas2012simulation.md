# Simulation of points of interest distribution in vehicular networks
David Antolino Rivas, Manel Guerrero-Zapata · SIMULATION 2012 · https://doi.org/10.1177/0037549712456440 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Over the last few years Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs) have become a major research topic.
**Approach:** Security mechanisms such as Public Key Infrastructure have been customized to provide privacy, authentication, integrity and non-repudiation to vehicle communications. Once the security foundations were established, different applications were built on top: intelligent driving systems, parking spot finders, peer-to-peer content, advertisements distribution, etc.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting. The available text does not describe a V2X simulator, radio stack, traffic model or SCMS-style PKI in enough detail to report; scale not stated.
**Results:** In order to verify the feasibility of those applications in the VANET environment, simulation tools such as ns-2 or Glomosim are used, basing their mobility model on non-uniform distributions. This article discusses how, by using customized application simulators, we can obtain more realistic results than by using general network simulators such as ns-2.
