# Artificial Intelligence Powered and Blockchain Based Certificateless Authentication Internet of Vehicles
Ayman Yafoz, Raed A. I. Alsini · SSRN Electronic Journal 2026 · https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7592689 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Internet of Vehicles (IoV) is a key component of intelligent transport systems, supportingroad safety, traffic-congestion management, and self-driving vehicles. However, vehicular communication networks are open wireless environments vulnerable to impersonation, replay, man-in-the-middle, Sybil, and insider attacks.
