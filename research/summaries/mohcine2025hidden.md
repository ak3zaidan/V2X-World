# The Hidden Threat: Analyzing Trust Manipulation Attacks in Trust Management Systems for Vehicular Networks
Baalla Mohcine, Driss Bouzidi · International Conference on Artificial Intelligence, Computer, Data Sciences and Applications (ACDSA) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/acdsa65407.2025.11166636 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** When it comes to securing networks, Trust Management Systems (TMS) are still an important and significant solution in providing a secure network by identifying and mitigating malicious behaviors.
**Approach:** However, these systems remain vulnerable to sophisticated or smarter Trust Manipulation Attacks (TMA), where there are adversaries that exploit trust evaluation mechanisms to evade detection. One of the most security-critical network environments are vehicular networks, including Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) and the Internet of Vehicles (IoV), where trust is very major.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** By looking closely at these attack methods, we show how they affect systems that rely on trust to stay secure, and how they can trick common detection approaches. This work shows why it's important to build better trust systems that can spot and respond to these changing threats.
