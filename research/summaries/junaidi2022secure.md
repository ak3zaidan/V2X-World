# Secure Vehicular Platoon Management against Sybil Attacks
Danial Ritzuan Junaidi, Maode Ma, Rong Su · Sensors 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s22229000 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Sybil attacks admitting fake vehicles into platoons.
**Approach:** ECC-based key exchange, signatures and encryption for platoon events with forward/backward secrecy; CK model, random oracle and CryptoVerif.
**Tools and scale:** Formal analysis and performance evaluation.
**Results:** Time and space efficient.
