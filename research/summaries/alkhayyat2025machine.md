# Machine Learning-Based Angle-Of-Arrival Estimation for Enhanced V2V Communication in Autonomous Navigation and Smart Cities
Mohammed Al-Khayyat, Mohammed Al-Hubaishi · 2025 5th International Conference on Pervasive Computing and Social Networking (ICPCSN) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icpcsn65854.2025.11035752 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** There are many applications for angle of arrival (AoA) estimations in car-to-car communications, and in this paper, machine learning methods are used to improve the accuracy of 21 AoA estimations.
**Approach:** Several machine learning algorithms, namely Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, LightGBM, and Support Vector Regression, are applied to AoA estimations. The best result was obtained by Gradient Boosting, with an R2 score of 0.9496 on freeways.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The most important features were distance, frequency, and interference, as these features have a significant impact on the accuracy of AoA estimations; therefore, they are used to improve the accuracy of AoA estimations. The proposed machine learning method reduces the root mean square error (RMSE) to less than 5° on freeways and less than 10° in other scenarios, thus allowing it to be used for real-time applications.
