# On-Premises–Edge–Cloud Traffic Analytics with a Temporal Causative Graph for Incident Understanding
I. Gusti Agung Vivekananda, Fadhil Hidayat · International Conferences on Information Science and System 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iciss66954.2025.11389603 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Timely explainable alerts in traffic operations.
**Approach:** Edge detection plus temporal causative graph and selective VLM offload.
**Tools and scale:** 10 camera streams; 1,146 incidents.
**Results:** Median 29.3 s latency; VLM cost -96.3%; root-vehicle F1 0.771; hallucination 17.7% to 9.7%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Internal evaluation only.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
