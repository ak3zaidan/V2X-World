# COACH: COllaborative certificate stAtus CHecking mechanism for VANETs
Jose L. Muñoz, Juan J. Alins-Delgado, Jorge Mata-Díaz, Juan Hernández-Serrano, Oscar Esparza, Carlos Gañán · J. Netw. Comput. Appl. 2013 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnca.2012.02.006 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Distributing revocation status quickly.
**Approach:** COACH: hash tree embedded in CRLs (extended-CRL) for short authenticated responses; EvCOACH with hash chains for low revocation rates.
**Tools and scale:** Analysis.
**Results:** Responses under 1 kB fitting one UDP message.

- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - revocation status distribution.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
