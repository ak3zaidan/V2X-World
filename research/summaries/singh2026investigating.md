# Investigating the effectiveness of IS-DOS prevention mechanisms for mitigating DOS attacks in VANET
Harsh Pratap Singh, Nagendra Singh, Sai Kiran Naik Banoth, Vedika Patil et al. · Sigma Journal of Engineering and Natural Sciences 2026 · https://doi.org/10.14744/sigma.2026.2020 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** DoS flooding attacks in VANET AODV.
**Approach:** IS-DOS intrusion detection from traffic-pattern analysis.
**Tools and scale:** NS-2.34, 30 nodes in 1100 x 1100 m, random mobility.
**Results:** PDR +15%, throughput +12%, delay -20%, routing overhead -18% vs DOS and QRT-DOS approaches.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not explicitly stated; small random-mobility scenario.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — routing DoS.
