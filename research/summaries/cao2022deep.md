# Deep Learning in Network-Level Performance Prediction Using Cross-Layer Information
Qi Cao, Man-On Pun, Yi Chen · IEEE Transactions on Network Science and Engineering 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnse.2022.3163274 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Network-level metrics are hard to compute model-based.
**Approach:** Customised DNNs on cross-layer PHY/MAC information with weighted co-teaching for noisy labels.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Accurate prediction of user average throughput and ACK/NACK.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - cross-layer learned network performance model.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
