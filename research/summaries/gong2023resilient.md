# Resilient Controller Synthesis Against DoS Attacks for Vehicular Platooning in Spatial Domain
Jian Gong, Carlos Murguia, Anggera Bayuwindra, Jinde Cao · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2025.3530963 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** DoS-induced delays raise collision risk in platoons.
**Approach:** Resilient controller via polytopic overapproximation, L2 string stability, spatial domain.
**Tools and scale:** Numerical simulation, eight vehicles.
**Results:** Tracking and spacing maintained under bounded DoS.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Hard upper bound on attack duration assumed.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — Control theory.
**Novelty implications:** None.
