# Efficient pseudonym changing schemes for location privacy protection in VANETs
Yeong-Sheng Chen, Tang-Te Lo, Chiu-Hua Lee, Ai-Chun Pang · 2013 International Conference on Connected Vehicles and Expo (ICCVE) 2013 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccve.2013.6799933 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Too many pseudonym changes disrupt services and routing.
**Approach:** Criteria combining pseudonym age, speed and direction (AS, AD, SD, ADS).
**Tools and scale:** SUMO simulation.
**Results:** ADS gives high protection with fewer changes.

- None stated in the abstract.
