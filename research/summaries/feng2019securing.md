# Securing Traffic-Related Messages Exchange Against Inside-and-Outside Collusive Attack in Vehicular Networks
Jing-Yu Feng, Nan Liu, Jie Cao, Yuqing Zhang et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2019.2933632 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Traffic-related messages exchange (TME) is considered as a powerful approach to improve traffic safety and efficiency in vehicular networks. However, TME assumes all vehicles always are honest, and thus offering opportunities for attackers to fake traffic-related messages.
**Approach:** In this article, a vulnerability for trust mechanism is found, that is, the ratings from initiator vehicles (IVs) are generally unchecked. We conduct an in-depth investigation on IOC attack and propose a defense scheme called TFAA from the design ideas of trust fluctuation association analysis.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset, blockchain (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** The simulation results show that the TFAA scheme can enhance the accuracy of trust value evaluation, and thus successfully reducing the power of IOC attack against TME.
