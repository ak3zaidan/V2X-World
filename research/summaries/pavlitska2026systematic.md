# Towards a Systematic Risk Assessment of Deep Neural Network Limitations in Autonomous Driving Perception
Svetlana Pavlitska, Christopher Gerking, J. Marius Zöllner · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20895 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Safety and security are essential for the admission and acceptance of automated and autonomous vehicles.
**Approach:** Deep neural networks (DNNs) are widely used for perception and further components of the autonomous driving (AD) stack. However, they possess several limitations, including lack of generalization, efficiency, explainability, plausibility, and robustness.
