# Token Communication in the Era of Large Models: An Information Bottleneck-Based Approach
Hao Wei, Wanli Ni, Wen Wang et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.01728 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This letter proposes UniToCom, a unified token communication paradigm that treats tokens as the fundamental units for both processing and wireless transmission.
**Approach:** Specifically, to enable efficient token representations, we propose a generative information bottleneck (GenIB) principle, which facilitates the learning of tokens that preserve essential information while supporting reliable generation across multiple modalities. Additionally, we develop $σ$-GenIB to address the challenges of variance collapse in autoregressive modeling, maintaining representational diversity and stability.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** By doing this, GenIB-based tokenization is conducive to improving the communication efficiency and reducing computational complexity. Simulation results validate the effectiveness and superiority of the proposed UniToCom compared to baselines under dynamic channel conditions.
