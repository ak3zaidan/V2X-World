# LLM-RIMSA: Large Language Models driven Reconfigurable Intelligent Metasurface Antenna Systems
Yunsong Huang, Hui-Ming Wang, Qingli Yan et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12728 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The evolution of 6G networks demands ultra-massive connectivity and intelligent radio environments, yet existing reconfigurable intelligent surface (RIS) technologies face critical limitations in hardware efficiency, dynamic control, and scalability.
**Approach:** This paper introduces LLM-RIMSA, a transformative framework that integrates large language models (LLMs) with a novel reconfigurable intelligent metasurface antenna (RIMSA) architecture to address these challenges. The proposed framework pave the way for LLM-driven intelligent radio environments.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: deep learning.
**Results:** Simulations demonstrate that LLM-RIMSA achieves state-of-the-art performance, outperforming conventional DL-based methods in sum rate while reducing training overhead.
