# Bottom-Up Design of Digital Twins for Optical Transport Networks: Challenges and Lessons Learned [Invited]
Renato Cherini, Nehuen Gonzalez-Montoro, Luis Compagnucci, J. Finochietto · International Conference on Optical Network Design and Modelling 2024 · https://doi.org/10.23919/ondm61578.2024.10582648 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** We discuss the design and development of a digital twin for optical transport networks, focusing on a bottom-up approach aligned with network operators' specific needs.
**Approach:** Our methodology was carved by prioritizing operator requirements, aiming to enhance the management and analysis of optical networks. Having a digital twin enabled gathering telemetry data from the network to assess its health and support preventive maintenance.
