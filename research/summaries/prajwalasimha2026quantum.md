# A quantum resistant chaos driven image encryption framework for secure visual data transmission in intelligent transportation systems
S. N. Prajwalasimha, Anurag Sharma, D. J. Saini, Bipin Kumar Rai et al. · Scientific Reports 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-57548-5 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** ITS image data needs quantum-resistant protection.
**Approach:** Chaos-based bit-level image encryption with Kyber-768 or QKD key negotiation.
**Tools and scale:** Python on i7; Lena, Cameraman, Peppers, Cityscapes images.
**Results:** NPCR > 99.60%, UACI about 33.5%, entropy about 8.0, key space > 2^256.
