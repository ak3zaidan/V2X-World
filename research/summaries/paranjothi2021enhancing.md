# Enhancing Security in VANETs with Efficient Sybil Attack Detection using Fog Computing
Anirudh Paranjothi, Mohammed Atiquzzaman · arXiv (Cornell University) 2021 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.10319 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Sybil attacks create fake congestion; detection has high delay, overhead and FPR.
**Approach:** FSDV: dynamic fog of OBUs for rogue node detection.
**Tools and scale:** OMNeT++ and SUMO.
**Results:** 43% lower processing delay, 13% lower overhead, 35% lower FPR at high densities.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Sybil detection in Veins-type co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** None.
