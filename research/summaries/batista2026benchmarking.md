# Benchmarking Wireless Representations: High-Dimensional vs. Compressed Embeddings for Efficiency and Robustness
Murilo Batista, Shirin Salehi, Saeed Mashdour, Paul Zheng et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02009 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cost-benefit of high-dimensional wireless embeddings.
**Approach:** Benchmark of foundation-model embeddings, autoencoder latents and raw data.
**Tools and scale:** LoS/NLoS, beam selection, power allocation.
**Results:** High-dimensional embeddings help few-shot but cost latency; compressed latents more robust (truncated).
