# Energy Management Strategy Based on V2X Communications and Road Information for a Connected PHEV and Its Evaluation Using an IDHIL Simulator
Seongmin Ha, Hyeongcheol Lee · Applied Sciences 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/app13169208 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** HEV energy management uses only in-vehicle information.
**Approach:** EMS combining cooperative eco-driving guidance and adaptive ECMS using V2X and road information; validated on IDHIL simulator.
**Tools and scale:** IDHIL: V2X device, VANET simulator, vehicle simulator.
**Results:** Reduced pedal usage and more efficient engine operating points (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Apply detailed real-time road information
- trajectory-based SOC reference
- evaluate other EMS methods in real V2X environments.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
