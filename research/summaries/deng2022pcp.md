# PCP: A Pseudonym Change Scheme for Location Privacy Preserving in VANETs
Xinyang Deng, Tianhan Gao, Nan Guo, Cong Zhao et al. · Entropy 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/e24050648 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Adaptive pseudonym change under varied conditions.
**Approach:** PCP: scenario-dependent change strategies with registration, authentication, issuance and revocation protocols; improved ID-based linearly homomorphic signature.
**Tools and scale:** Security and performance analysis.
**Results:** Resists global passive adversaries; better than traditional schemes.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Efficient signature mechanism for anonymous communication.
- How many pseudonyms a vehicle should store to balance security and performance.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — pseudonym pool sizing relates to our provisioning settings.
**Novelty implications:** Pool-size trade-off is open in their words; our engine can measure it.
