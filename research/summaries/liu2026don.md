# Don't Let One Lie Survive A Hundred Truths: A Selective Bayesian Trust Estimator for Collaborative Perception
Yutong Liu, Chenyi Wang, Ming F. Li et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07875 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Collaborative perception (CP) enables connected vehicles to see beyond their own sensors but makes them dependent on messages they cannot independently verify.
**Approach:** To address this problem, we propose SABER, a selective two-tier Bayesian trust estimator.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** We establish sufficient conditions for stronger attacker-side trust reductions with bounded additional benign false alarms at fixed thresholds. Compared with state-of-the-art CP defenses, SABER improves attack detection while reducing benign FPRs.
