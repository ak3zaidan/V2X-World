# Deep Learning-Based Intrusion Detection System for Internet of Vehicles
Imran Ahmed, Gwanggil Jeon, Awais Ahmad · IEEE Consumer Electronics Magazine 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mce.2021.3139170 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The growth of the Internet of Things (IoT) has resulted in several revolutionary applications, such as smart cities, cyber-physical systems, and the Internet of vehicles (IoV). Within the IoV infrastructure, vehicles are comprised of various electronic intelligent sensors or devices used to obtain data and communicate the necessary information with their surroundings.
**Approach:** One of the major concerns about the implementation of these sensors or devices is data vulnerability; thus, it is necessary to present a solution that provides security, trust, and privacy to communicating entities and to secure vehicle data from malicious entities. In modern vehicles, the controller area network (CAN) is a fundamental scheme for controlling the interaction among different in-vehicle network sensors.
**Tools and scale:** The experiments are performed using the CAN-intrusion-dataset.
**Results:** The overall accuracy of the system is 96% with FPR of 0.6%.
