# Large AI Models for Wireless Physical Layer
Jia-Jia Guo, Yi-Ming Cui, Shi Jin, Jun Zhang · IEEE Communications Magazine 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mcom.001.2500505 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Large artificial intelligence models (LAMs) are transforming wireless physical layer technologies through their robust generalization, multitask processing, and multimodal capabilities.
**Approach:** This article reviews recent advancements in applying LAMs to physical layer communications, addressing obstacles of conventional AI-based approaches. LAM-based solutions are classified into two strategies: leveraging pretrained LAMs and developing native LAMs designed specifically for physical layer tasks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Both strategies significantly improve performance and adaptability across diverse wireless scenarios.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future research directions, including efficient architectures, interpretability, standardized datasets, and collaboration between large and small models, are proposed to advance LAM-based physical layer solutions for next-generation communication systems.
