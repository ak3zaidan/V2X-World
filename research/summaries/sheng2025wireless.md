# A Wireless Foundation Model for Multi-Task Prediction
Yucheng Sheng, Jiacheng Wang, Xingyu Zhou, Le Liang et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05938 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** DL predictors of CSI, location and traffic generalise poorly.
**Approach:** Unified causal-Transformer foundation model with univariate decomposition and patch masking.
**Tools and scale:** Large-scale datasets.
**Results:** Strong generalisation and zero-shot on new tasks.
