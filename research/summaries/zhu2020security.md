**Problem:** C-V2X extended from cellular networks has been considered one of the promising communication techniques for Internet of Vehicles (IoV).
**Approach:** Correspondingly, it inherits the security issues from cellular networks, i.e., LTE or 5G, and also bring new types of security issues. In this paper, we take LTE-V2X as an example to investigate the potential security and privacy issues of LTE-V2X.
