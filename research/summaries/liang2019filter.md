# A Filter Model Based on Hidden Generalized Mixture Transition Distribution Model for Intrusion Detection System in Vehicle Ad Hoc Networks
Junwei Liang, Qiuzhen Lin, Jianyong Chen, Yingying Zhu · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2019.2905415 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle ad hoc networks (VANETs) are considered to be the next big thing that will remarkably change our lives, since this kind of technology is able to make our lives and roads safer. Due to the very fast move and high dynamic in VANETs, it is important to quickly ascertain the reliability of information.
**Approach:** Thus, in this paper, we propose a novel filter model based on a hidden generalized mixture transition distribution model (HgMTD) in VANETs, called FM-HgMTD, which can quickly filter the messages from neighboring vehicles so as to reduce the overhead and detection time.
