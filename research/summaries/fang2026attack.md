# Attack Chains Construction for Vehicular Networks Based on a Two‐Dimensional Vulnerability Combination Strategy
Xiyu Fang, Xueqing Yao, Waleed Younas et al. · International Journal of Communication Systems 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1002/dac.70405 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ABSTRACT Vehicular networks are central to intelligent transportation systems, yet their expanding attack surface yields complex multivulnerability paths that exceed traditional detection capabilities.
**Approach:** This article presents a high‐coverage attack‐chain generation framework that models, clusters, and composes vulnerabilities with reduced redundancy.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** A semantically enhanced K‐Modes method integrates semantic similarity with Hamming distance to improve categorization. Experiments on communication, system, and control‐layer vulnerabilities achieve over 70% coverage and 85% accuracy, outperforming baselines.
