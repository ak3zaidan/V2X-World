# ASATM: Automated security assistant of threat models in intelligent transportation systems
Mohammad Ali Ramazanzadeh, Behnam Barzegar, Homayun Motameni · IET Computers & Digital Techniques 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1049/cdt2.12045 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The evolution of technology has led to the appearance of smart cities. An essential element in such cities is smart mobility that covers the subjects related to Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS).
**Approach:** To reduce threats and secure software design, threat modelling has been proposed as a preventive solution in the software design phase. The approach proposed in this study, that is, Automated Security Assistant of Threat Models (ASATM), is an automated solution that is capable of achieving a high level of security assurance.
**Tools and scale:** Non-vehicular setting. Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
