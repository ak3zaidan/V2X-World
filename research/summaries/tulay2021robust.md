# Robust Sybil Attack Detection in Vehicular Networks
Halit Bugra Tulay, Can Emre Koksal · 2021 IEEE 94th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2021-Fall) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2021-fall52928.2021.9625060 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil attacks with multiple illegitimate identities.
**Approach:** Non-cryptographic detection from spatial variation of signal-level wireless measurements.
**Tools and scale:** Simulations plus real DSRC experiments.
**Results:** 95% detection in simulation, 99% in real-world experiments.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — PHY-based Sybil detection; our engine has per-message radio ground truth that could evaluate such detectors.
**Novelty implications:** PHY-layer Sybil detection with real DSRC exists; cannot claim first.
