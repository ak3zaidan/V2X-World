# Cloud-in-the-Loop simulation of C-V2X application relocation distortions in Kubernetes based Edge Cloud environment
Levente Maller, Peter Suskovics, Laszlo Bokor · 2022 26th International Conference on Information Technology (IT) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/it54280.2022.9743520 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cloud-based systems could be a solution for enabling one of the emerging technologies, Cellular-Vehicle-to-Everything (C-V2X) communication.
**Approach:** To eliminate the limitations of centralized infrastructure elements, the Edge Cloud architecture could be the key in enhancing 5G systems' service capabilities by placing computational resources to the edge of the network, close to the users. To evaluate and validate new systems in this domain is to use model-based simulation tools.
