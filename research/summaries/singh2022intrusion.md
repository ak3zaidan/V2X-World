# Intrusion Detection System-Based Security Mechanism for Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks for Industrial IoT
Saurabh Singh, Sparsh Sharma, Surbhi Sharma, Osama Alfarraj et al. · IEEE Consumer Electronics Magazine 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mce.2021.3138703 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Security is an absolutely crucial aspect of any Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) network that must be considered carefully to protect users while allowing for proper, error-free functioning of the entire network. Vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs) are a type of network that has emerged with the aim of strengthening the available intelligent transportation systems.
**Approach:** Various security frameworks and solutions including the use of intrusion detection systems (IDS) have been proposed in the literature by researchers across the globe. In this study, the various challenges and problems associated with IDS usage in VANETs are thoroughly examined.
