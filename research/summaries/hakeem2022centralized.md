# Centralized Threshold Key Generation Protocol Based on Shamir Secret Sharing and HMAC Authentication
Shimaa A. Abdel Hakeem, Hyung-Chan Kim · Italian National Conference on Sensors 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s22010331 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Many group key management protocols have been proposed to manage key generation and distribution of vehicular communication. However, most of them suffer from high communication and computation costs due to the complex elliptic curve and bilinear pairing cryptography.
**Approach:** This paper proposes an efficient centralized threshold shared secret protocol based on the Shamir secret sharing technique and supporting key authentication using Hashed Message Authentication Code Protocol (HMAC).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Moreover, it reduces the received messages at vehicles from 2t to two messages. The proposed protocol security level outperforms the other shared secret protocols security, as it supports key authentication and confidentiality using HMAC that prevents attackers from compromising or faking the key.
