# A deep learning and blockchain enabled adaptive routing framework for secure Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Jayashree M. Oli, N. Neelima, Juan C. Vasquez, Jitendra Bahadur · Discover Internet of Things 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s43926-026-00516-2 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Routing efficiency and security in dynamic VANETs.
**Approach:** DL-ARBTM: actor-critic DRL routing with PBFT blockchain trust registry.
**Tools and scale:** Simulated urban VANET with OBUs, RSU validators.
**Results:** Outperforms AODV and others (numbers not extracted).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Behavioural trust is reactive: a node honest then defecting succeeds for one trust-update interval.
- Guarantees hold only within the Byzantine bound.
- Observation poisoning by colluders is bounded, not eliminated.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — routing.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
