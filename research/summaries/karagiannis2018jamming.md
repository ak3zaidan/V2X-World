# Jamming attack detection in a pair of RF communicating vehicles using unsupervised machine learning
Dimitrios Karagiannis, Antonios Argyriou · Vehicular Communications 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vehcom.2018.05.001 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract Wireless radio frequency (RF) jamming, both intentional and unintentional, poses a serious threat for wireless networks and wireless communications in general. Vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANET) are a subset of the wireless networks that incorporate modern safety-critical applications, that are vulnerable to jamming attacks.
**Approach:** In this paper we present a jamming detection approach for wireless vehicular networks that leverages the use of unsupervised machine learning.
**Tools and scale:** The proposed method is applied to three different real-life scenarios with extensive simulations being presented.
