# Channel State Information Prediction for 5G Wireless Communications: A Deep Learning Approach
Changqing Luo, Jinlong Ji, Qianlong Wang, Xuhui Chen, Pan Li · IEEE Transactions on Network Science and Engineering 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnse.2018.2848960 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Channel state information (CSI) estimation is one of the most fundamental problems in wireless communication systems.
**Approach:** Various methods, so far, have been developed to conduct CSI estimation. However, they usually require high computational complexity, which makes them unsuitable for 5G wireless communications due to employing many new techniques (e.g., massive MIMO, OFDM, and millimeter-Wave (mmWave)).
