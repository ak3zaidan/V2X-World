# JaDe: Low Power Jamming Detection Using Machine Learning in Vehicular Networks
Nalam Venkata Abhishek, Mohan Gurusamy · IEEE Wireless Communications Letters 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/lwc.2021.3097162 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular networks, like any other wireless networks, are prone to jamming due to the inherent nature of the wireless environment. In this letter, we consider an attacker who jams the network whenever he observes that a packet is being transmitted, i.e., some communication happening in the network.
**Approach:** In this letter, we propose a mechanism that uses a support vector machine to detect the presence of a jammer in the network.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed detection system.

- None stated in the abstract.
