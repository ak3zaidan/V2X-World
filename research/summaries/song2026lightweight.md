# A Lightweight Identity Authentication Protocol for Vehicle Ad Hoc Network Based on PUF-Obfuscation
Jiaquan Song, Xiaofang Wang, Pengfei Lu · Sensors 2026 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s26102971 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The rapid growth of Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSs) necessitates secure and efficient Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) communication.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a decentralized, dynamic, anonymous authentication protocol tailored for Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs).
**Tools and scale:** Simulations in SUMO and Omnetpp demonstrate that the protocol is highly efficient, achieving a low computational overhead of 6.77 ms per entity and a communication cost of 192 bytes.
**Results:** This mechanism effectively mitigates modeling threats, reducing the prediction success rate of machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL) attacks by approximately 35% compared to raw SDL PUFs.
