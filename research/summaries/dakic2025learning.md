# Learning Without Forgetting: Predicting the Reliability of V2X Wireless Communication
Anja Dakić, Benjamin Rainer, Thomas Zemen · 2025 IEEE Wireless Communications and Networking Conference (WCNC) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wcnc61545.2025.10978340 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Predicting V2X frame error rate and adapting to new scenarios.
**Approach:** CNN FER predictor; fine-tuning, transfer learning and modified learning-without-forgetting.
**Tools and scale:** GSCM and HIL emulation.
**Results:** 90% validation accuracy; balanced accuracy 81.96% with modified LwF.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Adaptation methods reduce accuracy on original data.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
