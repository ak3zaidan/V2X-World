# A review and classification of various VANET Intrusion Detection Systems
Mohammed Erritali, Bouabid El Ouahidi · 2013 National Security Days (JNS3) 2013 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jns3.2013.6595459 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The use of wireless links renders a vehicular ad-hoc network (VANET) vulnerable to malicious attacks such as Denial of Service, blackhole attack, Sybil attack, selective forwarding and altering routing information. In wired networks the attacker needs to gain access to the physical media to make an attack.
**Approach:** In wireless networks the scenario is much different, there are no firewalls and gateways in place hence attacks can take place from any location within radio coverage area. Each mobile node in ad-hoc network is an autonomous unit in itself free to move independently.
