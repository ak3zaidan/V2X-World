# Internet of Vehicles Communication and Quantum Circuit Simulation Based on Quantum State Secure Transmission
Shuo Lin · Proceedings of the 2025 3rd International Conference on Communication Networks and Machine Learning 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3728199.3728244 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Internet of Vehicles (IoV) is a hot research issue at home and abroad. However, with the development of intelligent, sharing and network connection of IoV technology, the security protection of IoV communication has become a focus.
**Approach:** In this paper, based on the secure transmission of shared secret states, the author has designed a model for the secure transmission of data among multiple vehicles, which does not require trusted client nodes and thus improves the performance against node attacks.
