# Wireless Digital Twin for Assessing the Reliability of Vehicular Communication Links
Stefan Zelenbaba, Benjamin Rainer, Markus Hofer, Thomas Zemen · 2022 IEEE Globecom Workshops (GC Wkshps) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/gcwkshps56602.2022.10008559 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Need repeatable, cost-effective reliability verification of vehicular links.
**Approach:** Site-specific wireless digital twin: real-time GSCM drives HIL with two commercial modems to obtain time-variant PER and latency tails.
**Tools and scale:** GSCM, HIL commercial modems, multi-link scenario.
**Results:** Estimates 90% tail probability of link latency; geo-routing with relay link increases reliability and reduces latency.
