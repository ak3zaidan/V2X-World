# Generative AI-in-the-loop: Integrating LLMs and GPTs into the Next Generation Networks
Han Zhang, Akram Bin Sediq, Ali Afana et al. · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04276 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In recent years, machine learning (ML) techniques have created numerous opportunities for intelligent mobile networks and have accelerated the automation of network operations.
**Approach:** In this work, we propose the concept of "generative AI-in-the-loop" and utilize the semantic understanding, context awareness, and reasoning abilities of LLMs to assist humans in handling complex or unforeseen situations in mobile communication networks. We believe that combining LLMs and ML models allows both to leverage their respective capabilities and achieve better results than either model alone.
