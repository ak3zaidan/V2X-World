# Are VANETs pseudonyms effective? An experimental evaluation of pseudonym tracking in adversarial scenario
Giovanni Gambigliani Zoccoli, Dario Stabili, Mirco Marchetti · 2023 IEEE 98th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2023-Fall) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2023-fall60731.2023.10333561 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Pseudonym change may not prevent tracking.
**Approach:** Evaluates five pseudonym change mechanisms against realistic attacker.
**Tools and scale:** Experimental evaluation.
**Results:** Realistic attacker reliably tracks multiple vehicles across schemes.
