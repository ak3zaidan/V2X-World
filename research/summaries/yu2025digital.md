# Digital twin driven autonomous management and control architecture and key technologies for low-altitude intelligent networks
Peng YU, Can TAN, Wenjing LI, Lixin ZHANG, Shaoyong GUO, Xuesong QIU, Tao HONG, Shu FU, Shangguang WANG, Luoming MENG · SCIENTIA SINICA Informationis 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1360/ssi-2025-0071 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Facing emerging novel service paradigms in the future low-altitude domain, low-altitude intelligent networks (LAIN) serve as a critical enabler for eﬃcient operation and safety control of low-altitude airspace.
**Approach:** Due to the characteristics of LAIN, such as three-dimensional coverage, highly reliable communication, and ultra-precise service, the traditional passive network management and control technology is diﬃcult to meet the requirements of on-demand services. There is an urgent need to integrate new technologies such as digital twin (DT), autonomous networks, and distributed artiﬁcial intelligence to overcome control bottlenecks.
