# Estimating Latency-Reliability in B5G Radio Access Networks: An AI-Empowered Approach
Francesca Conserva, Carolina Gijón, Matías Toril, Davide Micheli et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2025.3603923 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Estimating latency-reliability per service class in cellular networks.
**Approach:** Hybrid queuing-theoretic plus supervised learning model from OSS KPIs.
**Tools and scale:** Real operator network data.
**Results:** Latency estimation error below 10% (rest truncated).
