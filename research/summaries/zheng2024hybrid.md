# Hybrid Message Authentication Scheme for Internet of Vehicles Based on Zero Knowledge Proof
Guoli Zheng, Li Cao, Yuanshuai Li, Hong-Lei Men · 2024 5th International Seminar on Artificial Intelligence, Networking and Information Technology (AINIT) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ainit61980.2024.10581761 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Additionally, through the design of a verification tuple, the message receiver can authenticate the sender's identity upon receiving the message, addressing security vulnerabilities arising from the inability of traditional schemes to promptly revoke malicious vehicles.
**Approach:** Hence, we propose an efficient hybrid anonymous message authentication scheme for the IoV environment. The feasibility analysis reveals that the total delay introduced by the scheme is significantly lower than the communication duration, meeting the requirements of high-speed dynamic scenarios in the Io V.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** The openness and high mobility of the IoV communication environment impose stringent requirements on privacy and efficiency. The scheme is based on utilizing discrete logarithm zero-knowledge proofs and combines global pseudo identity for communication between network entities and local pseudo identity for communication within a domain, aiming to achieve efficient and authenticated secure communication among vehicles with conditional privacy protection.
