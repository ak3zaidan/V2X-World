# Blockchain-based distributed management system for trust in VANET
Youssef Inedjaren, M. Maachaoui, Besma Zeddini, J. Barbot · Vehicular Communications 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vehcom.2021.100350 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Blockchain technology is gaining a lot of ground as a research topic lately. Wireless ad hoc network researchers are studying and applying blockchain to solve security and reliability issues.
**Approach:** Blockchain is a distributed database maintained by multiple nodes in the network, and it is used as a solution to trust and security issues. Within a vehicular ad hoc network (VANET), vehicles provide mutual road safety by generating and broadcasting messages.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The simulation shows that our system is very efficient to be used in environments with limited resources like VANET.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.

**Novelty implications:** Does not rule out any candidate direction; it does not model SCMS certificates, linkage-based revocation, per-message joint ground truth or counterfactual reruns.
