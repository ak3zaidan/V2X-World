# Digital Twin-Enabled Vehicular Edge Computing in Satellite-Terrestrial Network: A Combined DRL and Optimization Approach
Hongxia Zhang, Shiyu Xi, Luyao Cao et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2026.3670437 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the rapid advancement of generative AI technologies, vehicle intelligence devices (VIDs) are being deployed on a large scale.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a vehicle edge computing system supported by a digital twin and a satellite-terrestrial network, where low earth orbit (LEO) satellites and roadside units (RSUs) provide computational services for VIDs. In the first phase, we develop a multi-agent deep reinforcement learning- based algorithm to optimize offloading decisions, association variables, task ratios, and computational resource allocation and reduce system cost.

**Results:** Finally, the simulation results verify that our proposed scheme achieves an average system cost saving of 32.7% compared to benchmark schemes.
