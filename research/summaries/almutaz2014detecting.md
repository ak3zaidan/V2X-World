# Detecting Sybil attacks in vehicular networks
Muhammad Ibrahim Almutaz, Levi Malott, Sriram Chellappan · Journal of Trust Management 2014 · https://doi.org/10.1186/2196-064x-1-4 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Detecting Sybil identities in mobile vehicular networks without extra hardware or complex crypto.
**Approach:** RSUs collect vehicles' neighbour reports; platoon dispersion theory from transportation engineering flags anomalously close persistent neighbourhoods.
**Tools and scale:** Analysis plus simulation on a 10-mile road.
**Results:** Simple, efficient, robust detection; analysis agrees with simulation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Only simulation data reported
- detection weaker when natural dispersion is low (vehicles platooning).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — uses traffic physics for cyber detection, a cross-layer idea our engine supports.
**Novelty implications:** Traffic-theory-based Sybil detection exists.
