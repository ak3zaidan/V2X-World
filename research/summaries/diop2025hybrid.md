# A Hybrid McEliece Goppa-Polar Cryptosystem to Secure Post-Quantum IoT
Moussa Diop, Papis Ndiaye, Doudou Dione, I. Diop · 2025 IEEE 2nd International Conference on Cryptography, Informatics, and Cybersecurity (ICoCICs) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icocics68032.2025.11384033 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Quantum computing threatens classical public-key cryptography, raising urgent security concerns for the IoT. While McEliece offers strong post-quantum security, its large keys hinder practical use on constrained devices.
**Approach:** To address this, we propose a hybrid McEliece-polar cryptosystem that combines Goppa and polar codes in a two-layer design.
**Tools and scale:** Non-vehicular setting. Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** Despite relying on simulations and an informal security analysis, results highlight the promise of this approach for IoT.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "Future work will provide formal proofs and hardware benchmarks to validate scalability."
