# A Lightweight Coordinate-Conditioned Diffusion Approach for 6G C-V2X Radio Environment Maps
Liu Cao, Zhaoyu Liu, Dongyu Wei, Yuan Yang et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22535 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** 6G C-V2X broadcasters lack dynamic radio environment maps.
**Approach:** Coordinate-conditioned diffusion model predicting REMs for arbitrary transmitter positions.
**Tools and scale:** Historical transmitter REMs in a region.
**Results:** Predicted REMs match ground-truth statistics and structure; more stable than other generative methods.
