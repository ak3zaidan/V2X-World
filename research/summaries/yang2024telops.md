# TelOps: AI-Driven Operations and Maintenance for Telecommunication Networks
Yuqian Yang, Shusen Yang, Cong Zhao, Zongben Xu · IEEE Communications Magazine 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mcom.003.2300055 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Telecommunication Networks (TNs) have become the most important infrastructure for data communications over the last century.
**Approach:** Operations and maintenance (O&M) is extremely important to ensure the availability, effectiveness, and efficiency of TN communications. Different from the popular O&M technique for IT systems (e.g., the cloud), artificial intelligence for IT Operations (AIOps), O&M for TNs meets the following three fundamental challenges: topological dependence of network components, highly heterogeneous software, and restricted failure data.
