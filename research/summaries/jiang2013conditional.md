# A conditional privacy scheme based on anonymized batch authentication in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Shunrong Jiang, Xiaoyan Zhu, Liangmin Wang · IEEE Wireless Communications and Networking Conference 2013 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wcnc.2013.6554932 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** CRL broadcast overhead and revoked-vehicle privacy.
**Approach:** Anonymous batch authentication with IBS, hash-chain backward privacy, HMAC-based revocation with group key.
**Tools and scale:** WCNC 2013; analysis.
**Results:** Better authentication delay and revocation overhead than batch schemes.
