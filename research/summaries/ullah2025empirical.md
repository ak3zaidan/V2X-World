# Empirical Investigation of Cybersecurity Challenges in Driverless Vehicular Communication
Naeem Ullah, Siffat Ullah Khan, Ghulam Murtaza Khan · Annual Methodological Archive Research Review 2025 · https://doi.org/10.63075/f5dyd512 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cars without drivers are gaining reputation due to the growth in wireless communication technology. Driverless vehicular communication is a Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) technology that is evolving rapidly, addressing modern transportation needs and driving both economic and technological progress.
**Approach:** This paper aims to identify and analyze significant cybersecurity challenges that automotive organizations need to address to develop secure V2V communication for autonomous vehicles. We conducted an online survey with 51 experts from various countries.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting (VANET/IoV/V2X). Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
