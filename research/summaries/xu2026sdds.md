# SDDS: A Scalable and Distributed Data Sharing Scheme in Vehicular Clouds
Yan Xu, Yongkang Zhou, Jie Cui, Hong Zhong · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2026.3680323 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular clouds (VC) enable secure resource sharing among vehicles through ciphertext-policy attribute-based encryption (CP-ABE). However, most CP-ABE solutions depend on a Trusted Authority (TA) for private key generation, leading to the key escrow problem.
**Approach:** To overcome these limitations, we propose SDDS, a scalable and distributed data sharing scheme based on Registration-Based Encryption (RBE).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** SDDS leverages Secure Multi-Party Computation (SMPC) to achieve a decentralized framework, thereby removing the need for any trusted authorities or centralized curators. Formal analysis confirms the security, correctness of SDDS, while experimental evaluations show a significant reduction in computational and communication costs compared to related works.
