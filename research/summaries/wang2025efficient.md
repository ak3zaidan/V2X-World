# Efficient Multi-Layer Credential Revocation Scheme for 6G Using Dynamic RSA Accumulators and Blockchain
Guangchao Wang, Yanlong Zou, Jizhe Zhou, Houxiao Cui, Ying Ju · Electronics 2025 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics14153066 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** Credential revocation latency for massive 6G access.
**Approach:** Four-layer cuckoo filter, consistent hashing, dynamic RSA accumulator and blockchain.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Smaller, more stable revocation latency.

- Future: post-quantum cryptography integration.
