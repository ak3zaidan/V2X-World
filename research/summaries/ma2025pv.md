# PV-STR: An Efficient Pseudonym Verification Scheme With Spatial-Temporal Revocation in IoVs
Yajun Ma, Libing Wu, Enshu Wang, Bingyi Liu et al. · IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tmc.2025.3635688 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Pseudonym revocation at scale: local anomalies disrupt global status and synchronisation is costly and attackable.
**Approach:** PV-STR: spatial-temporal revocation with batch revocation in resilient cycles, local filtering of revoked pseudonyms in event areas, proofs of legitimacy for others, edge-assisted distributed caching. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Simulations on real-world large-scale datasets.
**Results:** Significantly lower vulnerability window, communication overhead and memory than state-of-the-art schemes (no numbers in abstract).
