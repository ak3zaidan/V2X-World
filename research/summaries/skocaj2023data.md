# Data-driven Predictive Latency for 5G: A Theoretical and Experimental Analysis Using Network Measurements
Marco Skocaj, Francesca Conserva, Nicol Sarcone Grande, Andrea Orsi, Davide Micheli, Giorgio Ghinamo et al. · 2023 IEEE 34th Annual International Symposium on Personal, Indoor and Mobile Radio Communications (PIMRC) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/pimrc56721.2023.10293861 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Predictive latency in 5G using MNO-available data.
**Approach:** Hypoexponential analytical model of user-plane latency; Bayesian and graph ML for regression, anomaly detection and forecasting.
**Tools and scale:** Real network data from vehicular, dense-urban and event scenarios.
**Results:** Traffic-related KPIs correlate more with latency than radio KPIs (DL resource utilisation about 0.79).
