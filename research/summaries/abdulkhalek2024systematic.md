# A Systematic Literature Review on Internet of Vehicles Security Challenges and Penetration Testing Solutions
Bassam Abdulkhalek · venue not stated 2024 · https://doi.org/10.36227/techrxiv.172296707.71456018/v1 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This systematic literature review examines security challenges and penetration testing mitigations within the Internet of Vehicles (IoV), integrated with 5G technology. While IoV improves traffic control, road safety and sustainable transportation, it incurs serious cybersecurity vulnerabilities.
**Approach:** Suggestions for future research include enhancing threat detection and prediction techniques, developing security frameworks tailored to IoV environments, and fostering cooperation between academia and industry.
