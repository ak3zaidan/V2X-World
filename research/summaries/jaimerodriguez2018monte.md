# A Monte Carlo Simulator of non-WSSUS Rayleigh Fading Channels for Vehicular Communications
Jose J. Jaime-Rodriguez, Carlos A. Gomez-Vega, Carlos A. Gutierrez, Jose M. Luna-Rivera, Daniel U. Campos-Delgado, Ramiro Velazquez · 2018 IEEE 38th Central America and Panama Convention (CONCAPAN XXXVIII) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/concapan.2018.8596375 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Simulating non-WSSUS Rayleigh vehicular channels.
**Approach:** Monte Carlo sum-of-cisoids simulator; LS and STA estimators analysed.
**Tools and scale:** Link-level.
**Results:** Non-stationarity transparent to LS, degrades STA; modified STA proposed.
