# Cyber-Physical Control of Indoor Multi-vehicle Testbed for Cooperative Driving
Ali Bemani, Niclas Bjorsell · 2020 IEEE Conference on Industrial Cyberphysical Systems (ICPS) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icps48405.2020.9274688 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Validating cooperative driving algorithms over wireless networked control.
**Approach:** Indoor testbed of 4 Raspberry Pi robot vehicles, BLE iBeacon + IMU particle-filter positioning, MPC with Kalman observer.
**Tools and scale:** Indoor testbed.
**Results:** RSSI indoor localisation error under 6 cm; control resilient to packet loss in simulation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: more intelligent cooperative scenarios with new wireless technology.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
