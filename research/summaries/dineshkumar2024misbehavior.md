# Misbehavior Detection for Position Falsification Attacks in VANETs Using Ensemble Machine Learning
R. Dineshkumar, Prateeksha Siddhanti, Sarangam Kodati, Ammar Hameed Shnain, V. Malathy · 2024 Second International Conference on Data Science and Information System (ICDSIS) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icdsis61070.2024.10594423 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Position falsification.
**Approach:** Three sender-location features; kNN, RF, DT stacked with GLM.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi (implied).
**Results:** Accuracy 99.82%, precision 97.34%, recall 97.56%, F1 97.28%.
