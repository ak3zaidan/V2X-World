# BAIV: An Efficient Blockchain-Based Anonymous Authentication and Integrity Preservation Scheme for Secure Communication in VANETs
Azees Maria, ArunSekar Rajasekaran, F. Al-turjman, Chadi Altrjman et al. · Electronics 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics11030488 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Providing a low computational cost with high serving capability, however, is a critical phenomenon in the current VANET system.
**Approach:** To overcome the above-mentioned challenge, a blockchain-based authentication protocol is developed in this work. Even though many blockchain-based schemes have been proposed recently, the existing schemes were not focused on conditional anonymity.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** As a result, the proposed scheme provides an efficient mechanism for anonymous authentication, privacy, and integrity preservation with conditional tracking.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.

**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
