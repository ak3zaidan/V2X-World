# Phase plane design based fast altitude tracking control for hypersonic flight vehicle with angle of attack constraint
Yang LIU, Chaoyang DONG, Wenqiang ZHANG, Qing WANG · Chinese Journal of Aeronautics 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cja.2020.04.026 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, fast setpoint altitude tracking control for Hypersonic Flight Vehicle (HFV) satisfying Angle of Attack (AOA) constraint is studied with a two-loop structure controller, in the presence of parameter uncertainties and disturbances.
**Approach:** For the outer loop, phase plane design is adopted for the simplified model under Bang-Bang controller to generate AOA command guaranteeing fast tracking performance. Modifications based on Feedback-Linearization (FL) technique are adopted to transform the phase trajectory into a sliding curve.
