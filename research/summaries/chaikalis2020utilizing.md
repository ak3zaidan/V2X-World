# Utilizing turbo codes for secure 5G V2X
Costas Chaikalis, Dimitrios Kosmanos, Nicholas S. Samaras · 2020 IEEE Microwave Theory and Techniques in Wireless Communications (MTTW) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mttw51045.2020.9245035 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Turbo codes is a very powerful and efficient error correction coding scheme since 1993.
**Approach:** 5G V2X services need a compromise between performance and latency. Having this in mind and despite the complexity of turbo codes, our approach shows that they can be used for enhancing safety related 5G V2V and V2I services for short frame lengths.
