# An Efficient Approach for the Detection and Prevention of Gray-Hole Attacks in VANETs
Abdul Malik, Muhammad Zahid Khan, Saeed Mian Qaisar, Mohammad Faisal, Gulzar Mehmood · IEEE Access 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2023.3274650 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Gray-hole attacks in AODV-based VANETs.
**Approach:** DPGHA with dynamic thresholds on packet counters and sequence numbers.
**Tools and scale:** NS-2 and SUMO.
**Results:** Routing overhead -10.85%, delay -3.85%, PDR +4.67%, throughput +6.58% vs benchmarks.

- None stated in abstract.
