# can-sleuth: Sleuthing out the capabilities, limitations, and performance impacts of automotive intrusion detection datasets
Brooke Kidmose, Andreas Kidmose, Weizhi Meng · International Journal of Information Security 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s10207-025-01038-8 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Quality of automotive CAN intrusion detection datasets.
**Approach:** Compares six CAN IDS datasets with sixteen ML IDSs; feature and train-test interdependence analysis.
**Tools and scale:** HCRL, can-train-and-test-v1.5, UNIMORE datasets.
**Results:** can-train-and-test-v1.5 gives clearer picture, reveals vehicle-type overfitting and has an order of magnitude more training data.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Only pre-labelled datasets used
- labelling unlabelled datasets left to future work
- UNIMORE datasets too large in raw form.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — in-vehicle CAN, but dataset-quality methodology is relevant to our benchmark direction.
**Novelty implications:** Dataset critique methodology can inform a V2X misbehaviour benchmark.
