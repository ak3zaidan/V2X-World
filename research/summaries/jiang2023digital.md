# Digital Twin Based Beam Prediction: Can We Train in the Digital World and Deploy in Reality?
Shuai-Feng Jiang, Ahmed Alkhateeb · 2023 IEEE International Conference on Communications Workshops (ICC Workshops) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccworkshops57953.2023.10283592 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Realizing the potential gains of large-scale MIMO systems requires the accurate estimation of their channels or the fine adjustment of their narrow beams. This, however, is typically associated with high channel acquisition/beam sweeping overhead that scales with the number of antennas.
**Approach:** To address this challenge, we propose a novel direction that utilizes digital replicas of the physical world to reduce or even eliminate the MIMO channel acquisition overhead. Further, we propose to use machine learning to approximate the digital replicas and reduce the ray tracing computational cost.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: ray trac, digital twin, real-world. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** In the proposed digital twin aided communication, 3D models that approximate the real-world communication environment are constructed and accurate ray-tracing is utilized to simulate the site-specific channels. The results show that a learning model trained solely with the data generated by the digital replica can achieve relatively good performance on real-world data. Moreover, a small number of real-world data points can quickly achieve near-optimal performance, overcoming the modeling mismatches between the physical and digital worlds and significantly reducing the data acquisition overhead.
