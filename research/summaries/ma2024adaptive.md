# Adaptive Service Provisioning for Dynamic Resource Allocation in Network Digital Twin
Yuyin Ma, Fei Song, Giovanni Pau, Ilsun You, Hongke Zhang · IEEE Network 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mnet.2023.3337245 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The extant physical networks are swiftly undergoing transformation, furnishing heightened bandwidth, diminished latency, and deterministic network services tailored for industries such as industrial manufacturing, smart transportation, and remote collaboration.
**Approach:** Nevertheless, the current efficiency in network resource aggregation is comparatively modest, presenting challenges in the provisioning of adaptive and discriminated services to users with heterogeneous requirements. In response to this, we proffer a resource aggregation and orchestration scheme grounded in the paradigm of network digital twinning.
