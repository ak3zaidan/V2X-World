# Learning Based Security for VANET with Blockchain
Canhuang Dai, Xingyu Xiao, Yuzhen Ding, Liang Xiao, Yu-Liang Tang, Sheng Zhou · International Conference on Conceptual Structures 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccs.2018.8689228 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Approach:** Indirect reciprocity reputation recorded on blockchain; RL action selection with hotbooting.
