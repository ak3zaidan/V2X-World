# A Privacy-Preserving Attribute-Based Authenticated Key Management Scheme for Accountable Vehicular Communications
Hao-Wen Tan, Wenying Zheng, Yun-Guo Guan, Rong-Xing Lu · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2022.3220410 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In recent years, the vehicular ad hoc network (VANET), recognized as the fundamental infrastructure of the intelligent transportation system (ITS), has played an increasingly momentous role in advancing real-time vehicular communications. With the purpose of achieving seamless and reliable connectivity, lots of research efforts have been witnessed.
**Approach:** Nevertheless, the flexibility of access control and authenticated key management (AKM) has not been properly guaranteed.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Particularly, exclusive V2V connections without third-party intervention or remote surveillance are crucial for privacy protection, while the non-repudiation and authenticity properties may be compromised in this case. Moreover, independent V2V connections with anonymity are achieved.
