# SmartFITLab: Intelligent Execution and Validation Platform for 5G Field Interoperability Testing
Tongwei Tu · 2025 7th International Conference on Next Generation Data-driven Networks (NGDN) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ngdn66208.2025.11182147 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The rapid deployment of 5G technology necessitates rigorous testing for interoperability among diverse components.
**Approach:** However, traditional testing methods often fall short in efficiency and adaptability. To address these shortcomings, we introduce SmartFITLab, an intelligent platform that enhances the interoperability testing process for 5G systems.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G.
**Results:** Through extensive experiments, SmartFITLab has demonstrated a notable reduction in error rates and an improvement in testing accuracy compared to conventional methods. The results highlight SmartFITLab’s role in promoting effective 5G deployments, serving as a critical tool for telecom stakeholders.
