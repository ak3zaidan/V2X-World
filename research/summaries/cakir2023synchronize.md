# How to synchronize Digital Twins? A Communication Performance Analysis
Lal Verda Cakir, Sarah Al-Shareeda, Sema F. Oktug, Mehmet Özdem, Matthew Broadbent, Berk Canberk · 2023 IEEE 28th International Workshop on Computer Aided Modeling and Design of Communication Links and Networks (CAMAD) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/camad59638.2023.10478422 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Synchronization is fundamental for mirroring real-world entities in real-time and supporting effective operations of Digital Twins (DTs).
**Approach:** Such synchronization is enabled by the communication between the physical and virtual realms, and it is mostly assumed to occur in real-time. However, this is not the case, as real-life scenarios witness performance degradation that may lead to synchronization problems.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: emulating.
**Results:** We propose a Twin Alignment Ratio metric to evaluate the synchronization performance to achieve this goal.
