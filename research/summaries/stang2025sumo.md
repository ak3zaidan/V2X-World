**Problem:** Microscopic traffic simulation tools enable the simulation of various traffic demands and their impacts.
**Approach:** By implementing realistic traffic models, they are commonly used to analyze traffic engineering measures. Since microscopic traffic simulations focus more on the network than on individual small-scale scenarios, such frameworks are appropriate for efficiency studies.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting. The available text does not describe a V2X simulator, radio stack, traffic model or SCMS-style PKI in enough detail to report; scale not stated.
**Results:** Besides the efficiency aspect, the proposed co-simulation bridge also offers the possibility to simulate sensing technology in a 3D environment with AURELION, where SUMO provides realistic traffic situations.
