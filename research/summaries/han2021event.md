# Event-Triggered Interval-Based Anomaly Detection and Attack Identification Methods for an In-Vehicle Network
Mee Lan Han, Byung Il Kwak, Huy Kang Kim · IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tifs.2021.3069171 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle communication technology has been steadily progressing alongside the convergence of the in-vehicle network (IVN) and wireless communication technology. The communication with various external networks further reinforces the connectivity between the inside and outside of a vehicle.
**Approach:** Next, we analyze the CAN ID’s event-triggered interval and measure statistical moments depending on the defined time-window. Finally, we conduct extensive evaluations of the proposed methods’ performance by considering different attack scenarios and three types of machine learning models.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The results demonstrate that the proposed method can effectively detect an abnormality in the IVN, with up to 99% accuracy. Our results suggest that when tree-based machine learning models are used as the classifier, the proposed method of attack identification can achieve more than 94% accuracy.
