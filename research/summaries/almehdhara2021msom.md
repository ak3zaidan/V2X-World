# MSOM: Efficient Mechanism for Defense against DDoS Attacks in VANET
Mohammed Al-Mehdhara, Na Ruan · Wireless Communications and Mobile Computing 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2021/8891758 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The wireless nature of the Vehicular Ad Hoc Network (VANET), a technology that offers facilities such as traffic management and safety services, makes it vulnerable to distributed denial‐of‐service (DDoS) attacks that exploit network communications and reduce network reliability and performance. This paper proposes a design of a secure VANET architecture using a Software‐Defined Networking (SDN) controller and Neural Network Self‐Organizing Maps 
**Approach:** In the proposed design, we adopt the SDN architecture by using its separation of the control plane from the data plane and adding intelligent capabilities to the VANET. Moreover, the proposed scheme achieves a high accuracy rate (99.67%).
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset, deep learning (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** Moreover, the proposed scheme achieves a high accuracy rate (99.67%).
