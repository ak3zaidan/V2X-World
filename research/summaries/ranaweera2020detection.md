# Detection of anomalous vehicles using physics of traffic
Malith Ranaweera, Aruna Prasad Seneviratne, David Rey, Meead Saberi et al. · Vehicular Communications 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vehcom.2020.100304 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract
