# Evaluating the Robustness of ADVENT on the VeReMi-Extension Dataset
Hamideh Baharlouei, Adetokunbo Makanju, Nur Zincir-Heywood · 2024 20th International Conference on Network and Service Management (CNSM) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.23919/cnsm62983.2024.10814616 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Robustness of ADVENT against DDoS variants.
**Approach:** Evaluate ADVENT (ML+FL early attack detection) on five DoS types; time slicing for malicious node detection.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi extension.
**Results:** Improved FPR and F1; consistent attack onset detection.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — early detection and attacker identification.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
