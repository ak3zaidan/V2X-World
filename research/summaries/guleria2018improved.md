# Improved Detection and Mitigation of DDoS Attack in Vehicular ad hoc Network
Charu Guleria, Harsh Kumar Verma · 2018 4th International Conference on Computing Communication and Automation (ICCCA) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ccaa.2018.8777539 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) are eminent type of Mobile ad hoc Networks. The network created in VANETs is quite prone to security problem.
**Approach:** In this work, a new mechanism is proposed to study the security of VANETs against DDoS attack. The proposed mechanism focuses on distributed denial of service attacks.
**Tools and scale:** NS-2 (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** The results of simulation represent that the proposed approach is better in the terms of network packet loss, routing overhead and network throughput.
