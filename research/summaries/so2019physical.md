# Physical layer plausibility checks for misbehavior detection in V2X networks
Steven So, J. Petit, David Starobinski · Wireless Network Security 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3317549.3323406 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Location spoofing evades application-layer checks.
**Approach:** Three RSSI-based physical-layer plausibility checks with multi-step mechanisms, run locally without honest-majority assumptions. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi (enhanced by the authors).
**Results:** Best check: detection rate 83.73%, precision 95.91%, outperforming application-layer ML MBD.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — cross-layer (PHY) misbehaviour detection, which our joint radio ground truth supports.
**Novelty implications:** PHY-layer plausibility in MBD is established; cross-layer claims must be about new combinations or closed-loop use.
