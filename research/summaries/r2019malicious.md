# Malicious node detection in VANET Session Hijacking Attack
Jeevitha R., N. Sudha Bhuvaneswari · 2019 IEEE International Conference on Electrical, Computer and Communication Technologies (ICECCT) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icecct.2019.8869452 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Adhoc Networks (VANETs) consists of lot of or thousands of moving nodes. Maintaining network topology and protecting every node from attack is not practical.
**Approach:** This paper primarily focuses on detecting the malicious node that pretends to be a legitimate vehicle throughout the session hijacking attack in VANETs and also discusses on the throughput, delay at end points, total counts of packet generated, exchanged and dropped using the Network Simulator-2 (NS2) tool and appropriate inference provided.
**Tools and scale:** NS-2 (mentioned in abstract)
