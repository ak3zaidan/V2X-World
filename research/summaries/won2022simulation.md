**Problem:** : We present a new approach to design the system of autonomous vehicles based on practical test scenarios in simulation.
**Approach:** As the level of driving automation functions advances, various events and problems have occurred in many unexpected or unseen situations, so the design of autonomous driving systems is required to be more robust and sufficiently practical. We propose a Simulation Driven Development Process (SDDP) based on practical test scenarios in a simulation environment.
