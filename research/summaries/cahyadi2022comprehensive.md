# A Comprehensive Survey on Certificateless Aggregate Signature in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
E. Cahyadi, Min-Shiang Hwang · IETE Technical Review 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1080/02564602.2021.2017800 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Research related to the authentication schemes in vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) has received significant attention recently. They substantially impact the security and privacy aspects of the message dissemination process in the road environment.
**Approach:** This paper comprehensively reviews most of them regarding their main feature, contributions, security, and performance efficiency, as the state-of-the-art of all CLAS mechanisms in VANETs.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Some authentication schemes with certificateless aggregate signature (CLAS) in VANETs have been published since the first related article emerged in 2015.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Finally, the conclusion and some open issues on the CLAS authentication scheme in VANETs are provided in this survey.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — Bespoke VANET/IoV authentication or privacy scheme (CPPA/certificateless/batch/blockchain style), evaluated by analysis or small simulation; not IEEE 1609.2/SCMS and no closed-loop safety evaluation.
**Novelty implications:** Adds to the large body of standalone authentication schemes; does not touch end-to-end SCMS simulation or safety impact of security.
