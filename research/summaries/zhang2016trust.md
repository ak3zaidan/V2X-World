# Trust-based Secure Routing in Software-defined Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Dajun Zhang, F. Richard Yu, Zhexiong Wei et al. · arXiv 2016 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.04012 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the rising interest of expedient, safe, and high-efficient transportation, vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) have turned into a critical technology in smart transportation systems.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a novel framework of software-defined VANETs with trust management. Using the on-demand distance vector routing (TAODV) protocol as an example, we present a routing protocol named software-defined trust based ad hoc on-demand distance vector routing (SD-TAODV).
**Tools and scale:** Simulation results are presented to show the effectiveness of the proposed software-defined VANETs with trust management.
