# Data Security Risk Assessment Method for Connected and Automated Vehicles
Shiying Zhou, Xuezhu Yang, Muxi Li, Huawei Yang et al. · 2022 IEEE 7th International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Engineering (ICITE) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icite56321.2022.10101389 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Considering the data security problems of connected and automated vehicles (CAVs), this paper analyzes the security vulnerability and risk challenges for the communication data in vehicles.
**Approach:** Based on state of the art technical manuals, we explore the security reverse engineering technology, and propose the risk assessment method for data security of CAVs. Considering the impact level of data security for the national security, public interests, personal legitimate rights and privacy, we present the possibility evaluation level of data security and get the security risk value of ICVs.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: ISO/SAE 21434 / TARA).
**Results:** The proposed method has been verfied and provides risk assessment results of the vehicle component, i.e., T-box.
