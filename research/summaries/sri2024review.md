**Problem:** The Internet of Vehicles (IoV) refers to an interconnected network where vehicles communicate with each other "Vehicle-to-Vehicle V2V", with infrastructure "Vehicle-to-Infrastructure (V2I)", with pedestrians "Vehicle-to-Pedestrian (V2P)", and with other entities through cloud services "Vehicle-to-Cloud (V2C)".
**Approach:** While IoV offers numerous benefits like enhanced safety, traffic management, and user experience, it also presents significant security challenges due to its complexity, distributed nature, and critical real-time operations.
