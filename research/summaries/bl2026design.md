# Design and Development of a Digital Twin Framework for V2I Channel Modeling
Sharan Karthick Bl, Raja Vara Prasad Yerra · IEEE Wireless Communications and Networking Conference 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wcnc65185.2026.11555637 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Statistical channel models lack site specificity for V2I.
**Approach:** Digital twin co-simulating Unity and SUMO with OSM 3D buildings and 3GPP geometry-aware LOS/NLOSv/NLOSb ray-casting.
**Tools and scale:** Unity, SUMO, OSM; path loss heatmaps.
**Results:** Site-specific path loss heatmaps demonstrated.
