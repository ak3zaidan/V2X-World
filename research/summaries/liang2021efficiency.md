# An Efficiency-Accuracy Tradeoff for IDSs in VANETs with Markov-based Reputation Scheme
Junwei Liang, Maode Ma · International Conference on Communications 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc42927.2021.9500320 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) are considered to be a next big thing that will remarkably change our lives, since this kind of technology is able to make our lives and roads safer.
**Approach:** Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS) is an important technology that can mitigate both inner and outer threats for the vulnerable networks like VANETs. However, it is difficult to adopt the same IDSs that have been appropriately used in wired networks, due to the fast moving and highly dynamic nature of VANETs.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** In EIDS-MRS, the Non-Linear Programming (NLP)-Optimization is designed as the efficient mechanism to reduce the execution time of IDSs in VANETs. Experiments show that the EIDS-MRS has better performance than other available IDSs in terms of detection rate, detection time and overhead.
