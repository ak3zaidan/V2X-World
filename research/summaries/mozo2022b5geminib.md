# B5GEMINI: Digital Twin Network for 5G and Beyond
Alberto Mozo, Amit Karamchandani, Mario Sanz, Jose Ignacio Moreno et al. · NOMS 2022-2022 IEEE/IFIP Network Operations and Management Symposium 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/noms54207.2022.9789810 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Digital Twin Network (DTN) is a new technology that builds on the concept of Digital Twins (DT) to create a virtual representation of the physical objects of a telecommunications network. DTN bridges physical and virtual spaces to enable coordination and synchronization of physical parts while eliminating the need to directly interact with them.
**Approach:** In this work, we present B5GEMINI a DTN for 5G and beyond networks that makes an extensive use of artificial intelligence (AI). First, we present the infrastructural and architectural components that support B5GEMINI.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G, digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Next, we explore five paradigmatic use cases where AI can leverage B5GEMINI for building new AI-powered applications. Finally, we identify the main components of the AI ecosystem of B5GEMINI.
