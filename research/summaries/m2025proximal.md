# Proximal policy optimization in event-triggered resource-efficient communications for dynamic platoon maneuvres
Kezia M, Anusuya K V · Physica Scripta 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1088/1402-4896/adbb31 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In modern transportation systems, dynamic platooning is a promising approach that leverages adaptable vehicle formations to optimize traffic flow and fuel efficiency.
**Approach:** However, reliance on periodic broadcasts to preserve string stability during platoon maneuvres poses a challenge, demanding substantial resources within a constrained spectrum. This paper presents a novel event-triggered approach using Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) in Reinforcement Learning (RL), specifically designed for dynamic autonomous vehicular platoons on highways and urban roads.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Simulation results demonstrate the efficacy of the trained Platoon Leader (PL) in maintaining string stability, even amidst comprehensive platoon dynamics, achieved through a reduced waiting time for safety messages in the buffer. The cost-benefit analysis of the proposed method demonstrates improved road space utilization, reduced fuel consumption, and a decrease in collisions, addressing critical challenges in modern transportation systems.
