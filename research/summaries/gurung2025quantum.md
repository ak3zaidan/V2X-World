# Quantum Vanguard: Server Optimized Privacy Fortified Federated Intelligence for Future Vehicles
Dev Gurung, Shiva Raj Pokhrel · arXiv.org 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02301 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Privacy and security in federated learning for AVs.
**Approach:** vQFL quantum federated learning with differential privacy, QKD and server-side fine-tuning.
**Tools and scale:** KITTI, Waymo, nuScenes; VQC, QCNN, SamplerQNN models.
**Results:** Accuracy comparable to standard QFL with better privacy and minimal overhead.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Assumes secure client/server laboratories.
- Future work: extend beyond object detection to route planning, lane detection, segmentation.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — FL.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
