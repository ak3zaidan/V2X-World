# Poster: V2X Simulation of C-ITS Message Misbehavior
Dorra Jerbi, João Almeida, Joaquim Ferreira, Paulo C. Bartolomeu · IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc69225.2026.11629129 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Lack of reproducible CPM misbehaviour datasets with ground truth.
**Approach:** Artery-based injection framework with standard CP service and central service-agnostic misbehaviour engine.
**Tools and scale:** Artery (Vanetza/OMNeT++/SUMO).
**Results:** Poster; framework design.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — reproducible in-simulation misbehaviour injection with message-level labels resembles our engine's attack injection and ground truth.
**Novelty implications:** Artery now has a misbehaviour engine; our differentiators must be SCMS loop, counterfactual reruns and joint ground truth.
