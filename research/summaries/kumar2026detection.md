# DETECTION AND PREVENTION OF CYBER ATTACKS IN VANET’S
Yaman Kumar, Ankush Kumar, Utsav Sharma et al. · Iterative International Publishers eBooks 2026 · https://doi.org/10.58532/nbennurmoml10 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) play a crucial role in enabling intelligent transportation systems by supporting real time communication between vehicles and roadside infrastructure.
**Approach:** Thos research aims to design an effective detection with cryptographic authentication mechanisms, The proposed system analyzes beacon message patterns, vehicle behavioral features, and network anomalies to identify suspicious nodes with high accuracy.
**Tools and scale:** Experimental evaluation using standard VANET simulators demonstrates improved detection rates, reduces false positives, and enhanced resilience against coordinate attacks.
**Results:** Experimental evaluation using standard VANET simulators demonstrates improved detection rates, reduces false positives, and enhanced resilience against coordinate attacks.
