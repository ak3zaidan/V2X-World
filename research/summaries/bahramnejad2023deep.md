# A Deep Learning Method for Automatic Reliability Prediction of CR-VANETs
Somayeh Bahramnejad, Naser Movahhedinia, Amineh Naseri · n/a 2023 · https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-2604220/v1 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Reliability prediction for cognitive-radio VANETs.
**Approach:** DL model trained on a generated reliability dataset.
**Tools and scale:** Generated dataset.
**Results:** Correlation 0.9862, RMSE 0.0381.
