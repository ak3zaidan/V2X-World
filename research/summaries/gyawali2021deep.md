# Deep Reinforcement Learning Based Dynamic Reputation Policy in 5G Based Vehicular Communication Networks
Sohan Gyawali, Yi Qian, Rose Qingyang Hu · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2021.3079379 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Attackers can send false feedback to collaborative MBD and exploit fixed reputation updates.
**Approach:** DRL at VEC servers chooses dynamic reputation update policy; Dempster-Shafer feedback fusion. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** More true feedback than existing reputation update policy.
