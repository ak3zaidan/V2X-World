# SMAD-LDS: enhanced secure message authentication and dissemination with lightweight digital signature in the Internet of Vehicles
Islam Z. Ahmed, Yasser Hifny, Rowayda A. Sadek · PeerJ Computer Science 2025 · https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj-cs.2982 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** However, the nature of the IoV communication exposes vehicles to potential security risks.
**Approach:** Consequently, this article proposes secure message authentication and dissemination with lightweight digital signature (SMAD-LDS), a robust message authentication and dissemination scheme incorporating a novel lightweight digital signature technique designed to minimize these overheads. There are four main phases in the proposed SMAD-LDS scheme such as the initiation phase, registration phase, send/receive message phase, and key updating phase.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** For the registration phase, our results demonstrate that the computation cost in SMAD-LDS is reduced by at least 46.8% compared to other works in the literature. Moreover, the computation overhead in the send/receive messages phase in SMAD-LDS is reduced by at least 94%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
