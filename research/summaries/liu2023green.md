**Approach:** Researchers have developed static security schemes based on the blockchain, but it results in excessive resource occupation. And it is difficult to resist some attacks such as desynchronization, Denial of Service (DoS), and covert intrusion.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: blockchain
