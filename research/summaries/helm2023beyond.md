# Beyond Mean: Spatio-Temporal Modeling of Queue Utilizations and Flow Latencies Using T-GNNs
Max Helm, Benedikt Jaeger, Christopher Pfefferle, Georg Carle · 2023 35th International Teletraffic Congress (ITC-35) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/itc-3560063.2023.10555788 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ML network models predict only mean metrics.
**Approach:** Temporal GNNs predicting queue utilisation and flow latency over time.
**Tools and scale:** 100 random topologies.
**Results:** Mean queue utilisation error 5.5%; latency MARE 5-55% by time resolution; beats static GNN.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - temporal surrogate.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
