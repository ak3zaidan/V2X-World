# Exploring Traffic Simulation and Cybersecurity Strategies Using Large Language Models
Lu Gao, Yongxin Liu, Hongyun Chen, Dahai Liu et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.16699 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ITS cybersecurity testing is manual and fragmented.
**Approach:** Multi-agent LLM framework that creates traffic scenarios, designs cyberattacks on connected-vehicle broadcasts, and develops defences.
**Tools and scale:** Traffic simulation case study.
**Results:** Attack raised travel time by 10.2%; defence reduced it by 3.3%.
