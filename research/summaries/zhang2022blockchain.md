# Blockchain-based anonymous authentication for traffic reporting in VANETs
Li Zhang, Jianbo Xu · Connection science 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1080/09540091.2022.2026888 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The application of vehicle ad-hoc networks (VANETs) has brought huge potential to the development of intelligent transportation systems. By traffic event reporting, traffic efficiency can be significantly improved.
**Approach:** However, people usually lack the motivation for reporting traffic events or driving violations, because once the identity of the reporter is leaked, he or she may be tracked by malicious attackers or be retaliated by violators.
**Tools and scale:** The security analysis and simulation results show that our scheme is secure and effective in the intelligent transportation system.
**Results:** Aggregation signature verification improves message authentication efficiency. The security analysis and simulation results show that our scheme is secure and effective in the intelligent transportation system.
