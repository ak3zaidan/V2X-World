# IntelliMD: A Hybrid Approach for Local Misbehaviour Detection in Cooperative Intelligent Transport Systems
Mohamed Ahzam Amanullah, Mohan Baruwal Chhetri, Seng W. Loke, Robin Doss · Proceedings of the Sixth Workshop on CPS&amp;IoT Security and Privacy 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3690134.3694817 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Local MBD for BSM/CAM/VAM.
**Approach:** IntelliMD: rule checks plus unsupervised ML.
**Tools and scale:** Raspberry Pi OBU emulation.
**Results:** 98.14% accuracy at scale.
