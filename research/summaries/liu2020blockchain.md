# A Blockchain-Based Trust Management With Conditional Privacy-Preserving Announcement Scheme for VANETs
Xingchen Liu, Haiping Huang, Fu Xiao, Ziyang Ma · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2019.2957421 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As the infrastructure of the intelligent transportation system, vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) have greatly improved traffic efficiency. **Approach:** In this article, a blockchain-based trust management model, combined with conditional privacy-preserving announcement scheme (BTCPS), is proposed for VANETs.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** As the infrastructure of the intelligent transportation system, vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) have greatly improved traffic efficiency. In addition, BTCPS also achieves conditional privacy since trusted authority can trace malicious vehicles’ identities in anonymous announcements with the related public addresses.
