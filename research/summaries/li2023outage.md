# Outage performance prediction of cooperative vehicle network based on sparrow search algorithm based on back‐propagation neural network
Ya Li, Yu Zhang, Xinji Tian, Ruipeng Liu · IET Networks 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1049/ntw2.12100 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Reliable links in a cooperative MIMO vehicular network with relays.
**Approach:** Closed-form outage probability for HDAF relaying with antenna selection, plus an SSA-optimised back-propagation network to predict outage.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical plus simulation; 6G IoV setting.
**Results:** Cascade order of channels worsens outage; SSA-BPNN prediction accuracy 64.8% higher than BPNN.
