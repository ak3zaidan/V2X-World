# On the Convergence of Large Language Model Optimizer for Black-Box Network Management
Hoon Lee, Wentao Zhou, Merouane Debbah, Inkyu Lee · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02689 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** LLM optimisers lack theory.
**Approach:** Markov-chain interpretation proving convergence of LLMO.
**Tools and scale:** Theory.
**Results:** Convergence proven, extended to multiple LLMs.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Prior studies limited to numerical simulations.
