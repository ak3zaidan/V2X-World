# Intelligent Hierarchical Intrusion Detection System for VANETs
Fábio Gonçalves, Joaquim Macedo, Alexandre Hugo Ribeiro dos Santos · International Congress on Ultra Modern Telecommunications and Control Systems and Workshops (ICUMT) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icumt54235.2021.9631589 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANETs) allow road entities to communicate with each other and with the infrastructure, enabling the development of more complex scenarios and applications. However, due to their nature and intrinsic characteristics, they are very attractive for attackers.
**Approach:** This paper proposes an Intelligent Hierarchical Intrusion Detection System (IDS) that divides the network into four levels and, each of them, into multiple clusters, enabling the usage of different Machine Learning (ML) based detection techniques.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** However, these did not originate from real-world data but from simulation.
