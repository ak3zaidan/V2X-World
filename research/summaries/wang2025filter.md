# Filter-and-Attend: Wireless Channel Foundation Model with Noise-Plus-Interference Suppression Structure
Yu-Wei Wang, Li Sun, Tingting Yang, Yuxuan Shi et al. · venue unknown 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.15993 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Wireless channel foundation model (WCFM) is a task-agnostic AI model that is pre-trained to learn a universal channel representation for a wide range of communications and sensing tasks. While existing works on WCFM have demonstrated its great potentials in various downstream tasks, the models are all trained using perfect (i.e., error-free and complete) channel information state (CSI) data.
**Approach:** To address this issue, this paper proposes a new paradigm for WCFM, termed as Filter-and-Attend.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: real-world. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Simulation results demonstrated that compared to the state-of-the-art solutions, WCFM with NPI suppression structure achieves improved performance on various downstream tasks including time-domain channel prediction, frequency-domain channel prediction, and localization.
