# A Fuzzy Reputation System in Vehicular Ad hoc Networks
Mohammad Jalali, Nasser Ghasem Aghaee · Procedia Computer Science 2011 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.procs.2011.07.134 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANET) rely on the cooperation of the nodes to forward packets for each other. Some nodes may decide not to cooperate to save their resource while still using network resources.
**Approach:** To address this problem, we propose a fuzzy reputation system to discipline selfish behavior and encourage packet forwarding. The simulation results have shown that the proposed scheme can successfully increase network performance.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset, fuzzy logic (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** The simulation results have shown that the proposed scheme can successfully increase network performance.
