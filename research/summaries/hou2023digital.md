# Digital Twin based Packet Reception Prediction for C-V2X Networks
Yun Hou, Zhi Zhang, Weizong Li, Man Ho Fan, Calvin Lam · 2023 IEEE 98th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2023-Fall) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2023-fall60731.2023.10333667 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** C-V2X transmitters cannot estimate broadcast reception.
**Approach:** Transmitter-side packet reception rate predictor trained on DT-generated data.
**Tools and scale:** City digital twins.
**Results:** R^2 up to 0.92 same city; 0.90 transferred to another city.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - learned C-V2X reception surrogate from DT data.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on security.
