# A Digital Twin Approach for Self-optimization of Mobile Networks
Juan Deng, Qingbi Zheng, Guangyi Liu, Jielin Bai et al. · 2021 IEEE Wireless Communications and Networking Conference Workshops (WCNCW) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wcncw49093.2021.9420037 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Most of the methods in operators' current 5G networks use expert knowledge assisted by machine learning algorithms to generate optimization decisions. However, these methods are inadaptive to the dynamic changes of high-dimensional network states, thus the result is often suboptimal.
**Approach:** To solve these problems, this paper proposes a combined approach of expert knowledge, reinforcement learning and digital twin for the self-optimization of mobile networks. This paper also describes several potential application scenarios for the proposed approach in 6G networks and discusses key issues for future research.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G, 6G, reinforcement learning, digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Simulation results have confirmed that the proposed scheme can achieve higher rewards than either expert knowledge or reinforcement learning, and can avoid negative impact on real network performance.
