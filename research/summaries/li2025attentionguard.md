# AttentionGuard: Transformer-based Misbehavior Detection for Secure Vehicular Platoons
Hexu Li, Konstantinos Kalogiannis, Ahmed Mohamed Hussain, Panos Papadimitratos · WiseML@WiSec 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3733965.3733966 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Authenticated insiders' falsification attacks on platoons.
**Approach:** Multi-head transformer-encoder MDS on kinematic sequences across steady state, join and exit.
**Tools and scale:** Extensive platoon simulation dataset (constant, gradual, combined falsifications; controller types, speeds, attacker positions).
**Results:** Up to 0.95 F1; effective at 100 ms decision intervals.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: more network attacks in platooning and optimisation for resource-constrained devices.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — fast insider MBD in platoons.
**Novelty implications:** Little new for our directions.
