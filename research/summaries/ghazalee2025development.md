# Development of Threats and Vulnerability Assessment Model for Electric Vehicles (EVs) Transportation Internet of Things (T-IoT)
Nurina Zulaikha Ghazalee, Raja Zahilah Raja Mohd Radzi · International Journal of Innovative Computing 2025 · https://doi.org/10.11113/ijic.v15n2.547 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The paper describes the proposal of a model for threats and vulnerability assessment for Electric Vehicles (EVs) in the Transportation Internet of Things (T-IoT) model.
**Approach:** EV systems are becoming more and more reliant on IoT devices and systems that encounter many security challenges. This reliance on many interconnected devices, sensors, and communication networks creates vulnerabilities where cyberattacks, such as hacking and data breaches, or manipulations can stem as entry points to EVs.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: real-world; scale not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Ultimately the study details a model to provide systematic attack scenario analysis and models for vulnerability assessment to provide a solid framework of identification and risk reduction to EV security and reliability in the future of transportation networks. The model developed in this study demonstrates improved capability in predicting, identifying, and mitigating cyber threats targeting electric vehicle (EV) systems.
