# Cooperative Adaptive Cruise Control for Vehicle Platoon Switching Topology under Mismatched Disturbances
Zhengfang Wang, Aobei Shen, Zhifei Wu · Unknown venue 2026 · https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6850161 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle platoon control is pivotal for enhancing traffic efficiency and safety.
**Approach:** Still, the coupled effects of dynamic communication topology switching (e.g., bidirectional (BD) and predecessor-following (PF) modes) and mismatched disturbances significantly compromise platoon stability. This paper proposes a cooperative adaptive cruise control framework integrating a finite-time disturbance observer (FTDO) and coupled sliding mode control (CSMC).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Simulation results demonstrate a 92.7% reduction in spacing error RMSE under significant sinusoidal disturbances (amplitude: 0.5 m/s) compared to fixed PF control.
