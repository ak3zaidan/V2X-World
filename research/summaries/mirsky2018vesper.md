# Vesper: Using Echo-Analysis to Detect Man-in-the-Middle Attacks in LANs
Yisroel Mirsky, Naor Kalbo, Yuval Elovici, Asaf Shabtai · arXiv 2018 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.02560 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) attack is a cyber-attack in which an attacker intercepts traffic, thus harming the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the network.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose Vesper: a novel plug-and-play MitM detector for local area networks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** We also investigate several possible adversarial attacks against Vesper, and demonstrate how Vesper mitigates these attacks.
