# Intrusion Detection Systems Based on Stacking Ensemble Learning in VANET
Mahshid Behravan, Ning Zhang, Arunita Jaekel, Marc Kneppers · International Conference on Communications, Signal Processing, and their Applications (ICCSPA) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccspa55860.2022.10019171 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad-hoc network (VANET) will play an important role in improving driving safety and efficiency in transport systems.
**Approach:** As various attacks arise in VANET, it is essential to design mechanisms that can detect these attacks and then mitigate them. In this paper, we make an effort to detect five different position falsification attacks in VANET, including constant attack, constant offset attack, random attack, random offset attack, and eventual stop attack.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: VEREMI, VeReMi
**Results:** Extensive experimental results are provided to evaluate the proposed solutions' effectiveness. Based on our results, stacking ensemble learning for classification algorithm can achieve the best performance in terms of accuracy and recall.
