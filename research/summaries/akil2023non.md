# Non-Interactive Privacy-Preserving Sybil-Free Authentication Scheme in VANETs
Mahdi Akil, Leonardo A. Martucci, Jaap-Henk Hoepman · venue unknown 2023 · https://doi.org/10.14722/vehiclesec.2023.23007 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** Pseudonym schemes either need infrastructure or allow concurrent pseudonyms (Sybil).
**Approach:** Attribute-based credentials (CL signatures, ZKPs) with epoch-bound self-generated pseudonyms; one-time registration.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical; cost in exponentiations.
**Results:** Sybil-free anonymous authentication without infrastructure (security argument).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Notes infrastructure-based schemes need RSUs, CRLs via RSUs and pseudonym reuse in rural areas.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Alternative to SCMS pseudonym provisioning; our engine models SCMS top-ups and CRL delivery.
**Novelty implications:** Alternative non-SCMS design; comparative simulation against SCMS open.
