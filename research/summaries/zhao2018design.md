# Design and Formal Verification of a VANET Lightweight Authentication Protocol
Guolei Zhao, Ruinyun Wang, Xuejian Wang, Xianwei Zhu · 2018 IEEE 18th International Conference on Communication Technology (ICCT) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icct.2018.8600064 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A lightweight authentication and key distribution protocol for VANET (Vehicle ad-hoc Network) was proposed by reducing the overload of network communication and vehicle gateway calculation.
**Approach:** Applicability analysis and simulation show that proposed protocol has the advantage in the aspect of calculation and access time, and it fits VANET well.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** Session key was generated and distributed by cloud server to reduce times of data switch and calculation complexity of vehicle gateway.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
