# Distinguishing Sensor Faults and Attacks in Connected Vehicle Networks Using Two-Step Verification
Heena Rathore, Pranay Chowdary Jasti, Henry Griffith · 2023 Eighth International Conference On Mobile And Secure Services (MobiSecServ) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mobisecserv58080.2023.10329092 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As the reliance on vehicle-to-vehicle communication within transportation networks increases, the risk of cyber attacks is an escalating concern.
**Approach:** In addition to attacks, faulty sensors within vehicles can also lead to transportation network failures. To address these challenges, this research proposes a two-step process for identifying and classifying malicious and faulty vehicles within a network using sequential reputation estimation and validation.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Simulation results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed approach in accurately detecting and distinguishing faulty and malicious vehicles.
