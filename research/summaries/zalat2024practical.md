# A Practical Network Digital Twin for IGP Weight Optimization
Mohamed Zalat, Maede Davoudzade, Chris Barber, David Krauss et al. · 2024 20th International Conference on Network and Service Management (CNSM) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.23919/cnsm62983.2024.10814633 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Network digital twins mostly predict in simulation rather than monitoring and optimising a physical network.
**Approach:** Online-learning NDT that monitors a physical network and applies IGP weight configurations, detecting poorly trained models.
**Tools and scale:** Physical small-network testbed.
**Results:** NDT optimised the testbed; fine-tuning on high-traffic scenarios improved performance.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Evaluated on a small network only.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — wired IGP twin.
**Novelty implications:** Shows learned twins with drift handling exist in wired networking; not V2X.
