# High-Fidelity Propagation-Centric Digital Twin With Admissible-Space Filtering for Indoor Wireless Coverage Optimization
Hussein Hijazi, Md. Zoheb Hassan, N. Hakem et al. · IEEE Open Journal of the Communications Society 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ojcoms.2026.3728274 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Maximizing indoor wireless coverage in underground and industrial facilities is challeng-ing due to severe blockage, scattering, and highly non-uniform attenuation caused by heterogeneous materials and complex geometric layouts.
**Approach:** In this work, we present SCOPE-DT (Spatial Coverage Optimization Enabled by Digital Twin), a DT-empowered, spatially consistent, cost-effective, and computationally efficient framework for maximizing wireless coverage in challenging indoor environments, built entirely on open-source tools. To address the challenge of optimizing AP placement over geometrically irregular and non-convex indoor layouts, a multi-stage spatial filtering approach is introduced that refines the feasible search space by excluding physically infeasible candidate AP locations and suppressing artificially inflated coverage contributions from geometrically inconsistent grid cells.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Extensive simulations and experiments demonstrate that SCOPE-DT (a) achieves superior coverage accuracy compared with standard 3GPP and COST Multi-Wall models, and (b) significantly improves both the optimality and convergence of meta-heuristic AP-placement algorithms when multi-stage spatial filtering is applied.
