# TEAM: A Trust Evaluation and Management Framework in Context-Enabled Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks
Farhan Ahmad, Virginia N. L. Franqueira, Asma Adnane · IEEE Access 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2018.2837887 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad-hoc network (VANET) provides a unique platform for vehicles to intelligently exchange critical information, such as collision avoidance messages. It is, therefore, paramount that this information remains reliable and authentic, i.e., originated from a legitimate and trusted vehicle.
**Approach:** To this end, we propose a novel trust evaluation and management (TEAM) framework, which serves as a unique paradigm for the design, management, and evaluation of TMs in various contexts and in presence of malicious vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The TEAM has been built using VEINS, an open source simulation environment which incorporates SUMO traffic simulator and OMNET++ discrete event simulator. Results indicate that the TEAM is effective to simulate a wide range of TMs, where the efficiency is evaluated against different quality of service and security-related criteria.
