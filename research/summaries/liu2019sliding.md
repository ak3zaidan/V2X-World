# Sliding mode based platoon control and simulation for a group of nonlinear vehicles in PLEXE
Xiaomin Liu, Maode Yan, Lei Zuo, Wenrui Ma · 2019 Chinese Control Conference (CCC) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.23919/chicc.2019.8866428 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we investigate the platoon control problems for a group of nonlinear vehicles, where both the model uncertainties and external disturbances are taken into consideration.
**Approach:** To converge the vehicles to the desired platoon, a novel control scheme is proposed based on the consensus mechanism and sliding mode techniques. The stability of proposed platoon control system is guaranteed by using the Lyapunov stability theorem.
