# A Comprehensive Survey of V2X Cybersecurity Mechanisms and Future Research Paths
Roshan Sedar, Charalampos Kalalas, Francisco Vázquez-Gallego, Luis Alonso et al. · IEEE Open Journal of the Communications Society 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ojcoms.2023.3239115 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Recent advancements in vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communication have notably improved existing transport systems by enabling increased connectivity and driving autonomy levels. The remarkable benefits of V2X connectivity come inadvertently with challenges which involve security vulnerabilities and breaches.
**Approach:** This paper surveys current literature on V2X security and provides a systematic and comprehensive review of the most relevant security enhancements to date. In addition, this paper delves into the potential of emerging security approaches leveraging artificial intelligence tools to meet security objectives.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting (VANET/IoV/V2X). Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
