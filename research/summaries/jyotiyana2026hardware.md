# A Hardware Implementation of Blockchain Enabled Cross Domain Authentication Scheme in Internet of Vehicles
Priya Jyotiyana, Jayaprakash Kar · Proceedings of the 23rd International Conference on Security and Cryptography 2026 · https://doi.org/10.5220/0015054500004103 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cross-domain vehicle authentication.
**Approach:** Ethereum smart contracts with TA and KGC; prototype with Wi-Fi router RSU, Raspberry Pi 5 servers, ESP32 vehicle nodes, Hardhat. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** 100-500 vehicles, 2-10 domains (hardware prototype).
**Results:** PDR 100%, latency 49-206 ms, throughput 391-723 requests/s.
