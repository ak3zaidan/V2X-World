# Analysis of Preference for Autonomous Driving Under Different Traffic Conditions Using a Driving Simulator
Udara Eshan Manawadu, , Masaaki Ishikawa, Mitsuhiro Kamezaki et al. · Journal of Robotics and Mechatronics 2015 · https://doi.org/10.20965/jrm.2015.p0660 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** <div class=""abs_img""><img src=""[disp_template_path]/JRM/abst-image/00270006/08.jpg"" width=""300"" /> Driving simulator</div>Intelligent passenger vehicles with autonomous capabilities will be commonplace on our roads in the near future.
**Approach:** This paper documents a driving simulator-based study conducted to identify the preferences and individual driving experiences of novice and experienced drivers of autonomous and conventional vehicles under different traffic and road conditions.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The results showed that experienced drivers opt for conventional driving overall, mainly due to the flexibility and driving pleasure it offers, while novices tend to prefer autonomous driving due to its inherent ease and safety.
