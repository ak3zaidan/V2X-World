# From TARA to Test: Automated Automotive Cybersecurity Test Generation Out of Threat Modeling
Stefan F Marksteiner, Christoph Schmittner, Korbinian Christl, Dejan Nickovic et al. · Proceedings of the 7th ACM Computer Science in Cars Symposium 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3631204.3631864 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract only

**Problem:** Automotive security testing is not automated enough to meet UNECE regulation.
**Approach:** Converts TARA threat models into attack trees labelled with a DSL, producing labelled transition systems and test scenarios.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Methodology for automated test generation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — partial: TARA-to-attack automation; we execute attacks in simulation and could close the TARA loop quantitatively.
**Novelty implications:** TARA-to-test generation exists; TARA-to-simulated-impact quantification is still open.
