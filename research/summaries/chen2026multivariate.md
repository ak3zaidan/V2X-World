# Multivariate Cryptography-Based Anonymous Certificate Scheme
Abel C. H. Chen · arXiv.org 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13554 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As quantum computing technology continues to mature, the US National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) has outlined a migration timeline for Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC), recommending the deprecation of certain elliptic curve cryptography (ECC) by 2030. Furthermore, privacy-sensitive application scenarios, such as vehicular communications, require the use of anonymous certificates.
**Approach:** Therefore, this study proposes a multivariate cryptography-based anonymous certificate scheme, aiming to design quantum-safe anonymous certificates suitable for privacy-sensitive application services.
