# Fuzzy Clustering for Misbehaviour Detection in VANET
Hanane Amirat, Naserddine Lagraa, Chaker Abdelaziz Kerrach, Youcef Ouinten · 2018 International Conference on Smart Communications in Network Technologies (SaCoNeT) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/saconet.2018.8585454 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Selective forwarding attackers in GPSR routing.
**Approach:** Fuzzy C-means clustering on trace-file features with tuned threshold.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** 87% accuracy with low false positives at 25% attackers.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Preliminary results.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
