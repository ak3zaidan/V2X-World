# Modeling Sybil attacker behavior in VANETs
Parastoo Kafil, Mahmoud Fathy, ‪Mina Zolfy Lighvan · International ISC Conference on Information Security and Cryptology (ISCISC) 2012 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iscisc.2012.6408215 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Modelling Sybil attacker behaviour in realistic traffic.
**Approach:** Attack models for an individual Sybil attacker under urban vs highway traffic, near source vs near destination.
**Tools and scale:** Traffic plus network simulation.
**Results:** Most serious threat is forged warnings near the packet source due to hop count.
