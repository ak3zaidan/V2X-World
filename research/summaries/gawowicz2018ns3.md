# ns3-gym: Extending OpenAI Gym for Networking Research
Piotr Gawłowicz, Anatolij Zubow · arXiv 2018 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03943 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** No integration of RL frameworks with ns-3.
**Approach:** ns3-gym: OpenAI Gym interface for ns-3.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3; two examples; open source GPL.
**Results:** Framework released.

- Not stated in the abstract.
