# Cooperative Awareness Messages’ Generation Frequencies, Trigger Distributions, and Pseudonym Changes of First Commercially Deployed Vehicles in Real Operating Scenarios
Maximilian Bauder, Tibor Kubjatko, Hans-Georg Schweiger · IEEE Access 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2023.3292576 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Real CAM generation behaviour of deployed vehicles for accident analysis.
**Approach:** Measured CAM frequencies, triggers and pseudonym changes of commercial EU vehicles (Golf 8, ID.3) in traffic and parking.
**Tools and scale:** Real commercially deployed vehicles; real traffic.
**Results:** Average CAM generation frequency exceeds the 2 Hz EDR recording rate; pseudonym change behaviour analysed.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — real-world pseudonym change and CAM data useful to validate our engine's ETSI behaviour.
**Novelty implications:** Ground-truth reference for deployed behaviour; no novelty conflict.
