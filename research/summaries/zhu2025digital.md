# Digital Twin Empowered In-Vehicular Channel Modeling and Wireless Planning in the Terahertz Band
Mingjie Zhu, Yejian Lyu, Chong Han · arXiv 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/twc.2026.3742409 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** In-vehicle THz channel characterisation.
**Approach:** VNA measurements at 300 GHz, vehicle DT from point clouds in Sionna ray tracing, hybrid RT-statistical model; wireless planning.
**Tools and scale:** Sionna RT, measurements.
**Results:** Strong agreement with measurement; Tx under the ceiling best for single Tx; two Tx best for coverage.
