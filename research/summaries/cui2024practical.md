# A Practical and Provably Secure Authentication and Key Agreement Scheme for UAV-Assisted VANETs for Emergency Rescue
Jie Cui, Xinzhong Liu, Hong Zhong, Jing Zhang et al. · IEEE Transactions on Network Science and Engineering 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnse.2023.3323972 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** However, most existing two-factor AKA schemes cannot achieve truly two-factor security while resisting password guessing attacks effectively.
**Approach:** To address this problem, we propose a lightweight, provably secure, and practical two-factor AKA scheme for UAV-assisted VANETs based on chaotic maps by adopting techniques including fuzzy verifiers and honeywords. In this study, considering the limited computational storage of vehicles and UAVs, a hybrid generation algorithm is proposed to optimize the storage of honeywords.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** Through rigorous security proofs and detailed security analyses, we demonstrate that the scheme meets the security requirements of VANETs and can resist more security attacks.
