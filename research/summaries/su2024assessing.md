# Assessing Trustworthiness of V2X Messages: A Cooperative Trust Model Against CAM- and CPM-Based Ghost Vehicles in IoV
Runbo Su, Yujun Jin, Ye‐Qiong Song · International Conference on Vehicle Technology and Intelligent Transport Systems (VEHITS) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.5220/0012605200003702 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Ghost vehicles via CAM/CPM by authenticated insiders.
**Approach:** Cooperative trust model against CAM- and CPM-based ghost vehicles; CPS integrated in Veins.
**Tools and scale:** Veins.
**Results:** Preliminary feasibility results.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Results are preliminary.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — CPM-based attacks with trust; we simulate attacks in closed loop.
**Novelty implications:** CPM ghost attacks with trust exist in Veins.
