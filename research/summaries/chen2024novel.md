# A Novel Fuzzy Trust-Based Secure Vehicular Data Forwarding Scheme Using Incentive Consensus
Tieming Chen, Zechen Liu, Yinglong Li, Tinghao Chen, Qingyan Jiang, Jiahui Li · 2024 IEEE International Conference on Fuzzy Systems (FUZZ-IEEE) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/fuzz-ieee60900.2024.10611811 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Secure and efficient data forwarding is the basic infrastructure for edge services in vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs).
**Approach:** Existing vehicular data transmission methods such as multi-criteria decision-making usually have problems such as complex computation and high communication costs, as well as privacy leakage. To this end, a novel fuzzy trust-based VANET data forwarding scheme using incentive consensus is proposed.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: blockchain
**Results:** To begin with, the fuzzy trust of each Candidate Relay Vehicle (CRV) is obtained based on both the historical forwarding records and the real-time forwarding capabilities of CRVs to achieve privacy protection. Experimental results show that our scheme outperforms the state-of-the-art methods in terms of packet loss rate and real-time performance.
