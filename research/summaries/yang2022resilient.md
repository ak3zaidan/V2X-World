# Resilient Recovery for In-vehicle Sensor Attack by LSTM
yun yang · preprint 2022 · https://doi.org/10.36227/techrxiv.19444673.v1 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Uncertain cyber-attack detection and effective recovery after attacks on in-vehicle sensors are a challenge in the domain of the Internet of Vehicles (IoV).
**Approach:** This paper is concerned with the resilient recovery after attacking the in-vehicle reference-free sensor. The proposed CNN-LSTM model can obtain 95% testing accuracy, which is better than 56% for CNN and 79% for LSTM in detecting attacks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The predicting experimental results support that the LSTM is suitable for recovering signals of in-vehicle sensors.
