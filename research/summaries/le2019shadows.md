# Shadows Don't Lie: n-Sequence Trajectory Inspection for Misbehaviour Detection and Classification in VANETs
Anhtuan Le, Carsten Maple · 2019 IEEE 90th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2019-Fall) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtcfall.2019.8891137 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Detect and classify misbehaviour from short location sequences.
**Approach:** Three features from n consecutive locations, used with k-NN and SVM.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi dataset.
**Results:** Precision up to 99.7%, recall >99%, confusion <4.7% across attack types; works after 3 observations.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — fast VeReMi MBD.
**Novelty implications:** Few-observation detection exists; detection latency to revocation remains.
