# Enhanced Ensemble Temporal Convolutional Network With Genetic Algorithm Optimization for Anomaly Detection in CAVs
Elvin Ugonna Eziama, Omar Abou Kasm, Mahreen Nasir · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2026.3688433 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Malicious attacks on Connected and Automated Vehicle (CAV) systems can severely impact transportation safety and efficiency.
**Approach:** They can manipulate sensor readings, disrupt service, or falsify environmental perceptions, leading to incorrect decisions and potential catastrophes. Detecting such anomalies is complex due to challenges such as false positives and negatives, sensor interference, resource constraints, and diverse types of anomalies.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: VEREMI, VeReMi
**Results:** Recent machine learning and hybrid methods improve on traditional techniques but struggle with frequency bias and time scale selection, limiting real-time applications in CAVs networks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Detecting such anomalies is complex due to challenges such as false positives and negatives, sensor interference, resource constraints, and diverse types of anomalies.
- To address these challenges, we propose ET-DRF-GA, a novel method that combines an ensemble-based exploration of Temporal Convolutional Networks (TCN), Differential Receptive Fields (DRF), and Genetic Algorithms (GA).
- Experiments on benchmark datasets, Safety Pilot Model Deployment (SPMD) and Vehicular Reference Misbehavior (VeReMi), demonstrate the method’s robustness and effectiveness in anomaly detection for CAV systems, paving the way for future research in this critical domain.
