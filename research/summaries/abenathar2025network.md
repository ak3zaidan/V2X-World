# Network Digital Twin for Route Optimization in 5G/B5G Transport Slicing with What-If Analysis
Rebecca Aben-Athar, Heitor Anglada, Lucas Costa, João Albuquerque, Abrahão Ferreira, Cristiano Bonato Both et al. · 2025 IEEE International Conference on Communications Workshops (ICC Workshops) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccworkshops67674.2025.11162404 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Route optimisation in 5G transport slicing.
**Approach:** GNN-based NDT with what-if recommendation system.
**Tools and scale:** Topologies of 8, 16 and 30 nodes.
**Results:** Low MAPE for URLLC and eMBB slice latency predictions.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: compare algorithms under congestion and variable traffic, include response time, precompute what-if analyses.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
