# Prediction of Packet Inter-Reception Time for Platooning using Conditional Exponential Distribution
Guillaume Jornod, Ahmad El Assaad, Andreas Kwoczek, Thomas Kurner · 2019 16th International Symposium on Wireless Communication Systems (ISWCS) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iswcs.2019.8877231 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Predicting packet inter-reception time for platooning.
**Approach:** Conditional exponential distribution with MLP-estimated rate from inter-antenna distance and spatial distribution.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3 and SUMO.
**Results:** Better than prior discrete model; online prediction example.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Further deployment and other scenarios needed.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
