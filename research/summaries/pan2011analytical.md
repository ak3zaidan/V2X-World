# An Analytical Model for Random Changing Pseudonyms Scheme in VANETs
Yuanyuan Pan, Jianqing Li, Li Feng, Ben Xu · 2011 International Conference on Network Computing and Information Security 2011 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ncis.2011.127 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Random changing pseudonyms not rigorously quantified.
**Approach:** Analytical model for RCP comparing uniform and age-based distributions.
**Tools and scale:** Analysis verified by simulation.
**Results:** Uniform discrete distribution better than age-based.
