# A Conditional Privacy-Preserving Certificateless Aggregate Signature Scheme in the Standard Model for VANETs
Hui-Wen Wang, Liangliang Wang, Kai Zhang, Jin-Guo Li et al. · IEEE Access 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2022.3147595 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) are the communication foundation for future intelligent transportation systems and guarantee safe driving of intelligent networked vehicles. Moreover, VANETs face a series of security challenges related to the protection of vehicle privacy, authenticity of transmitted information, and bandwidth limitations.
**Approach:** To resolve these contradictions, many certificateless aggregate signature (CLAS) schemes have been proposed.
**Tools and scale:** Meanwhile, the security of previous CLAS schemes in VANETs is only formally provided in the random oracle model (ROM), which might be insecure in actual implementation.
**Results:** According to the formal security proofs under the computational Diffie-Hellman problem (CDHP) given in the standard model (SM), the security level of this scheme is higher than that of other CLAS schemes under ROM in practical applications. The performance analysis shows that this scheme has a higher efficiency in terms of computation and communication overhead compared with several previous CLAS schemes.
