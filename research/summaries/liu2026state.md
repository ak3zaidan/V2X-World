# A State-Assisted Authentication and Key Agreement Scheme for Lightweight Multi-RSU Access in VANETs
Zhengze Liu, Nianmin Yao, Shengyuan Bai, Qi-Bin Li · Future Internet 2026 · https://doi.org/10.3390/fi18060292 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** However, repeated full authentication and key agreement for each new RSU access impose considerable computational and communication overhead.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a state-assisted privacy-preserving mutual authentication and key agreement scheme for lightweight multi-RSU access in VANETs. The proposed scheme consists of initial and subsequent authentication phases.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** Performance evaluation shows that it reduces the computational cost of subsequent authentication by more than 90% while maintaining low communication overhead.

- None stated in the abstract.

**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
