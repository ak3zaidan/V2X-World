# CyberSOCEval: Benchmarking LLMs Capabilities for Malware Analysis and Threat Intelligence Reasoning
Lauren Deason, Adam Bali, Ciprian Bejean, Diana Bolocan, James Crnkovich, Ioana Croitoru et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.20166 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** LLM evaluations do not cover realistic SOC scenarios.
**Approach:** CyberSOCEval benchmarks (malware analysis, threat intelligence reasoning) within CyberSecEval 4.
**Tools and scale:** Range of popular LLMs.
**Results:** Larger, newer models perform better; reasoning models with test-time scaling (finding truncated).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Discusses failure modes, outlier models and filtering of telemetry; future AI security evaluation needs.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - LLM security operations benchmark; not V2X.
**Novelty implications:** LLM SOC benchmarks exist; V2X-specific security-operations agents remain open.
