# An Efficient Intrusion Detection System Using Domain‐Aware Meta‐Learning with Adapter‐Based Few‐Shot Adaptation in Vehicular Ad‐Hoc Networks (VANETs)
Abuzar Khan, Ahmad Junaid, Ghassan Husnain, Khalid J. Alzahrani, Hend Khalid Alkahtani · IET Intelligent Transport Systems 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1049/itr2.70182 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Adapting IDS to new environments with little data.
**Approach:** Domain-aware reptile meta-learning with topology-aware encoder and adapter modules.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi (22 million records).
**Results:** Average AUC 0.70, F1 0.62; adapts in under 1 s.
