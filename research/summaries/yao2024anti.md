# Anti-Jamming Technique for IRS Aided JRC System in Mobile Vehicular Networks
Yu Yao, Bolin Zhao, Junhui Zhao, Feng Shu, Yuanyuan Wu, Xu Cheng · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2024.3384038 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Undesired jamming launched by malicious jammers can attack authorized communications, which is viewed as one of the critical challenges in vehicular networks. In this paper, in order to handle the problem, anti-jamming communication driven by reinforcement learning is studied in intelligent reflecting surface (IRS)-aided vehicular networks.
