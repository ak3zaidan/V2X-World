# Personalized Federated Learning for Generative AI Empowered Digital Twin Networks
Dongzi Jin, Yong Xiao, Yingyu Li, Guangming Shi · IEEE Transactions on Network Science and Engineering 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnse.2026.3655123 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) has emerged as a fundamental building block for Digital Twin Networks (DTNs) due to its advantage in synthesizing realistic data and modeling complex system behaviors in real time. However, training high-fidelity GenAI models for DTN while fulfilling latency constraints is challenging, and the presence of data heterogeneity further complicates this task.
**Approach:** To address this challenge, we propose a novel framework, termed Personalized Federated Generative Adversarial Network (PFGAN), which trains a high-fidelity model tailored to each client rather than relying on a shared global model. To improve model fidelity under data heterogeneity, we formulate an optimization problem that minimizes both training and generalization errors while satisfying the latency constraint.

**Results:** Experimental results on three datasets show that PFGAN generates more accurate data and achieves higher training efficiency than the baselines.
