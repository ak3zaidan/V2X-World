# Methodology and Performance Assessment of Three-Dimensional Vehicular Ad-Hoc Network Simulation
Alexander Brummer, Reinhard German, Anatoli Djanatliev · IEEE Access 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2023.3264668 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** 3D aspects of traffic are ignored in VANET simulation.
**Approach:** Veins 3D: environmental diffraction, n-ray ground interference, multi-floor communication, and a per-packet model selector; performance optimisations.
**Tools and scale:** Veins 3D; large urban reference scenario.
**Results:** Results differ significantly from 2D; execution times strongly increased, but optimisations bring them to the same order of magnitude as 2D.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Strongly increased execution times without optimisation.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — PHY realism in Veins, which we match.
**Novelty implications:** 3D-aware propagation in Veins exists.
