# Quantum-Resilient Secure Context-Aware Trust-Based Routing with Preemptive Verifiable Key Handover for Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
A. N., R. Nagaraj · International Journal of Environmental Science 2025 · https://doi.org/10.64252/3nxtyh78 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Trust-based VANET routing with PQ key handover.
**Approach:** QR-SCTR-PVKH: multi-dimensional trust plus LWE key handover.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO-integrated NS-3.
**Results:** PDR +23.7%, delay -31.2%, attack detection 89.4% (abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Existing evaluations focus on limited metrics without comprehensive security analysis under diverse attacks (stated about prior work)
**Overlap with our engine:** none — Routing.
**Novelty implications:** None.
