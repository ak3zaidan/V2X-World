# Demo: An Open-Source Hardware-in-the-Loop Testbed for Post-Quantum V2V Security Research
Geoff Twardokus, Hanif Rahbari · Proceedings Symposium on Vehicle Security &amp; Privacy 2024 · https://doi.org/10.14722/vehiclesec.2024.25007 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** No open testbed exists for PQ V2V authentication research.
**Approach:** PQ-V2Verifier: open-source hardware-in-the-loop testbed integrating NIST PQC signatures into IEEE 1609.2 on open-source V2V stacks, with SDRs and optional Cohda MK6 devices, for safety use cases and attacks.
**Tools and scale:** SDR over-the-air; Cohda MK6; OpenStreetMap-based scenarios; small scale (testbed).
**Results:** Demonstration; no quantitative results in the demo text.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Demo paper; limitations not stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — PQC integrated into 1609.2 V2V and evaluated in safety use cases against attacks, which our PQ signature support and direction 2 also do (we in simulation at scale, they over the air).
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim the first PQ-in-1609.2 V2V platform; open: city-scale and SCMS-backend PQ evaluation, which a HIL testbed cannot do.
