# Evaluating the Electrification of Vehicle Fleets Using the Veins Framework
Sebastian Schellenberg, Rüdiger Berndt, Reinhard German, David Eckhoff · arXiv 2014 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.1003 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Electrifying a 100-vehicle company fleet.
**Approach:** Extends Veins with a lightweight battery model and charging infrastructure.
**Tools and scale:** Veins (SUMO).
**Results:** Can identify fleet over-dimensioning.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Incorporate wireless communication (charging station coordination, GLOSA) in future work.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
