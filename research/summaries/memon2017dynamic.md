# Dynamic Distributed Mobility Management System based on Multiple Mix-Zones over Road Networks
Imran Memon, Qasim Ali Arain · arXiv 2017 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.02252 · relevance: low · basis: full text

**Problem:** Mobility (IP handover) management with mix zones for vehicles accessing the internet.
**Approach:** Dynamic distributed mobility management over multiple mix zones.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical/simulation of handover probability and signalling cost versus speed (0-100 m/s).
**Results:** Pre-FDMM variant has the lowest handover failure probability.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None explicit beyond speed analysis.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — none.
**Novelty implications:** Nothing of our candidate directions is ruled out.
