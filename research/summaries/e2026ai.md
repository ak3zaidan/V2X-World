# An AI-Driven Trust-Aware Secure Communication Framework Using AES-256-GCM for Next-Generation Vehicular Networks
Elamaran E, S. K, Viji H et al. · 2026 International Conference on Recent Advances in Electrical, Electronics, Ubiquitous Communication, and Computational Intelligence (RAEEUCCI) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/raeeucci67649.2026.11504519 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In modern connected vehicles, safety-critical data is continuously exchanged, making cooperative driving possible and provides the foundation for intelligent transportation systems.
**Approach:** Although current Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) security solutions are highly dependent on cryptographic protection, they are still susceptible to insider attacks and malicious nodes with legitimate credentials but malicious behavior. this paper proposes an AI-powered and trust-aware security solution that combines behavioral anomaly detection, adaptive trust assessment, and authenticated encryption in a single comprehensive system.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation experiments conducted under attack conditions shows the effectiveness of our approach in detecting anomalous vehicles and enforcing trust selectively, with no performance impact on communication.
