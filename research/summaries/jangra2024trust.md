# New Trust Based Smart Data Forwarding Mechanism in Vehicular Delay Tolerant Network
Seema Jangra, Amit Kant Pandit · Journal of Machine and Computing 2024 · https://doi.org/10.53759/7669/jmc202404077 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Delay Tolerant Network (VDTN) is the growing field with a considerable possibility to handle future wireless application’s requirements.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a trust-based data forwarding mechanism for Vehicular Delay Tolerant Network (VDTN). A threshold based social skeleton membership process along with new trust-based data forwarding mechanism is proposed in this paper.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation results demonstrate that the trust-based data forwarding improves the data delivery ratio with trust-based data forwarding approach. After simulation a comparative analysis of two different scenarios is presented with epidemic routing, prophet routing and spray and wait routing.
**Results:** Simulation results demonstrate that the trust-based data forwarding improves the data delivery ratio with trust-based data forwarding approach.
