# Migrating Software Systems Toward Post-Quantum Cryptography-A Systematic Literature Review
Christian Näther, Daniel Herzinger, S. Gazdag, J. Steghöfer et al. · IEEE Access 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3450306 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Across all papers we noticed three major challenges for adopters: missing experience of PQC and a high realization effort, concerns about the security of the upcoming system, and finally, high complexity.
**Approach:** On the process side, we found that terminology, migration steps, and roles are not defined precisely or consistently across the literature. Still, we identified four major phases and appropriate substeps which we matched with also emerging archetypes of roles.
