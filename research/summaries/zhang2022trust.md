# Trust Management Framework for Misbehavior Detection in Collective Perception Services
Jiahao Zhang, I. Ben Jemaa, F. Nashashibi · International Conference on Control, Automation, Robotics and Vision 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icarcv57592.2022.10004259 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** CPM misbehaviour with contradictory data.
**Approach:** Subjective-logic trust management for CPMs; impact on safety services and risk anticipation at intersections.
**Tools and scale:** Intersection simulation.
**Results:** Effective detection; higher CPM frequency and wider sensor range improve detection and reaction time.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: perception uncertainty, more complex attacks, new road scenarios, semi-global RSU-based detection.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — links MBD configuration parameters to safety reaction time.
**Novelty implications:** CPM-level safety-reaction analysis exists.
