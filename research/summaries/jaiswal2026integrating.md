# Integrating Digital Twin Technology Into V2X Communication
Swati Jaiswal, Prachi Joshi, Spandan Surdas · Digital Twin Approaches in Autonomous Vehicles 2026 · https://doi.org/10.4018/979-8-3373-7785-8.ch011 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The fast evolution of connected cars and autonomous cars demands intelligent transportation systems that can handle high amounts of real-time data. Nevertheless, conventional Vehicle-to-Everything communication systems have weaknesses of low predictivity, latency, interoperability difficulties, and poor traffic control.
**Approach:** To mitigate these problems, this chapter examines the application of the Digital Twin technology in conjunction with V2X communication to facilitate real-time monitoring, and predictive analysis.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** DT-V2X framework provides data-driven virtual models of vehicles, road infrastructure, and communication networks.
