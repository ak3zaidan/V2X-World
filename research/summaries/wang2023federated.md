# Federated Learning-based Vehicle Trajectory Prediction against Cyberattacks
Zhe Wang, Tingkai Yan · 10.1109/LANMAN58293.2023.10189424 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/lanman58293.2023.10189424 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Faulty data and privacy in IoV trajectory prediction.
**Approach:** FL-TP federated learning for trajectory prediction and attack detection.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi with five attack types.
**Results:** Up to 6.99% better detection and 54.86% better trajectory prediction at max attack permeability vs benchmarks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future work: extensive simulations in a large-scale realistic scenario.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — offline VeReMi learning.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
