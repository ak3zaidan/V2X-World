# Wireless Link Quality Prediction in IoT Networks
Miguel Landry Foko Sindjoung, Pascale Minet · 2019 8th International Conference on Performance Evaluation and Modeling in Wired and Wireless Networks (PEMWN) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.23919/pemwn47208.2019.8986920 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The knowledge of link quality in IoT networks allows a more accurate selection of wireless links to build the routes used for data gathering. The number of retransmissions is decreased, leading to a shorter end-to-end latency, a better end-to-end reliability and a larger network lifetime.
**Approach:** We propose to predict link quality by means of machine learning techniques applied on two metrics: RSSI and PDR.
