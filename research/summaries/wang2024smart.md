# Smart Mobility Digital Twin Based Automated Vehicle Navigation System: A Proof of Concept
Kui Wang, Zongdian Li, Kazuma Nonomura, Tao Yu, Kei Sakaguchi, Omar Hashash et al. · arXiv 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tiv.2024.3368109 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Digital twins (DTs) have driven major advancements across various industrial domains over the past two decades.
**Approach:** With the rapid advancements in autonomous driving and vehicle-to-everything (V2X) technologies, integrating DTs into vehicular platforms is anticipated to further revolutionize smart mobility systems. In this paper, a new smart mobility DT (SMDT) platform is proposed for the control of connected and automated vehicles (CAVs) over next-generation wireless networks.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: SUMO; scale not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Our experimental results using SUMO-based large-scale traffic simulations show that the proposed SMDT can reduce the average travel time and the blocking probability due to unexpected traffic incidents.
