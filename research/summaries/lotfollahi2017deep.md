# Deep packet: a novel approach for encrypted traffic classification using deep learning
M. Lotfollahi, M. Jafarisiavoshani, Ramin Shirali Hossein Zade, Mohammdsadegh Saberian · Soft Computing - A Fusion of Foundations, Methodologies and Applications 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s00500-019-04030-2 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Network traffic classification has become more important with the rapid growth of Internet and online applications.
**Approach:** Numerous studies have been done on this topic which have led to many different approaches. Most of these approaches use predefined features extracted by an expert in order to classify network traffic.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: dataset; scale not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Our experiments show that the best result is achieved when Deep Packet uses CNN as its classification model where it achieves recall of 0.98 in application identification task and 0.94 in traffic categorization task. To the best of our knowledge, Deep Packet outperforms all of the proposed classification methods on UNB ISCX VPN-nonVPN dataset.
