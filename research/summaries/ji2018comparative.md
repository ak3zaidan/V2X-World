# Comparative Performance Evaluation of Intrusion Detection Methods for In-Vehicle Networks
Haojie Ji, Yunpeng Wang, Hongmao Qin, Yongjian Wang, Honggang Li · IEEE Access 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2018.2848106 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The fifth-generation (5G) technology makes it widely applicable to connected vehicles. This would entail numerous transmitted data in communication networks and frequent information interactions between vehicles and other terminals, thus leading connected vehicles to be vulnerable to attacks from external communication interfaces.
**Approach:** This paper analyzes potential security threats of 5G vehicular network and focuses on intrusion detection methods for in-vehicle networks.
