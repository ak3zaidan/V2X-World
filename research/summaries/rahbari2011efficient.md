# Efficient Detection of Sybil Attack Based on Cryptography in Vanet
Mina Rahbari, Mohammad Ali Jabreil Jamali · International Journal of Network Security & Its Applications (IJNSA), Vol.3, No.6, November 2011 2011 · https://doi.org/10.5121/ijnsa.2011.3614 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Sybil detection with a fixed key infrastructure.
**Approach:** HMAC message digests and P-224 encrypted IDs with CA-side operations.
**Tools and scale:** Matlab simulation.
**Results:** Low detection delay because most work is at the CA.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future work: improve the method to detect Sybil attacks properly and completely.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — simple scheme.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
