# Large Language Models (LLMs) as Traffic Control Systems at Urban Intersections: A New Paradigm
Sari Masri, Huthaifa I. Ashqar, Mohammed Elhenawy · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10869 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This study introduces a novel approach for traffic control systems by using Large Language Models (LLMs) as traffic controllers.
**Approach:** To evaluate LLMs ability as traffic controllers, this study proposed a four-stage methodology. GPT-mini model exhibited a promising performance in generating actionable traffic management insights, with high ROUGE-L scores across conflict identification of 0.95, decision-making of 0.91, priority assignment of 0.94, and waiting time optimization of 0.92.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Results showed that the fine-tuned GPT-mini achieved 83% accuracy and an F1-score of 0.84.
