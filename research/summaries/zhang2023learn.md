# Learn to Augment Network Simulators Towards Digital Network Twins
Yuru Zhang, Ming Zhao, Qiang Liu · IEEE INFOCOM 2024 - IEEE Conference on Computer Communications Workshops (INFOCOM WKSHPS) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/infocomwkshps61880.2024.10620840 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Pure DL network twins raise generalisation and explainability concerns.
**Approach:** Augment ns-3 with context-aware neural agents; cost-aware Bayesian optimisation to query real network, BNN to bridge sim-to-real discrepancy.
**Tools and scale:** OpenAirInterface RAN/Core with USRP B210 and smartphone; replicated in ns-3.
**Results:** More than 92% reduction in sim-to-real discrepancy.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Small-scale testbed.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - simulator augmentation for DTs.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
