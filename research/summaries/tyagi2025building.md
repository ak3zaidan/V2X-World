# Building Trust and Improve Security of Vehicle User using Modern and Effective Solutions for Accessing Services in Smart Environment
Amit Kumar Tyagi, Khushboo Tripathi, Gesu Thakur et al. · 2025 International Conference on Intelligent and Secure Engineering Solutions (CISES) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cises66934.2025.11265681 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As smart environments develop, the dimensions of connected vehicles with urban services such as smart parking, automated tolling, and EV charging raise new challenging issues in trust and security.
**Approach:** In this work, we introduce a modular and adaptive framework for providing secure identity management, real-time trust assessment, and policy-based access control using various blockchain, and AI-based mechanisms. The architecture is developed using federated identity, public key infrastructure, and a dynamic trust tensor model to provide quantitative relationship representations of vehicles, users, and infrastructure interactions.
**Tools and scale:** We employ edge-cloud storage, Zero Trust architectures, and secure communications standards (DSRC, 5G, MQTT) that provide the lowest latency possible, the best privacy, and resistance to spoofing and Sybil attacks. **Results:** The implementation based on simulators from the SUMO and Veins platform, the Hyperledger network, and smart contract framework, shows better performance results across aggregate performance metrics (e.g., 96.8% trust assessment accuracy; 12ms avg response time; 0.3% breach rate).
