# Detection of Position Falsification Attacks in VANETs Applying Trust Model and Machine Learning
Jordan Montenegro, Cristhian Iza, Mónica Aguilar Igartua · Proceedings of the 17th ACM Symposium on Performance Evaluation of Wireless Ad Hoc, Sensor, &amp; Ubiquitous Networks 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3416011.3424757 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Which trust metrics best detect fake-position attacks.
**Approach:** Machine learning evaluation of trust metric parameters.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Receiver power coherency metric has superior discriminative power for fake positions.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — cross-layer (RSSI) plausibility for position falsification.
**Novelty implications:** RSSI coherency is known as a strong feature.
