# An Efficient Inter-Vehicle Communication Framework on Road Traffic Accident Detection using OMNET++ and SUMO
Sannidhya Bajpai, Goutam Kumar Sahoo, Santos Kumar Das, Poonam Singh · 2022 IEEE 2nd International Symposium on Sustainable Energy, Signal Processing and Cyber Security (iSSSC) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/isssc56467.2022.10051410 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Inter-vehicle communication for accident detection.
**Approach:** Veins (OMNeT++ + SUMO) model with event-triggered packets at about 300 m DSRC range and 9 mW transmit power.
**Tools and scale:** Veins, DSRC, manual map.
**Results:** Proper communication observed; qualitative.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Adapt model to different incidents
- add different beacons.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
