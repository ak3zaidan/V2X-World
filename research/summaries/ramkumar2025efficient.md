# Efficient Lightweight Trust Model and Traffic-Aware Clustered Routing in Internet of Things (IoT) Guided Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks
G. Ramkumar · 2025 3rd International Conference on Intelligent Cyber Physical Systems and Internet of Things (ICoICI) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icoici65217.2025.11254646 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad hoc Network (VANETs) plays a significant part in the field of smart communication which includes safety communication and traffic awareness. Due to the huge number of devices achieving maximum accuracy and security becomes a complicated task and it is under an open research area in vehicular communication.
**Approach:** Overcome such a drawback in this article, an efficient lightweight trust model with traffic aware clustering and routing is developed in the internet based vehicular communication. This proposed model is simulated in NS2 software and the parameters which are calculated for the performance findings are total trust value, anomaly detection ratio, computation cost and communication cost calculation.
