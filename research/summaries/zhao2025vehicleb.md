# Vehicle pseudonym change mechanism based on dynamic game
Zheng Zhao, Junren Wang, Qian Mao, Qi Zhao et al. · International Conference on Mechatronics and Intelligent Control (ICMIC 2024) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3051882 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract only

**Problem:** Pseudonym change frequency vs. privacy.
**Approach:** Entropy-based user-centric privacy model; dynamic game decision for cooperative changes.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO and NS-3.
**Results:** Better privacy with fewer pseudonym changes.
