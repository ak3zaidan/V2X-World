# A Privacy-Preserving Misbehavior Detection System in Vehicular Communication Networks
Sohan Gyawali, Yi Qian, Rose Hu · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2021.3079385 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Trust/feedback scores in MDS can violate location, trajectory or identity privacy.
**Approach:** Privacy-preserving MDS: encrypted weighted feedback aggregated via additive homomorphic encryption, decrypted at a trusted authority that updates reputation.
**Tools and scale:** Security and performance (computation cost) analysis; 5G vehicular context.
**Results:** Lower computation cost than existing schemes; resilience shown analytically.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — privacy-preserving reporting to an authority (cf. MA reporting).
**Novelty implications:** Privacy-preserving report aggregation exists.
