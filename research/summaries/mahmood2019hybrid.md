# A Hybrid Trust Management Heuristic for VANETs
A. Mahmood, B. Butler, Wei Emma Zhang, Quan Z. Sheng, S. A. Siddiqui · 2019 IEEE International Conference on Pervasive Computing and Communications Workshops (PerCom Workshops) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/percomw.2019.8730675 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** State-of-the-art advances in vehicular communication have fostered the development of smart mobility platforms, where moving vehicles exchange safety-critical information with minimal latency, to improve passenger and pedestrian safety. It is, therefore, of paramount importance that the information that is exchanged is not only authentic, but its sender should be trustworthy.
**Approach:** We propose a hybrid trust management scheme to identify such malicious vehicles and to inhibit them from being elected as the cluster head.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Simulations of the proposed scheme have been conducted using MATLAB and are also presented in this manuscript.
