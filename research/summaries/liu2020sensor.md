# Sensor Fusion of Camera and Cloud Digital Twin Information for Intelligent Vehicles
Yongkang Liu, Ziran Wang, Kyungtae Han, Zhenyu Shou et al. · 2020 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iv47402.2020.9304643 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the rapid development of intelligent vehicles and Advanced Driving Assistance Systems (ADAS), a mixed level of human driver engagements is involved in the transportation system.
**Approach:** To advance the development of visual guidance systems, we introduce a novel sensor fusion methodology, integrating camera image and Digital Twin knowledge from the cloud.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** Target vehicle bounding box is drawn and matched by combining results of object detector running on ego vehicle and position information from the cloud. The best matching result, with a 79.2% accuracy under 0.7 Intersection over Union (IoU) threshold, is obtained with depth image served as an additional feature source.
