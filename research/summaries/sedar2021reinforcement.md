# Reinforcement Learning-based Misbehaviour Detection in V2X Scenarios
Roshan Sedar, Charalampos Kalalas, Francisco Vazquez-Gallego, Jesus Alonso-Zarate · 2021 IEEE International Mediterranean Conference on Communications and Networking (MeditCom) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/meditcom49071.2021.9647514 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Detecting sudden-stop attacks.
**Approach:** RL-based detection with position and speed features.
**Tools and scale:** Parts of VeReMi.
**Results:** Accurate detection; position feature generally better than speed.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Near-zero speeds of legitimate vehicles cause incorrect RL actions.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — offline.
**Novelty implications:** Saturated.
