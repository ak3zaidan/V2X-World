# Net2d-LLM: Translating Structured Network Intents into CLI using LLMs with Execution in a Network Digital Twin
Jerônimo Menezes, Leonardo Bitzki, Diego Kreutz · Anais da XXII Escola Regional de Redes de Computadores (ERRC 2025) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.5753/errc.2025.17772 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Translating structured network intents into vendor CLI.
**Approach:** Deterministic single-pass LLM pipeline from desired-state models, executed in an NDT.
**Tools and scale:** Multiple LLMs compared on latency, tokens and consistency.
**Results:** General LLMs with structured prompts produce valid, correct configurations.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — LLM configuration validated in a twin, similar in spirit to our agent.
**Novelty implications:** No V2X-specific claim affected.
