# Privacy Protection of VANET Based on Traceable Ring Signature on Ideal Lattice
Longbo Han, Suzhen Cao, Xiaodong Yang, Zhiqiang Zhang · IEEE Access 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2020.3038042 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Ring signatures lack traceability and quantum resistance.
**Approach:** Traceable ring signature on ideal lattices.
**Tools and scale:** NS-2 evaluation.
**Results:** Improved efficiency and smaller signatures.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Lattice ring signatures need further optimisation.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - PQ VANET signatures.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
