# Dynamic Defense Strategy Against DoS Attacks Over Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks Based on Port Hopping
Yingmo Jie, Mingchu Li, Cheng Guo, Ling Chen · IEEE Access 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2018.2869399 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** To fight against denial of services (DoS) attacks on vehicular ad hoc networks, which can cause congestion over networks and degrading the user's experience, a lot of detective techniques and schemes have been proposed. However, the complex ones cannot keep pace with the growth of vehicle networks.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a simple but effective defense strategy scheme inspired by the port-hopping mechanism, which advantage is manifested in that the detection and filtering off of malicious packets launched by attackers can be achieved without any change in existing protocol. First, we design a dynamic defense strategy scheme to puzzle a DoS attacker, where the specific defense strategy will change according to a scheme of time.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** At last, in comparison with the non-strategy defense scheme, simulations considering some parameters are conducted, which can show that our scheme is an effective defense scheme used for protecting VANETs.
