# RETRACTED: An efficient deep learning intrusion detection technique using Panthera Leo Hunting Optimized method for vanet
Bennet Praba, S. S. Subashka Ramesh · Journal of Intelligent &amp; Fuzzy Systems: Applications in Engineering and Technology 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3233/jifs-234401 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A unique system that offers trafﬁc management, mobility management, and proactive vulnerability identiﬁcation is the vehicular ad hoc network (VANET).
**Approach:** With the use of efﬁcient deep learning algorithms, intrusion prevention practices can improve their reliability. Many assaults, like Sybil, Blackhole, Wormhole, DoS attack, etc. expose them to risk.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: SUMO, Veins, OMNeT++
**Results:** The improved LSTM model was evaluated using actual information that had been recorded. The outcomes from the various learning models were merged with performance measures to show the algorithm’s efﬁciency and individuality.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Many assaults, like Sybil, Blackhole, Wormhole, DoS attack, etc. expose them to risk.
- The use of amazingly precise learning models to anticipate a variety of threats in VANET has not yet been thoroughly explored.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
