# Integration of C-V2X Into a Hybrid Testbed to Co-Simulate ITS Applications and Scenarios
Paul Geppert, Jossekin Beilharz · 2022 IEEE International Conference on Cloud Engineering (IC2E) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ic2e55432.2022.00009 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** End-to-end testing of ITS apps combining virtual, physical nodes and co-simulation.
**Approach:** ns-3 C-V2X integrated into Marvis hybrid testbed with SDR C-V2X and ITS-G5; digitised railroad crossing case.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3, SDR, Marvis.
**Results:** ns-3 C-V2X adjustments add only 0.8 ms (1.4%) latency.
