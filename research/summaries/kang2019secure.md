# Toward Secure Blockchain-Enabled Internet of Vehicles: Optimizing Consensus Management Using Reputation and Contract Theory
Jiawen Kang, Zehui Xiong, Dusit Niyato, Dongdong Ye et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2019.2894944 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In the Internet of Vehicles (IoV), data sharing among vehicles is critical for improving driving safety and enhancing vehicular services. To ensure security and traceability of data sharing, existing studies utilize efficient delegated proof-of-stake consensus scheme as hard security solutions to establish blockchain-enabled IoV (BIoV).
**Approach:** To address the challenge, in this paper, we propose a two-stage soft security enhancement solution: 1) miner selection and 2) block verification. In the first stage, we design a reputation-based voting scheme to ensure secure miner selection.
**Tools and scale:** Numerical results based on a real-world dataset confirm the security and efficiency of our schemes for data sharing in BIoV.
**Results:** In the Internet of Vehicles (IoV), data sharing among vehicles is critical for improving driving safety and enhancing vehicular services. Numerical results based on a real-world dataset confirm the security and efficiency of our schemes for data sharing in BIoV.
