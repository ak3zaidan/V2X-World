# Digital-Twin-Enabled Remote Operation of Autonomous Systems: An Architecture-Driven Survey
Shahriar Hasan, Muhammad Hafiz, Ashrafur Rahman Chowdhury · IEEE Sensors Reviews 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/sr.2026.3729195 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Digital twins are emerging as a key enabling technology for the remote operation of autonomous systems, offering enhanced situational awareness, predictive insight, and decision support through synchronized virtual representations of physical systems and their environments.
**Approach:** To guide future work, we propose a minimum set of evaluation metrics and a validation-maturity roadmap, and identify semantic communication, network digital twins, quantitative safety assurance, and the transition from teledriving to telesupervision as the most promising directions toward safe and efficient digital-twin-enabled remote operation.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Based on this architecture, we classify how different types of digital copies support remote driving, remote assistance, and remote monitoring; synthesize the communication technologies, requirements, and achieved performance reported across domains; and examine the roles of artificial intelligence/machine learning, human–machine interaction, network digital twins, semantic communication, security, and dependability within the loop. The findings show that communication remains a major constraint, although end-to-end responsiveness is also affected by delays incurred by, e.g., sensing, processing, reconstruction, rendering, and mobility-induced events.
