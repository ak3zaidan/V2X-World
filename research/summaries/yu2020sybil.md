# Sybil Attack Identification for Crowdsourced Navigation: A Self-Supervised Deep Learning Approach
J. J. Yu · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2020.3036085 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil attacks inject fake GPS trajectories into crowdsourced navigation to create false jams.
**Approach:** Bayesian deep generative model embedding trajectories in a latent distribution space to identify Sybil-generated trajectories; fast training scheme.
**Tools and scale:** Three real-world vehicular trajectory datasets.
**Results:** At least 76.6% improvement over state-of-the-art baselines.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — crowdsourced navigation, not V2X.
**Novelty implications:** Adjacent Sybil detection background.
