# Applying Graph Neural Network in Deep Reinforcement Learning to Optimize Wireless Network Routing
Xiao Xu, You Lu, Qiming Fu · 2021 Ninth International Conference on Advanced Cloud and Big Data (CBD) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cbd54617.2021.00045 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** At present, the traffic in wireless sensor networks (WSN) is growing at an extremely fast speed, consuming more and more network resources.
**Approach:** This undoubtedly affects the transmission performance of WSN. Good and efficient routing technology is one of the key technologies to solve this problem.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Experimental results show that GRL-NET can not only learn the best routing strategy, but also keep good results in the never-seen network topology.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Limited by the dynamic network state, traditional routing technology faces some problems such as performance degradation and lack of learning ability.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — touches learned network models but not the joint traffic/radio/SCMS closed loop.
**Novelty implications:** Nothing in our candidate directions is ruled out; useful only as background on learned network models.
