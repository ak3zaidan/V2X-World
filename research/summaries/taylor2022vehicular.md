# Vehicular Platoon Communication: Architecture, Security Threats and Open Challenges
Sean Joe Taylor, Farhan Ahmad, Hoang Nga Nguyen, Siraj Ahmed Shaikh · Sensors 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s23010134 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Platooning is vulnerable to communication attacks; a structured view of attacks, risk and defences is missing.
**Approach:** Survey of platoon architecture, attacks by attacker goal (prevent formation, access, financial, data collection, disruption), a risk assessment of identified attacks, and countermeasures.
**Tools and scale:** Literature survey with a risk rating; no simulation.
**Results:** Taxonomy and risk ranking of platoon attacks (no new experiments).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Survey
- open challenges listed for platoon security (not extracted in detail).
**Overlap with our engine:** none — survey.
**Novelty implications:** Background for platoon attack taxonomies only.
