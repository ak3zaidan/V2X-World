# Privacy-Preserving Aggregation-Authentication Scheme for Safety Warning System in Fog-Cloud Based VANET
Yafang Yang, Lei Zhang, Yunlei Zhao, Kim-Kwang Raymond Choo, Yan Zhang · IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tifs.2022.3140657 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Fog-cloud VANETs suffer bottlenecks, privacy leakage and costly pseudonym management.
**Approach:** PPAAS based on a certificateless aggregate signcryption scheme with batch unsigncryption at fog nodes.
**Tools and scale:** Security proof and simulation.
**Results:** Claims first efficient conditional privacy without pseudonym management; practical per simulation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** none - non-standard alternative to SCMS.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
