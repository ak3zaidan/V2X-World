# Adversarial Machine Learning-Based Anticipation of Threats Against Vehicle-to-Microgrid Services
A. Omara, Burak Kantarci · Global Communications Conference 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/globecom48099.2022.10000757 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we study the expanding attack surface of Adversarial Machine Learning (AML) and the potential attacks against Vehicle-to-Microgrid (V2M) services.
**Approach:** We present an anticipatory study of a multi-stage gray-box attack that can achieve a comparable result to a white-box attack. Adversaries aim to deceive the targeted Machine Learning (ML) classifier at the network edge to misclassify the incoming energy requests from microgrids.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G
**Results:** Out of six ML models trained on the complete dataset, K-Nearest Neighbour (K-NN) is selected as the surrogate model, and through simulations, we demonstrate that the multi-stage gray-box attack is able to mislead the ML classifier and cause an Evasion Increase Rate (EIR) up to 73.2% using 40% less data than what a white-box attack needs to achieve a similar EIR.
