# BAC-CRL: Blockchain-Assisted Coded Caching Certificate Revocation List for Authentication in VANETs
Junwei Liang, Muhammad Sadiq, Geng Yang, Dongsheng Cheng · Journal of Network and Computer Applications 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnca.2023.103716 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Storing and distributing full CRLs is costly.
**Approach:** BAC-CRL: blockchain unites TAs and RSUs to store CRLs; multi-layer coded caching sends minimum CRL bits across TA regions.
**Tools and scale:** JNCA; analysis/simulation.
**Results:** Reduced communication overhead and processing time (truncated).
