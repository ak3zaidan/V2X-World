# IGNNITION: Bridging the Gap between Graph Neural Networks and Networking Systems
David Pujol-Perich, Jose Suarez-Varela, Miquel Ferriol, Shihan Xiao, Bo Wu, Albert Cabellos-Aparicio et al. · IEEE Network 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mnet.001.2100266 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Implementing GNNs for networking requires strong ML skills.
**Approach:** IGNNITION open-source framework with a multi-stage message passing abstraction over TensorFlow.
**Tools and scale:** Two state-of-the-art networking GNNs re-implemented.
**Results:** Equivalent accuracy and performance to native TensorFlow implementations.
