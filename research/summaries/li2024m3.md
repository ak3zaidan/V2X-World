# m3: Accurate Flow-Level Performance Estimation using Machine Learning
Chenning Li, Arash Nasr-Esfahany, Kevin Zhao, Kimia Noorbakhsh, Prateesh Goyal, Mohammad Alizadeh et al. · Conference on Applications, Technologies, Architectures, and Protocols for Computer Communication 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3651890.3672243 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Fast accurate aggregate tail-latency estimation for data centres.
**Approach:** Path-level fluid simulation fed to an ML model, sampled over paths.
**Tools and scale:** Data-centre workloads; compared with Parsimon.
**Results:** 5.7x faster and 45.9% less error than Parsimon; generalises over congestion control and routing.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - ML-accelerated network simulation (direction 3).
**Novelty implications:** No effect on V2X claims.
