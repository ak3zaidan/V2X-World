# Multi-Vehicle Trajectory Tracking towards Digital Twin Intersections for Internet of Vehicles
Zhanhao Ji, Guojiang Shen, Juntao Wang, Mario Collotta, Zhi Liu, Xiangjie Kong · Electronics 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics12020275 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Digital Twin (DT) provides a novel idea for Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS), while Internet of Vehicles (IoV) provides numerous positioning data of vehicles.
**Approach:** However, complex interactions between vehicles as well as offset and loss of measurements can lead to tracking errors of DT trajectories. In this paper, we propose a multi-vehicle trajectory tracking framework towards DT intersections (MVT2DTI).
