# An Efficient Blockchain-Based Conditional Privacy-Preserving Authentication Protocol for VANETs
Xiaotong Zhou, Debiao He, Muhammad Khurram Khan, Wei Wu et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2022.3204582 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs) have potential applications in improving the efficiency and safety for intelligent transportation systems. The openness of VANETs, however, also introduces privacy and security implications.
**Approach:** This paper proposes an efficient blockchain-based CPPA (EBCPPA) scheme, which is designed to mitigate the above limitations.

**Results:** Specifically, the performance evaluations show that EBCPPA is more efficient than other existing state-of-the-art solutions, in terms of signing (improving at least 49.71%), the verification (improving at least 32.84%) and bandwidth requirement (reducing at least 27.59%).
