# Adaptive Weighted Random Isolation (AWRI): a simple design to estimate causal effects under network interference
Changhao Shi, Haoyu Yang, Yichen Qin, Yang Li · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15670 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Estimating total treatment effect under network interference.
**Approach:** Weighted random isolation design with restricted difference-in-means and MSE surrogate.
**Tools and scale:** Simulations versus nine methods.
**Results:** Outperforms nine established methods.
