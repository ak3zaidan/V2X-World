# Mobile Edge Assisted Literal Multi-Dimensional Anomaly Detection of In-Vehicle Network Using LSTM
Konglin Zhu, Zhicheng Chen, Yuyang Peng, Lin Zhang · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2019.2907269 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The development of Internet of Vehicles (IoVs) have introduced more intrusions to the vulnerabilities of in-vehicle network. It is important to detect in-vehicle network anomaly for the purpose of driving safety.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a literal multi-dimensional anomaly detection approach using the distributed long-short-term-memory (LSTM) framework for in-vehicle network, especially the Control Area Network (CAN).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The extensive evaluation results show that the proposed anomaly detection achieves a satisfying accuracy of 90%.
