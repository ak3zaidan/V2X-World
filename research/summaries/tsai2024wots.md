# WOTS-based Conditional Privacy-preserving Authentication Protocol for VANETs
Kuo-Yu Tsai, Chung-Wei Kuo, L. Leung, Ying-Hsuan Yang · International Conference on Cryptography, Security and Privacy 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/csp62567.2024.00009 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the development of quantum computing, the present asymmetric crypto schemes could be significantly harmed by quantum algorithms. In the proposed protocol, a vehicle or a roadside unit can verify the validity of a received message, and any third party cannot retrieve the real identity of the vehicle except the trusted authority.
**Approach:** Hence, we propose a conditional privacy-preserving authentication (CPPA for short) protocol for vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs for short) based on Winternitz-type one-time signature (WOTS for short).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** Our proposed CPPA can not only ensure privacy but also achieve related security in VANETs, including authentication, non-repudiation, conditional privacy, un-linkability, resistance to quantum analysis, resistance to impersonation attacks, resistance to modification attacks, resistance to replay attacks, resistance to man-in-the-middle attacks, resistance to stolen verifier table attacks, etc. due to adopting WOTS, ...
