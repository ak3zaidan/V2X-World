# Defending Against Network Attacks for Secure AI Agent Migration in Vehicular Metaverses
Xinru Wen, Jin-Bo Wen, Ming Xiao, Jiawen Kang, Tao Zhang, Xiao-Huan Li et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2025.3633501 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular metaverses, blending traditional vehicular networks with metaverse technology, are expected to revolutionize fields such as autonomous driving.
**Approach:** As virtual intelligent assistants in vehicular metaverses, artificial intelligence (AI) agents empowered by large language models (LLMs) can create immersive 3-D virtual spaces for passengers to enjoy on-board vehicular applications and services. To provide users with seamless and engaging virtual interactions, resource-limited vehicles offload AI agents to RoadSide units (RSUs) with adequate communication and computational capabilities.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Numerical results demonstrate that the proposed solutions effectively defend against these network attacks and reduce the total latency of AI agent migration by approximately 12.8%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, potential network attacks pose significant challenges to ensuring reliable and efficient AI agent migration.
