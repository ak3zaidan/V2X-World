# Improved Recurrent Neural Network Schema for Validating Digital Signatures in VANET
Arpit Jain, Jaspreet Singh, Sandeep Kumar, Țurcanu Florin-Emilian et al. · Mathematics 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/math10203895 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) allow communication between stationary or moving vehicles with the assistance of wireless technology. Among various existing issues in smart VANETs, secure communication is the key challenge in VANETs with a 5G network.
**Approach:** Based on an improved perceptron model of an artificial neural network (ANN), this paper proposes an efficient technique for digital signature verification.
**Tools and scale:** The process of converting manual signatures to pseudo-digital-signatures was simulated using the improved Elman backpropagation (I-EBP) model.
**Results:** Because it contained information about the vehicle on the road, there was scope for improvement in protecting the data from attackers. Compared to existing schemes, the proposed technique achieved significant gains in computational overhead, aggregate verification delay, and aggregate signature size.
