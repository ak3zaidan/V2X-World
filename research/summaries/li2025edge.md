# Edge Computing-Based Anonymous Cross-Domain Authentication Scheme for VANETs
Bei Li, Hong Zhong, Cheng-Dong Gu, Jing Zhang et al. · IEEE International Conference on High Performance Computing and Communications 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/hpcc67675.2025.00093 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In the vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs), crossdomain communication among vehicles significantly improves traffic efficiency and road safety. However, due to the vulnerabilities of vehicle communication, it faces numerous security challenges when performing cross-domain communication.
**Approach:** To address these problems, we propose an efficient and anonymous cross-domain authentication scheme based on edge computing.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** In the vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs), crossdomain communication among vehicles significantly improves traffic efficiency and road safety. Existing schemes rely on trusted third parties for cross-domain authentication, resulting in issues such as low computational efficiency and weak privacy protection for vehicles, which cannot meet the demands of large-scale vehicle cross-domain communication.
