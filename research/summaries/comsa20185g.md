# Towards 5G: A Reinforcement Learning-Based Scheduling Solution for Data Traffic Management
Ioan-Sorin Comsa, Sijing Zhang, Mehmet Emin Aydin, Pierre Kuonen, Yao Lu, Ramona Trestian, Gheorghita Ghinea · IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnsm.2018.2863563 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Dominated by delay-sensitive and massive data applications, radio resource management in 5G access networks is expected to satisfy very stringent delay and packet loss requirements.
**Approach:** In this context, the packet scheduler plays a central role by allocating user data packets in the frequency domain at each predefined time interval. Standard scheduling rules are known limited in satisfying higher quality of service (QoS) demands when facing unpredictable network conditions and dynamic traffic circumstances.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G.
**Results:** Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed framework outperforms the conventional scheduling strategies in terms of delay and packet drop rate requirements.
