# A Computational Analysis of ECC Based Novel Authentication Scheme in VANET
S. Godse, P. Mahalle · International Journal of Electrical and Computer Engineering (IJECE) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.11591/ijece.v8i6.pp5268-5277 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A recent development in the adhoc network is a vehicular network called VANET (Vehicular Adhoc Network).
**Approach:** This paper addresses the computational analysis of authentication schemes based on ECC. We started analysis from comparing plain ECC with our proposed AECC (Adaptive Elliptic Curve Cryptography) and EECC (Enhanced Elliptic Curve Cryptography).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** The result of analysis shows proposed schemes improve speed and security of authentication.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
