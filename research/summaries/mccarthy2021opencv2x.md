# OpenCV2X: Modelling of the V2X Cellular Sidelink and Performance Evaluation for Aperiodic Traffic
Brian McCarthy, Andres Burbano-Abril, Victor Rangel Licea, Aisling O'Driscoll · arXiv 2021 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.13212 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Performance of Rel-14 C-V2X sidelink SB-SPS with aperiodic traffic.
**Approach:** OpenCV2X open-source sidelink model fully compliant with vehicular service/application layers.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the extracted sections.
**Results:** SB-SPS performs poorly for aperiodic traffic (ETSI CAM, 3GPP model); variability in inter-arrival times strongly affects mitigation schemes.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Need to investigate networks with mixed application traffic to schedule resources while maintaining reliability.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — partial: the model our LTE-V2X stack is matched against.
**Novelty implications:** Aperiodic-traffic SPS degradation is known; adding security (variable message size from certificates/digests) is an angle.
