# A Credible Beacon Based Trust Evaluation Model for Improving Communication Reliability of VANETs
Changwang Cai, Li Liang, Xiao Liu et al. · 2025 5th International Conference on Computer Science, Electronic Information Engineering and Intelligent Control Technology (CEI) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cei66465.2025.11398721 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs), the trust value of a vehicle is usually used to determine whether it serves as a communication node, which leads to a significant impact of trust evaluation on communication.
**Approach:** In this paper, to improve the accuracy of trust evaluation, a credible beacon based trust evaluation model (CBTE) is proposed, which carefully filters the opinions of neighboring vehicle. Then, a beacon reliability evaluation algorithm is designed to analyze whether the beacon is trustworthy and to determine whether to adopt the opinion of the beacon.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Due to the strict recommendation information screening, the accuracy of the proposed trust evaluation algorithm is high, which is beneficial for improving the success rate of communication in VANETs.
