# PQ-V2X: A Novel Postquantum Cryptographic Dataset for Secure Vehicular Communications
Shimaa Abdelnaby AbdelHakeem, HyungWon Kim · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2025.3618153 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** No V2X intrusion-detection dataset combining PQ crypto artefacts, mobility and channel.
**Approach:** PQ-V2X dataset: five million labelled records, 23 attack types across PQ, hybrid, classical; 10.05% real traces from Kyber, Dilithium, Falcon, SPHINCS+; ten ML models; DER metric.
**Tools and scale:** Mobility and zone-aware channel conditions (tunnel zones).
**Results:** LightGBM perfect detection with high DER; SPHINCS+ vectors remain challenging; tunnel zones amplify risk.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
