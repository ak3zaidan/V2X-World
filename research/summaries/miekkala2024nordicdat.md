# NordicDat: A Cross-Border Predictive QoS Dataset
Topi Miekkala, Pasi Pyykönen, Georgios Drainakis, P. Pantazopoulos, T. Muller, Konstantinos V. Katsaros et al. · Global Communications Conference 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/globecom52923.2024.10901462 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** pQoS needs large spatio-temporal QoS datasets.
**Approach:** NordicDat: two-week, three-country drive campaign with cross-border roaming.
**Tools and scale:** Over 90K samples; PHY, network, mobility features.
**Results:** Data analysis and baseline classical/distributed pQoS models.

- None stated.
