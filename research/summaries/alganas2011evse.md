# EVSE: An Efficient Vehicle Social Evaluation Scheme with Location Privacy Preservation for Vehicular Communications
Abdulelah Alganas, Xiaodong Lin, Ali Grami · 2011 IEEE International Conference on Communications (ICC) 2011 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc.2011.5962526 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Social-aware data diffusion can improve the dissemination performance in vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs).
**Approach:** However, if some vehicles lie their social claims and vehicle location information is not protected, social-aware data diffusion may not work well. In this paper, to tackle the security and privacy challenges existing in social-aware data diffusion, we propose an efficient vehicle social evaluation (EVSE) scheme, which enables each vehicle to show its authentic social evaluation to others while without disclosing its past location information.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: location privacy).
**Results:** In this paper, to tackle the security and privacy challenges existing in social-aware data diffusion, we propose an efficient vehicle social evaluation (EVSE) scheme, which enables each vehicle to show its authentic social evaluation to others while without disclosing its past location information. As a result, it can meet the prerequisites for the success of social-aware data diffusion in VANET.
