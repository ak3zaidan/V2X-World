# Novel Actor-Critic Algorithm for Robust Decision Making of CAV under Delays and Loss of V2X Data
Zine el abidine Kherroubi · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.05072 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** V2X data arrives aperiodically due to delay and loss, harming CAV control.
**Approach:** Blind Actor-Critic with virtual fixed sampling, TD/MC mix, reward approximation.
**Tools and scale:** Driving simulation.
**Results:** Robust driving performance (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — links V2X delay/loss to driving decisions (safety); no security cause.
**Novelty implications:** Communication-impairment-to-control studies exist; security-caused impairment (direction 2) remains open.
