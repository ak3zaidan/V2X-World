# World Model-Based Learning for Long-Term Age of Information Minimization in Vehicular Networks
Lingyi Wang, Rashed Shelim, Walid Saad, Naren Ramakrishnan · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.01712 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Model-free RL for wireless is data-inefficient and short-sighted.
**Approach:** World model of mmWave V2X environment used to imagine trajectories for link scheduling minimising completeness-aware AoI.
**Tools and scale:** mmWave V2X simulation.
**Results:** Better data efficiency and CAoI than RL baselines (numbers not extracted).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in extracted sections.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - learned V2X world model (direction 3), for scheduling not evaluation.
**Novelty implications:** V2X world models for control exist; for counterfactual evaluation with security state they do not.
