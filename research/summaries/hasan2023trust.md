# A Trust Model for Edge-Driven Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks Using Fuzzy Logic
Md. Mahmudul Hasan, Mosarrat Jahan, Shaily Kabir · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2023.3305342 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Trust establishment among vehicles is essential for vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) as it directly impacts the security and privacy of vehicular communication. Many trust estimation approaches have been introduced, however, they often suffer from ensuring effective trust for vehicles.
**Approach:** To address these limitations, this paper proposes a fuzzy logic-based approach to estimate vehicles’ trust. We evaluate the performance of the proposed scheme against the state-of-the-art approaches using both synthetic and real-world datasets.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The experimental results reveal that it outperforms existing schemes in detecting malicious vehicles with higher recall, precision, and accuracy.
