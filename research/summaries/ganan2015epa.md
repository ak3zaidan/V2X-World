# EPA: An efficient and privacy-aware revocation mechanism for vehicular ad hoc networks
C. Gañán, Jose L. Muñoz, O. Esparza, J. Mata-Díaz, J. Alins · Pervasive and Mobile Computing 2015 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pmcj.2014.01.002 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Revocation checking leaks privacy and CRL checks are slow.
**Approach:** EPA: Merkle hash trees and a Crowds-based anonymous protocol for status checks.
**Tools and scale:** Performance evaluation.
**Results:** Reliable, efficient, scalable.
