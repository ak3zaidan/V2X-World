# An efficient key agreement and anonymous privacy preserving scheme for vehicular ad‐hoc networks with handover authentication
A. Priyadharshini, Suresh Dannana, ArunSekar Rajasekaran, Azees Maria · Concurrency and Computation 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1002/cpe.7979 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In addition to improving safety and efficiency, VANETs also face several challenges.
**Approach:** In this manuscript, an anonymous authentication scheme for VANET users is proposed along with privacy preservation and confidentiality. An efficient session key agreement protocol is designed for transferring location‐based information between the vehicle user and the roadside unit (RSU).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** VANETs can also improve the efficiency of transportation systems by reducing traffic congestion and improving traffic flow. Moreover, re‐authentication of VANET users is completely evaded during to handover authentication, thus reducing the computational overhead.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
