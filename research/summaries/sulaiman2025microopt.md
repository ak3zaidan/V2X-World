# MicroOpt: Model-Driven Slice Resource Optimization in 5G and Beyond Networks
Muhammad Sulaiman, Mahdieh Ahmadi, Bo Sun, Mohammad A. Salahuddin et al. · IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnsm.2025.3584257 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A pivotal attribute of 5G networks is their capability to cater to diverse application requirements. This is achieved by creating logically isolated virtual networks, or slices, with distinct service level agreements (SLAs) tailored to specific use cases.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose MicroOpt, a novel framework that leverages a differentiable neural network-based slice model with gradient descent for resource optimization and Lagrangian decomposition for QoS constraint satisfaction. We evaluate MicroOpt against two state-of-the-art approaches using an open-source 5G testbed with real-world traffic traces.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G, real-world, testbed. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Our results demonstrate up to 21.9% improvement in resource allocation compared to these approaches across various scenarios, including different QoS thresholds and dynamic slice traffic.
