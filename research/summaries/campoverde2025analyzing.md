# Analyzing the Environmental Footprint in VANETs Through SUMO Simulator
Luis Miguel Samaniego Campoverde, Mauro Tropea, Alfredo Garro, Floriano De Rango · 2025 29th International Symposium on Distributed Simulation and Real Time Applications (DS-RT) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ds-rt68115.2025.11186072 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Do SUMO quick-start defaults distort emissions estimates?
**Approach:** SUMO OSM Web Wizard scenarios with varied vehicle types and behaviours, defaults vs calibrated.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO.
**Results:** Defaults lead to substantial deviations in emission and fuel estimates.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — default-parameter sensitivity, analogous to Bloessl 2019 for traffic.
**Novelty implications:** Supports careful configuration reporting.
