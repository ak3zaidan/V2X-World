# Secure Control Design for Cooperative Adaptive Cruise Control Under False Data Injection Attack
Parisa Ansari-Bonab, James C. Holland, Jonas Cunningham-Rush, S. Noei, Arman Sargolzaei · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2024.3395208 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** CACC assumes a secure channel; FDI attacks break it.
**Approach:** Secure nonlinear controller and nonlinear observer estimating FDI in real time with semi-global uniform boundedness.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation and experimental implementation.
**Results:** Maintained safe following distance while estimating FDI and noise in real time.
