# Constructing Future Vehicle Digital Twins with Insights from Cloud-Edge Collaboration
Xin Shi, Liang Zhao, Na Lin, Lexi Xu et al. · 2023 IEEE Intl Conf on Parallel &amp;amp; Distributed Processing with Applications, Big Data &amp;amp; Cloud Computing, Sustainable Computing &amp;amp; Communications, Social Computing &amp;amp; Networking (ISPA/BDCloud/SocialCom/SustainCom) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ispa-bdcloud-socialcom-sustaincom59178.2023.00047 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) is embarking on a new development road, in which Digital Twins (DT) provides crucial support for improving the smartness standard of vehicles and realizing a high-level of traffic management in this process.
**Approach:** First, we propose the concept of future-oriented DT aim to address these issues. Then, we introduce a collaborative learning method between two models to construct the DT network architecture and realize the vision of the future-oriented.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Finally, We use an Incremental Learning algorithm to improve the ability of the model that adapt to new scenarios and changing input data. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method, and the stability and performance of DTs have achieved excellent performance in the construction process.
