# Improving location privacy in mix-zones for VANETs
Antonio M. Carianha, Luciano Porto Barreto, George Lima · 30th IEEE International Performance Computing and Communications Conference 2011 · https://doi.org/10.1109/pccc.2011.6108111 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Providing location privacy to users is one of the important issues that must be addressed in Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks.
**Approach:** Recent solutions address it by using cryptographic “mix-zones”, which are anonymizing regions where nodes change their temporary identities (pseudonym) without being tracked. However, existing solutions are vulnerable to internal attackers since within a mix-zone messages are encrypted using a group secret key.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: pseudonym change/privacy).
**Results:** In this paper we improve location privacy of mix-zones via extensions to the CMIX protocol.
