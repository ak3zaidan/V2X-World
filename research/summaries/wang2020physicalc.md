# Physical-layer authentication based on adaptive Kalman filter for V2X communication
Jian Wang, Ya-Meng Shao, Yuming Ge, Rundong Yu · Vehicular Communications 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vehcom.2020.100281 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** To solve this problem, we propose a physical-layer authentication model based on channel characteristics in the V2X environment.
**Approach:** At present, V2X security schemes in most countries are based on public key infrastructure (PKI). Furthermore, we select CSI and RSSI to design a physical-layer authentication scheme based on this model, which can replace the digital signature.

**Results:** Consequently, V2X communication efficiency will be affected in the case of traffic congestion. As a result, the proposed scheme makes minor changes to the PKI-based V2X security and effectively reduces the overhead of security.
