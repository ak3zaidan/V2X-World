# Impacts of Security Attacks on The Effectiveness of Collaborative Adaptive Cruise Control Mechanism
Shantanu Sardesai, Denis Ulybyshev, Lotfi ben Othmane, Bharat Bhargava · 2018 IEEE International Smart Cities Conference (ISC2) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/isc2.2018.8656970 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Impact of attacks on CACC safety.
**Approach:** Simulation of four attacks on CACC in a merging scenario.
**Tools and scale:** Abstract system simulation.
**Results:** Communication attacks cause collisions with non-negligible proportion.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Abstract system.
- suggests need for strong assurance.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Attack-to-safety impact; our engine quantifies this in city scale.
**Novelty implications:** Collision impact of attacks shown in abstract settings.
