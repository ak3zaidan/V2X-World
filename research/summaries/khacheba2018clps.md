# CLPS: context-based location privacy scheme for VANETs
Ines Khacheba, Mohamed B. Yagoubi, Nasreddine Lagraa, Abderrahmane Lakas · International Journal of Ad Hoc and Ubiquitous Computing 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1504/ijahuc.2018.094404 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Location privacy preservation is considered as a challenge for deploying vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs).
**Approach:** In fact, the continuous periodical broadcast of spatiotemporal information in beacons allows a global passive adversary (GPA) to link pseudonyms and reconstruct vehicle traces. Consequently, it is imperative that a vehicle selects the right context to change pseudonym to confuse the adversary and ensure pseudonyms unlinkability.
