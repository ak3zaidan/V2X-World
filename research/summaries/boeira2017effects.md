# Effects of colluding Sybil nodes in message falsification attacks for vehicular platooning
Felipe Boeira, M. Barcellos, EDISON PIGNATON DE FREITAS, A. Vinel, Mikael Asplund · IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc.2017.8275641 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Impact of Sybil identities combined with message falsification on platoon safety.
**Approach:** Five attack scenarios quantified, including a novel "location hijacking" attack that binds fake identities to innocent physical vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Platooning simulation (Plexe/Veins type per VNC venue; not stated in abstract).
**Results:** False identities increase effectiveness of falsification attacks, making them easier and harder to detect in time.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — attack executed in simulation with safety impact on platoons; no PKI revocation loop.
**Novelty implications:** Sybil-plus-falsification safety impact in platoons is known; neutralisation via revocation is not.
