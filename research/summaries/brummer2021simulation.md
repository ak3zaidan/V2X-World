# On the Simulation of Vehicular Networking Scenarios in Multi-Story Parking Garages
Alexander Brummer, Thomas Deinlein, Anatoli Djanatliev · 2021 IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference (VNC) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc52810.2021.9644620 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Multi-story parking garages are special V2X environments.
**Approach:** Models for inter-floor communication, small-scale fading and obstacles; measurements in two garages.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular network simulator (Veins); measurements.
**Results:** Floor attenuation factors determined; simulated path loss agrees well with test drives.
