# A Hybrid Machine Learning Approach for Scalable and Uncertainty-Aware RSU Tracking in VANET
KRISHNA KOMARAM, NAGARJUNA KARYEMSETTY ·  2025 · https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-7633199/v1 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Real-time RSU tracking in V2V networks with noisy data and scale.
**Approach:** XGBoost plus gradient discriminant filter plus Bayesian DNN features.
**Tools and scale:** Simulated tracking data; 100-25,000 vehicles.
**Results:** Over 95% classification accuracy across densities.
