# MKF-ADS: Multi-Knowledge Fusion Based Anomaly Detection System in Vehicular Control Area Networks
Pengzhou Cheng, Shouxuan Liu, Zongru Wu, Lixing Tan, Gong-Shen Liu · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2025.3564575 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As the standard communication protocol for vehicular networks, the Controller Area Network (CAN) plays a crucial role in coordinating interactions among Electronic Control Units (ECUs).
**Approach:** However, intruders can launch malicious attacks on the CAN due to the lack of security mechanisms. Intrusion Detection Systems (IDSs), as a defense component, are deployed to monitor the CAN and detect suspicious behavior resulting from an attack.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Intrusion Detection Systems (IDSs), as a defense component, are deployed to monitor the CAN and detect suspicious behavior resulting from an attack. Compared to the baseline, the proposed method is more robust, achieving a high F1-score while maintaining controllable false alarms.
