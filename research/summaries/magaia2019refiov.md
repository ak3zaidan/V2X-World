# ReFIoV: A Novel Reputation Framework for Information-Centric Vehicular Applications
Naércio Magaia, Zheng-Guo Sheng · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2018.2886572 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Misbehaving nodes in information-centric vehicular caching/forwarding.
**Approach:** ReFIoV reputation framework: Bayesian learning and classification, k-means to integrate recommendations, artificial immune system for misbehaviour detection, distributed decisions.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation evaluation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** Very low number of misclassified misbehaving nodes vs another reputation scheme; low AIS overhead; recommendations reduce detection time.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — reputation-based detection; no PKI/revocation.
**Novelty implications:** Reputation systems for vehicular data are covered; unrelated to SCMS loop.
