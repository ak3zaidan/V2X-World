# Post-Quantum Security Overview of the Public Key Infrastructure
Fruzsina Bene, Attila Kiss · SYSTEM THEORY, CONTROL AND COMPUTING JOURNAL 2023 · https://doi.org/10.52846/stccj.2023.3.2.55 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** One of the pressing issues that needs to be made quantum secure is the TLS (Transport Layer Security) protocol.
**Approach:** The methods currently developed to ensure adequate security will become obsolete with the advent of quantum computers. It is therefore important to make the right preparations in time to ensure that we can transform our solutions into quantum secure solutions by the time quantum computing becomes a real threat.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** According to the Cloud Security Alliance, by around 2030, the performance of quantum computers will increase to the point where the risk of vulnerability of traditionally encrypted data will be very high.
