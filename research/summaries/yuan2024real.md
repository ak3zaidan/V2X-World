# Real-Time Tracking and Authentication Protocol for Anonymous Operating Vehicles in Vehicle-Infrastructure Collaborative Systems
Jiasheng Yuan, Bin Li, Xinming Mei, Yanfang Zhou · IEEE Access 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3357490 · relevance: medium · basis: full text

**Problem:** Road control centre cannot track anonymous operating vehicles in real time under pseudonym certificates.
**Approach:** Protocol embedding OAEP-encrypted real identity decryptable by the RCC, with pseudonym certificates and ECC.
**Tools and scale:** Security reduction (ECDLP, random oracle); MIRACL performance analysis; RSU service rate analysed with a traffic flow model.
**Results:** Satisfies security requirements; performance slightly weaker than others due to tracking cost; stable RSU service in the analysed scenario.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Authors note the performance cost of real-time tracking.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — partial: pseudonym certificates with authority traceability, which SCMS does via linkage values and LA/PCA collaboration.
**Novelty implications:** Nothing ruled out; differs from SCMS design.
