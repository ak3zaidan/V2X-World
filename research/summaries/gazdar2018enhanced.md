# An Enhanced Distributed Trust Computing Protocol for VANETs
Tahani Gazdar, Abdelfettah Belghith, Hassan Abutair · IEEE Access 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2017.2765303 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Trust establishment in vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) is a challenging task due essentially to the high speed of vehicles, the long distances, and the network topology dynamics. Furthermore, applications context evolves quickly at the same time that the lifetime validity of data messages is short.
**Approach:** We also propose a tier-based messages dissemination technique in order to efficiently detect eavesdropped messages and fake events. We analytically model the trust metrics evolution of malicious vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Extensive simulations are conducted to show the validity of the proposed model and evaluate the efficiency of the proposed trust computing framework.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- In this paper, we set up a new distributed trust computing framework tailored to VANETs characteristics and aiming to solve the aforementioned challenges.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Trust/reputation management for detecting malicious senders; an alternative to the SCMS misbehaviour-report -> MA -> CRL pipeline our engine models, with no linkage-based revocation or time-to-neutralise analysis.
**Novelty implications:** Does not rule out any candidate direction; it does not model SCMS certificates, linkage-based revocation, per-message joint ground truth or counterfactual reruns.
