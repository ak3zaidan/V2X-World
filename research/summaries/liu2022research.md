# Research on Cyberattack Detection of Connected Automated Vehicles Based on Support Vector Machine
Runzhe Liu, Xin Peng, Zhan-Bo Sun, Zhihang Huang, Hai Hu · International Conferences on Intelligent Transportation Engineering 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icite56321.2022.10101470 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Connected automated vehicles (CAVs) are facing the threat of cyberattacks while exchanging state information.
**Approach:** Most of the existing research focuses on the communication technologies and the network security technologies, with the purpose of providing corresponding preventive solutions for the specific technical means of attack behavior, which are highly targeted but weak in universality of application. In this paper, a type of adversary model based on attack effects, namely, disturbance attack, was established.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The model showed a sound classification performance, with a detection rate of more than 95% within 3s of the attack. The results help better understand the effects of CAV-related cyberattacks and shed light on proactive defense against such attacks.
