# Berlin V2X: A Machine Learning Dataset from Multiple Vehicles and Radio Access Technologies
Rodrigo Hernangómez, Philipp Geuer, Alexandros Palaios, Daniel Schäufele, Cara Watermann, Khawla Taleb-Bouhemadi et al. · 2023 IEEE 97th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2023-Spring) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2023-spring57618.2023.10200750 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** ML for V2X needs real labelled measurement datasets.
**Approach:** Berlin V2X measurement campaign: GPS-located, high-time-resolution measurements from multiple vehicles for cellular (two operators) and sidelink; public data and initial analysis.
**Tools and scale:** Real measurements in diverse urban Berlin environments.
**Results:** Initial analysis of challenges and features for ML (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Hints at potential research studies; dataset limited to the measured routes and RATs.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - V2X dataset, but radio-only measurements without traffic/PKI ground truth.
**Novelty implications:** Real V2X radio datasets exist; no dataset provides joint traffic/radio/PKI ground truth with counterfactuals.
