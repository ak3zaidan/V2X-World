# A Lightweight ML Approach for VANET Attack Detection in Connected Vehicle Networks
Mudit Mittal, Renu Bahuguna, Pallavi Gupta et al. · International Symposium Advanced Electrical and Communication Technologies 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/isaect68904.2025.11318364 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Traffic safety, mobility, and urban infrastructure have been revolutionised by intelligent transportation systems (ITS) and connected vehicles.
**Approach:** We propose a lightweight machine learning (ML) approach for detecting attacks in VANETs, which is suitable for resource-constrained onboard systems.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** According to experimental results, Random Forest consistently achieved the highest accuracy (up to 99.8%), albeit at the expense of higher computation time.
