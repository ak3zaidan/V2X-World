# Roadside unit‐based pseudonym authentication in vehicular ad hoc network
Steffie Maria Stephen, A. Jaekel · Security and Privacy 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1002/spy2.238 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Certificates and CRLs in every message cost OBU compute and channel.
**Approach:** RSU-based authentication with three-tier blockchain identity architecture.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Improved authentication delay and channel load.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Offloading verification to RSUs.
**Novelty implications:** None.
