# FlowPrint: Semi-Supervised Mobile-App Fingerprinting on Encrypted Network Traffic
Thijs van Ede, Riccardo Bortolameotti, Andrea Continella, Jingjing Ren, Daniel J. Dubois, Martina Lindorfer et al. · Network and Distributed System Security Symposium 2020 · https://doi.org/10.14722/ndss.2020.24412 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Mobile-application fingerprinting of network traffic is valuable for many security solutions as it provides insights into the apps active on a network.
**Approach:** Unfortunately, existing techniques require prior knowledge of apps to be able to recognize them. However, mobile environments are constantly evolving, i.e., apps are regularly installed, updated, and uninstalled.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Our approach is able to fingerprint previously unseen apps, something that existing techniques fail to achieve. We evaluate our approach for both Android and iOS in the setting of app recognition, where we achieve an accuracy of 89.2%, significantly outperforming state-of-the-art solutions.
