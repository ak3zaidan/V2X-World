# Investigating the Effects of Human-Machine Interface on Cooperative Driving at Non-Signalized Intersection Using an Integrated Co-Simulation Platform
Zijin Wang, Lishengsa Yue, Mohamed Abdel-Aty, Jiahao Zhu, Mohamed Zaki · Social Science Research Network 2022 · https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.4230161 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Effect of HMI design on cooperative driving at non-signalised intersections.
**Approach:** Three HMIs tested with human-in-the-loop co-simulation in pure CV and mixed traffic.
**Tools and scale:** Driving simulator co-simulation.
**Results:** Graphic HMI better for minor speed changes, more precise and accepted.
