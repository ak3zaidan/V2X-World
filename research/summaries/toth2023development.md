# Development and Functional Validation Method of the Scenario-in-the-Loop Simulation Control Model Using Co-Simulation Techniques
Balint Toth, Zsolt Szalay · Machines 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/machines11111028 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the facilitated development of highly automated driving functions and automated vehicles, the need for advanced testing techniques also arose.
**Approach:** With a near-infinite number of potential traffic scenarios, vehicles have to drive an increased number of test kilometers during development, which would be very difficult to achieve with currently utilized conventional testing methods. State-of-the-Art testing technologies such as Vehicle-in-the-Loop (ViL) or Scenario-in-the-Loop (SciL) can provide a long-term solution; however, validation of these complex systems should also be addressed.
