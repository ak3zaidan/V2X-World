# Adversarial Attacks on Deep Reinforcement Learning-based Traffic Signal Control Systems with Colluding Vehicles
Ao Qu, Yihong Tang, Wei Ma · ACM Transactions on Intelligent Systems and Technology 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3625236 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The rapid advancements of Internet of Things (IoT) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) have catalyzed the development of adaptive traffic control systems (ATCS) for smart cities.
**Approach:** In particular, deep reinforcement learning (DRL) models produce state-of-the-art performance and have great potential for practical applications. In the existing DRL-based ATCS, the controlled signals collect traffic state information from nearby vehicles, and then optimal actions (e.g., switching phases) can be determined based on the collected information.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: traffic signal attacks).
**Results:** We employ our framework to attack established DRL-based ATCS and demonstrate that the total travel time for the colluding vehicles can be significantly reduced with a reasonable number of learning episodes, and the colluding effect will decrease if the number of colluding vehicles increases. The research outcomes could help improve the reliability and robustness of the ATCS and better protect the smart mobility systems.
