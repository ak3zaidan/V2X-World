# Multi-Zone Authentication and Privacy-Preserving Protocol (MAPP) Based on the Bilinear Pairing Cryptography for 5G-V2X
Shimaa A. Abdel Hakeem, Hyungwon Kim · Italian National Conference on Sensors 2021 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s21020665 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** 1609.2 PKI reused in 3GPP C-V2X has high certificate/signature overhead.
**Approach:** MAPP: bilinear-pairing short-signature multi-zone authentication (three methods) with centralised dynamic key generation.
**Tools and scale:** Authentication simulator and pairing library.
**Results:** Signature generation 16-80 times faster than compared methods.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in extracted sections.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - targets verification/certificate overhead that direction 2 quantifies.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
