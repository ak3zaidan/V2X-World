# A Dynamic Mix-zone Scheme Considering Communication Delay for Location Privacy in Vehicular Networks
Rei Yamazaki, Masashi Yoshida, Hiroshi Shigeno · 2021 IEEE International Conference on Pervasive Computing and Communications Workshops and other Affiliated Events (PerCom Workshops) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/percomworkshops51409.2021.9431021 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In vehicular networks, vehicles periodically exchange messages that include their positions and IDs.
**Approach:** On the other hand, adversaries can violate the vehicles' location privacy by obtaining the vehicles' movement history from the messages. In recent studies, mix-zone-based schemes protect the vehicles' location privacy.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: pseudonym change/privacy).
**Results:** Simulation results show that the proposed scheme improves update frequency of pseudonyms by 50 at most in 1000 seconds compared with the previous mix-zone-based scheme.
