# Predictive Quality of Service: Adaptation of Platoon Inter-Vehicle Distance to Packet Inter-Reception Time
Andreas Pfadler, Guillaume Jornod, Ahmad El Assaad, Peter Jung · 2020 IEEE 91st Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2020-Spring) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2020-spring48590.2020.9129097 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Platoon must know tolerable communication degradation.
**Approach:** Derives relation between maximal tolerable packet inter-reception time and inter-vehicle distance via fitted statistical models.
**Tools and scale:** 802.11p platoon simulation with varying traffic density.
**Results:** Functional link derived (no numbers).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - quantifies communication-to-safety relationship (relevant to direction 2's safety metric).
**Novelty implications:** Safety-vs-PIR mapping exists for platoons; security-induced PIR degradation is not studied.
