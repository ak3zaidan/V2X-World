# Large Language Model Enabled Multi-Task Physical Layer Network
Tianyue Zheng, Linglong Dai · IEEE Transactions on Communications 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tcomm.2025.3626010 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The advance of Artificial Intelligence (AI) is continuously reshaping the future 6G wireless communications.
**Approach:** Particularly, the development of Large Language Models (LLMs) offers a promising approach to effectively improve the performance and generalization of AI in different physical-layer (PHY) tasks. However, most existing works finetune dedicated LLM networks for a single wireless communication task separately.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: simulations.
**Results:** To reduce the memory requirement during LLM fine-tuning, a LoRA fine-tuning-aware quantization method is introduced.
