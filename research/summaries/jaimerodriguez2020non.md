# A Non-WSSUS Channel Simulator for V2X Communication Systems
José Jimmy Jaime-Rodríguez, Carlos Antonio Gómez-Vega, Carlos A. Gutiérrez, José Martín Luna-Rivera, Daniel Ulises Campos-Delgado, Ramiro Velázquez · Electronics 2020 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics9081190 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** V2X channels are non-WSSUS; simulators should reproduce their time-frequency correlations.
**Approach:** Monte Carlo sum-of-cisoids non-WSSUS channel simulator with velocity variation and non-linear trajectories; evaluates LS and STA 802.11p channel estimators.
**Tools and scale:** Link-level simulation, DSRC and C-V2X multicarrier; three road-safety scenarios.
**Results:** Non-stationarity has nearly no effect on LS BER but significantly degrades STA; a temporal-only STA variant improves BER.
