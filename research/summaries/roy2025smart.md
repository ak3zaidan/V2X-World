# SMART: Secure Incentive-Based Mechanism for Addressing Reliability in Trustworthy VANETs
Ayan Roy, Rik Chakraborti · NOMS 2025-2025 IEEE Network Operations and Management Symposium 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/noms57970.2025.11073668 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In a connected vehicle environment, vehicles share traffic information to improve conditions and enhance safety for pedestrians and drivers.
**Approach:** Each exchange incurs costs from the vehicle's perspective, requiring payment through incentives. Increased participation boosts the reliability of shared information, making incentives essential.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Results demonstrate that the IAAB strategy effectively identifies malicious vehicles and extracts reliable information, even when most vehicles are malicious, provided the initial event originates from a malicious source.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, malicious vehicles can compromise quality and undermine trust by exploiting incentive systems.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
