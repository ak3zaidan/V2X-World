# Grade-Ids: Greatest Cane Rat Algorithm Based Deep Learning for Intrusion Detection System for VANET Security
S. Sam, Thirupurasundari D. R. · 2026 Innovations in Machine, Engineering, and Digital Conference (IMED) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/imed68921.2026.11484916 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) are regarded as essential components of Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS), as communication between vehicles and infrastructure is facilitated by them.
**Approach:** To address these limitations, a novel Greatest Cane Rat Algorithm-based Deep Learning Intrusion System (GRADE-IDS) technique is proposed in this paper. Initially, the data from the VANET environment is collected by the proposed model, and it is subsequently preprocessed through techniques such as data cleaning, normalization, and transformation.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The performance of the proposed model has been evaluated using accuracy (AC), precision (PR), recall (RE), f1-score (FS), and detection rate. Experimental results show that the proposed model achieves a higher accuracy of 99.24%, whereas the previous techniques, such as VAN-FED-IDS, 2PT-CIDS, and Light Bioptimum, achieve a lesser accuracy of 98.35%, 98% and 97% respectively.
