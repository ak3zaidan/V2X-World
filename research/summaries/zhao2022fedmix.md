# FedMix: A Sybil Attack Detection System Considering Cross-layer Information Fusion and Privacy Protection
Jing Zhao, Ruwu Wang · IEEE International Conference on Sensing, Communication, and Networking (SECON) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/secon55815.2022.9918586 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil detection uses single-layer data and ignores privacy.
**Approach:** FedMix federated detection fusing physical- and application-layer data; SFedAvg aggregation.
**Tools and scale:** Experiments.
**Results:** Equivalent performance to centralized with lower communication; cross-layer fusion improves detection.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — cross-layer fusion for Sybil detection (runner-up cross-layer).
**Novelty implications:** Cross-layer federated Sybil detection exists.
