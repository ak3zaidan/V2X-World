# SDRP: a secure distributed revocation protocol for vehicular environments
Noureddine Chaib, Nasreddine Lagraa, Mohamed Bachir Yagoubi, Abderrahmane Lakas · Security and Communication Networks 2012 · https://doi.org/10.1002/sec.561 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Distributed revocation vulnerable to colluding attackers.
**Approach:** SDRP: secure distributed revocation excluding misbehaving nodes from routing.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** High detection with low false positives with many attackers.

- None stated.
