# Movement Abnormality Evaluation Model in the Partially Centralized VANETs for Prevention Against Sybil Attack
Mandeep Kaur, Manish Mahajan · International Journal of Modern Education and Computer Science 2015 · https://doi.org/10.5815/ijmecs.2015.11.03 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Sybil and prankster attacks in clusters.
**Approach:** Movement abnormality model; central control orders malicious node to stop.
**Tools and scale:** Random waypoint mobility vs reference point group model.
**Results:** About 7% less energy than existing model.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Uses random waypoint mobility (unrealistic for vehicles).
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
