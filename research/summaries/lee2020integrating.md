# Integrating Driving Hardware-in-the-Loop Simulator with Large-Scale VANET Simulator for Evaluation of Cooperative Eco-Driving System
Geonil Lee, Seongmin Ha, Jae-il Jung · Electronics 2020 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics9101645 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Evaluating cooperative eco-driving needs ECU and V2X simulation together.
**Approach:** Integrated driving HIL simulator: QualNet, dSPACE HIL, MicroAutoBox, Cohda MK5, SPaT-based eco-speed guidance for a connected hybrid EV.
**Tools and scale:** QualNet, dSPACE, Cohda MK5 hardware; V2I SPaT.
**Results:** Fuel efficiency improved by 14.34% when driving at eco-speed.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Add partially and fully automated driving functions in future simulator.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — eco-driving HIL.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
