# RAPP: An efficient revocation scheme with authentication and privacy preserving for vehicular ad-hoc networks
Amirreza Sarencheh, Maryam Rajabzadeh Asaar, Mahmoud Salmasizadeh, Mohammad Reza Aref · International Conference on Computer and Knowledge Engineering 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccke.2017.8167905 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Revocation checking leaks privacy and is slow.
**Approach:** RAPP: RSU-managed regional revocation lists after enough protests; lightweight encryption instead of certificates.
**Tools and scale:** Performance evaluation.
**Results:** Key updating more than 423 times faster and revocation 15 times faster than prior scheme.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — protest-threshold revocation relates to MA decision rule.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
