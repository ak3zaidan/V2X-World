# Vehicle Shadowing Modeling for Deterministic Channel Emulator and Electromagnetic Simulation-Based Validation
Siraphop Saisa-ard, Nopphon Keerativoranan, Jun-ichi Takada · Proceedings of the XXXVth URSI General Assembly and Scientific Symposium – GASS 2023 2023 · https://doi.org/10.46620/ursigass.2023.3581.erkk6299 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Deterministic channel emulators need simple realistic vehicle shadowing models.
**Approach:** Knife-edge diffraction model using the shortest diffraction path, validated against EM simulation of a vehicle frame and PEC cuboid at 700 MHz.
**Tools and scale:** EM simulation; 700 MHz ITS band (ARIB STD-T109).
**Results:** Model predicts shadowing gain well in the shallow-shadow region.
