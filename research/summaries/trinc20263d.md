# 3D Urban Outdoor WiFi 7 Network Planning and Analysis Using Ray-Tracing and Machine Learning: Transformer-Based Surrogate Modeling for High-Resolution Digital Twin
Emanuel Trinc, Cosmin Ancuti, Andy Vesa, Calin Simu · Italian National Conference on Sensors 2026 · https://doi.org/10.20944/preprints202601.0082.v1 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Ray tracing for urban Wi-Fi 7 planning is too slow.
**Approach:** ML surrogate (FT-Transformer and others) trained on MATLAB ray-tracing data.
**Tools and scale:** University campus; multiple bands, powers and reflection settings.
**Results:** MAE 3.49 dB, RMSE 5.36 dB, R2 99.63%; months of simulation reduced to seconds.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — learned surrogate replacing an expensive propagation simulator, the PHY part of our surrogate direction.
**Novelty implications:** PHY-level surrogates are established; cross-layer V2X surrogates remain open.
