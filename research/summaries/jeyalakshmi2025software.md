# Software-Defined Federated AI Model for Privacy-Preserving Anomaly Detection in Vehicular Edge Environments
T. Ratha Jeyalakshmi, Pavithra G Shetty, Santhosh G S et al. · 2025 World Conference on Cutting-Edge Science and Technology (WCCEST) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wccest66994.2025.11390022 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Real-time anomaly detection methods ensuring data privacy and computational efficiency are needed owing to the rapid increase in networked and autonomous vehicles.
**Approach:** This paper introduces a software-defined, federated learning framework aimed at vehicular edge networks to overcome these obstacles.
**Tools and scale:** Experimentation based on a simulated car dataset demonstrates a detection accuracy of over 96% and considerable 40% communication overhead savings.
**Results:** Experimentation based on a simulated car dataset demonstrates a detection accuracy of over 96% and considerable 40% communication overhead savings.
