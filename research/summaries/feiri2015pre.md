# Pre-Distribution of Certificates for Pseudonymous Broadcast Authentication in VANET
M. Feiri, Rolf Pielage, J. Petit, Nicola Zannone et al. · IEEE Vehicular Technology Conference 2015 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtcspring.2015.7146029 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Certificate overhead and cryptographic packet loss during pseudonym changes.
**Approach:** Combine certificate omission with pre-distribution of new pseudonym certificates.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation (tool not named in abstract).
**Results:** Improves awareness quality during pseudonym changes.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial: engine models 1609.2 certificate/digest signing and pseudonym change; this paper studies the awareness effect of omission.
**Novelty implications:** Effect of certificate omission on awareness at pseudonym change is studied; closed-loop safety effect with full SCMS remains open.
