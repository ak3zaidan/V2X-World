# V2G authentication and quantum key distribution scheme based on extended chaotic mapping
Guo Sheng · Journal of Computational Methods in Sciences and Engineering 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1177/14727978251371188 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** V2G technology enables bidirectional energy transfer from EVs to the grid. It is possible to use the grid to charge EVs and to use EVs to charge the grid during peak grid hours, thus alleviating the problem of grid load fluctuations and improving grid efficiency.
**Approach:** In this paper, I propose an extended chaotic mapping-based authentication and quantum key distribution scheme to defend against quantum attacks and prevent known traditional security attacks.
**Tools and scale:** The comparative analysis shows that the protocol is suitable for practical implementation in a quantum environment.
**Results:** It is possible to use the grid to charge EVs and to use EVs to charge the grid during peak grid hours, thus alleviating the problem of grid load fluctuations and improving grid efficiency. Also, experimental results demonstrate that our protocol is lightweight compared to existing authentication protocols, as listed in the performance analysis section.
