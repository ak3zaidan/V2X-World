# Blockchain-Based Decentralized Trust Management in Vehicular Networks
Zhe Yang, Kan Yang, Lei Lei, Kan Zheng et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2018.2836144 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular networks enable vehicles to generate and broadcast messages in order to improve traffic safety and efficiency. However, due to the nontrusted environments, it is difficult for vehicles to evaluate the credibilities of received messages.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a decentralized trust management system in vehicular networks based on blockchain techniques.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation results reveal that the proposed system is effective and feasible in collecting, calculating, and storing trust values in vehicular networks.
**Results:** Vehicular networks enable vehicles to generate and broadcast messages in order to improve traffic safety and efficiency. Based on the validation result, the vehicle will generate a rating for each message source vehicle.
