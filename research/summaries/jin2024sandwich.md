Yifei Jin, Ali Maatouk, Sarunas Girdzijauskas, Shugong Xu et al. · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.08767 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Approach:** SANDWICH offline differentiable generative RT surrogate.
