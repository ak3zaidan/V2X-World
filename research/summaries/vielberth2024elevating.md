# Elevating TARA: A Maturity Model for Automotive Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment
Manfred Vielberth, Kristina Raab, Magdalena Glas, Patrick Grümer et al. · Proceedings of the 19th International Conference on Availability, Reliability and Security 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3664476.3670888 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, conclusion/future-work sections)

**Problem:** The importance of automotive cybersecurity is increasing in tandem with the evolution of more complex vehicles, fueled by trends like V2X or over-the-air updates.
**Approach:** Regulatory bodies are trying to cope with this problem with the introduction of ISO 21434, which standardizes automotive cybersecurity engineering. One piece of the puzzle for compliant cybersecurity engineering is the creation of a TARA (Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment) for identifying and managing cybersecurity risks.
