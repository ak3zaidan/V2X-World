# An evaluation of reputation with regard to the opportunistic forwarding of messages in VANETs
Luz M. Santos J., Edson Moreira · EURASIP Journal on Wireless Communications and Networking 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1186/s13638-019-1518-x · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Tracking behaviour under changing pseudonyms for reputation.
**Approach:** Centralized reputation system for message creation and forwarding, described with an ontology and finite state machines.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO 0.21, OMNeT++ 4.6, Veins 3.0, Crypto++ ECC; urban grid.
**Results:** Centralized reputation reflects behaviour as long as feedback reaches the reputation server.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Depends on feedback delivery to the server.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — centralized authority tracking behaviour across pseudonyms (MA-like), with crypto in Veins.
**Novelty implications:** Centralized MA-like reputation in Veins exists.
