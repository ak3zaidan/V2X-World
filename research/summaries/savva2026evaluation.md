# Evaluation of Adaptive Fuzzy Logic-based IDS (AFLIDS) for the Detection of Jamming Attacks in Vehicular Wireless Networks
Michael Savva, Iacovos I. Ioannou, Vasos Vassiliou · International Conference on Distributed Computing in Smart Systems and the Internet of Things (DCOSS-IoT) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/dcoss-iot69657.2026.00119 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular wireless networks are highly vulnerable to radio frequency (RF) jamming attacks, which can disrupt critical vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) and vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I) communications and compromise road safety.
**Approach:** This paper presents AFLIDS, an Adaptive Fuzzy Logic-based Intrusion Detection System designed for explainable and lightweight RF jamming detection in vehicular wireless networks. To further enhance detection robustness under high-mobility conditions, an adaptive Speed-High tightening mechanism is introduced to mitigate mobility-induced false negatives.
