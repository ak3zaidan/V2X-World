# Flexible Non-interactive Short-term Implicit Certificate Generation for VANETs
Rui Liu, Yun Lu, Jianping Pan · arXiv.org 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02607 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** SCMS provisioning cannot meet growing pseudonym demand (storage, connectivity, download overhead).
**Approach:** Non-interactive vehicle-generated short-term key pairs and implicit certificates using sanitizable signatures, on top of SCMS.
**Tools and scale:** Evaluation and comparison.
**Results:** Reduced communication cost and flexibility in generation and pseudonym change.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Many extensions and applications remain to be explored
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — Alternative to SCMS pseudonym provisioning/top-up which our engine models.
**Novelty implications:** Non-interactive provisioning proposed; network-level comparison with butterfly top-ups open.
