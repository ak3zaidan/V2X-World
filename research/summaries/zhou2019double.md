# Double Mix-Zone for Location Privacy in VANET
Yuye Zhou, Dongmei Zhang · Proceedings of the 2019 7th International Conference on Information Technology: IoT and Smart City 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3377170.3377250 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The mix-zone is considered to be a spatial privacy-based location privacy protection method.
**Approach:** The mix-zone confuses the tracker's sight by replacing the pseudonym when exiting the mix-zone, destroying the continuity of the location information, thereby ensuring vehicle safety. However, the mix-zone is generally distributed at traffic junctions such as intersections.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: pseudonym change/privacy).
**Results:** Therefore, the attacker can lock the information by the relative time, position and speed of the pseudonyms to increase the pseudonym's linkability.Thereby greatly reducing the protection of the mix-zone. In this paper, for inference attacks, we group vehicles in the mix-zone according to the direction of the exit and add noise into the vehicle location data to reduce the tracking probability.
