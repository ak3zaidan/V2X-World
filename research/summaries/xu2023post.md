# Post-Quantum Authentication Against Cyber-Physical Attacks in V2X-Based Autonomous Vehicle Platoon
Dongyang Xu, Keping Yu, Lei Liu, Guolong Chen, Neeraj Kumar, Mohsen Guizani et al. · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2023.3339787 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Securing platoon initial access against quantum and physical attacks.
**Approach:** QC-MDPC code-based PQ encryption plus ICA signal separation; analytical failure probability across cyber and physical planes.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Reduced system failures under attacks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - PQ for platoons with cyber-physical failure analysis.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on SCMS migration claims.
