# Towards a Maturity Model for Crypto-Agility Assessment
Julian Hohm, Andreas Heinemann, Alexander Wiesmaier · Lecture Notes in Computer Science 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-30122-3_7 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This work proposes the Crypto-Agility Maturity Model (CAMM for short), a maturity model for determining the state of crypto-agility of a given software or IT landscape.
**Approach:** Initial feedback from field experts confirms that CAMM has a well-designed structure and is easy to comprehend. We expect that this will enable companies and to respond better and faster to threats resulting from broken cryptographic schemes.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** Based on our model, the crytographic agility of an IT landscape can be systematically measured and improved step by step.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
