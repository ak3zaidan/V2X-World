# A Defense System: Jamming Detection and Mitigation for Safety Applications in Vehicular Networks
Huong Nguyen-Minh, Tran Hoang Tung, Huynh Vinh Nam et al. · International Journal of Emerging Technology and Advanced Engineering 2023 · https://doi.org/10.46338/ijetae0223_05 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The periodically exchanged basic safety messages (BSM) in vehicular networks have become a new attack target for jamming attacks that are easy to conduct in a vehicular environment.
**Approach:** This paper considers a comprehensive defense system detecting and mitigating jamming attacks. We analyze the impact of the jamming attack on BSMs on our initially proposed random channel surfing scheme coupling with a detection method.
**Tools and scale:** We study the defense system by a mathematical model which is validated by simulations in NS-3.
**Results:** The detection method can hardly provide 100% accuracy, and this consequently delays the reaction.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — addresses misbehaviour/attack detection (direction 1 area); security overhead or cryptography (direction 2 area), but per the abstract without an integrated SCMS backend, closed-loop revocation, per-message joint ground truth or counterfactual reruns.
**Novelty implications:** Does not rule out any candidate direction on the evidence of the abstract. Leaves open quantifying this in a closed loop with real SCMS revocation and traffic safety outcomes.
