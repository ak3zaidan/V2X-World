# Intrusion Detection Systems for Intra-Vehicle Networks: A Review
Omar Y. Al-Jarrah, Carsten R. Maple, Mehrdad Dianati, David Oxtoby et al. · IEEE Access 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2019.2894183 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A modern vehicle is a complex system of sensors, electronic control units, and actuators connected through different types of intra-vehicle networks to control and monitor the state of the vehicle. In addition, modern vehicles are becoming increasingly connected to the outside world through V2X technologies.
**Approach:** This paper provides a structured and comprehensive review of the state of the art of the intra-vehicle intrusion detection systems (IDSs) for passenger vehicles. We first provide an overview of intra-vehicle networks before reviewing contemporary research in intra-vehicle IDSs.
**Tools and scale:** The approach employed is to categorize the reviewed works based on their detection technique and to examine the used feature and feature selection methods, evaluation dataset, attack type, performance metrics, and benchmark models.
