# Two-layer consensus based on master-slave consortium chain data sharing for Internet of Vehicles
Feng Zhao, Benchang Yang, Chunhai Li, Chuan Zhang et al. · arXiv.org 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10680 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Due to insufficient scalability, the existing consortium chain cannot meet the requirements of low latency, high throughput, and high security when applied to Internet of Vehicles (IoV) data sharing. Therefore, we propose a two-layer consensus algorithm based on the master-slave consortium chain - Weighted Raft and Byzantine Fault Tolerance (WRBFT).
**Approach:** Therefore, we propose a two-layer consensus algorithm based on the master-slave consortium chain - Weighted Raft and Byzantine Fault Tolerance (WRBFT).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The inter-group consensus adopts practical Byzantine fault tolerance (PBFT) based on BLS aggregate signature with nonlinear coefficients to ensure that the inter-group consensus can tolerate 1/3 of Byzantine nodes. A large number of experimental results show that the proposed WRBFT algorithm reduces delay, and improves throughput and system security.
