# NetGen: A Network Digital Twin Generator
Thomas Sandholm, Sayandev Mukherjee, Ashwini Kadam, John Bahr, Irene Macaluso · ICC 2024 - IEEE International Conference on Communications 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc51166.2024.10623026 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Exploring network configurations from packet captures.
**Approach:** NetGen: generates a DES instance from a pcap, fits parameters with GA mixing real and NN-surrogate fitness.
**Tools and scale:** Wi-Fi testbed.
**Results:** Surrogate speeds fitting 17% and improves fit 1.8x; 153% better collision probability and 110% better throughput predictions vs random.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - simulator calibration with surrogates for what-if.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
