# Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) Datasets for Machine Learning-Based Predictive Quality of Service
Marco Skocaj, Nicola Di Cicco, Tommaso Zugno, Mate Boban, Jiri Blumenstein, Ales Prokes et al. · IEEE Communications Magazine 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mcom.004.2200723 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Lack of V2X datasets for ML-based predictive QoS.
**Approach:** Two public V2I and V2V radio-channel measurement datasets with Bayesian forecasting examples.
**Tools and scale:** Real measurement campaigns; IEEE Dataport; CodeOcean code.
**Results:** Illustrative uncertainty-aware QoS and CSI forecasting.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Novel exploratory directions discussed.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - V2X datasets, radio only.
**Novelty implications:** No joint traffic/radio/PKI ground-truth dataset here.
