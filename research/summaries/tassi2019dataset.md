# A Dataset of Full-Stack ITS-G5 DSRC Communications over Licensed and Unlicensed Bands Using a Large-Scale Urban Testbed
Andrea Tassi, Ioannis Mavromatis, Robert J. Piechocki · arXiv 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2019.104368 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Need empirical data to calibrate simplified PHY layers of full-stack vehicular simulators.
**Approach:** Dataset of ITS-G5 CAM exchanges between 2 OBUs and 4 RSUs over licensed DSRC and ISM bands with RSSI and positions.
**Tools and scale:** FLOURISH test track Bristol; real ITS-G5 hardware.
**Results:** PDR versus RSSI derivable across the track for each band; not intended for propagation modelling.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Dataset not intended for signal propagation modelling.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — calibration dataset for PDR(RSSI) PHY abstractions we could use.
**Novelty implications:** Real-world PDR data exists; can be used for our PHY validation.
