# A Comparative Analysis of a Novel Security Framework for Misbehaviour Detection in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Ila Naqvi, Alka Chaudhary, Anil Kumar · International Journal of Intelligent Systems and Applications in Engineering 2024 · https://ijisae.org/index.php/IJISAE/article/view/4991 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Robust VANET misbehaviour detection.
**Approach:** SFMD: genetic algorithm feature selection with ANN fitness, plus deep learning classifier with contextual data.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi-style JSON data converted to CSV.
**Results:** Binary precision/recall/F1 0.9999; multiclass F1 0.9977.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Validate in real-world VANET scenarios; large-scale experiments in urban and suburban settings.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — offline MBD.
**Novelty implications:** No impact; near-perfect offline scores illustrate dataset saturation.
