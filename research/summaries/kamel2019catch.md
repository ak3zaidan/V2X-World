# CaTch: A Confidence Range Tolerant Misbehavior Detection Approach
Joseph Kamel, Arnaud Kaiser, Ines ben Jemaa, Pierpaolo Cincilla et al. · 2019 IEEE Wireless Communications and Networking Conference (WCNC) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wcnc.2019.8885740 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** MBD plausibility/consistency checks ignore sensor inaccuracy information.
**Approach:** CaTch: confidence-range-tolerant checks integrating data inaccuracy and producing an uncertainty factor. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Extensive simulations (F2MD/Veins).
**Results:** Accurate uncertainty attribution and better performance than state-of-the-art.
