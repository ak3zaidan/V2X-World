# Systematic Literature Review of VANET Simulators: Comparative Analysis, Technological Advancements, and Research Challenges
Reham Almutairi, Giacomo Bergami, Graham Morgan · 2024 International Symposium on Parallel Computing and Distributed Systems (PCDS) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/pcds61776.2024.10743218 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Do VANET simulators meet new requirements (scalability, real-time, energy)?
**Approach:** Systematic review comparing simulators.
**Tools and scale:** Review.
**Results:** No simulator supports renewable energy integration or real-time battery recharging; significant gap in simulating effects of encryption without implementing specific encryption algorithms.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Gaps: renewable energy, recharging, encryption-effect simulation.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — identifies a gap in security-effect simulation that our engine addresses with real 1609.2 crypto.
**Novelty implications:** Supports our claim that real cryptography in simulators is uncommon; must reconcile with Riebl 2016 and VanetSim.
