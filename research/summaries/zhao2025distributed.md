# Distributed Unknown Input Observer Design with Relaxed Conditions: Theory and Application to Vehicle Platooning
Ruixuan Zhao, Guitao Yang, Thomas Parisini, Boli Chen · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08783 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Designing observers for linear systems with both known and unknown inputs is an important problem in several research contexts, for example, fault diagnosis and fault-tolerant control, and cyber-secure control systems, and presents significant challenges in distributed state estimation due to the limited sensing capabilities of individual nodes.
**Approach:** Existing methods typically impose an individual input-to-output rank condition on each estimator node, which severely restricts applicability in practical applications. This paper presents a novel distributed unknown-input observer design scheme based on a geometric approach under much weaker assumptions than the ones available in the literature.
