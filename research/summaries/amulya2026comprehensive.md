# A Comprehensive Review of Post-Quantum Cryptography Integration into Public Key Infrastructure: Challenges, Algorithms, and Future Directions
G. Amulya, Siva Rama Krishna T · 2026 IEEE International Conference on Emerging Computing and Intelligent Technologies (ICoECIT) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icoecit68303.2026.11497097 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Development of Quantum Computers are growing rapidly, the security of today’s cryptographic systems which are working with Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) is at serious risk. RSA and ECC algorithms are the backbone of secure communication and digital identity can be breakable by quantum algorithms such as Shor’s.
**Approach:** Hence, a transition is required to Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC), which is designed to handle quantum attacks. This work provides a detailed review of how PQC can be integrated with the existing PKI, with a special focus on the auto-enrollment process.
