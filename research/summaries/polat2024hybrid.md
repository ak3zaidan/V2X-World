# Hybrid AI-Powered Real-Time Distributed Denial of Service Detection and Traffic Monitoring for Software-Defined-Based Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks: A New Paradigm for Securing Intelligent Transportation Networks
Onur Polat, Saadin Oyucu, Muammer Türkoğlu, Hüseyin Polat et al. · Applied Sciences 2024 · https://doi.org/10.3390/app142210501 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) are wireless networks that improve traffic efficiency, safety, and comfort for smart vehicle users. However, with the rise of smart and electric vehicles, traditional VANETs struggle with issues like scalability, management, energy efficiency, and dynamic pricing.
**Approach:** This study proposes a security system that incorporates a hybrid artificial intelligence model to detect DDoS attacks targeting the SDN controller in SD-VANET architecture. The proposed system is designed to operate as a module within the SDN controller, enabling the detection of DDoS attacks.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset, deep learning (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** According to experimental results, the proposed attack detection system identified that approximately 90% of the traffic in the SD-VANET network under DDoS attack consisted of malicious DDoS traffic flows.
