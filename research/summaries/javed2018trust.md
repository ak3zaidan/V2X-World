# Trust-based security adaptation mechanism for Vehicular Sensor Networks
M. A. Javed, S. Zeadally, Zara Hamid · Comput. Networks 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.comnet.2018.03.010 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Sensor Networks (VSNs) are foreseen as a promising technology that can provide safe and reliable road travel in smart cities. By establishing pervasive connectivity among vehicles and infrastructure units on the road, various intelligent transport applications could be realized.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a trust-based security adaptation mechanism to improve the QoS of safety applications in VSNs. The simulation results we have obtained with our proposed research shows an improvement of 25–65% in terms of safety awareness and 33–53% improvement in terms of packet inter-arrival time of safety applications in VSNs.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** The simulation results we have obtained with our proposed research shows an improvement of 25–65% in terms of safety awareness and 33–53% improvement in terms of packet inter-arrival time of safety applications in VSNs.
