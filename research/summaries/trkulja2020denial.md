# Denial-of-Service Attacks on C-V2X Networks
Nataša Trkulja, David Starobinski, Randall A. Berry · Proceedings Third International Workshop on Automotive and Autonomous Vehicle Security 2020 · https://doi.org/10.14722/autosec.2021.23006 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Security posture of C-V2X Mode 4 sensing-based semi-persistent scheduling.
**Approach:** DoS via adversarial resource block selection: oblivious, smart (monitoring) and cooperative adversaries; analysis and simulation.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical model and simulation of Rel-14 Mode 4.
**Results:** At low density smart and cooperative attacks significantly hurt performance; at high density oblivious attacks are nearly as effective.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future work discussed in conclusion (not extracted).
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — LTE-V2X Mode 4 MAC-level DoS by resource selection, which our engine's LTE-V2X stack and attack set can reproduce.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first resource-selection DoS on C-V2X; coupling with security verification load and safety remains.
