# Automated vehicle’s behavior decision making using deep reinforcement learning and high-fidelity simulation environment
Yingjun Ye, Xiaohui Zhang, Jian Sun · Transportation Research Part C: Emerging Technologies 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trc.2019.08.011 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Automated vehicles are deemed to be the key element for the intelligent transportation system in the future.
**Approach:** Therefore, a framework of the decision-making training and learning is put forward in this paper.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** Many studies have been made to improve the Automated vehicles' ability of environment recognition and vehicle control, while the attention paid to decision making is not enough though the decision algorithms so far are very preliminary. The results show that on the premise of driving comfort, the efficiency of the trained Automated vehicle increases 7.9% compared to the classical traffic model, intelligent driver model.
