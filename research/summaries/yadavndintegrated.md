# Integrated Approaches for Privacy in Spatial Data: Geo-Obfuscation, Context-Aware Modeling, and Cooperative Perception
Sourabh Yadav · thesis  · https://doi.org/10.12794/metadc2482427 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Location-based services and autonomous mobility systems increasingly depend on real-time user data, raising critical concerns around location privacy.
**Approach:** The proposed LP-based obfuscation approach improves expected inference error by 41.2% and reduces utility loss by 19% while decreasing constraint complexity by 60%.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** A two-phase adversarial inference model using Bayesian filtering and Transformers increases attack accuracy by 23%, exposing the vulnerability of static defenses. To counter this, a Transformer-based generative model, TransProtect, is introduced, reducing attacker success by 37.6% and preserving 85% of service utility.
