# Misbehavior Detection Based on Support Vector Machine and Dempster-Shafer Theory of Evidence in VANETs
Chunhua Zhang, Kangqiang Chen, Xin Zeng, Xiaoping Xue · IEEE Access 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2018.2875678 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** False message and message suppression attacks in VANETs.
**Approach:** SVM data trust model and vehicle trust model with Dempster-Shafer aggregation at a trust authority. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Simulation (Veins per F2MD paper's related work).
**Results:** Gaussian kernel fits best; higher true positive rate than a compared model.
