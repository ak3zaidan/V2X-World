# ReLaDec: Reliable Latency Decision Algorithm for Connected Vehicle Applications
Haris Volos, Takashi Bando, Kenji Konishi · 2019 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ivs.2019.8814083 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Low latency is required for connected and intelligent vehicle applications. For instance, safety applications have strict latency requirements.
**Approach:** We seek to answer the question: is the current latency environment acceptable for our application? This paper presents our Reliable Latency Decision (ReLaDec) algorithm.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** We demonstrate the performance of the algorithm using 860000 latency samples that include stationary and driving data over multiple USA states.
