# Packet Delivery Impact of Predictive Resource Allocation for Quasi-Periodic Cellular V2X Communication
Hyeonji Seon, Hojeong Lee, Hyogon Kim · 2023 IEEE 97th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2023-Spring) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2023-spring57618.2023.10200198 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** CAM generation rules make traffic quasi-periodic, harming SPS.
**Approach:** ML predicts non-dropped CAM transmission times and reserves resources.
**Tools and scale:** C-V2X SPS simulation.
**Results:** Extends the distance meeting target PRR versus SPS.
