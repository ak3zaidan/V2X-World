# World Model-Enabled Causal Digital Twins for Semantic Communications in Physical AI Systems
Lingyi Wang, Tingyu Shui, Walid Saad, Pascal Adjakple · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16547 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Semantic communication has emerged as a promising paradigm for enabling goal-oriented networking. However, most existing semantic communication solutions are tailored to one-shot tasks and optimize instantaneous performance.
**Approach:** To address this gap, this paper investigates goal-oriented semantic communications for physical AI systems with closed-loop sensing-communication-inference-control.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: Sionna, AirSim, reinforcement learning, digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Extensive simulations on an AirSim-Sionna-based unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) navigation simulator show that the proposed WM-CDT framework achieves significant improvement in return-per-kbit and navigation success rate compared to existing reinforcement learning solutions.
