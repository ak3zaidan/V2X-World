# Artificial Intelligence in Cellular-V2X Networks: A Survey on Dynamic Spectrum Allocation, Traffic Prediction, and Anomaly Detection
B. Vishwanath, P. Reddy · 2024 3rd International Conference for Advancement in Technology (ICONAT) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iconat61936.2024.10774806 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As cellular-vehicle-to-everything (C-V2X) networks gain importance within emerging intelligent transportation systems, providing effective, secure network management is crucial.
**Approach:** This survey paper focuses on the opportunities for applying Artificial Intelligence (AI) to improve the performance of C-V2X networks. AI-assisted techniques for C-V2X network management are investigated, including Deep Q-learning techniques for dynamic spectrum allocation, Machine Learning for improved traffic prediction, and anomaly detection algorithms for improving C-V2X network security.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: C-V2X
