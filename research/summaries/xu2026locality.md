# A Locality-Enhanced Differential Attention Network for Robust Anomaly Detection in Consumer Vehicles
Bin Xu, Jincheng Zhao, Yan Tan, Gaofeng He · IEEE transactions on consumer electronics 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tce.2026.3665820 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Modern consumer vehicles rely on time series sensor data to operate safely.
**Approach:** However, this reliance creates a critical vulnerability, as cyberattacks can directly compromise navigation and safety functions, thereby endangering drivers and passengers. Existing security methods often perform poorly in real-world driving environments.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** They struggle to distinguish malicious attacks from benign sensor noise, which reduces their reliability. This framework combines dynamic weighting and region-sensitive scoring strategies to improve detection performance.
