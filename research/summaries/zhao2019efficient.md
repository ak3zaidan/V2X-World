# An efficient certificateless aggregate signature scheme for the Internet of Vehicles
Yanan Zhao, Yingzhe Hou, Lili Wang, Saru Kumari et al. · Transactions on Emerging Telecommunications Technologies 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1002/ett.3708 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract In recent years, the research of Internet of vehicles (IoV) has received extensive attention. In IoV, vehicles can make intelligent decisions by exchanging the real‐time traffic information between other vehicles and IoV infrastructures, thereby reducing the probability of traffic jams and accidents.
**Approach:** In this paper, we presented an advanced efficient CL‐AS scheme with elliptic curve cryptography for the IoV environment.
**Tools and scale:** We proved the security of our proposal in the random oracle based on computational Diffie‐Hellman assumption.
**Results:** To improve the efficiency, the function of aggregation was introduced into the CLS. The certificateless aggregate signature (CL‐AS) can aggregate multiple signatures into a brief signature, reducing the computational cost and the size of signature.
