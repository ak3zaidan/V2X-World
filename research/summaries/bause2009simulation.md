# A Simulation Environment for Hierarchical Process Chains Based on OMNeT++
Falko Bause, Peter Buchholz, Jan Kriege, Sebastian Vastag · SIMULATION 2009 · https://doi.org/10.1177/0037549709104236 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** OMNeT++ is a discrete event simulation environment primarily designed for communication networks.
**Approach:** In this paper we present an approach to enable OMNeT++ to simulate complex hierarchical process chains. This paper highlights the main steps of the automatic transformation of a hierarchical process chain model into a hierarchical model in OMNeT++.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: omnet, simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** Furthermore, we show how the transformation has been validated and how detailed performance figures can be evaluated with OMNeT++.
