# Detection of False Data Injection Attack in Connected and Automated Vehicles via Cloud-Based Sandboxing
Chunheng Zhao, Jasprit Singh Gill, Pierluigi Pisu, Gurcan Comert · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2021.3090361 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** False data injection on CAVs via V2X.
**Approach:** Cloud-based sandboxing of exchanged data within the control framework. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Microscopic traffic simulation.
**Results:** Detects FDI and identifies the attacker in real time under 0.2 s.
