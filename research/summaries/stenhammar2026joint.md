# Joint Clustering and Prediction of the Quality of Service in Vehicular Cellular Networks
Oscar Stenhammar, Gábor Fodor, Carlo Fischione · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12903 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Concept drift complicates QoS prediction in cellular networks.
**Approach:** Distributed BCD framework jointly clustering cells and training cluster-level Gaussian/lognormal QoS models.
**Tools and scale:** Sionna + ns-3 simulated data; one-hour horizon for latency, jitter, RSRP.
**Results:** Outperforms a single global model; MAE 9-27% lower than local cell-level predictors.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: larger deployments, uplink KPIs, cell migration between rounds, online SLA integration.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
