# Deep Learning Surrogate Models for Network Simulation
Matthew T. Dearing · Proceedings of the 38th ACM SIGSIM Conference on Principles of Advanced Discrete Simulation 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3615979.3662151 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** PDES of HPC networks is computationally heavy.
**Approach:** 1D CNN + LSTM + dense surrogate trained on CODES simulation time series.
**Tools and scale:** CODES framework.
**Results:** RMSE improved 81.5% and MAE up to 25.6% over statistical baselines (preliminary).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Preliminary results.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - DL surrogate for DES (direction 3).
**Novelty implications:** No effect on V2X.
