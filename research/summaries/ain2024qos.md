# QoS prediction in radio vehicular environments via prior user information
Noor Ul Ain, Rodrigo Hernangómez, Alexandros Palaios, Martin Kasparick, Sławomir Stańczak · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17689 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Reliable minute-range QoS prediction.
**Approach:** Tree ensembles using measurements of prior vehicles to predict a target vehicle's QoS.
**Tools and scale:** AI4Mobile 'Motorway A9 5G-ConnectedMobility' campaign data.
**Results:** Prior-vehicle information improves accuracy; longer horizons supported.
