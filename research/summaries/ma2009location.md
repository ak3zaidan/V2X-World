# A location privacy metric for V2X communication systems
Zhendong Ma, Frank Kargl, Michael Weber · 2009 IEEE Sarnoff Symposium 2009 · https://doi.org/10.1109/sarnof.2009.4850318 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The emerging vehicle-to-vehicle/vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2X) communication systems enable a new way of collaboration among the vehicles, the operators of transportation systems, and the service providers.
**Approach:** However, many functionalities of V2X systems rely on detailed location information. This raises concerns on location privacy of the users of such systems.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: location privacy).
**Results:** Without a proper metric, preserving location privacy in V2X communication systems becomes difficult due to the lack of a benchmark to evaluate any given protection mechanisms. In this paper, we develop a quantitative metric to measure and quantify location privacy in V2X systems.
