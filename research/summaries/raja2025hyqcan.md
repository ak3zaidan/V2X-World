# HyQCAN: A Quantum-Classical Synergy for Secure and Low-Latency Emergency Communication in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Gunasekaran Raja, Sudhakar Theerthagiri, Priyadarshni Vasudevan, Jeyadev Needhidevan et al. · Global Communications Conference 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/globecom59602.2025.11431673 · relevance: high · basis: abstract
