# RoNet: Toward Robust Neural Assisted Mobile Network Configuration
Yuru Zhang, Yongjie Xue, Qiang Liu et al. · arXiv 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.03206 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Automating configuration is the key path to achieving zero-touch network management in ever-complicating mobile networks.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a novel RoNet framework to improve the robustness of neural-assisted configuration policies. We design three integrated stages with novel normal training, learn-to-attack, and robust defense method for balancing the robustness and performance of policies.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: NS-3, deep learning.
**Results:** We evaluate RoNet via the NS-3 simulator extensively and the simulation results show that RoNet outperforms existing solutions in terms of robustness, adaptability, and scalability.
