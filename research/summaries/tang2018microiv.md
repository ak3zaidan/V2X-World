# MicroIV: A Cooperative Driving Hardware Simulation Platform for Cooperative-ITS
Wei Tang, Ming Yang, Zheming Lv, Qiyang Qian, Ti Su, Bing Wang et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2018.2862416 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Hardware C-ITS platforms lack global positioning, scenarios and are costly.
**Approach:** MicroIV micro-vehicle platform with camera-based positioning and IMM filtering, point-to-multipoint communication.
**Tools and scale:** Micro vehicles; platoon of 20, intersection with 8 vehicles, merging, parking.
**Results:** Meets C-ITS positioning accuracy requirement; applications shown feasible.
