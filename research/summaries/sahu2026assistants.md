# Assistants, Not Architects: The Role of LLMs in Networked Systems Design
Pratyush Sahu, Rahul Bothra, Venkat Arun, Brighten Godfrey et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25506 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Can LLMs do networked-systems architecture reasoning.
**Approach:** Finds they cannot reliably; proposes Kepler structured reasoning framework.
**Tools and scale:** Architecture design tasks.
**Results:** LLMs miss constraints, encode wrong assumptions, show stickiness.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Iterative validation by simulation is often too expensive.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — relevant to limits of our configuring agent; simulation-backed validation is what our engine provides.
**Novelty implications:** Supports value of a cheap deterministic simulator behind an LLM agent.
