# Dynamic Key-Based Certificateless Authentication Protocol for Seamless RSU switching in VANETs
Kun Wang, Yuanyuan Zhang, Ying Fang, Yang Hu · 2025 2nd Asia Pacific Conference on Computing Technologies, Communications and Networking (CTCNet) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ctcnet68009.2025.11405744 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs) are fundamental to intelligent transportation systems, enabling real-time V2V and V2I communication. However, the highly dynamic topology of modern VANETs introduces significant challenges, specifically the inefficiencies caused by frequent Roadside Unit (RSU) switching and the heavy verification burden of massive broadcast messages.
**Approach:** To resolve these security and efficiency bottlenecks, this paper presents a certificateless cryptography framework designed for rapid RSU switching and streamlined message verification. We initially introduce a fast switching mechanism utilizing a two-layer key architecture.
**Tools and scale:** Performance evaluations using Python simulations and OMNeT++ demonstrate that our framework significantly outperforms existing methods.
**Results:** By leveraging a long-term key from a Trusted Authority alongside short-term RSU session keys, this approach achieves seamless cross-RSU authentication and eliminates redundant verification steps. Performance evaluations using Python simulations and OMNeT++ demonstrate that our framework significantly outperforms existing methods.
