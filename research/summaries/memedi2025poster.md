# Poster: Simulator for Reinforcement Learning-Based Resource Management in Vehicular Edge Computing
Agon Memedi, Chunghan Lee, Seyhan Ucar, Onur Altintas, Falko Dressler · 2025 IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference (VNC) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc64509.2025.11054184 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** We aim to investigate Reinforcement Learning (RL) methods for efficient resource management in Vehicular Edge Computing (VEC).
**Approach:** To this end, we present an open-source, modular, lightweight, discrete-event simulation framework which integrates state-of-the-art tools for improved performance evaluation. By integrating realistic mobility traces, our approach presents an opportunity to evaluate the performance and scalability of different RL-based task scheduling and resource allocation policies in diverse scenarios.
