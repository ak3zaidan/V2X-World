# Variable Granularity Vehicle Digital Twin Construction Scheme for DT-Assisted IoVs
Lun Tang, Zhoulin Pu, Zhangchao Cheng, Dongxu Fang, Qianbin Chen · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2024.3447031 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As an important application scenario for 5G, the Internet of Vehicles (IoVs) achieves extensive and stable connections and real-time information interaction and sharing between vehicles and traffic infrastructure.
**Approach:** To address the challenges of low-latency synchronization, high computational overhead, and multidimensional resource scheduling faced by the vehicle digital twin (VDT) construction process within IoVs, we propose a construction scheme for variable granularity VDT. First, a variable granularity VDT construction framework for IoVs is proposed to reduce the communication pressure, edge load, and energy consumption in the network.

**Results:** Finally, simulation results verify that the proposed scheme can effectively improve the utility of VDT in different simulation scenarios while reducing system resource consumption.
