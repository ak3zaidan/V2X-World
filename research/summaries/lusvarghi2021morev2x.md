# MoReV2X - A New Radio Vehicular Communication Module for ns-3
Luca Lusvarghi, Maria Luisa Merani · 2021 IEEE 94th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2021-Fall) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2021-fall52928.2021.9625478 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Need open simulation of 5G NR-V2X sidelink.
**Approach:** MoReV2X ns-3 module following 3GPP specs with traffic models, NR V2V channel and KPIs.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3, NR-V2X Mode 2; periodic and aperiodic traffic.
**Results:** Shows impact of subcarrier spacing on performance for periodic and aperiodic traffic.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — NR-V2X sidelink simulation, which our engine also provides.
**Novelty implications:** NR-V2X sidelink simulation modules exist; we cannot claim the NR stack as novel.
