# Cyber attacks and detection mechanisms for driverless cars
Xinzhuo Wang · DR-NTU (Nanyang Technological University) 2026 · https://hdl.handle.net/10356/216207 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Due to the speedy development of autonomous vehicle systems and Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANET), cars are quickly changing as transportation tools into intelligent and connected mobile devices.
**Approach:** In order to overcome these issues, two main methods are used in this paper: ADASYN (Adaptive Synthetic Sampling Approach), an oversampling algorithm that creates synthesized data of minority class examples depending on their learning rate of minority classes to balance the class distribution; and GMM (Gaussian Mixture Model), a probabilistic modelling approach that maintains and adds value to the intrinsic distribution and diversity of majority classes.
