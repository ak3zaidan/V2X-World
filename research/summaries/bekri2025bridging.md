# Bridging Language Models and Formal Methods for Intent-Driven Optical Network Design
Anis Bekri, Amar Abane, Abdella Battou, Saddek Bensalem · 2025 IEEE/ACS 22nd International Conference on Computer Systems and Applications (AICCSA) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/aiccsa66935.2025.11315283 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** LLMs translate natural-language intents into optical network designs unreliably.
**Approach:** Hybrid pipeline: LLM intent parsing, formal verification and optical RAG.
**Tools and scale:** Optical network design.
**Results:** Qualitative claims of verifiable designs.
