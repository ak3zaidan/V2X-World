# Software-Defined Vehicular Networks With Trust Management: A Deep Reinforcement Learning Approach
Dajun Zhang, F. Richard Yu, Ruizhe Yang, Li Zhu · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2020.3025684 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The appropriate design of a vehicular ad hoc network (VANET) has become a pivotal way to build an efficient smart transportation system, which enables various applications associated with traffic safety and highly-efficient transportation. VANETs are vulnerable to the threat of malicious nodes stemming from its dynamicity and infrastructure-less nature and causing performance degradation.
**Approach:** In this article, we propose a novel software-defined trust based VANET architecture (SD-TDQL) in which the centralized SDN controller is served as a learning agent to get the optimal communication link policy using a deep $Q$ -learning approach. Moreover, we design a trust model to avoid the bad influence of malicious vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Simulation results prove that the proposed SD-TDQL framework enhances the link quality.
