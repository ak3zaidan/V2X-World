# Can Vehicles Foresee Communication Disruptions?: Feasibility of V2X Network Digital Twin
Takamasa Higuchi, M. Takanashi, Kengo Sasaki, Yuma Taguchi, K. Sanda · 2025 IEEE International Conference on Pervasive Computing and Communications Workshops and other Affiliated Events (PerCom Workshops) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/percomworkshops65533.2025.00150 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicles need to foresee communication disruptions.
**Approach:** V2X Network Digital Twin concept with digital representations of vehicles, roads and networks predicting performance via simulation or ML; proof of concept on a co-simulation framework.
**Tools and scale:** Co-simulation framework.
**Results:** Initial feasibility shown.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Initial feasibility only.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - V2X DT built on co-simulation; no security.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
