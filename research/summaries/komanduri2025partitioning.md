# Partitioning Prompts for Higher Efficacy in Network Design with Large Language Model
Vishnu Komanduri, Scott Alessio, Sebastian Estropia, Gokhan Yerdelen et al. · 2025 IEEE 26th International Conference on High Performance Switching and Routing (HPSR) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/hpsr64165.2025.11038889 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** LLM-generated network configurations are often incomplete or wrong.
**Approach:** Partitioning prompts into focused sub-deliverables with a scoring policy.
**Tools and scale:** ChatGPT, Copilot, DeepSeek; three complexity levels.
**Results:** Partitioned prompting more accurate and consistent than non-partitioned.
