# Poster: QueuePilot: Towards Adaptive Queue Scheduling via Live Digital Twins
Francisco Germano Vogt, Zhiheng Yang, Victor Hugo Schneider Lopes, Fabricio Rodriguez et al. · Proceedings of the 21st International Conference on emerging Networking EXperiments and Technologies 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3765515.3771755 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Changing switch queue policies in production is risky.
**Approach:** QueuePilot live digital twin cloning traffic across queueing strategies.
**Tools and scale:** Two Tofino switches.
**Results:** Preliminary: twin synchronises and evaluates alternatives in parallel.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Preliminary results only.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — parallel evaluation of alternative policies on identical input, akin to counterfactual reruns, in switches.
**Novelty implications:** No V2X claim affected.
