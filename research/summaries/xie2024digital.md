# Digital Twin Vehicular Edge Computing Network: Task Offloading and Resource Allocation
Yu Xie, Qiong Wu, Pingyi Fan · 2024 7th International Conference on Information Communication and Signal Processing (ICICSP) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icicsp62589.2024.10809180 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the increasing demand for multiple applications on internet of vehicles. It requires vehicles to carry out multiple computing tasks in real time.
**Approach:** In this paper, a multi task digital twin (DT) VEC network is established. To solve it, we propose a multi-agent reinforcement learning method on the task offloading and resource allocation.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: reinforcement learning, digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Numerous experiments demonstrate that our method is effective compared to other benchmark algorithms.
