# A Systematic Literature Review on the Security Evaluation of Autonomous Vehicle Networks Using Common Criteria
I Wayan Widi Pradnyana, Muhammad Ghozi Aqsha Munadi, Ashja Radithya Lesmana, Muhammad Nur Alif Ramadhan · 2025 International Conference on ICT for Smart Society (ICISS) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iciss66954.2025.11389474 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper presents a systematic literature review (SLR) analyzing the implementation of Common Criteria and ISO/SAE 21434 standards in securing communication within autonomous vehicle networks (AVNs). The study aims to identify vulnerabilities, mitigation strategies, and technical gaps related to securing both in-vehicle and vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communication.
**Approach:** Our analysis shows that many AVNs suffer from weaknesses stemming from the lack of strong authentication, incomplete data encryption, and poor network segmentation, which collectively undermine the confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity of messages exchanged in these networks.
