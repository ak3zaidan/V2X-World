# Efficient Privacy-Preserving and Secure Authentication for Electric-Vehicle-to-Electric-Vehicle-Charging System Based on ECQV
Abdullah M. Almuhaideb, Sammar S. Algothami · Journal of Sensor and Actuator Networks 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/jsan11020028 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Privacy-preserving authentication for EV-to-EV charging.
**Approach:** ECQV implicit-certificate based protocol.
**Tools and scale:** Security analysis.
**Results:** Meets security requirements with fewer messages than related works.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Protocol for selling energy to the grid; blockchain and hybrid models with ECQV.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — EV charging; shares ECQV with SCMS.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
