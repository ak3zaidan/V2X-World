# Distributed control under compromised measurements: Resilient estimation, attack detection, and vehicle platooning
Xingkang He, Ehsan Hashemi, Karl H. Johansson · Automatica 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.automatica.2021.109953 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Platoon state estimation and control when some sensors are compromised.
**Approach:** Resilient observer with saturated innovation, distributed attack detector (attacked/attack-free/suspected sets), observer-based controller.
**Tools and scale:** Platooning simulation.
**Results:** Finite-time identification of attacked sensors; error bounds; closed-loop stability.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future work: detection on actuators; more general vehicle/sensor models; topologies beyond strings with multiple leaders.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — control.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
