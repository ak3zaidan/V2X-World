# Digital Twin-Based Channel Generation Toolchain and Foundation Model for Low-Altitude XL-MIMO
Mengyuan Li, Yu Han, Jia-Chen Tian et al. · IEEE Journal on Selected Topics in Signal Processing 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jstsp.2026.3705369 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The rapid development of the low-altitude economy (LAE) has created growing demand for reliable aerial communication systems.
**Approach:** To address this issue, we develop LAETwin-XL, a digital twin (DT)-based toolchain and dataset for XL-MIMO research in LAE scenarios. Built on the Sionna ray-tracing (RT) module, the proposed toolchain simulates near-field and far-field channels with diverse wireless labels for practical environments.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed framework achieves effective zero-shot channel extrapolation performance. Furthermore, using lightweight task heads and limited training data, it enables parameter-efficient transfer to various downstream tasks (e.g., channel estimation, classification, and localization), delivering high accuracy and robustness even under sparse antenna observations.
