# LLM-based Continuous Intrusion Detection Framework for Next-Gen Networks
Frederic Adjewa, Moez Esseghir, Leila Merghem-Boulahia · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.03354 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Continuous detection of emerging network attacks.
**Approach:** Transformer encoder plus GMM clustering of BERT embeddings.
**Tools and scale:** Network traffic data.
**Results:** 100% recall in detection; 95.6% accuracy and recall after new clusters.
