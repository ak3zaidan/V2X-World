# Creating a realistic sybil attack dataset for inter-vehicle communication
Taner Guven, Z. Cihan Taysi · Peer-to-Peer Networking and Applications 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s12083-025-02058-w · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** Lack of realistic RSSI datasets for Sybil detection.
**Approach:** Veins environment on 11 km2 Istanbul map with calibrated propagation, four Sybil methods and power control; public code and dataset.
**Tools and scale:** Veins, SUMO, OpenStreetMap, municipal traffic data.
**Results:** Dataset with power-control attacks; no public dataset had these features.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future work on building and landscape data effects on propagation.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — Realistic-map simulation producing PHY-level labelled attack datasets, similar to our engine's dataset/benchmark role.
**Novelty implications:** Realistic RSSI Sybil dataset exists; per-message counterfactual and PKI ground truth remain open.
