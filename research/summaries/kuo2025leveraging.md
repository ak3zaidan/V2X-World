# Leveraging Large Language Models for Malicious Packet Generation in Simulated V2X Frameworks
Jong-Yih Kuo, S.-H. Chang, Shaoke Fang, Cheng-Hsuan Kuo · 2025 5th International Conference on Robotics, Automation, and Artificial Intelligence (RAAI) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/raai67517.2025.11423371 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the rapid development of Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) technologies, real-time interactions among vehicles, roadside units, pedestrians, and communication networks have become a vital component of intelligent transportation systems (ITS). However, the high connectivity and complexity of V2X systems also introduce significant cybersecurity risks.
**Approach:** However, the high connectivity and complexity of V2X systems also introduce significant cybersecurity risks. This study develops a simulation framework based on Taiwan's TCROS standard and Eclipse-MOSAIC, integrating LLMs to automate the generation of malicious packets.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting (VANET/IoV/V2X). Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** Results indicate that, with appropriate prompt design and domain knowledge, LLMs can effectively generate structurally valid attack packets that disrupt system behavior.
