# The impact of ECDSA in a VANET routing service: Insights from real data traces
Pedro Cirne, A. Zúquete, S. Sargento, Miguel Luís · Ad hoc networks 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adhoc.2018.08.017 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ECDSA validation cost may exhaust devices' capacity for routing control-plane messages.
**Approach:** Evaluates ECDSA impact on a VANET routing control plane using WAVE/ETSI curves, several libraries and hardware.
**Tools and scale:** Emulation driven by day-long real VANET connectivity traces (Porto vehicular network).
**Results:** ECDSA validation delays may force discarding many routing updates (abstract).
