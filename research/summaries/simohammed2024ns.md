# NS+NDT: Smart integration of Network Simulation in Network Digital Twin, application to IoT networks
Samir Si-Mohammed, Anthony Bardou, T. Begin, I. G. Lassous, P. Vicat-Blanc · Future generations computer systems 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.future.2024.03.038 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Network simulation and network digital twins are confused.
**Approach:** Clarifies NS vs NDT and proposes NS+NDT, using NDT data to calibrate simulators (e.g., energy models) for IoT.
**Tools and scale:** IoT networks.
**Results:** Qualitative plus calibration example.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- NS barriers of credibility and reliability.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - simulator credibility via real data; conceptual.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
