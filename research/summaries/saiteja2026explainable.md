# Explainable AI-Driven BiGRU Model for Cyber-Attack Detection in Internet of Vehicles (IoV) and Intelligent Connected Vehicles (ICV) Networks
Bethi Saiteja, Sudha Rani V · International Conference on Emerging Trends in Engineering and Medical Sciences (ICETEMS) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icetems66917.2026.11469271 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The safety, automation and efficiency have out most important factors in Intent of vehicles (IoVs) and intelligent connected vehicle (ICVs).
**Approach:** The advanced digital era completely depends on smart mobility, autonomous navigation UAV's and UGV's. The increased connectivity exposes huge vehicular network so that cyber-attacks, spoofing, false data injection, jamming and denial of services (DoS) have the challenging components.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The proposed XAI-BiGRU model attains high detection accuracy 98 %, faster inference and true positive rate 98.23 % compared to state of art model like LSTM, GRU, CNN and RCNN based IDS.
