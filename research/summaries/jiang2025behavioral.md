# Behavioral profiling of abnormal network traffic in security
Yi-Yi Jiang, Hailan Wang, Xi-Lai Wang · Other Conferences 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3060427 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the wide application of the Internet and the spread of information technology in all walks of life, network security issues have become increasingly prominent.
**Approach:** Abnormal network traffic is usually a manifestation of attack behavior and also contains important indicators of network security threats. Analyzing abnormal network traffic and establishing corresponding patterns or models is particularly important.

**Results:** We verify the effectiveness and practicality of the proposed method through experiments, and analyze the experimental results, and effectively reflect various network attack behaviors, and discuss the challenges and improvement directions among them.
