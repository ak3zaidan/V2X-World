# V2X enhanced Digital Twin for public bus priority. Towards Agentic-enabled Transport Systems
Antonio Lalaguna, Elena Lopez-Aguilera, Ivan Rodero, Juan Jose Ruiz et al. · 2026 IEEE Conference on Artificial Intelligence (CAI) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cai68641.2026.11536542 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper presents a vision-oriented systems contribution that advances the integration of V2X-standardized data, distributed Digital Twin (DT) architectures, and emerging Agentic AI concepts to enhance public Transport Signal Priority (TSP) in urban mobility environments. We propose a V2X-first Transportation Digital Twin architecture deployed across the cloud–edge continuum, enabling real-time synchronization between physical infrastructure, predictive AI models, and simulation components.
**Approach:** The value of this approach is illustrated through preliminary operational evidence, including a real-world deployment of a TSP service along public bus Line 4 in Granada, achieving a promising reduction in transit time, as well as initial LSTM-based experiments for short-term traffic intensity forecasting.
