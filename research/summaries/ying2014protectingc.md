# Protecting location privacy in vehicular networks against location-based attacks
Bidi Ying, Dimitrios Makrakis · International Journal of Parallel, Emergent and Distributed Systems 2014 · https://doi.org/10.1080/17445760.2014.910665 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Location privacy is an important issue in location-based services (LBSs), current protecting location privacy methods cannot be used in vehicular networks due to the constrained vehicular mobility.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a new cloaking algorithm which takes road segments and vehicles into consideration. We start with a definition of a location-based attack, and then design a new cloaking algorithm based on neighbour spaces.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: location privacy).
**Results:** The experimental results show that our method can be used for continuous LBSs under vehicular networks, and resist a location-based attack while having efficiencies in terms of cloaking time and successful rate.
