# Misbehavior Detection in Intelligent Transportation Systems Based on Federated Learning
Enrique Mármol Campos, José  Luis Hernández Ramos, Aurora González Vidal, Gianmarco Baldini, Antonio Skarmeta · Internet of Things 2023 · https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.4432351 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Centralized ML MBD raises privacy and latency issues.
**Approach:** Federated learning MBD on VeReMi re-balanced with SMOTE-Tomek; optimised MLP for multi-class.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi; varied balancing and node counts.
**Results:** Overall accuracy 93% multi-class.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — FL MBD offline.
**Novelty implications:** FL MBD exists.
