# Integrating Lightweight Algorithms with Blockchain for Secure and Efficient Authentication in VANETs
Dumpala Prasanth, S. Christy · 2024 International Conference on System, Computation, Automation and Networking (ICSCAN) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icscan62807.2024.10894204 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** However, existing authentication mechanisms often face challenges such as high computational overhead, scalability issues, and latency, particularly in resource-constrained environments.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a novel framework that integrates lightweight algorithms with Blockchain technology to achieve secure and efficient authentication in VANETs. The proposed approach leverages Blockchain's decentralized trust management and tamper-proof ledger with the computational efficiency of lightweight algorithms like TESLA.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** A detailed performance evaluation demonstrates the efficacy of the proposed method, highlighting significant improvements in scalability, security, and resource efficiency.
