# Federated Learning-Based Intrusion Detection for Vehicular Networks Using Robust LSTM
Bohan Zeng, Bo Mi · International Conference on Artificial Intelligence, Internet of Things and Cloud Computing Technology (AIoTC) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/aiotc66747.2025.11198605 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In recent years, advancements in the Internet of Things (IoT) and its integration into vehicles have given rise to a promising new paradigm: the Internet of Vehicles (IoV).
**Approach:** The IoV enables the sharing of information among vehicles through onboard communication technologies, thereby enhancing both traffic safety and efficiency. However, due to its openness and highly dynamic nature, the IoV is particularly vulnerable to network attacks.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: VEREMI, VeReMi
**Results:** Our approach leverages an optimized LSTM model and incorporates the latest FedAware aggregation algorithm to address the challenge of non-independent and identically distributed (non-IID) data across vehicle nodes, thus improving detection accuracy and efficiency. Experimental results on the publicly available VeReMi Extension IoV intrusion detection dataset validate the effectiveness of the proposed method.
