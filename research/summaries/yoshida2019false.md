# A false density information attack detection scheme using overlap of communication range in VANET
Masashi Yoshida, Hiromu Asahina, Shuichiro Haruta et al. · IEICE Communications Express 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1587/comex.2019xbl0003 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** : In Vehicular Ad Hoc Network (VANET), it is important to detect false information.
**Approach:** We propose a false density information attack detection scheme using overlap of communication range of vehicles. Simulation results show that the proposed scheme improves the true positive rate of false information detection.
