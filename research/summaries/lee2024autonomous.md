# Autonomous Traffic and Communication Integrated Simulator for V2X Performance Evaluation
Seungjae Lee, Taesik Nam, Kiwoong Park, Donghyeok Shin, Wonyul Kang, Yongjae Jang et al. · 2024 IEEE 100th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2024-Fall) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2024-fall63153.2024.10757513 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Evaluating V2X in real road environments.
**Approach:** Integrates WiLabV2Xsim (C-V2X and NR-V2X) with HEXAGON VTD and ASAM OpenSCENARIO.
**Tools and scale:** WiLabV2Xsim, VTD.
**Results:** Integration presented.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: 5GAA TR scenarios, resource allocation, decentralised congestion control.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — LTE/NR-V2X system-level with traffic.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
