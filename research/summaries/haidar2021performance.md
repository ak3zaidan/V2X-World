# Performance Evaluation of Pseudonym Reload over Cellular Technology
Farah Haidar, Farah-Emma Braiteh, B. Lonc, Arnaud Kaiser et al. · International Conference on New Technologies, Mobility and Security 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ntms49979.2021.9432646 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Performance of pseudonym certificate reloading from the C-ITS PKI over cellular.
**Approach:** Measured pseudonym reload over 4G, static and while driving.
**Tools and scale:** Real 4G field tests with a C-ITS PKI.
**Results:** Reload performance measured (figures not in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong: engine simulates pseudonym top-ups over cellular TCP/TLS; this paper measures the same process on real 4G.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first evaluation of pseudonym reload over cellular; can use it to validate engine top-up latency.
