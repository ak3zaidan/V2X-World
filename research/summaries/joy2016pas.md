Josh Joy, Mario Gerla · arXiv 2016 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.04892 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In today's digital world, personal data is being continuously collected and analyzed without data owners' consent and choice.
**Approach:** This paper introduces PAS-MC, the first practical privacy-preserving and anonymity stream analytics system.
