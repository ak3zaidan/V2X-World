# The Design and Implementation of Kerberos-Blockchain Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks Authentication Across Diverse Network Scenarios
Maya Rahayu, Md. Biplob Hossain, Samsul Huda, Yuta Kodera et al. · Italian National Conference on Sensors 2024 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s24237428 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** However, the network exposure makes VANETs susceptible to cyber threats, making authentication crucial for ensuring security and integrity.
**Approach:** We propose an authentication system for VANETs using blockchain and Kerberos for storing authentication messages in a blockchain ledger accessible to Trusted Authentication Servers (TASs) and Roadside Units (RSUs). We evaluate the system in three diverse network scenarios: suburban, urban with 1 TAS, and urban with 2 TASs.
