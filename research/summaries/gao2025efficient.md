# Efficient Vehicle–Fog–Cloud Anonymous Authentication and Group Key Agreement Scheme Based on QRNG in VANETs
Jie Gao, Teng Cheng, Qin Shi, Ze Yang et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2025.3598304 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs), efficient anonymous authentication and group key update schemes have always been a focal point of research.
**Approach:** Therefore, this article proposes an efficient anonymous authentication and group key agreement scheme for vehicle–fog–cloud systems based on quantum random numbers. In this scheme: 1) the anonymous authentication of vehicles is generated by combining random numbers from the vehicle and the trusted authority (TA), thereby achieving privacy protection for vehicles during the registration and authentication process and 2) a method for generating and updating a group key is designed.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** Through security analysis and real-vehicle testing, the security and feasibility of the proposed scheme are demonstrated.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "However, there remains significant room for improvement in ensuring privacy protection as well as the security and efficiency of key distribution."
- "Furthermore, compared to other schemes, as the number of vehicles increases, the computational overhead at the vehicle and TA remain almost unchanged, while the signaling overhead is reduced by nearly half."
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — post-quantum/hybrid authentication scheme; the engine implements PQ and hybrid signatures, but the abstract states no SCMS-scale or traffic-coupled evaluation.
**Novelty implications:** We cannot claim PQ authentication for vehicles is new; the end-to-end safety cost of PQ in a full SCMS remains open as far as this abstract shows.
