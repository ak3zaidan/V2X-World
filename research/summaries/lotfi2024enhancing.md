# Enhancing Trust and Security in the Vehicular Metaverse: A Reputation-Based Mechanism for Participants with Moral Hazard
Ismail Lotfi, Marwa Qaraqe, Ali Ghrayeb, Niyato Dusit · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.19355 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Moral hazard of sensing devices supplying a vehicular metaverse.
**Approach:** Reputation mechanism using subjective logic with fading of old ratings; incentive design.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical/numerical simulation.
**Results:** Mechanism retains good devices despite false-positive ratings (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Find ideal weights for diminishing the influence of previous data.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — metaverse market, not V2X security.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
