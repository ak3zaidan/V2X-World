# A Vehicular DAA Scheme for Unlinkable ECDSA Pseudonyms in V2X
Christopher Hicks, Flavio D. Garcia · 2020 IEEE European Symposium on Security and Privacy (EuroS&amp;P) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/eurosp48549.2020.00036 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** V2X standards allow long-term pseudonym tracking by colluding CAs.
**Approach:** Vehicular DAA scheme for unlinkable ECDSA pseudonyms with centralised revocation and one pseudonym request per epoch to limit Sybil.
**Tools and scale:** New security model and reductions.
