# A Traffic Flow Theory Aided Physical Measurement-Based Sybil Nodes Detection Mechanism in Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks
Dongxu Jin, JooSeok Song · IEEE/ACIS International Conference on Computer and Information Science (ICIS) 2014 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icis.2014.6912147 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Sybil detection without keys or infrastructure.
**Approach:** PMSD: physical measurements of beacons plus traffic flow theory and safety guard distance.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** 97% detection with about 2% error rate; no extra overhead.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- More realistic situations to be studied in future.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
