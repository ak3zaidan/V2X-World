# Reliable Task Offloading Mechanism Based on Trusted Rsu Service for Internet of Vehicles
Ming Mao, Tao Hu, Wei Zhao · Social Science Research Network 2022 · https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.4142302 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The introduction of the internet of vehicles (IoV) paradigm has greatly improved the task transmission and processing capabilities of vehicular ad hoc networks (VANET).
**Approach:** To deal with the above problems, this paper proposes a task offloading mechanism for IoV based on a trusted RSU service. First, we construct a novel infrastructure trust management model and introduce social factors into it to strengthen RSU’s security management.
**Tools and scale:** The simulation results show that the proposed scheme can still maintain a low delay and a high task success rate under the attack state.
**Results:** The introduction of the internet of vehicles (IoV) paradigm has greatly improved the task transmission and processing capabilities of vehicular ad hoc networks (VANET). With the continuous increase of information processing quantity, due to the limited computation capacity of vehicles, mobile edge computing technology is required to offload vehicle task data to the network edge to improve task processing capabilities.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "In the malicious attack environment, facing the vehicle task processing requirements, how to securely, reliably, and quickly allocate vehicle tasks to roadside units (RSU) to assist in processing is another challenge."
