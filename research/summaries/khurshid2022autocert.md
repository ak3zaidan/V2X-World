# AutoCert: Automated TOCTOU-secure digital certification for IoT with combined authentication and assurance
Anum Khurshid, Shahid Raza · Computers & Security 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cose.2022.102952 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Internet of Things (IoT) network is comprised of heterogeneous devices which are part of critical infrastructures throughout the world.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose AutoCert, the first TOCTOU-secure mechanism to combine software-state integrity with PKI for IoT which resolves the TOCTOU problem in RA and Integrity Certificates.
