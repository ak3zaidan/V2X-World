# MAR_Sybil: Cooperative RSU Based Detection and Prevention of Sybil Attacks in Routing Process of VANET
Mahabaleshwar Kabbur, V. Arul Kumar · Journal of Physics: Conference Series 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/1427/1/012009 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Sybil attacks on emergency message routing.
**Approach:** RSU cooperative detection using triangulation and fake propagation; vehicles accept only RSU-signed, source-validated messages.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation, 100-500 vehicles.
**Results:** Detection accuracy 87-92% vs 65-82% for an RSSI-based scheme as vehicle count rises from 100 to 500.
