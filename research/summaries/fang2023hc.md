# HC-TUS: Human Cognition-Based Trust Update Scheme for AI-Enabled VANET
Weidong Fang, Chunsheng Zhu, Mohsen Guizani, J. Rodrigues et al. · IEEE Network 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mnet.2023.3320934 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Recently, Artificial Intelligence (AI) has received more attention for being used in many applications. It is expected to play a key role in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANET).
**Approach:** Although many trust schemes are proposed for addressing these issues, the reliance on only trust updates could increase the risk of long-term attacks before being detected. In this article, we design a human cognition-based trust update scheme (HC-TUS) for AI-VANET.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in abstract.
**Results:** The simulation results indicate that 1) HC-TUS could better meet the principle of ÒHard to get, easy to loseÓ for trust than BRSN and BTDS; 2) HC-TUS could detect and resist the collusion attack more quickly than BRSN and BTDS.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- The open issues in terms of trust for AI-VANET are also investigated and highlighted.

**Novelty implications:** No effect on our novelty claims; background only.
