# A Vehicle Trajectory Privacy Preservation Method Based on Caching and Dummy Locations in the Internet of Vehicles
Qianyong Huang, Xianyun Xu, Huifang Chen, Lei Xie · Sensors 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s22124423 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In the internet of vehicles (IoVs), vehicle users should provide location information continuously when they want to acquire continuous location-based services (LBS), which may disclose the vehicle trajectory privacy.
**Approach:** To solve the vehicle trajectory privacy leakage problem in the continuous LBS, we propose a vehicle trajectory privacy preservation method based on caching and dummy locations, abbreviated as TPPCD, in IoVs. In the proposed method, when a vehicle user wants to acquire a continuous LBS, the dummy locations-based location privacy preservation method under road constraint is used.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: location privacy).
**Results:** Moreover, the cache is deployed at the roadside unit (RSU) to reduce the information interaction between vehicle users covered by the RSU and the LBS server. Two cache update mechanisms, the active cache update mechanism based on data popularity and the passive cache update mechanism based on dummy locations, are designed to protect location privacy and improve the cache hit rate.
