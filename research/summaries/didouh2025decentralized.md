# Decentralized Sybil Attack Mitigation Using Bayesian-Stackelberg Games in V2X Networks
A. Didouh, Mohamed Amine Bouzaidi Tiali, Marwane Ayaida, Yassin El Hilali et al. · Global Communications Conference 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/globecom59602.2025.11432460 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil mitigation with scalability and privacy.
**Approach:** Blockchain with dynamic Proof of Location tuned by a Bayesian-Stackelberg game; RSUs apply spectral clustering on PoL graphs.
**Tools and scale:** NS-3; three urban scenarios.
**Results:** Resilience to Sybil infiltration; inter-vehicle distance and link quality dominate detectability.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Sybil detection in a network simulator.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
