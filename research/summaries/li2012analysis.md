# An analysis of anonymity for cooperative pseudonym change scheme in one-dimensional VANETs
Jianqing Li, Yuanyuan Pan · CSCWD 2012 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cscwd.2012.6221826 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Anonymity of cooperative pseudonym change.
**Approach:** Approximate analytic model in one-dimensional VANETs.
**Tools and scale:** Analysis verified by simulation.
**Results:** Gain over non-cooperative change first rises then falls with neighbour count.
