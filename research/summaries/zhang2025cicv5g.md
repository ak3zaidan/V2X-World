# CICV5G: A 5G Communication Delay Dataset for PnC in Cloud-based Intelligent Connected Vehicles
Xinrui Zhang, Peizhi Zhang, Junpeng Huang, Haojie Feng, Yining Ma, Feng Shen et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08255 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Lack of real 5G V2N2V delay data for cloud-based planning and control.
**Approach:** CICV5G dataset: over 300,000 records of delay with network indicators and position from a Tongji University testbed.
**Tools and scale:** Real 5G testbed vehicle tests.
**Results:** Delay effects of velocity, frequency, RSRP characterised; delay impacts PnC; fitted distributions (Normal, Gamma, Nakagami, Rayleigh).
