# A CARLA-Based Simulation Study of Quantum Key Distribution for Securing Vehicle-to-Infrastructure Communication
Shiksha Kumari, Mukta Mukta · International Journal of Drug Delivery Technology 2026 · https://doi.org/10.25258/ijddt.16.79s.107 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Securing V2I with QKD.
**Approach:** CARLA 0.10.0 simulation of the logical behaviour of a QKD-keyed zero-trust V2I link: two vehicles and a simulated RSU, AES-256-GCM, credential check rejecting a compromised device.
**Tools and scale:** CARLA; two vehicles.
**Results:** Demonstration only.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Very small logical simulation; QKD not physically modelled (implied by design).
**Overlap with our engine:** none — QKD demo.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
