# Analyzing Security Attacks Against Platoon of Intelligent and Connected Vehicles
Liqun Sun, Yulu Qi, Shuqiang Yang, Weihong Han et al. · 2023 8th International Conference on Data Science in Cyberspace (DSC) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/dsc59305.2023.00021 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Impact of cyber attacks on vehicle platoons.
**Approach:** Attack simulation and analysis framework with four attack models on a simulated platoon.
**Tools and scale:** Simulated platoon (simulator not named in abstract).
**Results:** Attacks disturb platoon randomly or regularly; platoon returns to normal afterwards (resilience).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — attack impact on vehicle dynamics in simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Platoon attack-impact studies exist; city-scale safety impact with PKI response remains open.
