# A Digital Twin for Enhanced Cybersecurity in Connected Vehicles
Chiara Grasselli, Andrea Melis, Roberto Girau, Franco Callegati · 2023 23rd International Conference on Transparent Optical Networks (ICTON) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icton59386.2023.10207369 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we propose an architecture and implementation methodology based on the NFV-MANO approach used in the telco industry to achieve automated deployment and configuration of digital twin instances for modern connected vehicles.
**Approach:** The digital twin instance serves as a cyber range to safely experiment with potential attacks and countermeasures tailored for in-vehicle scenarios within a controlled virtualized environment.
