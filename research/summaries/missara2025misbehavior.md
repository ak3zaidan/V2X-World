# Misbehavior Detection System in V2X 5G Edge Networks based on Transformer
Razine Missara, Hadi Yakan, Hamza Kchok, Ilhem Fajjari, Nadjib Aitsaadi · Global Information Infrastructure and Networking Symposium 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/giis64151.2025.10921812 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** LSTM MBD fails on attacks mimicking legitimate behaviour.
**Approach:** Transformer MBD in 5G edge with federated learning and personalisation.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi Extension.
**Results:** Accuracy 99.37%, recall 98.69%, F1 99.30%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Edge MBD in 5G.
**Novelty implications:** Dataset accuracy saturated; online consequence open.
