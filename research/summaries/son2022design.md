# Design of Blockchain-Based Lightweight V2I Handover Authentication Protocol for VANET
Seunghwan Son, Joonyoung Lee, Yohan Park et al. · IEEE Transactions on Network Science and Engineering 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnse.2022.3142287 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Connected vehicle means providing different services, such as advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) from vehicles connected to the network.
**Approach:** Using the strength of the blockchain technology, we design a blockchain-based handover authentication protocol for VANETs. In the proposed protocol, vehicles only perform lightweight computations in handover situations for efficiency of the network.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: NS-3, blockchain, AVISPA.
**Results:** Finally, we compare the computational cost and security features of the proposed protocol with existing protocols to show that the proposed protocol is more secure and efficient.
