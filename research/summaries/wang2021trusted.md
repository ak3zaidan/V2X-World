# Trusted and Collaborative Data Sharing with Quality Awareness in Autonomous Driving
Yuntao Wang, Zhou Su, Qichao Xu, Dongfeng Fang · ICC 2021 - IEEE International Conference on Communications 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc42927.2021.9500680 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Autonomous vehicles (AVs) are coming with great potentials to bring safer, greener, and more convenient transportation systems.
**Approach:** As AVs rely on radar, camera, and other advanced sensors to sense its surroundings, a salient challenge of AVs is the intrinsic limitations of onboard sensors (e.g., limited awareness range, blind spots, and failure in foggy days). To tackle this problem, we propose a collaborative data sharing scheme for AVs to make up for sensor deficiencies by promoting sensory information sharing in autonomous driving.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Lastly, extensive simulations validate that our approach can effectively improve sensing data quality and user utility, compared with conventional schemes.
