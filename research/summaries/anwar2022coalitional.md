# A coalitional security game against data integrity attacks in autonomous vehicle networks
Anika Anwar, Talal Halabi, Mohammad Zulkernine · Vehicular Communications 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vehcom.2022.100517 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Autonomous Vehicles (AV) possess a fully automated driving system that allows the vehicle to respond to external conditions and provide advanced and intelligent transportation services without human intervention. For AV, the legitimacy and reliability of sensed and transmitted data have direct influence on road network operations.
**Approach:** Moreover, the very large scale of future AV networks mandates an innovative design of intelligent communication among the vehicles to identify authentic traffic information broadcasted all across the network. In this paper, we design a vehicular collaboration framework that drives the interactions among autonomous vehicles based on trustworthy communications to prevent data integrity attacks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in abstract.
**Results:** The experimental results show that our collaboration framework is resilient to data corruption and fabrication attacks.
