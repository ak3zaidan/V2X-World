# Toward Blockchain-Based Spoofing Defense for Controlled Optimization of Phases in Traffic Signal System
Yingxiao XIANG, Chao LI, Tong CHEN, Yike LI et al. · IEICE Transactions on Information and Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1587/transinf.2021bcp0014 · relevance: medium · basis: full text

**Problem:** Last-vehicle data spoofing against COP in I-SIG causes congestion; coordinated attacks can cascade.
**Approach:** Blockchain of RSUs across intersections storing immutable trajectories; COP-oriented smart contract detects and filters attack vehicles periodically.
**Tools and scale:** VISSIM and Hyperledger Fabric.
**Results:** Experiments show superiority of the defence; continued congestion avoided.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None explicit in extracted sections.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — partial: spoofing detection and exclusion for signal control; exclusion is local, not SCMS revocation.
**Novelty implications:** Nothing of our candidate directions is ruled out.
