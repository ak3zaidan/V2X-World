# DISTINQT: A Distributed Privacy Aware Learning Framework for QoS Prediction for Future Mobile and Wireless Networks
Nikolaos Koursioumpas, Lina Magoula, Ioannis Stavrakakis, Nancy Alonistioti, M. A. Gutierrez-Estevez, Ramin Khalili · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2025.3542499 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Centralised QoS prediction raises privacy, compute and operational concerns.
**Approach:** DISTINQT: multi-headed, input-privacy-aware distributed learning across heterogeneous nodes.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3, 3GPP urban macro; tele-operated driving UL throughput, 20 s horizon.
**Results:** Statistically identical to centralised version; up to 65% lower prediction error on average vs six centralised baselines.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Communication resource limitations considered.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
