# CascadMLIDS: A Cascaded Machine Learning Framework for Intrusion Detection System in VANET
Argha Chandra Dhar, Arna Roy, M. A. H. Akhand, M. A. S. Kamal · Electronics 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics12183779 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) incorporating vehicles as an active and fast topology are gaining popularity as wireless communication means in intelligent transportation systems (ITSs).
**Approach:** The cybersecurity issue in VANETs has drawn attention due to the potential security threats these networks face. An effective cybersecurity measure is essential as security threats impact the overall system, from business disruptions to data corruption, theft, exposure, and unauthorized network access.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** ML-based IDS studies have focused on improving accuracy as a typical classification task without focusing on malicious data. In addition, the study also investigates the efficiency of typical classification tasks and compares them with results of the proposed framework.
