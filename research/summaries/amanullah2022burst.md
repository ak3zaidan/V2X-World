# BurST-ADMA: Towards an Australian Dataset for Misbehaviour Detection in the Internet of Vehicles
Mohamed Ahzam Amanullah, Mohan Baruwal Chhetri, Seng W. Loke, Robin Doss · IEEE International Conference on Pervasive Computing and Communications Workshops and other Affiliated Events (PerCom Workshops) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/percomworkshops53856.2022.9767505 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Existing MBD datasets (VeReMi, VeReMi Extension) are only for the Luxembourg LuST scenario.
**Approach:** BurST SUMO scenario for Burwood, Melbourne and the BurST-ADMA dataset with false position, speed and heading injections in BSMs; baseline ML evaluation.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO + VEINS/F2MD-type pipeline; one suburb.
**Results:** First public extensible Australian MBD dataset; ML baselines reported.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Preliminary dataset.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — misbehaviour dataset generation from simulation; we generate per-message ground truth across 50+ US maps.
**Novelty implications:** Non-European datasets exist; US multi-city, PKI-coupled ground truth still a contribution.
