# Simulation framework for the development of autonomous small scale vehicles
Marc Rene Zofka, Florian Kuhnt, Ralf Kohlhaas, J. Marius Zollner · 2016 IEEE International Conference on Simulation, Modeling, and Programming for Autonomous Robots (SIMPAR) 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1109/simpar.2016.7862413 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The development and validation of highly automated driving functions towards autonomous driving requires efficient frameworks to reduce the necessity of expensive, time-consuming and dangerous real test drives.
**Approach:** In the present work we introduce a simulation framework for the development and virtual validation of small scale vehicles to tackle this issue.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** At the concrete challenge of the student competition AUDI Autonomous Driving Cup, we demonstrate how a closed world can be transformed into appropriate simulation models in order to stimulate higher level automated driving functions. The framework is demonstrated at the example of highly automated driving functions on a small scale vehicle.
