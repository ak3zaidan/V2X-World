# Risk Assessment of Connected Heavy Commercial Road Vehicles under Cyber Attacks
P. Philip, K. B. Devika, Shankar C. Subramanian · International Conference on Communication Systems and Networks 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/comsnets67989.2026.11418301 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Real-time deployment of cooperative-intelligent transportation systems (C-ITS) necessitates the automotive industry to adopt structured cybersecurity frameworks.
**Approach:** Heavy commercial road vehicle (HCRV) platoons equipped with vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) communication are susceptible to data interferences and hence cyber attacks. The effects of cyber attacks are modelled as varied magnitudes of end-to-end message latency (E2E) and packet delivery ratio (PDR) of the V2V network.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: MATLAB
**Results:** For the considered class of cyber attacks, the sliding mode control (SMC) -based platoon controller results in low to moderate risk indices ranging from 0.04–0.12 on a 0–1 scale, where 1 indicates severe cyber attack impact.
