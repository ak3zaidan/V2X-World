# Efficient Certificateless Aggregate Authentication Scheme for Real-Time Secure Communication in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Dong-Wei Li, Mu-Hua Liu, Lin Wang, Ming-Chuan Zhang · 2025 3rd International Conference on Ubiquitous Communication (Ucom) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ucom67224.2025.11336849 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks (VANETs), as a core component of Intelligent Transportation Systems, provide realtime traffic information exchange through inter-vehicle communication technologies. However, its open communication environment causes serious privacy protection and security problems.
**Approach:** To address the above problems, this paper proposes an efficient certificateless aggregate authentication scheme for VANETs.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** The existing Certificateless Aggregate Signature (CLAS) schemes struggle to improve efficiency while ensuring security, resulting in low aggregate authentication efficiency. This approach improves communication efficiency.
