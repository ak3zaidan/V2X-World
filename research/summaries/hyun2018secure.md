# Secure and DoS-Resilient Fragment Authentication in CCN-Based Vehicular Networks
Sangwon Hyun, Hyoungshick Kim · Wireless Communications and Mobile Computing 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2018/8071267 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** It is thus challenging to securely protect fragmented packets against attackers who intentionally inject malicious fragments to disrupt normal services on CCN-based vehicular networks.
**Approach:** This paper presents a new secure content fragmentation method that is resistant to Denial-of-Service (DoS) attacks in CCN-based vehicular networks. Our experiment results demonstrate that the proposed approach provides much stronger security than the existing approach named FIGOA, without imposing a significant overhead in the process.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** The proposed method achieves a high immediate verification probability of 98.2% on average, which is 52% higher than that of FIGOA, while requiring only 14% more fragments than FIGOA.
