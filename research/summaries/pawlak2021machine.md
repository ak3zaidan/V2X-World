# A Machine Learning Approach for Detecting and Classifying Jamming Attacks Against OFDM-based UAVs
Jered Pawlak, Yuchen Li, Joshua Price, Matthew Wright, Khair A. Al Shamaileh, Quamar Niyaz, Vijay Devabhaktuni · WiseML@WiSec 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3468218.3469049 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, a machine learning (ML) approach is proposed to detect and classify jamming attacks on unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs). Four attack types are implemented using software-defined radio (SDR); namely, barrage, single-tone, successive-pulse, and protocol-aware jamming.
**Approach:** In this paper, a machine learning (ML) approach is proposed to detect and classify jamming attacks on unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs).
**Tools and scale:** Four attack types are implemented using software-defined radio (SDR); namely, barrage, single-tone, successive-pulse, and protocol-aware jamming. Then, an SDR is utilized in proximity to the drone and in systematic testing scenarios to record the radiometric parameters before and after each attack is launched.
**Results:** The resulting ML approach detects and classifies jamming with an accuracy of 92.2% and a false-alarm rate of 1.35%.
