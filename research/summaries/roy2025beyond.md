# Beyond V2X: A Review of Large Language Models in In-Vehicle Autonomous Driving Systems
Ayan Roy · Unknown venue 2025 · https://doi.org/10.36227/techrxiv.175571972.28517288/v1 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Large Language Models (LLMs) are emerging as key enablers of intelligent in-vehicle systems, supporting conversational interfaces, diagnostics, and decision support in autonomous driving.
**Approach:** While prior surveys have focused on LLMs in vehicular infrastructure, little attention has been paid to their integration within the in-vehicle environment-where safetycritical decisions occur. This paper offers a comprehensive review of LLM applications inside vehicles, presenting a structured taxonomy and analyzing recent developments across academia and industry.
