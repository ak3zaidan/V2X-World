# Detection of Sybil &amp; DDoS attacks in VANET using intelligent technique
Urmila Bhanja, Ankit Majhi, Smrutirekha Sahu, Debapriya Parida · International Journal of Computers and Applications 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1080/1206212x.2024.2389476 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Detect Sybil and DDoS.
**Approach:** Fuzzy logic controller model (FBM).
**Tools and scale:** Veins (OMNeT++/SUMO).
**Results:** Small gains over RF (0.10% accuracy DDoS) and DT (3.9% Sybil).

- None stated.
