# Multimodal-Wireless: A Large-Scale Dataset for Sensing and Communication
Tianhao Mao, Le Liang, Jie Yang et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.03220 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper presents Multimodal-Wireless, a large-scale open-source dataset for multimodal sensing and communication research.
**Approach:** The dataset is generated through an integrated and customizable data pipeline built upon the CARLA simulator and Sionna framework, and features high-resolution communication channel state information (CSI) fully synchronized with five other sensor modalities, namely LiDAR, RGB and depth camera, inertial measurement unit (IMU) and radar, all sampled at 100 Hz. This paper provides a comprehensive overview of the dataset, outlining its key features, overall framework, and technical implementation details.
