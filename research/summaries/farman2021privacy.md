# A Privacy Preserved, Trust Relationship (PTR) Model for Internet of Vehicles
Haleem Farman, Abizar Khalil, Naveed Ahmad, Waleed Albattah, Muazzam A. Khan, Muhammad Islam · Electronics 2021 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics10243105 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Internet of vehicles (IoV) depicts a reality where ordinary things are connected to vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs), allowing them to transmit and collaborate.
**Approach:** By placing these regular objects in VANETs and making them available at any time, this network and data sharing may raise real privacy and security issues. Thus, group-based communication is mostly preferred in the literature.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The multi-phantom node scheme is proposed to reduce the phantom node load, where the multi-lateral nodes in a cluster act as a phantom node to share the load. The results show checking the stability of parameters and using sensitivity analysis by considering different scenarios for the most optimal phantom node to preserve vehicle location privacy.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, in heavy network scenarios, cluster-based communication mostly leads to additional overload in the form of the group leader that causes delay and disrupts the performance of a network.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
