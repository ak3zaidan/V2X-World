# Hyperparameter Optimization-Based Trust Quantification for Misbehavior Detection Systems
Artur Hermann, Nataša Trkulja, Dennis Eisermann, Benjamin Erb, Frank Kargl · 2025 IEEE 28th International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/itsc60802.2025.11423703 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Quantifying MBD outputs into trust opinions.
**Approach:** Subjective-logic trust quantification and hyperparameter optimisation of MBDs.
**Tools and scale:** Three MBD variants.
**Results:** F1 increased over 13% vs other variants.

- Not stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — MA/detector decision tuning, related to MA decision design.
**Novelty implications:** Trust quantification exists; revocation threshold tuning by safety outcome open.
