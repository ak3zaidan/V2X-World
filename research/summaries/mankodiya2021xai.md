# XAI-AV: Explainable Artificial Intelligence for Trust Management in Autonomous Vehicles
Harsh Mankodiya, M. Obaidat, Rajesh Gupta, Sudeep Tanwar · International Conference on Communications, Computing, Cybersecurity, and Informatics 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ccci52664.2021.9583190 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Artificial intelligence (AI) is the most looked up technology with a diverse range of applications across all the fields, whether it is intelligent transportation systems (ITS), medicine, healthcare, military operations, or others. One such application is autonomous vehicles (AVs), which comes under the category of AI in ITS.
**Approach:** Vehicular Adhoc Networks (VANET) makes communication possible between AVs in the system. The performance of each vehicle depends upon the information exchanged between AVs.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Recent papers that used the stacking ML approach gave an accuracy of 98.44%. We achieved accuracy and F1 score of 98.43% and 98.5% respectively on the VeRiMi dataset in this paper.
