# GenVRAM: Dataset Generator for Vehicle to Roadside Attacks and Misbehavior
Devaj Ramsamooj, Prinkle Sharma, Hong Liu · IEEE Access 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3416840 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** No datasets for V2I security, especially misbehaving RSUs.
**Approach:** GenVRAM simulated CAV dataset generator for normal and attack data focused on misbehaving RSUs; ML and DL evaluation.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation-based generator.
**Results:** Traditional ML inadequate; DL promising but needs refinement.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Deep learning efficacy needs further investigation.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — attack dataset generator including RSU misbehaviour; our engine generates data with PKI ground truth.
**Novelty implications:** V2I/RSU-misbehaviour dataset generator exists; PKI-coupled ground truth still distinctive.
