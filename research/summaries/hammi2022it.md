# Is it Really Easy to Detect Sybil Attacks in C-ITS Environments: A Position Paper
Badis Hammi, Y. Idir, S. Zeadally, Rida Khatoun, J. Nebhen · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2022.3165513 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Are published Sybil detection approaches compliant with C-ITS standards and properly evaluated?
**Approach:** Position paper analysing recent Sybil detection proposals for standards compliance and evaluation methodology; recommends network and attack models and urban/highway datasets.
**Tools and scale:** Analysis of literature; dataset recommendations.
**Results:** Finds compliance and evaluation shortcomings (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Calls for standard-compliant network and attack models and common datasets for future Sybil research.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — critique that detection work ignores standard PKI/pseudonym constraints, which our engine models.
**Novelty implications:** Supports the claim that standards-faithful evaluation is lacking; we cannot claim to be first to point it out.
