# Machine Learning Aided NR-V2X Quality of Service Predictions
Aslihan Reyhanoglu, Emrah Kar, Feyzi Ege Kumec, Yahya Sukur Can Kara, Sercan Karaagac, Bugra Turan et al. · 2023 IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference (VNC) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc57357.2023.10136346 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Predicting NR-V2X QoS for safety applications.
**Approach:** Seven ML regressors predict PDR and throughput from MCS, distance, SINR and packet size.
**Tools and scale:** NR-V2X simulation data.
**Results:** CatBoost and RF perform best for throughput and PDR.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - learned NR-V2X performance surrogate from simulation data.
**Novelty implications:** Minor: per-link QoS surrogates for NR-V2X exist.
