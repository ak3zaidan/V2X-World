# Exponential Leaf in Wind Optimization based Siamese Dense Block Capsule Network for detection of anomaly in Fog-Assisted IoVs Network
L. B, V. G · 2025 Third International Conference on Industry 4.0 Technology (I4Tech) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/i4tech64670.2025.11277917 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Anomaly detection in Fog-Assisted Internet of Vehicles (IoVs) networks is essential for ensuring real-time security, reliability and efficient data handling in intelligent transportation systems.
**Approach:** In this work, a novel model named Exponential Leaf in Wind Optimization-Enabled Siamese Dense Block Capsule Network (ELiWO_SDBCN) is devised for detecting anomalies in Fog-Assisted IoVs. Finally, the anomaly detection is performed by SDBCN, where the proposed ELiWO will train the hyperparameters of SDBCN.
**Tools and scale:** Initially, the Fog-Assisted IoVs Network is simulated and then the recorded log file from the ISCX NSL-KDD dataset is sent for transforming the data, which is performed by Yeo-Johnson transformation to transform the data.
**Results:** The ELiWO_SDBCN is analyzed by assuming the metrics, such as sensitivity, specificity and F1-Score, that obtained superior values of 92.66%, 92.93% and 92.02%.
