# Zero-Rendered Lightweight Digital Twin Campus Network Operation and Maintenance System Based on AI Foundation
Ming-Xiu Tong, R. Lu · 2025 2nd Asia Pacific Conference on Computing Technologies, Communications and Networking (CTCNet) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ctcnet68009.2025.11405805 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As campus networks expand, device numbers surge and scenarios diversify, traditional O&M struggles with real-time accuracy and cost control.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a lightweight digital-twin framework for campus network O&M. It deploys a locally-hosted high-performance AI large model (e.g., DeepSeek) as the foundation, fuses a graph database with a zero-rendered twin engine, uses an “entity-attribute-event” logical data model (e.g., “AP-signal strength-alarm”) instead of 3D meshes to achieve precise, real-time mapping and millisecond-level synchronization of the physical campus network.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Experiments show that the solution outperforms traditional GPU-rendering-heavy digital twins in resource consumption, response speed and automation, offering a quickly replicable and efficient path for campus network O&M.
