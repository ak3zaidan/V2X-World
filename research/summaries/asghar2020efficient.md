# An efficient voting based decentralized revocation protocol for vehicular ad hoc networks
Miraj Asghar, Lei Pan, Robin Doss · Digital Communications and Networks 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcan.2020.03.001 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Misbehaving vehicles must be revoked immediately; CRLs are large and slow.
**Approach:** Decentralised revocation via Shamir secret sharing voting; revocation list limited to revoked vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Experimental evaluation.
**Results:** Very low revocation delay (no figure in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Fast revocation of misbehaving vehicles; non-SCMS decentralised.
**Novelty implications:** Fast-revocation designs exist; SCMS-conformant closed-loop time-to-neutralise and safety impact remain open.
