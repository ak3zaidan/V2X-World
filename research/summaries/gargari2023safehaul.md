# Safehaul: Risk-Averse Learning for Reliable mmWave Self-Backhauling in 6G Networks
Amir Ashtari Gargari, Andrea Ortiz, Matteo Pagin, Anja Klein, Matthias Hollick, Michele Zorzi, Arash Asadi · IEEE INFOCOM 2023 - IEEE Conference on Computer Communications 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/infocom53939.2023.10228969 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Wireless backhauling at millimeter-wave frequencies (mmWave) in static scenarios is a well-established practice in cellular networks.
**Approach:** However, highly directional and adaptive beamforming in today’s mmWave systems have opened new possibilities for self-backhauling. Tapping into this potential, 3GPP has standardized Integrated Access and Backhaul (IAB) allowing the same base station to serve both access and backhaul traffic.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: simulations, simulator.
**Results:** We develop a novel simulator and show via extensive simulations that Safehaul not only reduces the latency by up to 43.2% compared to the benchmarks, but also exhibits significantly more reliable performance, e.g., 71.4% less variance in achieved latency.
