# FedsLLM: Federated Split Learning for Large Language Models over Communication Networks
Kai Zhao, Zhaohui Yang, Chongwen Huang et al. · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09250 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Addressing the challenges of deploying large language models in wireless communication networks, this paper combines low-rank adaptation technology (LoRA) with the splitfed learning framework to propose the federated split learning for large language models (FedsLLM) framework.
**Approach:** The method introduced in this paper utilizes LoRA technology to reduce processing loads by dividing the network into client subnetworks and server subnetworks. Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed optimization algorithm reduces delays by an average of 47.63% compared to unoptimized scenarios.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** As the training data are transmitted through a wireless network between clients and both main and federated servers, the training delay is determined by the learning accuracy and the allocation of communication bandwidth.
