Hanmei Li, YingXu Lai, Ye Chen · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2023.3279121 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Approach:** Broadcast and silence period strategy at frequent stop locations with RSU-assigned pseudonyms.

**Results:** Prevents syntactic and semantic linking while limiting radio-silence safety impact.
