# Federated Intrusion Detection for VANETs under Byzantine Model Poisoning Attacks
Shivanshi Mishra, Shruti Singh, Kanchan Sharma et al. · 2026 2nd International Conference on Cognitive Computing in Engineering, Communications, Sciences and Biomedical Health Informatics (IC3ECSBHI) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ic3ecsbhi67834.2026.11468974 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Federated Learning (FL) has drawn interest because of its decentralised and privacy-preserving features for intrusion detection in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs).
**Approach:** The impact of gradient-scaling model poisoning on federated VANET intrusion detection under realistic non-IID data distribution and partial client participation is examined in this work.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The findings show that robust aggregation, especially Krum, significantly improves network stability and improves malicious detection.
