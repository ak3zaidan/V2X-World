# Generative AI Empowered Network Digital Twins: Architecture, Technologies, and Applications
Tong Li, Qing-Yue Long, Haoye Chai, Shi-Yuan Zhang et al. · ACM Computing Surveys 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3711682 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The rapid advancement of mobile networks highlights the limitations of traditional network planning and optimization methods, particularly in modeling, evaluation, and application. Network Digital Twins, which simulate networks in the digital domain for evaluation, offer a solution to these challenges.
**Approach:** This survey provides insights into generative AI-empowered network digital twins. We begin by outlining the architecture of a network digital twin, which encompasses both digital and physical domains.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** This architecture involves four key steps: data processing and network monitoring, digital replication and network simulation, designing and training network optimizers, Sim2Real, and network control.
