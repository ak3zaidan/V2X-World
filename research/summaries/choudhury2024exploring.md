# Exploring Attack Resilience in Distributed Platoon Controllers with Model Predictive Control
Tashfique Hasnine Choudhury · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04736 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The extensive use of distributed vehicle platoon controllers has resulted in several benefits for transportation systems, such as increased traffic flow, fuel efficiency, and decreased pollution.
**Approach:** The findings emphasize the significance of integrating security issues into their design and implementation, which helps to construct safe and resilient distributed platoon controllers.
**Tools and scale:** Various attack techniques, including man-in-the-middle (MITM) and false data injection (FDI), are simulated using Model Predictive Control (MPC) controller to identify vulnerabilities and weaknesses of the platoon controller.
**Results:** This thesis aims to improve the security of distributed vehicle platoon controllers by investigating attack scenarios and assessing their influence on system performance.
