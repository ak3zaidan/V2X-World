# Cyber Attacks via Consumer Electronics: Studying the Threat of Covert Malware in Smart and Autonomous Vehicles
M. S. Haghighi, Faezeh Farivar, A. Jolfaei, Azin Bayrami Asl, Wei Zhou · IEEE transactions on consumer electronics 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tce.2023.3297965 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In Industry 5.0, man and machine work alongside each other in production, but smart and autonomous vehicles are examples that show this notion is now being extended to the end consumers. In 2015, a Jeep was remotely hacked through its head unit.
**Approach:** In this paper, we study a novel covert attack that can be launched by malwares spreading through Intelligent Transportation Systems, e.g., via consumer electronics. We show how this is done and analyze the effect mathematically. We also propose a new Intrusion Detection System (IDS) whose architecture is non-disruptive and can be readily adopted by car manufacturers.
**Tools and scale:** We evaluate our proposal with real-world datasets.
