# GA‐LMI‐Assisted Event‐Triggered H∞ PID Control for Networked Systems Under Hybrid Cyber Attacks
Tao Yu, Zhanpeng Wang, Chengcheng Ren, Shuping He · International Journal of Robust and Nonlinear Control 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1002/rnc.70338 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ABSTRACT This paper addresses the challenge of event‐triggered proportional‐integral‐derivative (PID) control for networked systems under hybrid cyber attacks.
**Approach:** The simulation of hybrid attacks involves the use of two groups of Bernoulli variables. Due to the constrained network bandwidth between the controller and actuator, an event‐triggered PID control mechanism is designed to alleviate the network load.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Finally, a numerical example illustrates the reduced conservativeness of the proposed approach, achieving improved performance under varying system parameters.
