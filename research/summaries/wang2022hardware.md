# A Hardware-in-the-Loop V2X Simulation Framework: CarTest
Jian Wang, Yu Zhu · Sensors 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s22135019 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** V2X field testing has low efficiency, coverage and repeatability and cannot recreate hazards.
**Approach:** CarTest HIL laboratory framework with test cases and evaluation system for FCW, ICW, CACC and GNSS injection.
**Tools and scale:** HIL with real OBUs; lab simulation.
**Results:** Reliable output, strong repeatability, ability to simulate severe danger scenarios, scalable (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Discusses HIL challenges and solutions.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — V2X application testing with GNSS injection (attack-like), but HIL and no PKI.
**Novelty implications:** HIL V2X application testing exists; our software-only closed-loop security is different.
