# A Multi-Modal Simulation Framework to Enable Digital Twin-based V2X Communications in Dynamic Environments
Lorenzo Cazzella, Francesco Linsalata, Maurizio Magarini, Matteo Matteucci, Umberto Spagnolini · 2024 IEEE 100th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2024-Fall) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2024-fall63153.2024.10757947 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Creating DTs of V2X environments for mmWave/sub-THz.
**Approach:** Data-driven DT workflow and multi-modal simulation combining an automotive simulator with ray-tracing channels; blockage-handover use case.
**Tools and scale:** Automotive simulator plus ray tracer; urban scenario.
**Results:** Realistic sensor and channel generation shown (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - multi-modal V2X co-simulation, PHY focus.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on security directions.
