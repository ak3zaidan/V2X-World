Bhuvaneshwari A. J., K. P., K. R., M. R · Scientific Reports 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-10503-2 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Vehicular IoT data integrity against quantum.
**Approach:** Ascon with quantum key exchange; Qiskit simulations.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
