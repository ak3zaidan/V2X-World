# Transitioning to Quantum-Resistant Infrastructure: A Comprehensive Evaluation of Lattice-Based Anonymous Authentication in Vehicle-to-Grid Networks
Neha Sharma · Journal of Intelligent Decision Making and Information Science 2026 · https://doi.org/10.17762/jidmis.v3.4662 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** V2G anonymous authentication is quantum-vulnerable.
**Approach:** Lattice-based certificateless aggregate signcryption with accumulator-based revocation.
**Tools and scale:** ROM proofs; comparison with ECC and PQ V2G schemes.
**Results:** About 1.8x ECC compute; 22% lower communication than other PQ V2G systems.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Conservative parameters (n=1024, q about 2^32) may need change; future: adaptive parameter selection.
- Accumulator revocation needs periodic witness updates; scaling to millions of EVs is a challenge.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — V2G.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
