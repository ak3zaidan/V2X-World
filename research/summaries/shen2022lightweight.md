# A Lightweight Authentication with Dynamic Batch-Based Group Key Management Using LSTM in VANET
Xie-Yang Shen, Chuanhe Huang, W. Pu, Dan-Xin Wang · Security and Communication Networks 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2022/9779670 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** High CRL-checking and verification cost of group signatures in VANET.
**Approach:** Hash- and group-key-based lightweight authentication; LSTM traffic-flow prediction decides when to update group keys in batches.
**Tools and scale:** Security and performance analysis; LSTM on traffic flow.
**Results:** Claims faster authentication and lower delay/overhead; no figures in abstract.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none: group-key scheme, not SCMS.
**Novelty implications:** Traffic-predicted key update timing exists for group keys; not for SCMS pseudonym top-ups.
