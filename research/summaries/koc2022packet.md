# Packet Loss Rate Prediction for Vehicular Networks with Regression Methods
Osman Nuri Koc, Engin Masazade · 2022 30th Signal Processing and Communications Applications Conference (SIU) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/siu55565.2022.9864805 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Predicting PLR from density, rate and power.
**Approach:** Regression models on simulated PLR data.
**Tools and scale:** OMNeT++, SUMO, Veins; urban and highway.
**Results:** CatBoost lowest error.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - learned surrogate of Veins PLR.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
