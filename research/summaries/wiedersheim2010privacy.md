# Privacy in inter-vehicular networks: Why simple pseudonym change is not enough
Bjorn Wiedersheim, Zhendong Ma, Frank Kargl, Panos Papadimitratos · 2010 Seventh International Conference on Wireless On-demand Network Systems and Services (WONS) 2010 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wons.2010.5437115 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Whether simple pseudonym change protects against area-wide eavesdroppers.
**Approach:** Tracking analysis reconstructing vehicle traces from IVC beacons.
**Tools and scale:** WONS 2010; simulated traces.
**Results:** An eavesdropper can reconstruct long traces of most vehicles; strong location privacy is questionable.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — linkability despite pseudonyms.
**Novelty implications:** We cannot claim novelty for showing pseudonym-change trackability at the application layer.
