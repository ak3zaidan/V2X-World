# AdaptIDS: Adaptive Intrusion Detection for Mission-Critical Aerospace Vehicles
Marwa A. Elsayed, Michael Wrana, Ziad Mansour, K. Lounis, Steven H. H. Ding, M. Zulkernine · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2022.3214095 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Aerospace and defense industries are particularly vulnerable to cyber threats given their sensitive nature, significantly extending the consequences of security breaches to the national level. Aerospace vehicles are augmented by cooperative control, intelligent, connected, and autonomous systems.
**Approach:** This paper proposes, AdaptIDS, a novel adaptive intrusion detection system as a security analytics framework for the MIL-STD-1553 communication bus.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Extensive experiments are conducted using two datasets generated from an open-source simulation system, reflecting dynamic real-life scenarios. The evaluation results demonstrate that our solution outperforms existing solutions with high detection effectiveness of 0.99 F1-measure and computational time efficiency.
