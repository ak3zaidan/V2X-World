# Simulation of V2X Applications with the iTETRIS System
Daniel Krajzewicz, Laura Bieker, Jérôme Härri, Robbin Blokpoel · Procedia - Social and Behavioral Sciences 2012 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sbspro.2012.06.1124 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Large-scale evaluation of V2X-based traffic management needs an integrated traffic and network simulation system.
**Approach:** Reports the results of the EU iTETRIS project: city traffic problem analysis, metrics, V2X traffic-management applications and extensions of the coupled simulators (SUMO with ns-3 via a central control system).
**Tools and scale:** SUMO + ns-3 coupled via iTETRIS control system; city scale (Bologna in the project); details not in abstract.
**Results:** Project results reported qualitatively; most results released as open source.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — coupled city-scale traffic + V2X simulation; no PKI/SCMS, multi-process coupling rather than single-process deterministic engine.
**Novelty implications:** Coupled traffic/V2X city-scale simulation is long established; integrated security/PKI ground truth remains open.
