# Suitability of the wireless testbed w-iLab.t for VANET research
Wim Vandenberghe, Ingrid Moerman, Piet Demeester, Hans Cappelle · 2011 18th IEEE Symposium on Communications and Vehicular Technology in the Benelux (SCVT) 2011 · https://doi.org/10.1109/scvt.2011.6101301 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Whether a generic indoor wireless testbed can serve VANET research between simulation and field trials.
**Approach:** Adapts the w-iLab.t testbed: 802.11a hardware emulating 802.11p, mobility emulated by link impairment (PER tables from SNR), low transmit power and manual topology.
**Tools and scale:** IBBT w-iLab.t indoor testbed; highway and urban settings.
**Results:** Highway densities up to 25% of maximum VANET density in intense flowing traffic.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Urban experiments are labour-intensive and limited in topology size
- highway density capped at 25% of real maximum.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — testbed methodology.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
