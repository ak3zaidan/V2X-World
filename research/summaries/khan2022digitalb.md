# Digital Twin of Wireless Systems: Overview, Taxonomy, Challenges, and Opportunities
Latif U. Khan, Zhu Han, Walid Saad, Ekram Hossain et al. · IEEE Communications Surveys &amp; Tutorials 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/comst.2022.3198273 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Future wireless services will focus on improving the quality of life by enabling various applications, such as extended reality, brain-computer interaction, and healthcare. These applications will have diverse performance requirements (e.g., user-defined quality of experience metrics, latency, and reliability) which will be challenging to be fulfilled by existing wireless systems.
**Approach:** In this tutorial, we present a comprehensive overview on digital twins for wireless systems. First, we present the fundamental concepts (i.e., design aspects, high-level architecture, and frameworks) of digital twins for wireless systems.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 6G, digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** A digital twin uses a virtual representation along with security-related technologies (e.g., blockchain), communication technologies (e.g., 6G), computing technologies (e.g., edge computing), and machine learning, so as to enable the smart applications.
