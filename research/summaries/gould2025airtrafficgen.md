# AirTrafficGen: Configurable Air Traffic Scenario Generation with Large Language Models
Dewi Sid William Gould, George De Ath, Ben Carvell, Nick Pepper · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02269 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Manual ATC training scenario design.
**Approach:** LLMs generate scenarios from graph-encoded sectors.
**Tools and scale:** Gemini 2.5 Pro, o3, GPT-oss-120b, GPT-5.
**Results:** High-traffic realistic scenarios with fine control; iterative refinement.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Initial findings only.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — LLM configures simulation scenarios.
**Novelty implications:** No V2X claim affected.
