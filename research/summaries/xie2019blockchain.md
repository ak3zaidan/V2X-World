# Blockchain-Based Secure and Trustworthy Internet of Things in SDN-Enabled 5G-VANETs
Lixia Xie, Ying Ding, Hongyu Yang, Xinmu Wang · IEEE Access 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2019.2913682 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The modern intelligent transportation system brings not only new opportunities for vehicular Internet of Things (IoT) services but also new challenges for vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs). Apart from enhanced network performance, a practical and reliable security scheme is needed to handle the trust management while preserving user privacy at the same time.
**Approach:** This paper investigates the security and privacy issue in the transportation system and the vehicular IoT environment in SDN-enabled 5G-VANET. Through case studies and theoretical analysis, we demonstrate our design substantially guarantees a secure and trustworthy vehicular IoT environment with user privacy preserved.
**Tools and scale:** The numerical simulation results also show that malicious vehicular nodes or messages can be well detected while the overhead and impact on the network performance are acceptable for large-scale scenarios.
