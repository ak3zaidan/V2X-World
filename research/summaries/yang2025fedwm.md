# FedWM-IoV: A Watermarking-Based Defense Method Against Federated Learning Model Poisoning Attacks in IoV
Ying Yang, Meiiao Li, Tingrui Zhang · 2025 IEEE 102nd Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2025-Fall) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2025-fall65116.2025.11310264 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Federated learning (FL) is widely used in the Internet of Vehicles.
**Approach:** Nevertheless, the distributed collaboration and trustless relationships of federated learning make the system suffer from numerous security issues. Meanwhile, the strong mobility, data heterogeneity, and low latency requirements of vehicles as federated learning participants exacerbate the security risks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The objective is to detect malicious local models through watermark embedding and watermark integrity detection and select benign local models for aggregation to improve the robustness of the system. The experimental results show that our proposed scheme achieves a global accuracy of more than 85% and up to 97% within 10 rounds of communication, which has a better defensive effect than previous works.
