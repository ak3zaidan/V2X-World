# TBNDG: Trust-Based Malicious Node Detection for Grey-Hole Attacks in VANET’s
Soumya Ranjan Mohapata, Rekha Jain, S. K, Sandeep Varma Kalidindi, Umashankar Ghugar · 2024 Eighth International Conference on Parallel, Distributed and Grid Computing (PDGC) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/pdgc64653.2024.10984014 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular advertising networks (VANETs) are vulnerable to various security threats due to their dynamic and decentralized nature.
**Approach:** One of the most critical threats is the grey hole attack, where malicious nodes selectively drop packets, which poses a significant threat to communication reliability and security in VANETs. This paper proposes a robust approach to detect malicious nodes involved in grey-hole attacks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** In addition, we introduced updates to the trust and consensus mechanisms between neighbouring nodes to improve the strength of our approach against reconciliation and false accusations. Nodes periodically exchange trust information and check the consistency of their trust estimates, thus reducing the impact of false positives and negatives.
