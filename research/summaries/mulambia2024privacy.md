# Privacy-preserving authentication approach for vehicular networks
Chindika Mulambia, Sudeep Varshney, Amrit Suman · Indonesian Journal of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science 2024 · https://doi.org/10.11591/ijeecs.v34.i3.pp1674-1681 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle AdHoc networks have an important role in intelligent transport systems that enhance safety in road usage by transmitting real traffic updates in terms of congestion and road accidents.
**Approach:** The proposed approach is a privacy preserving authentication approach that authenticates vehicles before they have access to the vehicular network thereby identifying malicious vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** The model was executed in docker container that simulates the network in a Linux environment running Ubuntu 20.04. The model enhances privacy by assigning Pseudo IDs to authenticated vehicles and the results demonstrate effectiveness of the solution in that unlike other models it boasts faster authentication and lower computational overhead which is necessary in a vehicular network scenario.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
