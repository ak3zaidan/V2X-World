# A Trust‐Based Random Forest Approach for Detecting Blackhole Attacks in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
M. Al-Shabi, A. Abuhamdah, Fawaz Alanazi, Asma A. Alahashmi et al. · Engineering Reports 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1002/eng2.70888 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Blackhole attacks in VANETs (also smart mining).
**Approach:** Multi-metric trust with a Random Forest classifier on KPIs (PDR, delay, jitter, collisions, route stability) and quarantine. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** NS-3 generated dataset.
**Results:** PDR above 92%, average delay below 42 ms, detection accuracy above 96.3%.
