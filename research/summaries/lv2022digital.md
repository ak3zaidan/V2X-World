# Digital Twins Based VR Simulation for Accident Prevention of Intelligent Vehicle
Zhihan Lv, Jinkang Guo, Amit Kumar Singh, Haibin Lv · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2022.3152597 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Traffic accident prediction and prevention.
**Approach:** Double-scale decomposition with LSTM prediction of accident time series, particle-filter tracking, and a DT-based video analysis system.
**Tools and scale:** Video analysis system; datasets not specified.
**Results:** Prediction accuracy improved by 6% vs LSTM and 8% vs SAE.
