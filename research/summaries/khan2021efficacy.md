# Efficacy of Statistical and Artificial Intelligence-based False Information Cyberattack Detection Models for Connected Vehicles
Sakib Mahmud Khan, Gurcan Comert, Mashrur Chowdhury · arXiv.org 2021 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.01124 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Connected vehicles (CVs), because of the external connectivity with other CVs and connected infrastructure, are vulnerable to cyberattacks that can instantly compromise the safety of the vehicle itself and other connected vehicles and roadway infrastructure.
**Approach:** In this paper, we have evaluated three change point-based statistical models; Expectation Maximization, Cumulative Summation, and Bayesian Online Change Point Algorithms for cyberattack detection in the CV data. We have used six AI models to detect false information attacks and compared the performance for detecting the attacks with our developed change point models.
**Tools and scale:** Also, data-driven artificial intelligence (AI) models, which can be used to detect known and unknown underlying patterns in the dataset, have the potential of detecting a real-time anomaly in the CV data.
**Results:** No quantitative results stated in the abstract.
