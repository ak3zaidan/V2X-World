# Efficient and Secure Certificateless Aggregate Signature-Based Authentication Scheme for Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Gowri Thumbur, G. Rao, P. V. Reddy, N. Gayathri et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2020.3019304 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In recent years, IoT has opened new opportunities for the development of various industries to improve people’s lives. Vehicular ad hoc network (VANET) uses IoT applications for secure communication among the vehicles and to improve road safety and traffic management.
**Approach:** In this article, we proposed a new certificateless aggregate signature-based authentication scheme for VANETS.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Also, aggregate signature aggregates various individual signatures on different messages from different vehicles into a single signature, which in turn results in the reduction of verification time and storage space at the roadside unit. Moreover, our scheme does not use the pairing operation, which is the most expensive operation than others in modern cryptography, thus significantly reduces the computation overhead.
