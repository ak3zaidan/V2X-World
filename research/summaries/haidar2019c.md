# C-ITS PKI protocol: Performance Evaluation in a Real Environment
Farah Haidar, Arnaud Kaiser, B. Lonc, P. Urien · Wireless on Demand Network Systems and Service 2019 · https://doi.org/10.23919/wons.2019.8795479 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** How well does C-ITS PKI certificate reloading work while driving?
**Approach:** Field tests of pseudonym reload over the PKI protocol at different speeds.
**Tools and scale:** Real environment, 802.11p, European C-ITS PKI.
**Results:** End-to-end latency to the PKI is non-negligible; successfully reloaded certificates decrease as speed increases.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — Pseudonym top-up over the network while driving, which our engine simulates (cellular TCP/TLS and RSU relay).
**Novelty implications:** Field data for reload rates exists; can be used to validate our provisioning model.
