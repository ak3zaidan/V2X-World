# The scrambler attack: A robust physical layer attack on location privacy in vehicular networks
Bastian Bloessl, Christoph Sommer, Falko Dressler, David Eckhoff · 2015 International Conference on Computing, Networking and Communications (ICNC) 2015 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccnc.2015.7069376 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular networks provide the basis for a wide range of both safety and non-safety applications.
**Approach:** One of the key challenges for wide acceptance is to which degree the drivers' privacy can be protected. The main technical privacy protection mechanism is the use of changing identifiers (from MAC to application layer), so called pseudonyms.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: location privacy).
**Results:** The effectiveness of this approach, however, is clearly reduced if specific characteristics of the physical layer (e.g., in the transmitted signal) reveal the link between two messages with different pseudonyms. We show how the scrambler attack bypasses the privacy protection mechanism of state-of-the-art approaches and quantify the degradation of drivers' location privacy with an extensive simulation study.
