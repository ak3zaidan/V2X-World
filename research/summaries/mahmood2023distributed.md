# Toward a Distributed Trust Management System for Misbehavior Detection in the Internet of Vehicles
Adnan Mahmood, Quan Z. Sheng, Wei Emma Zhang, Yan Wang, Subhash Sagar · ACM Transactions on Cyber-Physical Systems 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3594637 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** PKI-based security is vulnerable to compromised authorities and deceptive insiders.
**Approach:** Distributed trust management weighting reputation segments by familiarity (subjective logic), similarity and timeliness; intelligent trust threshold for identification and eviction.

**Results:** Improved misbehaviour detection and resilience (qualitative).
