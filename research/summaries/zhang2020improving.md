# Improving Efficiency of Pseudonym Revocation in VANET Using Cuckoo Filter
Han Zhang, Dong-Mei Zhang, Hong-Fei Chen, Jieyao Xu · International Conference on Speech Technology and Human-Computer Dialogue 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icct50939.2020.9295788 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** CRLs incur storage/communication overhead; Bloom filters cannot delete and have false positives.
**Approach:** Cuckoo-filter CRL compression plus Certificate Expiration List.
**Tools and scale:** Experiments.
**Results:** Reduced storage and communication; moderately low false positives; faster lookups.

- None stated in the abstract.
