# Bridging the Trust-Latency Gap in 6G NTN V2X: An Edge-Native Architecture and Standardisation Perspective
Muhammad Toaha Raza Khan, Enver Ever, Omer Chughtai, Yoney Kirsal Ever · IEEE Communications Standards Magazine 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mcomstd.2026.3684793 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Satellite latency in 6G NTN V2X conflicts with centralised security verification.
**Approach:** Edge-native architecture moving MBD and trust scoring to the vehicle; standardisation discussion.
**Tools and scale:** NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano implementation.
**Results:** End-to-end trust assessment including crypto verification about 7.14 ms; energy down up to 68% (50 mJ/inference) in eco mode.
