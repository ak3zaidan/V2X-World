# D-BlockAuth: An Authentication Scheme-Based Dual Blockchain for 5G-Assisted Vehicular Fog Computing
H. D. Al-janabi, S. Lashari, Mahmood A. Al-Shareeda, Ayman Khalil et al. · IEEE Access 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3428830 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular fog computing (VFC) is a developing concept that utilizes the ideas of fog computing to facilitate immediate communication and cooperative decision-making among vehicles.
**Approach:** This paper introduces D-BlockAuth, an innovative authentication mechanism for 5G-assisted VFC that utilizes a dual blockchain approach.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** To reach the most vehicles, this link makes use of all the features of the fifth-generation base station (5G-BS). The D-BlockAuth concept incorporates advanced cryptographic techniques such as ring signatures and group signatures, which provide improved privacy and anonymity for vehicles within the network.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "However, guaranteeing safe authentication in VFC presents notable difficulties as a result of characteristics such as dynamic network topology, extensive mobility, and limitations on resources."
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
