# Cyber Mobility Mirror for Enabling Cooperative Driving Automation in Mixed Traffic: A Co-Simulation Platform
Zhengwei Bai, Guoyuan Wu, Xuewei Qi, Yongkang Liu, Kentaro Oguchi, Matthew J. Barth · IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Magazine 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mits.2022.3203662 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Traffic simulation for CDA uses intrinsic object attributes rather than authentic perception, reducing fidelity.
**Approach:** Cyber Mobility Mirror co-simulation: a "real-world" simulator with high-fidelity sensors and perception, and a mirror-world simulator rebuilding objects; roadside LiDAR case study.
