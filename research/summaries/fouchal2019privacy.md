# Privacy Protection with Less Certificates in C-ITS
H. Fouchal · International Symposium on Computers and Communications 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iscc47284.2019.8969729 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Privacy is a very important feature nowadays on any shared system. This issue should be considered on deployed C-ITS (Cooperative Intelligent Transport Systems) in order to avoid tracking drivers who send continously messages containing their locations.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a method which reduces the number of PCs used by vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** It also reduces the PC costs for drivers.
