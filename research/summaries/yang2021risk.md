# Risk Assessment for Connected Vehicles under Stealthy Attacks on Vehicle-to-Vehicle Networks
Tianci Yang, Carlos Murguia, Chen Lv · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2023.3292188 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Detectors in CACC cannot distinguish attacks hidden in uncertainty, delays, dropouts and noise.
**Approach:** Physics-based attack detection plus semidefinite-program risk assessment of stealthy attacks' reachable effect.
**Tools and scale:** Numerical case study; network effects modelled abstractly.
**Results:** Methods illustrated effectively on case study.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: use analysis to guide controller redesign to minimise stealthy-attack impact.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — quantifies residual risk of undetected attacks, conceptually related to the safety cost of detection thresholds.
**Novelty implications:** Analytical stealthy-attack bounds exist for CACC; not with a PKI/revocation loop.
