# PriMiRe: Privacy-Preserving Misbehavior Reporting for ETSI C-ITS
Alexander Willecke, Fynn Schulze, Lars C. Wolf · 2024 IEEE 100th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2024-Fall) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2024-fall63153.2024.10757730 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** MA needs to aggregate evidence per sender despite pseudonyms, without breaking privacy.
**Approach:** PriMiRe: privacy-preserving misbehaviour reporting so the MA can aggregate reports for unique senders without learning real or pseudonymous IDs.
**Tools and scale:** ETSI C-ITS CCMS architecture; protocol and storage analysis.
**Results:** Minor changes to architecture and protocols; modest storage.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — MA report aggregation and revocation evidence is our MA/direction 1.
**Novelty implications:** Privacy-preserving per-sender aggregation at the MA is proposed (EU); its effect on time-to-neutralise in a US SCMS is open.
