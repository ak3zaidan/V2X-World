# AoA-aided Kalman Filter to Mitigate False Data Injections Attacks in Vehicular Scenarios
Luis Javier Puente Lam, Pedro M. Santos · 2024 IEEE 100th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2024-Fall) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2024-fall63153.2024.10757691 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** FDI corrupts position.
**Approach:** AoA over sidelinks with Kalman filter among trusted participants.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Impulse FDI errors below 10 m in 60%; pulse FDI mitigated in 66.39%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Cross-layer position verification.
**Novelty implications:** None.
