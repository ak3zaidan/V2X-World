# SEE-V2X: C-V2X Direct Communication Dataset: An Application-Centric Approach
Ruoshen Mo, Bo Wu, Zhaowei Tan, Hang Qiu · Proceedings of the 23rd ACM Conference on Embedded Networked Sensor Systems 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3715014.3722077 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Simulated C-V2X apps rest on ungrounded assumptions.
**Approach:** SEE-V2X: real C-V2X direct communication dataset from COTS radios emulating application traffic, with cross-layer details.
**Tools and scale:** COTS standard-compliant C-V2X radios.
**Results:** Severe collisions and jitter easily occur; configuration matters.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - real C-V2X PC5 dataset useful to validate our LTE-V2X MAC; no security.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on security claims.
