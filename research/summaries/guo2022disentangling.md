# Disentangling Perception-failure-induced Corner Cases by Counterfactual Reasoning
Xiaolan Guo, Chi Zhang, Li-Heng Xu, Li Li, Linhai Xu · 2022 IEEE International Conference on Unmanned Systems (ICUS) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icus55513.2022.9986744 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The reliability of the perception module of the autonomous driving system (ADS) faces tremendous and numerous challenges, although it has been verified following standards like ISO 26262.
**Approach:** Therefore, finding safety-critical traffic scenarios is essential for testing autonomous driving perception. It is worth to note that, a corner case related to perception failure does not necessarily mean it is induced by perception failure.
