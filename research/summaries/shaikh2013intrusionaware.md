# Intrusion‐aware trust model for vehicular ad hoc networks
Riaz Ahmed Shaikh, Ahmed Saeed Alzahrani · Security and Communication Networks 2013 · https://doi.org/10.1002/sec.862 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In vehicular ad hoc networks, peers make decisions on the basis of the information provided by the other peers. If the received information is fake, then the result could be catastrophic, such as road accidents.
**Approach:** In order to overcome these limitations, we propose a novel trust management scheme for identity anonymous vehicular ad hoc networks. Also, we prove that the proposed method is not only robust, but it also detects false location and time information.
