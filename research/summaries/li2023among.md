# Among Us: Adversarially Robust Collaborative Perception by Consensus
Yiming Li, Qi Fang, Jiamu Bai, Siheng Chen et al. · arXiv 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.09495 · relevance: medium · basis: full text

**Problem:** Adversarial attacks on deep collaborative perception.
**Approach:** ROBOSAC: sampling-based hypothesize-and-verify consensus over teammate subsets; attacker-ratio probing.
**Tools and scale:** Collaborative 3D detection (V2X-Sim).
**Results:** Resists attackers within a compute budget while using benign messages.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None explicit
- generalisability claimed.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — none: perception-level MBD, no PKI.
**Novelty implications:** Nothing of our candidate directions is ruled out.
