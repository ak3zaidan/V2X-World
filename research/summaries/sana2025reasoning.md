# Reasoning Language Models for Root Cause Analysis in 5G Wireless Networks
Mohamed Sana, Nicola Piovesan, Antonio De Domenico, Yibin Kang et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21974 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Root Cause Analysis (RCA) in mobile networks remains a challenging task due to the need for interpretability, domain expertise, and causal reasoning. In this work, we propose a lightweight framework that leverages Large Language Models (LLMs) for RCA.
**Approach:** To do so, we introduce TeleLogs, a curated dataset of annotated troubleshooting problems designed to benchmark RCA capabilities. To address this issue, we propose a two-stage training methodology that combines supervised fine-tuning with reinforcement learning to improve the accuracy and reasoning quality of LLMs.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G, LLM, reinforcement learning. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Our evaluation reveals that existing open-source reasoning LLMs struggle with these problems, underscoring the need for domain-specific adaptation. These results demonstrate the promise of domain-adapted, reasoning-enhanced LLMs for practical and explainable RCA in network operation and management.
