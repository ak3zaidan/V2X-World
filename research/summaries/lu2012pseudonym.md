# Pseudonym Changing at Social Spots: An Effective Strategy for Location Privacy in VANETs
Rongxing Lu, Xiaodong Lin, Tom H. Luan, Xiaohui Liang et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2012 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2011.2162864 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Pseudonym changes at wrong times/places do not protect location privacy.
**Approach:** Pseudonym changing at social spots (PCS); anonymity-set analytic models; game-theoretic feasibility.
**Tools and scale:** IEEE TVT; analytical models and simulation.
**Results:** Changing at highly social spots (red lights, parking lots) gives better location privacy.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — pseudonym change policy (cross-layer linkability runner-up).
**Novelty implications:** Pseudonym-change placement is classic; cross-layer identifier co-change is a different question.
