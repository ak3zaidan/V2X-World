# Blockchain-Based Lightweight Certificateless Authenticated Key Agreement Protocol for V2V Communications in IoV
Hui-Tang Lin, Wei-Li Jhuang · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2024.3400320 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** However, like most information technology systems, IoV is vulnerable to the cyberattack.
**Approach:** IoV is expected to play a key role in the development of future smart cities and intelligent transportation systems. Currently, most proposed works suffers the single-point-of-failure (SPOF) problem and the trusted third party (TTP) compromise issue.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** Consequently, improving the security of IoV is essential in ensuring its future success. In addition, an authenticated key agreement (AKA) protocol based on the legal key pairs is proposed to facilitate efficient mutual authentication and session key agreement between the vehicles.
