# Location Privacy in Usage-Based Automotive Insurance: Attacks and Countermeasures
Lu Zhou, Suguo Du, Haojin Zhu, Cailian Chen et al. · IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tifs.2018.2848227 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Usage-based insurance (UBI) is regarded as a promising way to provide accurate automotive insurance rates by analyzing the driving behaviors (e.g., speed, mileage, and harsh braking/accelerating) of drivers.
**Approach:** The best practice that has been adopted by many insurance programs to protect users' location privacy is the use of driving speed rather than GPS data. However, in this paper, we challenge this approach by presenting a novel speed-based location trajectory inference framework.
