# Influence of Realistic Perception and Surroundings on Qualitative Results in Automated and Connected Vehicle Simulation
Vitaly G. Stepanyants, Aleksandr Y. Romanov · IEEE Access 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3380369 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Automated and connected vehicles are emerging in the market.
**Approach:** Currently, solutions are being proposed to use these technologies for cooperative driving, which can significantly improve road safety. This paper analyzes the requirements for an integrated connected and automated vehicle simulation environment for simulating vehicle behavior with consideration of surrounding objects’ influence on machine perception and signal propagation.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** Scenarios of object detection and signal exchange were simulated, and data on the influence of surrounding objects on quantitative and qualitative changes in scenario simulation results was obtained.
