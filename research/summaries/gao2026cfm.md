# CFM-Bench: A Unified Multi-Domain, Multi-Task Benchmark for Channel Foundation Models
Yuan Gao, Wenjun Yu, Jun Jiang, Yunfan Li et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14975 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Channel foundation models are not reproducibly compared.
**Approach:** CFM-Bench: 157,900 examples from six domains incl. synchronised vehicular multimodal simulation; leakage-safe splits.
**Tools and scale:** 3GPP statistical, ray tracing, measurements, vehicular simulation.
**Results:** Benchmark released.

- Not stated in the abstract.
