# Coop-IntelliMD: Toward Collaborative Detection of Data Integrity Attacks in Cooperative Intelligent Transport Systems
Mohamed Ahzam Amanullah, M. B. Chhetri, S. Loke, Robin Doss · IEEE Access 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2025.3638793 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** MBD relying on self-reported messages is limited; trust approaches can be gamed.
**Approach:** Coop-IntelliMD: nodes monitor nearest neighbours, share observations, and run consensus checks on self- and neighbour-reported data.
**Tools and scale:** C-ITS simulation.
**Results:** At least 3.05% better than local MBD.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — collaborative MBD.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
