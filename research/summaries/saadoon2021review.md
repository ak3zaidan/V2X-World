# Review of Custom Grids for Updated Vehicles on VANET Simulators
Intisar Mohsin Saadoon, Qabas Abdal Zarhaa Jabbar, Dalia Shihab Ahmad · Journal La Multiapp 2021 · https://doi.org/10.37899/journallamultiapp.v2i6.519 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Do VANET simulators support new technologies and safety/security procedures?
**Approach:** Updated evaluation of VANET simulators, their components and support for 5G, SDN, edge and security.
**Tools and scale:** Review.
**Results:** Veins appears to offer these characteristics best at time of writing.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Several difficulties identified that must be solved to raise VANET simulation quality.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — reviews security support in simulators.
**Novelty implications:** Cite as evidence that integrated security support is limited.
