# Integrating Safety in VANETs: A Taxonomy and Systematic Review of VEINS Models
Z. Al-Mekhlafi, Mahmood A. Al-Shareeda, B. Mohammed, Abdulaziz M. Alayba, Ahmed M. Shamsan Saleh, Hamad A. Al-Reshidi et al. · IEEE Access 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3476512 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Organising safety-related Veins literature.
**Approach:** Systematic review and taxonomy (Applications, Solutions, Networks) of Veins-based safety research; discusses Veins constraints.
**Tools and scale:** Review of Veins works.
**Results:** Qualitative taxonomy.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Veins constraints: simulation-to-reality gap, computational complexity, integration with emerging technologies
- future: Beyond-5G, AI, blockchain security, semi-virtual/hybrid simulation, wider V2X.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — maps Veins-based safety simulation landscape.
**Novelty implications:** Useful to show that security-integrated Veins work is limited; cite.
