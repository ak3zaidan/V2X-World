# Security‐aware optimal actuator placement in vehicle platooning
Mohammad Hossein Basiri, Mohammad Pirani, Nasser L. Azad, Sebastian Fischmeister · Asian Journal of Control 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1002/asjc.2567 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract Vehicle platooning, as a large class of cyber‐physical systems, is prone to be under the risk of cyber attacks.
**Approach:** One (or more) external intelligent intruder(s) might attack one (or more) of the vehicles participating in a platoon. This paper proposes a general approach to find an optimal actuator placement strategy according to the Stackelberg game between the attacker and the defender.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: platoon/CACC attacks).
**Results:** Simulation results for h –nearest neighbor platoon formations along with experimental results using the scaled cars governed by robotic operating system (ROS) verify the effectiveness of the method.
