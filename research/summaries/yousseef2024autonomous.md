# Autonomous Vehicle Security: A Deep Dive into Threat Modeling
Amal Yousseef, Shalaka Satam, Banafsheh Saber Latibari et al. · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15348 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Autonomous vehicles (AVs) are poised to revolutionize modern transportation, offering enhanced safety, efficiency, and convenience.
**Approach:** However, the increasing complexity and connectivity of AV systems introduce significant cybersecurity challenges. This paper provides a comprehensive survey of AV security with a focus on threat modeling frameworks, including STRIDE, DREAD, and MITRE ATT\&amp;CK, to systematically identify and mitigate potential risks.
