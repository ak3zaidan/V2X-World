# A Novel Fuzzy Logic-Based Scheme for Malicious Node Eviction in a Vehicular Ad Hoc Network
Bashar Igried, Ayoub Alsarhan, Igried Al-Khawaldeh, Ahmad AL-Qerem, Amjad Aldweesh · Electronics 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics11172741 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Malicious node eviction.
**Approach:** Fuzzy logic trust level from message alert exchange behaviour; encryption.
**Tools and scale:** Evaluation.
**Results:** Throughput up to +23% and end-to-end delay up to -60%.
