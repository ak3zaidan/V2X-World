# MiTM Attack Detection Scheme Using Monitoring Information in V2X Communication
Wonjin Chung, Jungsub Ahn, Taeho Cho · 2023 9th International Conference on Computer and Communications (ICCC) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccc59590.2023.10507270 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communication technique is required for driving autonomous vehicles.
**Approach:** It calculates the optimal driving route using collected real-time traffic information. Therefore, the integrity of messages transmitted via V2X communication must be guaranteed and they must be correctly transmitted to the driving vehicle.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** No quantitative results stated in the abstract.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, messages transmitted through V2X communication can be modified or dropped by an attacker attempting a man-in-the-middle attack.
- However, in certain situations, attack detection is impossible using the existing schemes.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
