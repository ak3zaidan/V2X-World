# A Co-Simulation System in CarMaker and MATLAB for HEVs under V2X Connected Environment
Zitong Pang, Xiongxiong You, Xiaohong Jiao, Yahui Zhang · 2021 5th CAA International Conference on Vehicular Control and Intelligence (CVCI) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cvci54083.2021.9661217 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A co-simulation system in CarMaker and MAT-LAB is built for the study of control problems such as energy management optimization of hybrid electric vehicles (HEVs) under a V2X connected environment in this paper.
**Approach:** The Carmaker can provide a complete V2X connected environment and certain vehicle models, but can’t establish an individual and complete powertrain model for specific control research. Meanwhile, it is also necessary to build some control strategies of HEVs in MATLAB/Simulink environment.
