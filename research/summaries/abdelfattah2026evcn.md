# EVCN-Simulator: An Agent Based Simulator for Electric Vehicle Charging Network Resilience During and After Urban Power Outages
Mohamed Abdelfattah, Timo Hartmann · unknown venue 2026 · https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7528635 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Large scale power outages remove charging access in affected districts and redirect demand to neighbouring stations, increasing pressure on electric vehicle charging networks and the grids supplying them.
**Approach:** Observed records of charging behaviour, station queues, and grid loading during outages remain scarce, limiting analysis of disruption and recovery. We present EVCN-Simulator, an open agent based simulator that advances traffic, charging, power grid, and outage state on one clock at 30 minute resolution and carries that state across days.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting. The available text does not describe a V2X simulator, radio stack, traffic model or SCMS-style PKI in enough detail to report; scale not stated.
**Results:** We present EVCN-Simulator, an open agent based simulator that advances traffic, charging, power grid, and outage state on one clock at 30 minute resolution and carries that state across days. We assess the simulator in a published six district test case and in Berlin under two fleet horizons, where we simulate the 31.2 hour Köpenick outage.
