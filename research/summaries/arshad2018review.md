# A Review of Performance, Energy and Privacy of Intrusion Detection Systems for IoT
Junaid Arshad, Muhammad Ajmal Azad, Khaled Salah, Wei Jie et al. · Electronics 2018 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics9040629 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Internet of Things (IoT) is a disruptive technology with applications across diverse domains such as transportation and logistics systems, smart grids, smart homes, connected vehicles, and smart cities. Alongside the growth of these infrastructures, the volume and variety of attacks on these infrastructures has increased highlighting the significance of distinct protection mechanisms.
**Approach:** This paper presents a comprehensive study of existing intrusion detection systems for IoT systems including emerging systems such as Internet of Vehicles (IoV).
