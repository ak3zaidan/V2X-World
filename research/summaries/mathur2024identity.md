# Identity Spoofing Sybil Attack Protective Measures using Physical & Logical Address Mapping for the VANET (ISPLM)
Shubhra Mukherjee Mathur, Prof Dr Ravindra Gupta · International Journal of Intelligent Systems and Applications in Engineering 2024 · https://ijisae.org/index.php/IJISAE/article/view/5108 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Identity spoofing Sybil attacks.
**Approach:** ISPLM: RSU-resident mapping of physical to logical addresses, blocking one-to-many mappings.
**Tools and scale:** AODV routing simulation.
**Results:** Better throughput, data received and detection than AODV-Sybil.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Authors plan to adopt ISPLM further (truncated future-work statement).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Sybil detection at RSU; MAC-address mapping conflicts with pseudonym change.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
