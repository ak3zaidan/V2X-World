# Digital Twin-Empowered Routing Management for Reliable Multi-Hop Millimeter Wave V2X
Supat Roongpraiwan, Zongdian Li, Tao Yu, Kei Sakaguchi · 2024 IEEE 100th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2024-Fall) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2024-fall63153.2024.10757737 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** mmWave V2X needs dynamic multi-hop routing.
**Approach:** DT-based routing management with real-time calculation and trajectory-prediction planning, in Unity-based AWSIM.
**Tools and scale:** AWSIM; urban intersection; fully connected and mixed traffic.
**Results:** DT-based algorithms significantly improve reliability over VANET routing.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Reduce vehicle-DT latency
- more accurate prediction
- more sophisticated routing.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
