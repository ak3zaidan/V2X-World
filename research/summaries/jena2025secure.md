# Secure and Privacy-Preserving Vehicle Renting in VANETs Using a Zero-Trust Framework
Madhab Chandra Jena, Tamal Paul, Om Krishna Jena · International Journal of Creative and Open Research in Engineering and Management 2025 · https://doi.org/10.55041/ijcope.v2i9.265 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The rapid development of connected and intelligent transportation systems has increased the adoption of Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) for vehicle sharing and rental services.
**Approach:** However, vehicle renting in VANETs introduces significant security and privacy challenges, including unauthorized access, identity disclosure, driver tracking, Sybil attacks, credential misuse, and difficulties in establishing accountability. This paper presents a privacy-preserving and zero-trust approach for secure vehicle renting in VANETs that integrates anonymous authentication, controlled accountability, and Sybil resistance.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The proposed approach provides a unified perspective for achieving secure, privacy-aware, and accountable vehicle-rental services in future connected vehicular environments.
