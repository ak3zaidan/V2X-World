# Fast Verification of Signatures With Shared ECQV Implicit Certificates
Hee-Yong Kwon, Mun-Kyu Lee · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2019.2902382 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** RSUs must verify many signatures with ECQV implicit certificates quickly.
**Approach:** Sharing intermediate results of public-key extraction and merging extraction into batch verification; adaptive selection.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Verification five times faster than existing methods.
