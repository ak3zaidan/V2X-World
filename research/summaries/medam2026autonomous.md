# Autonomous Cyber Defense in Connected Vehicles: A Multi-Agent Approach to V2X Security
Krishna Teja Medam · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19135 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A connected vehicle has roughly 100 milliseconds to decide whether an incoming Basic Safety Message is real or fabricated.
**Approach:** If a false emergency braking alert reaches the planning pipeline in time, the car brakes - a safety failure triggered by a security failure. Existing intrusion detection systems are not designed to handle that coupling.
