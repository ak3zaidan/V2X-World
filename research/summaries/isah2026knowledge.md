# Knowledge Graph-Based Propagation for Failure Localization in 5G Core Networks
Abubakar Isah, I. Aliyu, Seungmin Oh et al. · International Conference on Ubiquitous and Future Networks 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icufn69619.2026.11628718 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Failure localization in 5G core networks remains challenging due to heterogeneous network functions and tightly coupled control-plane interactions, which obscure failure propagation paths.
**Approach:** To address this, we propose a unified framework for knowledge graph-based failure localization derived from multisource telemetry data. Within this framework, we investigate two complementary paradigms.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: 5G.
**Results:** Experimental results demonstrate that both approaches significantly improve localization accuracy and robustness while offering distinct advantages in predictive performance and explainability.
