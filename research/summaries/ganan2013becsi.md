# BECSI: Bandwidth Efficient Certificate Status Information Distribution Mechanism for VANETs
Carlos Gañán, José L. Muñoz, Óscar Esparza, Jonathan Kok Keong Loo et al. · Mobile Information Systems 2013 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2013/139246 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** CRL distribution in 1609.2 VANETs requires high bandwidth and frequent updates.
**Approach:** BECSI: Merkle hash trees plus delta-CRLs, vehicles as mobile repositories.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Reduced repository load and response time; improved freshness.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — CRL distribution efficiency with vehicles relaying status, analogous to our RSU/V2V CRL broadcast.
**Novelty implications:** None.
