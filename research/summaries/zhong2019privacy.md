# Privacy-preserving authentication scheme with full aggregation in VANET
Hong Zhong, Shunshun Han, Jie Cui, Jing Zhang et al. · Information Sciences 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ins.2018.10.021 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract Vehicular Ad-hoc Network (VANET) is the fundamental of intelligent transportation systems. Security and privacy are the important issues needed to be addressed.
**Approach:** In order to overcome the challenge, we propose a privacy-preserving authentication scheme with full aggregation in VANET, using certificateless aggregate signature to achieve secure vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I) communications.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** When a vehicle enters an area under a new road side unit (RSU)’s coverage, we pre-calculate some data for once, thus the computation cost in sign phase can be reduced. The length of aggregated signature is constant which reduces the communication and storage overhead.
