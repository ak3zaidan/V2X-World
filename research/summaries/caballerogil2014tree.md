# Tree-Based Revocation for Certificateless Authentication in Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks
Pino Caballero‐Gil, Francisco Martín-Fernández, Cándido Caballero‐Gil · Journal of Computer and Communications 2014 · https://doi.org/10.4236/jcc.2014.29003 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Revocation for certificateless authentication.
**Approach:** Perfect k-ary hash trees with duplex SHA-3.
**Tools and scale:** Analysis.
**Results:** Single hash iteration per insertion.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Analyse optimal parameters and compare with previous proposals.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
