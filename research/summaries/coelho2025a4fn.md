# A4FN: an Agentic AI Architecture for Autonomous Flying Networks
André Coelho, Pedro Ribeiro, Helder Fontes et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.03829 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This position paper presents A4FN, an Agentic Artificial Intelligence (AI) architecture for intent-driven automation in Flying Networks (FNs) using Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) as access nodes.
**Approach:** A4FN leverages Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs) to enable real-time, context-aware network control via a distributed agentic system.
