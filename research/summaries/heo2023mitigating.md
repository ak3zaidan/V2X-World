# Mitigating Packet Collisions by Predicting Collective Perception Message Transmissions in Cellular V2X Environment
Songmu Heo, Hyogon Kim · 2023 Fourteenth International Conference on Ubiquitous and Future Networks (ICUFN) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icufn57995.2023.10199643 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ETSI CPM generation rules make CPM traffic aperiodic, disrupting NR sidelink Mode 2 sensing-based SPS and causing collisions.
**Approach:** Deep learning predicts when sensed objects will qualify for CPM inclusion, and reserves resources accordingly.
**Tools and scale:** NR-V2X sidelink Mode 2 simulation with CPM traffic; scale not stated.
**Results:** Wasted resources and packet collisions "greatly reduced" versus standard SPS.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — NR sidelink Mode 2 SPS behaviour with aperiodic traffic is modelled in our radio stack; no security dimension.
**Novelty implications:** Interaction of aperiodic traffic and SPS already studied; the verification-load angle remains open.
