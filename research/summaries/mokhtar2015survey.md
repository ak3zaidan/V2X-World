# Survey on Security Issues in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Bassem Mahmoud Mokhtar, Mohamed Azab · Alexandria Engineering Journal 2015 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aej.2015.07.011 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad hoc NETworks are special case of ad hoc networks that, besides lacking infrastructure, communicating entities move with various accelerations. Accordingly, this impedes establishing reliable end-to-end communication paths and having efficient data transfer.
**Approach:** In this survey, we discuss the security features, challenges, and attacks of VANETs, and we classify the security attacks of VANETs due to the different network layers.
