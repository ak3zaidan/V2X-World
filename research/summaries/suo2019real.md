# Real-time Trust-Building Schemes for Mitigating Malicious Behaviors in Connected and Automated Vehicles
Da-Jiang Suo, S. Sarma · International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/itsc.2019.8917078 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communication has raised security and safety concerns. A malicious attacker can not only have a compromised vehicle broadcast spoofed positioning information to nearby vehicles, but also send fake emergency reports to traffic management systems.
**Approach:** This paper proposes two types of architectures (centralized and distributed) for mitigating malicious behaviors based on the collaboration between trust authorities and vehicles. In addition, the proposed architectures for trust-building can be extended in future works to encourage benign behaviors in the long run.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** The pros and cons of each architecture are evaluated through an experimental simulation.
