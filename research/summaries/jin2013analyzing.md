# Analyzing the impact of false-accident cyber attacks on traffic flow stability in connected vehicle environment
Peter J. Jin, Guohui Zhang, C. Michael Walton, Xiaowen Jiang, Amit Singh · 2013 International Conference on Connected Vehicles and Expo (ICCVE) 2013 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccve.2013.6799866 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Traffic impact of false-accident attacks considering driver visual verification.
**Approach:** Ring-road linear stability tests with sensitivity to attack duration, attacker position and reaction range.
**Tools and scale:** Traffic simulation (ring road).
**Results:** False-accident attacks do not necessarily significantly affect traffic flow stability.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — traffic impact of V2X attacks with human/AV verification.
**Novelty implications:** Shows attack impact can be small; supports measuring rather than assuming harm.
