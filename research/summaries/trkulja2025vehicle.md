# Vehicle-to-Everything Trust: Enabling Autonomous Trust Assessment of V2X Data by Vehicles
Nataša Trkulja, Artur Hermann, Paul Lukas Duhr, Echo Meißner, Michael Buchholz, Frank Kargl et al. · Computer Science in Cars Symposium 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3736130.3762691 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** MBD alone cannot detect all attacks in cooperative driving.
**Approach:** Trust Assessment Framework prototype combining multiple security mechanisms as trust sources, integrated with CACC simulation.
**Tools and scale:** CACC simulation; three injection attacks.
**Results:** Crashes reduced by 86% vs single mechanism.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — Quantifies safety outcome (crashes) of security mechanism design in closed loop, overlapping our safety-cost-of-security and closed-loop MBD directions.
**Novelty implications:** Crash reduction from trust fusion shown for CACC; PKI revocation timing and city scale remain.
