# ATTACK RESISTANT VANET DATA COMMUNICATION USING VEHICLE MOVEMENT BEHAVIOR ANALYSIS
K. Girija, K. Saravanan · International Journal of Modern Trends in Engineering &amp; Research 2017 · https://doi.org/10.21884/ijmter.2017.4285.jusvx · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** -The vehicular ad-hoc networks are deployed to support data communication over the vehicles.
**Approach:** The Sybil attacks are initiated by the malicious nodes to forge the identity of the neighbor nodes. The network layer and application layers are damaged by the Sybil attacks with more channel resource utilization activities.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The vehicle driving pattern discovery process is improved with spatio temporal parameters. The vehicle movement behavior information is maintained in the infrastructure environment to improve the efficiency of the attacker discovery process.
