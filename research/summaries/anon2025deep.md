# Deep Learning-Based Intrusion Detection System for Smart Cars
Unknown authors · REST Journal on Data Analytics and Artificial Intelligence 2025 · https://doi.org/10.46632/jdaai/4/3/11 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The increasing integration of smart and autonomous functionalities in modern vehicles has introduced new cybersecurity challenges, particularly concerning vehicular communication and control systems.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a deep learning-based intrusion detection system designed to identify and classify cyberattacks in smart cars in real-time. The proposed system exhibits strong adaptability, low detection latency, and potential for real-time deployment in connected vehicle environments, thereby contributing to the development of secure intelligent transportation systems.
**Tools and scale:** Three deep learning models—Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN), Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN), and Long Short-Term Memory networks (LSTM)—are implemented and evaluated on a labeled dataset.
**Results:** The experimental results demonstrate that the LSTM model outperforms CNN and RNN in terms of accuracy (98.1%) and F1-score (97.9%), making it particularly effective for detecting sequential and time-dependent attacks.
