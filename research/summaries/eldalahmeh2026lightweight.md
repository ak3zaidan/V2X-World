# Lightweight Mutual Authentication in VANETs: A Software‐Defined Networking and Edge Computing Approach
Adi El-Dalahmeh, Layla Albdour · IET Communications 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1049/cmu2.70189 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) operating within extended Internet of Vehicles (IoV) infrastructures face critical challenges related to scalability, authentication latency, privacy preservation, and infrastructure‐level attack resilience. Traditional authentication schemes often optimize cryptographic efficiency in isolation, without addressing network‐wide coordination and dynamic trust enforcement.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a lightweight and architecture‐aware mutual authentication framework that integrates elliptic curve cryptography (ECC), edge‐assisted batch verification, and software‐defined networking (SDN)‐based trust orchestration.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** Edge nodes perform aggregated batch verification to significantly reduce computational overhead in dense traffic scenarios, while the SDN controller enables global trust management, real‐time revocation propagation, and programmable flow enforcement across multiple RSU zones. Security analysis under the Dolev–Yao adversary model demonstrates robustness against classical and infrastructure‐level threats.
