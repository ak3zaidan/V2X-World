# A Cooperative Detection Model Based on Artificial Neural Network for VANET QoS-OLSR Protocol
Amjad El Khatib, Azzam Mourad, Hadi Otrok, Omar Abdel Wahab, Jamal Bentahar · IEEE International Conference on Ubiquitous Wireless Broadband 2015 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icuwb.2015.7324400 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Speed misbehaviour breaking QoS-OLSR clusters.
**Approach:** Cooperative ANN-based aggregation of detection judgments with continuous learning.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Higher detection probability, fewer false alarms.
