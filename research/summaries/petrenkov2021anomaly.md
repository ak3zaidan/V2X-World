# Anomaly Detection in Vehicle Platoon with Third-Order Consensus Control
Denis Petrenkov, Anton Agafonov · 2021 Ural Symposium on Biomedical Engineering, Radioelectronics and Information Technology (USBEREIT) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/usbereit51232.2021.9455022 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The development of autonomous connected vehicles, in particular, moving as a platoon formation, has received great attention in recent years.
**Approach:** The autonomous movement allows to increase the efficiency of the transportation infrastructure usage, reduce the fuel consumption, improve road safety, decrease traffic congestion, and others. To maintain an optimal spacing policy in a platoon formation, it is necessary to exchange information between vehicles.
