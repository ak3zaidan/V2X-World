# Continuously Weighted Majority Voting for Detection of Data Tampering Attacks in Redundant CACC Systems
Taisei Awaji, Ryogo Kubo · 2024 IEEE International Conference on Industrial Technology (ICIT) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icit58233.2024.10541011 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Data tampering in redundant CACC.
**Approach:** Continuously weighted majority voting.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation, three redundant paths.
**Results:** Detects attacks with modelling errors and multipath attacks.
