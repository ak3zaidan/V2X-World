# Schnorr-Based Authentication Framework for Secure Vehicle to Vehicle Communication
Adi El-Dalahmeh, Eman Abu Mariah · 2025 12th International Conference on Information Technology (ICIT) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icit64950.2025.11049259 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The increasing adoption of vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) communication in intelligent transportation systems (ITS) brings both benefits and challenges, particularly in the areas of security and privacy. In high-traffic scenarios, traditional authentication methods such as the Elliptic Curve Digital Signature Algorithm (ECDSA) often struggle with scalability and computational demands.
**Approach:** To address these limitations, we propose a lightweight, privacy-aware authentication scheme that integrates elliptic curve cryptography (ECC) with Schnorr signatures.
**Tools and scale:** Simulated evaluations show that it can handle up to 950 messages per second at roadside units (RSUs), lowers the success rate of replay attacks to just 0.02%, and offers improved resilience against Sybil attacks.
**Results:** Compared to ECDSA, our solution achieves a 40% reduction in computational overhead. Simulated evaluations show that it can handle up to 950 messages per second at roadside units (RSUs), lowers the success rate of replay attacks to just 0.02%, and offers improved resilience against Sybil attacks.
