# SRPV: A Scalable Revocation Scheme for Pseudonyms-Based Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Vitaly Ford, Miao Pan, Zhu Han, Khaled Rabieh · ICC 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc.2018.8422736 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Revocation lists for unlinkable pseudonyms are large and slow to check.
**Approach:** Chameleon-hash pseudonyms revoked by adding a single trapdoor; multi-signature cooperative voting to revoke.
**Tools and scale:** Experiments and evaluations.
**Results:** Revokes many pseudonyms with minimal storage and computation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — compact pseudonym revocation (alternative to SCMS linkage seeds).
**Novelty implications:** Compact revocation alternatives exist.
