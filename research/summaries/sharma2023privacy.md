# Privacy Aware Post Quantum Secure Ant Colony Optimization Ad Hoc On-Demand Distance Vector Routing in Intent Based Internet of Vehicles for 5G Smart Cities
Tannu Sharma, M. Ranjith Kumar, Sachin Kaushal, Dharminder Chaudhary, Kashif Saleem · IEEE Access 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2023.3311515 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Secure routing avoiding suspicious vehicles in IoV.
**Approach:** Ring-LWE key exchange, suspicious vehicle detection and ACO-AODV routing.
**Tools and scale:** MATLAB; four roads, 300-600 vehicles, ~30% malicious.
**Results:** Better drop, throughput, delay and overhead than three frameworks.
