# Authentication-based secure data dissemination in 5G-enabled VANETs
A. Verma, Gulista Khan · International Journal on Information Technologies and Security 2026 · https://doi.org/10.59035/ldns9118 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the extensive usage of Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) in intelligent transportation, issues such as securing and lowering the latency period become increasingly important, particularly related to potential malicious nodes.
