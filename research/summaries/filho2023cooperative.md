**Problem:** Cooperative Vehicular Platooning (Co-VP) is a prime example of Cooperative Cyber-Physical Systems (Co-CPS), offering great potential for enhancing road safety by reducing human involvement in driving.
**Approach:** However, this domain presents significant challenges, incorporating control theory, communications, vehicle dynamics, security, and traffic engineering. This survey explores recent advancements in Co-VP, covering control strategies, communication infrastructures, and cybersecurity.
