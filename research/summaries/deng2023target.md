# TARGET: Automated Scenario Generation from Traffic Rules for Testing Autonomous Vehicles via Validated LLM-Guided Knowledge Extraction
Yao Deng, Jiaohong Yao, Zhi Tu et al. · IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering, Jul. 2025, pp. 1950-1968, vol. 51 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tse.2025.3569086 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Recent incidents with autonomous vehicles highlight the need for rigorous testing to ensure safety and robustness.
**Approach:** We propose TARGET, an end-to-end framework that automatically generates test scenarios from traffic rules. To address complexity, we leverage a Large Language Model (LLM) to extract knowledge from traffic rules.
