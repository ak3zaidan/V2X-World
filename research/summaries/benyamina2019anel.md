# ANEL: A novel efficient and lightweight authentication scheme for vehicular ad hoc networks
Zakarya Benyamina, Khelifa Benahmed, F. Bounaama · Comput. Networks 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.comnet.2019.106899 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Hence, VANET requires not only efficient communications, but also lightweight security mechanisms to overcome security challenges.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a novel efficient and lightweight authentication scheme (ANEL). In addition, the proposed scheme can achieve security objectives of VANET such as authentication, privacy-preservation, non-repudiation, etc.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** Moreover, our scheme provides an efficient biological password login, system key update, biological password update, and other security services. The comparison results of our scheme with existing MAC-based authentication schemes in terms of computation overhead, communication overhead, and security services show that the proposed scheme is more efficient and provides a high level of security.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "They considered as the best schemes in terms of computation and communication overheads when compared with other schemes that use other cryptography techniques, but they have some limitations."
- "The ANEL is a MAC-based authentication scheme that offers all the advantages of the MAC-based authentication schemes and overcomes all their limitations at the same time."
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
