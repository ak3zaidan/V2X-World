# Model Context Protocol-based Internet of Experts For Wireless Environment-aware LLM Agents
Zongxi Liu, Hongyang Du · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.01834 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Large Language Models (LLMs) exhibit strong general-purpose reasoning abilities but lack access to wireless environment information due to the absence of native sensory input and domain-specific priors.
**Approach:** To overcome these limitations, we propose a Model Context Protocol (MCP)-based Internet of Experts (IoX) framework that equips LLMs with wireless environment-aware reasoning capabilities. The framework incorporates a set of lightweight expert models, each trained to solve a specific deterministic task in wireless communications, such as detecting a specific wireless attribute, e.g., line-of-sight propagation, Doppler effects, or fading conditions.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Evaluated across multiple mainstream LLMs, the proposed wireless environment-aware LLM agents achieve 40%-50% improvements in classification tasks over LLM-only baselines.
