# Impact of Autonomous Vehicles on Traffic Flow in Rural and Urban Areas Using a Traffic Flow Simulator
Makoto Fujiu, Yuma Morisaki, Jyunich Takayama · Sustainability 2024 · https://doi.org/10.3390/su16020658 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Autonomous vehicles have the potential to significantly improve modes of transportation, and many businesses and research facilities are developing such systems.
**Approach:** Although there are studies on the social implementation of autonomous vehicles, these studies are based on limited conditions such as predetermined driving environments. Therefore, in this study, we target urban areas and rural areas, and we simulate a behavioral algorithm for autonomous vehicles being developed and owned by Kanazawa University.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** The results obtained show that as the mixing rate of autonomous vehicles increases, the delay between OD intervals increases. The delay time for all vehicles slightly increases as the mixing rate of autonomous vehicles increased from 10 to 45%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "When the mixing rate increased from 45 to 50%, the delay time for all vehicles decreased notably, and when the mixing rate was 50 to 100%, it remained constant."
