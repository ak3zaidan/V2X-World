# Large Language Model-Empowered Intent-Driven Network Configuration Generator
Qiao Li, Chungang Yang, Yao Wang, Rongqian Fan et al. · 2025 IEEE 102nd Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2025-Fall) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2025-fall65116.2025.11310185 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Translating ambiguous intents into network configurations for 6G.
**Approach:** LLM framework with fine-tuning, RAG, prompt engineering and distillation.
**Tools and scale:** Network slicing use case with open-source tools.
**Results:** Generation time and accuracy improved by 24% and 25%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — LLM configuration generation, like our configuring agent, but generic networks.
**Novelty implications:** LLM intent-to-config is established.
