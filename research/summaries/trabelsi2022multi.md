# Multi-BSM: An Anomaly Detection and Position Falsification Attack Mitigation Approach in Connected Vehicles
Zouheir Trabelsi, Syed Sarmad Shah, Kadhim Hayawi · Electronics 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics11203282 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** Position falsification attacks.
**Approach:** Multi-BSM: consecutive BSMs with different parameters, RSUs linked to central database warn vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** High detection even in high density.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Limited to position falsification.
- future fog computing.
- V2X credential management being explored.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — RSU-assisted MBD with warnings.
**Novelty implications:** None.
