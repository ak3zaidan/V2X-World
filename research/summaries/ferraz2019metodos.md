# Métodos para melhorar os procedimentos de ligação e revogação de certificados em redes veiculares.
Leonardo T. D. Ferraz · LA Referencia (Red Federada de Repositorios Institucionales de Publicaciones Científicas) 2019 · https://openalex.org/W7120773706 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular communication technologies, also called Vehicle-to-everything (V2X) systems, are expected to become common in the future, providing better effciency and safety in transportation.
**Approach:** This envisioned large-scale deployment, however, critically depends on addressing some requirements. For example, to prevent abuse by drivers, messages exchanged among authorized vehicles must be authenticated, which implies the need of a Vehicular Public Key Infrastructure (VPKI).
