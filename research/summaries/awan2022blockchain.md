# A Blockchain-Assisted Trusted Clustering Mechanism for IoT-Enabled Smart Transportation System
Kamran Ahmad Awan, Ikram Ud Din, Ahmad Almogren · Sustainability 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/su142214889 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-hoc Network (VANET) is a modern concept of transportation that was formulated by extending Mobile Ad-hoc Networks (MANETs). VANET presents diverse opportunities to modernize transportation to enhance safety, security, and privacy.
**Approach:** The most prominent solution proposed is to divide these nodes into clusters. In this paper, we propose a clustering mechanism that provides security and maintains quality after the cluster formulation based on the pre-defined Quality-of-Service (QoS) parameters.
**Tools and scale:** blockchain (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** The results show a significant improvement in terms of security and clustering quality.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Direct communication raises various limitations, most importantly, the overhead ratio.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — Generic VANET/IoV trust, reputation or intrusion-detection scheme evaluated in isolation; no SCMS, revocation pipeline, or counterfactual cross-layer evaluation.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on our novelty claims; background only.
