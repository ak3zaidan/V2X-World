# Estimating Quality-of-Service in Urban Vehicular Networks Through Machine Learning
Duarte Dias, Miguel Luís, Pedro Rito, Susana Sargento · IEEE Access 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3424304 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** QoS estimation for ITS-G5 and 5G in urban vehicular networks.
**Approach:** ML regressors on data from collection campaigns in Aveiro.
**Tools and scale:** Real data, Aveiro, Portugal; ITS-G5 and 5G.
**Results:** RF and XGBoost similar errors, XGBoost faster; location-independent configurations discussed.
