# Classifying Network Flows through a Multi-Modal 1D CNN Approach Using Unified Traffic Representations
Ravi Veerabhadrappa, Poornima Athikatte Sampigerayappa · Computer systems science and engineering 2025 · https://doi.org/10.32604/csse.2025.061285 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** : In recent years, the analysis of encrypted network traffic has gained momentum due to the widespread use of Transport Layer Security and Quick UDP Internet Connections protocols, which complicate and prolong the analysis process.
**Approach:** Classification models face challenges in understanding and classifying unknown traffic because of issues related to interpret ability and the representation of traffic data. To tackle these complexities, multi-modal representation learning can be employed to extract meaningful features and represent them in a lower-dimensional latent space.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: dataset.
**Results:** For the classification task, both the Random Forest and 1D-CNN models, when trained on multimodal representations, achieve over 90% accuracy on a highly imbalanced dataset.
