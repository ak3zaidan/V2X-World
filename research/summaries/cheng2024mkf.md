# MKF-ADS: Multi-Knowledge Fusion Based Self-supervised Anomaly Detection System for Control Area Network
Pengzhou Cheng, Zongru Wu, Gongshen Liu · arXiv (Cornell University) 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.04293 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Control Area Network (CAN) is an essential communication protocol that interacts between Electronic Control Units (ECUs) in the vehicular network.
**Approach:** However, CAN is facing stringent security challenges due to innate security risks. Intrusion detection systems (IDSs) are a crucial safety component in remediating Vehicular Electronics and Systems vulnerabilities.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Compared with the baseline in the same paradigm, the error rate and FAR are 2.62\% and 2.41\% and achieve a promising F1-score of 97.3\%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, CAN is facing stringent security challenges due to innate security risks.
- However, existing IDSs fail to identify complexity attacks and have higher false alarms owing to capability bottleneck.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
