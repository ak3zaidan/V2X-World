# Privacy-preserving schemes for Ad Hoc Social Networks: A survey
Mohamed Amine Ferrag, Leandros Maglaras, Ahmed Ahmim · arXiv 2016 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.06095 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we review the state of the art of privacy-preserving schemes for ad hoc social networks, including, mobile social networks (MSNs) and vehicular social networks (VSNs).
**Approach:** Specifically, we select and in-detail examine thirty-three privacy preserving schemes developed for or applied in the context of ad hoc social networks. These schemes are published between 2008 and 2016.
