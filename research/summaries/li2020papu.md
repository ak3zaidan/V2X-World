# PAPU: Pseudonym Swap With Provable Unlinkability Based on Differential Privacy in VANETs
Xinghua Li, Huijuan Zhang, Yanbing Ren, Siqi Ma et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2020.3001381 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Pseudonym swaps lack theoretical unlinkability; existing swaps linked with probability above 60%.
**Approach:** Pseudonym indistinguishability via generalised differential privacy and an exponential-mechanism swap.
**Tools and scale:** IEEE IoT-J; experiments.
**Results:** Existing works linkable at more than 60%; PAPU gives provable unlinkability for similar driving states.
