# Adaptive and Low-Cost Traffic Engineering: A Traffic Matrix Clustering Perspective
Yi Liu, Nan Geng, Mingwei Xu, Yuan Yang, Enhuan Dong, Chenyi Liu, Qiaoyin Gan, Qing Li, Jianping Wu · IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jsac.2025.3528818 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Traffic engineering (TE) has attracted extensive attention over the years.
**Approach:** Operators expect to design a TE scheme that accommodates traffic dynamics well and achieves good TE performance with little overhead. Some approaches like oblivious routing compute an optimal static routing based on a large traffic matrix (TM) range, which usually leads to much performance loss.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: simulations.
**Results:** The results show that ALTE achieves near-optimal performance for dynamic traffic and introduces little overhead of routing updates.
