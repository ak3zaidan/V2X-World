# Lightweight anonymous identity authentication protocol for Internet of vehicles based on fog and blockchain
Mingxin Chen, Yefeng He, Xi Zhang et al. · venue not stated 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3703935.3703976 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the rapid development of the Internet of vehicles, vehicle identity anonymous authentication has become the key to ensure network security and user privacy.While traditional authentication mechanisms are limited by communication delays and single point of failure issues, blockchain technology combined with fog computing provides a completely new solution for the Internet of vehicles.Based on blockchain and fog computing, this paper proposes a lightweight anonymous identity authentication protocol for the Internet of vehicles.This protocol fully utilizes the decentralized nature of blockchain technology to build a distributed system that maintains information consistency without the need for a central authority, enhancing security.The protocol is further combined with fog computing to divide the network of vehicles into multiple fogs and disperse the authentication process to each fog area, reducing the communication overhead between data centers, thereby reducing the communication delay and improving the authentication efficiency.The agreement assigns Roadside Unit (RSU) work to foghead vehicles, which is an incremental modification of current work.This improvement reduces equipment consumption and makes the authentication procedure more simple and more clear.At the same time, the fogging factor and foghead factor of the vehicle in fog are defined by combining the real-time position and driving information of the vehicle, so as to support more accurate driving decision and safety control.Through the security analysis of this paper, this scheme can resist a variety of existing attack methods,
