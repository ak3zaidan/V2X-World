# Hierarchical Index Retrieval-Driven Wireless Network Intent Translation With LLM
Jingyu Wang, Lingqi Guo, Jianyu Wu, Caijun Yan, Haifeng Sun, Lei Zhang, Zirui Zhuang, Qi Qi, Jianxin Liao · IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tmc.2025.3564937 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Intent-Based Networking (IBN) represents an emerging network management concept that is designed to fulfill user service requirements through automation.
**Approach:** At its core, IBN is capable of translating user intent into network policies, thereby enabling automated configuration and management. However, the application of IBN has been limited by challenges associated with automation and intelligence.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** LIT incorporates Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to reference hardware manuals and enhance the generation results of LLMs. To reduce noise from retrieval results, we optimized the general RAG process.
