# Is Your Autonomous Vehicle Safe? Understanding the Threat of Electromagnetic Signal Injection Attacks on Traffic Scene Perception
Wenhao Liao, Sineng Yan, Youqian Zhang, Xinwei Zhai, Yuanyuan Wang, Eugene Yujun Fu · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05239 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Autonomous vehicles rely on camera-based perception systems to comprehend their driving environment and make crucial decisions, thereby ensuring vehicles to steer safely.
**Approach:** However, a significant threat known as Electromagnetic Signal Injection Attacks (ESIA) can distort the images captured by these cameras, leading to incorrect AI decisions and potentially compromising the safety of autonomous vehicles. Despite the serious implications of ESIA, there is limited understanding of its impacts on the robustness of AI models across various and complex driving scenarios.
