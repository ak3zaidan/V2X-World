# Improving Network Delay Predictions Using GNNs
Miquel Farreras, Paola Soto, Miguel Camelo, Lluís Fàbrega, Pere Vilà · Journal of Network and Systems Management 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s10922-023-09758-9 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** GNN delay models fail to generalise to larger networks.
**Approach:** GAIN models extending RouteNet with networking concepts; compared to RouteNet-Erlang and Queuinx.
**Tools and scale:** OMNeT++-generated BNN-UPC dataset; real traffic traces.
**Results:** Challenge entry 28.739% MAPE; later versions improved.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Generalisation to larger topologies remains the core limitation of GNN models.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - network surrogate (direction 3).
**Novelty implications:** No effect on V2X.
