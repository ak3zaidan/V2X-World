# VANETs Security Issues and Challenges: A Survey
Muawia Abdelmagid Elsadig, Yahia A. Fadlalla · Indian Journal of Science and Technology 2016 · https://doi.org/10.17485/ijst/2016/v9i28/97782 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs) are sought to control traffic, avoid accidents, and control other aspects of traffic. It (VANET) is a piece of critical infrastructure that bolsters traffic management efficiency and road safety.
**Approach:** As a result, we conclude that security is the key to success for VANET applications, but still some critical challenges remain. Moreover, when designing a sufficient security solution; privacy preservation, productivity, and usability should be taken into account.
