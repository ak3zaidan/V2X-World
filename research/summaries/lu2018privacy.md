# A Privacy-Preserving Trust Model Based on Blockchain for VANETs
Zhao-Jun Lu, Wenchao Liu, Qian Wang, G. Qu et al. · IEEE Access 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2018.2864189 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The public key infrastructure-based authentication protocol provides basic security services for the vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs). However, trust and privacy are still open issues due to the unique characteristics of VANETs.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a blockchain-based anonymous reputation system (BARS) to establish a privacy-preserving trust model for VANETs.
**Tools and scale:** The certificate and revocation transparency is implemented efficiently with the proofs of presence and absence based on the extended blockchain technology.
**Results:** A set of experiments is conducted to evaluate BARS in terms of security, validity, and performance, and the results show that BARS is able to establish a trust model with transparency, conditional anonymity, efficiency, and robustness for VANETs.
