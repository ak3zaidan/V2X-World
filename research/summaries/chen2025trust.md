# Trust Model-Based Consensus Optimization for Vehicle Platooning Networks: A Novel Deep Reinforcement Learning Approach With GenAI
Haotian Chen, Xiaoyuan Fu, Quan Yuan, Zirui Zhuang et al. · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2025.3559672 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle platooning has emerged as a promising solution for efficient traffic management. Multiple platoons traveling in a cooperative way can alleviate congestion and enhance driving safety by information sharing and consensus.
**Approach:** To tackle above challenges, we design a role-adaptive trust model for trust evaluation of platoons in consideration of different roles of vehicles within a platoon. Based on the proposed model, we formulate a blockchain consensus optimization problem to facilitate both reliability and efficiency of data consensus among multiple platoons.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset, blockchain (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** Experiment results demonstrate the effectiveness and efficiency of the proposed consensus optimization approach.
