# Simulating Cellular Communications in Vehicular Networks: Making SimuLTE Interoperable with Veins
Giovanni Nardini, Antonio Virdis, Giovanni Stea · arXiv 2017 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.02208 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** VeinsLTE merged SimuLTE, Veins and INET, preventing independent evolution.
**Approach:** Modifications making SimuLTE interoperable with Veins and INET without modifying them.
**Tools and scale:** OMNeT++, SimuLTE, Veins, INET, SUMO; LTE.
**Results:** Configuration example; vehicles moved by SUMO with LTE connectivity and optional handover.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Previous VeinsLTE prevented independent upgrades.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — LTE + vehicular co-simulation; we have LTE-V2X natively.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
