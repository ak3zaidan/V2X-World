# Fault self-healing and unmanned aerial vehicle autonomous inspection system for park distribution network based on digital twin technology
Yifei Sun, Ruolin Liang, Zilong Guo · 2025 IEEE 5th International Conference on Electronic Technology, Communication and Information (ICETCI) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icetci64844.2025.11084004 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper uses digital twin technology to explore a fault self-healing and unmanned aerial vehicle autonomous inspection system for a park distribution network.
**Approach:** By constructing a digital twin model of the distribution network, this system achieves real-time monitoring and accurate simulation of its operation status. When a fault occurs, the system can quickly locate the fault location, automatically select the optimal fault isolation and recovery strategy, achieve rapid self-healing of the fault, and thus improve the reliability and safety of the distribution network.
