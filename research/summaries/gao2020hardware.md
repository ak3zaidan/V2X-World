# Hardware-in-the-Loop Simulation Platform for Autonomous Vehicle AEB Prototyping and Validation
Ying Gao, Zhigang Xu, Xiangmo Zhao, Guiping Wang et al. · 2020 IEEE 23rd International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/itsc45102.2020.9294704 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the evolution of autonomous vehicle (AV), especially its ability to cope with harsh conditions, which is attracting intense attention from both academia and industry practitioners.
**Approach:** In this paper, we developed a novel indoor virtual-reality combined HIL simulation platform for AV testing. Further, a group of comparison experiments on both real road environment and the proposed indoor HIL platform is conducted.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** The experimental results show that the testing data obtained from the proposed HIL platform have a high similarity to those of the real road tests.
