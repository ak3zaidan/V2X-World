# Integrating Two-Tier Optimization Algorithm With Convolutional Bi-LSTM Model for Robust Anomaly Detection in Autonomous Vehicles
Moneerah Alotaibi, Manal Abdullah Alohali, Khalid Mahmood, Asma A. Alhashmi et al. · IEEE Access 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3523539 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Industrial development has changed vehicles of traditional into autonomous vehicles (AVs). AVs play a significant role since they are measured as a vital module of smart cities.
**Approach:** This article develops an improved whale optimization algorithm-based feature selection using explainable artificial intelligence for robust anomaly detection (IWOAFS-XAIAD) technique in autonomous driving.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi, deep learning (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** The experimental validation of the IWOAFS-XAIAD technique portrayed a superior accuracy value of 98.52% over the existing methods.
