# Detection and Identification of CPS Attacks with Application in Vehicle Platooning: a Generalized Luenberger Approach
Yu Xuan, Mohammad Naghnaeian · 2021 American Control Conference (ACC) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.23919/acc50511.2021.9483074 · relevance: low · basis: abstract only

**Problem:** Detection and localisation of deception attacks in distributed CPS.
**Approach:** Generalized Luenberger Observer for LTI systems with delays.
**Tools and scale:** Applied to a longitudinal platoon example.
**Results:** Distinguishes attacks from bounded noise and identifies attack location.
