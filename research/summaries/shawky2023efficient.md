# An Efficient Cross-Layer Authentication Scheme for Secure Communication in Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks
Mahmoud A. Shawky, Mirko Bottarelli, Gregory Epiphaniou et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2023.3244077 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Intelligent transportation systems contribute to improved traffic safety by facilitating real-time communication between vehicles and infrastructures.
**Approach:** This paper presents a cross-layer authentication scheme for vehicular communication, incorporating the short-term reciprocal features of the wireless channel for re-authenticating the corresponding terminal, reducing the overall complexity and computation and communication overheads.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation results show the effectiveness of the proposed scheme even at small signal-to-noise ratios.
