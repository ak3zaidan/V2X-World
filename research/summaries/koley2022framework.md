# A Framework for Evaluating Connected Vehicle Security against False Data Injection Attacks
Ipsita Koley, Sunandan Adhikary, Rohit Rohit, Soumyajit Dey · 2022 25th Euromicro Conference on Digital System Design (DSD) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/dsd57027.2022.00128 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** No automated tool to test CV prototypes against FDI.
**Approach:** Automated tool flow for FDI attack synthesis and simulation on customisable platoons.
**Tools and scale:** Platoon dynamics models; four topologies.
**Results:** Tool generates safety- and performance-violating attacks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Homogeneous platoons only.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Automated attack synthesis.
**Novelty implications:** None.
