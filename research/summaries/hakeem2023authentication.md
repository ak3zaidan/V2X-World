# Authentication and encryption protocol with revocation and reputation management for enhancing 5G-V2X security
Shimaa A. Abdel Hakeem, Hyung-Chan Kim · Journal of King Saud University: Computer and Information Sciences 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jksuci.2023.101638 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** 5G-V2X key management and revocation lack real-time update.
**Approach:** Bilinear pairing + AES protocol with secret-shared master key and reputation-based revocation.
**Tools and scale:** MIRACL library.
**Results:** Short signing/verification times vs other pairing protocols.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Reputation-based revocation.
**Novelty implications:** None.
