# A Plausibility-Based Fault Detection Method for High-Level Fusion Perception Systems
Florian Geißler, Alexander Unnervik, Michael Paulitsch · IEEE Open Journal of Intelligent Transportation Systems 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ojits.2020.3027146 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Detecting systematic perception faults in roadside-infrastructure-augmented perception.
**Approach:** Collaborative, sensor-generic plausibility checks (miss ratios, unexpected observation ratios, collaborative existence probability) integrated in object fusion.
**Tools and scale:** MATLAB simulation of a 600 m highway with 12 radars and a city intersection, based on KoRA9 and P++ deployments.
**Results:** Statistical fingerprints allow conclusive fault detection for the injected faults.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Faults derived from experience
- generality across modalities claimed but tested on two scenarios.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — perception faults, not V2X security.
**Novelty implications:** Adjacent.
