# EAMI: Efficient Privacy Preserving Authentication and Machine Learning Based Intrusion Detection in VANETs
Lasya Uma Sri Lingala, Venkata Purnima Karri, Nishanth Reddy Jangam et al. · International Conference on Cloud Computing and Intelligence Systems 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ccis63231.2024.10931969 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) offer an effective communication platform between the vehicles and Road Side Units (RSUs), providing the backbone of intelligent transportation systems.
**Approach:** This paper explores anonymous authentication with the support of Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC) and bilinear pairing to allow only the authenticated entities to communicate in VANETs. Moreover, Support Vector Machine (SVM) based intrusion detection system is introduced in this paper to avoid unauthorized intrusion of malicious users.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Through experimental analysis, the effectiveness of SVM based intrusion detection system is demonstrated in this paper to identify various types of intrusion, providing a robust solution to secure VANETs.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "However, ensuring secure communication in VANET is a major challenge due to the Open nature of wireless communication."
