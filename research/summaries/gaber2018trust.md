# Trust-based secure clustering in WSN-based intelligent transportation systems
Tarek Gaber, Sarah Abdelwahab, Mohamed Elhoseny, Aboul Ella Hassanien · Computer Networks 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.comnet.2018.09.015 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Increasing the number of vehicles on roads leads to congestion and safety problems. Wireless Sensor Network (WSN) is a promising technology providing Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) to address these problems.
**Approach:** In this paper, we introduce a bio-inspired and trust-based cluster head selection approach for WSN adopted in ITS applications.
**Tools and scale:** The simulation results showed that our proposed model is energy efficient (i.e., its power consumption is more efficient than many well-known clustering algorithm such as LEACH, SEP, and DEEC under homogeneous and heterogeneous networks).
**Results:** The simulation results showed that our proposed model is energy efficient (i.e., its power consumption is more efficient than many well-known clustering algorithm such as LEACH, SEP, and DEEC under homogeneous and heterogeneous networks). In addition, the results demonstrated that our proposed model achieved longer network lifetime, i.e., nodes are kept alive longer than what LEACH, SEP and DEEC can achieve.
