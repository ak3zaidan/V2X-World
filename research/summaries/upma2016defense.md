# Defense against Prankster Attack in VANET Using Genetic Algorithm
Gaba Upma, Tanisha Saini · Indian Journal of Science and Technology 2016 · https://doi.org/10.17485/ijst/2016/v9i35/98094 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Objectives: Detection and prevention of prankster attack in the network.
**Approach:** One of the popular attacks in VANET is prankster attack that is affecting at high rate in the performance of the system. Method: In this paper, an algorithm based on genetic optimization has been presented for prevention as well detection of the attack.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: MATLAB
**Results:** Findings: From the result evaluation it has been seen that proposed algorithm is better in terms of throughput, error rate, and delay and energy consumption. Improvement: Performance of the network get enhanced highlyKeywords: Genetic Algorithm, Optimization, Prankster Attack, Security, VANET
