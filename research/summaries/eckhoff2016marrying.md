# Marrying safety with privacy: A holistic solution for location privacy in VANETs
David Eckhoff, Christoph Sommer · 2016 IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference (VNC) 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc.2016.7835971 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Existing privacy measures often conflict with the requirements of future vehicular networks.
**Approach:** First and foremost, in attempting to achieve local privacy, they interfere with the primary purpose of vehicular networks: to improve road safety. Other solutions undermine accountability or introduce too high overhead to be of practical use.
