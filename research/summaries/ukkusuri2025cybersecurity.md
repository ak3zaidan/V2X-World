# Cybersecurity for Next-Generation Road Transportation: A Review
S. Ukkusuri, O. F. Hamim, Zeng-Xiang Lei, Eunhan Ka et al. · ACM Journal on Autonomous Transportation Systems 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3744352 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cybersecurity for AV, CV, EV.
**Approach:** Survey of 227 studies (2021-2025); S2I/I2D/D2A lifecycle and 11 attack paths.
**Tools and scale:** Survey.
**Results:** Stream and data processing stages most exploited; limited standardisation of evaluation metrics; weak emphasis on real-world operational consequences.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Limited standardisation in evaluation metrics
- inadequate emphasis on real-world operational consequences.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial: identifies lack of operational-consequence evaluation, which the engine's closed-loop safety measurement targets.
**Novelty implications:** Supports novelty of measuring safety consequences; does not do it itself.
