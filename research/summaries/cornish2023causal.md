# Causal Falsification of Digital Twins
Rob Cornish, Muhammad Faaiz Taufiq, Arnaud Doucet, Chris Holmes · arXiv 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.07210 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** How to assess whether a digital twin is correct for interventions.
**Approach:** Causal formulation; statistical procedure to falsify twins from observational trajectories.
**Tools and scale:** Theory plus application (truncated).
**Results:** Sound even with confounded data under i.i.d. assumption.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Observational data cannot certify a twin without strong assumptions.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — relevant to validating a V2X surrogate (direction 3).
**Novelty implications:** Our exact counterfactual reruns could provide interventional ground truth that this paper notes is missing.
