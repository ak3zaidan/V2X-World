# EBCPA: Efficient Blockchain-Based Conditional Privacy-Preserving Authentication for VANETs
Chao Lin, Xin-Yi Huang, De-Biao He · IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tdsc.2022.3164740 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs) are with great potentials to facilitate traffic management and improve driver safety.
**Approach:** Blockchain-based conditional privacy-preserving authentication (BCPPA) is proposed to achieve an optimal tradeoff among anonymity, traceability and key/certificate management in VANETs. In this article, we design three new system building blocks namely key derivation (KeyDer), signatures of knowledge (SoK) and smart contract, following by a more efficient BCPPA protocol (named as EBCPA).
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: NS-2, Ethereum, Hyperledger.
**Results:** As a result, current solutions fail to meet high mobility, low latency, and real-time requirements of VANETs. Finally, we evaluate its communication overhead and computational cost via comparing to existing BCPPA protocols that strive to achieve similar properties.
