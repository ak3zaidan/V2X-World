# HYBRIST Mobility Model- A Novel Hybrid Mobility Model for VANET Simulations
Wiseborn Manfe Danquah, Turgay D Altilar · IJCA 86(14):15-21, January 2014 2014 · https://doi.org/10.5120/15053-3408 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Urban or highway mobility models do not represent hybrid environments.
**Approach:** Hybrist hybrid mobility model using OpenStreetMap data, exportable to Veins, ns2, QualNet.
**Tools and scale:** OSM; VANET simulators.
**Results:** Hybrid model differs from urban and highway models on performance metrics.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Implement more efficient routing protocols and evaluate AODV, DSDV, DSR in the hybrid model.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
