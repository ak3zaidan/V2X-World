# From Flows to Words: Can Zero-/Few-Shot LLMs Detect Network Intrusions? A Grammar-Constrained, Calibrated Evaluation on UNSW-NB15
Mohammad Abdul Rehman, Syed Imad Ali Shah, Abbas Anwar, Noor Islam · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.17883 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Whether prompt-only LLMs detect intrusions.
**Approach:** Flows to text with boolean flags, grammar-constrained output, calibrated threshold.
**Tools and scale:** UNSW-NB15; balanced subset of 200 flows.
**Results:** Unguided prompting unreliable; 7B model with flags reaches macro-F1 near 0.78.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Small evaluation subset.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
