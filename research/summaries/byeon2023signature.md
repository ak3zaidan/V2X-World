# Signature Generated Scheme for Trusted Data Forwarding Scheme Over Internet of Vehicles
Haewon Byeon, K Raghavendra Prasad, Mehul Manu, Shweta Singh et al. · 2023 International Conference on New Frontiers in Communication, Automation, Management and Security (ICCAMS) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccams60113.2023.10525794 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** However, due to the high-speed movement of vehicle nodes, the network topology changes rapidly, leading to poor network connectivity.
**Approach:** In this context, a vehicle networking data forwarding scheme is proposed that ensures anonymity and trust evaluation.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** The traffic management center (TMC) evaluates the reliability of the received message and forwards it to the corresponding vehicle node to improve the timeliness of forwarding. The use of certificateless signature technology reduces computing overhead, and the TMC classification and evaluation of messages ensure reliability and integrity.
