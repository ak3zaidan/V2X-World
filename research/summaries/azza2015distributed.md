# DISTRIBUTED CERTIFICATE AUTHORITY IN CLUSTER-BASED MANET USING MULTI SECRET SHARING SCHEME
Mohammed Azza, Sofiane Hacene · Advances in Science and Technology Research Journal 2015 · https://doi.org/10.12913/22998624/60776 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Providing secure communications in mobile ad hoc networks (MANET) is an important and difficult problem, due to a lack of a key management infrastructure.
**Approach:** To provide a node authentication service we use a fully distributed certificate authorities (FDCA) based on the threshold cryptography. In this paper we propose an efficient and verifiable multi secret sharing scheme in cluster-based MANET with a low computation system.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** Our scheme is based on the overdetermined linear system equation in Galois fields GF(2 r ). The efficiency of our proposed schemes was verified and evaluated by simula tion.
