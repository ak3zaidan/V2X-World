# V2X Communication Reliability Improvement through QoS Prediction and Reservation Using Network Virtualization Technology
Tensei Kunimoto, Kenya Sato · Communications and Network 2026 · https://doi.org/10.4236/cn.2026.183004 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** V2X reliability under congestion.
**Approach:** QoS prediction and reservation via network virtualisation controller.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3 5G-LENA; 20-70 vehicles.
**Results:** Keeps admitted-vehicle latency below 100 ms (about 89-92 ms at saturation).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Architectural overhead analysed; safety margin may need expansion.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
