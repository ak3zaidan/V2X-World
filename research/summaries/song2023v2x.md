# V2X-Boosted Federated Learning for Cooperative Intelligent Transportation Systems with Contextual Client Selection
Rui Song, Lingjuan Lyu, Wei Jiang, Andreas Festag et al. · arXiv 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11654 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle connection quality degrades federated learning.
**Approach:** Client selection using V2X messages to predict latency and topology, plus data-distribution clustering.
**Tools and scale:** Various datasets.
**Results:** Outperforms baselines, especially non-iid.
