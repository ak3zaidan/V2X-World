# A Survey on Post-Quantum Public-Key Signature Schemes for Secure Vehicular Communications
Kyung-Ah Shim · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2021.3131668 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Quantum computers threaten ECDSA in vehicular communications.
**Approach:** Survey of PQ signature families (multivariate, hash, lattice, code, isogeny) with guidelines for vehicular requirements.
**Tools and scale:** Survey.
**Results:** Guidelines (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Prioritising which fields to migrate is necessary as simultaneous replacement is hard.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - background for direction 2 PQ migration.
**Novelty implications:** Survey-level PQ guidance exists; quantified safety cost of PQ SCMS in closed loop remains open.
