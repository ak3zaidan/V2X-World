# ZAMA: A ZKP-Based Anonymous Mutual Authentication Scheme for the IoV
Ning Xi, Weihui Li, Lv Jing et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2022.3186921 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Internet of Vehicles (IoV) is a typical application of mobile Internet of Things (IoT), which aims to improve road conditions and enhance the driving experience.
**Approach:** To enhance the user’s privacy and the service’s efficiency, we propose a novel efficient anonymous authentication approach for the IoV based on the zero-knowledge proof (ZKP) and elliptic curve cryptography (ECC). Meanwhile, we design a fast reconnection procedure based on the security context from the last access, which can reduce the computation overhead effectively.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** We achieve the user’s strong anonymity and authenticity based on the Fujisaki–Okamoto Commitment algorithm. The experimental results indicate that our scheme has a better performance compared with the classic protocols in the IoV.
