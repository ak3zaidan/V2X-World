# Trust Management Strategy for Digital Twins in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Bohan Li, Xinyang Song, Tianlun Dai, Wenlong Wu, Di Zhu, Xiangping Zhai et al. · IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jsac.2023.3310070 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Malicious vehicles interfere with Digital Twin construction of VANETs; tracking attacks threaten privacy.
**Approach:** Decentralized blockchain trust management with blockchain-recorded certificates used as pseudonyms; direct trust from authenticity verification and active detection, recommendation trust filtered by RTF; malicious vehicles' certificates revoked.
**Tools and scale:** Experiments (details not in abstract).
**Results:** Effectively resists malicious attacks in DT-VANETs.

- None stated in abstract.
