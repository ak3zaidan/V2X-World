# AI-Empowered Lightweight In-Vehicle Network Security Mechanisms: From Cryptographic Algorithms to Collaborative Defense Architectures
You Zhou, Ji-Gui Zhang, Kani Ding, Guo-Zhi Yang · SAE technical paper series 2025 · https://doi.org/10.4271/2025-99-0132 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the rapid development of Internet of Vehicles (IoV) and cyber-physical systems (CPS), connected autonomous vehicles (CAVs) have also developed rapidly. However, at the same time, in-vehicle networks also face more security challenges, mainly in terms of resource constraints, dynamic attacks, protocol heterogeneity, and high real-time requirements.
**Approach:** Finally, we propose a dynamic adaptive collaborative defense framework (DACDF), which integrates federated learning with dynamic weight distillation, blockchain authentication with lightweight verifiable delay function (Light-VDF) and cross-domain IDS with hierarchical attention feature fusion to deal with collaborative attacks in resource-constrained environments.
