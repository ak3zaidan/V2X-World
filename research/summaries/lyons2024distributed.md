# Distributed Attack-Resilient Platooning Against False Data Injection
Lorenzo Lyons, Manuel Boldrer, Laura Ferranti · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2025.3614452 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** CACC safety under undetected FDI.
**Approach:** Distributed CACC with bounded communication authority, Kalman-based detector, coordinator isolating compromised vehicle.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation vs distributed MPC and robust control.
**Results:** No rear-end collisions guaranteed; comparison in extended study.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Undetected attacks can still degrade performance.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Attack-to-safety and isolation reaction.
**Novelty implications:** None at PKI level.
