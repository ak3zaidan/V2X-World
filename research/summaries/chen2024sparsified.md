# Sparsified federated learning with differential privacy for intrusion detection in VANETs based on Fisher Information Matrix
Rui Chen, Xiaoyu Chen, Jing Zhao · PLoS ONE 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0301897 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the continuous development of vehicular ad hoc networks (VANET) security, using federated learning (FL) to deploy intrusion detection models in VANET has attracted considerable attention. Compared to conventional centralized learning, FL retains local training private data, thus protecting privacy.
**Approach:** To address this challenge, we propose FFIDS, a FL algorithm integrating model parameter pruning with differential privacy.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset, federated learning (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** Extensive experiments on two public datasets and two F2MD simulation datasets have validated the utility and superior performance of the FFIDS algorithm.
