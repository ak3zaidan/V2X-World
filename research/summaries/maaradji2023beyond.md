**Problem:** This article presents an ongoing research study investigating the potential of using Grand Theft Auto V (GTAV) as a simulation environment for testing edge cases in self-driving vehicles.
**Approach:** Our hypothesis is that GTAV can offer a more accessible and cost-effective solution for testing edge cases compared to established simulation environments such as CARLA. In this work-in-progress study, we aim to assess the feasibility, scalability, and effectiveness of utilizing GTAV by simulating complex driving scenarios.
