# Adaptive Ship Rescue Optimization Enabled Deep Learning for Sybil Attack Detection with Secure Transmission in Urban VANET
Nitha C Velayudhan, Arun Pradeep, Mukesh Madanan · International Journal of Electronics and Communication Engineering 2025 · https://doi.org/10.14445/23488549/ijece-v12i8p102 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil detection in urban VANETs.
**Approach:** Deep Kronecker Network tuned by Adaptive Ship Rescue Optimization at the base station.
**Tools and scale:** VANET simulation.
**Results:** Precision 90.84%, recall 90.48%, F 90.13%.
