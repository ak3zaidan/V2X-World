# Blockchain-based Multi-Levels Trust Mechanism Against Sybil Attacks for Vehicular Networks
Achref Haddaji, Samiha Ayed, Lamia Chaari Fourati · International Conference on Big Data Science and Engineering 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/bigdatase50710.2020.00028 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil attack detection.
**Approach:** BMLT-SA: local ML at vehicles (horizontal), RSU verification into trust lists (vertical), RSUs share lists via blockchain.
**Tools and scale:** Simulations and experiments.
**Results:** Effective detection (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — multi-level reporting to infrastructure, blockchain not SCMS.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
