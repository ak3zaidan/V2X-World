# LLM4WM: Adapting LLM for Wireless Multi-Tasking
Xuanyu Liu, Shijian Gao, Boxun Liu, Xiang Cheng et al. · IEEE Transactions on Machine Learning in Communications and Networking 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tmlcn.2025.3585845 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The wireless channel is fundamental to communication, encompassing numerous tasks collectively referred to as channel-associated tasks. These tasks can leverage joint learning based on channel characteristics to share representations and enhance system design.
**Approach:** To capitalize on this advantage, LLM4WM is proposed—a large language model (LLM) multi-task fine-tuning framework specifically tailored for channel-associated tasks.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: LLM. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Experiments on a channel-associated multi-task dataset demonstrate that LLM4WM outperforms existing methodologies in both full-sample and few-shot evaluations, owing to its robust multi-task joint modeling and transfer learning capabilities.
