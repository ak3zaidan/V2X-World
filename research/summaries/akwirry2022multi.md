# A Multi-Tier Trust-Based Security Mechanism for Vehicular Ad-Hoc Network Communications
Brian Akwirry, N. Bessis, Hassan Malik, Sarah Mchale · Italian National Conference on Sensors 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s22218285 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Malicious vehicles modify or drop VANET messages.
**Approach:** Three-tier trust system: watchdog-based trust values (delay, loss, history), watchdog protection, integrity of trust data; RSU-executed.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation with stationary/low-speed vehicles.
**Results:** Successful identification of malicious vehicles (abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- RSU-based federated model fails where RSUs are sparse
- suggest cloud-based integration
- evaluated only with stationary or low-speed vehicles
- future work at high speeds
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Trust-based misbehaviour detection, but not linked to SCMS revocation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open closed-loop detection-to-revocation at realistic speeds.
