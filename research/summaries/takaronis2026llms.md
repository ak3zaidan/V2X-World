# Using LLMs to Elicit Security Requirements for Service-Oriented Cyber Ranges
Michail Takaronis, Athanasia Kollarou, Georgios Kavallieratos, Vasileios Gkioulos et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00886 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cyber ranges are complex environments comprising many interacting components and stakeholders with different security concerns. The Service-Oriented Cyber Range (SOR) is no exception, particularly when it comes to training scenarios targeting critical infrastructure.
**Approach:** This work examines how large language models can assist in eliciting security requirements for a service-oriented range and help produce a useful baseline for designers and developers.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Testability was lower at 44.4%, indicating a slight lack of information on how these requirements could be tested. These findings show that LLMs can support early stages of the elicitation of security requirements, although human review is still needed, especially to improve or adjust certain aspects of the requirements.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Testability was lower at 44.4%, indicating a slight lack of information on how these requirements could be tested.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — LLM agents for network/security operations; our engine has an LLM agent that configures and analyses runs, but this work is not V2X and not tied to a deterministic simulator with ground truth.
**Novelty implications:** LLM agents for SOC/NetOps/RCA are established; cross-layer V2X root-cause analysis against simulator ground truth is not covered here.
