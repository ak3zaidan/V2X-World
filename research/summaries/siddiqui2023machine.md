# Towards a Machine Learning Driven Trust Management Heuristic for the Internet of Vehicles
Sarah Ali Siddiqui, Adnan Mahmood, Quan Z. Sheng, Hajime Suzuki et al. · Sensors 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s23042325 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The rapid proliferation of the emerging yet promising notion of the Internet-of-Vehicles (IoV) has led to the development of a variety of conventional trust assessment schemes to tackle insider attackers. The primary reliance of these frameworks is on the accumulation of individual trust attributes.
**Approach:** While aggregating these influential parameters, weights are often associated with each individual attribute to reflect its impact on the final trust score. It is of paramount importance that such weights be precise to lead to an accurate trust assessment.
