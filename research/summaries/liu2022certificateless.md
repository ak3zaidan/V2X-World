# A Certificateless Anonymous Cross-Domain Authentication Scheme Assisted by Blockchain for Internet of Vehicles
Xue-Yan Liu, Li Wang, Linpeng Li, Xiao-Yun Zhang et al. · Wireless Communications and Mobile Computing 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2022/3488977 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Certificate management overhead, authentication load and privacy in cross-domain IoV authentication.
**Approach:** Certificateless anonymous cross-domain authentication; first RSU authenticates and records on blockchain so other RSUs skip re-authentication; TA-issued pseudonyms for conditional privacy.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical security/performance analysis; no named simulator in abstract.
**Results:** Claims reduced repeated authentication load (no figures in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none: certificateless/blockchain alternative to PKI; engine uses IEEE 1609.2/SCMS.
**Novelty implications:** Cross-domain authentication via blockchain already proposed; multi-provider SCMS interoperability with standard certificates remains distinct.
