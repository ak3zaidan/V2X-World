# Efficient Post-Quantum ID-Based Signature Schemes for IoT Smart Devices
Kyung-Ah Shim · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2025.3646675 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Security protocols usually rely on public-key cryptographic algorithms, particularly, public-key signatures (PKSs; digital signatures) to achieve integrity, authentication, and nonrepudiation.
**Approach:** In this article, we propose practical post-quantum ID-based signature schemes based on post-quantum signature (PQS) schemes, suitable for resource-constrained IoT devices. We evaluate their practical feasibility on Skylake/Haswell as a server-side platform and Cortex-M4 as an IoT device.
