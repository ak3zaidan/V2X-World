# Conditional Anonymous Authentication With Abuse-Resistant Tracing and Distributed Trust for Internet of Vehicles
Jiangtao Li, Yufeng Li, Chenhong Cao, Kwok-Yan Lam · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2021.3116422 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Internet of Vehicles (IoV) was proposed as an approach to enable intelligent traffic management and enhance road safety. In order to achieve the intended objective of improving road safety, vehicles are required to constantly broadcast messages to the traffic management infrastructure as well as to other vehicles in the vicinity.
**Approach:** To address this challenge, this article proposes a privacy-preserving authentication scheme with abuse-resistant tracing.
