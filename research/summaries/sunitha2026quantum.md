# Quantum-Resistant Certificateless Lattice-Based Signature Scheme for IoT Security
G. Sunitha, B. A, K. J. Abinava Jashwanth, Kothapati Jasmitha et al. · International Conference Trends Material Science and Inventive Materials 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ictmim68190.2026.11507905 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The rapid proliferation of IoT technology has brought billions of devices into play that need to operate in a variety of diverse, resource-constrained environments. Secure authentication among these devices is a critical challenge, as traditional certificate-based public key infrastructures incur heavy computation and management overhead.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a post-quantum certificateless authentication framework designed to address the efficiency-long-term security requirements of IoT environments. The proposed framework uses lattice-based cryptography to resist quantum-enabled adversaries while eliminating the need for digital certificates.
**Tools and scale:** Non-vehicular setting. Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** The performance evaluation shows that our proposed framework reduces the communication and computational overheads compared with the traditional certificatebased authentication schemes.
