# Local Voting Games for Misbehavior Detection in VANETs in Presence of Uncertainty
Ali Behfarnia, Ali Eslami · 2019 57th Annual Allerton Conference on Communication, Control, and Computing (Allerton) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/allerton.2019.8919734 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** Cooperative malicious-node identification under uncertainty.
**Approach:** Bayesian local voting game with incentives.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Shows how uncertainty and incentives change strategies and identification.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- One stage of the game only.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — Game theory, not simulated stack.
**Novelty implications:** None.
