# LiDaSim: A Lightweight Dataset-Based Simulation Framework for Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Edmir Xhoxhi, Vincent Albert Wolff, Alexey Orychshenko · IEEE Vehicular Technology Conference 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2023-fall60731.2023.10333499 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Release 2 services testing needs highly realistic movement.
**Approach:** LiDaSim dataset-based simulation framework with real recordings; integrates CAM, VAM and Release 2 services.
**Tools and scale:** Real-world recordings; compared with an established simulator.
**Results:** Significant differences in CAM/VAM generation frequencies and triggers versus established simulator, shifting channel requirements.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — trace-driven V2X message generation; shows mobility realism affects message load.
**Novelty implications:** Our cross-validation against SUMO-like mobility should consider message generation statistics.
