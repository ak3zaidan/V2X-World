# Smart Infrastructure-Based Anomaly Detection Under Cyberattacks
Cong Zhang, Jun Ying, Xiao-Bo Qu, Yi-Heng Feng · IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Magazine 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mits.2025.3642054 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Connected and autonomous vehicles (CAVs) are increasingly exposed to cyberphysical attacks, yet most existing detection methods overlook the potential of infrastructure-based monitoring.
**Approach:** In this article, we propose a novel infrastructure-based anomaly detection framework to identify cyberattacks on CAVs under time interference attacks and vehicle-to-everything communication attacks. The optimal attack strategies are generated using a Pareto optimization that jointly maximizes safety risk and stealthiness.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The results show that the proposed anomaly detection model achieves high accuracy with low false positive and false negative rates in both offline and online settings. These results demonstrate that smart infrastructure can significantly improve the safety of CAVs by enabling timely and accurate detection of cyberattacks.
