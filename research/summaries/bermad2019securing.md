# Securing Vehicular Platooning against Vehicle Platooning Disruption (VPD) Attacks
Nabila Bermad, Salah Zemmoudj, Mawloud Omar · 2019 8th International Conference on Performance Evaluation and Modeling in Wired and Wireless Networks (PEMWN) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.23919/pemwn47208.2019.8986956 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The traffic problems related to bottlenecks, accidents and delays on the road intersections create a significant need for the application of platoon mechanism.
**Approach:** In this context, we consider a scenario when a platoon of negotiating vehicles approaches the intersection. The platoon leader should negotiate the passage of its following vehicles from the traffic light to enable them to reach their destinations as soon as possible.
