# Privacy-enhancing Interleaved Pseudonym Distribution for Vehicular Communication Systems
Ke Huang, Hong-Yu Jin, P. Papadimitratos · 2025 IEEE 102nd Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2025-Fall) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2025-fall65116.2025.11310074 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With multiple PCAs, the issuing PCA identity in pseudonyms enables linking.
**Approach:** Interleaved Pseudonym Distribution (IPD) and Random IPD with multi-PCA collaboration; a Kalman-filter PCAid-based linking attack with road information to evaluate. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** IPD moderately improves pseudonym confusion; RIPD significantly disrupts PCAid-based linking.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in available abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — cross-layer/issuer linkability in a multi-PCA VPKI, which is both our runner-up direction (cross-layer linkability) and multi-provider SCMS setting.
**Novelty implications:** Issuer-based linking with multiple PCAs is already shown and mitigated; open: linkability across many signals (radio, issuer, timing) jointly with ground truth in an SCMS with multiple providers.
