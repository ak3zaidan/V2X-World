# Managing and Complementing Public Key Infrastructure for Securing Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Albert Wasef · UWSpace (University of Waterloo) 2011 · https://openalex.org/W2142853715 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Managing and complementing PKI for VANETs.
**Approach:** PhD thesis: DCS certificate management, EDR decentralised threshold revocation to shrink the revocation vulnerability window, and other schemes.
**Tools and scale:** Waterloo thesis; analysis and simulation.
**Results:** Reduced certificate-management complexity; faster revocation of nearby misbehaving vehicles.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the full text.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — explicitly targets the revocation vulnerability window (time-to-neutralise, direction 1).
**Novelty implications:** Vulnerability window reduction via local revocation is prior work; quantifying it under SCMS linkage and CRL cadence with safety outcome remains open.
