# An AI-Enabled Decision Support System for Predictive QoS in Vehicular Networks
Orestis Kritsotakis, Eirini Liotou · 2026 7th International Conference in Electronic Engineering &amp; Information Technology (EEITE) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/eeite69609.2026.11679768 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Proactive QoS management in 5G vehicular networks.
**Approach:** DSS combining Vienna 5G system-level simulation, multi-criteria KPIs and tree-based ML.
**Tools and scale:** Vienna 5G SLS; Manhattan grid.
**Results:** Over 90% QoS-class accuracy with GB and RF.
