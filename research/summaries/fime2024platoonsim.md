# PlatoonSim: Autonomous Truck Platooning Simulation Framework
Awal Ahmed Fime, Nafiul Alam, Hadj Ahmed Chikh Dahmane, Shaymaa Mahmoud, M. I. R. Shuvo, Xiangxu Lin et al. · 2024 IEEE 8th International Conference on Information and Communication Technology (CICT) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cict64037.2024.10899575 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The emergence of autonomous technologies has presented the concept of autonomous truck platooning (ATP), which provides a substantial chance to improve fuel efficiency and road safety by allowing convoys of semi-autonomous trucks to drive in a coordinated fashion.
**Approach:** Nonetheless, regulatory limitations and insufficient data have impeded its implementation. To tackle these difficulties, we introduce PlatoonSim, a simulation framework intended to replicate real-world ATP operations and gather comprehensive data on system performance and driver behavior.
