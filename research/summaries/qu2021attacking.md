# Attacking Deep Reinforcement Learning-Based Traffic Signal Control Systems with Colluding Vehicles
Ao Qu, Yihong Tang, Wei Ma · arXiv 2021 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.02845 · relevance: medium · basis: full text

**Problem:** Colluding vehicles can cheat DRL-based adaptive signal control with falsified information to save their own travel time.
**Approach:** COLLUSION_VEH DRL framework with global embeddings and communication among colluders.
**Tools and scale:** Traffic simulation with DRL signal controllers.
**Results:** Colluding is more effective than greedy individual falsification; the effect decreases as colluder count rises.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- More diverse action spaces (volume, speed, position)
- test on other DRL ATCS policies
- real-life considerations.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — partial: colluding insiders attacking signal control.
**Novelty implications:** Nothing of our candidate directions is ruled out.
