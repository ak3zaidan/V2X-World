# Designing and Testing A Secure Cooperative Adaptive Cruise Control under False Data Injection Attack
Jonas Cunningham-Rush, James C. Holland, S. Noei, Arman Sargolzaei · International Conference on Data Science in Cyberspace 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/dsc61021.2023.10354170 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** CACC assumes secure channels and is vulnerable to false data injection.
**Approach:** Secure controller with real-time FDI detection and estimation.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation plus golf-cart vehicle-in-the-loop.
**Results:** Accurate tracking under FDI (no numbers in abstract).

- None stated.
