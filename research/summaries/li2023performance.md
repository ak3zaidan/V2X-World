# Performance Evaluation of Urban Intersection C-V2X Simulation System
Huipeng Li, Hanbin Xu, Yiying Wei, Jinlei Wang · 2023 5th International Conference on Robotics, Intelligent Control and Artificial Intelligence (RICAI) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ricai60863.2023.10489530 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** C-V2X system-level performance at urban intersections.
**Approach:** Platform with traffic simulation, network simulation and evaluation; LTE-V2X and NR-V2X sidelink broadcast.
**Tools and scale:** Own system-level platform.
**Results:** LTE-V2X degrades at high density; larger NR SCS improves performance.
