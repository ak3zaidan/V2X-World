# Machine Learning-Based Architecture for DDoS Detection in VANETs System
Naam Alkadiri, Muhammad Ilyas · 2022 International Conference on Artificial Intelligence of Things (ICAIoT) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icaiot57170.2022.10121900 · relevance: high · basis: abstract
