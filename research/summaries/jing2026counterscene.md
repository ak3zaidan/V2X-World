# CounterScene: Counterfactual Causal Reasoning in Generative World Models for Safety-Critical Closed-Loop Evaluation
Bowen Jing, Ruiyang Hao, Weitao Zhou, Haibao Yu · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21104 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Safety-critical scenario generation lacks causal structure.
**Approach:** CounterScene: causal adversarial agent identification and conflict-aware interactive world model with minimal counterfactual interventions.
**Tools and scale:** nuScenes; diffusion BEV world model.
**Results:** Long-horizon collision rate from 12.3% to 22.7% over strongest baseline; ADE reduced 24.2%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in extracted sections.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - counterfactual reasoning in closed-loop driving world models; traffic only.
**Novelty implications:** Counterfactual driving scenario generation exists; communication/PKI counterfactuals remain open.
