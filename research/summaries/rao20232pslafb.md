# 2PSLAF: Privacy-Preserving Secure lightweight authentication framework for Vehicle-to-Vehicle communications
P Muralidhara Rao · preprint 2023 · https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1298797/v3 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Internet of Vehicular Things (IoVT) intended to connect vehicles in urban and rural areas to provide communication among the vehicles.
**Approach:** This paper proposes an efficient privacy-preserving secure vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) communication. Firstly, we propose a privacy-protected identity-based mutual authentication.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The security analysis shows our scheme withstands several potential attacks, including unknown key share attacks, replay attacks, and key-compromised impersonation attacks. The empirical results show the efficiency of the proposed scheme is computationally taking less time.
