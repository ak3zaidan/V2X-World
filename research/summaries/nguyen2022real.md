# A Real-time Explainable Anomaly Detection System for Connected Vehicles
Duc Cuong Nguyen, Kien Nguyen, Simy Chacko · International Conference on Internet of Things, Big Data and Security 2022 · https://doi.org/10.5220/0010968500003194 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** : Anomaly detection is one of the key factors to identify and prevent attacks on connected vehicles.
**Approach:** It makes cars more secure and safer to use in the new era of connectivity. In this paper, we propose a real-time explainable deep learning-based anomaly detection system that effectively identiﬁes anomalous activities in connected vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** By evaluating our approach on a simulated driving environment, we can showcase its effectiveness (AUC value of 0.95) and provide insights on different attack scenarios that would threaten the safety of car users.
