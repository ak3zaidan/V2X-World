# Tetherless THz CV-QKD – Would It Work?
Xin Liu, Nan Wang, Chao Xu, S. X. Ng et al. · IEEE wireless communications 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mwc.2025.3631612 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Can THz CV-QKD work for wireless (SAGIN, V2X) channels?
**Approach:** Codeword-based reconciliation with IRCCs, secure boundaries for optical vs THz, MIMO and OTFS designs.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical/numerical.
**Results:** Reverse reconciliation has no transmissivity threshold, direct needs T > 0.5; cannot operate below 1.3 THz under one scheme; MIMO and OTFS extend secure regions.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Low-frequency CV-QKD requires stringent conditions.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — QKD.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
