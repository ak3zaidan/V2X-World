# MISO- V: Misbehavior Detection for Collective Perception Services in Vehicular Communications
Xiruo Liu, Lily L. Yang, Ignacio J. Alvarez, K. Sivanesan, Arvind Merwaday, Fabian Oboril et al. · 2021 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iv48863.2021.9575970 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Collective Perception Messages cannot be locally verified; malicious CPMs are dangerous.
**Approach:** MISO-V uses overlapping observations from multiple vehicles to verify semantic correctness of CPM data.
**Tools and scale:** CARLA-based simulation; ghost-car injection at a suburban T-junction.
**Results:** Very effective detecting ghost-car attacks and removing their impact on the receiver.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — CPM misbehaviour detection with in-loop effect.
**Novelty implications:** CPM ghost detection exists.
