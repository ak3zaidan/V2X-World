# Detecting Unknown Cyberattacks on Traffic Management Center in C-ITS
Seunghyun Jang, Jisu Kim, H. Shon, Chanmin Park, Myungkeun Yoon · IEEE Communications Magazine 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mcom.001.2500291 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cooperative Intelligent Transport Systems (C-ITS) improve road safety and traffic efficiency by enabling real-time data exchange among vehicles, infrastructure, and other road users.
**Approach:** Given the critical role of the Traffic Management Center (TMC) in orchestrating this ecosystem, its network infrastructure must be resilient to cyber threats. While prior studies have primarily focused on securing vehicles and roadside units, the detection of unknown cyberattacks targeting the TMC remains largely unexplored, and few studies have utilized real-world datasets for this purpose.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Experimental results on realworld TMC traffic data demonstrate that FIC effectively detects cyberattack attempts with high accuracy and minimal computational overhead.
