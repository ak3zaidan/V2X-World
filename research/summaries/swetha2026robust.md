# A Robust Multi-Layer Stacking Model for Malicious Node Classification in Vehicular Networks
Kulla Swetha, P. Shireesha, Chiluveru Dinesh et al. · American Journal of AI Cyber Computing Management 2026 · https://doi.org/10.64751/ajaccm.2026.v6.n2.pp422-432 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) play a vital role in modern Intelligent Transportation Systems by enabling real-time communication between vehicles and infrastructure to enhance road safety and traffic efficiency.
**Approach:** Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed STT model achieves exceptional accuracy, reaching 100.00% for denial-of-service attacks, 99.90% for sybil attack attempts, and 100.00% for blackhole attacks.
**Tools and scale:** With the rapid advancement of 5G networks and the rise of connected and autonomous vehicles, VANETs have evolved into highly dynamic and data-intensive environments.
**Results:** Additionally, advanced data balancing methods, including Adaptive Synthetic Sampling (ADASYN) was employed to address class imbalance and improve learning performance.
