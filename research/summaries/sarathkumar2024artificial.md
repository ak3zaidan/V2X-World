# Artificial Hummingbird Algorithm with Optimal Deep Learning-Based Intrusion Detection on Vehicular Adhoc Networks
K. Sarathkumar, P. Sudhakar, Clara Kanmani A · International Journal of Engineering Trends and Technology 2024 · https://doi.org/10.14445/22315381/ijett-v72i8p136 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANETs) permit transmission between the vehicle infrastructure, improving Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS).
**Approach:** In this article, an artificial hummingbird algorithm with optimal DL-based ID (AHAODL-ID) technique on VANET is developed. Lastly, the hyperparameter election of the BiLSTM approach involves the design of a Manta Ray Foraging Optimization (MRFO) model.
**Tools and scale:** The experimental results of the AHAODL-ID technique are assessed using a benchmark IDS dataset.
**Results:** Vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANETs) permit transmission between the vehicle infrastructure, improving Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS). DL methods, like recurrent NNs (RNNs) and convolutional NNs (CNNs), will efficiently analyze intricate and dynamic datasets for improved ID proficiencies.
