# LASER: Script Execution by Autonomous Agents for On-demand Traffic Simulation
Hao Gao, Jingyue Wang, Wenyang Fang, Jingwei Xu et al. · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.16197 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Generating diverse safety-critical driving scenarios.
**Approach:** LLM generates scripts from descriptions executed by autonomous agents.
**Tools and scale:** CARLA.
**Results:** Generates complex on-demand scenarios.
