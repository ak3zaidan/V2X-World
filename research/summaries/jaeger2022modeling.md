# Modeling TCP performance using graph neural networks
Benedikt Jaeger, Max Helm, Lars Schwegmann, Georg Carle · Proceedings of the 1st International Workshop on Graph Neural Networking 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3565473.3569190 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Analytical TCP models cannot handle many congestion algorithms; ML models are topology-specific.
**Approach:** Gated GNN predicting TCP bandwidth and RTT across algorithms and topologies.
**Tools and scale:** Simulated network traffic.
**Results:** Median relative error 2.29% for RTT, 13.31% for throughput.
