# On the Design of a Network Digital Twin for the Radio Access Network in 5G and Beyond
Irene Vilà, Oriol Sallent, Jordi Pérez-Romero · Sensors 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s23031197 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A Network Digital Twin (NDT) is a high-fidelity digital mirror of a real network. Given the increasing complexity of 5G and beyond networks, the use of an NDT becomes useful as a platform for testing configurations and algorithms prior to their application in the real network, as well as for predicting the performance of such algorithms under different conditions.
**Approach:** While an NDT can be defined for the different subsystems of the network, this paper proposes an NDT architecture focusing on the Radio Access Network (RAN), describing the components to represent and model the operation of the different RAN elements, and to perform emulations.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G, O-RAN, reinforcement learning, digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Presented results demonstrate that the implemented RAN NDT is a suitable platform to successfully train the RL solution, achieving service-level agreement satisfaction values above 85%.
