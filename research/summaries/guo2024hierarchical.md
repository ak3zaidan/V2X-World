# Hierarchical Secure Federated Learning Based on Secret Sharing for Emergency Situations in IoV
Chao Guo, Buxin Guo, Ling-Cui Zhang, Hai-Tao Xu et al. · 2024 IEEE Globecom Workshops (GC Wkshps) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/gcwkshp64532.2024.11100443 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Federated learning in IoV emergencies is vulnerable to inference by the aggregator.
**Approach:** Hierarchical FL with Shamir secret sharing and MPC between vehicles and RSUs. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** About 30% higher accuracy than a differential-privacy baseline.
