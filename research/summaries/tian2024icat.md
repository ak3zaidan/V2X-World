# ICAT: An Indoor Connected and Autonomous Testbed for Vehicle Computing
Zhaofeng Tian, William He, Boyang Tian, Ren Zhong, Erfan Foorginejad, Weisong Shi · arXiv 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/most60774.2024.00033 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Indoor testbeds lack sensing/computing for V2X and vehicle computing.
**Approach:** ICAT indoor testbed (6x5 m, 10 robots) with CARLA/SUMO digital twins.
**Tools and scale:** Robots, CARLA, SUMO.
**Results:** Demonstrated dual-purpose operation; small lags noticeably delay responses.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Optimise localisation accuracy
- localisation-error-aware MPC
- learned spatio-temporal dynamics model.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
