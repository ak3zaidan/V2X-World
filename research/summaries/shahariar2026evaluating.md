# Evaluating Granularity in Markov Chain-Based Trust Models for Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs)
Rezvi Shahariar · arXiv 2026 · https://doi.org/10.14738/tmlai.1401.20018 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Trust management is a critical research pillar in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs), where the reliability of shared data depends entirely on driver integrity.
**Approach:** In these networks, a driver's reputation is dynamically constructed based on the veracity of their recent message history: consistent reliability builds trust, while frequent misinformation leads to exclusion.
**Tools and scale:** By simulating announcement and reporting patterns, each model's ability to reflect nuanced behavioural shifts is assessed.
