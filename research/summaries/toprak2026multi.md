# A Multi-Scenario Machine Learning Framework for Predicting 5G Network Quality of Service
Amine Gonca Toprak, Ö. Mercan · 2026 International Conference on Artificial Intelligence, Computer, Data Sciences and Applications (ACDSA) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/acdsa67686.2026.11468167 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Fifth-generation (5G) mobile networks demand dynamic and predictive Quality of Service (QoS) management mechanisms to meet high data rate, low latency, and reliability requirements.
**Approach:** This study proposes an advanced machine learning-based modeling framework for estimating key QoS parameters in 5 G networks. The framework models four critical QoS indicators-allocated bandwidth, bandwidth difference, end-to-end latency, and resource allocation ratio-using a dataset of 399 samples collected from real network recordings.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: 5G.
**Results:** However, existing network monitoring systems are predominantly reactive, resulting in inefficiencies in resource allocation and latency optimization. The best-performing models achieved the following results: bandwidth allocation with XGBoost ($R^{2}=0.99$), bandwidth difference with Gradient Boosting ($\mathbf{R}^{2}=0.7913$), delay estimation with Random Forest ($\mathbf{R}^{2} \boldsymbol{=} \mathbf{0. 9 9}$), and resource allocation with Linear Regression ($\mathbf{R}^{2}=1.00$).
