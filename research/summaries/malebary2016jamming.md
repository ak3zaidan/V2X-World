# Jamming mobility in 802.11p networks: Modeling, evaluation, and detection
Sharaf Jameel Malebary, Wenyuan Xu, Chin‐Tser Huang · IEEE International Performance, Computing, and Communications Conference 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1109/pccc.2016.7820665 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The development of wireless Vehicular Ad-Hoc Network (VANET) aimed to enhance road's safety and provide comfortable driving environment by delivering early warning and infotainment messages. Intentional jamming attacks target at undermining such a goal by disrupting wireless communications.
**Approach:** To overcome these challenges, in this work, we study jamming attack mobility and behaviors in IEEE802.11p networks. In particular, we focus on analyzing jamming impact based on jammers behaviors, and mobility patterns.
**Tools and scale:** IEEE 802.11p/DSRC (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** Our approach shows promising results to detect different types of jammers accurately in IEEE802.11p networks.
