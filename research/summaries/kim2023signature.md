# Signature Split Method for a PQC-DSA Compliant with V2V Communication Standards
Youngbeom Kim, S. Seo · Applied Sciences 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/app13105874 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** PQC signatures exceed WAVE maximum packet size causing segmentation.
**Approach:** Two methods to apply PQC within 1609.2 at the application layer, incl. a split method for Dilithium; analysis of Dilithium and Falcon with BSM structure.
**Tools and scale:** V2Verifier simulation of on-board unit.
**Results:** Optimised PQC-DSA achieved a rate the authors state is recommended for autonomous driving ("5 m/s" as written).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — PQ signatures in 1609.2 BSMs with packet-size constraints (direction 2).
**Novelty implications:** Signature splitting for PQ 1609.2 done; open: channel/safety impact at scale.
