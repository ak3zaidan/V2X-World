# Secure Content Distribution in Vehicular Networks
Viet T. Nguyen, Jubin Jose, Xinzhou Wu, Tom Richardson · arXiv 2016 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.06181 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Pollution attacks on V2V CRL distribution with rateless coding.
**Approach:** Precode-and-Hash: fixed low-rate precode with signed hashes enabling verification before forwarding.
**Tools and scale:** Analysis on multi-hop line network.
**Results:** Prevents pollution attack; performance analysis in paper.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — V2V CRL distribution under attack overlaps our CRL broadcast/RSU relay; we do not model coded distribution.
**Novelty implications:** Same topic area; does not provide the closed-loop, SCMS-complete, cross-layer evaluation our engine targets, so our claims stand but this work must be cited as related.
