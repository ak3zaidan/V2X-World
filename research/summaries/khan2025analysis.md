# Analysis of Blackhole Attacks Using Machine Learning Adaptation in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANET)
Kashifa Khan, Devdas Saraswat · 2025 IEEE 5th International Conference on ICT in Business Industry & Government (ICTBIG) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ictbig68706.2025.11324007 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In a Vehicular Ad hoc Network (VANET), vehicles talk to each other to confirm or provide traffic status updates without a central authority. There are always vehicles moving on the road at varying speeds.
**Approach:** This work proposes ML-adaptive, secure VANET communication amidst numerous hostile nodes. The goal of this research is to protect against malicious assaults and to let new proposed models build atop strong, realistic models that are safe against hole attacks.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting (VANET/IoV/V2X). Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** The simulation findings show that RSU does not discard packets when there is an attacker, which is a sign of secure communication.
