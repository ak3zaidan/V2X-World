# Cyberattack Identification in Smart EV Charging Systems Using Remaining Useful Life-Guided Autoencoder - LSTM Networks
Rathika S, C. S., N. D. et al. · 2026 International Conference on Intelligent Computing, Networks, and Security (IC-ICNS) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ic-icns68863.2026.11537792 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Electric vehicle (EV) Charging infrastructure has increased and is incorporating smart grid technology.
**Approach:** The real-time integration of smart grid technology enables the rapid development of infrastructures for electric vehicle (EV) charging. These systems are often developed concurrently because of the need to have cybersecurity.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The proposed study has taken a radical approach of integrating an Autoencoder and LSTM hybrid system that will improve the Remaining Useful Life (RUL) to increase cybersecurity and threat resilience of electric vehicle charging infrastructure. The hybrid RUL-guided Autoencoder-LSTM has proven to be better in terms of detection accuracy and lowering false positives and resilience than other methodologies found in literature.
