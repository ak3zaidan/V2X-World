# Authentication and Message Verification Scheme for Internet of Vehicles Based on Elliptic Curve Cryptography
Jin-zhong Huang, Yong Kuang, Yujing Gong, Hongyun Xu · 2026 9th International Conference on Communication Engineering and Technology (ICCET) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccet70051.2026.11659013 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In Internet of Vehicles (IoV) communication, vehicles must frequently verify many messages for authenticity, integrity, and identity privacy. Existing schemes based on PKI, bilinear pairing, or certificateless signatures suffer from high overhead or complexity.
**Approach:** This paper proposes ECAVS, an ECC-based authentication and message verification scheme for IoV.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** It uses lightweight ECC signatures, periodically updated pseudonyms for conditional privacy, and batch verification to reduce overhead. ECAVS achieves faster signing and batch verification than SL-CPPA, ES-CLAS, and EA-SSTA, with comparable single-verification time and competitive communication overhead.
