# 3D Simulation Model for IoD-to-Vehicles Communication in IoD-assisted VANET
GAMIL ABDULLAH AHMED, Tarek Rahil Omar Sheltami, Ashraf S Mahmoud, Ansar Yasar · Frontiers in Built Environment 2023 · https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-2494639/v1 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle ad-hoc networks (VANETs) have gradually emerged to enhance transportation information, entertainment, safety, and other services.
**Approach:** However, such infrastructures have certain limitations, causing intermittent network disconnection. Further, in urban areas, terrain heights act as obstacles and hinder or attenuate transmitted signals.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Improved particle swarm optimization is used to optimally deploy IoD nodes in 3D terrain for minimizing the number of isolated vehicles. Simulation results show different behaviors of IoD nodes in two-dimensional (2D) and 3D scenarios.
