# Wireless V2X Communication Testbed for Connected, Cooperative and Automated Mobility
Anja Dakić, Benjamin Rainer, Peter Priller, Guo Nan, Anamarija Momić, Xiaochun Ye et al. · 2024 IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference (VNC) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc61989.2024.10575958 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** CCAM needs knowledge of spatial V2X reliability regions.
**Approach:** Testbed architecture with FER prediction via HOPE framework, HiL and non-stationary GSCM using OSM.
**Tools and scale:** City measurements, HiL, GSCM.
**Results:** Measured FER aligns with HiL/GSCM; 80% accuracy in predicting FER class.
