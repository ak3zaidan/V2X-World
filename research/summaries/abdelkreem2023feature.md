# Feature Engineering Impact on Position Falsification Attacks Detection in Vehicular Ad-Hoc Network
Eslam Abdelkreem, Sherief Hussein, Ashraf Tammam · International Journal of Information Security 2023 · https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-3280061/v1 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Impact of feature engineering on position falsification detection is under-studied.
**Approach:** Literature survey of features/algorithms plus six models (3 algorithms x 2 feature sets) on VeReMi; cross-simulation evaluation.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi plus another simulation.
**Results:** Comparable to prior work on two attack types, better on others; feature engineering raises average accuracy by 6.31-47% on another simulation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — shows dataset-shift sensitivity, relevant to benchmark direction 3.
**Novelty implications:** Cross-dataset generalisation gap documented.
