# A Collaboratively Hidden Location Privacy Scheme for VANETs
Ying Mei, Guozhou Jiang, Wei Zhang, Yongquan Cui · International Journal of Distributed Sensor Networks 2014 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/473151 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Communication messages in vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) can be used to locate and track vehicles, and this can lead to threats on location privacy of vehicle users.
**Approach:** In this paper, we address the problem of privacy and liability in VANETs. We propose a scheme that provides location privacy by utilizing a variant of ring signature.
