# A Decentralized Blockchain-Based Trust Management Framework for Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Tahani Gazdar, Ohoud Alboqomi, Asmaa Munshi · Smart Cities 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/smartcities5010020 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANETs) are one of the pillars of the Internet of Vehicles, they provide plenty of applications ranging from safety to entertainment. Safety applications largely depend on reliable and authentic traffic-related data.
**Approach:** This paper addresses these challenges by proposing a decentralized Blockchain-based trust management framework (BC-TMF) aiming to compute trust metrics for vehicles. To investigate the efficiency and consistency of the proposed framework, extensive simulations are conducted.
**Tools and scale:** blockchain (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** To investigate the efficiency and consistency of the proposed framework, extensive simulations are conducted. The obtained results show that the proposed BC-TMF has an excellent capability in computing accurate trust metrics for vehicles.
