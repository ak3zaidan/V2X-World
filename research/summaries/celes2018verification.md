# Verification Based Authentication Scheme for Bogus Attacks in VANETs for Secure Communication
A. Asline Celes, N. Edna Elizabeth · International Conference on Cryptography, Security and Privacy 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccsp.2018.8524540 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A Vehicular Ad Hoc Network (VANET) is a subclass of mobile ad hoc network (MANET), which includes smart vehicles and roadside units (RSU). It maintains vehicle to vehicle (V2V) and vehicle to infrastructure (V2I) communications.
**Approach:** In this paper bogus attack is mitigated by using Position Verification Technique in which the effects of falsified position information are analyzed primarily.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** The solution is evaluated based on simulation and the position verification system, that successfully prevent nodes from spreading false positions and thus the bogus attack can be mitigated.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Addresses V2X attacks or misbehaviour/trust evaluation that our engine can execute and detect in closed loop; it does not model the SCMS, revocation or joint traffic/radio/PKI ground truth.
**Novelty implications:** Does not rule out our candidate directions; any claim about this specific attack/detection/trust mechanism must cite it. Left open: end-to-end detection-report-revocation evaluation with safety outcomes.
