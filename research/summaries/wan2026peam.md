# PEAM: On-device Communication Agent for Personalized, Efficient and Autonomous Mobile Network Configuration
Haowen Wan, Shangzhuo Xie, Syue-Ju Syue et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14855 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The growing deployment of compact large language models on smartphones is enabling autonomous on-device decision-making, such as emerging mobile GUI agents.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose PEAM, an on-device LLM-based communication agent that autonomously generates personalized network-configuration rules executable within milliseconds. Specifically, PEAM first fine-tune the on-device LLM with a dataset constructed from manually annotated real-user data to integrate the communication knowledge into the local model.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** In the rule-generation phase, which is performed when the device is idle, such as overnight, PEAM analyzes historical network-configuration records in batches and reflects across batches to consolidate consistent evidence, reduce local bias, and derive robust configuration rules. Experiments demonstrate that PEAM, based on a fine-tuned 4B model, achieves 99.13% correct network configuration decisions, while 92.50% of the rules generated by PEAM are valid, outperforming the traditional expert-rule based method by 4.41% and 84.50%, respectively.
