# Strong and affordable location privacy in VANETs: Identity diffusion using time-slots and swapping
David Eckhoff, Christoph Sommer, Tobias Gansen, Reinhard German et al. · 2010 IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference 2010 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc.2010.5698239 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Public acceptance, and thus the economical success of Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs), is highly dependent on the quality of deployed privacy mechanisms.
**Approach:** Neither users nor operators should be able to track a given individual. One approach to facilitate this is the usage of pseudonym pools, which allow vehicles to autonomously switch between different identities.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: location privacy).
**Results:** We extend this scheme with that of a time-slotted pseudonym pool of static size, reducing the storage and computation needs of the envisioned Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) while further improving users' privacy.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Here, we support the exchange of both the currently used pseudonym and those of future time-slots, further enhancing users' privacy.
