# Evaluation of Jamming Attacks on NR-V2X Systems: Simulation and Experimental Perspectives
Antonio Santos da Silva, Kevin Herman Muraro Gularte, Giovanni Almeida Santos, Davi Salomão Soares Corrêa, Luís Felipe Oliveira de Melo, João Paulo Javidi da Costa et al. · Signals 2025 · https://doi.org/10.3390/signals7010001 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** Robustness of NR-V2X under jamming.
**Approach:** Simulation of DNPW and IMA with four jamming strategies plus lab RF experiment.
**Tools and scale:** OMNeT++, Simu5G, Veins, SUMO; AnaPico and Anritsu lab equipment.
**Results:** Periodic jamming degrades packet loss and signal quality in safety windows; effects persist after attack; lab confirms decoding loss.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Simulation and experiment differ in scenario structure and parameters (stated limitation).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — NR-V2X attack impact on safety apps; our NR sidelink runner-up.
**Novelty implications:** Jamming of NR-V2X with safety metrics exists; verification-load attacks open.
