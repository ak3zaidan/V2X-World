# Resilient Control of Switched Vehicle Platoons under False Data Injection Attacks
Ali Eslami, Jiangbo Yu · arXiv.org 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28794 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** FDI on V2V links of switched platoons.
**Approach:** Per-link attack observers and resilient controller; UUB and string-stability analysis.
**Tools and scale:** Numerical case studies.
**Results:** Effective mitigation in case studies.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Asynchronous switching, delays and packet loss, actuator saturation, heterogeneous parameters, stochastic noise, CBF collision guarantees, higher-fidelity and hardware validation.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
