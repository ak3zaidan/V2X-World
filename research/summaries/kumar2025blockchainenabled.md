# Blockchain‐Enabled Privacy‐Preserving Anomaly Detection and Reputation Framework for <scp>VANETs</scp>
Mungara Kiran Kumar, Yogini Dilip Borole, Shrabani Mallick et al. · Transactions on Emerging Telecommunications Technologies 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1002/ett.70290 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ABSTRACT To address issues in traditional vehicular network trust mechanisms such as the untrustworthiness of centralized reputation servers, threats to user privacy, and limited detection scope a blockchain‐based vehicular network anomaly detection and reputation model with privacy protection is proposed.
**Approach:** Leveraging blockchain technology, a distributed and trustworthy reputation update framework for vehicular networks is designed.
**Tools and scale:** According to the simulation results, which demonstrate accuracy with low false positive rates in identifying malicious cars, the suggested method effectively protects user privacy while attaining high anomaly detection rates.
**Results:** The evaluation data is encrypted and computed using a multi‐key fully homomorphic encryption technique, which reduces the danger of user privacy leakage. An adaptive adjustment approach is implemented for the retrospective time interval to improve anomaly detection.
