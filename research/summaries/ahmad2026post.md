# Post-Quantum Weighted Anonymous Authentication for Hybrid VANET MAC Protocol
Tanveer Ahmad, Muhammad Usman Hadi, Vasos Vassiliou, Nahida Nigar et al. · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2025.3582080 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** 802.11p MAC needs QoS enhancement and PQ security.
**Approach:** HVMAC hybrid MAC (contention + scheduled channels, Markov model) plus Post-Quantum Weighted Anonymous Authentication with weighted CA.
**Tools and scale:** Markov-chain analysis versus 802.11p.
**Results:** Energy savings up to 90% claimed.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — PQ authentication combined with MAC modelling.
**Novelty implications:** None.
