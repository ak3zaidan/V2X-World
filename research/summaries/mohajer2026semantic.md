# Semantic-Aware Edge Intelligence for Cooperative Perception in UAV–V2X Mobility
Amin Mohajer, Xavier Fernando · Secure and Intelligent V2X Systems for Autonomous Mobility 2026 · https://doi.org/10.4018/979-8-2600-4398-1.ch009 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This chapter presents a semantic-aware edge intelligence framework for cooperative perception in UAV–V2X mobility.
**Approach:** It addresses the limitations of continuous raw-data sharing among vehicles, UAVs, roadside units, MEC servers, and cloud platforms under bandwidth, latency, energy, synchronization, and computing constraints. The framework shifts cooperation from data-heavy exchange to task-oriented sharing of meaningful information, including occlusion alerts, uncertainty indicators, risk levels, motion intentions, and compact feature representations.
