# Intrusion Detection and Prevention for Connected Vehicles
Abinash Borah, Gideon Adele, Anirudh Paranjothi · Wiley Encyclopedia of Electrical and Electronics Engineering 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1002/047134608x.w8452 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract Vehicular Ad Hoc NETworks (VANETs) have contributed to the progress of connected vehicles by forming the core of intelligent transportation systems.
**Approach:** In this article, we introduce the architecture and characteristics of VANETs, discuss the challenges and requirements involved in intrusion detection and prevention, and explain different security attacks and types of attackers in VANETs.
