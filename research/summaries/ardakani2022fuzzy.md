# Fuzzy clustering algorithm to identify Sybil attacks in Vehicular ad hoc networks
Mehdi MalekNasab Ardakani, Mohammad Ali Tabarzad, Mohammad Amin Shayegan · Research Square 2022 · https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-2216291/v1 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Sybil attack detection in VANETs.
**Approach:** RSU selects cluster heads with fuzzy logic; cluster heads use directional antennas and a second fuzzy system to validate members and detect Sybil nodes locally.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation (preprint, Research Square); no PKI.
**Results:** Average improvement of 0.4 in FPR and 0.6 in lost-packet ratio versus baselines (as stated).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Learn optimal fuzzy rules per environment with intelligent methods.
- Evaluate with different scenarios and attacker models.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Sybil detection; physical-layer (antenna) cue.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on our novelty; cross-layer (radio+PKI) Sybil detection with ground truth remains open.
