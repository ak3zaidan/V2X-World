# A Macroscopic Traffic Model-based Approach for Sybil Attack Detection in VANETs
Marwane Ayaida, Nadhir Messai, Sameh Najeh, Kouame Boris N'Djore · Ad Hoc Networks 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adhoc.2019.01.010 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil attacks in VANETs.
**Approach:** Compare a vehicle's speed with speed estimated from neighbour density via the traffic flow fundamental diagram; analytical detection-rate model vs density. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** NS-3 + SUMO.
**Results:** Model validated by simulations.
