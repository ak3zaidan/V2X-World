# A Survey of Simulation Tools for Cooperative Positioning in Autonomous Vehicles
Ivo Silva, Hélder Silva, Fabricio Botelho, Cristiano Pendão · IEEE Access 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3508473 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) and autonomous driving require high positioning performance (high accuracy, reliability, and availability).
**Approach:** These requirements are not always possible due to disruptions and limitations on Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) signals. Cooperative positioning approaches aim to mitigate the drawbacks of GNSS by exploring the collaboration of road participants enhancing positioning performance.
