# A Game-Theoretic Approach and Evaluation of Adversarial Vehicular Platooning
Imran Sajjad, Rajnikant Sharma, Ryan Gerdes · Proceedings of the 1st International Workshop on Safe Control of Connected and Autonomous Vehicles 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3055378.3055383 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we consider an attack on a string of automated vehicles, or platoons, from a game-theoretic standpoint.
**Approach:** Game theory enables us to ask the question of optimality in an adversarial environment; what is the optimal strategy that an attacker can use to disrupt the operation of automated vehicles, considering that the defenders are also optimally trying to maintain normal operation. We formulate a zero-sum game and find optimal controllers for different game parameters.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: platoon/CACC attacks).
**Results:** It is shown that with the constraint of optimality, the attacker cannot significantly degrade the stability of a vehicle platoon in nominal cases.
