# AI-Driven Ensemble Classifier for Jamming Attack Detection in VANETs to Enhance Security in Smart Cities
Walid El‐Shafai, Ahmad Taher Azar, Saim Ahmed · IEEE Access 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2025.3552544 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Extensive simulations were conducted to rigorously test the model’s efficacy, which yielded encouraging results. This ensemble classifier, RF+ET+CNN, achieved the highest detection accuracy, outperforming the individual classifiers. Specifically, the CNN algorithm demonstrated an exceptional detection accuracy of 99.133%, while the RF and ET classifiers were the most accurate among the ML algorithms tested, with accuracy rates of 97.4359% and 97.4357%, respectively.
