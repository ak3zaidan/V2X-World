# Causal Digital Twin from Multi-channel IoT
PG Madhavan · arXiv 2021 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.02135 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Treating data from each sensor in an IoT installation on its own separately is wasteful.
**Approach:** This article shows how to treat them as a multi-channel time series and introduces the State-space model formulation of Structural Vector Autoregressive (SVAR) model and the use of time-varying Kalman Filter for optimal estimation of causal parameters.
