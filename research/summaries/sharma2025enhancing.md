# Enhancing Edge Intelligence in Wireless Communication Networks Using Large Models for Security and Adaptive Control
Anshika Sharma, S. Rani · Internet Technology Letters 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1002/itl2.70096 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Wireless communication networks (WCN) are becoming more complicated and dynamic, especially when it comes to edge computing.
**Approach:** As a result, intelligent, self‐governing systems that can manage security and control duties in real time are required. This paper presents a novel Edge Transformer for Security and Adaptive Control (EdgeFormer‐SAC), a Transformer‐based large model (LM) designed for edge environments that is compact and effective.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: simulated.
**Results:** The proposed EdgeFormer‐SAC model maintained a real‐time latency of 17.5 ms and low energy consumption at 1.3 W, while achieving the greatest accuracy and F1‐score of 94.8% and 93%, respectively, and a false positive rate (FPR) of only 2.3% and an adaptation score of 89%.
