# Reflection-Driven Self-Optimization 6G Agentic AI RAN via Simulation-in-the-Loop Workflows
Yunhao Hu, Xinchen Lyu, Chenshan Ren et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20640 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The escalating complexity of sixth-generation (6G) networks demands unprecedented levels of autonomy beyond the capabilities of traditional optimization-based and current AI-based resource management approaches.
**Approach:** We present the first reflection-driven self-optimization framework that integrates agentic AI with high-fidelity network simulation in a closed-loop architecture.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** While agentic AI has emerged as a promising paradigm for autonomous RAN, current frameworks provide sophisticated reasoning capabilities but lack mechanisms for empirical validation and self-improvement. Extensive experiments validate significant performance improvements over non-agentic approaches: 17.1\% higher throughput in interference optimization, 67\% improved user QoS satisfaction through intent recognition, and 25\% reduced resource utilization during low-traffic periods while maintaining service quality.
