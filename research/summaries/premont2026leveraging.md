# Leveraging Proof-of-Authority Blockchain for Decentralized Certificate Revocation
Arthur Premont, Hossam Afifi · 2026 IEEE 12th International Conference on Network Softwarization (NetSoft) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/netsoft70012.2026.11603527 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Managing certificate revocation remains a recurring challenge in public key infrastructure (PKI).
**Approach:** This paper presents a decentralized revocation architecture in which certificate authorities collectively manage a permissioned blockchain using a “Proof-of-Authority” consensus mechanism.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Based on Hyperledger Fabric, our system achieves deterministic finality within seconds, incurs no transaction fees, and distributes trust across multiple independent certificate authorities.
