# Improving Vehicular Authentication in VANET Using
Rasha Al-Mutiri, Mznah A. Al-Rodhaan, Yuan Tian · Int. J. Commun. Networks Inf. Secur. 2018 · https://doi.org/10.17762/ijcnis.v10i1.3124 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Security is the most difficult issue belonging to VANETs since messages are exchanged through open wireless environments.
**Approach:** In this paper, we applied security algorithms to improve authentication in VANETs with four stages of cryptography techniques: challenge-response authentication, digital signature, timestamping, and encryption/decryption respectively. Also, we also proposed an algorithm model and framework.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** The fundamental advantages of VANETs are enhancing the road and driver's safety and improving the vehicle security against adversaries’ attacks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
