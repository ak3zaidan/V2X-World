# TrafficGPT: Viewing, Processing and Interacting with Traffic Foundation Models
Siyao Zhang, Daocheng Fu, Zhao Zhang, Bin Yu et al. · arXiv.org 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.06719 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the promotion of chatgpt to the public, Large language models indeed showcase remarkable common sense, reasoning, and planning skills, frequently providing insightful guidance. These capabilities hold significant promise for their application in urban traffic management and control.
**Approach:** To bridge this gap, we present TrafficGPT, a fusion of ChatGPT and traffic foundation models.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: LLM, GPT. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** This integration yields the following key enhancements: 1) empowering ChatGPT with the capacity to view, analyze, process traffic data, and provide insightful decision support for urban transportation system management; 2) facilitating the intelligent deconstruction of broad and complex tasks and sequential utilization of traffic foundation models for their gradual completion; 3) aiding human decision-making in traffic control through natural language dialogues; and 4) enabling interactive feedback and solicitation of revised outcomes.
