# Secure Traffic Lights: Replay Attack Detection for Model-based Smart Traffic Controllers
Pratham Oza, Mahsa Foruhandeh, Ryan Gerdes, Thidapat Chantem · arXiv 2020 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.04244 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** Smart traffic controllers vulnerable to FDI/replay.
**Approach:** Replay attack on loop sensors at isolated intersection; threshold detector.
**Tools and scale:** VISSIM.
**Results:** Existing model-based detectors fail; replay maximises queue lengths.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: evasive attackers.
- broader traffic models.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Infrastructure attacks with traffic impact.
**Novelty implications:** None.
