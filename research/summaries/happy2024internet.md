# Internet of Vehicle (IoV) Cyber Attack Detection using Machine Learning Techniques
Happy, Asif Iqubal, Subodh Kant Tiwari · 2024 4th International Conference on Advancement in Electronics &amp;amp; Communication Engineering (AECE) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/aece62803.2024.10911517 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In the modern century, sophisticated interconnected technologies, also known as the Internet of Things (IoT), evolve quickly and become more adaptable and intelligent, influencing our existence, occupation, transportation, and interaction.
**Approach:** The capacities of IoT equipment render the traditional transportation framework a progressively more sophisticated transportation infrastructure, designated as the IoV (Internet of Vehicles). In IoV interactions, the controller area network (CAN) protocol manages vehicle sensors.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The proposed research's primary goal is to improve the broad cybersecurity concerns in IoVs using machine learning (ML) methodologies and ensure consumer safety on the street.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Due to a lack of communication confidentiality and authentication in the IoV CAN protocol, its communications are exposed to data vulnerability, fuzzy attacks, spoofing, denial of service attacks, and several other significant security issues.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
