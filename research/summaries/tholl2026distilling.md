# Distilling Knowledge from Large Language Models into Lightweight Reinforcement Learning Agents for Autonomous Cyber Operations
Konur Tholl, François Rivest, Mariam El Mezouar, Adrian Taylor et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28826 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** RL cyber-defence agents explore poorly.
**Approach:** LLM defensive policy distilled into a 64,910-parameter RL agent.
**Tools and scale:** Modified CybORG CAGE 2.
**Results:** 8B LLM beats baseline RL; distilled agent remains effective.
