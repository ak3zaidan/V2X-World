# SMART: A Holistic Simulation Framework for End-to-End V2X Communication
Athanasios Papadakis, Dimitrios Palianopoulos, G. Kalfas, M. Gatzianas, Sophia G. Petridou · SMART: A Holistic Simulation Framework for End-to-End V2X Communication 2024 · https://doi.org/10.52545/3-13 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Impact of vehicular traffic on end-to-end V2X delay for delay-sensitive applications.
**Approach:** SMART framework integrating simulators with CQI/SINR, speed and acceleration; single- and multi-vehicle scenarios with vehicle classes and traffic periods.
**Tools and scale:** Integrated simulators; real road infrastructure.
**Results:** Mean delays below 100 ms; heavy traffic has the largest impact on delay.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Need mechanisms to dynamically prioritise safety-critical traffic.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — traffic-to-network delay coupling.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
