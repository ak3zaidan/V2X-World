# AI-native 6G Network based on Large Language Model (LLM)
Yujie Zhai · ITM Web of Conferences 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1051/itmconf/20269101035 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The sixth-generation (6G) mobile communication is evolving towards the AI-native paradigm of integrated communication, sensing, computing and intelligence.
**Approach:** It defines the core connotation and three core design principles of AI-native 6G, proposes LLM-driven centralized and distributed top-level architectures as well as a three-layer cloud-edge-terminal collaborative deployment scheme, comprehensively sorts out the research progress and enabling mechanisms of LLMs across four core dimensions including spectrum resource allocation and network slice management, systematically summarizes the key technical challenges and corresponding solutions in the integration process, and prospects the priority research directions in the future.
