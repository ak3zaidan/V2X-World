# Detection of Sybil attack in vehicular ad hoc networks by analyzing network performance
Nirbhay Chaubey, Dhananjay Yadav · International Journal of Electrical and Computer Engineering (IJECE) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.11591/ijece.v12i2.pp1703-1710 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Sybil attack detection.
**Approach:** Poisson traffic prediction and PDR analysis in benign vs malicious settings.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** PDR decreases with fake vehicles.
