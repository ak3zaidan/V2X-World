# PureTrust: A Soulbound Token-Based Blockchain Framework for Incentive-Driven Trust Management in V2X Networks
H. L. Nakayiza, Love Allen Chijioke Ahakonye, Dong-Seong Kim, Jae-Min Lee · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2026.3656576 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Ensuring reliable trust management in vehicle-to-everything (V2X) networks is challenging due to the presence of malicious behavior and identity spoofing.
**Approach:** This article proposes PureTrust, a blockchain-enabled trust management framework that integrates PureSBT, a soulbound token (SBT)-based incentive mechanism, for secure and nontransferable reputation tracking. PureTrust employs a hybrid trust computation model combining Bayesian estimation, cooperation decay, malicious behavior penalties, and temporal trust aging to generate adaptive trust scores.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: blockchain
**Results:** The simulation results demonstrate that PureTrust achieves consistently high detection accuracy, maintains approximately 80% successful message delivery, and provides significantly higher throughput with a low average latency of 1.54 s compared to existing public and private blockchain networks.
