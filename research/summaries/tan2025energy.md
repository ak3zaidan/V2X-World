# Energy-Efficient Federated Learning Training Optimization for Digital Twin Driven 6G Air-Ground Integrated Vehicular Networks
Can Tan, Peng Yu, Zhaowei Qu, Lixin Zhang et al. · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2025.3577308 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The rapid development of autonomous vehicles and smart city has led to an exponential increase in data generation within Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS). However, comprehensive extraction and utilization of these data are severely hindered by communication and energy constraints, security and privacy concerns, vehicle mobility limitations, and spatial distribution challenges.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a DT-based model training architecture for vehicular networks and introduce Federated Learning (FL) to preserve data privacy. We formulate an optimization model to improve the selection of FL participating nodes and energy management strategies, aiming to maximize accuracy while minimizing energy consumption.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 6G, reinforcement learning, federated, digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Using 6G and Digital Twin (DT) technologies offers a promising solution to these problems. Experiments show that our scheme achieves higher training accuracy and energy efficiency compared to the benchmark.
