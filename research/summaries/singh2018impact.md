# Impact of Security Attacks on Cooperative Driving Use Case: CACC Platooning
Pranav Kumar Singh, Geetesh Saikamal Tabjul, Mohd Imran, Sunit Kumar Nandi, Sukumar Nandi · TENCON 2018 - 2018 IEEE Region 10 Conference 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tencon.2018.8650174 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Impact of communication attacks on CACC platooning.
**Approach:** Model various attacks on CACC platoons and show their impacts.
**Tools and scale:** VENTOS (SUMO + OMNeT++).
**Results:** Attacks cause significant safety and stability problems in the platoon.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — attacks executed in co-simulation with safety impact; no PKI or detection loop.
**Novelty implications:** Attack-impact-on-platoon studies exist.
