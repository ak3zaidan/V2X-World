# ECA-VFog: An efficient certificateless authentication scheme for 5G-assisted vehicular fog computing
Abdulwahab Ali Almazroi, Eman Ali aldhahri, ‪Mahmood A. Al-Shareeda‬‏, Selvakumar Manickam · PLoS ONE 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0287291 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Fifth-generation (5G)-enabled vehicular fog computing technologies have always been at the forefront of innovation because they support smart transport like the sharing of traffic data and cooperative processing in the urban fabric. Nevertheless, the most important factors limiting progress are concerns over message protection and safety.
**Approach:** Therefore, this paper proposes an efficient certificateless authentication called the ECA-VFog scheme for fog computing with 5G-assisted vehicular systems.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** In the performance ovulation section, the computation costs for signing and verification process are 2.3539 ms and 1.5752 ms, respectively. While, the communication costs and energy consumption overhead of the ECA-VFog are 124 bytes and 25.610432 mJ, respectively.
