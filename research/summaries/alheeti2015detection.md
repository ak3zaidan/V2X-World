# On the detection of grey hole and rushing attacks in self-driving vehicular networks
Khattab M. Ali Alheeti, Anna Gruebler, Klaus Dieter McDonald-Maier · Computer Science and Electronic Engineering Conference 2015 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ceec.2015.7332730 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc networks play an important role in the success of a new class of vehicles, i.e. self-driving and semi self-driving vehicles. These networks provide safety and comfort to passengers, drivers and vehicles themselves.
**Approach:** In this paper, we present an intelligent Intrusion Detection System (IDS) which relies on anomaly detection to protect external communications from grey hole and rushing attacks. The proposed IDS is based on features that have been extracted from a trace file generated in a network simulator.
