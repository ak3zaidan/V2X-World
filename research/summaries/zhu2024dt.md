# DT-GCN Based Digital Twin for 6G Self-Intelligent Network
Yanhong Zhu, Guangyi Liu, Na Li, Mancong Kang et al. · 2024 IEEE/CIC International Conference on Communications in China (ICCC Workshops) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icccworkshops62562.2024.10693733 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the rapid development of Sixth Generation (6G) self-intelligent network, higher requirements are put forward for intelligent decision, high-precision configuration, self-learning and self-optimization of the network. Digital twin, as digital representation of physical network, can enhance the capabilities of physical network in terms of data perception, intelligent decision-making, and pre-validation to enable the realization of closed-loop autonomy in 6G network.
**Approach:** Therefore, a data-driven Digital Twin architecture for 6G self-intelligent network is proposed in this paper.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 6G, digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** The results show that DT -GCN improves policies accuracy by 4.98% and convergence speed by 7.14% compared to traditional GCN method.
