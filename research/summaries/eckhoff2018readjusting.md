# Readjusting the privacy goals in Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks: A safety-preserving solution using non-overlapping time-slotted pseudonym pools
David Eckhoff, Christoph Sommer · Computer Communications 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.comcom.2018.03.006 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Privacy measures either harm safety, are ineffective, or break accountability.
**Approach:** Synchronised time-slotted non-overlapping pseudonym pools with local announcement of pseudonym changes.
**Tools and scale:** Computer Communications; simulation.
**Results:** Local privacy cannot be attained without compromising safety, and need not be; scheme overcomes privacy-safety problem.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — explicit privacy versus safety trade-off of pseudonym policy (direction 2 adjacent).
**Novelty implications:** Privacy-safety tension of pseudonym change is established; safety cost of security mechanisms in a full SCMS remains open.
