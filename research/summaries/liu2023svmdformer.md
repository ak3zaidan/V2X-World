# SVMDformer: A Semi-Supervised Vehicular Misbehavior Detection Framework Based on Transformer in IoV
Zhikang Liu, Hongyun Xu, Yong Kuang, Feng Li · IEEE International Conference on Distributed Computing Systems 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icdcs57875.2023.00035 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Incomplete misbehaviour coverage and multi-model inefficiency.
**Approach:** Semi-supervised Transformer producing misbehaviour scores with threshold; trained in cloud, deployed at edge.
**Tools and scale:** Open-source dataset (VeReMi extension, 19 misbehaviours).
**Results:** AUC 99.87%, accuracy 99.66%, precision 99.68%, F1 99.66%, false detection 0.34%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — offline.
