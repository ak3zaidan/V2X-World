# Enhancing Urban Traffic Safety with VANET: A Simulation-Based Multi-Scenario Alert System
Vo Duc Tai, Nguyen Xuan Tuyen, Hanh Dang-Ngoc, Vo Que Son, Duc Ngoc Minh Dang · 2025 International Symposium on Electrical and Electronics Engineering (ISEE) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/isee68370.2025.11223481 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The rapid growth of urban traffic and rising concerns over road safety have underscored the need for real-time inter-vehicle communication.
**Approach:** This paper introduces a safety alert framework based on Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs), designed to operate in urban environments using IEEE 802.11p for low-latency vehicle-to-vehicle (V 2 V) and vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I) communication. The proposed system addresses two highimpact scenarios: emergency vehicle alerts, which require immediate right-of-way coordination, and accident notifications, which enable proactive rerouting to mitigate congestion.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: real-world, SUMO; scale not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Performance evaluation on a real-world urban map of Orlando, Florida, shows that the system reduces emergency vehicle delay by 53.36% and cuts travel time by up to 50% in accident scenarios. These findings validate the practical feasibility of VANET-based safety systems and highlight their potential for improving urban mobility, traffic coordination, and emergency response operations.
