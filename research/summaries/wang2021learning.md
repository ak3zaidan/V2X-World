# Learning Interactive Driving Policies via Data-driven Simulation
Tsun-Hsuan Wang, Alexander Amini, Wilko Schwarting, Igor Gilitschenski, Sertac Karaman, Daniela Rus · IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icra46639.2022.9812407 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Data-driven simulators promise high data-efficiency for driving policy learning.
**Approach:** When used for modelling interactions, this data-efficiency becomes a bottleneck: small underlying datasets often lack interesting and challenging edge cases for learning interactive driving. We address this challenge by proposing a data-driven simulation engine† that uses inpainted ado vehicles for learning robust driving policies.
