# Packet Inter-Reception Time Conditional Density Estimation Based on Surrounding Traffic Distribution
Guillaume Jornod, Ahmad El Assaad, Thomas Kurner · IEEE Open Journal of Intelligent Transportation Systems 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ojits.2020.2995304 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Platoon control needs predictions of V2V message inter-reception time.
**Approach:** Conditional density estimation of an exponential distribution with an MLP using annulus-sector features of surrounding vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3 and SUMO; one hour of five-truck platoon simulation; IEEE 802.11p.
**Results:** Annulus-sector abstraction models compared; on-line learning potential shown.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Single test-track scenario.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - learned 802.11p performance model from ns-3/SUMO co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on security directions.
