# A Decentralized Lightweight Authentication and Privacy Protocol for Vehicular Networks
Shimaa Abdelnaby AbdelHakeem, Mohamed A. Abd El-Gawad, HyungWon Kim · IEEE Access 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2019.2937182 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Recently many security protocols have been proposed for road safety applications in Vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communications. Most of them, however, do not fully satisfy the requirements of light-weight and fast processing, which are special properties for V2X.
**Approach:** We propose a light-weight security protocol for authentication and privacy protection for V2X.
**Tools and scale:** We implemented the proposed key generation and authentication protocol using NS-3 simulator.
**Results:** Our extensive simulations demonstrated that the proposed authentication protocol significantly enhances the security level while protecting the conditional privacy of vehicles by utilizing anonymous identities. The proposed protocol has a 20% ~ 85% less communication overhead compared with the previous protocols.
