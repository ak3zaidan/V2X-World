# QSAFE-V: Quantum-Enhanced Lightweight Authentication Protocol Design for Vehicular Tactile Wireless Networks
Shakil Ahmed, Amika Tabassum, Ibrahim Almazyad, Ashfaq Khokhar · Advanced Quantum Technologies 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1002/qute.202500864 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Quantum-capable adversaries threaten authentication in 6G vehicular Tactile Internet.
**Approach:** QSAFE-V: hybrid quantum-classical authentication with QKD, context-driven reauthentication; proofs in extended ROR.
**Tools and scale:** Formal proofs and cost analysis.
**Results:** Comparable cost to classical schemes with stronger security.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future work: adaptive quantum resource management, QML threat detection.
- Future work: validation on real vehicular edge testbeds with quantum simulators and QKD hardware.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — QKD.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
