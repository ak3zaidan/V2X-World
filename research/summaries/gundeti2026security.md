# Security Challenges in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks: Threat Mitigation Strategies
Maruthikumar Gundeti · 2026 IEEE International Conference on Advanced Telecommunication and Networking Technologies (ATNT) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/atnt70190.2026.11709570 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) have become a critical component of intelligent transportation systems (ITS) that have the potential to revolutionize the transportation industry.
**Approach:** While existing literature surveys separate architectural concepts from mitigation mechanics, this paper fills a critical gap by providing an integrated, cross-layer taxonomy that directly maps architectural vulnerabilities and mobility dynamics to specific threat mitigation protocols, AI-driven intrusion detection frameworks, and decentralized blockchain infrastructures.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** VANETs allow for vehicles to communicate with other vehicles and with roadside infrastructure to improve safety, traffic movement, and passenger comfort within the transportation industry. Furthermore, the technical limitations of these strategies, performance evaluation metrics, and open challenges that must be addressed in the future to improve the security of VANETs are systematically analyzed.
