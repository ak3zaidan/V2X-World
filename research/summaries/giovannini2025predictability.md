# On the Predictability of the Best V2X Path for Infrastructure-Assisted Automated Driving
Andrea Giovannini, Claudia Campolo, Vittorio Todisco, Antonella Molinaro et al. · 2025 IEEE Conference on Standards for Communications and Networking (CSCN) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cscn67557.2025.11230595 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Whether long-term QoS maps predict the actual QoS a CAV experiences along a path.
**Approach:** Sionna RT radio environment maps offline versus per-position channel realisations online.
**Tools and scale:** Sionna RT ray tracing.
**Results:** Point-by-point matching not feasible, but (truncated) long-term prediction still useful.
