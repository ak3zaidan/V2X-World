# An Authentication Scheme Against Sybil Attacks Based on TLC in the IoV
Huiyong Liu, Congcong Li, Zirui Shao, Di Luan et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2025.3556851 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil attacks by insiders and outsiders in IoV.
**Approach:** Time-Limited Certification issued by RSUs.
**Tools and scale:** IEEE TVT; experimental calculation.
**Results:** Reduces Sybil incidence and improves delivery rate.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — short-lived certificates as Sybil defence relates to pseudonym validity policy.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
