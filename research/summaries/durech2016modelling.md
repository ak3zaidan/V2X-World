# Modelling of Security Principles Within Car-to-Car Communications in Modern Cooperative Intelligent Transportation Systems
Ján Ďurech, Mária Franeková, Peter Holečko et al. · Advances in Electrical and Electronic Engineering 2016 · https://doi.org/10.15598/aeee.v14i1.1279 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Intelligent transportation systems (ITS) bring advanced applications that provide innovative services for various transportation modes in the area of traffic control, and enable better awareness for different users.
**Approach:** Communication connections between intelligent vehicles with the use of wireless communication standards, so called Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs), require ensuring verification of validity of provided services as well as services related to transmission confidentiality and integrity.
**Tools and scale:** The designed models simulate a transmission of authorised alert messages in Car-to-Car communication for several traffic scenarios with recommended Elliptic Curve Integrated Encryption Scheme (ECIES).
