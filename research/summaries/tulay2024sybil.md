# Sybil Attack Detection Based on Signal Clustering in Vehicular Networks
H. B. Tulay, Can Emre Koksal · IEEE Transactions on Machine Learning in Communications and Networking 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tmlcn.2024.3410208 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil attacks with multiple identities.
**Approach:** Detect Sybils by clustering channel state information spatio-temporal variations.
**Tools and scale:** Real-world DSRC V2X experiments; ray-tracing simulations of urban intersections.
**Results:** Over 98% detection in real-world experiments; high efficacy in ray-tracing.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — PHY-layer Sybil detection; our PHY abstraction may not produce CSI.
**Novelty implications:** CSI-based Sybil detection with real data exists.
