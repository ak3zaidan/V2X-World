# A Privacy-Preserving Key Management Scheme with Support for Sybil Attack Detection in VANETs
L. Ellen Funderburg, Im-Yeong Lee · Sensors 2021 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s21041063 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil detection conflicts with privacy.
**Approach:** Hierarchical key management with short group signatures; mid-level nodes detect Sybils; top-level trusted nodes reveal identities to prevent rejoining; argues for relaxing backward secrecy when no misbehaviour detected.
