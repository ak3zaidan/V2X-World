# zk-ScalHard: Scalable and Hardware-Rooted Privacy-Preserving Authentication for Secure OTA Updates in Zonal SDVs
Shrikant S. Tangade, Bansi Pambhar, Valeria Loscrì, Mauro Conti · arXiv.org 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07371 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The automotive industry is transitioning to Zonal-oriented Architectures (ZoA) for Software-Defined Vehicles (SDVs), enabling frequent over-the-air (OTA) updates for 100+ Electronic Control Units (ECUs). While OTA updates improve efficiency, they introduce safety-critical security risks.
**Approach:** To address this, we propose zk-ScalHard, a hardware-rooted, privacy-preserving authentication protocol. We introduce a decentralized, hierarchical trust-promotion model utilizing Silicon Physical Unclonable Functions (PUFs) and two novel Zero-Knowledge Proof (ZKP) circuits: (1) Zonal Identity and Integrity (ZIDI) and (2) High-Performance Computing Aggregation (HPCA).
