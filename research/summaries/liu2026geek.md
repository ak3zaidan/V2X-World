# GEEK-Explainer: An Efficient Interpretation Method for Graph Neural Networks in SDN
Shiyuan Liu, Chuan-Huang Li, Chao Chen, Bo Ma, Zebin Chen, Xiao-Yang Wang, Ying Zhang · IEEE Transactions on Cognitive Communications and Networking 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tccn.2025.3586942 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Graph neural networks (GNNs) have been widely applied in software-defined network (SDN) to enhance network modeling and performance forecasting.
**Approach:** However, the closed-box nature of deep learning makes GNNs difficult to interpret, hindering their broad use and the application of GNN-based SDN systems in engineering. In this paper, we propose a novel interpretation framework named GEEK-Explainer, designed to efficiently provide instance-level interpretation of GNNs in SDN.

**Results:** These findings improve the transparency and robustness of the model and promote the application of GNN-based SDN systems in engineering practice.
