# A Privacy Conserves Pseudonym Acquisition Scheme in Vehicular Communication Systems
Sheraz Haider, D. Gao, Rehan Ali, Arif Hussain et al. · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2022.3141744 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular communication systems rely on temporary anonymous identities, i.e., pseudonyms, to avoid the possibility of tracking vehicles. If a vehicle uses only one pseudonym, an adversary can easily follow the vehicle by observing and linking messages signed under that pseudonym.
**Approach:** Therefore, the vehicles acquire a set of pseudonyms from the Pseudonym Certificate Authority.
**Tools and scale:** We conduct simulations to verify the performance of the Gao Algorithm.
**Results:** Results show that the Gao Algorithm is expressively better than current strategies. Besides, even in the absence of roadside unit connections and low traffic on the road, the Gao Algorithm can achieve promising performance.
