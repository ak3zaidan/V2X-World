# Towards Explainable Network Intrusion Detection using Large Language Models
Paul R. B. Houssel, Priyanka Singh, Siamak Layeghy, Marius Portmann · arXiv 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/bdcat63179.2024.00021 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Whether LLMs can act as explainable NIDS without skewed benchmark training.
**Approach:** Compare GPT-4 and Llama3 zero-knowledge detection of malicious NetFlows with traditional and transformer models.
**Tools and scale:** NetFlow data.
**Results:** LLMs struggle with precise detection but show potential for explainability.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Preliminary exploration.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
