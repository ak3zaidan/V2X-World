# PQ-STARKChain: A Post-Quantum Secure Communication Framework for Connected and Autonomous Vehicles Using zk-STARKs and CRYSTALS-Dilithium
Anjum Aslam, Aditya Bhardwaj · preprint 2026 · https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7116985 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Quantum vulnerability and latency of CAV communication frameworks.
**Approach:** PQ-STARKChain: zk-STARKs, Dilithium, IOTA Tangle with mobility-aware tip selection and reputation consensus. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** SUMO, OMNeT++, Veins.
**Results:** Up to 64% higher throughput, 40% lower latency, 45% lower energy.
