# Misbehavior Detection Using Collective Perception under Privacy Considerations
Manabu Tsukada, Shimpei Arii, Hideya Ochiai, Hiroshi Esaki · 2022 IEEE 19th Annual Consumer Communications &amp; Networking Conference (CCNC) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ccnc49033.2022.9700564 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Pseudonym IDs make MBD harder; CAM checks alone produce false positives.
**Approach:** Use Collective Perception Messages from other vehicles to verify CAMs.
**Tools and scale:** Realistic traffic scenarios (simulation).
**Results:** False positive rate reduced by about 15 percentage points with true positives maintained.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future work section not detailed in extract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — CPM-assisted CAM verification with pseudonyms.
**Novelty implications:** CPM-based verification exists.
