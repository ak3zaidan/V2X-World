# Detection and prevention from misbehaving intruders in vehicular networks
Hichem Sedjelmaci, Tarek Bouali, Sidi‐Mohammed Senouci · Globecom 2014 · https://doi.org/10.1109/glocom.2014.7036781 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** IDSs detect only current attackers, without prevention.
**Approach:** Game-theoretic prediction of a monitored vehicle's future behaviour and classification into White, White&Gray, Gray and Revocation_Black lists, targeting false alert generation.
**Tools and scale:** NS-3.17; compared with IDFV and DCMD.
**Results:** High detection rate, low false positives, low overhead (no numbers in extracted text).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None extracted.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — graded revocation lists from detection, related to MA decision design.
**Novelty implications:** Graded response lists exist; not coupled to a real CRL/linkage infrastructure.
