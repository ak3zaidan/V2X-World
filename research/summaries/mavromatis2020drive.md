# DRIVE: A Digital Network Oracle for Cooperative Intelligent Transportation Systems
Ioannis Mavromatis, Robert J. Piechocki, Mahesh Sooriyabandara, Arjun Parekh · arXiv 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iscc50000.2020.9219683 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Traditional vehicular and network simulators are inflexible for ML-based C-ITS development at city scale.
**Approach:** DRIVE: modular, scalable digital twin acting as an 'oracle' that answers sequential queries with the state of the world, with bidirectional interaction with external agents and realistic mobility traces plus uncertainty modelling.
**Tools and scale:** City-wide experimentation; realistic mobility traces; reference scenario evaluated.
**Results:** Reference scenario evaluation (numbers not extracted).

- None stated in extracted sections.
