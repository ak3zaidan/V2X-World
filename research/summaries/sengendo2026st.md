# ST-NDT: A Topological Framework for Reducing Communication Overhead in Network Digital Twins
John Sengendo, Fabrizio Granelli · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26107 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Next-generation networks (NGN) are becoming increasingly complex, especially with the increasing size of topologies.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a topological signal processing framework for sparse network monitoring in NDTs. Results show that the proposed Sparse Topological Network Digital Twin (ST-NDT) framework consistently outperforms all four baseline frameworks across most of the edge monitoring budgets.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** We represent network edge flows as signals defined on a cell complex, and exploit the Hodge spectral structure of the graph to identify a minimal set of maximally informative sensor edges, thereby reducing the measurement overhead between the physical network and its digital twin replica. For example, at a 20\% monitoring budget (48 of 243 edges monitored), ST-NDT achieves improvements of 10.85\%, 11.24\%, 7.36\%, and 6.15\% over degree-based, core-number, betweenness centrality, and random-selection frameworks, respectively.
