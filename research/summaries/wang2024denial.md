# A Denial of Service Attack Defense Method for Roadside Units
Huanbin Wang, Gaofeng He, Haiting Zhu, Bingfeng Xu, Lu Zhang, Naixuan Guo · International Conference on Advanced Cloud and Big Data 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cbd65573.2024.00037 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Roadside Units (RSUs) constitute a vital component of Vehicular Ad Hoc Network (VANET) due to their primary role in gathering vehicle information.
**Approach:** However, as indicated in the literature, they are vulnerable to Denial of Service (DoS) attacks. Existing research has proposed several methods for detecting DoS attacks in RSUs, while subsequent steps such as mitigation and elimination remain unexplored.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Simulation results demonstrate that our proposed method effectively defends against DoS attacks and successfully traces the attackers.
