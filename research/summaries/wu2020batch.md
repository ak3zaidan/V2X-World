# Batch-Assisted Verification Scheme for Reducing Message Verification Delay of the Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Fan Wu, Xi Zhang, Cong Zhang, Xi Chen et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2020.3004811 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Message verification load exceeds VANET computing capacity at scale.
**Approach:** Distributed collaborative verification with selected assisting terminals plus RSU.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation (tool not named).
**Results:** Verification delay reduced to about one tenth of centralized verification.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial: verification load is studied in engine; cooperative verification is a different design.
**Novelty implications:** Cooperative verification already proposed; not for 1609.2 certificates in NR sidelink.
