# Hi-FLoop: Hierarchical State-Feedback Loops for Multi-Timescale World Modeling
Rx Fan, Z Han · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08796 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Multi-agent traffic simulation seeks diverse, coordinated, and physically realistic futures from maps and observed history.
**Approach:** We present Hi-FLoop, a branch-consistent multi-timescale state-feedback framework. For generated-state recovery, a prefix-frozen A-to-B cascade transfers typed physical state and the branch index--but no latent state--from a frozen prefix model to an independently parameterized recovery model.
