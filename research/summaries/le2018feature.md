**Problem:** An emerging trend to improve automotive safety is the development of Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) safety applications.
**Approach:** These applications use information gathered from the vehicle's sensors and from surrounding vehicles to detect and prevent imminent crashes. Vehicles have been equipped with external communication interfaces to make these applications possible, but this also exposes them to security threats.
