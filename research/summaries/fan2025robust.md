# Robust Intrusion Detection System for Vehicular Networks: A Federated Learning Approach Based on Representative Client Selection

**Problem:** The rapid development of network technology has allowed numerous vehicular applications to be deployed in vehicles, thereby enriching the driving experience of users.
**Approach:** However, the openness of vehicular networks enables attackers to launch network attacks on vehicles through network ports, leading to the destruction of vehicular networks. To develop an intrusion detection system suitable for distributed vehicular networks, researchers have utilized federated learning to train detection models.
