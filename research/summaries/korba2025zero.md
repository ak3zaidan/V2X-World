# Zero-Day Botnet Attack Detection in IoV: A Modular Approach Using Isolation Forests and Particle Swarm Optimization
Abdelaziz Amara Korba, Nour Elislem Karabadji, Yacine Ghamri-Doudane · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.18814 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Internet of Vehicles (IoV) is transforming transportation by enhancing connectivity and enabling autonomous driving.
**Approach:** However, this increased interconnectivity introduces new security vulnerabilities. To address these challenges, we propose an edge-based Intrusion Detection System (IDS) that monitors network traffic to and from CAVs.
**Tools and scale:** The proposed IDS has been evaluated on a vehicular botnet dataset, achieving an average detection rate of 92.80% for N-day attacks and 77.32% for zero-day attacks.
**Results:** Bot malware and cyberattacks pose significant risks to Connected and Autonomous Vehicles (CAVs), as demonstrated by real-world incidents involving remote vehicle system compromise. The proposed IDS has been evaluated on a vehicular botnet dataset, achieving an average detection rate of 92.80% for N-day attacks and 77.32% for zero-day attacks.
