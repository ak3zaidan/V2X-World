# CDA-PTA: Cognitive Digital Twin-Based Predictive Trust Assessment for Proactive VANET Security
Prabhaharan A · Journal of Propulsion Technology 2026 · https://propulsiontechjournal.com/index.php/journal/article/view/11278 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) require reliable trust assessment because vehicles exchange safety-critical information over highly dynamic communication links.
**Approach:** This paper proposes Cognitive Digital Twin-Based Predictive Trust Assessment (CDA-PTA), a predictive framework combining continuously updated Digital Twin states, cyber-physical behavioral features, temporal interaction graphs, Temporal Graph Neural Network (TGNN) learning, explainable trust assessment, and trust-aware Software-Defined Networking (SDN) routing. Each vehicle is represented by a Digital Twin, heterogeneous behavioral features are fused, and evolving interactions are modeled as a temporal graph.
