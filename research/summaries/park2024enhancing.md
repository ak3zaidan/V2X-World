# Enhancing V2X Security Through Combined Rule-Based and DL-Based Local Misbehavior Detection in Roadside Units
Seung‐Young Park, Duksoo Kim, Seokwoo Lee · IEEE Open Journal of Intelligent Transportation Systems 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ojits.2024.3479716 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** DL LMBD at RSUs suffers delay and truncated sequences.
**Approach:** MEC-connected RSUs forward BSMs directly; rule-based pre-screening for single/two BSMs combined with DL.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Combined approach improves detection.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Fixed RSUs see truncated sequences.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — RSU/MEC MBD latency.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
