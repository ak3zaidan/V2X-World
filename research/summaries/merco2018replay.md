# Replay Attack Detection in a Platoon of Connected Vehicles with Cooperative Adaptive Cruise Control
Roberto Merco, Zoleikha Abdollahi Biron, Pierluigi Pisu · 2018 Annual American Control Conference (ACC) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.23919/acc.2018.8431538 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Using emerging wireless communication technologies, vehicles can exchange information among each other with the aim of cooperation to improve safety, efficiency and fuel economy of the transportation system.
**Approach:** This advancement in technology highlights challenges in designing control systems that are able to deal with an unreliable and a compromised network while guaranteeing stability, safety and reliability of the vehicles: network failures and cyber-attacks could cause loss in performances, vehicle collisions or loss of lives.
