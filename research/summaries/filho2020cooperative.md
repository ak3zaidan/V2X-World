# Towards a Cooperative Robotic Platooning Testbed
Enio Vasconcelos Filho, Nuno Guedes, Bruno Vieira, Miguel Mestre, Ricardo Severino, Bruno Goncalves et al. · 2020 IEEE International Conference on Autonomous Robot Systems and Competitions (ICARSC) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icarsc49921.2020.9096132 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Testing V2X platooning safely and cheaply.
**Approach:** 1/10-scale robotic platooning testbed with real ITS-G5 OBUs and communication-only cooperative control.
**Tools and scale:** Robots, real ITS-G5 OBUs.
**Results:** Cooperative control relying solely on communications successfully implemented.
