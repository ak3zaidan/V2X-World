# SRCON: A Data-Driven Network Performance Simulator for Real-World Wireless Networks
Z. Luo, Xi Zheng, David López-Pérez, Qing-Qiang Yan, Xin Chen, Nanbin Wang et al. · IEEE Communications Magazine 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mcom.001.2200179 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Real-world 4G/5G optimisation relies on drive tests and trial and error.
**Approach:** SRCON: data-driven simulator mixing white-box and ML models for traffic arrival, association and propagation.
**Tools and scale:** Commercial 4G/5G networks.
**Results:** Notable gains after SRCON-enabled optimisation of commercial networks (no numbers).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Challenges: problem size, network coupling, stochasticity.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - hybrid data-driven network simulator; no V2X or security.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on security directions.
