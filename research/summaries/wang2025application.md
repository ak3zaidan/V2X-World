# Application Research of a V2X Semi-Physical Simulation Platform in Vehicle–Road Collaboration Experiments
Lei Wang, Heng Zhang, Yue Huang, Jian Liu, Kaixuan Ji, Bohao Shi · World Electric Vehicle Journal 2025 · https://doi.org/10.3390/wevj16060304 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Teaching vehicle-road collaboration with V2X semi-physical simulation.
**Approach:** Sand table + digital twin platform; OBE/BOPPPS curriculum; global path planning example.
**Tools and scale:** Semi-physical platform.
**Results:** Over 80% satisfaction; observation group performance 17.39% higher.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Needs better simulation of highly complex scenarios
- add diverse scenarios, more authenticity, AI integration.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
