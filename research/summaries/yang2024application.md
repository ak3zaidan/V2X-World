# Application of Vehicular Digital Twin System in Night Driving Based on EAIS Acceleration
Yi Yang · 2024 5th International Conference on Computer Engineering and Application (ICCEA) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccea62105.2024.10603925 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Aiming at the problem of night driving safety, a vehicular digital twin system based on EAIS acceleration is proposed, which uses cloud platform and target detection technology to calculate and identify obstacles such as pedestrians at the digital twin layer in the cloud, and issues real-time early warning to the vehicle entity.
**Approach:** Meanwhile, identity authentication and data encryption technology based on the zero-trust security model are applied to ensure the security and reliability of intra-twin communication.
