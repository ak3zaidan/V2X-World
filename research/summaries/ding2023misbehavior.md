# Misbehavior Detection and Identification in DF Multi-Relay VANETs
Xinyao Ding, Yan Wang · IEEE Wireless Communications Letters 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/lwc.2022.3220564 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Detecting misbehaving relays in decode-and-forward multi-relay VANETs.
**Approach:** Hypothesis variance ratio metric with closed-form error rates, optimal threshold and pre-detection scheme.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical and numerical.
**Results:** Outperforms average-power Neyman-Pearson in weighted detection error.

- None stated.
