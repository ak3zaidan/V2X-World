# Robust Zero Trust Systems Based on Collaborative ai to Secure the 6G-Enabled VANETs
Hichem Sedjelmaci, Marwane Ayaida · IEEE Wireless Communications 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mwc.003.2300571 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The research in cyber security for vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs) has received great attention from the scientific community.
**Approach:** However, the intrusion detection and prevention for Sixth Generation (6G)-enabled VANETs has not attracted much attention up to this point. In this research article, we propose new robust zero trust agents based on collaborative artificial intelligence (AI) algorithms to protect the 6G-enabled VANETs from attacks targeting simultaneously the VANETs and 6G infrastructure.
