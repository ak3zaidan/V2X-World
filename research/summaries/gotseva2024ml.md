# ML-Driven Prediction of QoS in C-V2X Scenarios
Nikol Gotseva, Atanas Vlahov, V. Poulkov, A. Manolova · International Scientific Conference on Information, Communication and Energy Systems and Technologies 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icest62335.2024.10639683 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Downlink throughput prediction in C-V2X.
**Approach:** LightGBM on a minimal feature set.
**Tools and scale:** Berlin V2X dataset.
**Results:** R^2 0.97, MAE about 3 Mbps.
