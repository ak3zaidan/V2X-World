# L2PIAS: Lightweight Privacy-Preserving Identity Authentication for Secure V2V Communication
Islam Z. Ahmed, Yasser Hifny, Rowayda A. Sadek · National Radio Science Conference 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/nrsc65659.2025.11018562 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) is a common communication model in the Internet of Vehicles (IoV) that can disseminate messages swiftly. Through this communication model, vehicles can directly exchange different types of messages with each other through a public channel.
**Approach:** Recently, many different types of schemes have been proposed to mitigate these issues. To address these issues, a Lightweight Privacy-Preserving Identity Authentication scheme for Secure V2V Communication (L2PIAS) is proposed.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting (VANET/IoV/V2X). Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** The performance analysis of the L2PIAS demonstrates that the overall computation overhead in the registration phase and the V2V message dissemination phase is enhanced by 60 % and 88 %, respectively. Moreover, the overall communication overhead in the V2V message dissemination phase is enhanced by 85 %.
