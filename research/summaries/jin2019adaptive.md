# An adaptive learning and control architecture for mitigating sensor and actuator attacks in connected autonomous vehicle platoons
Xu Jin, Wassim M. Haddad, Zhong‐Ping Jiang, Aris Kanellopoulos, Kyriakos G. Vamvoudakis · International Journal of Adaptive Control and Signal Processing 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1002/acs.3032 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Securing a platoon where an autonomous tail vehicle receives V2V kinematics from human-driven predecessors under sensor and actuator attacks.
**Approach:** Adaptive controller mitigating time-invariant state-dependent sensor/actuator attacks with uniform ultimate boundedness guarantees, plus adaptive learning to identify state-space parameters under relaxed persistence of excitation.
**Tools and scale:** Illustrative numerical platoon example; no network simulator or PKI.
**Results:** Demonstrated effectiveness on the numerical example (no aggregate figures).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — control-theoretic resilience.
**Novelty implications:** Control-layer mitigation is a complementary approach to revocation.
