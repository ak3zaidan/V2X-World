# Location Anomalies Detection for Connected and Autonomous Vehicles
Xiaoyang Wang, Ioannis Mavromatis, Andrea Tassi, Raúl Santos-Rodríguez et al. · Connected and Automated Vehicles Symposium 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cavs.2019.8887778 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Future Connected and Automated Vehicles (CAVs), and more generally ITS, will form a highly interconnected system. Such a paradigm is referred to as the Internet of Vehicles (herein Internet of CAVs) and is a prerequisite to orchestrate traffic flows in cities.
**Approach:** In this paper, a novel unsupervised learning model based on deep autoencoder is proposed to detect the self-reported location anomaly in CAVs, using vehicle locations and the Received Signal Strength Indicator (RSSI) as features. Quantitative experiments on simulation datasets show that the proposed approach is effective and robust in detecting self-reported location anomalies.
