# A Novel Dataset and Approach for Adversarial Attack Detection in Connected and Automated Vehicles
Tae Hoon Kim, Moez Krichen, Meznah A. Alamro, Gabreil Avelino Sampedro · Electronics 2024 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics13122420 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Adversarial attacks have received much attention as communication network applications rise in popularity.
**Approach:** Connected and Automated Vehicles (CAVs) must be protected against adversarial attacks to ensure passenger and vehicle safety on the road. Nevertheless, CAVs are susceptible to several types of attacks, such as those that target intra- and inter-vehicle networks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Results reveal that TAAD-CAV achieves the highest accuracy with a value of 70% compared with individual ML and DL classifiers.
