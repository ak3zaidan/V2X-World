# Future Mining: Learning for Safety and Security
Md Sazedur Rahman, Mizanur Rahman Jewel, Sanjay Madria · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11472 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Approach:** However, real world mining environments impose severe constraints, including poor illumination, GPS denied conditions, irregular underground topologies and intermittent connectivity. These factors degrade perception accuracy, disrupt situational awareness and weaken distributed learning systems.
