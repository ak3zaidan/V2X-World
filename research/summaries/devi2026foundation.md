# Foundation Model-Augmented Split Federated Learning with Graph Transformer-Based Digital Twin for Self-Healing Industrial IoT Systems
K. Devi, Shangara Narayanee N. P, A. Anuradha et al. · 2026 International Conference on Computational Robotics, Testing and Engineering Evaluation (ICCRTEE) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccrtee68719.2026.11566516 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The nature of the industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) systems, with their distributed, heterogeneous nature and resource-constrained nature, is becoming increasingly susceptible to cyber-physical attacks.
**Approach:** In this paper, the authors offer a Foundation Model-Enhanced Split Federated Learning (SFL) architecture coupled with a Graph Transformer-Based Digital Twin that would allow to detect anomalies and self-heal IIoT systems. The suggested approach has an accuracy 97.8 percent, 98.1 percent recall, 97.7 percent $F 1$-score, and an AUC value of 0.992 with less false positives of 2.1 percent.
