# Reachable Set Analysis of Vehicular Platooning in Adversarial Environment
Soodeh Dadras, Sara Dadras, Chris Winstead · 2018 Annual American Control Conference (ACC) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.23919/acc.2018.8431759 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we propose a method based on reachable set theory to investigate adversarial behavior in automated vehicle platoons.
**Approach:** Vehicular platoons have been developed to increase highway throughput and safety, and to enhance driving comfort. The resulting deployment of cyber-physical technology in critical infrastructure is increasingly attractive to both hackers and security researchers.

**Results:** The resulting deployment of cyber-physical technology in critical infrastructure is increasingly attractive to both hackers and security researchers. Using ellipsoidal techniques, we investigate the extent of an attacker's ability to manipulate the control variables and states of a platoon resulting in oscillatory motion or collision.
