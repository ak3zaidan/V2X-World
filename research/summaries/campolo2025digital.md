# Digital Twin-assisted Context-aware Object Detection for Connected and Automated Vehicles
Claudia Campolo, Antonella Molinaro, Bruno Pizzimenti, Gurtaj Singh, Domenico Mario Zappalà · 2025 IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference (VNC) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc64509.2025.11054167 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Connected and Automated Vehicles (CAVs) rely heavily on on-board sensing technologies and machine learning (ML) algorithms to perceive the surrounding environment and behave accordingly.
**Approach:** Hence, the speed and accuracy of ML models that detect surrounding objects is crucial to ensure road safety. In this work, we propose to ensure detection accuracy while maintaining efficiency by adapting the object detection model to the specific driving context where the CAV is traveling.
