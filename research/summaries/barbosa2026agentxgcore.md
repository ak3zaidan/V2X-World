# AgentxGCore: Agentic AI for Next-Generation Mobile Core Network
Maria Katarine Santana Barbosa, Kelvin L. Dias · arXiv 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mnet.2026.3699249 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** To meet the stringent requirements of emerging applications and the increasingly complex network management and operation, the Next Generation Mobile Networks (NextG), or 6G, will adopt an AI-native architecture on the Core Network (CN).
**Approach:** However, those new functionalities are constrained by a centralized approach and managerial complexity. Our approach involves a multi-agent specialized system, divided into a network planner agent, capable of visualizing the network state and developing a plan to meet the intents, and a network executor, responsible for criticizing and executing the plan.
