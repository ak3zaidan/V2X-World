# A Data-Driven Digital Twin Network Architecture in the Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) Applications
Abubakar Isah, Hyeju Shin, Ibrahim Aliyu, Sangwon Oh et al. · arXiv 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.14930 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A new network named the "Digital Twin Network" (DTN) uses the "Digital Twin" (DT) technology to produce virtual twins of real things. The network load and size continue to grow as a result of the development of 5G, the Internet of Things, and cloud computing technology as well as the advent of new network services.
**Approach:** The framework of the Industrial Internet of Things, data processing, and digital twin network is taken into consideration in this article as a key aspect. This paper proposed a data-driven digital twin network architecture, that comprises the physical network layer (PNL), the digital twin layer(DTL), the application layer (AL), and what those layers encompass and beyond.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G, digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** A digital twin connects the real and digital worlds, exchanging data in both directions and revealing information about the progression of a network process.
