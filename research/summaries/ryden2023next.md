**Problem:** Machine learning (ML) is an important component for enabling automation in radio access networks (RANs).
**Approach:** The work on applying ML for RAN has been under development for several years and is now also drawing attention in 3GPP standardization fora. A key component of multiple features, highlighted in the recent 3GPP specification work, is the use of mobility, traffic and radio channel prediction.
