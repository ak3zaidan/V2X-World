# An Implementation of A Bus Following Model in an 802.11p Based Simulator in MATLAB
Felipe Inigo Xavier Reyes Gan, Gian Paolo T. Mayuga, Luke Bennett King Lao, Elmer R. Magsino · 2023 1st International Conference on Advanced Innovations in Smart Cities (ICAISC) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icaisc56366.2023.10085008 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Bus-to-stop communication for BRT.
**Approach:** Bus-following model in MATLAB VANET Toolbox with 802.11p.
**Tools and scale:** MATLAB; EDSA passenger data.
**Results:** Latency and PDR acceptable; 2:1 bus-to-stop ratio insufficient for 7 AM rush.
