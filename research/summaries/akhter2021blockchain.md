# A Blockchain-Based Authentication Protocol for Cooperative Vehicular Ad Hoc Network
A. F. M. Suaib Akhter, Mohiuddin Ahmed, A. F. M. Shahen Shah et al. · Italian National Conference on Sensors 2021 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s21041273 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The efficiency of cooperative communication protocols to increase the reliability and range of transmission for Vehicular Ad hoc Network (VANET) is proven, but identity verification and communication security are required to be ensured.
**Approach:** In this paper, a blockchain based secure and privacy preserving authentication protocol is proposed for the Internet of Vehicles (IoV). The proposed cooperative protocol is validated by numerical analyses which show that the protocol successfully increases the system throughput and decreases PDR and delay.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: blockchain, Ethereum.
**Results:** However, cooperating with unknown or unauthorized vehicles could result in information theft, privacy leakage, vulnerable to different security attacks, etc.
