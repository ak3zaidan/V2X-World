# Distributed Neural Network-Based DDoS Detection in Vehicular Communication Systems
Nicholas Jaton, Sohan Gyawali, Yi Qian · International Conference on Signal Processing and Communication Systems 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icspcs58109.2023.10261135 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** DDoS detection.
**Approach:** Distributed MLP on Apache Spark/AWS.
**Tools and scale:** Simulated attacks.
**Results:** Median F1 95% (RF and GBT 97%; LR 89%; SVM 88%).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Underperforms RF and GBT.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
