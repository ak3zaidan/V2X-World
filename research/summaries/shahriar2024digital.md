# Digital Twin Enabled Data-Driven Approach for Traffic Efficiency and Software-Defined Vehicular Network Optimization
M. S. Shahriar, S. Subramaniam, M. Matsuura, Hiroshi Hasegawa, Shih-Chun Lin · 2024 IEEE 100th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2024-Fall) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2024-fall63153.2024.10757450 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** CAV coordination and SDVN flow-table overflow.
**Approach:** DT design with roundabout decision support and flow-entry lifespan optimisation.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Average waiting time -22% at 40% CAV penetration; flow-table space -50% at 100% connected.
