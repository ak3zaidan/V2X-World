# A Diverse AdaBoost Based Misbehavior Detection System in VANET
Yu-Feng Chen, Jia-Sheng Yang, Chun-Wei Tsai · International Conference on Intelligent Computing and its Emerging Applications 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3659154.3659163 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Single-classifier MDSs miss some attacks.
**Approach:** He-AdaBoost: AdaBoost over heterogeneous component classifiers.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi Extension dataset.
**Results:** Average F1 of 94.81%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — offline VeReMi MBD.
**Novelty implications:** Offline ML MBD on VeReMi is saturated; no closed-loop novelty impact.
