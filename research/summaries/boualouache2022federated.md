# Federated Learning-based Inter-slice Attack Detection for 5G-V2X Sliced Networks
Abdelwahab Boualouache, Thomas Engel · 2022 IEEE 96th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2022-Fall) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2022-fall57202.2022.10012736 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As a leading enabler of 5G, Network Slicing (NS) aims at creating multiple virtual networks on the same shared and programmable physical infrastructure. Integrated with 5GVehicle-to-Everything (V2X) technology, NS enables various isolated 5G-V2X networks with different requirements such as autonomous driving and platooning.
**Approach:** To this end, this paper addresses detecting inter-slice V2X attacks. To do so, we leverage both Virtual Security as a Service (VSaS) concept and Deep learning (DL) together with Federated learning (FL) to deploy a set of DL-empowered security Virtual Network Functions (sVNFs) over V2X-NSs.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset, C-V2X/LTE-V2X/5G, federated learning, deep learning (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** The results show our scheme’s accuracy and efficiency in detecting inter-slice V2X attacks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Addresses V2X attacks or misbehaviour/trust evaluation that our engine can execute and detect in closed loop; it does not model the SCMS, revocation or joint traffic/radio/PKI ground truth.
**Novelty implications:** Does not rule out our candidate directions; any claim about this specific attack/detection/trust mechanism must cite it. Left open: end-to-end detection-report-revocation evaluation with safety outcomes.
