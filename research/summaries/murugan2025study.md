# A Study of Security Issues and Attacks in Wireless Ad Hoc Networks
Dr. K. Murugan · Zenodo (CERN European Organization for Nuclear Research) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23099937 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract Wireless ad hoc networks enable mobile devices to communicate without relying on fixed infrastructure or centralized access points.
**Approach:** However, the absence of centralized control, dynamic topology, wireless broadcast communication, limited bandwidth, constrained energy resources, and the possibility of compromised nodes introduce significant security challenges. This paper presents a study of security issues and attacks in wireless ad hoc networks, with particular emphasis on communication security, authentication, confidentiality, integrity, availability, and secure routing.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Security is achieved through a combination of multiple technologies that address various authentication, information integrity, and identification requirements. The objective of this paper is therefore to examine the major security issues and attacks affecting wireless ad hoc networks, analyze their effects, review existing security mechanisms, and identify directions for improving secure communication and routing. 2.
