# Hybrid Algorithm to Detect the Sybil Attacks in VANET
Salam Hamdan, Amjad A. Hudaib, Arafat Awajan · 2018 Fifth International Symposium on Innovation in Information and Communication Technology (ISIICT) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/isiict.2018.8613724 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil detection.
**Approach:** Hybrid of footprint and P2DAP methods.
**Tools and scale:** ns2 with SUMO and MOVE.
**Results:** P2DAP better at higher density, footprint better at higher speed.
