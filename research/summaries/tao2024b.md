# B-DSPA: A Blockchain-Based Dynamically Scalable Privacy-Preserving Authentication Scheme in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Q. Tao, Hong-Wei Ding, T. Jiang, Xiaohui Cui · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2023.3289057 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The big data of Internet of Vehicles contributes to the development of intelligent transportation. Privacy protection in vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) is the core factor to improve user and vehicle participation.
**Approach:** This article proposes a novel blockchain-based dynamic extensible privacy protection and message authentication scheme for VANETs.
