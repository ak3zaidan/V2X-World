# A Trust-Based Model for Security Cooperating in Vehicular Cloud Computing
Zhipeng Tang, An-Feng Liu, Zhe-Tao Li, Young-June Choi et al. · Mobile Information Systems 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2016/9083608 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** VCC is a computing paradigm which consists of vehicles cooperating with each other to realize a lot of practical applications, such as delivering packages. Security cooperation is a fundamental research topic in Vehicular Cloud Computing (VCC).
**Approach:** In this paper, a trust-based model for security cooperating, named DBTEC, is proposed to promote vehicles’ security cooperation in VCC. DBTEC combines the indirect trust estimation in Public board and the direct trust estimation in Private board to compute the trust value of vehicles when choosing cooperative partners; a trustworthy cooperation path generating scheme is proposed to ensure the safety of cooperation and increase the cooperation completion rates in VCC.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in abstract.
**Results:** Extensive experiments show that our scheme improves the overall cooperation completion rates by 6~7%.
