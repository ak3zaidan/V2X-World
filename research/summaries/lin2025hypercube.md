# Hypercube Graph Self-Attention Mechanisms for Intelligent Vehicular Intrusion Detection in Autonomous Transport Systems
Li-Mei Lin, Xiao-Ding Wang, Xiu-Zhen Zhu, Yan-Ze Huang, Ding-Bang Fang, M. Piran · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2025.3555715 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Autonomous Transportation Systems (ATS) make the transportation system transition from “passive transportation” to “autonomous service”.
**Approach:** The wireless nature of communication in ATS presents significant cybersecurity challenges. Conventional intelligent vehicular intrusion detection methods may not suffice in situations where vehicular data is produced at an unprecedented scale and diverse cybersecurity threats are launched.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Experiments results show that our proposed QnGSA method achieves the best results compared with state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy, macro precision/recall/F1.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- The wireless nature of communication in ATS presents significant cybersecurity challenges.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
