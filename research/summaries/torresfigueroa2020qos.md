# QoS Evaluation and Prediction for C-V2X Communication in Commercially-Deployed LTE and Mobile Edge Networks
Luis Torres-Figueroa, Henning F. Schepker, Josef Jiru · 2020 IEEE 91st Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2020-Spring) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2020-spring48590.2020.9129382 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Are commercial LTE networks ready for Day-1 C-V2X applications and can underperformance be predicted?
**Approach:** Measurement system with high-resolution LTE KPIs (I/Q capture), PTP-synchronised OBUs measuring E2E delay across MNOs and a live MEC; ML client-based QoS predictor.
**Tools and scale:** Real urban drive tests, multiple MNOs, MEC.
**Results:** Readiness assessment and QoS forecasts (numbers not extracted).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- COTS modems give identical KPI readings for 1-3 s, limiting precision.
**Overlap with our engine:** none - cellular QoS measurement.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
