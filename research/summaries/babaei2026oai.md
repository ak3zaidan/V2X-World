# OAI-Powered Network Digital Twin for RAN SLA Assurance
Mohammadreza Babaei, Elçin Can · 2026 IEEE International Conference on Communications Workshops (ICC Workshops) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccworkshops63917.2026.11586732 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper presents a network digital twin (NDT)-based closed-loop framework for proactive service assurance in 5G networks. In the proposed approach, live network parameters and key performance indicators (KPIs) are continuously collected from the OpenAirInterface (OAI) end-to-end emulator.
**Approach:** This paper presents a network digital twin (NDT)-based closed-loop framework for proactive service assurance in 5G networks.
**Tools and scale:** In the proposed approach, live network parameters and key performance indicators (KPIs) are continuously collected from the OpenAirInterface (OAI) end-to-end emulator.
**Results:** The proposed framework is demonstrated in an enhanced mobile broadband scenario with five user equipments, and results indicate that the method can maintain SLA compliance while improving network resource utilization.
