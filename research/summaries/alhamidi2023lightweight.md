# Lightweight Certificate Authorization and Revocation Scheme for Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks
Mashail Alhamidi · preprint 2023 · https://doi.org/10.31224/3325 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Certificate management in VANETs is a security/performance trade-off.
**Approach:** Dynamic CA framework with RSA encryption for authorisation and revocation.
**Tools and scale:** NS-2; latency, jitter, PDR in three modes.
**Results:** Competitive with less secure settings.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in extracted text.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — certificate authorisation and revocation evaluated in network simulation; uses RSA rather than 1609.2.
**Novelty implications:** Minor.
