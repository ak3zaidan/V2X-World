# A comparative study of traditional machine learning models and the KNN-KFSC method for optimizing anomaly detection in VANETs
Ravikumar ch, D. Kavitha, S. Sowjanya C. et al. · International journal of systematic innovation 2025 · https://ojs.ijosi.org/index.php/IJOSI/article/view/1487 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this research, we conducted a comparative analysis of traditional machine learning techniques and the innovative K-nearest neighbors-K-fuzzy subspace clustering (KNN-KFSC) methodology to detect anomalies in vehicular ad hoc network (VANET) infrastructures.
**Approach:** Our evaluation included models such as support vector machine (SVM), random forest (RF), logistic regression (LR), and KNN.
**Tools and scale:** While effective in identifying specific types of attackers, these models showed weaknesses, potentially due to overfitting or inadequate management of dataset complexity.
**Results:** The KNN-KFSC model demonstrated exceptional performance with an overall accuracy rate of 99% in handling densely contextual data. It consistently exhibited high accuracy, recall, and F1 score metrics, indicating its effectiveness in detecting a broad spectrum of anomalies across various types of attacks in VANETs.
