# The Need Of Trustworthy Announcements To Achieve Driving Comfort
Rezvi Shahariar, Chris Phillips · arXiv 2024 · https://doi.org/10.5121/ijwmn.2024.16202 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** An Intelligent Transport System (ITS) is more demanding nowadays and it can be achieved through deploying Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs).
**Approach:** Vehicles and Roadside Units (RSUs) exchange traffic events. Malicious drivers generate false events.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: Veins
**Results:** Results confirm that false announcements and refraining from announcements increase travel time. However, the travel time is reduced with trustworthy announcements.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, a trust model can isolate false messages.
- However, the travel time is reduced with trustworthy announcements.
