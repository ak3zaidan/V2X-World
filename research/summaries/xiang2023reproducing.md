# Toward Reproducing Network Research Results Using Large Language Models
Qiao Xiang, Yuling Lin, Mingjun Fang, Bang Huang, Siyong Huang, Ridi Wen et al. · Proceedings of the 22nd ACM Workshop on Hot Topics in Networks 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3626111.3628189 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Reproducing network research is labour-intensive due to missing prototypes.
**Approach:** Students reproduce four published networking systems by prompting ChatGPT (GPT-3.5).
**Tools and scale:** Small-scale experiment, four systems.
**Results:** All four reproduced; efficiency similar to open-source prototypes.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Small-scale experiment; open research questions discussed.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
