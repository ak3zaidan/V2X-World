# Iridescence: Improving Configuration Tuning in the Presence of Confounders for 5G NSA Networks
Changhan Ge, Ajay Mahimkar, Zihui Ge, Romeo Fernandez, Joseph Maniaci, Shomik Pathak et al. · Proceedings of the ACM on Networking 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3709378 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Confounders hinder attributing performance changes to configuration changes in 5G NSA.
**Approach:** Iridescence: XGBoost/transformer de-confounding before impact classification.
**Tools and scale:** Large operational 5G NSA dataset; preliminary trials.
**Results:** Performance improvements in operational networks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Trials preliminary.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - causal attribution of configuration changes; our engine eliminates confounding by exact reruns.
**Novelty implications:** Causal configuration-impact analysis in cellular exists; exact counterfactual V2X evaluation remains distinct.
