# CSTM: Cluster-based Security Trust Mechanism for VANET
Jijin Wang, Xiaoqiang Xiao, Lu Peng · IOP Conference Series Materials Science and Engineering 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1088/1757-899x/688/4/044051 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract Vehicular Ad hoc Network (VANET) allows communications between vehicles and roadside infrastructure.
**Approach:** In order to ensure the security and stability of VANET communication, we can introduce the concept of trust and improve the credibility of communication between vehicles through trust management. Therefore, this paper proposes a cluster-based security trust mechanism for VANET.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Various information can be propagated by the vehicles to ensure the safety and security of the driver and passengers by reducing the transport accidents and traffic congestion, besides, VANET communication can also provide navigation and entertainment service.
