# Self-Healing Hybrid Control as a Proxy for Detection and Mitigation of Sensor Attacks in Cooperative Driving
Mischa Huisman, Carlos Murguia, Erjen Lefeber, Nathan van de Wouw · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05958 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** FDI attacks on CACC sensors.
**Approach:** Self-healing hybrid controller with redundant sensor-subset realisations and majority voting.
**Tools and scale:** Control simulation (Matlab hybrid systems).
**Results:** Effective detection and switching in simulation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Robustness under non-ideal conditions; sensor noise may cause Zeno switching; detection and transmission delays may cause late switching; minimise required sensors.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — control-layer mitigation.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
