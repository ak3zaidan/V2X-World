Yan Zhang · Applied and Computational Engineering 2026 · https://doi.org/10.54254/2755-2721/2026.ch35917 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Internet public-key cryptography faces long-term risk from quantum computers, especially when traffic can be collected now and decrypted later. After the NIST post-quantum cryptography standards, the deployment task has shifted from algorithm selection to protocol migration.
**Approach:** This paper reviews ML-KEM hybrid key exchange in TLS and SSH through a standards-first narrative review and protocol comparison. The paper develops a deployment-readiness framework with four layers: security continuity, protocol integration, operational observability, and crypto-agility.
