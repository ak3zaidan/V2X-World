# System Modeling and Performance Evaluation of Predictive QoS for Future Tele-Operated Driving
Hendrik Schippers, Cedrik Schuler, Benjamin Sliwa, Christian Wietfeld · 2022 IEEE International Systems Conference (SysCon) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/syscon53536.2022.9773810 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Tele-operated driving needs guaranteed data rates and latency.
**Approach:** ToD system model, 5G NSA measurement campaign, REM-based end-to-end data-rate prediction with individually optimised REM layers, scaled cyber-physical testbed.
**Tools and scale:** 5G NSA measurements; scaled vehicle platform.
**Results:** Improved prediction accuracy with optimised REM layers (numbers not extracted).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- No ready-proven ToD application available at large scale.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
