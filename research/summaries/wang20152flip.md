# 2FLIP: A Two-Factor Lightweight Privacy-Preserving Authentication Scheme for VANET
Fei Wang, Yongjun Xu, Hanwen Zhang, Yujun Zhang et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2015 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2015.2402166 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Efficient privacy-preserving authentication with flexible revocation.
**Approach:** 2FLIP: decentralised CA and biological-password two-factor auth; hash and MAC based signing.
**Tools and scale:** IEEE TVT; analysis.
**Results:** Computation cost reduced 100-1000x; communication overhead down 55.24-77.52%; no CRL overhead on vehicles.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — CRL overhead alternative.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
