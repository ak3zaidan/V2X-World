# Multi-Layered Explainable AI for Handover Management in 5G and Beyond Networks
Abdullah Veli Özcan, Müge Erel-Özçevı̇k, Elif Bozkaya-Aras et al. · 2026 IEEE International Conference on Communications Workshops (ICC Workshops) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccworkshops63917.2026.11586441 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The latest developments in 5G and beyond networks have the potential to provide a wide range of new services.
**Approach:** Therefore, in this paper, we propose a multi-layer explainable AI (XAI) framework for 5G and beyond networks to predict spatio-temporal peaks and handover triggers. We present a multi-layered deep learning model by combining temporal features of Long Short Term Memory (LSTM) network and static attributes in a Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP).
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: 5G, deep learning, LSTM.
**Results:** This can be achieved with a QoS prediction and "what-if" analysis in handover management. In the simulation results, the number of critical latency peaks and total mobility interruption time are reduced by 45.08% and 20.95%, respectively.
