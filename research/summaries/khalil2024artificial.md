# Artificial Intelligence-based intrusion detection system for V2V communication in vehicular adhoc networks
Abizar Khalil, Haleem Farman, Moustafa M. Nasralla, Bilal Jan, Jamil Ahmad · Ain Shams Engineering Journal 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.asej.2023.102616 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Adhoc Networks (VANETs) enable vehicle-infrastructure communication, enhancing Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS). Vehicles interconnect wirelessly to share information.
**Approach:** This study proposes an Artificial Intelligence-based Intrusion Detection System (IDS) in an edge-envisioned environment, combining edge computing and deep learning.
**Tools and scale:** This poses a challenge since regular traffic samples are typically more prevalent than anomalous traffic, and the dataset is unstructured and highly imbalanced.
**Results:** Experimental results demonstrate superior performance on the NSL-KDD dataset compared to similar methods.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- This poses a challenge since regular traffic samples are typically more prevalent than anomalous traffic, and the dataset is unstructured and highly imbalanced.
- Our BiGAN-based model overcomes synchronization challenges between generator and discriminator networks.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — V2X/VANET attack or intrusion detection; overlaps our misbehaviour-detection component only at the detector level, without SCMS reporting, revocation or closed-loop safety measurement.
**Novelty implications:** Does not rule out any candidate direction; it does not model SCMS certificates, linkage-based revocation, per-message joint ground truth or counterfactual reruns.
