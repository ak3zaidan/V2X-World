# Ensemble Miscellaneous Classifiers Based Misbehavior Detection Model for Vehicular Ad-Hoc Network Security
S. Sumithra, R. Vadivel · International Journal of Computer Networks and Applications 2021 · https://doi.org/10.22247/ijcna/2021/208890 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** Illusion-type attacks manipulate shared mobility data; existing MBD fails under dynamic conditions with high false positives.
**Approach:** MVC-MDM and ensemble EMVC-MDM: context features, Hampel-filter context reference, random-forest ensemble of supervised and unsupervised classifiers.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation-based evaluation; details not in extracted text.
**Results:** Average more than 15% better than existing MDS (as stated).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: replace supervised methods with deep learning and ANNs.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Data-centric MBD.
**Novelty implications:** None beyond ensemble MBD.
