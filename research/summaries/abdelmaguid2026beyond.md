# Beyond the Attack: Strategies for Attack Endgame Mitigation in VANETs
Mohammed A. Abdelmaguid, Hossam S. Hassanein, Mohammad Zulkernine · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2025.3624720 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs), neutral-izing attack effects is essential for safeguarding human safety and maintaining network performance.
**Approach:** This paper proposes new strategies to preempt and neutralize attack endgames, focusing on Fake Reporting Attacks that generate deceptive messages leading to hazardous and life-threatening situations. The frequency and severity of such attacks underscore the need for focused research.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: VEREMI, VeReMi
**Results:** Experimental findings indicate a reduction in hazardous events by over 40% and a threefold increase in vehicular speed recovery, demonstrating the efficacy of the proposed solution in strengthening VANET security and safety.
