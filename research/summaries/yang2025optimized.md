# Optimized Adversarial Tactics for Disrupting Cooperative Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning
Guangze Yang, Xinyuan Miao, Yabin Peng, Wei Huang et al. · Electronics 2025 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics14142777 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Multi-agent reinforcement learning has demonstrated excellent performance in complex decision-making tasks such as electronic games, power grid management, and autonomous driving.
**Approach:** This paper aims to bridge the gap by proposing a heuristic exploration-based attack method named the Search for Key steps and Key agents Attack (SKKA).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Currently, research on adversarial attacks in reinforcement learning primarily focuses on single-agent scenarios, while studies in multi-agent settings are relatively limited, especially regarding how to achieve optimized attacks with fewer steps. Experimental results demonstrate that our attack method ensures maximum attack effectiveness while greatly reducing the number of attack steps, thereby improving attack efficiency.
