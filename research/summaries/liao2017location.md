# Towards Location and Trajectory Privacy Preservation in 5G Vehicular Social Network
Dan Liao, Gang Sun, Ming Zhang, Victor Chang et al. · 22017 IEEE International Conference on Computational Science and Engineering (CSE) and IEEE International Conference on Embedded and Ubiquitous Computing (EUC) 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cse-euc.2017.197 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** 5G-based Vehicular Social Network (VSN) fiercely calls for an advanced requirement of vehicle's location and trajectory privacy preserving scheme.
**Approach:** Since the VSN owns the characteristics of high mobility and multiple hops relay, we first construct an architecture of 5G-based VSN with the Mobile Femtocell (MFemtocell). Then we propose the Dynamic Group Division algorithm (DGD), which is suitable for the dynamic property of 5G and meets the real-time demand of VSN.
