# Revolutionizing Wireless Modeling and Simulation with Network-Oriented LLMs
Jiewen Liu, Zhiyuan Peng, Dongkuan Xu, Yuchen Liu · 2024 IEEE International Performance, Computing, and Communications Conference (IPCCC) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ipccc59868.2024.10850042 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Network simulators require substantial domain knowledge.
**Approach:** Network-oriented LLM (parameter-efficient fine-tuning plus RAG) mediating natural-language simulation with Sionna.
**Tools and scale:** Sionna; state-of-the-art LLMs.
**Results:** Effective adaptation; improved operational efficiency (no numbers).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - LLM as natural-language interface to a simulator, like our LLM agent.
**Novelty implications:** LLM front-ends for simulators exist; our novelty cannot be the interface alone.
