# Change Point Models for Real-time V2I Cyber Attack Detection in a Connected Vehicle Environment
Gurcan Comert, Mizanur Rahman, Mhafuzul Islam, Mashrur Chowdhury · arXiv.org 2018 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.12620 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Connected vehicle (CV) systems are cognizant of potential cyber attacks because of increasing connectivity between its different components such as vehicles, roadside infrastructure and traffic management centers.
**Approach:** However, it is a challenge to detect security threats in real-time and develop appropriate/effective countermeasures for a CV system because of the dynamic behavior of such attacks, high computational power requirement and a historical data requirement for training detection models. To address these challenges, statistical models, especially change point models, have potentials for real-time anomaly detections.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Results from numerical analysis revealed that EM and CUSUM could detect these cyber attacks, DOS, impersonation, and false information, with an accuracy of 99\%, 100\%, and 98\%, and 100\%, 100\% and 98\%, respectively.
