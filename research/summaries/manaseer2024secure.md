# A Secure Blockchain-Based Vehicular Collision Avoidance Protocol: Detecting and Preventing Blackhole Attacks
Mosab Manaseer, Maram Bani Younes · Computer Systems Science and Engineering 2024 · https://doi.org/10.32604/csse.2024.055128 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Blackhole attacks on collision-avoidance protocols.
**Approach:** Trusted AODV with promiscuous-mode trust scores and a private PBFT blockchain mined by RSUs.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation of AODV-based collision avoidance.
**Results:** Improved PDR and throughput after removing blackhole nodes.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Investigate other attacks and adapt the protocol to them.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — attack effect on a safety application.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
