# Design of a Trust-Based Authentication Scheme for Blockchain-Enabled IoV System
Debashis Das, Sourav Banerjee, Pushpita Chatterjee, Uttam Ghosh et al. · 2023 Advances in Science and Engineering Technology International Conferences (ASET) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/aset56582.2023.10180814 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Internet of Vehicles (IoV) has become a remarkable vehicular communication network for exchanging information between vehicles and other infrastructures. It plays a significant role in the Intelligent Transport System (ITS) by improving vehicle safety and traffic efficiency.
**Approach:** Many researchers proposed various blockchain-enabled IoV applications to enhance trust in the IoV network. This paper presents a review of existing Blockchain-enabled IoV systems and analyzes them.
