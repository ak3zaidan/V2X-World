# Certificate Revocation Scheme using MOT Protocol over T-DMB Infrastructure
Hyun-Gon Kim, Min-Soo Kim, Seok-Won Jung, Jae-Hyun Seo · Journal of Korea Multimedia Society 2011 · https://doi.org/10.9717/kmms.2011.14.12.1583 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A Certificate Revocation List(CRL) should be distributed quickly to all the vehicles for vehicular communications to protect them from malicious users and malfunctioning equipment as well as to increase the overall security and safety of vehicular networks.
**Approach:** Thus, a major challenge in vehicular networks is how to efficiently distribute CRLs. This paper proposes a Multimedia Object Transfer(MOT) protocol based on CRL distribution scheme over T-DMB infrastructure.
