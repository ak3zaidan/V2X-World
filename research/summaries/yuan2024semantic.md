# A Semantic-Driven Data Generative Traffic Modeling Method for Digital Twin Networks
Hongye Yuan, Xin Yan, Chunlin Li · 2024 International Conference on Engineering and Emerging Technologies (ICEET) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iceet65156.2024.10913930 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Digital twin networks struggle with accuracy, efficiency and communication cost.
**Approach:** Semantic extraction from real data feeds Mininet; a Transformer diffusion model (DiffNTG) refines simulated traffic.
**Tools and scale:** Mininet plus diffusion model; real network data.
**Results:** Error between simulated and real reduced by up to 96%; compute cost reduced about 83%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — wired traffic generation.
**Novelty implications:** Generative refinement of simulator output exists generically; not for V2X ground truth.
