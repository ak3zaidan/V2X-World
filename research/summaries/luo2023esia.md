# ESIA: An Efficient and Stable Identity Authentication for Internet of Vehicles
Hao-Xiang Luo, Jin Zhang, Xinling Li, Zonghang Li et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2023.3330547 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Decentralized, tamper-proof blockchain is regarded as a solution to a challenging authentication issue in the Internet of Vehicles (IoVs).
**Approach:** To address this issue, vehicular fog computing has been introduced to improve efficiency. With the above considerations, we propose an efficient and stable identity authentication (ESIA) empowered by hierarchical blockchain and fog computing.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** If the composition of vehicular fog is unstable, the blockchain formed by this fog computing system will be unstable, which can affect the consensus efficiency. By grouping vehicles efficiently, ESIA has low communication complexity and achieves high stability.
