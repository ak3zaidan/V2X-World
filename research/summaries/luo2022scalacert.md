# ScalaCert: Scalability-Oriented PKI with Redactable Consortium Blockchain Enabled "On-Cert" Certificate Revocation
Xinyi Luo, Zhuo Xu, Kaiping Xue et al. · 2022 IEEE 42nd International Conference on Distributed Computing Systems (ICDCS) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icdcs54860.2022.00121 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As the voucher for identity, digital certificates and the public key infrastructure (PKI) system have always played a vital role to provide the authentication services.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose ScalaCert to mitigate the scalability problems of blockchain-based PKIs by utilizing redactable blockchain for "on-cert" revocation.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Specifically, we utilize the redactable blockchain to record revocation information directly on the original certificate ("on-cert") and remove additional data structures such as CRL, significantly reducing storage overhead.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Although blockchain enhances security, it brings new problems in scalability due to the inherent limitations of blockchain’s data structure and consensus mechanism, which become much severe for the massive access in the era of 5G and B5G.
