# SlotSwap: strong and affordable location privacy in intelligent transportation systems
David Eckhoff, Reinhard German, Christoph Sommer, Falko Dressler et al. · IEEE Communications Magazine 2011 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mcom.2011.6069719 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Public acceptance, and thus the economic success, of an ITS is highly dependent on the quality of deployed privacy mechanisms.
**Approach:** In general, neither users nor operators should be able to track a given individual. One approach to facilitate this is the usage of pseudonym pools, which allow vehicles to autonomously switch between different identities.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: location privacy).
**Results:** We extend this scheme with that of a time-slotted pseudonym pool of static size, reducing the storage and computation needs of the envisioned ITS while further improving users' privacy.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- We support the exchange of both current and future pseudonyms, further enhancing users' privacy.
