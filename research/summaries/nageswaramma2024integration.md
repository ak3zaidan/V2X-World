# Integration of Quantum-Inspired Evolutionary Algorithms for Enhanced Mitigation of Complex Gray Hole Attacks in VANETs
Thelidela Nageswaramma · International Journal of Intelligent Systems and Applications in Engineering 2024 · https://ijisae.org/index.php/IJISAE/article/view/7195 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks (VANETs) play a pivotal role in Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS), facilitating real-time communication among vehicles and roadside units to enhance traffic management and road safety. However, VANETs are susceptible to gray hole attacks, where malicious nodes selectively drop packets, undermining network reliability.
**Approach:** This study proposes a novel hybrid methodology integrating Quantum-Inspired Particle Swarm Optimization (QPSO) and Blockchain-Assisted Trust Mechanism (BATM) to detect and mitigate gray hole attacks effectively. The proposed method achieved a Packet Delivery Ratio (PDR) of 94.8%, surpassing the benchmarks set by Abdul Malik et al. (90.3%) and Rini & Meena (91.2%).
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting (VANET/IoV/V2X). Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** Throughput increased to 238.7 kbps, compared to 230.0 kbps and 225.3 kbps reported in recent studies. Furthermore, the end-to-end delay was reduced to 112.4 ms , significantly lower than 120.3 ms and 118.7 ms achieved by previous methods.
