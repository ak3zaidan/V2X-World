# CritBench: A Framework for Evaluating Cybersecurity Capabilities of Large Language Models in IEC 61850 Digital Substation Environments
Gustav Keppler, Moritz Gstür, Veit Hagenmeyer · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06019 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Evaluating LLM cyber capability in OT substations.
**Approach:** CritBench: 81 IEC 61850 tasks with tool scaffold.
**Tools and scale:** Five models incl. GPT-5.
**Results:** Good at static analysis, degraded on dynamic tasks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Performance degrades on dynamic tasks.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
