# A Digital Signature Architecture Suitable for V2V Applications
H. Mosanaei-Boorani, Siavash Bayat-Sarmadi · IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems Part 1: Regular Papers 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tcsi.2023.3337121 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ECDSA latency in congested V2V.
**Approach:** High-throughput ECDSA P-256 FPGA with double point multiplication and new multiplier.
**Tools and scale:** Virtex-7 FPGA.
**Results:** Throughput at least 4.9x and efficiency 7.2x better; signature in 167 us.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial: verification latency is a parameter in engine.
**Novelty implications:** Hardware ECDSA timings can parameterise verification load models.
