# Towards Cooperative Data Rate Prediction for Future Mobile and Vehicular 6G Networks
Benjamin Sliwa, Robert Falkenberg, Christian Wietfeld · 2020 2nd 6G Wireless Summit (6G SUMMIT) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/6gsummit49458.2020.9083767 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** UE-only data-rate prediction is limited because the UE does not know cell load.
**Approach:** Cooperative prediction combining UE channel indicators with network load from the FALCON SDR control-channel sniffer.
**Tools and scale:** Real-world LTE drive tests on a campus with a three-sector cell; ANN, RF, M5, SVM regressors.
**Results:** Average prediction error reduced by up to 30% (25% uplink).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Argues future 6G networks should provide load information to UEs; evaluation limited to one campus cell.
**Overlap with our engine:** none - cellular data-rate prediction.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
