# Context-Aware Intrusion Detection in Vehicular Communication Networks: Enhanced Attack Modeling and Dataset
Muhammad Danish Khan, Vinh-Thong Ta, Husnain Rafiq, Nonso Nnamoko · Applied Artificial Intelligence 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1080/08839514.2025.2538453 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Approach:** Attack simulation framework for spoofing, Sybil and wormhole with mobility; dataset.
**Tools and scale:** Dataset: spoofing 45,975/589, wormhole 52,237/5,219, Sybil 14,829/1,753.
