# Grouping Sensors for the Key Distribution of Implicit Certificates in Wireless Sensor Networks
Ray-I Chang, Chien-Wen Chiang, Yu-Hsin Hung · Electronics 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics12132815 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As sensor nodes communicate via wireless channels, information security is essential for wireless sensor networks.
**Approach:** This study developed the bi-directed grouping (top-down grouping (TDG) and bottom-up grouping (BUG)) methods.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** For a network with maximum group members of 50 (total sensor nodes = 1000), compared with TDG and BUG, ABG reduced the number of encryption and decryption operations by ~36%.
