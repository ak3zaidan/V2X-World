# An Empirical Testing of Autonomous Vehicle Simulator System for Urban Driving
John Seymour, Dac-Thanh-Chuong Ho, Quang-Hung Luu · 2021 IEEE International Conference on Artificial Intelligence Testing (AITest) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/aitest52744.2021.00031 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Safety is one of the main challenges that prohibit autonomous vehicles (AV), requiring them to be well tested ahead of being allowed on the road.
**Approach:** We propose 576 test cases which are inspired by four naturalistic driving situations with pedestrians and surrounding cars. In summary, this paper provides a new empirical evidence to strengthen the assertion that the simulator-based system can be an indispensable tool for a comprehensive testing of the AV.
