# Authentication Protocol for Secure Automotive Systems: Benchmarking Post-Quantum Cryptography
Prasanna Ravi, Vijaya Kumar Sundar, Anupam Chattopadhyay, Shivam Bhasin et al. · 2020 IEEE International Symposium on Circuits and Systems (ISCAS) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iscas45731.2020.9180847 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Automotive network authentication protocols rely on pre-quantum public-key crypto.
**Approach:** First practical implementation of an automotive authentication protocol with NIST PQC candidates, compared to pre-quantum counterparts.
**Tools and scale:** Embedded implementation; speed and bandwidth benchmarks.
**Results:** Comparative speed/bandwidth evaluation; no numbers in abstract.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — PQC benchmarking for automotive authentication (in-vehicle), not V2X SCMS.
