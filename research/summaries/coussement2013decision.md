# Decision support protocol for intrusion detection in VANETs
Romain Coussement, Boucif Amar Bensaber, Ismaïl Biskri · Design and Analysis of Intelligent Vehicular Networks and Applications 2013 · https://doi.org/10.1145/2512921.2512928 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANETs) are so difficult to secure due to the wireless technology and its several known security holes. To protect against attacks, methods and techniques have been developed.
**Approach:** This paper designs a decision making protocol for security information in VANETs. Our aim is to design a decision support mechanism.
