# TDMRP: A Transparent and Decentralized Management of Reputation and Pseudonym in IoV
Fei Tong, Hai-Tao Yang, Fang-Yuan Xing, Shi-Bo He et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2026.3665926 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The development of Internet of vehicle (IoV) enables vehicles to share road condition data, while the honesty and anonymity of vehicles have become the core issues. The effective solutions to these two issues involving reputation and pseudonym management engender a dilemma.
**Approach:** To this end, we propose a blockchain-based transparent and decentralized management of reputation and pseudonym (referred to as TDMRP), which can reduce the reputation loss caused by the conventional cloak mechanism and protect privacy of identity. To reduce the reputation loss, we propose a dynamic cloak algorithm, which is proved to achieve the theoretically minimal reputation loss.
