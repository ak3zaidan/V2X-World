# Location Verification Systems for VANETs in Rician Fading Channels
Shihao Yan, R. Malaney, I. Nevat, G. Peters · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2014 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2015.2453160 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Detecting location spoofing in Rician fading.
**Approach:** Multi-antenna base-station location verification; optimal attack and detection analysis with tracking.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical.
**Results:** LVS limit independent of attacker channel above an antenna count.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Extendable to multiple BS.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - PHY-based location verification (MBD input).
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
