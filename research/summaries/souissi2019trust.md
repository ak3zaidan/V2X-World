# Trust management in vehicular ad hoc networks: a survey
Ilhem Souissi, Nadia Ben Azzouna, Tahar Berradia · International Journal of Ad Hoc and Ubiquitous Computing 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1504/ijahuc.2019.10022743 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) provide a variety of applications that aim to ensure a safe and comfort driving experience. These applications rely on the communication and the exchange of data between vehicles.
**Approach:** In this paper, we survey the recent advances in trust management for VANETs. The aim of this paper is to show the importance of an adaptive trust model that can deal with the requirements of each class of applications.
