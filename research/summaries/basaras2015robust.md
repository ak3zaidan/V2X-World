# A Robust Eco-Routing Protocol against Malicious Data in Vehicular Networks
Pavlos Basaras, Leandros A. Maglaras, Dimitrios Katsaros, Helge Janicke · IFIP Wireless and Mobile Networking Conference (WMNC) 2015 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wmnc.2015.27 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular networks have a diverse range of applications that vary from safety, to traffic management and comfort. Vehicular communications (VC) can assist in the ecorouting of vehicles in order to reduce the overall mileage and CO2 emissions by the exchange of data among vehicle-entities.
**Approach:** In this article we investigate how tweaked information originating from malicious nodes can affect the performance of a real time eco-routing mechanism that uses Dedicated Short Ranged Communications (DSRC), namely Erou Ve. We improve the routing decision mechanism of the original algorithm and also develop and evaluate defense mechanisms that exploit vehicular communications in order to filter out tweaked data.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting (VANET/IoV/V2X). Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** Vehicular communications (VC) can assist in the ecorouting of vehicles in order to reduce the overall mileage and CO2 emissions by the exchange of data among vehicle-entities.
