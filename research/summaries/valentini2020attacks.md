# An Attacks Detection Mechanism for Intelligent Transport System
Edivaldo Pastori Valentini, Rodolfo I. Meneguette, Adil Alsuhaim · IEEE International Conference on Big Data (BigData) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/bigdata50022.2020.9378309 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The increase in computational technologies for means of transport, especially vehicles, has provided great benefits through Intelligent Transport Systems (ITS). Drivers, passengers, and pedestrians rely on computer applications that aim to protect human life, including agility in handling emergencies, improvements in traffic, and even leisure and entertainment resources.
**Approach:** Prioritizing the safety of life and protecting VANET against this type of attack, a security mechanism is proposed.
**Tools and scale:** NS-3, SUMO, Car-Hacking/CAN dataset, IEEE 802.11p/DSRC (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** Through extensive stages of simulations using the NS-3 and SUMO simulators, the mechanism showed excellent efficiency in detection rates and minimum rates of false positives and false negatives.
