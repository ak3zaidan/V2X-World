# An Efficient Multiparty Threshold ECDSA Protocol against Malicious Adversaries for Blockchain‐Based LLMs
Jing Wang, Xue Yuan, Yingjie Xu, Yudi Zhang · IET Information Security 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1049/2024/2252865 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** However, concerns over management burden and data security have grown alongside their capabilities.
**Approach:** To solve the problem, we design a blockchain‐based distributed LLM framework, where LLM works in the distributed mode and its outputs can be stored and verified on a blockchain to ensure integrity, transparency, and traceability. To address these requirements, we propose a threshold elliptic curve digital signature algorithm that counters malicious adversaries in environments with three or more participants.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** Our approach relies on discrete logarithmic zero‐knowledge proofs and Feldman verifiable secret sharing, reducing complexity by forgoing multiplication triple protocols.
