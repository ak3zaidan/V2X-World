# SECMACE: Scalable and Robust Identity and Credential Management Infrastructure in Vehicular Communication Systems
Mohammad Khodaei, Hongyu Jin, Panos Papadimitratos · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2017.2722688 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Deep understanding of deploying a VPKI is lacking despite 1609.2/ETSI standardisation.
**Approach:** SECMACE: VPKI compatible with IEEE 1609.2 and ETSI, multi-domain operation, timing-unlinkable pseudonym issuance, protection against honest-but-curious entities, Sybil prevention, availability against DDoS; multiple vehicle-VPKI interaction policies.
**Tools and scale:** Implemented VPKI with performance evaluation (per paper); requirements R1-R7.
**Results:** Efficient pseudonym provisioning at scale (numbers not extracted).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in extracted sections.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong - implements and evaluates a standards-compatible VPKI with pseudonym provisioning, separation of duties and revocation, the backend role our SCMS plays; European-style, not the US SCMS with butterfly keys, and not coupled to a traffic/radio simulation.
**Novelty implications:** We cannot claim first implemented standards-compatible VPKI. Open: US SCMS with butterfly keys and linkage revocation executed inside a city-scale traffic/radio simulation.
