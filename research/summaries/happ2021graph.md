# Graph-neural-network-based delay estimation for communication networks with heterogeneous scheduling policies
Martin Happ, M. Herlich, Christian Maier, Jia-Lei Du, Peter Dorfinger · ITU Journal on Future and Evolving Technologies 2021 · https://doi.org/10.52953/tejx5530 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** RouteNet assumes one scheduling policy.
**Approach:** Extension supporting strict priority, DRR and WFQ mixed in one network.
**Tools and scale:** GNN Challenge dataset.
**Results:** Per-path delay MAPE about 0.897%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - network surrogate.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
