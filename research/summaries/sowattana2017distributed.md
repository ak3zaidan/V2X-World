# Distributed consensus-based Sybil nodes detection in VANETs
Chea Sowattana, Wantanee Viriyasitavat, Assadarat Khurat · International Joint Conference on Computer Science and Software Engineering 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jcsse.2017.8025908 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil detection without infrastructure.
**Approach:** Neighbour info exchanged in beacons; vote on nodes positioned in an intersection area but unacknowledged.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Detection rate rises with more neighbours.
