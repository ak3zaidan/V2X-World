# Dual-Signature Blockchain-Based Key Sharing Protocol for Secure V2V Communications in Multi-Domain IoV Environments
Dariush Abbasinezhad-Mood, Hadi Ghaemi · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2024.3410114 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle to vehicle (V2V) communications can facilitate traffic congestion control, reduce crashes, and enhance general safety.
**Approach:** To this end, numerous key sharing protocols have been devised and proposed in recent years. In the proposed protocol, we utilize the blockchain and an inventive dual-signature approach.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: blockchain.
**Results:** The former eases cross-domain authentication and the latter reduces the communication rounds.
