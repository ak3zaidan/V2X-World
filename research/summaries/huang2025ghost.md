# Ghost Vehicle: A Game-Theoretical Attack Strategy Targeting CAV Platoons
Lanai Huang, Winston Ellis, Sana Belguith · IEEE International Conference on Trust, Security and Privacy in Computing and Communications (TrustCom) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/trustcom66490.2025.00310 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sophisticated ghost-vehicle attacks on CAV platoons.
**Approach:** Game-theoretic attack framework with MPC and FSM controlling a ghost vehicle in leader, mid-platoon and follower modes.
**Tools and scale:** Highway scenario simulation.
**Results:** Destabilises platoon in leader and mid attacks; blends in as follower; max computation 40 s average.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — attack strategy with traffic impact.
**Novelty implications:** Adaptive attacker strategies exist for platoons.
