# Sybil Attack Detection in Internet of Flying Things-IoFT: A Machine Learning Approach
Donpiti Chulerttiyawong, Abbas Jamalipour · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2023.3257848 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil attacks in FANET-based Internet of Flying Things.
**Approach:** Supervised ML on RSSD and TDoA of UAV signals at two ground nodes (Weka classifiers). (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Above 91% average accuracy, including power-controlling attackers at untrained power levels.
