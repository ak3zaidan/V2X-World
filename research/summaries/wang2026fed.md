# Fed-EALE: Efficient Authentication With Lightweight Encryption for Federated Learning in Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks Using SSI
Ping Wang, Fei Tang, An-Kui Jing, Lei Liu et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2025.3626732 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANET), federated learning enables vehicles to collaboratively train global models for intelligent transportation without sharing raw data. However, global model training faces various potential risks, such as identity leakage, privacy inference, and malicious attacks, due to the dynamic network structure and untrusted wireless communication of VANET.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose an efficient and privacy-preserving authentication scheme with lightweight encryption for federated learning in VANET using self-sovereign identity (SSI), called Fed-EALE. We perform a security analysis of Fed-EALE.

**Results:** To address these issues, a robust authentication mechanism for federated learning must be achieved to ensure the trustworthiness of model parameters. Vehicle participants use unlinkable pseudonyms to achieve privacy protection.
