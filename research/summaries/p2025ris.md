# RIS-Assisted Full Duplex MAC Protocol for NR-V2X with ML-Driven QoS Prediction
Indulekha K. P., T. Venkatesh · 2025 IEEE Future Networks World Forum (FNWF) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/fnwf66845.2025.11317280 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Full-duplex NR-V2X suffers interference and blind spots.
**Approach:** RIS-assisted FD MAC augmenting SB-SPS with ML predictions of PRR and CBR; DTMC reliability model.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Favourable PRR vs blind-spot trade-off.
