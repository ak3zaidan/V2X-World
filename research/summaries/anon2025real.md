# Real-time Sybil Attack Detection Based on Channel Characterization in VANET
, Reham Almesaeed · International Journal of Computer Network and Information Security 2025 · https://doi.org/10.5815/ijcnis.2025.04.05 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Sybil detection in dense V2V.
**Approach:** Channel characterisation with RSSI plus azimuth and elevation angular spread.
**Tools and scale:** Ray-tracing-based channel model.
**Results:** Higher detection and lower false detection than RSSI-only across densities.
