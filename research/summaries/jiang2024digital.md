# Digital Twin Assisted Beamforming Design for Integrated Sensing and Communication Systems
Shuai-Feng Jiang, Ahmed Alkhateeb · Asilomar Conference on Signals, Systems and Computers 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ieeeconf60004.2024.10942924 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper explores a novel research direction where a digital twin is leveraged to assist the beamforming design for an integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) system.
**Approach:** In this setup, a base station designs joint communication and sensing beamforming to serve the communication user and detect the sensing target concurrently. Utilizing the electromagnetic (EM) 3D model of the environment and ray tracing, the digital twin can provide various information, e.g., propagation path parameters and wireless channels, to aid communication and sensing systems.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Simulation results show that the proposed digital twin-assisted beamforming design achieves near-optimal target sensing SNR in both LoS and NLoS dominant areas, while ensuring the required SINR for the communication user.
