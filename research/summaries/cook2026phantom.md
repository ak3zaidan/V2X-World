# Phantom Jam Sybil Attack Against Vehicular Networks
Colby Cook, Ahmed Mohamed, Mengjun Xie · 2026 International Conference on Intelligent Multimedia, Networking, and Security (IMNS) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/imns67862.2026.11655306 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Motion-consistent Sybil attacks evading MBD.
**Approach:** Phantom Jam: map-aware trajectory replay plus TimeGAN braking/acceleration dynamics for ghost vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** F2MD with LuST Nano; deep-learning MDS benchmark.
**Results:** Recall reduced to as low as 0.66.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — adaptive attacks in the loop of an MBD simulator (F2MD).
**Novelty implications:** Generative evasive attacks exist; their effect on revocation latency remains open.
