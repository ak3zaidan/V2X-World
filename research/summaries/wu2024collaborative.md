# Collaborative Attack Sequence Generation Model Based on Multiagent Reinforcement Learning for Intelligent Traffic Signal System
Yalun Wu, Yingxiao Xiang, Thar Baker, Endong Tong et al. · International Journal of Intelligent Systems 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2024/4734030 · relevance: high · basis: abstract only

**Problem:** Connected-vehicle-based intelligent traffic signal systems (I-SIG type) are vulnerable to data spoofing that causes congestion; the paper studies coordinated multi-vehicle spoofing.
**Approach:** Models single- and multi-intersection spoofing as a POMDP and generates collaborative, stealthy attack sequences with the MAAC multi-agent RL algorithm.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Experiments at multiple intersections show efficient and stealthy collaborative attacks (exact numbers truncated in the abstract).
