# On Scalable Multi-flow and Multi-channel Traffic Steering through Hybrid Learning
Xiaotian Li, Yong Liu, Shivendra Panwar et al. · ACM SIGMETRICS Performance Evaluation Review 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3764944.3764954 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Multi-flow and multi-channel tra!c steering cannot be accurately optimized using the traditional queueing models due to highly dynamic tra!c and channel characteristics.
**Approach:** We propose a hybrid learning approach to per-flow tra!c steering, where a base-level controller generates a base tra!c splitting policy using the measured channel delays and a simplified queueing model, and the base policy is further enhanced by a reinforcement learning agent to control the actual tra!c steering.
