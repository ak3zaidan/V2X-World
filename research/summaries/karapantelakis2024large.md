# Using Large Language Models to Understand Telecom Standards
Athanasios Karapantelakis, Mukesh Thakur, Alexandros Nikou, Farnaz Moradi et al. · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02929 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Accessing 3GPP standards is hard.
**Approach:** Benchmark of LLMs as 3GPP QA assistants; fine-tuning guidelines; TeleRoBERTa.
**Tools and scale:** 3GPP documents.
**Results:** LLMs credible as reference tools; TeleRoBERTa on par with an order of magnitude fewer parameters.
