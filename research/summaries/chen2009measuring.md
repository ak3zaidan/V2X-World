# Measuring the performance of movement‐assisted certificate revocation list distribution in VANET
Jiming Chen, Xianghui Cao, Yu Zhang, Weiqiang Xu et al. · Wireless Communications and Mobile Computing 2009 · https://doi.org/10.1002/wcm.858 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Distributing CRLs to areas without RSUs.
**Approach:** Movement-assisted CRL distribution via public-safety mobile nodes with optimal routes under delay and cost.
**Tools and scale:** Large-scale experiments.
**Results:** Movement-assisted approach clearly improves CRL distribution performance.
