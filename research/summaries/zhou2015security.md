# A security authentication method based on trust evaluation in VANETs
Ao Zhou, Jinglin Li, Qibo Sun, Cunqun Fan, Tao Lei, Fangchun Yang · EURASIP Journal on Wireless Communications and Networking 2015 · https://doi.org/10.1186/s13638-015-0257-x · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) have a high degree of openness. Therefore, if a new vehicle node wants to access the network, we need to validate the vehicle node carefully to ensure the security of the entire networking.
**Approach:** To attack this challenge, this paper proposes a security authentication method based on trust evaluation.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Simulation results show the advantage of our proposed method.

- None stated in the abstract.
