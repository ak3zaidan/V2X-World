# Performance Comparison of Machine Learning and Federated Learning for Intrusion Detection in Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks
Tim Leonhardt, Ahmed Sherif, Mohamed Elsersy · 2025 IEEE 4th International Conference on Computing and Machine Intelligence (ICMI) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icmi65310.2025.11141279 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The proliferation of connected autonomous vehicles (CAVs) underscores the necessity for cybersecurity protocols.
**Approach:** Cyberattacks on CAVs may result in data breaches and the manipulation of vehicle control, potentially leading to accidents or safety risks. Federated learning (FL) decentralizes machine learning (ML) by training remote-device models while maintaining data locality.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The results reveal that strong centralized ML performance does not always translate to FL.
