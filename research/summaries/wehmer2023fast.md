# The Fast Rise of Cautious Vehicle-to-X: Towards Evaluating Misbehavior Detection in the Field
Marek Wehmer, Ingmar Baumgart · 2023 IEEE 26th International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/itsc57777.2023.10422080 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Quantifying the real-world safety effect of deployed misbehaviour detection.
**Approach:** Analyses German vehicle registrations (2020-2022) and motorway measurements showing V2X ubiquity; proposes causal-inference guidance for observational field studies of MBD effectiveness.
**Tools and scale:** Real registration data and motorway V2X measurements; causal inference framework.
**Results:** V2X-equipped vehicles show a market breakthrough; V2X must be treated as ubiquitous.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Quantification of MBD effect in real systems is 'still elusive'; the causal design is a proposal, not executed.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — causal evaluation of MBD impact on road safety matches directions 1 and 3 (safety effect of detection, counterfactual evaluation).
**Novelty implications:** We cannot claim to be first to frame MBD effectiveness causally; we can claim simulated exact counterfactuals and closed-loop revocation, which they do not do.
