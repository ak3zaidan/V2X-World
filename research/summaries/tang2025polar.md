# POLAR: Automating Cyber Threat Prioritization through LLM-Powered Assessment
Luoxi Tang, Yuqiao Meng, Ankita Patra, Weicheng Ma et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01552 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** LLM vulnerabilities in CTI.
**Approach:** Failure categorisation with stratification and human-in-the-loop.
**Tools and scale:** Multiple CTI benchmarks and real reports.
**Results:** Three vulnerabilities: spurious correlations, contradictory knowledge, constrained generalisation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Same three vulnerabilities.

**Novelty implications:** No impact.
