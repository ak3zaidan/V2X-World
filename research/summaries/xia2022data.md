# Data Dissemination With Trajectory Privacy Protection for 6G-Oriented Vehicular Networks
Youhua Xia, Libing Wu, Xi Zheng, Tianqi Yu et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2022.3183406 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Data dissemination of vehicles is critical for vehicular networks because of the extensive impact of traffic information.
**Approach:** The existing works for data dissemination in vehicular networks mainly use data scheduling algorithms to transmit data among vehicles. However, it is challenging to meet the ultrareliable and low-latency requirements of data transmission among vehicular networks due to the intrinsic movement characteristic of vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: location privacy).
**Results:** Simulation results indicate that the proposed adaptive data dissemination algorithm is approximately 60%, 69%, and 50% better than the state-of-the-art scheduling algorithms in terms of connectivity degree, transmission delay, and average distance deviation for the vehicular network.
