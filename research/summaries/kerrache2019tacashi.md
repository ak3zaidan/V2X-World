# TACASHI: Trust-Aware Communication Architecture for Social Internet of Vehicles
Chaker Abdelaziz Kerrache, Nasreddine Lagraa, Rasheed Hussain, Syed Hassan Ahmed, Abderrahim Benslimane, Carlos T. Calafate, Juan-Carlos Cano, Anna Maria Vegni · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2018.2880332 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Internet of Vehicles (IoV) has emerged as a new spin-off research theme from traditional vehicular ad hoc networks. It employs vehicular nodes connected to other smart objects equipped with a powerful multisensor platform, communication technologies, and IP-based connectivity to the Internet, thereby creating a possible social network called Social IoV (SIoV).
**Approach:** As a result, we propose a trust-aware communication architecture for social IoV (TACASHI).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Extensive simulation results evidence the efficiency of our proposal, ensuring high detection ratios >87% and high accuracy with reduced error ratios, clearly outperforming previous proposals, known as RTM and AD-IoV.
