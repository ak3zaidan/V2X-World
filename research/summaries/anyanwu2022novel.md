# Novel hyper-tuned ensemble Random Forest algorithm for the detection of false basic safety messages in Internet of Vehicles
Goodness Oluchi Anyanwu, Cosmas Ifeanyi Nwakanma, Jae Min Lee, Dong‐Seong Kim · ICT Express 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icte.2022.06.003 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Detecting false BSMs in IoV.
**Approach:** Hyper-tuned ensemble Random Forest. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi.
**Results:** Validation accuracy 99.60%; 604 misclassifications of 153,730 points; data balancing irrelevant.
