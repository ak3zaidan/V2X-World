# Quantum Key Distribution for Secure Vehicle-To-Vehicle Communication in Autonomous Electric Vehicle Networks
Zeseya Sharmin, Tuhibur Rahman, Md. Saklain Mostak, R. M. Noor et al. · 2025 IEEE International Conference on Energy Technologies for Future Grids (ETFG) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/etfg61999.2025.11402573 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Quantum attacks on AEV V2V.
**Approach:** Mobile QKD protocol combining satellite and terrestrial QKD, predictive key pre-distribution, adaptive error correction. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Simulation at speeds above 120 km/h.
