# BlockVoke – Fast, Blockchain-Based Certificate Revocation for PKIs and the Web of Trust
Abba Garba, Arne Bochem, Benjamin Leiding · Lecture Notes in Computer Science 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-62974-8_18 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A reliable certificate revocation mechanism is crucial, as illustrated by the recent revocation of 1.7 million certificates issued by the Let’s Encrypt certificate authority.
**Approach:** To address these issues, we propose the BlockVoke scheme, which decentralizes revocations, allowing certificate owners as well as CAs to revoke certificates, and distribute revocation information rapidly.
