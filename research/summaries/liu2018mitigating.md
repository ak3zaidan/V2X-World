# Mitigating DoS Attacks Against Pseudonymous Authentication Through Puzzle-Based Co-Authentication in 5G-VANET
Puguang Liu, Bo Liu, Yipin Sun, Bao-Kang Zhao et al. · IEEE Access 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2018.2826518 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The upcoming Fifth Generation (5G) networks can provide ultra-reliable ultra-low latency vehicle-to-everything for vehicular ad hoc networks (VANET) to promote road safety, traffic management, information dissemination, and automatic driving for drivers and passengers. However, 5G-VANET also attracts tremendous security and privacy concerns.
**Approach:** Although several pseudonymous authentication schemes have been proposed for VANET, the expensive cost for their initial authentication may cause serious denial of service (DoS) attacks, which furthermore enables to do great harm to real space via VANET.
