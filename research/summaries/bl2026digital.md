# A Digital Twin Framework with Field Calibrated Propagation Modeling for Vehicular Network Applications
Sharan Karthick BL, Raja VaraPrasad Yerra, Purav Shah, Ramona Trestian, Hrishikesh Venkataraman, Deepak Gangadharan · 2026 IEEE International Conference on Communications Workshops (ICC Workshops) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccworkshops63917.2026.11586361 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular DTs need accurate propagation models.
**Approach:** Field-calibrated DT with 3D topography, vehicle telemetry and 3GPP UMa with log-normal shadowing in Unity.
**Tools and scale:** IIIT Sri City campus; LTE modem on a personal mobility vehicle.
**Results:** Correlation 0.756, RMSE 10.95 dB vs measurements.
