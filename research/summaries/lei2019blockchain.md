# A blockchain based certificate revocation scheme for vehicular communication systems
Ao Lei, Yue Cao, Shihan Bao, Dasen Li, Philip Michael Asuquo, Haitham S. Cruickshank et al. · Future Generation Computer Systems 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.future.2019.03.039 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Low-cost certificate revocation against insider attacks.
**Approach:** Blockchain-based CRL maintenance tracing pseudonym set ownership, combined with group key management; misbehaviour report mechanism.
**Tools and scale:** Simulations and analysis.
**Results:** Significantly compressed CRL size and reduced broadcast overhead.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in extracted sections.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - misbehaviour reporting plus revocation pipeline (non-standard).
**Novelty implications:** No effect on SCMS-specific claims.
