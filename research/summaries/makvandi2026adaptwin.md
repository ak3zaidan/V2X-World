# AdaPTwin: Adaptive Multi-Fidelity Predictive Digital Twin for Proactive Radio Resource Management in Vehicular Networks

**Approach:** AdaPTwin adaptive multi-fidelity predictive NDT: cloud fidelity selection, edge RRM with transformer trajectory prediction and look-ahead Sionna ray tracing.
