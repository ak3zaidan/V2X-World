# Secure Opportunistic Routing Protocol in VANETs
Eqbal Darraji, Iain Phillips, Asma Adnane · International Conference on Information Systems Security and Privacy 2025 · https://doi.org/10.5220/0013371500003899 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** VANET routing lacks mobility and trust metrics.
**Approach:** TCOR, a trusted context-aware opportunistic routing protocol with direct/indirect reputation and a similarity-based recommendation check (TCOR-Rec). (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** OMNeT++ with mobility and fading models.
**Results:** TCOR and TCOR-Rec outperform COR, AODV and trusted AODV (numbers truncated).
