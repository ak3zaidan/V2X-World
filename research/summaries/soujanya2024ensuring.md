# Ensuring Security and Privacy in VANET: A Comprehensive Survey of Authentication Approaches
B K Soujanya, Farooque Azam · Journal of Computer Networks and Communications 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2024/1818079 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc networks (VANET) are revolutionizing intelligent transportation systems (ITS), and as a result, research on their security is becoming increasingly important. As the primary security concern for VANET, authentication security is still quite difficult to achieve.
**Approach:** To set itself apart from previous surveys, this study suggests security properties and challenges among VANET. More importantly, the report on the most recent developments in VANET concentrates on the authentication schemes that have been proposed recently.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting (VANET/IoV/V2X). Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** The abstract gives no specific quantitative results.
