# Vehicle Trajectory Prediction and Collision Warning via Fusion of Multisensors and Wireless Vehicular Communications
Minjin Baek, Donggi Jeong, Dongho Choi, Sangsun Lee · Sensors 2020 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s20010288 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Driver inattention is one of the leading causes of traffic crashes worldwide.
**Approach:** Providing the driver with an early warning prior to a potential collision can significantly reduce the fatalities and level of injuries associated with vehicle collisions. In order to monitor the vehicle surroundings and predict collisions, on-board sensors such as radar, lidar, and cameras are often used.
