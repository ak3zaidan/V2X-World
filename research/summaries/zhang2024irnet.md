# IRNet: INVANETs Performance Prediction via Spatio-Temporal Graph Attention Networks
Tengwen Zhang, Tao Luo, Shaoyuan Huang, Yuting Li, Xiaofei Wang · 2024 IEEE 21st International Conference on Mobile Ad-Hoc and Smart Systems (MASS) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mass62177.2024.00025 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Affordable INVANET performance models.
**Approach:** Spatio-temporal graph attention network with topology snapshots predicting delay, jitter and drops.
**Tools and scale:** Simulated VANETs.
**Results:** 17.64% better average-case MRE than baselines.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - learned VANET surrogate (direction 3).
**Novelty implications:** VANET GNN surrogates exist; with PKI/security state they do not.
