# AI-Driven Digital Twin Framework for Predictive Maintenance in Autonomous Vehicles
Vasavi Bande, Mallareddy Adudhodla, Prasadarao Karu, M. Swapna, M. Sravani, Saavanth Veerumneni · Digital Twin Approaches in Autonomous Vehicles 2026 · https://doi.org/10.4018/979-8-3373-7785-8.ch003 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The fusion of Digital Twin (DT) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) is changing the face of maintenance practices for autonomous vehicles.
**Approach:** This chapter offers an AI-based digital twin framework for predictive maintenance to ensure timely, efficient, and reliable maintenance decisions. The framework combines various data sources related to the vehicle for continuous monitoring of the condition of the components of the vehicle to detect any signs of failure.
