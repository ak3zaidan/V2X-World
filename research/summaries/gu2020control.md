# Control Channel Anti-Jamming in Vehicular Networks via Cooperative Relay Beamforming
Pengwenlong Gu, Cunqing Hua, Wenchao Xu, Rida Khatoun, Yue Wu, Ahmed Serhrouchni · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2020.2973753 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In vehicular networks, radio-frequency (RF) jamming attacks are considered a major threat to the availability of control channel (CCH). In particular, vehicles may not be able to receive control messages from roadside units (RSUs) due to persistent interference in the CCH, which may claim human lives and result in significant economic losses.
**Approach:** In this article, a cooperative anti-jamming beamforming scheme is proposed to address the CCH jamming problems in vehicular networks. Then, we address this challenging problem by relaxing it into a series of convex subproblems via the semi-definite relaxation (SDR) and convex–concave process (CCP) methods, and then propose to solve these convex subproblems iteratively.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The simulation results show that our proposed method convergences rapidly, and compared to the benchmark schemes, significant performance gains can be observed.
