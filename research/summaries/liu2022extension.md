# Extension of elliptic curve Qu–Vanstone certificates and their applications
Ziyuan Liu, Yi‐Fan Tseng, Raylin Tso et al. · Journal of Information Security and Applications 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jisa.2022.103176 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In public key infrastructure, a certificate, issued by a certificate authority (CA), is used to guarantee the connection between a user and her/his public key.
**Approach:** In this paper, to address the above issues, we proposed two schemes from the most widely used elliptic curve Qu–Vanstone implicit certificate scheme (ECQV).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** In order to improve the efficiency, the concept of implicit certificate protocol is introduced by Girault and Gönther. Experimental results show that our schemes not only greatly improve the performance, but also reduce the storage cost.
