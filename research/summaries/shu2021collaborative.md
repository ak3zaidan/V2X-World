# Collaborative Intrusion Detection for VANETs: A Deep Learning-Based Distributed SDN Approach
Jiangang Shu, Lei Zhou, Wei-Zhe Zhang, Xiao-Jiang Du et al. · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2020.3027390 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad hoc Network (VANET) is an enabling technology to provide a variety of convenient services in intelligent transportation systems, and yet vulnerable to various intrusion attacks. Intrusion detection systems (IDSs) can mitigate the security threats by detecting abnormal network behaviours.
**Approach:** To address this problem, we utilize deep learning with generative adversarial networks and explore distributed SDN to design a collaborative intrusion detection system (CIDS) for VANETs, which enables multiple SDN controllers jointly train a global intrusion detection model for the entire network without directly exchanging their sub-network flows. **Tools and scale:** We prove the correctness of our CIDS in both IID (Independent Identically Distribution) and non-IID situations, and also evaluate its performance through both theoretical analysis and experimental evaluation on a real-world dataset.
**Results:** Detailed experimental results validate that our CIDS is efficient and effective in intrusion detection for VANETs.
