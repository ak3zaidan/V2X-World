# Propagation and Packet Error models in VANET simulations
Luis Urquiza, Carolina Tripp, Isabel Martin, Monica Aguilar · IEEE Latin America Transactions 2014 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tla.2014.6827879 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Choice of propagation and packet error models in VANET simulation.
**Approach:** Reviews channel/PHY workflow and compares propagation models with a realistic packet error model.
**Tools and scale:** Network simulator numerical simulations.
**Results:** No single most conservative propagation model; conservativeness varies with channel capacity; VANET-specific models give close results at intermediate capacities.
