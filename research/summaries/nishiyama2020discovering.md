# Discovering Avoidable Planner Failures of Autonomous Vehicles using Counterfactual Analysis in Behaviorally Diverse Simulation
Daisuke Nishiyama, Mario Ynocente Castro, Shirou Maruyama et al. · The 23rd IEEE International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC2020) 2020 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.11991 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Automated Vehicles require exhaustive testing in simulation to detect as many safety-critical failures as possible before deployment on public roads.
**Approach:** We introduce a planner testing framework that leverages recent progress in simulating behaviorally diverse traffic participants. In particular, we propose methods to distinguish between unavoidable and avoidable accidents, focusing especially on automatically finding planner-specific defects that must be corrected before deployment.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Through experiments in complex multi-agent intersection scenarios, we show that our method can indeed find a wide range of critical planner failures.
