# Proof of Travel for Trust-Based Data Validation in V2I Communication
Dajiang Suo, Baichuan Mo, Jinhua Zhao, Sanjay E. Sarma · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2023.3236623 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Too few witness vehicles early in deployment for blacklisting.
**Approach:** Proof-of-Travel whitelisting: reputation from distance travelled, testified by infrastructure signatures; enhances voting-based V2I validation; game-theoretic analysis.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** At least 10% decrease in invalid events; all-cheat is not a pure Nash equilibrium; trade-off between event criticality and timing.
