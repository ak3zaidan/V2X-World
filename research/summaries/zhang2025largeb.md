# Large Language Models powered Malicious Traffic Detection: Architecture, Opportunities and Case Study
Xinggong Zhang, Haotian Meng, Qingyang Li et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18487 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Malicious traffic detection is a pivotal technology for network security to identify abnormal network traffic and detect network attacks.
**Approach:** We present a holistic view of the architecture of LLM-powered malicious traffic detection, including the procedures of Pre-training, Fine-tuning, and Detection. Finally, we present our design on LLM-powered DDoS detection as a case study.
