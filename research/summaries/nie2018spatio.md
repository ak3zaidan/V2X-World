# Spatio-Temporal Network Traffic Estimation and Anomaly Detection Based on Convolutional Neural Network in Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks
Laisen Nie, Yongkang Li, Xiangjie Kong · IEEE Access 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2018.2854842 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Over the last decade, vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs) have received a greater attention in academia and industry due to their influence in intelligent transportation systems. Providing reliability and security to the VANET is essential in order to guarantee the efficiency of its applications.
**Approach:** In this paper, a method using the spatio-temporal feature of network traffic is proposed to implement network traffic estimation at first, and on this basis, an anomaly detection algorithm is put forward.
