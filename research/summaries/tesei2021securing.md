# Securing Seaport Logistic Vehicles Using a Distributed Ledger-Based Credential Management System
Andrea Tesei, Domenico Lattuca, Alexandr Tardo, Luca Di Mauro et al. · IEEE Open Journal of Vehicular Technology 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ojvt.2021.3067209 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Credential management for seaport logistic vehicles.
**Approach:** Customised IOTA-VPKI distributed-ledger credential management (US/EU standard compliant) with authentication, authorisation and revocation; ETSI TVRA threat model.
**Tools and scale:** Lab validation; H2020 COREALIS testbed.
**Results:** 95% of cargo-release operation delays at or below 99 ms, independent of port size.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the full text.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — standards-compliant VPKI with revocation in a niche domain.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
