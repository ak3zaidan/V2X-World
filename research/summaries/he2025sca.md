# SCA-LLM: Spectral-Attentive LLM-Based Wireless World Modeling for Agentic Communications
Ke He, Le He, Lisheng Fan et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08139 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Future AI-native wireless networks are moving from reactive optimization to agentic decision-making that can sense, predict, and plan under fast-varying channels.
**Approach:** To this end, we propose SCA-LLM, a spectral-attentive LLM-based wireless world modeling framework that bridges CSI to LLMs via a spectral-channel attention (SCA) adapter. Extensive simulations show that SCA-LLM achieves state-of-the-art prediction performance and strong zero-shot generalization, yielding up to -2.4 dB normalized mean squared error (NMSE) advantage over the previous LLM based method.
