# Development of an Attack Detection Module for Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks
Rajmund Drenyovszki, Z. C. Johanyák · International Symposium on Applied Computational Intelligence and Informatics 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/saci60582.2024.10619806 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs) are rapidly expanding and providing benefits such as smarter traffic control, improved road safety, and increased passenger convenience.
**Approach:** However, security remains a significant concern due to the rise in malicious activities aiming to compromise data security and endanger vehicle and passenger safety. The article reports the development of a classification module for VANET Intrusion Detection Systems (IDSs), which continuously monitors and analyzes data traffic to identify patterns indicative of malicious behavior within the network.
