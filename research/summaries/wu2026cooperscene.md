Bo Wu, Ruoshen Mo, Justin Yue, Yanyu Zhang et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31219 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Approach:** CooperScene dataset with real COTS C-V2X communication characterisation.
**Tools and scale:** Three CAVs and one RSU; 344K objects, 59K frames.
