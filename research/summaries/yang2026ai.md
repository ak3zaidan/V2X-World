# AI-enabled vehicle-to-infrastructure communication for smart traffic control: a comprehensive review
Chen Yang · International Conference on Intelligent Transportation and Smart Cities 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3118229 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The integration of artificial intelligence (AI) with vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I) communication systems represents a transformative paradigm shift in intelligent transportation systems (ITS). This comprehensive review examines the state-of-the-art developments in AI-enabled V2I communication for smart traffic control, analyzing recent advances in machine learning algorithms, deep reinforcement learning ...
**Approach:** We systematically evaluate 32 recent studies (2022-2025) addressing key aspects including communication protocols, AI-based traffic signal optimization, security frameworks, and real-world deployment challenges. We propose a unified framework integrating federated learning, quantum-resistant cryptography, and multiagent coordination to address these challenges.

**Results:** Our analysis reveals that deep reinforcement learning (DRL) methods, particularly Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) and Deep Q-Networks (DQN), achieve average traffic delay reductions of 24-34% compared to traditional fixed-timing systems. Furthermore, edge computing integration reduces end-to-end latency by 45-60%, enabling real-time decision-making critical for autonomous vehicle coordination.
