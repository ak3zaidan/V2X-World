# A trust-driven privacy architecture for vehicular ad-hoc networks
Jetzabel Maritza Serna-Olvera · venue unknown 2013 · https://doi.org/10.5821/dissertation-2117-94875 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-Hoc NETworks (VANETs) are an emerging technology which aims to improve road safety by preventing and reducing traffic accidents.
**Approach:** While VANETs offer a great variety of promising applications, such as, safety-related and infotainment applications, they remain a number of security and privacy related research challenges that must be addressed. A common approach to security issues widely adopted in VANETs is the use of Public Key Infrastructures (PKI) and digital certificates in order to enable authentication, authorization and confidentiality.
