# Blockchained Federated Learning for Secure and Privacy-Preserving Internet of Vehicles: A Comprehensive Review
Adil Attiaoui, A. Kobbane, Marwane Ayaida, Mouna Elmachkour et al. · International Conference on Advanced Communication Technologies and Networking 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/commnet68224.2025.11288848 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Internet of Vehicles (IoV) connects vehicles, infrastructure, and cloud services to improve transportation efficiency through autonomous driving and intelligent traffic management. However, its strong reliance on wireless communication exposes IoV systems to significant security and privacy threats such as data tampering, GPS spoofing, and network intrusion.
**Approach:** This paper presents a comprehensive review of how blockchain technology and federated learning (FL) can be leveraged to mitigate these vulnerabilities.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting (VANET/IoV/V2X). Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** The abstract gives no specific quantitative results.
