# A Scheme for Distributed Vehicle Authentication and Revocation in Decentralized VANETs
Sujash Naskar, Carlo Brunetta, Gerhard Petrus Hancke, Tingting Zhang, Mikael Gidlund · IEEE Access 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3400530 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Central CA bottleneck and lack of immediate revocation.
**Approach:** Distributed authentication with multiple independent CAs and local inspectors; immediate revocation of disputed senders; ECC.
**Tools and scale:** Efficiency analysis.
**Results:** Up to ~3.9x faster V2I key sharing and ~7.5x faster V2V message sharing vs pairing-based schemes; alerts within about 4 ms.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — multi-authority PKI with immediate revocation (runner-up: multi-provider), non-SCMS.
**Novelty implications:** Immediate revocation claims exist in non-SCMS schemes.
