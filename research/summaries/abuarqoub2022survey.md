# A Survey on Vehicular Ad hoc Networks Security Attacks and Countermeasures
Abdelrahman Abuarqoub, Ahmad Alzu’bi, Mohammad Ali A. Hammoudeh, Arslan Ahmad, Bassam Al-Shargabi · International Conference on Future Networks & Distributed Systems (ICFNDS) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3584202.3584309 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANETs), a subcategory of Mobile ad hoc networks (MANETs), is a promising approach that is now evolving, gaining attention and momentum.
**Approach:** VANET consists of highly mobile nodes and roadside infrastructure to communicate essential information across a network, such as collisions, traffic congestion, and vehicular destinations. Due to its frequent vehicle movement, time-critical response and hybrid architecture, it comes with a plethora of network security and privacy issues ranging from the potential of malicious attacks to the availability of essential information across the network and its overall integrity.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The review of these different countermeasures shows their strengths and weaknesses and how they can be implemented independently or in a hybrid model best suited to the situation.
