# A Lightweight and Privacy-Preserving Authentication Protocol for Mobile Edge Computing
Kuljeet Kaur, Sahil Garg, Georges Kaddoum, Mohsen Guizani et al. · arXiv 2019 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.08896 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the advent of the Internet-of-Things (IoT), vehicular networks and cyber-physical systems, the need for real-time data processing and analysis has emerged as an essential pre-requite for customers' satisfaction.
**Approach:** Thus, in this paper, we propose an efficient and lightweight mutual authentication protocol for MEC environments; based on Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC), one-way hash functions and concatenation operations. The paper also presents a comparative assessment of the proposed scheme relative to the current state-of-the-art schemes.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** In this direction, Mobile Edge Computing (MEC) provides seamless services with reduced latency, enhanced mobility, and improved location awareness. The obtained results demonstrate that the proposed scheme incurs relatively less communication and computational overheads, and is appropriate to be adopted in resource constraint MEC environments.
