# GNN-based End-to-end Delay Prediction in Software Defined Networking
Zhun Ge, Jiacheng Hou, Amiya Nayak · 2022 18th International Conference on Distributed Computing in Sensor Systems (DCOSS) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/dcoss54816.2022.00066 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** End-to-end delay prediction in SDN.
**Approach:** STGCN on a graph of the Abilene network vs RF and NN.
**Tools and scale:** Abilene topology.
**Results:** In the most complex condition STGCN is 68.5% and 78.7% better than RF and NN.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - network surrogate.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
