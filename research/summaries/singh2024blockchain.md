# Blockchain-Based Lightweight Authentication Protocol for Next-Generation Trustworthy Internet of Vehicles Communication
Avaneesh Singh, Preeti Rani, J. V. N. Ramesh, S. Athawale et al. · IEEE transactions on consumer electronics 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tce.2024.3351221 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Intelligent transport systems (ITSs) aim to improve the performance of vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs). It provides new opportunities for the Internet of Vehicle (IoVs) environment but poses some security concerns, especially concerning the Vehicle-to-Vehicle secure communication connection.
**Approach:** To overcome these issues, this paper introduces BLAP-IOVs, a Blockchain-based Lightweight Authentication Protocol for generating Trustworthy IOVs communication.
**Tools and scale:** Comprehensive analysis and simulation results verify that the proposed BLAP-IOVs approach is efficient for secure information transmission in IOVs.
**Results:** Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed BLAP-IOVs approach achieves high success communication rates with acceptable vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) loss and computation overhead. BLAP-IOVs suggest using Blockchain to improve Security, trust, and collaboration.
