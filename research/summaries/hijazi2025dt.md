# DT-ECO: Digital Twin-Empowered Coverage Optimization for Indoor Wireless Networks
Hussein Hijazi, Nahi Kandil, Nadir Hakem, Paul Fortier, Zoheb Hassan · 2025 Sixth International Conference on Advances in Computational Tools for Engineering Applications (ACTEA) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/actea66485.2025.11189870 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Wireless coverage maximization in indoor environments, such as underground mines and industrial sites, is a non-trivial task due to the complex propagation conditions caused by blockages, scattering, diverse materials, and nonuniform RF signal attenuation.
**Approach:** Standard statistical models often fail to capture these inherent complexities, frequently overestimating or underestimating RF attenuation. Digital twin (DT) technology offers a promising alternative by accurately replicating RF propagation in real-world environments through its awareness of geometries, material properties, and positions of 3D scattering objects.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: simulations.
**Results:** Extensive simulations demonstrate that DT-ECO significantly outperforms conventional RF propagation models in terms of signal strength distribution and coverage uniformity in indoor environment, confirming its potential as a low-cost, effective solution for optimizing indoor wireless infrastructure.
