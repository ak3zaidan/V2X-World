# Intelligence-Based Strategies with Vehicle-to-Everything Network: A Review
Navdeep Bohra, Ashish Kumari, Vikash Kumar Mishra, Pramod Kumar Soni, Vipin Balyan · Future Internet 2025 · https://doi.org/10.3390/fi17020079 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Advancements in intelligent vehicular networks and computing systems have created new possibilities for innovative approaches that enhance traffic safety, comfort, and transportation performance.
**Approach:** Machine Learning (ML) has become widely employed for boosting conventional data-driven methodologies in various scientific study domains. The integration of a Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) system with ML enables the acquisition of knowledge from multiple places, enhances the operator’s awareness, and predicts future crashes to prevent them.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: simulation.
**Results:** The information serves multiple functions, such as determining the most efficient route, increasing the driver’s knowledge, forecasting movement strategy to avoid risky circumstances, and eventually improving user convenience, security, and overall highway experiences.
