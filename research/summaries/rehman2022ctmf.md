# CTMF: Context-Aware Trust Management Framework for Internet of Vehicles
Abdul Rehman, Mohd Fadzil Hassan, Yew Kwang Hooi, Muhammad Aasim Qureshi, Saurabh Shukla, Erwin Susanto, Saddaf Rubab, Abdel-Haleem Abdel-Aty · IEEE Access 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2022.3189349 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Secure communication is the top concern of the Internet of Vehicles (IoV). The trust between nodes can have a considerable impact on ensuring IoV security.
**Approach:** In this study, a unique trust framework is presented that considers most of the aspects of trust in IoV to accurately identify malicious nodes and events.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The performance of the framework was examined using an experimental simulation. The results show inclining performance indicators.
