# Location privacy scheme for VANETs
Ines Khacheba, Mohamed B. Yagoubi, Nasreddine Lagraa, Abderrahmane Lakas · 2017 International Conference on Selected Topics in Mobile and Wireless Networking (MoWNeT) 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mownet.2017.8045942 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs), a vehicle could be identified and tracked by eavesdropping its messages (e.g., beacons) by an adversary, since these messages contain personal information such as the location of the vehicle.
**Approach:** This attack leads to threats on the user's location privacy. Frequent changing pseudonyms are accepted as a solution to protect the location privacy of users in VANETs.
