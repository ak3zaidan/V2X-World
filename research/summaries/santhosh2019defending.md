# Defending against Sybil Attacks in Vehicular Platoons
J. Santhosh, S. Sankaran · International Workshop on Ant Colony Optimization and Swarm Intelligence 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ants47819.2019.9117945 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil attacks in platoons.
**Approach:** Model Sybil attacks; defence with hybrid key management (public-key bootstrapped pairwise symmetric keys) and witnesses.
**Tools and scale:** OMNeT++, SUMO, Veins.
**Results:** Significantly limits impact with minimal overhead.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — attack and PKI-based defence in Veins co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
