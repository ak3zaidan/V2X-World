# Detection of malicious data in vehicular ad-hoc networks for traffic signal control applications
Bartlomiej Placzek, Marcin Bernas · Computer Networks. CCIS, vol. 608, pp. 72-82, 2016 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-39207-3_7 · relevance: medium · basis: full text

**Problem:** Sybil-injected malicious data degrades VANET-based traffic signal control.
**Approach:** Rule-based detection combining expected driver behaviour (signal reaction, preferred speed), unrealistic overtaking, and neighbour position verification.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation of a decentralised self-organising signal control system at multiple urban intersections.
**Results:** Combining detection mechanisms correctly recognises malicious data and is essential to mitigate signal-control degradation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Integrate more sophisticated driver behaviour models
- automatic parameter calibration from collected data
- test in more realistic scenarios with various signal control algorithms.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — partial: Sybil detection for signal control in simulation; no PKI/revocation.
**Novelty implications:** Nothing of our candidate directions is ruled out.
