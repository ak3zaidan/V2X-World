# An Optimisation driven Deep Residual Network for Sybil attack detection with reputation and trust-based misbehaviour detection in VANET
Nitha C Velayudhan, A. Anitha, Mukesh Madanan · Journal of Experimental &amp; Theoretical Artificial Intelligence 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1080/0952813x.2022.2104387 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil and RSU misbehaviour detection.
**Approach:** Competitive Dolphin Echolocation Optimisation trained Deep Residual Network; FGWSO routing.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Precision 0.9197, F1 0.9121, recall 0.9046.

- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
