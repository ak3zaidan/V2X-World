# An Efficient Dynamic Solution for the Detection and Prevention of Black Hole Attack in VANETs
Abdul Malik, Muhammad Zahid Khan, Mohammad Shahzeb Faisal, Faheem Khan, Jung-Taek Seo · Italian National Conference on Sensors 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s22051897 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Black hole attacks in VANET routing.
**Approach:** DPBHA with dynamic threshold and forged RREQ.
**Tools and scale:** NS-2.
**Results:** Average PDR 28% vs 25.06% (SAODV) and 26.48% (IDBA), 20.44% for AODV.
