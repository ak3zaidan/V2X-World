# A Hardware-in-the-Loop Evaluation of the Impact of the V2X Channel on the Traffic-Safety Versus Efficiency Trade-offs
Alessandro Bazzi, Thomas Blazek, Michele Menarini, Barbara M. Masini, Alberto Zanella, Christoph Mecklenbrauker et al. · arXiv 2020 · https://doi.org/10.23919/eucap48036.2020.9135714 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Impact of real V2X channel conditions on safety versus efficiency of intersection collision warning.
**Approach:** HIL platform with real ITS-G5 message exchange and a channel emulator with real signals evaluating ICW.
**Tools and scale:** HIL, ITS-G5 hardware, channel emulator; intersection scenario.
**Results:** Collision risk drastically reduced with a safety-efficiency trade-off; real channels cannot guarantee the zero-risk achieved with ideal propagation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — links radio channel to safety outcomes (closed loop), which our engine does; no security.
**Novelty implications:** Channel-to-safety closed loop exists in HIL; the security-to-safety link remains to be checked against security papers.
