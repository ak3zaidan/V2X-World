# A Federated Flower Learning-Empowered Intrusion Detection System for Vehicle 6G Fog Networks
P. Khuwuthyakorn, O. Thinnukool, Abdullah Lakhan · IEEE Conference on Communications and Network Security 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cns66487.2025.11194963 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The evolution of intelligent transportation systems in the era of 6G has introduced new challenges in cybersecurity, particularly for vehicular networks interconnected through fog and edge nodes.
**Approach:** This research proposes a Federated Flower Learning-Empowered Intrusion Detection System (IDS) for Ve-hicle 6G Fog Networks using the TON_loT dataset, aiming to achieve high-accuracy, low-latency attack detection while preserving data privacy. The proposed IDS leverages a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) trained on this rich dataset to extract spatial features and detect intrusion patterns.
**Tools and scale:** This research proposes a Federated Flower Learning-Empowered Intrusion Detection System (IDS) for Ve-hicle 6G Fog Networks using the TON_loT dataset, aiming to achieve high-accuracy, low-latency attack detection while preserving data privacy. **Results:** The federated CNN - based IDS achieves high detection accuracy and minimises end-to-end latency, ensuring timely responses in critical vehicle control scenarios. Simulation results show that the proposed system reduces bandwidth consumption and response delays compared to traditional cloud-based IDS solutions and identifies the highest security risk from both known and unknown malicious threats in distributed vehicle 6G networks.
