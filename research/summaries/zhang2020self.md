# A Self-Learning Detection Method of Sybil Attack Based on LSTM for Electric Vehicles
Yi-Ying Zhang, Jing Shang, Xi Chen, Kun Liang · Energies 2020 · https://doi.org/10.3390/en13061382 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Sybil attacks on EV charging pile networks; detectors need labelled data.
**Approach:** Self-learning LSTM detection treating training and prediction as a closed loop over false packets, with reputation and trust thresholds.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Detects early Sybil attacks quickly and accurately (no figures extracted).
