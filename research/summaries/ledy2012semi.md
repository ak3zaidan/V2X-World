# A Semi-Deterministic Channel Model for VANETs Simulations
Jonathan Ledy, Hervé Boeglen, Anne-Marie Poussard, Benoît Hilt, Rodolphe Vauzelle · International Journal of Vehicular Technology 2012 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2012/492105 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** PHY channel model choice strongly affects VANET simulation results; ray tracing is slow and statistical models inaccurate.
**Approach:** UM-CRT semi-deterministic model: SCME-UM statistical model tuned by ray-tracer (CRT) parameters, integrated in 802.11p/802.11n PHYs in NS-2.
**Tools and scale:** NS-2, 802.11p, urban scenarios.
**Results:** Good approximation of realistic propagation while significantly reducing simulation time (no numbers in abstract).

- Not stated in the abstract.

**Novelty implications:** Hybrid deterministic/statistical channels are prior art.
