# End-to-End Co-Simulation Testbed for Cybersecurity Research and Development in Intelligent Transportation Systems
Minhaj Uddin Ahmad, Akid Abrar, Sagar Dasgupta, Mizanur Rahman · arXiv.org 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16489 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Cybersecurity evaluation of ITS is costly; simulation platforms rarely cover mobility, sensing, networking and applications together.
**Approach:** OpenCAMS: open-source co-simulation linking CARLA (3D/sensors), SUMO (traffic) and OMNeT++ (V2X), with a case study of a C-V2X proactive safety alert system using post-quantum cryptography.
**Tools and scale:** CARLA + SUMO + OMNeT++; C-V2X; open source on GitHub.
**Results:** Case study demonstration; no headline numbers in extracted text.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Motivated by limitations of existing platforms in capturing full ITS complexity; own limitations not detailed in extracted text.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — end-to-end traffic + V2X co-simulation for cybersecurity with a PQ C-V2X safety case study, which overlaps our integrated engine and direction 2.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim the first integrated traffic/V2X security testbed with PQ case studies; we differ by single-process determinism, full SCMS backend, joint ground truth and counterfactual reruns, which co-simulation coupling does not give.
