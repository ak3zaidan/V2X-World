# Tiny-WiFo: A Lightweight Wireless Foundation Model for Channel Prediction via Multi-Component Adaptive Knowledge Distillation
Haotian Zhang, Shijian Gao, Xiang Cheng · IEEE Wireless Communications Letters 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/lwc.2026.3664439 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Approach:** Multi-component adaptive knowledge distillation.
**Tools and scale:** WiFo FM.
**Results:** Tiny-WiFo 5.5M parameters, 1.6 ms inference, over 98% of WiFo performance.
