# Accountable credential management system for vehicular communication
Salabat Khan, Liehuang Zhu, Xiaoyan Yu, Zijian Zhang, Mussadiq Abdul Rahim, Maqbool Khan et al. · Vehicular Communications 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vehcom.2020.100279 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Trust problems remain in V-PKI.
**Approach:** ACMS: transparency log with accumulation tree covering provisioning, registration, validation and revocation without CRLs; Tamarin verification.
**Tools and scale:** Time cost and performance analysis.
**Results:** Feasible and more scalable than existing V-PKI schemes.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - alternative credential management architecture.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on SCMS claims.
