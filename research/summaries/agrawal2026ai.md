# "AI-Driven Secure Vehicular Networks: Intelligent Threat Detection and Trust-Aware Defense for V2X Systems"
Rakesh Kumar Agrawal · IEEE DataPort 2026 · https://doi.org/10.21227/t0er-ym02 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** "Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) and Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) systems are fundamental to intelligent transportation ecosystems.
**Approach:** Traditional cryptographic defenses alone are insufficient against adaptive and zero-day threats.This paper proposes an AI-driven secure vehicular network framework that integrates federated learning, graph neural networks, and deep reinforcement learning for privacy-preserving intrusion detection, trust-aware routing, and autonomous attack mitigation.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The architecture enables vehicles, roadside units, and edge servers to collaboratively train distributed models without sharing raw data, preserving privacy while improving detection generalization. Experimental evaluation demonstrates superior performance in detection accuracy, false alarm reduction, attack response latency, and secure routing efficiency compared with conventional ML and signature-based IDS baselines"
