# Survey on Misbehavior Detection in Cooperative Intelligent Transportation Systems
Rens Wouter van der Heijden, Stefan Dietzel, Tim Leinmuller, Frank Kargl · IEEE Communications Surveys &amp; Tutorials 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/comst.2018.2873088 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** PKI excludes outsiders only; insider attackers with valid keys need misbehaviour detection.
**Approach:** Survey and taxonomy of misbehaviour detection in cITS (node-centric/data-centric, behavioural, trust, plausibility, consistency), shortcomings of PKI, evaluation practices.
**Tools and scale:** Survey.
**Results:** Notes multi-antenna attackers remain open for RSSI schemes; voting schemes require preventing parallel pseudonym use; critiques of game-theoretic assumptions.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Multi-antenna attackers remain an open issue for most physical-layer schemes.
- Future work: exploit orthogonality of the four detection classes; generalise to CPS.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — canonical MBD survey; frames the MBD part of direction 1.
**Novelty implications:** Lists open problems we can cite; does not cover closed-loop revocation timing.
