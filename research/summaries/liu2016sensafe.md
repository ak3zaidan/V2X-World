# SenSafe: A Smartphone-Based Traffic Safety Framework by Sensing Vehicle and Pedestrian Behaviors
Zhenyu Liu, Mengfei Wu, Konglin Zhu, Lin Zhang · Mobile Information Systems 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2016/7967249 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Traffic accident involving vehicles is one of the most serious problems in the transportation system nowadays. How to detect dangerous steering and then alarm drivers in real time is a problem.
**Approach:** In this paper, a smartphone-based framework named SenSafe is developed to improve the traffic safety.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Besides, the Wi-Fi association and authentication overhead is reduced to broadcast the compressed sensing data using the Wi-Fi beacon to inform the drivers of the surroundings. Finally, an Android-based implementation of SenSafe framework has been achieved to demonstrate the application reliability in real environments.
