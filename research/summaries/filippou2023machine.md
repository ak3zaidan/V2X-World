# A Machine Learning Approach for Detecting GPS Location Spoofing Attacks in Autonomous Vehicles
Stylianos Filippou, Achilleas Achilleos, Syeda Zillay Nain Zukhraf, Christos Laoudias, Kleanthis Malialis, M.K. Michael et al. · IEEE Vehicular Technology Conference 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2023-spring57618.2023.10200857 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** GPS spoofing detection without attack labels.
**Approach:** Outlier detection trained on normal location data.
**Tools and scale:** CARLA-generated data.
**Results:** >98% detection accuracy; 15% better than a non-ML state-of-the-art method.
