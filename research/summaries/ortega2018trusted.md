Victor Ortega, Faiza Bouchmal, Jose F. Monserrat · IEEE Vehicular Technology Magazine 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mvt.2018.2813422 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular communications, though a reality, must continue to evolve to support higher throughput and, above all, ultralow latency to accommodate new use cases, such as the fully autonomous vehicle. Cybersecurity must be assured since the risk of losing control of vehicles if a country were to come under attack is a matter of national security.
**Approach:** This article presents the technological enablers that ensure security requirements are met.
