# A Multi-Modal Foundational Model for Wireless Communication and Sensing
Vahid Yazdnian, Yasaman Ghasempour · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04016 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Artificial intelligence is a key enabler for next-generation wireless communication and sensing. Yet, today's learning-based wireless techniques do not generalize well: most models are task-specific, environment-dependent, and limited to narrow sensing modalities, requiring costly retraining when deployed in new scenarios.
**Approach:** This work introduces a task-agnostic, multi-modal foundational model for physical-layer wireless systems that learns transferable, physics-aware representations across heterogeneous modalities, enabling robust generalization across tasks and environments.
