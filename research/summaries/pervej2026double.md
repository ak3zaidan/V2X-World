# Double-Directional Wireless Channel Generation Using Statistics-Informed Machine Learning
Ferdous Pervej, Pratik R. Patel, K. Manjunatha, P. Shamain, A. Molisch · IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Electromagnetics, Antennas and Propagation 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jsteap.2026.3701014 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Channel models are important for the design and testing of wireless communication systems.
**Approach:** To represent the complete propagation channel, we need a double-directional (DD) channel model describing the directions at transmitter and receiver (Rx), as well as delays and amplitudes of the multipath components. Traditional statistical and/or semideterministic models may not perform well when the evolutions of the different MPC features are considered.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: simulation.
**Results:** Our extensive simulation results validate that this statistics-aided training method effectively generates future channel realizations with statistics that closely match the ground truth, and the proposed Stat-HTransformer method mostly outperforms all other baselines.
