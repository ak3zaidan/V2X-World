# LLM-Guided Open RAN: Empowering Hierarchical RAN Intelligent Control
Lingyan Bao, Sinwoong Yun, Jemin Lee, Tony Q. S. Quek · arXiv.org 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.18062 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Despite recent advances in applying large language models (LLMs) and machine learning (ML) techniques to open radio access network (O-RAN), critical challenges remain, such as insufficient cooperation between radio access network (RAN) intelligent controllers (RICs), high computational demands hindering real-time decisions, and the lack of domain-specific fine-tuning. Therefore, this article introduces the LLM-empowered hierarchical RIC (LLM-hRIC) framework to improve the collaboration between RICs in O-RAN.
**Approach:** We evaluate the feasibility and performance of the LLM-hRIC framework in an integrated access and backhaul (IAB) network setting, and finally, discuss the open challenges of the LLM-hRIC framework for O-RAN.
