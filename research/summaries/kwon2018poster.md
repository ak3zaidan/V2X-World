# POSTER
HyukSang Kwon, Shahid Raza, JeongGil Ko · Proceedings of the 2018 on Asia Conference on Computer and Communications Security 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3196494.3201591 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Certificate-based Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) schemes are used to authenticate the identity of distinct nodes on the Internet.
**Approach:** This work outlines our approach in compressing standards-compliant X.509 certificates so that their sizes are reduced and can be effectively used on IoT nodes.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** This work outlines our approach in compressing standards-compliant X.509 certificates so that their sizes are reduced and can be effectively used on IoT nodes. Our scheme shows a certificate compression rate of up to ~30%, which allows effective energy reduction when using X.509-based certificates on IoT platforms.
