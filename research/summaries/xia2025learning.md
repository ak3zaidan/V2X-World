# Learning-Based Proactive and Adaptive Link Flooding Attack Mitigation in AIoT
Yu Xia, Weiting Zhang, Ying Liu, Jiawen Kang, Hongke Zhang · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2025.3553250 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Artificial Intelligence of Things (AIoT) is a new networking paradigm incorporating AI and IoT, empowering multiple industries.
**Approach:** Due to the high value of AI infrastructure in AIoT, its security issues are becoming increasingly prominent. A new type of covert DDoS attack, link flooding attack (LFA) is emerging as a vital threat.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed scheme can quickly perceive LFA and effectively resist the link congestion caused by LFA.
