# A Fuzzy Logic-Based Trust Estimation in Edge-Enabled Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Md. Mahmudul Hasan, Mosarrat Jahan, Shaily Kabir, Christian Wagner · 2021 IEEE International Conference on Fuzzy Systems (FUZZ-IEEE) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/fuzz45933.2021.9494428 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Trust estimation of vehicles is vital for the correct functioning of Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) as it enhances their security by identifying reliable vehicles.
**Approach:** However, accurate trust estimation still remains distant as existing works do not consider all malicious features of vehicles, such as dropping or delaying packets, altering content, and injecting false information. Moreover, data consistency of messages is not guaranteed here as they pass through multiple paths and can easily be altered by malicious relay vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Experimental results reveal that the proposed scheme detects malicious vehicles with high precision and recall and makes decisions with higher accuracy compared to the state-of-the-art.
