# Computing inversions in finite fields based on cellular automata
Haibo Yi · Journal of Intelligent & Fuzzy Systems 2019 · https://doi.org/10.3233/jifs-179289 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Over the past few years, there has been a sustaining interest in research of secure communication architectures and protocols based on asymmetric cryptographic techniques. However, it is showed that quantum computers can break most asymmetric cryptographic techniques and they will be insecure.
**Approach:** We propose a variant of Fermat’s theorem inversion based on cellular automaton. We implement inversions in finite fields on Application Specific Integrated Circuit (ASIC) and accelerate some quantum-safe signature schemes, which shows that they are efficient for constructing quantum-safe communications.
**Tools and scale:** In addition, cellular automata can simulate many real-world systems, such as biological system, Vehicular Communication (VC) systems and neural networks.
**Results:** In this paper, we focus on accelerating quantum-safe cryptography for communication systems by improving inversions in finite fields.
