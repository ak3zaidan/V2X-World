# SUMO-MCP: Leveraging the Model Context Protocol for Autonomous Traffic Simulation and Optimization
Cheng-Long Ye, Gang Xiong, Junyou Shang, Xing-Yuan Dai et al. · ACM Cloud and Autonomic Computing Conference 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cac67268.2025.11487664 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Traffic simulation tools, such as Simulation of Urban Mobility (SUMO), are essential for urban mobility research. However, such tools remain challenging for users due to complex manual workflows involving network download, demand generation, simulation setup, and result analysis.
**Approach:** In this paper, we introduce SUMO-MCP, a novel platform that can wrap SUMO’s core utilities into a unified tool suite and provide additional auxiliary utilities for common preprocessing and postprocessing tasks.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: SUMO. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Experiments demonstrate that SUMO-MCP significantly makes traffic simulation more accessible and reliable for researchers.
