# F-RouND: Fog-based Rogue Nodes Detection in Vehicular Ad hoc Networks
Anirudh Paranjothi, Mohammed Atiquzzaman, Mohammad S. Khan · Global Communications Conference 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/globecom42002.2020.9322131 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Rogue nodes in VANET.
**Approach:** F-RouND: dynamic fog of OBUs for rogue node detection.
**Tools and scale:** OMNeT++ and SUMO.
**Results:** 45% lower processing delay, 12% lower overhead, 36% lower FPR at high density vs existing.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial: simulated misbehaviour detection with OMNeT++/SUMO.
**Novelty implications:** Simulation-based rogue node detection exists; no revocation loop.
