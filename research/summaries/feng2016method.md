# A method for defensing against multi-source Sybil attacks in VANET
Xia Feng, Chunyan Li, Dexin Chen, Jin Tang · Peer-to-Peer Networking and Applications 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s12083-016-0431-x · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Multi-source Sybil attacks with conspiring legitimate identities.
**Approach:** Event-based reputation system with per-event reputation and trust thresholds.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** High defence and detection performance.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Strong assumption of a trusted RSU issuing certificates; future work to loosen it.
- Future work: automatic trust establishment among vehicles.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Sybil detection with thresholds.
**Novelty implications:** Minor.
