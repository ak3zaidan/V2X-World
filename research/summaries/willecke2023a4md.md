# A4MD: Artery for Misbehavior Detection, Reporting, and Reaction in the ETSI C-ITS
Alexander Willecke, Bastian Kulke, Lars C. Wolf · IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc57357.2023.10136315 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Misbehaviour must be detected, reported and sanctioned; research tooling is missing.
**Approach:** A4MD framework on Artery and F2MD covering detection, reporting and reaction in ETSI C-ITS.
**Tools and scale:** Artery/Veins/SUMO; ETSI TR 103 460 misbehaviour reporting protocol.
