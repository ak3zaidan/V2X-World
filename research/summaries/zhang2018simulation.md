# Simulation Study on Collaborative Content Distribution in Delay Tolerant Vehicular Networks
Rusheng Zhang, Bo Yu, Hariharan Krishnan · arXiv 2018 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.01372 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Distributing large files (incl. certificates) via V2V.
**Approach:** Hybrid cellular+V2V collaborative content distribution simulation.
**Tools and scale:** Simulated metropolitan area.
**Results:** With 1% cellular vehicles, 90% coverage in under 24 h; 99% in 48-72 h.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Certificate/file distribution simulation; we provision via cellular and RSU.
**Novelty implications:** Same topic area; does not provide the closed-loop, SCMS-complete, cross-layer evaluation our engine targets, so our claims stand but this work must be cited as related.
