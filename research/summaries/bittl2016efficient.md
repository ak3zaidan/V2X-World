# Efficient Authorization Authority Certificate Distribution in VANETs
Sebastian Bittl, Karsten Roscher · Proceedings of the 2nd International Conference on Information Systems Security and Privacy 2016 · https://doi.org/10.5220/0005685600850096 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Distribution of multi-level certificate chains (incl. authorization authority certificates) in VANETs affects channel load and causes packet loss.
**Approach:** Analysis of standardised mechanisms; finds a severe DoS attack; adapts position-based routing strategies to chain distribution.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Combining pseudonym certificate buffering with requester-based responder selection meets chain distribution requirements.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — Certificate chain distribution (P2PCD-like) and its channel cost/DoS, as in our P2PCD over-the-air implementation.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first analysis of P2PCD/chain distribution DoS; open: PQ chain sizes and safety effects.
