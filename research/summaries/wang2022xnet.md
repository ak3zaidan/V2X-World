# xNet: Improving Expressiveness and Granularity for Network Modeling with Graph Neural Networks
Mowei Wang, Linbo Hui, Yong Cui, Ru Liang, Zhen Liu · IEEE Conference on Computer Communications 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/infocom48880.2022.9796726 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Network models lack configuration awareness and fine-grained temporal predictions.
**Approach:** xNet: general GNN framework with relation-graph representations that learns a state transition function and rolls it out over time.
**Tools and scale:** Three use cases (wired).
**Results:** Accurate predictions with over two orders of magnitude speedup vs conventional simulation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - learned world-model-style network simulator (direction 3), wired.
**Novelty implications:** Learned state-transition network simulators exist; V2X with mobility and security not covered.
