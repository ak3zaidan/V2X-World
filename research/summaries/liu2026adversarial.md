# Adversarial Trust Poisoning in Vehicular Collaborative Perception
Yutong Liu, Chenyi Wang, Ming F. Li, Qingzhao Zhang · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22122 · relevance: medium · basis: full text

**Problem:** Consistency-based CP defences can be weaponised to poison trust in benign vehicles.
**Approach:** TrustFlip: physical adversarial objects causing legitimate disagreement; TrustReflect self-reflection mitigation.
**Tools and scale:** OPV2V and V2X-Real, open-source CP models.
**Results:** Victims downweighted or excluded across models and defences.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Dual-use
- evaluated mainly in digital simulation.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — none directly; relevant warning for misbehaviour-detection designs that exclude vehicles.
**Novelty implications:** Shows MBD consensus can be gamed to target benign vehicles — relevant to our revocation false-positive analysis.
