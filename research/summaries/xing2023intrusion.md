# Intrusion Detection Method for Internet of Vehicles Based on Parallel Analysis of Spatio-Temporal Features
Ling Xing, Kun Wang, Honghai Wu, Huahong Ma et al. · Italian National Conference on Sensors 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s23094399 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The problems with network security that the Internet of Vehicles (IoV) faces are becoming more noticeable as it continues to evolve. Deep learning-based intrusion detection techniques can assist the IoV in preventing network threats.
**Approach:** To solve the above problems, this paper proposes an intrusion detection method for IoV based on parallel analysis of spatio-temporal features (PA-STF). First, we built an optimal subset of features based on feature correlations of IoV traffic.
**Tools and scale:** NSL-KDD, UNSW-NB15, Car-Hacking/CAN dataset, deep learning (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** The experimental results show that the PA-STF method reduces the false-positive rate by 1.95% and 1.57% on the NSL-KDD and UNSW-NB15 datasets, respectively, with the accuracy and F1 score also being superior.
