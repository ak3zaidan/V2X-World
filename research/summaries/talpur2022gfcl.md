# GFCL: A GRU-based Federated Continual Learning Framework against Adversarial Attacks in IoV
Anum Talpur, G. Mohan · arXiv.org 2022 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.11010 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Integration of machine learning (ML) in 5G-based Internet of Vehicles (IoV) networks has enabled intelligent transportation and smart traffic management.
**Approach:** Nonetheless, the security against adversarial poisoning attacks is also increasingly becoming a challenging task. Specifically, Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) is one of the widely used ML designs in IoV applications.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G
**Results:** The results demonstrate the effectiveness of our proposed solution in terms of different performance metrics.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Nonetheless, the security against adversarial poisoning attacks is also increasingly becoming a challenging task.
- We use GRU to predict a future data sequence to analyze and detect illegitimate behavior from vehicles in a federated learning-based distributed manner.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
