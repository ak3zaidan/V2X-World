# AngularWM: Wireless World Modeling for AoA Prediction from Multi-Antenna I/Q
Omar Mashaal, Hatem Abou-Zeid · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05543 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Learning AoA evolution from raw multi-antenna IQ under mobility.
**Approach:** AngularWM self-supervised JEPA world model with geometric regulariser.
**Tools and scale:** Large-scale over-the-air measurements.
**Results:** Mean AoA error over eight steps from 29.97 to 0.49 degrees.
