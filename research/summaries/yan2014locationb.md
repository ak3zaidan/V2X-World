# Location Spoofing Detection for VANETs by a Single Base Station in Rician Fading Channels
Shihao Yan, Robert Malaney, Ido Nevat, Gareth W. Peters · 2015 IEEE 81st Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC Spring) 2014 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtcspring.2015.7145917 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Verifying locations in 1609.2 safety messages with a single base station.
**Approach:** Multi-antenna BS location spoofing detection in Rician fading; optimal attacker power and beamformer.
**Tools and scale:** Theory and Monte Carlo simulation.
**Results:** Performance rises with Rician K-factor and antenna count; attacker's optimal antenna count equals legitimate vehicle's.
