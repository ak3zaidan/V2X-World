# ACPC: Efficient revocation of pseudonym certificates using activation codes
Marcos A. Simplicio, Eduardo Lopes Cominetti, Harsh Kupwade Patil, Jefferson E. Ricardini, Marcos Vinicius M. Silva · Ad Hoc Networks 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adhoc.2018.07.007 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** SCMS's original revocation leads to large CRLs.
**Approach:** ACPC: activation codes periodically distributed to non-revoked vehicles so revoked vehicles' pre-issued certificates become unusable; integrable into SCMS.
**Tools and scale:** Design and analysis.
**Results:** Reduced CRL size and overhead (no numbers in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong - SCMS revocation-mechanism alternative directly applicable to our engine's revocation path (activation-code designs are also in CAMP SCMS work).
**Novelty implications:** Activation-code revocation is not new; comparing it with linkage CRLs on time-to-neutralise in closed loop is open.
