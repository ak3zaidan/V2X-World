# Misbehavior-Aware On-Demand Collaborative Intrusion Detection System Using Distributed Ensemble Learning for VANET
Fuad A. Ghaleb, Faisal Saeed, Mohammed Al-Sarem, Bander Ali Saleh Al-rimy et al. · Electronics 2020 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics9091411 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Collaborative IDS can be poisoned by compromised collaborators.
**Approach:** MA-CIDS: vehicles share locally trained random forests, evaluate them locally, exclude outliers and build weighted ensembles.
**Tools and scale:** NSL-KDD dataset.
**Results:** Better effectiveness and efficiency than existing models.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Evaluated on a generic IT dataset (NSL-KDD), not V2X data.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — generic IDS.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
