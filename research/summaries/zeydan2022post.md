# Post-Quantum Era in V2X Security: Convergence of Orchestration and Parallel Computation
Engin Zeydan, Yekta Türk, Berkin Aksoy, Yaman Yagiz Tasbag · IEEE Communications Standards Magazine 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mcomstd.0001.2100060 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** PQ algorithms must be integrated into mobile network orchestration for V2X.
**Approach:** Service & Computation Orchestrator selecting NTRU computation method (Toom-Cook vs Karatsuba) by CPU load.
**Tools and scale:** Simulations of parallel polynomial multiplication under CPU load.
**Results:** Parallel performance depends on CPU load; Toom-Cook uses cores better than Karatsuba.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Standardisation and coexistence of PQ with mobile networks discussed as open directions
**Overlap with our engine:** none — Network-side compute orchestration.
**Novelty implications:** None.
