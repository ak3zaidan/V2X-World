**Problem:** Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) improves road safety and traffic efficiency, with Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks (VANETs) enabling real-time vehicular communication. However, VANETs face security challenges due to network dynamics, open wireless channels, device heterogeneity, and decentralized architectures.
**Approach:** This paper surveys the key security factors—including authentication, integrity, privacy, and availability—and reviews techniques such as cryptographic protocols, trust management, pseudonym schemes, and intrusion detection systems.
