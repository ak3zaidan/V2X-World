# Intrusion detection system using deep learning for in-vehicle security
Jiayan Zhang, Fei Li, Haoxi Zhang, Ruxiang Li, Yalin Li · Ad Hoc Networks 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adhoc.2019.101974 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the development of vehicle intelligence technology, the combination of network and vehicle becomes inevitable, which brings much convenience to people. At the same time, hackers can also use technical vulnerabilities to attack vehicles, leading to severe traffic accidents and even vehicle crash.
**Approach:** This paper studies the vehicle intrusion detection system (IDS) based on the neural network algorithm in deep learning, and uses gradient descent with momentum (GDM) and gradient descent with momentum and adaptive gain (GDM/AG) to improve the efficiency and accuracy of IDS.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The veracity rate ranges from 97% to 98% in directing the adaptation when facing unknown attack types.
