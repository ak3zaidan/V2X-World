# What Is a Causal Effect When Firms Interact? Counterfactuals and Interdependence
Mariluz Mate · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.00279 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Many empirical studies estimate causal effects in environments where economic units interact through spatial or network connections. In such settings, outcomes are jointly determined, and treatment induced shocks propagate across economically connected units.
**Approach:** We develop a causal framework for firm-level economies in which interaction structures are unobserved but can be learned from predetermined characteristics.
**Tools and scale:** A Monte Carlo simulation illustrates that equilibrium and partial-equilibrium effects differ mechanically even before estimation, and that network feedback can amplify bias when identifying assumptions fail.
**Results:** We formalize three economically meaningful counterfactual regimes partial equilibrium, local interaction, and network, consistent equilibrium, and show that standard spatial autoregressive estimates map into distinct causal effects depending on the counterfactual adopted. We derive identification conditions for each regime and demonstrate that equilibrium causal effects require substantially stronger assumptions than direct or local effects.
