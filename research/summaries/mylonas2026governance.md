# A Governance-Aware Large Language Model Orchestrated Agentic Digital Twin for Transmission System Operator Control Room Decision Support
Costas Mylonas, Magda Foti, Emmanouel Varvarigos · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22476 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Transmission system operators face rising complexity from renewable integration, reduced inertia, and tighter security margins.
**Approach:** The large language model only selects and parameterizes whitelisted analysis tools, and every proposed action passes through a governance layer that the model cannot bypass. Across 590 runs of the primary model, tool selection reaches 96.5% and task success 93.7%, and all four rules hold without exception.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Every number in an answer is rendered by the layer from backend results with its unit, variable, and time. In a separate three-repetition study across four large language models, 1416 runs in total, the rules again hold on every run, with an approximate 95% lower bound of 99.8%.
