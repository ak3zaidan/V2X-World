# Secure message confirmation scheme based on batch verification in vehicular cloud computing
Trupil Limbasiya, Debasis Das · Physical Communication 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.phycom.2018.07.015 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract In the present world, we are enabled with distinct technology devices and/or systems mostly to reduce human effort in completing various tasks. To fulfill our activities within a reasonable period, we expect reliable communication system(s) to exchange significant data securely.
**Approach:** Thus, we propose an improved message confirmation system for the VCC to protect various security attacks, e.g., replay, plain-text, impersonation, man-in-the-middle, and modification.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Next, we compare the proposed system with different message verification protocols and the results show that the suggested method is more secure and effective compared to other related communication protocols.
