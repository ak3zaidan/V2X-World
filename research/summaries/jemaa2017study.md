# Study of the impact of pseudonym change mechanisms on vehicular safety
Ines Ben Jemaa, Arnaud Kaiser, Brigitte Lonc · 2017 IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference (VNC) 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc.2017.8275632 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Do pseudonym change mechanisms hurt safety?
**Approach:** Simulation of existing pseudonym change mechanisms' effect on neighbourhood view and danger anticipation.
**Tools and scale:** IEEE VNC 2017; simulation.
**Results:** Pseudonym change creates a gap between real and perceived neighbourhood and degrades anticipation of dangerous situations.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — quantifies a safety cost of a security/privacy mechanism (direction 2).
**Novelty implications:** We cannot claim first study of privacy-mechanism safety cost; joint PKI/radio/traffic safety cost of verification and revocation remains open.
