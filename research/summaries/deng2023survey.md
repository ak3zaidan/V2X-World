# A Survey on Vehicular Cloud Network Security
Junyi Deng, Jikai Deng, Peihao Liu, Huan Wang et al. · IEEE Access 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2023.3339192 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The novel Vehicular Cloud Network (VCN) is a close combination of Vehicular Ad hoc Network, Cloud Computing, and Edge Computing. However, VCN is facing the enormous challenge of security and privacy before it is generalized.
**Approach:** And we summarize the security solutions into two categories: security defense measures based on identity authentication and access control, and security protection measures based on traffic detection and anomaly detection. Thirdly, this paper proposes a reliable VCN security architecture consisting of three types of clouds: vehicular, edge, and central clouds.
