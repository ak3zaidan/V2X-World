# Towards Misbehavior Intelligent Detection Using Guided Machine Learning in Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANET)
Omessaad Slama, Bechir Alaya, Salah Zidi · Inteligencia Artificial 2022 · https://doi.org/10.4114/intartif.vol25iss70pp138-154 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Imbalanced multi-class MBD on VeReMi extension.
**Approach:** Guided learning approach merging similar misbehaviours into classes.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi extension.
**Results:** Random Forest best; binary better than 3-class.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Imbalanced data complicates multi-class learning.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — offline.
**Novelty implications:** Saturated.
