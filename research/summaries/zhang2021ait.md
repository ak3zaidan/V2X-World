# AIT: An AI-Enabled Trust Management System for Vehicular Networks Using Blockchain Technology
Chen-Yue Zhang, Wei Li, Yuan-Sheng Luo, Yupeng Hu · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2020.3044296 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Currently, connected vehicles have gradually stepped into our daily lives, and they generally rely on vehicular networks to generate and exchange traffic-related messages to improve the overall travel safety and efficiency. However, due to the open nature of vehicular networks, these traffic-related messages could be erroneous, which may be caused by various reasons, ranging from an onboard device (OBD) sensor ...
**Approach:** To address these rapidly increasing security challenges, we have proposed an AI-enabled trust management system (AIT) in this article, which is an AI-enabled trust management system for vehicular networks using the blockchain technique. We have conducted an extensive experimental study to evaluate the AIT system.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation results clearly indicate that AIT performs better than existing approaches and can manage the trust of vehicles and detect malicious ones in an accurate and efficient manner.
