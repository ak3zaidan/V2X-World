# VATT&EK: Formalization of Cyber Attacks on Intelligent Transport Systems - a TTP based approach for Automotive and Rail
Ali Recai Yekta, D. Spychalski, Erhan Yekta, Cenk Yekta, Stefan Katzenbeisser · Computer Science in Cars Symposium 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3631204.3631867 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The rapid evolution of Intelligent Transport Systems (ITS) has heightened the cyber vulnerability of modern vehicles, transforming them from isolated mechanical entities to complex interconnected systems.
**Approach:** This paper bridges the existing gaps in the formalization of cyber attacks on ITS, extending common frameworks, like MITRE ATT&CK, to encompass the unique challenges of the automotive and rail sectors. We introduce a multi-modal approach grounded in real-world events, exploring the implications of new technologies in ITS and the convergence of IT and OT security in transportation.
