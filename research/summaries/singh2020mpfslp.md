# MPFSLP: Masqueraded Probabilistic Flooding for Source-Location Privacy in VANETs
Pranav Kumar Singh, Anup Agarwal, Gaurav Nakum, Danda B. Rawat et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2020.3009763 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A major impediment to the adoption of vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs) today is their privacy and security concerns.
**Approach:** In this paper, we address the privacy aspects of VANETs. Specifically we build a scheme that provides non-repudiation, message authentication, integrity, and non-traceability (or source location privacy) while maintaining low overhead in communication to support the security requirements for the beaconing mechanism.
