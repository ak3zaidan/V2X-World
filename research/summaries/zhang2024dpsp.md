# DPSP: A Dynamic Pseudonym Swap Program Based Location Privacy Protection Algorithm for Internet of Vehicles
Wenbo Zhang, Zhiru Guo, Guangjie Han, Hongbo Zhu et al. · IEEE Transactions on Network Science and Engineering 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnse.2024.3392709 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Pseudonym management latency and association attacks.
**Approach:** Fog-based pseudonym management with profit-driven on-demand distribution and dynamic swap areas.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation with 100 vehicles.
**Results:** Anonymity entropy 10.1% and 4% higher than PSNV and DMLP.
