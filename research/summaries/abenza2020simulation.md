# A Simulation Tool for Evaluating Video Streaming Architectures in Vehicular Network Scenarios
Pedro Pablo Garrido Abenza, Manuel P. Malumbres, P. Piñol, Otoniel López Granado · Electronics 2020 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics9111970 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Video-over-VANET research requires many disconnected tools.
**Approach:** VDSF-VN with GatcomSUMO and GatcomVideo automating SUMO/OMNeT++ scenario setup, encoding, runs and reports.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO, OMNeT++, video codecs.
**Results:** Results consistent with literature; reduces setup effort and time through parallel jobs.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Planned: FEC error-protection module
- adaptive video protection
- platform-specific optimisation
- further platform support.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — video streaming; partial overlap only in automating configuration and analysis.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
