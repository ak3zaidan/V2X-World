# Hybrid Scheme of Post-Quantum Cryptography and Elliptic-Curve Cryptography for Certificates ─ A Case Study of Security Credential Management System in Vehicle-to-Everything Communications
Abel C. H. Chen, Bon‐Yeh Lin · International Conference on Circuit, Power and Computing Technologies 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccpct61902.2024.10673280 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** SCMS uses ECC vulnerable to quantum; PQC certificates too large for V2X.
**Approach:** Hybrid certificate scheme: PQC for quantum-resistant trust, ECC for anonymous pseudonym certificates to keep packets short, as a case study of SCMS.
