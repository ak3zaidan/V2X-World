# Exploring Cyber Trends and Threats Towards V2X Connected Vehicles in Malaysia: A Systematic Literature Review
A’in Hazwani Ahmad Rizal, Noor Afiza Mat Razali, Sakinah Ali Pitchay et al. · International Journal of Advanced Computer Science and Applications 2026 · https://doi.org/10.14569/ijacsa.2026.0170157 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The rapid expansion of 5G enabled Vehicle to Everything (V2X) communication has evolved into an intelligent transportation system by supporting applications such as autonomous driving, real-time traffic optimization, and road safety management.
**Approach:** This study proposes a conceptual model, the SCARF-V2X model, an NGSOC integrated concept that utilizes SIEM, SOAR, and Malaysian cyber threat intelligence platforms to enable automated detection and first-layer auto-response, specifically towards supply chain threats in CVs.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The proposed model aims to improve Malaysia's V2X cybersecurity landscape and introduces a proactive and adaptive model to protect CVs against evolving cyber threats.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, the growing connectivity and diverse communication protocols also create major cybersecurity challenges, especially in the network tier of connected vehicles.
- The existing approaches are yet to face limitations in real-time performance, contextual accuracy, and supply chain resilience under Malaysia's tropical urban conditions.
