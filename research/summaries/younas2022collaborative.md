# Collaborative Detection of Black Hole and Gray Hole Attacks for Secure Data Communication in VANETs
Shamim Younas, Faisal Ur Rehman, Tahir Maqsood, Saad Mustafa et al. · Applied Sciences 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/app122312448 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle ad hoc networks (VANETs) are vital towards the success and comfort of self-driving as well as semi-automobile vehicles. Such vehicles rely heavily on data management and the exchange of Cooperative Awareness Messages (CAMs) for external communication with the environment.
**Approach:** In this work, we present a neural network-based technique for detection and prevention of rushed Black and Gray Hole attacks in vehicular networks. Experimental results show a superior detection rate of the proposed system in comparison with state-of-the-art techniques.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting (VANET/IoV/V2X). Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** Experimental results show a superior detection rate of the proposed system in comparison with state-of-the-art techniques.
