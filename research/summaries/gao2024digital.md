# Digital Twin-Enabled Internet of Vehicles Applications
Junting Gao, Chunrong Peng, Tsutomu Yoshinaga, Guorong Han, Siri Guleng, Celimuge Wu · Electronics 2024 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics13071263 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The digital twin (DT) paradigm represents a groundbreaking shift in the Internet of Vehicles (IoV) landscape, acting as an instantaneous digital replica of physical entities.
**Approach:** This synthesis not only refines vehicular design but also substantially augments driver support systems and streamlines traffic governance. Diverging from the prevalent research which predominantly examines DT’s technical assimilation within IoV infrastructures, this review focuses on the specific deployments and goals of DT within the IoV sphere.
