# Intelligent performance inference: A graph neural network approach to modeling maximum achievable throughput in optical networks
Robin Matzner, Ruijie Luo, Georgios Zervas, Polina Bayvel · APL Machine Learning 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0137426 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Max achievable throughput in optical networks is NP-hard to compute.
**Approach:** MPNNs predicting throughput from node/edge features and structure.
**Tools and scale:** Optical networks 10-100 nodes.
**Results:** Up to five orders of magnitude faster than ILP or heuristics with accurate predictions.
