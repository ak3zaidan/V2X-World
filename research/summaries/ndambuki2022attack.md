# Attack Mitigation and Security for Vehicle Platoon
Daniel Kyalo Ndambuki, Hitmi Khalifa Alhitmi · Journal of Cyber Security and Mobility 2022 · https://doi.org/10.13052/jcsm2245-1439.1141 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** Security of VLC-based vehicle platoons.
**Approach:** Colour-shift keying with light-to-frequency converter to detect Sybil, delay and DoS attacks.
**Tools and scale:** Simulated VLC platoon.
**Results:** Qualitative detection via intensity changes.
