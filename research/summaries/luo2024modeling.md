# Modeling and analyzing self-resistance of connected automated vehicular platoons under different cyberattack injection modes
Dongyu Luo, Jiangfeng Wang, Yu Wang, Jiakuan Dong · Accident Analysis &amp; Prevention 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aap.2024.107494 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Whether car-following models themselves affect conclusions about platoon cyberattack resistance.
**Approach:** Review of car-following models; cooperative generalized force model; vulnerable variants of IDM, OVM and platoon dynamics; six self-resistance indicators across bogus, replay/delay and interruption attacks.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical/simulation models.
**Results:** Characterises self-resistance of different car-following models (qualitative in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — choice of traffic model (IDM) affects attack impact; our engine uses IDM.
**Novelty implications:** Model-dependence of attack impact is known; supports counterfactual model-swap studies.
