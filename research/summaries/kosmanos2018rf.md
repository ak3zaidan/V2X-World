# RF Jamming Classification using Relative Speed Estimation in Vehicular Wireless Networks
Dimitrios Kosmanos, Dimitrios Karagiannis, Antonios Argyriou, Spyros Lalis et al. · University of Thessaly Institutional Repository (University of Thessaly) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2021/9959310 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Wireless communications are vulnerable against radio frequency (RF) jamming which might be caused either intentionally or unintentionally. A particular subset of wireless networks, vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANET) which incorporate a series of safety-critical applications, may be a potential target of RF jamming with detrimental safety effects.
**Approach:** In this paper we introduce a detection scheme that is based on supervised learning. Through offline training and the proposed KNN-VRS, RF-VRS classification algorithms, we are able to efficiently detect various cases of Denial of Service Attacks (DoS) jamming attacks, differentiate them from cases of interference as well as foresee a potential danger successfully and act accordingly.
