# Brain Storm Optimization with Deep Learning-Based Intrusion Detection System in Vehicular Adhoc Networks
M. R, Prabakaran G, Priyaradhikadevi T · International Journal of Electrical and Electronics Engineering 2023 · https://doi.org/10.14445/23488379/ijeee-v10i1p117 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular adhoc network (VANET) is an empowering technology in recent transportation systems for offering valuable information and safety, but prone to several attacks, such as active interference and passive eavesdropping.
**Approach:** So, distributed ML becomes a highly suitable structure for designing scalable and applicable collaborative detection techniques over VANETs. Therefore, this paper proposes a brain storm optimization with a deep learning-based intrusion detection system (BSODL-IDS) for VANET.
**Tools and scale:** The experimental validation on the benchmark dataset illustrates the BSODL-IDS method's supremacy over other DL approaches.
**Results:** In addition, the collaborations between vehicles in VANETs could enhance the accuracy level of detection by interacting with their experiences among nodes.
