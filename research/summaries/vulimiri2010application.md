# Application of Secondary Information for Misbehavior Detection in VANETs
Ashish Vulimiri, Arobinda Gupta, Pramit Roy, Skanda N. Muthaiah, Arzad Alam Kherani · Lecture notes in computer science 2010 · https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-12963-6_31 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** False alerts in VANET safety applications.
**Approach:** Probabilistic framework using correlated secondary alerts (Slow/Stopped Vehicle Advisories) to derive a degree of belief in a primary Post-Crash Notification; MDS design.
**Tools and scale:** Traffic simulation of crash scenarios to estimate probabilities.
**Results:** Shows secondary information can be used efficiently for MDS (no figures extracted).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- PCN alerts are periodic so received alerts may be time-shifted from the model grid
- designing MDS for other applications using secondary information.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — event-based MBD.
**Novelty implications:** Early secondary-evidence MBD.
