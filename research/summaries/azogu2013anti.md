# A new anti-jamming strategy for VANET metrics-directed security defense
Ikechukwu K. Azogu, Michael T. Ferreira, Jonathan A. Larcom, Hong Liu · IEEE Globecom Workshops (GC Wkshps) 2013 · https://doi.org/10.1109/glocomw.2013.6825181 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As Vehicular Ad-hoc Network (VANET) becomes a critical infrastructure for road safety and traffic efficiency, its standardization and deployment face serious security challenges. The nature of VANET hinders ineffective most of existing defense schemes for wireless/mobile networks.
**Approach:** This paper studies the impact of jamming on 802.11p, the standard of vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) communications. This work proposes a hideaway strategy, suitable for antijamming in VANET.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** A simulation study measures VANET efficiency protected by the new strategy compared to traditional schemes such as channel surfing.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- As Vehicular Ad-hoc Network (VANET) becomes a critical infrastructure for road safety and traffic efficiency, its standardization and deployment face serious security challenges.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Radio-layer jamming of vehicular links; our engine simulates 802.11p/C-V2X PHY/MAC and can run attacks in closed loop, but this work does not touch PKI/SCMS revocation.
**Novelty implications:** Does not rule out any candidate direction; it does not model SCMS certificates, linkage-based revocation, per-message joint ground truth or counterfactual reruns.
