# Digital Twin of Autonomous Vehicle using Time-Varying Vehicle Parameters Estimation
Shinta Rohmatika Kosmaga, Augie Widyotriatmo, Estiko Rijanto · 2025 9th International Conference on Instrumentation, Control, and Automation (ICA) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ica65945.2025.11252033 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A reliable digital twin requires accurate knowledge of a vehicle’s internal parameters to replicate its dynamic behavior in real time.
**Approach:** However, these parameters may vary due to operational or environmental changes, necessitating an adaptive estimation approach. This study proposes the use of Wide-Array Nonlinear Dynamic Approximation (WyNDA) to estimate key vehicle parameters—including yaw moment of inertia, center of gravity location, and tire cornering stiffness—in real time.
