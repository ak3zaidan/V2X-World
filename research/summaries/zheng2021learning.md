# Learning Based Task Offloading in Digital Twin Empowered Internet of Vehicles
Jinkai Zheng, Tom H. Luan, Longxiang Gao, Yao Zhang, Yuan Wu · arXiv 2021 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.09076 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Mobile edge computing has become an effective and fundamental paradigm for futuristic autonomous vehicles to offload computing tasks.
**Approach:** However, due to the high mobility of vehicles, the dynamics of the wireless conditions, and the uncertainty of the arrival computing tasks, it is difficult for a single vehicle to determine the optimal offloading strategy. In this paper, we propose a Digital Twin (DT) empowered task offloading framework for Internet of Vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Simulation results demonstrate that our algorithm can effectively find the optimal offloading strategy, as well as achieve the fast convergence speed and high performance, compared with other existing approaches.
