# Blockchain-Based Traffic Event Validation and Trust Verification for VANETs
Yao-Tsung Yang, Li-Der Chou, Chia-Wei Tseng, Fan-Hsun Tseng et al. · IEEE Access 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2019.2903202 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sharing traffic information on the vehicular network can help in the implementation of intelligent traffic management, such as car accident warnings, road construction notices, and driver route changes to reduce traffic congestion earlier. In the future, in the case of autonomous driving, traffic information will be exchanged more frequently and more immediately.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a proof-of-event consensus concept applicable to vehicular networks rather than proof-of-work or proof-of-authority approaches.
**Tools and scale:** Sharing traffic information on the vehicular network can help in the implementation of intelligent traffic management, such as car accident warnings, road construction notices, and driver route changes to reduce traffic congestion earlier.
**Results:** Sharing traffic information on the vehicular network can help in the implementation of intelligent traffic management, such as car accident warnings, road construction notices, and driver route changes to reduce traffic congestion earlier. The simulation results show that the proposed mechanism can effectively feedback the correctness of traffic events and provide traceable events with trust verification.
