# Estimation of Causal Effects Under K-Nearest Neighbors Interference
Samirah Alzubaidi, Michael J. Higgins · arXiv 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.15204 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Considerable recent work has focused on methods for analyzing experiments which exhibit treatment interference -- that is, when the treatment status of one unit may affect the response of another unit.
**Approach:** We consider a model of treatment interference -- the K-nearest neighbors interference model (KNNIM) -- for which the response of one unit depends not only on the treatment status given to that unit, but also the treatment status of its $K$ ``closest'' neighbors. We propose unbiased estimators for these estimands and derive conservative variance estimates for these unbiased estimators.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** We apply our methodology to an experiment designed to assess the impact of a conflict-reducing program in middle schools in New Jersey, and we give evidence that the effect of treatment propagates primarily through a unit's closest connection.
