# Secure Event-Triggered Control for Vehicle Platooning in the Presence of Modification Attacks
Ali Nikoutadbir, Sajjad Torabi, Sadegh Bolouki · arXiv 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13415 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper addresses the problem of achieving secure consensus in a vehicular platoon using event-triggered control.
**Approach:** To tackle this problem, the paper proposes a resilient event-triggered control scheme that ensures secure consensus while considering constraints on the duration and frequency of attacks. The paper also introduces an attack mitigation strategy through a topology-switching procedure, which is employed when the attack frequency and duration constraints are not met.
**Tools and scale:** Finally, the effectiveness of the proposed methodology is demonstrated through simulation scenarios in various case studies.
