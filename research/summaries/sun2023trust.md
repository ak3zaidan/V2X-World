# Trust Model Based on Recommendation Filtering in Internet of Vehicles
Sheng Sun, Xiumei Fan, Yan Xiao · 2023 2nd International Conference on Computing, Communication, Perception and Quantum Technology (CCPQT) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ccpqt60491.2023.00068 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Trust management can help vehicles judge whether the received messages are reliable and identify malicious nodes in the network.
**Approach:** To solve the problem that the current trust model fails to select appropriate recommendation nodes and dynamically adjust the weight of recommendation trust according to the environment, this paper proposes a trust model based on recommendation value filtering based on the topology structure of the Internet of Vehicles. The model is divided into three layers: local trust management, cluster trust management and global trust management.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The three concepts of time decay function, sliding window mechanism and penalty factor are introduced to calculate the direct trust value, which effectively reduces the timeliness of trust and the influence of switch attack. Then, according to the actual dynamic environment of the Internet of Vehicles, the adaptive weight is used to conduct the comprehensive evaluation of the nodes to improve the adaptability of the model.
