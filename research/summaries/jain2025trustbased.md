# Trust‐Based Reliability Scheme for Secure Data Sharing With Internet of Vehicles Networks
Arpit Jain, Ashok Kumar, Mahadev, Jitendra Kumar Chaudhary, Saurabh Singh · Internet Technology Letters 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1002/itl2.70000 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the Internet of Things (IoT) increasingly integrated into vehicles, drivers and passengers can access information anywhere, anytime. As the number of connected vehicles increases, new requirements for vehicular networks arise, including securing, robust, and scalable communication between vehicles and pedestrians.
**Approach:** It encompasses the communication between vehicles and infrastructure, as well as the communication between vehicles and pedestrians. A real‐time exchange of road condition information can be achieved through this method.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** According to simulation results, the proposed model is highly accurate and maintains a low detection delay while protecting evaluation data privacy.
