# How Does a Digital Twin Network Work Well for Connected and Automated Vehicles: Joint Perception, Planning, and Control
Ya Kang, Qingyang Song, Jing Song, Fengsheng Pan et al. · IEEE Vehicular Technology Magazine 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mvt.2023.3328107 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The cutting-edge technology of connected and automated vehicles (CAVs) will advance transportation systems for the foreseeable future.
**Approach:** In this article, we design a DT network (DTN) consisting of connected DTs to help CAVs in terms of ubiquitous perception, adaptive path planning, and precise motion control. Qualitative evaluation of the proposed system is performed with the final goal of demonstrating the DTN’s assistance in improving the performance and effectiveness of CAVs, ultimately leading to a safer, more efficient, and more sustainable transportation system.
