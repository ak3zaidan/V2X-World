# A cooperative trust model addressing CAM-and CPM-based Ghost Vehicles in IoV
Runbo Su, Jin, Yujun, Ye‐Qiong Song · HAL (Le Centre pour la Communication Scientifique Directe) 2024 · https://hal.science/hal-04453209 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Ghost vehicles injected via CAM and CPM by insiders after PKI authentication.
**Approach:** Cooperative trust assessment model against CAM- and CPM-based ghost vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Veins with an integrated ETSI Collective Perception Service; small scenarios (few vehicles).
**Results:** Preliminary feasibility; effective assessment of message trustworthiness.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Analyse the resilience of the countermeasures (future work).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — insider attacks inside a simulation with CPM.
**Novelty implications:** CPM ghost-vehicle MBD in Veins exists; scale and PKI loop remain open.
