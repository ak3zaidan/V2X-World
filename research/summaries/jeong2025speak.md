# Speak to Simulate: An LLM-Guided Agentic Framework for Traffic Simulation in SUMO
Minwoo Jeong, Jeeyun Chang, Yoonjin Yoon · GeoSim@SIGSPATIAL 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3764921.3770151 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Traffic simulation plays a pivotal role in shaping transportation policy amid increasing urban traffic congestion and evolving mobility patterns. As a leading simulator, Simulation of Urban MObility (SUMO) is a widely adopted open-source platform for urban mobility research.
**Approach:** To address these limitations, we propose AgentSUMO, an LLM-guided agentic framework for traffic simulation.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: SUMO, LLM. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** On the Manhattan network, our framework demonstrates that AgentSUMO addresses high-level policy goals through task planning and policy selection, resulting in consistent improvements in key traffic flow metrics.
