# An Examination of Vehicle Ad Hoc Network (VANET) Affected by Wormhole Attacks
Asroni, Ronald Adrian, Raditya Pratama Putra, Jeckson et al. · International Conference on Intelligent Computing 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icic68054.2025.11309455 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Mobile Ad-hoc Networks (MANETs) and VeVehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs) have significant potential to improve connectivity and transportation efficiency in urban areas. However, both types of networks are also vulnerable to wormhole attacks that can disrupt the network operations and endanger the safety of road users.
**Approach:** In In this study, SUMO and MATLAB were used to simulate a VANET near RS PKU Muhammadiyah Yogyakarta to improve the security of the AODV protocol against wormholes.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting (VANET/IoV/V2X). Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** The results of the simulation show that the AODV algorithm effectively finds the best route, and the An n-type sensor is useful for finding the base station node, Even though the wormhole causes the delay to increase by 0.17 degrees and the loss of throughput.
