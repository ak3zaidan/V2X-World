# VoI-Driven Joint Optimization of Control and Communication in Vehicular Digital Twin Network
Lei Lei, Kan Zheng, Jie Mei,  Xuemin,  Shen · IEEE Network 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mnet.2024.3522588 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Joint optimisation of control and communication in vehicular DT networks.
**Approach:** VDTN architecture, two Value-of-Information definitions rooted in control performance, iterative pair of DRL modules for control and communication.
**Tools and scale:** Platoon simulation.
**Results:** Stability and transmission efficiency shown (no numbers extracted).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- VoI definitions can be expanded; VoI estimation and VoI-driven optimisation are future directions.
