# A Blockchain-Based Hierarchical Reputation Management Scheme in Vehicular Network
Wenbin Dong, Yang Li, Rong-Hui Hou, Xixiang Lv et al. · Global Communications Conference 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/globecom38437.2019.9013631 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The vehicular reputation management scheme based on blockchain has been studied with the rapidly development of blockchain technology. Existing works mainly focus on provisioning the security of the reputation management scheme by using the blockchain technology.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a hierarchical blockchain based reputation management scheme in vehicular networks. Moreover, our designed blockchain structure aims at reducing the confirmation time of reputation values and increasing the capacity of blockchain.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset, blockchain (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** We finally conduct simulation experiments to compare our proposed scheme with the existing schemes.
