# Detecting Sybil Attacks Using Proofs of Work and Location in VANETs
Mohamed Baza, Mahmoud Nabil, Mohamed M. E. A. Mahmoud, Niclas Bewermeier et al. · IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tdsc.2020.2993769 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Pseudonym abundance enables Sybil attacks that pollute traffic data.
**Approach:** RSU-signed time-stamped location tags forming anonymous trajectories (threshold signatures across RSUs) plus computational puzzles.
**Tools and scale:** Analysis and simulation.
**Results:** Attackers need to compromise infeasibly many RSUs (details in full text).
