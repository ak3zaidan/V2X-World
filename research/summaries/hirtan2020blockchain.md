# Blockchain-based Reputation for Intelligent Transportation Systems
Liviu-Adrian Hîrţan, Ciprian Dobre, Horacio González-Vélez · Sensors 2020 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s20030791 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A disruptive technology often used in finance, Internet of Things (IoT) and healthcare, blockchain can reach consensus within a decentralised network—potentially composed of large amounts of unreliable nodes—and to permanently and irreversibly store data in a tamper-proof manner. In this paper, we present a reputation system for Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS).
**Approach:** In this paper, we present a reputation system for Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS). We present results with a simulation for three cities: San Francisco, Rome and Beijing. We have demonstrated the impact of malicious attacks as the average speed decreased if erroneous information was stored in the blockchain as an implemented routing algorithm guides the honest cars on other free routes, and thus crowds other intersections.
