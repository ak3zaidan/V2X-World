# Pseudonym Certificate Validations under Heavy Vehicular Traffic Loads
S. Jha, Chaitanya Yavvari, D. Wijesekera · IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc.2018.8628399 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** SCMS message validation at the OBU takes about 22 ms; 10 Hz BSMs under heavy traffic create processing delays.
**Approach:** Three-level priority message processing based on ego vehicle's immediate neighbourhood; proposes SCMS cross-certification across administrative boundaries.
**Tools and scale:** Realistic highway scenarios; deadline miss ratio and queue length.
**Results:** Algorithm performs well under increasing traffic loads.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Proposes extending SCMS with cross-certification for validation across individualised trust roots
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — Verification-load prioritisation under heavy traffic in SCMS (direction 2) and multi-root SCMS interoperability (runner-up).
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first SCMS verification-priority policy under load, nor first proposal of SCMS cross-certification; open: safety outcome and multi-provider simulation.
