# A Privacy-Preserving Mutual Authentication Resisting DoS Attacks in VANETs
Cong Sun, Jiao Liu, Xinpeng Xu, Jian-Feng Ma · IEEE Access 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2017.2768499 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Providing efficient anonymous authentication in vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) is a challenging issue. Identity-based signature schemes have been used to provide privacy-preserving authentication effectively for VANETs.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a conditional privacy-preserving mutual authentication framework with denial-of-service attack resistance called MADAR.
**Tools and scale:** We have formally proved the privacy preservation, unlinkability, mutual authenticity, and correctness of pseudonym with ProVerif, and analyzed other security objectives.
**Results:** Beyond the privacy preservation and non-repudiation achieved by the existing framework, our authentication framework provides asymmetric inter-vehicle mutual authentication and strength-alterable computational DoS-attack resistance. The performance evaluations are conducted and the results demonstrate that our framework can achieve these security objectives with moderate computation and communication overheads.
