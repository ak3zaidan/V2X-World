# Intelligent Detection of Black Hole Attacks for Secure Communication in Autonomous and Connected Vehicles

**Problem:** Black hole attacks in VANET routing.
**Approach:** IDBA using hop count, sequence number, PDR and E2E delay.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation vs BAODV, IdsAODV, EAODV.
**Results:** Better PDR, E2E, overhead, loss and throughput.
