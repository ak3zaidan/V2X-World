# VANETGuard: A Scalable Lightweight Trust Management System for 5G-Enabled Smart Vehicular Networks
Reem Almaziad, Heba A. Kurdi · Preprints.org 2025 · https://doi.org/10.20944/preprints202505.0242.v1 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) are essential to intelligent transportation systems (ITS), enabling secure, real-time communication among vehicles and infrastructure.
**Approach:** This paper presents VANETGuard, a lightweight scalable trust management system that enhances security and scalability in 5G-enabled smart vehicular networks. The proposed system integrates entropy-based anomaly detection, Bayesian inference for adaptive trust scoring, and a lightweight distributed ledger for decentralized, tamper-resistant trust storage.
**Tools and scale:** This paper presents VANETGuard, a lightweight scalable trust management system that enhances security and scalability in 5G-enabled smart vehicular networks. **Results:** Large-scale simulations under realistic traffic and attack conditions demonstrate that VANETGuard achieves 99.97% detection accuracy, significantly reduces false positives, and maintains low latency and computational overhead while supporting over 300 vehicles.
