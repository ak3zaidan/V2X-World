# Lattice-Based Revocable Certificateless Public Key Encryption for Team Score Orienteering
You Zhao, Mingyan Yan, Kaien Yan, Juyan Li · Mathematics 2024 · https://doi.org/10.3390/math12111706 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Team score orienteering, a challenging and interesting sport, is gradually becoming known by the majority of sports enthusiasts.
**Approach:** Therefore, this paper proposes an efficient lattice-based revocable certificateless public key encryption (RCL-PKE) scheme with decryption key exposure resistance (DKER). Experiments indicated that the proposed RCL-PKE scheme had lower computation and communication costs, making it particularly suitable for team score orienteering.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** The constructed RCL-PKE scheme was proven to be IND-CPA secure under the learning with errors (LWE) assumption.
