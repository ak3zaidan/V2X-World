# Enhancing Autonomous Vehicles System Security: Advanced Attack Detection for Robust Safeguarding
Fatemeh Akbarian, Dimitrios Papageorgiou, Seyedezahra Chamideh, Jeppe Heini Mikkelsen, Peter Iwer Hoedt Karstensen, Maria Kihl · 2024 10th International Conference on Control, Decision and Information Technologies (CoDIT) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/codit62066.2024.10708181 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The advances in highly automated and autonomous transportation systems over the last decade have generated great interest in topics in the safe navigation of land vehicles.
**Approach:** With distributed control strategies employed in the majority of applications of autonomous vehicles, such as traffic and formation control, the much-required resilience takes the form of fault-tolerance with respect to information corruption, especially, when such information is utilized in closed-loop control. This study addresses the topic of detection of malicious attacks in a decentralized traffic control system for land vehicles.
