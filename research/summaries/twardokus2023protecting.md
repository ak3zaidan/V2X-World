# Toward Protecting 5G Sidelink Scheduling in C-V2X Against Intelligent DoS Attacks
Geoff Twardokus, Hanif Rahbari · IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/twc.2023.3249665 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** 5G C-V2X security covers only payloads; PHY attributes and decentralized MAC scheduling are exploitable.
**Approach:** Two stealthy low-duty-cycle DoS attacks on NR sidelink scheduling, plus ML detection and mitigation using Rel-17 features.
**Tools and scale:** Hardware testbed with USRPs and commercial C-V2X kits, plus network and roadway simulations.
**Results:** Target PDR reduced by 90% or channel PDR below 25% within seconds; mitigation thwarts one attack completely and reduces the other's impact by 80%.

- None stated in abstract.
