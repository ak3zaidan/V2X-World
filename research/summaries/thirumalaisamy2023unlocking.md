# Unlocking the Potential of VANETs: Trust-Based Authentication and Deep Learning for Enhanced Security and Efficiency
Manikandan Thirumalaisamy, Michael George, Arul Uthirapathy, Gnanajeyaraman Rajaram, Selvakumar Alagappan, Ramesh Sundar · venue unknown 2023 · https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-3091458/v1 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Adhoc Networks (VANETs) are emerging as a crucial component in the development of Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS).
**Approach:** These networks aim to enhance traffic operations, increase safety, and facilitate communication between vehicles and infrastructure. However, VANETs face significant privacy and security challenges.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The hybrid multipath energy-efficient routing protocol contributes to improved energy efficiency. The proposed technique achieved energy efficiency of 99%, latency of 63%, throughput of 95%, PDR of 88%, computational cost of 57%, communication overhead of 59%, and trust value analysis of 77%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, VANETs face significant privacy and security challenges.
