# Baa-iov: Blockchain-enabled anonymous authentication for internet of vehicles

**Problem:** As a core component of intelligent transportation systems, the Internet of Vehicles (IoV) relies heavily on secure and efficient authentication mechanisms to support real-time information exchange and data sharing among network entities. **Approach:** To address these challenges, this paper proposes a novel blockchain-based multi-Trusted Authority (TA) collaborative authentication and key agreement scheme.
