# Revocation in Vehicular Public Key Infrastructures: Balancing privacy and efficiency
Marcos A. Simplicio, Eduardo Lopes Cominetti, Harsh Kupwade Patil, Jefferson E. Ricardini et al. · Vehicular Communications 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vehcom.2020.100309 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Revocation with many pseudonyms is complex.
**Approach:** Compares C-ITS frequent provisioning, CRLs, and ACPC activation codes (SCMS extension) on efficiency and privacy; unicast activation code distribution.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** ACPC has advantages over C-ITS (distributed caching) and CRLs (no vehicle processing overhead); unicast 16-byte codes trade privacy vs bandwidth.

- None stated in the abstract.
