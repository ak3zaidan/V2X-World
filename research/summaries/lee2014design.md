# Design of V2I Based Vehicle Identification number In a VANET Environment
Joo-Kwan Lee, Byeong-Il Park, Jae-Pyo Park, Mun-Seok Jun · Journal of the Korea Academia-Industrial cooperation Society 2014 · https://doi.org/10.5762/kais.2014.15.12.7292 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Secure V2I messaging with vehicle identification numbers (Korean, English abstract).
**Approach:** Identity-based encryption using VIN and RSU serial for registration and messaging.
**Tools and scale:** Comparison with VPKI.
**Results:** About 44% faster than VPKI.
