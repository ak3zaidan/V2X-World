# Large-Scale VANET Simulations and Performance Analysis using Real Taxi Trace and City Map Data
Pietro E. Carnelli, Mahesh Sooriyabandara, R. Eddie Wilson · 2018 IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference (VNC) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc.2018.8628352 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Can taxi-only V2V networks deliver data across cities?
**Approach:** Real taxi traces (Rome, San Francisco) with OSM buildings; LOS analysis; all-to-one and one-to-all scenarios.
**Tools and scale:** Real traces, OSM.
**Results:** 50 m LOS threshold in over 90% cases; doubling fleet cuts delay by factor 0.6-0.7; delivery saturates at 0.67-0.71; over 90% of taxis receive broadcast within one hour.
