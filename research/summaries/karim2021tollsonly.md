# TollsOnly Please—Homomorphic Encryption for Toll Transponder Privacy in Internet of Vehicles
H. Karim, D. Rawat · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2021.3056240 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cities have circumvented privacy norms and deployed sensors to track vehicles via toll transponders (like E-Zpass tags). The ethical problems regarding these practices have been highlighted by various privacy advocacy groups.
**Approach:** Specifically, we present a privacy risk reduction model for electronic toll transponder data.
