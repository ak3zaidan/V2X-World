# Adaptive Digital Twin for UAV-Assisted Integrated Sensing, Communication, and Computation Networks
Bin Li, Wen-Shuai Liu, Wancheng Xie, N. Zhang, Yan Zhang · IEEE Transactions on Green Communications and Networking 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tgcn.2023.3298039 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we study a digital twin (DT)-empowered integrated sensing, communication, and computation network.
**Approach:** Specifically, the users perform radar sensing and computation offloading on the same spectrum, while unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) are deployed to provide edge computing service. We first formulate a multi-objective optimization problem to minimize the beampattern performance of multi-input multi-output (MIMO) radars and the computation offloading energy consumption simultaneously.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** To track this challenge, we reformulate the original problem as a multi-agent Markov decision process and design a multi-agent proximal policy optimization (MAPPO) framework to achieve a flexible learning policy. Furthermore, the Beta-policy and attention mechanism are used to improve the training performance.
