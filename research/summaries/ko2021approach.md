# An Approach to Detecting Malicious Information Attacks for Platoon Safety
Byungjin Ko, Sang Hyuk Son · IEEE Access 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2021.3095480 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Malicious information attacks destabilise CACC platoons.
**Approach:** LMID LSTM detector for correlated and non-correlated attacks by an insider member.
**Tools and scale:** PLEXE; various trajectories, attack types and emergency braking.
**Results:** Over 96% accuracy and fast detection; without fast detection, crashes may occur.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future work section not extracted.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — attack causes crashes and detection speed matters.
**Novelty implications:** Supports time-to-detect importance; does not cover revocation.
