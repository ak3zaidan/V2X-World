# An Aggregate Signature Scheme Based on a Trapdoor Hash Function for the Internet of Things
Hong Shu, Fulong Chen, Dong Xie, Li-Ping Sun et al. · Italian National Conference on Sensors 2019 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s19194239 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Bandwidth and computation in IoT signatures.
**Approach:** Multi-trapdoor hash based aggregate signature, constant length.
**Tools and scale:** ECDLP proof.
**Results:** Higher efficiency than pairing schemes.
