# Trust Based Approaches for Secure Routing in VANET: A Survey
Nirav J. Patel, Rutvij H. Jhaveri · Procedia Computer Science 2015 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.procs.2015.03.112 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract Vehicular Ad-hoc networks (VANETs) require trusted vehicles to vehicles communication. VANET is multidimensional network in which the vehicles continuously change their locations.
**Approach:** In this paper, we present survey of various mechanisms to improve different ad-hoc routing protocols for secure routing process by enhancing the trust among different nodes in VANETs.
