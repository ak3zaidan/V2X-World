# Look One Step Ahead: Forward-Looking Incentive Design with Strategic Privacy for Proactive Service Provisioning over Air-Ground Integrated Edge Networks

**Problem:** In air-ground integrated networks (AGINs), unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) provide on-demand edge services to ground vehicles.
**Approach:** To address these challenges, we propose look one-step ahead (LOSA), a novel framework for efficient and privacy-aware service provisioning. We analytically corroborate that LOSA guarantees truthfulness, individual rationality, and budget balance.
