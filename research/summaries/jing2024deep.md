**Problem:** Internet of Vehicles is an intelligent transportation network system with complex and highly distributed characteristics.
**Approach:** The ability to share information between vehicles, pedestrians, infrastructure, and the cloud has created rich opportunities for deploying various transportation applications and services for Intelligent Transportation System. However, the problem of sharing the results of node trust evaluation has been seriously neglected in existing studies.
