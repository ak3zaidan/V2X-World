# A HIL platform for evaluating OBU performance in C-V2X scenarios
Wenxiong Yi, Fei Ma, Bowen Rao, Xiaofan Yu, Guoping Tan, Siyuan Zhou · International Conference on Intelligent Traffic Systems and Smart City (ITSSC 2021) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2627784 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper we describe a hardware-in-the-loop platform (HIL) for evaluating the performance of On-Board-Unit (OBU) in C-V2X scenario.
**Approach:** We use CARLA as the simulation environment to obtain high fidelity scenario data. In this platform we design the basic evaluation scenarios based on current standards and then design random scenario parameters for obtaining a large number of test scenarios with different parameters to test the robustness of the algorithm.
