# Digital-Twin-Enabled City-Model-Aware Deep Learning for Dynamic Channel Estimation in Urban Vehicular Environments
Cao Ding, Ivan Wang-Hei Ho · IEEE Transactions on Green Communications and Networking 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tgcn.2022.3173414 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Deterministic or stochastic channel models are unrealistic for urban vehicular channels.
**Approach:** Digital twin ray tracing of a 3D city model trains a city-model-aware DNN channel estimator; a basis expansion model reduces computation.
**Tools and scale:** Ray-tracing DT plus empirical measurements.
**Results:** More than 32% BER improvement over generic DNN estimators.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Ray tracing is computationally expensive (addressed partially with BEM).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - city-scale vehicular channel modelling; our engine uses Veins-style PHY models.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on security directions.
