# A Comprehensive Survey on Jammer Detection and Localization in 5G Networks with Higher-Order Statistics and Unsupervised Learning
Michel Kulhandjian, Hovannes Kulhandjian · Pattern Recognition and Image Analysis 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1134/s1054661825701421 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract Jamming attacks pose a significant threat to 5G networks and connected systems such as vehicular networks, critical infrastructure, and public safety communications.
**Approach:** This paper presents an extensive survey of jammer types, detection algorithms, mitigation strategies, and localization techniques. In addition to the survey, we present a unified framework to detect and localize Gaussian noise jammers (and extendable to smart/protocol-aware jammers) by combining fourth-order cumulant-based 2D-MUSIC for angle-of-arrival estimation with unsupervised anomaly detection via adversarial autoencoders.
