# A Review on Security Attacks in Vehicular Ad hoc Network
R. Priya, N. Sivakumar, M. Thirumaran · International Journal on Future Revolution in Computer Science & Communication Engineering 2017 · http://ijfrcsce.org/index.php/ijfrcsce/article/view/437 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Whenever a communication takes place between two or more vehicles there has been a need for protection.
**Approach:** The attacker can gain access to the network by compromising either the vehicle or road side unit or the communication medium that transfers the messages between vehicles. Vehicular Ad hoc Network (VANET) have motivated the interest towards the passenger comfort and secure driving environment.
