# It is not (only) about privacy: How multi-party computation redefines control, trust, and risk in data sharing
Wirawan Agahari, H. Ofe, M. Reuver · Electronic Markets 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s12525-022-00572-w · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Firms are often reluctant to share data because of mistrust, concerns over control, and other risks. Multi-party computation (MPC) is a new technique to compute meaningful insights without having to transfer data.
**Approach:** This paper investigates if MPC affects known antecedents for data sharing decisions: control, trust, and risks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Through 23 qualitative interviews in the automotive industry, we find that MPC (1) enables new ways of technology-based control, (2) reduces the need for inter-organizational trust, and (3) prevents losing competitive advantage due to data leakage. Our findings show that known antecedents of data sharing should be specified differently with MPC in place.
