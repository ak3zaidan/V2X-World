# Privacy-Preserving Certificate Linkage/Revocation in VANETs Without Linkage Authorities
Marcos Antonio Simplicio Junior, Eduardo Lopes Cominetti, Harsh Kupwade Patil, Jefferson E. Ricardini, Leonardo T. D. Ferraz, Marcos Vinicius Maciel da Silva · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2020.2981907 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** SCMS revocation has privacy weaknesses and LAs add cost and attack surface.
**Approach:** Describes and fixes two birthday attacks on SCMS certificate revocation; proposes an SCMS variant without Linkage Authorities; unveils replay attacks.
**Tools and scale:** Cryptographic analysis.
**Results:** Improved long-term privacy and reduced attack surface (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong - directly analyses the US SCMS linkage/revocation mechanism our engine implements (LA1/LA2, linkage values).
**Novelty implications:** Known SCMS revocation attacks and LA-free variants exist; our engine could execute these attacks and variants, but the attacks themselves are not new.
