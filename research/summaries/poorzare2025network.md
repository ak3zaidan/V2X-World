# Network Digital Twin Toward Networking, Telecommunications, and Traffic Engineering: A Survey
Reza Poorzare, Dimitris N. Kanellopoulos, Varun Kumar Sharma, Poulami Dalapati et al. · IEEE Access 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2025.3531947 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Network Digital Twin (NDT) is an evolving technology that provides a framework through which a network administrator can have a virtual representation of a computer network. As a result, analysis, monitoring, testing, running new protocols, and more can be performed using the NDT before the final deployment of the developed approach.
**Approach:** Accordingly, this paper presents a comprehensive survey on how NDT can facilitate advancements in network traffic engineering across a wide range of networking domains. First, we start with an in-depth analysis of the evolution of the network digital twin technology and provide a comparison with simulation tools.
