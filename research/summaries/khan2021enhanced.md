# An Enhanced Multi-Stage Deep Learning Framework for Detecting Malicious Activities From Autonomous Vehicles
I. A. Khan, N. Moustafa, De-Chang Pi, Waqas Haider et al. · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2021.3105834 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS), particularly Autonomous Vehicles (AVs), are susceptible to safety and security concerns that impend people’s lives. Nothing like manually controlled vehicles, the safekeeping of communications and computing constituents of AVs can be threatened using sophisticated hacking techniques, consequently disrupting AVs from the operative usage in our daily life routines.
**Approach:** In this paper, we present a multi-stage intrusion detection framework to identify intrusions from ITSs and produce low rate of false alarms.
**Tools and scale:** The outcomes indicated that the proposed framework achieves high performance that outperforms various current state-of-the-art systems with an accuracy rate of 98.88% for the UNSWNB-15 dataset and 99.11% for the car hacking dataset.
**Results:** The outcomes indicated that the proposed framework achieves high performance that outperforms various current state-of-the-art systems with an accuracy rate of 98.88% for the UNSWNB-15 dataset and 99.11% for the car hacking dataset.
