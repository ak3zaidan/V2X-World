# DT-FU: Digital Twin-Driven Federated Unlearning for Resilient Vehicular Networks in the 6G Era
Wathsara Daluwatta, Shehan Edirimannage, Charitha Elvitigala, Ibrahim Khalil, Mohammed Atiquzzaman · IEEE Communications Magazine 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mcom.001.2400229 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In the evolving landscape of federated learning management systems (FLMS), ensuring robust security against adversarial attacks is paramount.
**Approach:** However, traditional methods that filter harmful updates before aggregation often fail in already compromised networks. This research introduces a pioneering approach that integrates digital twin (DT) technology with an FLMS, bolstering the network's resilience and safeguarding communication among distributed networks in the 6G era.
