# A Blockchain-Assisted Anonymous Authentication Protocol for Data Reporting in VANETs
Ruiguo Su · International Conference on the Software Process 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icsp69961.2026.11540692 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) have recently gained growing attention. In VANETs, vehicles report traffic data to Roadside Units (RSUs) to improve Intelligent Transport Systems (ITS) services.
**Approach:** Many anonymous authentication data reporting protocols have been proposed to ensure security and privacy. To address these issues, this paper proposes a blockchain-assisted anonymous authentication protocol for data reporting in VANETs.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting (VANET/IoV/V2X). Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** Our protocol ensures security and privacy with lower computational overhead than P2BA and TRAJ (slightly higher than GSAA-PS), and the lowest communication overhead overall.
