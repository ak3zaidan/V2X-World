# An Adversarial Machine Learning Approach for Generating Stealthy BSM Data in V2V Communication
Saumya Buch, Ikjot Saini · International Conference on High Performance Switching and Routing 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/hpsr68369.2026.11615176 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** MDSs are vulnerable to evasive BSMs.
**Approach:** Neural adversarial generator producing realistic perturbed BSMs that evade a fixed MDS.
**Tools and scale:** BSM dataset with a pretrained MDS.
**Results:** MDS with perfect test classification is still evaded under constrained perturbations.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Assumes fixed detection model and constrained feature modifications.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — adaptive attacks against MBD.
**Novelty implications:** Adversarial evasion of MBD is shown offline; closed-loop evasion against MA revocation remains open.
