# Research on Identity-based Batch Anonymous Authentication Scheme for VANET
Cheng Song, Xinan Gu, Lei Wang, Zhi-Zhong Liu et al. · KSII Transactions on Internet and Information Systems 2019 · https://doi.org/10.3837/tiis.2019.12.021 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** To slove the security and efficiency problem of anonymous authentication in vehicular ad-hoc network (VANET), we adopt the bilinear pairing theory to propose an identity-based batch anonymous authentication scheme for VANET.
**Approach:** The tamper-proof device in the on-board unit and the trusted authority jointly realize the anonymity of vehicle identity and the signature of messages, which further enhances the security of this scheme, as well as reduces the overhead of trusted authority.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** The tamper-proof device in the on-board unit and the trusted authority jointly realize the anonymity of vehicle identity and the signature of messages, which further enhances the security of this scheme, as well as reduces the overhead of trusted authority. Batch authentication can improve the efficiency of anonymous authentication for VANET.
