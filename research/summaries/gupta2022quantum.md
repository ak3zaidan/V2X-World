# Quantum-Defended Blockchain-Assisted Data Authentication Protocol for Internet of Vehicles
Daya Sagar Gupta, Arijit Karati, Walid Saad, D. D. da Costa · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2022.3144785 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The so-called Internet of Vehicle (IoV) systems will interconnect numerous vehicles to communicate significant information through an Internet of Things (IoT) enabled network. It has emerged as a promising system wherein various data authentication techniques have been introduced using clumsy certificate management and Diffie-Hellman (DH) assumption.
**Approach:** In this paper, a novel certificateless data authentication protocol is designed, enabling security features in open wireless communication in the IoV.
**Tools and scale:** Performance analysis exhibits the simulation orchestration and shows the way the proposed protocol outperforms other related techniques in energy consumption, data computation, communication, and cryptographic key storage overheads.
