# A PUF-Based Lightweight Authentication Scheme for UAV-Assisted Internet of Vehicles
Jih-Ye Choi, D. Kwon, Seunghwan Son, Yohan Park et al. · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2025.3564581 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs)-assisted Internet of Vehicles (IoV) utilizes flexible mobility of UAVs to improve communication issues in traditional IoV model.
**Approach:** Therefore, UAV-assisted IoV can contribute to the development of Intelligent Transportation System (ITS). In 2024, Miao et al. proposed an elliptic curve cryptography (ECC)-based authentication scheme for UAV-assisted IoV.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** To prove the robustness of the proposed scheme, we perform “Burrows-Abadi-Needham (BAN) logic”, “Real-or-Random (RoR) model”, and “Automated Verification of Internet Security Protocols and Applications (AVISPA)” based formal security analysis, and informal analysis. Consequently, we establish that the proposed scheme is appropriate and efficient for UAV-assisted IoV.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "In this article, we propose a secure and lightweight authentication scheme for UAV-assisted IoV, considering the computational limitations of UAVs."
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
