# A Delay Analysis for Distributed Model Predictive Control on Vehicles Platooning through a Low-Cost Hardware-In-the-Loop Testbed
Enrico Landolfi, Alessandro Salvi, Alfredo Troiano, Ciro Natale · 2022 IEEE Conference on Control Technology and Applications (CCTA) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ccta49430.2022.9966150 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Platoon DMPC must be robust to communication delays.
**Approach:** Low-cost Raspberry Pi HIL testbed emulating a DMPC platoon.
**Tools and scale:** Raspberry Pi HIL.
**Results:** Good robustness to variable transmission delay of 100-400 ms.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Real-road tests are next.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
