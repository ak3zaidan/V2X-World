# Detection of false position attacks in VANETs through bagging ensemble learning
Bekan Kitaw Mekonen, Lemi Bane, Negasa Berhanu Fite · PLoS ONE 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0328829 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Position falsification detection.
**Approach:** Bagging ensembles of CART, RF, kNN, MLP at RSU level.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi.
**Results:** kNN-bagging 100% on attack 1, 99.87% attack 2, 97.85% attack 16.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Needs real-world validation, broader attacks and network conditions
- future hybrid/DL/RL and OMNeT++ simulation for deployment constraints.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — offline.
**Novelty implications:** Saturated.
