# Blockchain-Based Pseudonym Management Scheme for Vehicular Communication
Sonia Alice George, Steffie Maria Stephen, A. Jaekel · Electronics 2021 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics10131584 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A vehicular ad hoc network (VANET) consists of vehicles, roadside units, and other infrastructures that communicate with each other with the goal of improving road safety, reducing accidents, and alleviating traffic congestion. **Approach:** In this paper, we propose a new blockchain-based decentralized pseudonym management scheme for VANET.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Privacy can be improved by using pseudonyms instead of actual vehicle identities during communication. We compared the performance of the proposed approach with a traditional PKI scheme and shown that it significantly reduces the authentication delay.
