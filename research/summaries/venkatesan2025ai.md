# AI-Driven Digital Twin Framework for Security Threat Simulation and Compliance Optimization
V. Venkatesan, Chakkaravarthy Arunachalam · International Carnahan Conference on Security Technology 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccst63435.2025.11293889 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Security operations face increasing complexity due to evolving cyber threats and regulatory compliance requirements.
**Approach:** Traditional incident response methods often suffer from delayed triage and limited readiness for compliance audits. This paper presents an AI-driven Digital Twin framework designed to simulate and analyze security threats, enabling faster incident triage and improved compliance readiness.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: simulate, simulations.
**Results:** Experimental evaluations using synthetic and public cybersecurity datasets (CICIDS2017, UNSW-NB15) demonstrate up to 40% faster compliance reporting, 35% faster incident triage, and improved anomaly detection accuracy compared to both SIEM and SOAR workflows.
