# AI-empowered Real-Time Line-of-Sight Identification via Network Digital Twins
Michele Zhu, Silvia Mura, Francesco Linsalata, Lorenzo Cazzella et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15478 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Quality of AI models trained on NDT-generated data for LoS identification.
**Approach:** Training strategies for a general DL model using ray-traced NDT data.
**Tools and scale:** Ray tracing.
**Results:** +5% accuracy at very low SNR, about +10% at medium-high SNR; reduced input size.
