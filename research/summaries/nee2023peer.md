# Peer-to-Peer Misbehavior Reporting Using Non-Interactive Zero-Knowledge Proofs for Intelligent Transport Systems
Lukas Nee, Gurjot Singh Gaba, Andrei Gurtov · 2023 IEEE CHILEAN Conference on Electrical, Electronics Engineering, Information and Communication Technologies (CHILECON) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/chilecon60335.2023.10418717 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ETSI TR 103 460 lacks P2P misbehaviour reporting and reporter privacy.
**Approach:** P2P NIZK-based privacy-preserving misbehaviour reporting.
**Tools and scale:** Suburban and highway scenarios.
**Results:** Exposure to misbehaviour reduced by 67.7% (suburban) and 79.2% (highway).

- Not stated.
