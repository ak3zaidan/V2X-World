# Trust-based Model for Securing Vehicular Networks Against RSU Attacks
Aljawharah Alnasser, Hongjian Sun · IEEE INFOCOM 2021 - IEEE Conference on Computer Communications Workshops (INFOCOM WKSHPS) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/infocomwkshps51825.2021.9484458 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) is one of the Internet of Things (IoT) systems that can achieve reliable transportation by providing communications between vehicles and infrastructure units.
**Approach:** The interaction between them is called Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) communication that is bridged by LTE-V2X protocol. However, a V2X communication link faces a significant challenge in cyber-security.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: LTE-V2X
**Results:** As a result, a trust model was suggested by existing work to protect the network against internal attackers. Additionally, the central server has the responsibility to make the global decision and reduce the impact of various RSU attacks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, a V2X communication link faces a significant challenge in cyber-security.
- Internal attackers have valid credentials; thus, detecting them is still a challenge.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
