# Toward Viable Security Solution for VRUs in V2X Communication
Takahito Yoshizawa, An Braeken, Bart Preneel · 2024 IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference (VNC) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc61989.2024.10576015 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Enrolment and certificate issuance for VRU devices (smartphones) in V2X.
**Approach:** Solution using 3GPP mobile-network/ITS interconnection with minimal changes to ITS standards; concrete standards change proposal.
**Tools and scale:** Standards analysis.
**Results:** Claims first proposal addressing VRU security/privacy in ITS.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Recommends relevant standards bodies adopt the changes.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Our engine provisions credentials over cellular TCP/TLS and simulates pedestrians/cyclists; VRU-device enrolment is a related gap.
**Novelty implications:** Same topic area; does not provide the closed-loop, SCMS-complete, cross-layer evaluation our engine targets, so our claims stand but this work must be cited as related.
