# Propagation-Model Sensitivity in Urban VANETs: A SUMO–ns-3 Benchmark Across Traffic Load Scenarios
Zhou Zuoqian, Sang-Chul Kim · 2026 Seventeenth International Conference on Ubiquitous and Future Networks (ICUFN) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icufn69619.2026.11628817 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Higher-layer VANET conclusions about routing, delivery, and delay depend on the underlying wireless channel as-sumptions, yet comparative evidence across traffic-load regimes remains limited in executable SUMO–ns-3 stud-ies.
**Approach:** This paperaddresses that gap with a benchmark-oriented runtime study that makes propagation profile an explicit experimental factor rather than a hidden sim-ulator default. Three profiles—open-road, urban-normal, and dense-obstructed—are instantiated with explicit ns-3 configuration differences and compared with a rule-based auto policy through a repeated executable matrix of four traffic-load scenarios, four profile settings, and ten matched repeats per condition (160 SUMO–ns-3 runs).
