# Heartbeat Message Based Misbehavior Detection Scheme for Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks
Rajesh P. Barnwal, Soumya K. Ghosh · 2012 International Conference on Connected Vehicles and Expo (ICCVE) 2012 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccve.2012.14 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Verifying position/kinematics in heartbeat messages to identify sources of false information.
**Approach:** Short-term misbehaviour detection scheme on heartbeat (beacon) messages.
**Tools and scale:** ns-2 and VanetMobiSim.
**Results:** Identifies false-information sources with very high probability (no figures).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Authors say results pave the way for further research.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — beacon plausibility detection.
**Novelty implications:** Early plausibility work.
