# Performance assessment of IP over vehicular delay-tolerant networks through the VDTN@Lab testbed
João AFF Dias, João NG Isento, Bruno MC Silva, Vasco NGJ Soares, Joel JPC Rodrigues · EURASIP Journal on Wireless Communications and Networking 2012 · https://doi.org/10.1186/1687-1499-2012-13 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Need lab testbed for vehicular delay-tolerant network protocols.
**Approach:** VDTN@Lab emulation testbed on robotic cars, desktops and netbooks; Epidemic and Spray-and-Wait with scheduling/dropping policies.
**Tools and scale:** Lab testbed; bus and random road mobility.
**Results:** Results confirm earlier simulation studies (no numbers in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Add more routing protocols
- implement fragmentation/aggregation.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
