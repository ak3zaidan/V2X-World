# A Reinforcement Learning-Based Attack Generator for Testing the Security of Connected and Autonomous Vehicles
Arman Sargolzaei, Farahnaz Javidi-Niroumand · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2025.3634061 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Connected and autonomous vehicles (CAVs) are employed to enhance the safety and efficiency of transportation systems.
**Approach:** Therefore, there is a need to develop a framework to test and verify the security of CAVs under such attacks. In this paper, for the first time, we look at testing and verification as a feedback control system problem with unknown dynamics.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** We employ the proposed reinforcement learning (RL)-based FDI attack generator on a cooperative adaptive cruise control (CACC) system as a case study, demonstrating its capability to create challenging and unsafe scenarios.
