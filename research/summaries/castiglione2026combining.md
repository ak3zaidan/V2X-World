# Combining Lightweight and Post-Quantum Cryptography With Physically Unclonable Functions for Secure In-Vehicle Communications
Arcangelo Castiglione, Teresa Elia, Christian Esposito, Franco Cirillo · IEEE transactions on consumer electronics 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tce.2026.3684325 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** CAN bus is insecure, and post-quantum risks increase.
**Approach:** PUF-based ECU authentication, lightweight AEAD and PQC for confidentiality, fitting 8-byte CAN frames and 2n messages for n nodes. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** In-vehicle CAN; analytical/implementation evaluation.
**Results:** Low overhead; 2n authentication messages.
