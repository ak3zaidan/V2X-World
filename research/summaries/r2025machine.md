# Machine Learning-Based DDoS Attack Detection in Vehicular Adhoc Networks
Venkateswari R, Venkatalakshmi R, Shankar N et al. · International Conference on Intelligent Innovations in Engineering and Technology (ICIIET) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iciiet65921.2025.11377535 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks(VANETs) are an essential component of intelligent transportation systems that enable real-time communication to enhance road safety and efficiently manage traffic.
**Approach:** The proposed PSO-BAT + XGBoost model significantly surpassed other models in detecting DDoS attacks, achieving 99.98% accuracy and 99% precision, as well as a low packet loss rate of 0.04 %. The results clearly show that the proposed method offers a highly accurate, efficient, and reliable solution for detecting DDoS attacks in VANETs.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** To reduce data dimensionality and improve detection accuracy, a hybrid feature selection technique combining particle swarm optimization (PSO) and the bat algorithm was created. This method helped identify only the most relevant features, thereby reducing the computational overhead and enhancing model performance.
