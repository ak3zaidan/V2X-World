# TCEMD: A Trust Cascading-Based Emergency Message Dissemination Model in VANETs
Zhiquan Liu, Jian Weng, Jianfeng Ma, Jingjing Guo, Bingwen Feng, Zhongyuan Jiang, Kaimin Wei · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2019.2957520 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs) have recently attracted considerable attention from both industry and academia for improving road safety and traffic efficiency. Trust modeling plays a significant role in VANETs, however, the existing trust models cannot primely conform to the characteristics of VANETs.
**Approach:** This article proposes a novel trust cascading-based emergency message dissemination (TCEMD) model which incorporates the entity-oriented trust values into data-oriented trust evaluation in an efficient manner.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Afterwards, a series of simulations and analyses are conducted in a typical highway environment, and the results reveal that the proposed model significantly outperforms the existing models in several cases.
