# Reliable Multiservice Delivery in Fog-Enabled VANETs: Integrated Misbehavior Detection and Tolerance
Xiaomei Zhang, Chen Lyu, Zhicai Shi, Dongmei Li, Naixue N. Xiong, Chi‐Hung Chi · IEEE Access 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2019.2928365 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Multiservice delivery reliability in VANETs with time-varying misbehaviour.
**Approach:** Fog-based dynamic trust weights; trust-aware multipath routing (TMRP) and heterogeneous traffic allocation for misbehaviour tolerance.
**Tools and scale:** Extensive simulations vs three routing protocols.
**Results:** 12-40% higher successful delivery; 10-20% higher effective network utility and better fairness.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Centralised cloud approaches cannot meet delay requirements (motivation).
**Overlap with our engine:** none — routing QoS.
**Novelty implications:** Off-direction.
