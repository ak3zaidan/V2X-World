# Post-Quantum Authentication for Emergency Messaging in 5G Vehicular Fog Networks
Mahmood A. Al-Shareeda, Rouaa Dheyaa Abdul-Jabbar, Hiba R.ALqanatee, M. A. Alghrawy et al. · Journal of Robotics and Control (JRC) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.18196/jrc.v7i1.28191 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** PQ authentication for emergency messages in 5G vehicular fog.
**Approach:** Dilithium-based scheme with distributed revocation.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** 2.65 ms signing, 1.43 ms verification, 3.2 KB token; over 30% computation delay reduction.

- None stated in the abstract.
