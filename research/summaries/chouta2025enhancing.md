# Enhancing V2X Simulation Framework for Motorcycles
Gnanambica Chouta, Basak Ulas, Christian Facchi · 2025 IEEE International Automated Vehicle Validation Conference (IAVVC) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iavvc61942.2025.11219479 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Traffic simulators use car parameters for motorcycles, introducing errors in V2X message content.
**Approach:** Motorcycle-specific dynamics in V2X simulation mobility layer.
**Tools and scale:** V2X simulation framework.
**Results:** More accurate motorcycle representation in cornering and lane changes.
