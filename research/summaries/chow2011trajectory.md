# Trajectory privacy in location-based services and data publication
Chi-Yin Chow, Mohamed F. Mokbel · ACM SIGKDD Explorations Newsletter 2011 · https://doi.org/10.1145/2031331.2031335 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The ubiquity of mobile devices with global positioning functionality (e.g., GPS and AGPS) and Internet connectivity (e.g., 3G andWi-Fi) has resulted in widespread development of location-based services (LBS).
**Approach:** Typical examples of LBS include local business search, e-marketing, social networking, and automotive traffic monitoring. Although LBS provide valuable services for mobile users, revealing their private locations to potentially untrusted LBS service providers pose privacy concerns.
