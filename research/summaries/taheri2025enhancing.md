# Enhancing the Robustness of Federated Learning-Based Intrusion Detection Systems in Transportation Networks
R. Taheri, Zahra Pooranian, Fabio Martinelli · IEEE International Conference on High Performance Computing and Communications 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/hpcc67675.2025.00171 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As transportation systems become more connected and automated, strong cybersecurity is essential.
**Approach:** This paper presents a novel Intrusion Detection System (IDS) based on Federated Learning (FL) to improve security in transportation networks without compromising user privacy. FL enables multiple devices, such as vehicles and roadside units, to collaboratively train a shared model using local data.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** We focus on major challenges such as adversarial and data poisoning attacks that can disrupt system operations and reduce detection accuracy. Our experimental results on realworld datasets show that model diversity can improve accuracy by up to 25% under attack scenarios.
