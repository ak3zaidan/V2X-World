# An Efficient (t, n) Threshold Authentication Scheme for Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Wen-Hui Kong, Peng-Fei Wen, Xin-Ran Zheng, Shuo Yang et al. · IEEE Wireless Communications and Networking Conference 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wcnc57260.2024.10570881 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Reliance on a trusted authority and fault tolerance in VANET authentication.
**Approach:** User-generated keys, ephemeral DH pseudo-ID authentication, partial key delivery without secure channel, Shamir-based (t,n) threshold batch signature. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Computation and communication cost comparison with four schemes.
**Results:** Lower costs than the compared schemes.
