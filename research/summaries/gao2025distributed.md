# A Distributed V2X Hardware-in-the-loop Test System Based on the PanoSim for Intelligent Connected Vehicles
Tianfang Gao, XingHui Zhang, Liang Chen, Zhichen Huang, Dong Ni · SAE Technical Paper Series 2025 · https://doi.org/10.4271/2025-99-0041 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Testing ICV V2X applications cost-effectively.
**Approach:** Distributed HIL test system with PanoSim, GNSS simulator, V2X simulator and control software with time sync and coordinate conversion.
**Tools and scale:** PanoSim, GNSS simulator, V2X simulator.
**Results:** 89 first-stage and 5 second-stage V2X scenarios validated; FCW passes with high confidence.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: lateral distance impact analysis.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — GNSS simulation in V2X HIL (spoofing-capable infrastructure).
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
