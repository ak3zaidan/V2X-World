# Hierarchical Cognitive Digital Twin Framework for Coordinated V2X in Networked Building Microgrids
Mohammed Mousa · IET Generation, Transmission &amp; Distribution 2026 · https://doi.org/10.22541/au.177267257.74897159/v1 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ABSTRACT Electric vehicles can provide valuable grid services through bidirectional charging, but coordinating these vehicle‐to‐everything (V2X) services across multiple buildings remains challenging. This paper develops a hierarchical cognitive digital twin framework that enables intelligent coordination of V2X services across networked building microgrids.
**Approach:** The framework uses local digital twins at each building connected to a central coordinator that manages peer‐to‐peer (P2P) energy trading between buildings.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: reinforcement learning, GNN, digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Validated on IEEE 33‐bus test systems with 3–20 buildings using real‐world load and EV datasets, the framework achieves 47.7% reduction in daily operating costs compared to a no‐V2X baseline, with 8.6%–16.4% improvement over state‐of‐the‐art methods (GNN‐RL, MAPPO, QMIX), with consistent performance confirmed across 10 independent trials ().
