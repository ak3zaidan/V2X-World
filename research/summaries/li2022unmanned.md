# Unmanned Ground Vehicle Platooning Under Cyber Attacks: A Human-Robot Interaction Framework
Fangjian Li, Chengshi Wang, Dariusz Mikulski, John R. Wagner et al. · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2022.3150745 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we propose a human-robot interaction (HRI) framework to reduce the vulnerability of an unmanned ground vehicle (UGV) platoon under cyber attacks.
**Approach:** An observer-based autonomous resilient control strategy is first designed to mitigate the effects of vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) cyber attacks. Rigorous proof of the safety conditions of the platoon under V2V cyber attacks is derived.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: platoon/CACC attacks).
**Results:** Rigorous proof of the safety conditions of the platoon under V2V cyber attacks is derived. To deal with unexpected severe vehicle-to-cloud (V2C) cyber attacks, human observations can also be leveraged to bolster the resilience of the TIMS.
