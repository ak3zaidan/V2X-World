# TrafficNetQA: Question Answering Datasets for Evaluating LLM Performance on Traffic Network Files
Donghoon Kwon, Seungmo Kang, Seongjin Choi · SIGSPATIAL/GIS 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3748636.3763215 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** No dataset to test LLM understanding of traffic network files.
**Approach:** TrafficNetQA QA benchmark: feature retrieval, pathfinding, comprehension.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO XML and OSM networks; Llama, Gemma, Qwen, GPT, Gemini zero-shot.
