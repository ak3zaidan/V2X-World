# WITHDRAWN: SFDP: Adaptive Noise and Dual-Weighted Aggregation for Privacy-Preserving Federated Vehicle Trajectory Prediction
Weiwen Wang, Yuping Zhou, Shengqie Liao, Pengzhan Zheng et al. · preprint 2026 · https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-9220964/v2 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As intelligent transportation systems continue to evolve, reliable vehicle trajectory prediction be achieved without compromising data privacy.
**Approach:** In addition, widely used aggregation such as FedAvg does not explicitly account for client diversity or transmission latency, which can slow or destabilize optimization in fast-changing vehicular environments.To overcome these limitations, we develop SFDP (Self-adaptive Federated Distributed Prediction), a federated learning framework combining adaptive noise regulation with dual-weight model aggregation.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: dataset (details not in abstract).
**Results:** Federated learning (FL) supports collaborative training across distributed participants while keeping raw records local; yet, when differential privacy (DP) is incorporated, model accuracy is frequently reduced because the injected noise is usually set to a constant magnitude. In parallel, the server aggregation assigns greater influence to local updates that are both precise and fresh by jointly weighting validation error and communication staleness.Experiments on the NGSIM US-101 and I-80 highway datasets demonstrate that SFDP outperforms representative baselines, achieving approximately 20–25% lower prediction error and about 20% faster convergence than FedAvg-based methods.
