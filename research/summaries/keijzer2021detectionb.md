# Detection of Network and Sensor Cyber-Attacks in Platoons of Cooperative Autonomous Vehicles: a Sliding-Mode Observer Approach
Twan Keijzer, Riccardo M. G. Ferrari · 2021 European Control Conference (ECC) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.23919/ecc54610.2021.9654903 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Platoons of autonomous vehicles are being investigated as a way to increase road capacity and fuel efficiency.
**Approach:** Cooperative Adaptive Cruise Control (CACC) is an approach to achieve such platoons, in which vehicles collaborate using wireless communication. While this collaboration improves performance, it also makes the vehicles vulnerable to cyber-attacks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** While this collaboration improves performance, it also makes the vehicles vulnerable to cyber-attacks.
