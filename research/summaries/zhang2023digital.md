# Digital Twin-Assisted Edge Computation Offloading in Industrial Internet of Things With NOMA
Long Zhang, Han Wang, Hongmei Xue, Hongliang Zhang, Qi-Lie Liu, D. Niyato, Zhu Han · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2023.3270859 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Integrating digital twins (DTs) and multi-access edge computing (MEC) is a promising technology that realizes edge intelligence in 6 G, which has been recognized as the key enabler for Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT).
**Approach:** In this paper, we explore a DT-assisted MEC system for the IIoT scenario where a DT server is created as a virtual representation of the physical MEC server, via estimating the computation state of the MEC server within the DT modelling cycle. To achieve spectrally efficient offloading, we consider that IIoT devices communicate with industrial gateways (IGWs) through a non-orthogonal multiple access (NOMA) protocol.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: simulations.
**Results:** Validated via simulations, the proposed scheme shows superiority to the benchmarks in reducing the total task completion delay and increasing the percentage of offloading IIoT devices.
