# Emulation of Multi-Connectivity in Hybrid Vehicular Networks
Vadym S. Hapanchak, Antonio D. Costa · 2022 International Conference on Electrical, Computer and Energy Technologies (ICECET) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icecet55527.2022.9873013 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Assessing multi-connectivity (MPTCP) in heterogeneous vehicular networks in real time.
**Approach:** ns-3 real-time emulation integrating simulated networks with virtualised devices and traffic-simulator mobility.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3 emulation, traffic simulator, MPTCP.
**Results:** Expected MPTCP behaviour; emulation performance limited only by host hardware.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Limited by host hardware.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
