# Privacy‐preserving authentication scheme for on‐road on‐demand refilling of pseudonym in VANET
L. Benarous, B. Kadri, S. Bitam, A. Mellouk · International Journal of Communication Systems 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1002/dac.4087 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** IEEE 1609.2 does not define the pseudonym request procedure between CA and vehicle.
**Approach:** On-road on-demand pseudonym refilling with anonymous tickets and challenge-based authentication to a regional authority.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical security analysis.
**Results:** Not stated in the abstract.
