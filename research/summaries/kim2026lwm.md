# LWM-Spectro: A Foundation Model for Wireless Baseband Signal Spectrograms
Namhyun Kim, Sadjad Alikhani, Ahmed Alkhateeb · ICC 2026 - IEEE International Conference on Communications 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc59461.2026.11587404 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The received in-phase and quadrature (I/Q) baseband signals inherently encode physical-layer and channel characteristics of wireless links. Learning robust and transferable representations directly from such raw signals, however, remains challenging due to heterogeneous communication systems, diverse propagation environments, and limited labeled data.
**Approach:** To address this, we present LWM-Spectro1, a transformer-based foundation model pretrained on large-scale I/Q data represented as time–frequency spectrograms.

**Results:** Across tasks, LWM-Spectro consistently outperforms state-of-the-art deep learning baselines in both few-shot and data-rich regimes, providing a unified foundation for wireless learning.
