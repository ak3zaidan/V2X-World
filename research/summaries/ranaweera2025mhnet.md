# MHNet: A Multi-Head GNN Architecture for Efficient Network Modeling
Sandushan Ranaweera, Ren-Ping Liu, Ying He, B. Jayawickrama · IEEE Open Journal of the Communications Society 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ojcoms.2025.3641933 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** GNN network models predict one metric at a time and lack interpretation.
**Approach:** MHNet multi-head GNN for delay, jitter and loss with balanced normalised-gradient training and gradient-based interpretation.
**Tools and scale:** RouteNet datasets.
**Results:** Accuracy comparable to RouteNet with 67% fewer inference FLOPs.
