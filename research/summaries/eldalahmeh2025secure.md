# Secure Key Management Schemes for High-Mobility VANET Environments
Moawiah El-Dalahmeh, Adi El-Dalahmeh · International Journal of Advanced Science Computing and Engineering 2025 · https://doi.org/10.62527/ijasce.7.1.243 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** VANET key management under high mobility and quantum threats.
**Approach:** Hierarchical hybrid scheme: cluster-head symmetric keys, asymmetric and lattice-based PQ for inter-cluster and V2I.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO integrated with NS-3.
**Results:** About 30-40% lower key distribution latency and up to 30% lower computational overhead.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Open challenges: adaptive security management, blockchain integration, compatibility with emerging communication standards.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — PQ in SUMO/NS-3 VANET simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Minor; not SCMS-based.
