# A New Paradigm of User-Centric Wireless Communication Driven by Large Language Models
Kuiyuan Ding, Caili Guo, Yang Yang et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.11696 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The next generation of wireless communications seeks to deeply integrate artificial intelligence (AI) with user-centric communication networks, with the goal of developing AI-native networks that more accurately address user requirements.
**Approach:** To address this gap, we propose a novel LLM-driven paradigm for wireless communication that innovatively incorporates the nature language to structured query language (NL2SQL) tool. To validate the feasibility of the proposed paradigm, we present a prototype system.
