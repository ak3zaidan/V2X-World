# NDT Factory: Synthesizing Verified Network Digital Twins from Semantic Models via Multi-Agent LLM
Sudipta Acharya, Petar Djukic, Burak Kantarci · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12170 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** NDTs rely on predefined analytic logic.
**Approach:** NDT factory: multi-agent LLM synthesis of executable behavioural NDTs from YAML semantic models with compile-test-debug loop.
**Tools and scale:** CAC case study over NSFNet; 300 NSIs.
**Results:** 100% compilation and test pass; 99.3% decision agreement; correct attribution of all rejections.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Ongoing: evaluation of LLM backends, semantic model refinement, dynamic intent classification, packet-level simulation.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
