# Federated Intrusion Detection in Blockchain-Based Smart Transportation Systems
Mohamed Abdel-Basset, Nour Moustafa, Hossam Hawash, Imran Razzak et al. · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2021.3119968 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the integration of the Internet of Things (IoT) in the field of transportation, the Internet of Vehicles (IoV) turned to be a vital method for designing Smart Transportation Systems (STS). STS consist of various interconnected vehicles and transportation infrastructure exposed to cyber intrusion due to the broad usage of software and the initiation of wireless interfaces.
**Approach:** This study proposes a federated deep learning-based intrusion detection framework (FED-IDS) to efficiently detect attacks by offloading the learning process from servers to distributed vehicular edge nodes.
**Tools and scale:** The experiments on two public datasets (i.e., Car-Hacking, TON_IoT) demonstrated the efficiency of FED-IDS against state-of-the-art approaches.
**Results:** The experiments on two public datasets (i.e., Car-Hacking, TON_IoT) demonstrated the efficiency of FED-IDS against state-of-the-art approaches.
