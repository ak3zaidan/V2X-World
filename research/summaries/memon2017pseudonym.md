# Pseudonym changing strategy with multiple mix zones for trajectory privacy protection in road networks
Imran Memon, Ling Chen, Qasim Ali Arain, Hina Memon et al. · International Journal of Communication Systems 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1002/dac.3437 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Summary In road network, vehicles' location may be identified, and their transmissions may even tracked by eavesdrops (eg, safety messages) that contain sensitive personal information such as identity and location of the vehicle.
**Approach:** This type of communication leads to breaking the users' trajectory privacy. Frequently changing pseudonyms are widely accepted as a solution that protects the trajectory privacy of users in road networks.
