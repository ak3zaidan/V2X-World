# A Certificate Revocation List Distribution Scheme over the eMBMS for Vehicular Networks
Hyungon Kim · Journal of the Korea Society of Computer and Information 2016 · https://doi.org/10.9708/jksci.2016.21.10.077 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Timely large CRL distribution with low bandwidth.
**Approach:** CRL distribution via LTE eMBMS point-to-multipoint broadcast, enabling regional CRLs. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** ns-3 simulation over varying MBSFN coverage.
**Results:** More resource-efficient than unicast CRL distribution; latency and execution time analysed.

- Not stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — CRL distribution over LTE; we do unicast cellular and RSU broadcast.
