# Predict and prevent from misbehaving intruders in heterogeneous vehicular networks
Hichem Sedjelmaci, Sidi‐Mohammed Senouci, Tarek Bouali · Vehicular Communications 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vehcom.2016.12.005 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Security of heterogeneous (DSRC + cellular) vehicular networks against false alerts and Sybil attacks.
**Approach:** Intrusion detection and game-theoretic prediction of future misbehaviour; rules-based normal-behaviour model for detection.
**Tools and scale:** NS-3 simulation.
**Results:** High prediction accuracy, faster detection and low overhead vs current frameworks (no numbers in abstract).

- None stated in abstract.
