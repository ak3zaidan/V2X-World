# VeReMi Extension: A Dataset for Comparable Evaluation of Misbehavior Detection in VANETs
Joseph Kamel, Michael Wolf, Rens W. van der Hei, Arnaud Kaiser et al. · ICC 2020 - 2020 IEEE International Conference on Communications (ICC) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc40277.2020.9149132 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** MBD results are hard to compare; original VeReMi lacked realistic sensor errors and attack variety.
**Approach:** VeReMi Extension: adds a realistic sensor error model, new attacks (including DoS, Sybil, data replay and others) and many more data points, with benchmark metrics from local detectors and a simple MBD mechanism. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Generated with the F2MD framework on Veins/SUMO (LuST).
**Results:** Benchmark detection metrics provided (no headline number in abstract).
