# OmniDrive: A Holistic Vision-Language Dataset for Autonomous Driving with Counterfactual Reasoning
Shi-Hao Wang, Zhi-Ding Yu, Xiaohui Jiang, Shi-Yi Lan, Min Shi, Nadine Chang et al. · Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cvpr52734.2025.02090 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The advances in vision-language models (VLMs) have led to a growing interest in autonomous driving to leverage their strong reasoning capabilities.
**Approach:** However, extending these capabilities from 2D to full 3D understanding is crucial for real-world applications. To address this challenge, we propose OmniDrive, a holistic vision-language dataset that aligns agent models with 3D driving tasks through counter-factual reasoning.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: dataset, real-world; scale not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Significant improvements on the DriveLM Q&A benchmark and nuScenes open-loop planning demonstrate the effectiveness of our dataset and methods.
