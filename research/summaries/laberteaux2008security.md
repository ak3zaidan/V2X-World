# Security certificate revocation list distribution for vanet
Kenneth P. Laberteaux, Jason J. Haas, Yih‐Chun Hu · Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks 2008 · https://doi.org/10.1145/1410043.1410063 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Distributing CRLs to vehicles efficiently.
**Approach:** Car-to-car epidemic CRL distribution.
**Tools and scale:** Large-scale simulations on realistic mobility traces.
**Results:** Epidemic distribution significantly outperforms RSU-only distribution.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - CRL dissemination over V2V/RSU in mobility simulation; our engine does RSU relay and CRL broadcast.
**Novelty implications:** Epidemic CRL distribution is established; its effect on time-to-neutralise in a full SCMS loop is not measured.
