# Practical Undeniable Multiparty Drawing-Straw Protocol in Asynchronous Networks for Resource-Constrained Information Systems
Ching-Fang Hsu, L. Harn, Z. Xia, Hang Xu · Security and Communication Networks 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2022/6841428 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The next generation of mobile networks and communications (5G networks) has a very strong ability to compute, store, and so on. Group-oriented applications demonstrate their potential ability in resource-constrained information systems (RISs) towards 5G.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a novel cryptographic primitive, called multiparty drawing-straw (MDS) protocol, which can be used by a group of users to determine the order of the group to participate in the multiparty applications.

**Results:** Group-oriented applications demonstrate their potential ability in resource-constrained information systems (RISs) towards 5G.
