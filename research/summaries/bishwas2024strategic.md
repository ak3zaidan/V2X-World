# Strategic Roadmap for Quantum- Resistant Security: A Framework for Preparing Industries for the Quantum Threat
Arit Kumar Bishwas, M. Sen · arXiv.org 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.09995 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As quantum computing continues to advance, its ability to compromise widely used cryptographic systems projects a significant challenge to modern cybersecurity. This paper outlines a strategic roadmap for industries to anticipate and mitigate the risks posed by quantum attacks.
**Approach:** We, here, propose a novel strategic framework, coined name STL-QCRYPTO, outlines tailored, industry-specific methodologies to implement quantum-safe security systems, ensuring long-term protection against the disruptive potential of quantum computing.
