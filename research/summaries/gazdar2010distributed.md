# A Distributed Secure Architecture for Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Tahani Gazdar, Abdelfettah Belghith, Abderrahim Benslimane · International Journal of Business Data Communications and Networking 2010 · https://doi.org/10.4018/jbdcn.2010100103 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Central CA bottleneck in VANET PKI.
**Approach:** Dynamic PKI with CAs elected by trust/mobility clustering; Dynamic DMZs protect elected CAs acting as RAs.
**Tools and scale:** Simulations varying speed, arrival rate, share of confident vehicles.
**Results:** Share of confident nodes has little impact on stability/efficiency.

- None stated in the abstract.
