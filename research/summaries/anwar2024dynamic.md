# A Dynamic Threat Prevention Framework for Autonomous Vehicle Networks based on Ruin-theoretic Security Risk Assessment
Anika Anwar, Talal Halabi, Mohammad Zulkernine · ACM Journal on Autonomous Transportation Systems 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3660527 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In recent years, Autonomous Vehicle Networks (AVNs) have gained significant attention for their potential to make transportation safer and more efficient.
**Approach:** These networks rely on Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) communication to exchange critical information, such as location, speed, and driving intentions. However, V2V communication also introduces security vulnerabilities that can be exploited to compromise the safety and privacy of drivers and passengers.
