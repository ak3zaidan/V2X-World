# Trust Management Scheme Based on Hybrid Cryptography for Secure Communications in VANETs
Shrikant Tangade, Sunilkumar S. Manvi, Pascal Lorenz · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2020.2981127 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANETs) are used to improve traffic management and reduce the number of road accidents by providing safety applications. However, VANETs are susceptible to a number of security attacks from malicious entities.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a trust management scheme based on hybrid cryptography (TMHC) to secure VANET to a larger extent.
**Tools and scale:** The results are gained with extensive simulations to validate the proposed scheme.
**Results:** Vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANETs) are used to improve traffic management and reduce the number of road accidents by providing safety applications. The results are gained with extensive simulations to validate the proposed scheme.
