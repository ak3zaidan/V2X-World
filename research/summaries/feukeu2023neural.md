# Using Neural Network and Levenberg–Marquardt Algorithm for Link Adaptation Strategy in Vehicular Ad Hoc Network
Etienne Alain Feukeu, Mbuyu Sumbwanyambe · IEEE Access 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2023.3309870 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Network (VANET) was initiated about two decades ago in view of saving lives by mitigating and reducing the number of accidents and incidents on public roads. Moreover, this objective can only be achieved if VANETs mobiles regularly exchange Road State Information (RSI) with their neighborhood and take decisive actions based on the RSI received.
**Approach:** Therefore, it becomes paramount to ensure that the transmitted message is well received.

**Results:** The simulation results definitively demonstrate that the NN approach outperforms its counterparts by a significant margin. It achieves a performance of 1075% in transmission duration, 180% in transmitted bit, and 115% in model efficiency when compared to the Cte, ARF, and AMC algorithms, respectively.
