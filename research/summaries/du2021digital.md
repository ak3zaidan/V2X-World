# Digital Twin Based Trajectory Prediction for Platoons of Connected Intelligent Vehicles
Hao Du, Supeng Leng, Jianhua He, Longyu Zhou · 2021 IEEE 29th International Conference on Network Protocols (ICNP) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icnp52444.2021.9651970 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle platooning is one of the advanced driving applications expected to be supported by the 5G vehicle to everything (V2X) communications.
**Approach:** It holds great potentials on improving road efficiency, driving safety and fuel efficiency. Apart from the organization and internal communication of the platoons, real-time prediction of surrounding road users (such as vehicles and cyclists) is another critical issue.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: dataset, real-world; scale not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The simulation results show that the proposed trajectory prediction scheme can maintain a prediction accuracy for safe platooning and reduce the delay of updating the neural networks by up to 40%.
