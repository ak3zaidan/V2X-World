# VANET SECURITY: DEFENSE AND DETECTION, A REVIEW
Manal Gamal, Abdurrahman A. Nasr, Sayed Abd El-Hady Nouh · Journal of Al-Azhar University Engineering Sector 2020 · https://doi.org/10.21608/auej.2020.103823 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) are networks that deal with transferring data between moving vehicles in order to avoid accidents and to provide journey comfort and traffic safety. Like all other networks it is subjects to vulnerable attacks, hence, security is a hot topic to consider.
**Approach:** This article provides a review on the researches and publications focusing on how to secure the communication between vehicles while transferring the data. Therefore, in this paper, we shed lights on the researches dealing with the different types of attacks with a focus on Sybil attacks.
