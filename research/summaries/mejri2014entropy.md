# Entropy as a new metric for denial of service attack detection in vehicular ad-hoc networks
Mohamed Nidhal Mejri, Jalel Ben‐Othman · ACM International Conference on Modeling, Analysis and Simulation of Wireless and Mobile Systems (MSWiM) 2014 · https://doi.org/10.1145/2641798.2641800 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANETs) aim to enhance driving conditions and provide with drivers and road users a high level of safety. Thus, they are exposed to several kinds of attacks, especially Denial Of Service attacks family (DOS) which affect the availability of services of authentic users.
**Approach:** In this paper, we define "Packets entropy" as a new metric to be used for VANETs denial of service attack detection. We propose also a new detection scheme for this effect.
**Tools and scale:** IEEE 802.11p/DSRC (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** Simulations show the high efficiency of the newly defined metric and the related proposed detection method.
