# ES-CLAS: An Efficient Cerificateless Fully Aggregate Signature Scheme for Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Q. Tao, Xiao-Ning Cui · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2025.3650665 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The previously proposed scheme solved a few of the security problems in VANETs, but it cann’t resist forgery attacks. Besides, the computation and communication cost is not sufficiently low that would affect the efficient delivery of emergency traffic messages.
**Approach:** In this work, it reviewed the recent authentication scheme based on certificateless aggregate signature (CLAS) in vehicular ad hoc network (VANET). In this paper, we propose a new efficient cerificateless fully aggregate signature scheme for VANETs.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** It inherits the characteristics of CLAS by aggregating various message signatures from different vehicles into a single signature, thus reducing the computation and communication overhead of the scheme. It will reduce at least 24.3% in computation cost than compared schemes.
