# Predefined Accuracy Adaptive Control for Vehicle Platoon With Time-Delay Against Deception Attacks
Dajie Yao, Lipo Mo, Xiangpeng Xie · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2025.3591366 · relevance: low · basis: abstract only

**Problem:** Platoon control with time delay under deception attacks.
**Approach:** Adaptive distributed controller with switched functions, Nussbaum functions and RBF neural networks.
**Tools and scale:** Numerical simulation.
**Results:** Spacing errors converge within predefined accuracy 0.2 (and 0.1 in a second run).

- None stated in the abstract.

**Novelty implications:** Nothing of our candidate directions is ruled out.
