# A Systematic Review of the Intrusion Detection Techniques in VANETS
Ila Naqvi, Alka Chaudhary, Anil Kumar · TEM Journal 2022 · https://doi.org/10.18421/tem112-51 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad hoc Networks are an important subset of MANETs. VANET is the group of vehicles that are connected to each other wirelessly.
**Approach:** The IDS analyzes the network and detects any malicious node if present. This paper presents a comprehensive review of some of the recent and important intrusion detection research works and presents a comparative analysis of the techniques used in detecting intrusions as well as attacks focused.
