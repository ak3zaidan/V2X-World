# Comparative Performance Evaluation of RSA and PQC PKI Schemes for Secure Symmetric Encryption
M. Pravallika, P. Bhargavi · 2026 International Conference on Advanced Computing and Knowledge Engineering (ICACKE) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icacke65470.2026.11651113 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the increased growth of cyber attacks and the emerging threat of quantum attacks against classical public key cryptographic schemes like RSA, which are widely deployed for secure key exchange and authentication. Therefore, there is a need for high security to protect against quantum computing posed attacks such as Shor’s algorithm and Grover’s algorithm.
**Approach:** This paper presents a performance evaluation of Post Quantum based PKI and RSA based PKI schemes, and their impact on symmetric encryption algorithms such as AES 256, Blowfish and ChaCha20.
**Tools and scale:** Non-vehicular setting. Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** This evaluation shows AES 256 is most stable and efficient under both PKIs, but AES 256 integrated with PQC PKI is the most future proof solution for securing cloud data.
