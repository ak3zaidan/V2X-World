# A Survey on VANET Intrusion Detection Systems
Mohammed Erritali, B. El Ouahidi · International journal of applied mathematics and informatics 2021 · https://doi.org/10.46300/91014.2021.15.7 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In recent years, the security issues on Vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) have become one of the primary concerns. The VANET is inherently very vulnerable to attacks than wired network because it is characterized by high mobility, shared wireless medium and the absence of centralized security services offered by dedicated equipment such as firewalls and authentication servers.
**Approach:** However, these techniques have limited prevention in general, and they are designed for a set of known attacks. They are unlikely to avoid most recent attacks that are designed to circumvent existing security measures.
