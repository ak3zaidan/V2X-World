# Comparison of Emergency Vehicle Preemption methods using a Hardware-In-The-Loop simulation
Ilsoo Yun, Byungkyu Brian Park, Choul Ki Lee, Young Tae Oh · KSCE Journal of Civil Engineering 2012 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s12205-012-1447-2 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Modern traffic signal control systems provide Emergency Vehicle Preemption (EVP), which provides the right-of-way to emergency vehicles in preference to other vehicles.
**Approach:** This paper compares the various EVP methods available with a controller used in the USA.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: vissim, simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** The results of the comparison indicated that the resulting impact on the network-wide and intersection-specific performances varied with the selected EVP method. The results also indicated that the EVP function using a shortway transition method with two or three cycles worked the best, as it could minimize the impacts of EVP.
