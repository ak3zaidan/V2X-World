# TiHAN-V2X: A Comprehensive Dataset for Dynamic C-V2X Communication in Indian Context
Annu, V. S. S. Phaneendra, Rajalakshmi Pachamuthu, S. V. Srikanth, Munjagala Prasad · 2025 IEEE 101st Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2025-Spring) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2025-spring65109.2025.11174865 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Lack of real C-V2X data in Indian conditions.
**Approach:** TiHAN-V2X dataset with V2V, V2I, I2V, V2C sub-datasets with QoS and spatial metrics; data-driven Markov chain/MDP optimisation.
**Tools and scale:** Real measurements; urban, suburban, highway, cloud.
**Results:** High-resolution data released.
