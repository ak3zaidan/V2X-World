# Safety, Stability and Environmental Impact of FDI Attacks on Vehicular Platoons
S. J. Taylor, Farhan Ahmad, H. Nguyen, S. Shaikh, D. Evans · IEEE/IFIP Network Operations and Management Symposium 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/noms54207.2022.9789808 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Impact of FDI on platoons.
**Approach:** FDI attacker in Plexe: inside/outside the platoon, constant and intelligent on-off FDI.
**Tools and scale:** Plexe.
**Results:** FDI has drastic impact on CO2, distance (safety) and speed (stability).
