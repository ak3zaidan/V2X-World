# Graph Neural Network-Driven Traffic Forecasting for the Connected Internet of Vehicles
Qin Zhang, Ke-Ping Yu, Zhiwei Guo, S. Garg et al. · IEEE Transactions on Network Science and Engineering 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnse.2021.3126830 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Due to great advances in wireless communication, the connected Internet of vehicles (CIoVs) has become prevalent. Naturally, internal connections among active vehicles are an indispensable factor in traffic forecasting.
**Approach:** To address this challenge, a graph neural network-driven traffic forecasting model for CIoVs is proposed in this work, which is denoted as Gra-TF. In this paper, we regard the dynamics of traffic data as a temporal evolution scenario.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: graph neural, real-world. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Numerical results indicate that the proposed Gra-TF improves the prediction accuracy by 30% to 40% compared with several baseline methods.
