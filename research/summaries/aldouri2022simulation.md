# Simulation of Vehicular Network Use in Emergency Situations and Security Applications on a Pakistan Highway
Asaad T. Al-Douri, Noor Mohammed Kadhim, A. A. Hamad Mohamad, Melese Abeyie · Security and Communication Networks 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2022/2902263 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Emergency message dissemination on highways must meet time and reliability needs.
**Approach:** Large-scale simulation of accident scenarios with real traffic data from a Pakistan highway, with and without fixed roadside units.
**Tools and scale:** Real road traffic data; VANET simulation.
**Results:** RSUs reduce accident notification delay (qualitative).
