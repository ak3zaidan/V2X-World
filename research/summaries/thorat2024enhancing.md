# Enhancing Cybersecurity and Threat Detection in Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks (VANETs) using Blockchain and Fallback Policy
Samrat Subodh Thorat, Dinesh Vitthalrao Rojatkar, Prashant Deshmukh · 2024 2nd DMIHER International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare, Education and Industry (IDICAIEI) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/idicaiei61867.2024.10842850 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In the era of autonomous vehicles, Vehicular Ad-hoc networks (VANETs) are critical components of any Intelligent Transportation System (ITS). A Cyber security challenge makes VANETs vulnerable to various attacks, threats and challenges such as data manipulation, data tampering, denial-of-service attacks, privacy breaches and jamming of networks.
**Approach:** This paper tries to explore the various cyber security challenges in VANETs and proposes an enhanced threat detection framework comprising deep-learning techniques and blockchain technology. The proposed framework aims to improve the detection accuracy of malicious activities and ensure secure communication among vehicles and infrastructure.
