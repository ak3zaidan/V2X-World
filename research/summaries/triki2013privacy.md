# A privacy preserving solution for the protection against sybil attacks in vehicular ad hoc networks
Bayrem Triki, Slim Rekhis, M'hamed Chammem, N. Boudriga · Joint IFIP Wireless and Mobile Networking Conference 2013 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wmnc.2013.6549051 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Preventing Sybil attacks while preventing tracking.
**Approach:** RFID tags authenticate to RSUs to obtain short-lifetime certificates; zone-based CAs force certificate changes; observers in nodes to avoid false negatives.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation scenarios.
**Results:** Not quantified in abstract.

- None stated in abstract.
