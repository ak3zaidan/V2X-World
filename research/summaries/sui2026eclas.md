# ECLAS: Efficient Certificateless Aggregate Signature With Minimal Latency in VANETs
Zhiyuan Sui, Ya-Xing Zha, Yujiao Sun, Maoning Wang et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2026.3681069 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) are a critical component of modern transportation systems, offering significant benefits in terms of safety, efficiency, and user experience. However, addressing challenges related to security, privacy, and scalability is essential for their successful implementation and widespread adoption.
**Approach:** In this article, we propose an efficient certificateless aggregate signature scheme (ECLAS) to address the above three challenges.

**Results:** Furthermore, the performance comparison shows that the ECLAS scheme achieves the shortest signature size and the most efficient verification cost for traffic-related message transmissions. Consequently, the ECLAS scheme achieves minimal communication latency in VANETs.
