# An Implementation of Efficient Group Signature for Anonymous Authentication System in Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks
Amang Sudarsono · 2020 International Electronics Symposium (IES) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ies50839.2020.9231899 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Due to features of mobility and velocity of movement all nodes in Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs), these infrastructureless wireless ad-hoc networks become challenging.
**Approach:** Therefore, this paper proposes an anonymous authentication system to enable a vehicle proves that he/she is a legitimated vehicle in the VANET without revealing his/her privacy information to other vehicles or RSUs. The measurement results showed the practicality of proposed system to achieve only about 185 ms or 121 ms in conducting an anonymous authentication either in Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) communication or Vehicle-to-RSU (V2R) communication.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** To realize this aim, an efficient group signature scheme is adopted.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
