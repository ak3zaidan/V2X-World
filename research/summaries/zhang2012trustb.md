# Trust modeling for message relay control and local action decision making in VANETs
Jie Zhang, Chen Chen, Robin Cohen · Security and Communication Networks 2012 · https://doi.org/10.1002/sec.519 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we present a trust‐modeling framework for message propagation and evaluation in vehicular ad hoc networks. In the framework, peers share information regarding road condition or safety, and others provide opinions about whether the information can be trusted.
**Approach:** In this paper, we present a trust‐modeling framework for message propagation and evaluation in vehicular ad hoc networks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Experimental results demonstrate that our proposed trust‐modeling framework promotes network scalability and system effectiveness, which are the two essentially important factors for the popularization of vehicular ad hoc networks, in information propagation and evaluation under the pervasive presence of false information.
