# GNN-IR: An Intelligent Routing Method Based on Graph Neural Network in the Underwater Acoustic Sensor Network
Shuyun Zhang, Huifang Chen, Lei Xie · IEEE Sensors Journal 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jsen.2024.3398375 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The underwater acoustic sensor network (UASN) is crucial for monitoring marine environments and detecting underwater targets.
**Approach:** Reliable networking, especially routing technique, is essential in the UASN due to the challenges of underwater acoustics. In this article, an intelligent routing method based on graph neural network (GNN), named as the GNN-IR method, is proposed to address complex network topology and adapt to dynamic network environment.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Simulations show that the GNN-IR method performs well in terms of effective data transmission, long network lifetime, and low additional routing information overhead.
