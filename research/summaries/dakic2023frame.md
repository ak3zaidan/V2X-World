# Frame Error Rate Prediction for Non-Stationary Wireless Vehicular Communication Links
Anja Dakić, Benjamin Rainer, Markus Hofer, Thomas Zemen · 2023 IEEE 34th Annual International Symposium on Personal, Indoor and Mobile Radio Communications (PIMRC) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/pimrc56721.2023.10293870 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Reliable coverage for non-stationary vehicular links.
**Approach:** DNN predicting 802.11p FER from channel frequency response sequences; labels from hardware-in-the-loop emulation of a GSCM.
**Tools and scale:** GSCM, HIL emulation, road measurement campaign.
**Results:** 85% accuracy on emulated data, 78% on road measurements.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in extracted sections.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - 802.11p PHY abstraction realism.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on security directions.
