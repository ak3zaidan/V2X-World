# Survey on Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks and Its Access Technologies Security Vulnerabilities and Countermeasures
Kaveh Bakhsh Kelarestaghi, Mahsa Foruhandeh, Kevin Heaslip, Ryan Gerdes · arXiv (Cornell University) 2019 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.01541 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this study, we attempt to add to the literature of Connected and Automated Vehicle (CAV) security by incorporating the security vulnerabilities and countermeasures of the Vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANETs) and their access technologies. Compounding VANETs and modern vehicles will allow adversaries to gain access to the in-vehicle networks and take control of vehicles ...
**Approach:** That is, in this paper we contribute to the CAV security literature in threefold. First, we synthesize the current literature in order to investigate security attacks and countermeasures on VANETs as an ad hoc network.
