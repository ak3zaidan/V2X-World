# A geometry-based stochastic approach to emulate V2V communications’ main propagation channel metrics
Jessen Narrainen, Philippe Besnier, Martine Gatsinzi Ibambe · International Journal of Microwave and Wireless Technologies 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1017/s1759078715001749 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Modelling V2V propagation channels for performance assessment.
**Approach:** GBSC approach using an EM simulator with metallic plates as scatterers to emulate rural, highway, urban channels.
**Tools and scale:** EM simulation.
**Results:** Delay spread, AoA, delay-Doppler spectrum adjusted via plate number/location.
