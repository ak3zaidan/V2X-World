# The graph neural networking challenge
José Suárez-Varela, Miquel Ferriol-Galmés, Albert López, Paul Almasan, Guillermo Bernárdez, David Pujol-Perich et al. · ACM SIGCOMM Computer Communication Review 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3477482.3477485 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Training a community in ML for networking.
**Approach:** Report on the Graph Neural Networking Challenge 2020 (ITU AI/ML in 5G): problem, datasets, platform, top solutions, lessons.
**Tools and scale:** Simulator-generated datasets; 50k test samples, 17.1M delay labels; MAPE scoring.
**Results:** Challenge statistics and top-3 solutions described.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Lessons learned for organising future competitions.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - simulator-generated benchmark for learned network models (direction 3 benchmark concept), wired.
**Novelty implications:** Simulator-generated ML benchmarks for networking exist; a V2X counterfactual benchmark is not covered.
