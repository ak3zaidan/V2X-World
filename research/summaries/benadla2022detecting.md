# Detecting Sybil Attacks in Vehicular Fog Networks Using RSSI and Blockchain
Sarra Benadla, Omar Rafik Merad-Boudia, S. Senouci, Mohamed Lehsaini · IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnsm.2022.3216073 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil attacks in vehicular fog computing.
**Approach:** Two-level detection: fog nodes verify position via RSSI and store position proofs on a blockchain; trajectories through the same fog nodes at the same time flagged as Sybil.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation; powerful adversary scenarios.
**Results:** Up to 98% detection when multiple aliases send position requests.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Sybil with pseudonyms via infrastructure proofs.
**Novelty implications:** Blockchain/RSSI approach, not SCMS.
