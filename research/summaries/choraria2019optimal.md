# Optimal deception attack on networked vehicular cyber physical systems
Moulik Choraria, Arpan Chattopadhyay, Urbashi Mitra, Erik G. Ström · 2018 52nd Asilomar Conference on Signals, Systems, and Computers 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ieeeconf44664.2019.9048730 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Herein, design of false data injection attack on a distributed cyber-physical system is considered.
**Approach:** A stochastic process with linear dynamics and Gaussian noise is measured by multiple agent nodes, each equipped with multiple sensors. The agent nodes form a multi-hop network among themselves.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Numerical results demonstrate the efficacy of the attack.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
