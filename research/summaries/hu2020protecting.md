# Protecting Platoons from Stealthy Jamming Attack
Yaodan Hu, Haoqi Shan, R. Dutta, Yier Jin · Asian Hardware-Oriented Security and Trust Symposium 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/asianhost51057.2020.9358269 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Stealthy jamming of DSRC control channel destabilises platoons.
**Approach:** Stealthy probabilistic jamming and QDA detector on received power and delay.
**Tools and scale:** Plexe simulator and SDRs.
**Results:** Max distance error up to 8x without detection; PLR thresholds fail.

- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Jamming attack impact on platoons.
