# Cluster based Certificate Blocking Scheme using Improved False Accusation Algorithm
Chetan Shripal Arage, K. V. V. V. Satyanarayana · International Journal of Advanced Computer Science and Applications 2021 · https://doi.org/10.14569/ijacsa.2021.0120671 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The aggregation of mobile nodes without the use of a base station is known as Mobile Ad Hoc Networks (MANETS).
**Approach:** There are several mechanisms proposed to prevent mishaps while routing of the packets in such networks methods: The methodology outlined in Mobile Ad Hoc Networks to protect against various types of assaults is based on a recent method known as Cooperative Bait Detection Scheme.
**Tools and scale:** Results: With the help of NS2 simulation, the clustering approach was evaluated by considering several Sybil-attack network scenarios.
**Results:** Our goal is to propose a cluster-based methodology to improve delays, packet delivery ratio, and other performance assessment criteria. Improved Cooperative Bait Detection recommends a disjointed multipath technique to avoid attacks.
