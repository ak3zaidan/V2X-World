# HRC-DKB: A Lightweight Dual-Key Batch Authentication Protocol for Heterogeneous Resource-Constrained ITS
Mo Wang, Xiang-Wei Meng, Zulong Diao, Sheng Huo et al. · Fall Joint Computer Conference 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jcc72984.2026.00036 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Heterogeneous Resource-Constrained Intelligent Transportation Systems (HRC-ITS) refers to an ITS integrated with Wireless Body Area Networks (WBAN), which can overcome the limitations of traditional ITS in monitoring drivers' physiological conditions. In HRC-ITS, there exist massive authentication demands.
**Approach:** Aiming at the signature pollution problem existing in the batch verification process, this paper proposes a lightweight dual-key batch authentication protocol, namely HRC-DKB.
**Tools and scale:** Formal security proofs (ROR model), ProVerif simulations, and informal analyses confirm resilience against eavesdropping, forgery, and signature pollution.
**Results:** Performance evaluations indicate that HRC-DKB reduces computational and communication overheads by approximately 24.6% and 24.9% compared to the latest state-of-the-art, effectively enhancing V2V communication security and efficiency in “human-vehicleroad” collaborative systems.
