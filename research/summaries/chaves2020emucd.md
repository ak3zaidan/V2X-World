# EmuCD: An Emulator for Content Dissemination Protocols in Vehicular Networks
Ricardo Chaves, Carlos Senna, Miguel Luís, Susana Sargento, André Moreira, Diogo Recharte et al. · Future Internet 2020 · https://doi.org/10.3390/fi12120234 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Testing content dissemination protocols in VANETs.
**Approach:** EmuCD distributed container-based emulator driven by real mobility/connectivity traces.
**Tools and scale:** Containers; real VANET traces; IPFS, Sprinkler, BitTorrent.
**Results:** Performs close to the real VANET under similar conditions.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Lacks finer details such as varying hardware loads.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
