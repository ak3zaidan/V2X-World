**Problem:** This chapter examines the integration of digital twin technology within intelligent transportation systems (ITS) to enhance optimization, safety, and sustainability in modern mobility networks.
**Approach:** Despite rapid advancements in autonomous vehicles and smart infrastructure, existing transportation systems remain largely reactive, lacking predictive and system-wide coordination capabilities. This chapter addresses this gap by proposing a unified framework that integrates real-time data synchronization, artificial intelligence, and simulation-driven optimization through digital twin architectures.
