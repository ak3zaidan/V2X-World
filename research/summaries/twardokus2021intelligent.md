# Intelligent Lower-Layer Denial-of-Service Attacks Against Cellular Vehicle-to-Everything
Geoff Twardokus · RIT Scholar Works (Rochester Institute of Technology) 2021 · https://scholarworks.rit.edu/theses/10914 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communication promises a wide range of benefits for society.
**Approach:** Within future V2X-enabled intelligent transportation systems, vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) communication will allow vehicles to directly exchange messages, improving their situational awareness and allowing drivers or (semi-)autonomous vehicles to avoid collisions, particularly in non-line-of-sight scenarios. Thus, V2V has the potential to reduce annual vehicular crashes and fatalities by hundreds of thousands.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: MATLAB, C-V2X
**Results:** Thus, V2V has the potential to reduce annual vehicular crashes and fatalities by hundreds of thousands. Cellular Vehicle-to-Everything (C-V2X) is rapidly supplanting older V2V protocols and will play a critical role in achieving these outcomes.
