# Block4Forensic: An Integrated Lightweight Blockchain Framework for Forensics Applications of Connected Vehicles
Mumin Cebe, Enes Erdin, Kemal Akkaya, Hidayet Aksu et al. · IEEE Communications Magazine 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mcom.2018.1800137 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Today's vehicles are becoming cyber-physical systems that not only communicate with other vehicles but also gather various information from hundreds of sensors within them. These developments help create smart and connected (e.g., self-driving) vehicles that will introduce significant information to drivers, manufacturers, insurance companies, and ...
**Approach:** With the opportunity of being able to access various information in cars, we propose a permissioned blockchain framework among the various elements involved to manage the collected vehicle-related data.
