# Investigating Adversarial Robustness of Heterogeneous Cooperative Perception
Chenyi Wang, Yutong Liu, Qingzhao Zhang, Ming F. Li · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17856 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Whether sensor heterogeneity protects cooperative perception from adversarial features.
**Approach:** Matched-objective harness for iterative attacks; HetPoison single-pass label-free generator.
**Tools and scale:** OPV2V and OPV2V-H; HEAL, STAMP, CodeFilling, GenComm.
**Results:** Tuned attacks close or reverse the apparent robustness gap; HetPoison transfers across heterogeneous designs.
