# Joint Communication and Sensing Enabled Cooperative Perception Testbed for Connected Automated Vehicles
Qixun Zhang, Xinye Gao · IEEE INFOCOM 2022 - IEEE Conference on Computer Communications Workshops (INFOCOM WKSHPS) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/infocomwkshps54753.2022.9798074 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Single-vehicle sensing is limited; JCS can share raw perception data at mmWave.
**Approach:** Weighted mean accuracy fusion of perception data from two time-division JCS systems; field tests.
**Tools and scale:** mmWave JCS testbed, field tests.
**Results:** Target positioning RMSE reduced by 31% versus a single JCS system.
