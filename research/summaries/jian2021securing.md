# Securing Blockchain Wallets Efficiently Based on Threshold ECDSA Scheme Without Trusted Center
Zhou Jian, Qu Ran, Sun Liyan · 2021 Asia-Pacific Conference on Communications Technology and Computer Science (ACCTCS) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/acctcs52002.2021.00018 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Using the private key stored in the blockchain wallet to sign the transaction is the only way to trade on the blockchain.
**Approach:** At present, the mainstream wallet management schemes store the wallet in a single location or rely on the participation of trusted center, which can not resist single point failure. Aiming at the protect the security of blockchain wallet, this paper proposes a blockchain wallet protection scheme against single point failure based on threshold elliptic curve digital signature without trusted center.
