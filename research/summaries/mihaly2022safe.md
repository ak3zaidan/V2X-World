# Safe eco-cruise control for connected vehicles against trained cyber attacks*
András Mihály, Balázs Németh, Péter Gáspár · IFAC-PapersOnLine 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ifacol.2022.07.574 · relevance: low · basis: abstract only

**Problem:** Safe eco-cruise control for CAVs against cyber attacks.
**Approach:** Cloud speed-profile optimisation plus vehicle-level safety layer; RL-trained attacker agents.
**Tools and scale:** Real velocity profile data; multi-agent training.
**Results:** Validates the safety layer against attacks aiming to cause collisions (no numbers).
