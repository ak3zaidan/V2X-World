# An MEC-Based DoS Attack Detection Mechanism for C-V2X Networks
Yang Li, Ronghui Hou, King-Shan Lui, Hui Li · 2018 IEEE Global Communications Conference (GLOBECOM) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/glocom.2018.8647323 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** New DoS in C-V2X where few nodes reserve resources by frequently requesting high-priority V2X services.
**Approach:** MEC server estimates each node's malicious probability from its resource consumption state.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Attack feasible; detection performs well.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — C-V2X resource-reservation DoS.
**Novelty implications:** Network-side C-V2X DoS detection exists.
