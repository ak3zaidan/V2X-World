# Large Language Model (LLM)-enabled In-context Learning for Wireless Network Optimization: A Case Study of Power Control
Hao Zhou, Chengming Hu, Dun Yuan et al. · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00214 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Large language model (LLM) has recently been considered a promising technique for many fields.
**Approach:** Different from existing machine learning (ML) methods, our proposed in-context learning algorithm relies on LLM's inference capabilities. It avoids the complexity of tedious model training and hyper-parameter fine-tuning, which is a well-known bottleneck of many ML algorithms.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: reinforcement learning.
**Results:** Finally, the simulations demonstrate that the proposed algorithm can achieve comparable performance as conventional deep reinforcement learning (DRL) techniques without dedicated model training or fine-tuning.
