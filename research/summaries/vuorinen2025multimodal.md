# Towards Multimodal Digital Twin Environments for Automotive Applications
Aleksi Vuorinen, Mikko Mäkitalo, Ella Peltonen · Proceedings of the 15th International Conference on the Internet of Things 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3770501.3770506 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With electric and autonomous vehicles becoming more popular, digital twins have rapidly grown in popularity in the automotive sector, yet the environments in which they operate remain underexplored.
**Approach:** While vehicle-level modeling has matured significantly, there are still many unanswered questions on creation, fidelity, and depth of the virtual environments in which to operate traffic scenarios. In this article, we investigate the current landscape of Digital Twin Environments (DTE) in automotive applications.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting. The available text does not describe a V2X simulator, radio stack, traffic model or SCMS-style PKI in enough detail to report; scale not stated.
**Results:** We propose a framework architecture for constructing a high-fidelity and multi-modal DTE, which can represent an accurate 3D model and soundscape as a second modality.
