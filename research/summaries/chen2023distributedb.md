# Distributed Dynamic Event-Triggered Secure Model Predictive Control of Vehicle Platoon Against DoS Attacks
Ji-Cheng Chen, Hui Zhang, Guodong Yin · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2022.3215966 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper studies the distributed dynamic event-triggered model predictive control (MPC) of vehicle platoon systems subject to denial-of-service (DoS) attacks and external disturbances. If the MPC problem is solved, the constructed vehicular beacon information will be transmitted to its neighbouring vehicles through the vehicular ad-hoc network (VANET).
**Approach:** We develop a novel DoS-attack-aware event-triggered mechanism for each vehicle to determine whether the MPC problem should be solved or not at each sampling time instant.
**Tools and scale:** Finally, extensive simulation examples are provided to illustrate the effectiveness of the proposed algorithm in terms of communication efficiency and platoon resilience control performance.
