**Problem:** The Internet of things (IoT) and advancements of wireless technology have evolved intelligent transport systems to integrate billion of smart objects ready to connect to the Internet. **Results:** In terms of recall, precision, and F-score, our presented model significantly outperformed compared to MARINE. The simulation results have validated that the proposed lightweight model realized a high trust level with 40% of MiTM attackers and in terms of F-score 95%, whereas the MARINE model has 90%, which leads to the model to attain high detection accuracy.
