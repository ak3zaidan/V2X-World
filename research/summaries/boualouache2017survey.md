# A survey on pseudonym changing strategies for Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks
Abdelwahab Boualouache, Sidi-Mohammed Senouci, Samira Moussaoui · IEEE Communications Surveys &amp; Tutorials 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1109/comst.2017.2771522 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Effective pseudonym changing strategies remain open.
**Approach:** Survey classifying strategies (mix-zone and other) by mode, radio silence, encryption, metrics (ASS, ASR, MTT, DA).
**Tools and scale:** IEEE COMST survey.
**Results:** Classification and comparison; open issues listed.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Effective pseudonym changing strategy is 'still an open issue'.
- Strategies must be achieved before real-world deployment.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial.
**Novelty implications:** Pseudonym strategy design is well surveyed.
