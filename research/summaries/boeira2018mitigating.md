# Mitigating Position Falsification Attacks in Vehicular Platooning
Felipe Boeira, Mikael Asplund, Marinho P. Barcellos · 2018 IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference (VNC) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc.2018.8628427 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Position falsification and Sybil attacks on platoons.
**Approach:** Detection via Vouch location proofs plus several reaction strategies.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation (Plexe/Veins).
**Results:** Possible to prevent collisions by reacting to false beacons in time without reacting to false positives.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — closed-loop detection-to-reaction showing collision prevention depends on reaction timing, overlapping direction 1 at the local level.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first to show detection reaction timing prevents collisions; infrastructure revocation timing and its safety value remain open.
