# Explainable Anomaly Detection in Network Traffic Using LLM
Kamil Jerabek, Josef Koumar, Jiří Setinský, Jaroslav Pesek · NOMS 2025-2025 IEEE Network Operations and Management Symposium 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/noms57970.2025.11073574 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Network anomaly detection is essential for modern cybersecurity, yet existing systems often generate numerous alerts without clear explanations, leading to inefficiencies and high false-positive rates. This paper proposes a novel approach that integrates Large Language Models (LLMs) with an anomaly detection framework to enhance explainability in network traffic analysis.
**Approach:** We evaluated our approach using real-world network traffic data, demonstrating its ability to enhance situational awareness, reduce false positives, and support more effective cybersecurity practices.
