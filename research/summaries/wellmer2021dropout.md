# Dropout's Dream Land: Generalization from Learned Simulators to Reality
Zac Wellmer, James T. Kwok · arXiv 2021 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.08342 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A World Model is a generative model used to simulate an environment.
**Approach:** In some cases, a World Model offers an agent the opportunity to learn entirely inside of its own dream environment. We present a general approach to improve a controller's ability to transfer from a neural network dream environment to reality at little additional cost.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** In this work we explore improving the generalization capabilities from dream environments to real environments (Dream2Real). These improvements are gained by drawing on inspiration from Domain Randomization, where the basic idea is to randomize as much of a simulator as possible without fundamentally changing the task at hand.
