# Lightweight Multi-Class Support Vector Machine-Based Medical Diagnosis System with Privacy Preservation
Sherif M. Abdelfattah, Mohamed Baza, Mohamed Mahmoud, Mostafa M. Fouda et al. · Italian National Conference on Sensors 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s23229033 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Machine learning, powered by cloud servers, has found application in medical diagnosis, enhancing the capabilities of smart healthcare services. Research literature demonstrates that the support vector machine (SVM) consistently demonstrates remarkable accuracy in medical diagnosis.
**Approach:** To achieve this, we have introduced modifications to an inner product encryption cryptosystem and incorporated it into our medical diagnosis framework.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Our findings demonstrate that our proposed method successfully fulfills our security and privacy objectives while maintaining high classification accuracy and minimizing communication and computational overhead.
