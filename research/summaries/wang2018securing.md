# Securing On-Body IoT Devices By Exploiting Creeping Wave Propagation
Wei Wang, Lin Yang, Qian Zhang, Tao Jiang · arXiv 2018 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.09224 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** On-body devices are an intrinsic part of the Internet-of-Things (IoT) vision to provide human-centric services.
**Approach:** Motivated by this real-world security vulnerability, this paper proposes SecureTag, a system designed to add defense in depth against active attacks by integrating physical layer (PHY) information with upper-layer protocols.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Extensive experiments are conducted in a lab, apartments, malls, and outdoor areas, involving 12 volunteer subjects of different age groups, to demonstrate the robustness of our system. Results show that our system can mitigate 96.13% of active attack attempts while triggering false alarms on merely 5.64% of legitimate traffic.
