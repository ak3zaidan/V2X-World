# Distributed Misbehavior Detection in UAV Flocks
Mohamed Anis Aguida, Sergio A. Salinas Monroy, Monowar Hasan · IEEE Access 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2026.3711670 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Rogue UAVs in flocks.
**Approach:** GRIFFIN: GPS plus RSSI majority voting.
**Tools and scale:** ArduSim and Raspberry Pi/Navio testbed.
**Results:** High detection, low FN while swarm is mostly benign; less than 1 MB memory and less than 1% CPU.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Requires predominantly benign swarm (at least four honest nodes).
**Overlap with our engine:** none — UAVs.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
