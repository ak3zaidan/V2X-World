# Blockchain-Based Secure Mist Computing Network Architecture for Intelligent Transportation Systems
P. Sharma, Jong Hyuk Park · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2020.3040989 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Conventional centralized architectures are sufficiently educated to provide high scalability, availability, and low latency and bandwidth usages for the Internet of Things (IoT) network. The exponential increase in volume and number of IoT devices in Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) turns our physical world into the cyber world.
**Approach:** In this model, we present IoT user/device registration and authentication algorithms and enable the computing resources at the extreme edge of the network by deploying a smart contract.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The proposed model gains 81% of lower median latency at local nodes compared to the core model.
