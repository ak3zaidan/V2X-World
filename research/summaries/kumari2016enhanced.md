# An enhanced and secure trust‐extended authentication mechanism for vehicular ad‐hoc networks
Saru Kumari, Marimuthu Karuppiah, Xiong Li, Fan Wu, Ashok Kumar Das, Vanga Odelu · Security and Communication Networks 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1002/sec.1602 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad‐hoc Networks (VANETs) are a move towards regulating safe traffic and intelligent transportation system. A VANETs is characterized by extremely dynamic topographical conditions owing to speedily moving vehicles.
**Approach:** In this paper, to eradicate the vulnerabilities found in Chuang‐Lee's scheme, an enhanced trust‐extended authentication scheme for VANET is proposed. We display the efficiency of our scheme through security analysis and comparison.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Through simulation results using widely accepted NS‐2 simulator, we show that our scheme authenticates vehicles faster than Chuang‐Lee's scheme.
