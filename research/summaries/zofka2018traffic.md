# Traffic Participants in the Loop: A Mixed Reality-Based Interaction Testbed for the Verification and Validation of Autonomous Vehicles
Marc Rene Zofka, Stefan Ulbrich, Daniel Karl, Tobias Fleck et al. · 2018 21st International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/itsc.2018.8569226 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In order to verify and validate autonomous vehicles, testbeds integrating the whole system from perception to actuation are necessary.
**Approach:** In order to solve this issue, we present a mixed reality testbed for the verification and validation of autonomous vehicles faced with realistic road user behavior in critical, worst case traffic scenarios.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: testbed (details not in abstract).
**Results:** We achieve this by registering an immersed pedestrian and the automated driving function within a common environment model, providing challenging traffic scenarios.
