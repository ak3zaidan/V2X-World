# The Resilience of Massive MIMO PNC to Jamming Attacks in Vehicular Networks
Bismark Okyere, Leila Musavian, Berna Özbek, Sherif Adeshina Busari, Jonathan González · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2020.3016907 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this article, we investigate the resilience of Massive MIMO Physical Layer Network Coding (PNC) to jamming attack in both sub-6 GHz and millimeter-Wave (mmWave) systems in vehicular networks.
**Approach:** Massive MIMO generally is resilient to jamming attacks, and we investigate the impact that PNC has on this resilience, if combined with Massive MIMO. The combination of Massive MIMO and PNC has shown a significant improvement in the bit error rate (BER) in our previous investigation.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The combination of Massive MIMO and PNC has shown a significant improvement in the bit error rate (BER) in our previous investigation. Over Rayleigh channel, our simulation results reveal that Massive MIMO PNC performs better in the lower signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) regions to jamming attacks and this is achieved at twice the spectral efficiency.
