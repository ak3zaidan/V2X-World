# ESGA: Efficient and Secure Geo-Aware Certificate Revocation for Blockchain-Based Multi-Domain VANETs
Yingjie Xia, Tiancong Cao, Xuejiao Liu, Yun Wang · IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tmc.2026.3654964 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Timely, private CRL synchronisation across multi-domain VANETs.
**Approach:** ESGA: hierarchical Geo-CRL (blockchain global plus geo local CRLs), adaptive geo-aware synchronisation, linkage-value-chain revocation for privacy.
**Tools and scale:** IEEE TMC; experiments.
**Results:** Higher CRL sync efficiency, lower query latency and storage.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — linkage-value-based revocation and multi-domain CRL synchronisation overlap our linkage-based revocation and multi-provider CRLs.
**Novelty implications:** Multi-domain linkage-value CRL designs exist; faithful US SCMS multi-provider interoperability measured end-to-end remains open.
