# Malicious Agent Detection for Robust Multi-Agent Collaborative Perception
Yangheng Zhao, Zhen Xiang, Sheng Yin, Xianghe Pang et al. · arXiv 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11901 · relevance: medium · basis: full text

**Problem:** Malicious agents degrade multi-agent collaborative perception.
**Approach:** MADE: multi-test with match loss and collaborative reconstruction loss, Benjamini-Hochberg FDR control.
**Tools and scale:** V2X-SIM and DAIR-V2X.
**Results:** 58.49% and 59.15% AP@0.5, only 1.27% and 0.28% below an oracle defender.

- None explicit.

**Novelty implications:** Nothing of our candidate directions is ruled out.
