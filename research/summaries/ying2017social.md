# Social Location privacy Protection method in vehicular social networks
Bidi Ying, Amiya Nayak · 2017 IEEE International Conference on Communications Workshops (ICC Workshops) 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccw.2017.7962836 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Location-disclosure is a major privacy issue in vehicular social networks when people are interested in traffic-related cooperation drive, location-aware services, and other applications.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a Social-aware Location-privacy Protection (SLP) method in vehicular social networks to protect location privacy. This method enables trustworthy users to obfuscate an original sender's location with the help of their regions.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: location privacy).
**Results:** Experiments show that our method improves success ratio by more than 20% compared to the existing method, and the probability of an attacker guessing user's location correctly is about 0.05 which is quite small.
