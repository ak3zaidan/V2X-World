# Learnable Digital Twin for Efficient Wireless Network Evaluation
Boning Li, Timofey Efimov, Abhishek Kumar, Jose Cortes, Gunjan Verma, Ananthram Swami et al. · MILCOM 2023 - 2023 IEEE Military Communications Conference (MILCOM) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/milcom58377.2023.10356320 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Simulator-based KPI estimation is slow for configuration optimisation.
**Approach:** Learning-based network DT with node, edge and path embeddings mapping configuration to KPIs in one forward pass.
**Tools and scale:** Wired and wireless network simulator data.
**Results:** Outperforms baseline learned models; comparable to simulators with much higher efficiency.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in extracted sections.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - wireless network simulator surrogate (direction 3), no mobility/security.
**Novelty implications:** Wireless simulator surrogates exist; V2X cross-layer counterfactual surrogates remain open.
