# MixGroup: Accumulative Pseudonym Exchanging for Location Privacy Enhancement in Vehicular Social Networks
Rong Yu, Jiawen Kang, Xumin Huang, Shengli Xie et al. · IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tdsc.2015.2399291 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sparse meeting opportunities limit pseudonym mixing in vehicular social networks.
**Approach:** MixGroup: group-signature-based extended regions where vehicles successively exchange pseudonyms.
**Tools and scale:** IEEE TDSC; simulation.
**Results:** Significantly outperforms existing schemes in location privacy.
