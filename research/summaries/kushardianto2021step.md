# 2-Step Prediction for Detecting Attacker in Vehicle to Vehicle Communication
Nur Cahyono Kushardianto, Yassin El Hillali, C. Tatkeu · IEEE Vehicular Technology Conference 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2021-fall52928.2021.9625513 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Smart vehicles can be more adaptive to the road condition by exchange information between the vehicle.
**Approach:** They can avoid traffic congestion, dangerous obstacles, even traffic accident earlier. This technology is closely related to the safety of the driver, therefore it must receive special attention.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** We show that the proposed scheme can improve the attack detection performance compared to one detection step.
