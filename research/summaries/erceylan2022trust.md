# Trust-Chain-Based Certificate Revocation Control in Autonomous Vehicle Networks
Gizem Erceylan, M. Ali Akcayol · 2022 5th International Conference on Information and Communications Technology (ICOIACT) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icoiact55506.2022.9972060 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Central CRL distribution causes latency and traffic.
**Approach:** Semi-distributed trust-chain revocation control reducing central CRL server demand.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Requests to the central CRL server reduced by 35% on average; secure communication when RSU or server unreachable.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — CRL server load.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
