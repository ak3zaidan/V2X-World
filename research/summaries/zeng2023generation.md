# Generation and Recognition of Pedestrian-Vehicle Scenarios for C-V2X Digital Twin Testing
Lingqiu Zeng, Jiaxuan Jin, Qingwen Han, Yang Long, Lei Ye · 2023 IEEE 3rd International Conference on Digital Twins and Parallel Intelligence (DTPI) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/dtpi59677.2023.10365422 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** C-V2X test scenarios rarely include VRUs.
**Approach:** Genetic-algorithm scenario reconstruction, K-means++ ranking and CNN-GRU critical scenario identification.
**Tools and scale:** DT test datasets.
**Results:** CNN-GRU precision better than other algorithms.
