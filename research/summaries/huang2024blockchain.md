# Blockchain-Enabled Collaborative Task Offloading for Zero-Trust Vehicular Fog Computing
Chenran Huang, Yiting Zhao, Zhiwei Wei et al. · GLOBECOM 2024 - 2024 IEEE Global Communications Conference 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/globecom52923.2024.10901474 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we focus on the task offloading problem for zero-trust vehicular fog computing (VFC) to promote trustworthy collaborative computing among vehicles.
**Approach:** We propose a blockchain-enabled zero-trust VFC framework (BlockZT-VFC) to continuously verify and dynamically authorize the vehicle nodes. To overcome the reliability and efficiency issues in BlockZT-VFC, a multi-attribute task offloading and group-based continuous verification (MTOCV) scheme is designed.
**Tools and scale:** Experimental simulations demonstrate an 18% increase in throughput and a 34% decrease in latency across two different scenarios, showcasing the superiority of our mechanism in improving system performance and reliability.
**Results:** Experimental simulations demonstrate an 18% increase in throughput and a 34% decrease in latency across two different scenarios, showcasing the superiority of our mechanism in improving system performance and reliability.
