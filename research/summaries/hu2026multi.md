# Multi-Agent Embodied Autonomous Driving (MAEAD): From V2X Information Exchange to Shared World Models
Senkang Hu, Zhengru Fang, Yihang Tao, Zihan Fang, Yiqin Deng, Yuguang Fang · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13840 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Survey of multi-agent embodied autonomous driving via shared world models.
**Approach:** Review of about 400 publications on V2X, collaborative perception, cooperative planning and simulation/data engines.
**Tools and scale:** Survey.
**Results:** Evaluation concentrated in simulation and offline protocols; foundation-model coordination lacks verifiable real-time safety guarantees.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Priorities: verifiable shared-state maintenance, robust intent alignment, safe coordinated action under communication and compute constraints.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - background on V2X world models and closed-loop validation engines.
**Novelty implications:** Notes gap in verifiable shared state; security of shared state is relevant to our directions.
