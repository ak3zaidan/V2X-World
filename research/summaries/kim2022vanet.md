# VANET Jamming and Adversarial Attack Defense for Autonomous Vehicle Safety
Haeri Kim, Jong-Moon Chung · Computers, Materials &amp; Continua 2022 · https://doi.org/10.32604/cmc.2022.023073 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Jamming degrades safety message reception.
**Approach:** SVM jamming detection; DBSCAN outlier removal against evolving adversarial jammers.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation with normal and heavy traffic.
**Results:** Accuracy 98% or higher; DBSCAN improves accuracy.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- More advanced jamming attacks need consideration.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — jamming detection.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
