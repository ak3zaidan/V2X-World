# AoI-Aware Privacy-Preserving Task Allocation in Vehicular Crowdsensing
Jia-Ling Hong, Yantao Yu, Jingxiao Ma, Cheng Huang et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2026.3680331 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In vehicular crowdsensing (VCS) systems, task allocation is a critical process that connects sensing demands with distributed vehicular resources. Its effectiveness fundamentally relies on optimizing the spatiotemporal features of vehicles, where information freshness is quantified by the Age of Information (AoI).
**Approach:** To achieve privacy-preserving and spatiotemporally optimized task allocation, this paper proposes a novel AoI-aware privacy-preserving task allocation framework based on secret sharing. Furthermore, we design a budget-constrained, spatiotemporally near-optimal vehicle selection algorithm that simultaneously maximizes spatial coverage and minimizes AoI under budget constraints.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** Security analysis and experimental results demonstrate that our scheme effectively protects location privacy while achieving near-optimal spatiotemporal coverage. Moreover, it significantly reduces both communication and computational overhead compared with a homomorphic encryption (HE) baseline, confirming its practical effectiveness for time-sensitive VCS applications.
