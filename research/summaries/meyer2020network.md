# Network Anomaly Detection in Cars based on Time-Sensitive Ingress Control
Philipp Meyer, Timo Hackel, Franz Korf, Thomas C. Schmidt · 2020 IEEE 92nd Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2020-Fall) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2020-fall49728.2020.9348746 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Connected cars need robust protection against network attacks. Network anomaly detection and prevention on board will be particularly fast and reliable when situated on the lowest possible layer.
**Approach:** In this paper, we introduce and evaluate a concept for detecting anomalous traffic using the ingress control of Time-Sensitive Networking (TSN). We build on the idea that already defined TSN traffic descriptors for in-car network configurations are rigorous, and hence any observed violation should not be a false positive.
