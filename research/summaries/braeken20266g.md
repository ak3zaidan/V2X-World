**Problem:** The sixth generation (6G) of mobile networks presents transformative possibilities but also introduces significant security challenges.
**Approach:** This paper surveys the state-of-the-art in 6G security from 2020 to 2025, highlighting advancements, challenges, and potential solutions. We distinguish our survey by offering a comprehensive, cross-layer analysis of the role of artificial intelligence (AI) in both enabling and threatening 6G security.
