# Development of a Multimodal Fusion Intrusion Detection Intelligent System for Spatiotemporal Data of Intelligent Connected Vehicles
Jing-Tao Chen, Jie Liu, Qingyu Zhang et al. · 2026 IEEE 6th International Conference on Electronic Technology, Communication and Information (ICETCI) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icetci68937.2026.11610716 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper develops a multimodal fusion intrusion detection intelligent system for spatiotemporal data of intelligent connected vehicles.
**Approach:** Results show that the proposed method achieves overall accuracy, recall, and F1-score of 98.42%, 97.86%, and 98.11%, respectively, with an average detection latency of 18.7 ms, meeting the real-time detection requirements of the edge side.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Studies have shown that multimodal spatiotemporal fusion can significantly improve the detection capabilities of intelligent connected vehicle intrusion detection systems against complex attacks, weak anomalies, and cross-source data tampering.
