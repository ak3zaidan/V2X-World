# Resilient Post-Quantum Cryptographic Schemes for IoT Data Protection in 6G and beyond Networks
Vasanthamma H, S. Kannadhasan · Proceedings of the 1st International Conference on Intelligent Methods and Advanced Computer Scientific Innovations 2025 · https://doi.org/10.5220/0014207600004932 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** : The rapid evolution of Internet of Things (IoT) ecosystems in the era of 6G and beyond networks introduces critical challenges for data protection, particularly in the face of emerging quantum threats. Existing studies on post-quantum cryptography (PQC) for IoT remain domain-specific, conceptual, or outdated, often neglecting resilience, scalability, and real-world efficiency.
**Approach:** This research addresses these limitations by proposing resilient post-quantum cryptographic schemes tailored for IoT data protection in heterogeneous 6G environments.
