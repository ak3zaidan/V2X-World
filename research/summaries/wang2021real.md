# Real-Time Sensor Anomaly Detection and Recovery in Connected Automated Vehicle Sensors
Yiyang Wang, Neda Masoud, Anahita Khojandi · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2020.2970295 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The proposed method combines model-based signal filtering and anomaly detection methods. Specifically, we use adaptive extended Kalman filter (AEKF) to smooth sensor readings of a CAV based on a nonlinear car-following model.
**Approach:** In this paper we propose a novel observer-based method to improve the safety and security of connected and automated vehicle (CAV) transportation.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** Our experiments show that compared with the AEKF with a traditional $\chi ^{2}$ -detector, our proposed method achieves a better anomaly detection performance. We also demonstrate that a larger time delay factor has a negative impact on the overall detection performance.
