# Securing the Road Ahead: A Survey on Internet of Vehicles Security Powered by a Conceptual Blockchain‐Based Intrusion Detection System for Smart Cities
Fatima Al‐Quayed, Noshina Tariq, Mamoona Humayun, Farrukh Aslam Khan et al. · Transactions on Emerging Telecommunications Technologies 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1002/ett.70133 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ABSTRACTThe Internet of Vehicles (IoV) is a critical component of the smart city. Various nodes exchange sensitive data for urban mobility, such as identification, position, messages, speed, and traffic statistics.
**Approach:** Security is of the highest priority, considering various security‐privacy risks from the wellness, safety, and confidentiality of men and women inside the vehicle. This survey presents a detailed analysis of state‐of‐the‐art and evolving security challenges to IoV systems.
