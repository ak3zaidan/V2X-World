# DATZ-TM: Zero-Trust intrusion detection for VANETs with temporal graph neural networks
Nguyen Thi Dieu Linh, Vijender Kumar Solanki, Aman Kataria et al. · Scientific Reports 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-61006-7 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks (VANETs) are vital for intelligent transport systems, yet they are extremely vulnerable to cyberattacks like Sybil, Blackhole, Denial-of-Service, Position Falsification, Message Suppression, and Replay.
**Approach:** We introduce DATZ-TM (Dynamic Adaptive Zero-Trust Traffic Management), which consists of four integrated components: a calibrated behavioural Trust Scorer with temporal decay, a behavioural Anomaly Detector that couples a learned detection model with a Temporal Graph Neural Network (T-GNN), a Zero-Trust Enforcement layer, and a DQN + LLM traffic agent (Llama-3.3-70B).

**Results:** On the synthetic benchmark DATZ-TM attains the best overall performance, leading every baseline on detection rate (86.0%), F1-score (88.5%), AUC (0.955), Matthews Correlation Coefficient (0.788), balanced accuracy (89.2%) and G-mean (89.1%) at a low 7.7% false-positive rate, surpassing the strongest baseline (Random Forest: F1 87.3%) on every aggregate metric.
