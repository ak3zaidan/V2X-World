# Federated learning merges with v2x privacy protection
Yuhan Zhou · Journal of Computing and Electronic Information Management 2024 · https://doi.org/10.54097/f10t6523 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) communication systems are poised to revolutionize road safety, traffic efficiency, and driver experience through real-time data exchange among vehicles and infrastructure.
**Approach:** This paper explores the integration of FL techniques into V2X environments, where vehicles act as decentralized participants in model training processes. Through a detailed review of existing FL methodologies and their adaptation to V2X settings, this paper proposes practical solutions for privacy-preserving model aggregation and efficient learning across dynamic vehicular networks.
