# Blockchain-based Trust Management in Security Credential Management System for Vehicular Network
SangHyun Byun, Arijet Sarker, Sang-Yoon Chang, Jugal Kalita · Conference on Innovation in Clouds, Internet and Networks 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icin64016.2025.10942972 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** SCMS policy generator and global certificate chain file management could be decentralised.
**Approach:** Blockchain-Based Trust Management replacing PG, managing policies, aggregating GCCF.
**Tools and scale:** Hyperledger Fabric.
**Results:** Lightweight processing, efficient chain/ledger size, multiple tx/s.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Develop framework for various advanced multi-authority PKI designs emphasising privacy and compromise resilience
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — SCMS trust management (GCCF/GPF) our engine models via trust lists.
**Novelty implications:** None.
