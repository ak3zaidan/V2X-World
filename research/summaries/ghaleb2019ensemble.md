# Ensemble-Based Hybrid Context-Aware Misbehavior Detection Model for Vehicular Ad Hoc Network
Fuad A. Ghaleb, Mohd Aizaini Maarof, Anazida Zainal, Bander Ali Saleh Al-rimy et al. · Remote Sensing 2019 · https://doi.org/10.3390/rs11232852 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** MBD uses static thresholds and limited context, hurting generalisation.
**Approach:** EHCA-MDS: dynamic thresholds from Kalman-filtered neighbour mobility, consistency/plausibility/behaviour features, Hampel-filter context reference, ensemble of statistical and RF classifiers.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation (details in full text).
**Results:** HCA-MDS improved overall performance by 17.93% over Stuebing's MDS; ensemble better than individual classifiers.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Static thresholds are a recognised limitation of prior work (addressed here).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — adaptive detection thresholds, related to direction 2's detection threshold design.
**Novelty implications:** Dynamic thresholds exist; their safety trade-off under closed-loop revocation is open.
