# VeSPA
Νικόλαος Αλεξίου, Marcello Laganà, Stylianos Gisdakis, Mohammad Khodaei et al. · arXiv 2013 · https://doi.org/10.1145/2463183.2463189 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** How often and for how long vehicles should acquire pseudonyms from the VPKI.
**Approach:** VeSPA: standard-compliant VPKI design and three pseudonym acquisition policies evaluated on two large mobility datasets.
**Tools and scale:** KTH; implemented VPKI; large-scale mobility traces.
**Results:** Most privacy-protective efficient policy supported with moderate overhead; VPKI can serve sizable areas with modest computing.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Integrate privacy-preserving methods and anonymous authentication into protocols.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — pseudonym provisioning policy and VPKI load evaluation overlaps our SCMS provisioning and top-ups.
**Novelty implications:** VPKI provisioning-policy performance is studied (EU-style); US butterfly provisioning under cellular TCP/TLS in a city sim remains open.
