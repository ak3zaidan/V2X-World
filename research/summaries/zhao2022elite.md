# ELITE: An Intelligent Digital Twin-based Hierarchical Routing Scheme for Softwarized Vehicular Networks
Liang Zhao, Zhenguo Bi, Ammar Hawbani, Keping Yu, Yan Zhang, Mohsen Guizani · IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tmc.2022.3179254 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Routing in SDVNs suffers from topology change, complex requests and long training.
**Approach:** ELITE: junction-based hierarchical routing; policies trained by parallel agents in a digital twin, combined and deployed by the controller; hop-by-hop relay selection.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** Improved packet delivery ratio, end-to-end delay and overhead vs counterparts (no numbers in abstract).
