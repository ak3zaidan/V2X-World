# A context-aware on-board intrusion detection system for smart vehicles
Davide Micale, Ilaria Matteucci, Florian Fenzl, Roland Rieke et al. · International Journal of Information Security 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s10207-024-00821-3 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Modern vehicles are becoming more appealing to potential intruders due to two primary reasons. Firstly, they are now equipped with various connectivity features like WiFi, Bluetooth, and cellular connections, e.g., LTE and 5G, which expose them to external networks.
**Approach:** In this article, we introduce CAHOOTv2, a context-sensitive intrusion detection system (IDS), aiming at enhancing the vehicle’s security and protect against potential intrusions. To demonstrate the validity of the algorithm, we collected driving data from both an artificial intelligence (AI) and 39 humans.
**Tools and scale:** C-V2X/LTE-V2X/5G (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** As a concluding remark, the results of CAHOOTv2 show great promise in detecting intrusions effectively.
