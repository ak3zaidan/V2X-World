# Simulation Framework for Cooperative Adaptive Cruise Control with Empirical DSRC Module
Zijia Zhong, Joyoung Lee · IECON 2018 - 44th Annual Conference of the IEEE Industrial Electronics Society 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iecon.2018.8592909 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Traffic simulations of CACC often assume perfect V2X.
**Approach:** Vissim with an analytical DSRC packet-reception module derived from ns-2; four managed-lane strategies.
**Tools and scale:** Vissim, ns-2-derived DSRC model.
**Results:** Average PRR above 93% in all scenarios; V2V reliability depends on deployment strategy.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: network-level deployment strategies.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — analytical radio abstraction inside a traffic simulator (single process).
**Novelty implications:** Embedded analytical DSRC in a traffic simulator exists.
