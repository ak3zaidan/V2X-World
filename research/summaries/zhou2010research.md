# Research on the Jamming Attack of VANET
Xianwei Zhou · Journal of Hebei Normal University 2010 · http://en.cnki.com.cn/Article_en/CJFDTOTAL-HBSZ201001017.htm · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Due to the reasons for the wireless channel,and its own characteristics,the vehicular Ad Hoc networks(VANET) is vulnerable to jamming attack,and main target is the bottom layer.According to the network environment of VANET,jamming attack model is built,and relevant security detection architecture is raised.Finally,the corresponding countermeasure is put forward.
