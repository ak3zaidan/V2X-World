# L-ECQV: Lightweight ECQV Implicit Certificates for Authentication in the Internet of Things
Manisha Malik, Kamaldeep, Maitreyee Dutta et al. · IEEE Access 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2023.3261666 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The vast expansion of the Internet of Things (IoT) devices and related applications has bridged the gap between the physical and digital world.
**Approach:** Due to its inherent scalability, Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) is the well-known and classic approach to bring public-key certificate based security to IoT.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Our evaluation results show that the proposed L-ECQV certificate approach reduces energy consumption by 27%, message overhead of EDHOC handshake by 52%, and shows improvements in certificate validation time.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Unfortunately, security remains a major challenge and the lack of secure links have fueled the increased attacks on IoT devices and networks.
