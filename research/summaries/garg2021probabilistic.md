# A Probabilistic Data Structures-Based Anomaly Detection Scheme for Software-Defined Internet of Vehicles
S. Garg, Amritpal Singh, G. Aujla, Sukhdeep Kaur et al. · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2020.2988065 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Internet of Vehicles (IoV) has escalated the movement of big data across moving vehicles which create a huge burden on the network infrastructure. In IoV environment, effective handling of streaming data has to face various challenges like; traffic monitoring, flow management, re-configuration and security.
**Approach:** Software-defined networks (SDN) provides improved flexibility, and centralized control of the network to overcome (almost) the above-mentioned challenges.
**Tools and scale:** The proposed scheme has been evaluated in a simulated environment.
**Results:** The results obtained depict that the proposed scheme is faster, accurate, and efficient concerning detection ratio and false-positive ratio.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — LLM agents for network/security operations; our engine has an LLM agent that configures and analyses runs, but this work is not V2X and not tied to a deterministic simulator with ground truth.
**Novelty implications:** LLM agents for SOC/NetOps/RCA are established; cross-layer V2X root-cause analysis against simulator ground truth is not covered here.
