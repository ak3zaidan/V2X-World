# Energy-Efficient Consensus for V2V Communication via VDF-Enabled PoA and Secret-Sharing-Based CH Selection
Hamid Ullah, Qian Ning, Xiang-Rong Tang, Yongnan Xu et al. · 2025 IEEE 102nd Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2025-Fall) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2025-fall65116.2025.11310161 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The VDF guarantees unbiased, verifiable, and tamper-resistant selection of CHs. Each CH is validated through a decentralized mechanism and entrusted with maintaining the blockchain ledger.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a secure and energy-efficient cluster-based V2V communication framework that integrates a Proof of Authority (PoA) consensus mechanism, Verifiable Delay Function (VDF)-based Cluster Head (CH) selection, and Shamir’s Secret Sharing (SSS).
**Tools and scale:** Simulation results demonstrate a 58% reduction in latency, a 54% improvement in energy efficiency, and a 22% increase in packet delivery ratio compared to existing schemes such as ECT-POW, CBT-PoS, and VPKI-DAG.
**Results:** Elliptic curve cryptography ensures lightweight encryption, while a cluster-based architecture reduces communication overhead and enhances scalability. Simulation results demonstrate a 58% reduction in latency, a 54% improvement in energy efficiency, and a 22% increase in packet delivery ratio compared to existing schemes such as ECT-POW, CBT-PoS, and VPKI-DAG.
