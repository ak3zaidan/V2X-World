# Threat Detection for Collaborative Adaptive Cruise Control in Connected Cars
Matthew Jagielski, Nicholas Jones, Chung-Wei Lin, C. Nita-Rotaru et al. · Wireless Network Security 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3212480.3212492 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Attacks on collaborative ACC via reported acceleration or local sensors.
**Approach:** Attack analysis and two detection methods (physics constraints and ML). (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Attacks affect comfort, efficiency and safety; two can cause crashes; detectors effective with discussed limitations.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Detection methods have limitations (discussed by authors).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — V2X attacks causing crashes with detection.
