# A Deep Learning-Based Integrated Algorithm for Misbehavior Detection System in VANETs
Hsiao-Yuan Hsu, Nai-Hsin Cheng, Chun‐Wei Tsai · International Conference on Intelligent Computing and its Emerging Applications 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3491396.3506509 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Rule-based MBD is ineffective in VANETs.
**Approach:** CNN+LSTM reconstruct location information; SVM classifies vehicles as compromised.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi extension dataset (offline).
**Results:** Detects 95.37% of compromised vehicles; better F1 than compared algorithms.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - offline ML MBD.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on closed-loop claims.
