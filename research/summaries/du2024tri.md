# A Tri-Phases Message Oriented Trust Model in FANET
Xueru Du, Yue Cao, Di Wang, Chenchen Lv et al. · IEEE Transactions on Network Science and Engineering 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnse.2024.3374733 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With advantages of convenience and flexibility, Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) are widely applied in various fields, such as inspection, agriculture, transportation, and so on. However, due to characteristics of Flying Ad Hoc Network (FANET) consisting of UAVs, such as dynamic topology and limited bandwidth, the security of messages is threatened by cyber attacks.
**Approach:** A Tri-Phases Message Oriented Trust Model for FANET is proposed, called TPMOTM, to secure messages. In the proposed work, the message collection process is divided into three phases, including message generation, message transmission, and message integration.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in abstract.
**Results:** Extensive simulations are conducted based on the Opportunistic Network Environment (ONE) simulator, with attack message detection rates reaching up to 95% ($\pm 0.89\%$) and event detection accuracy rates typically above 85% ($\pm 1.21\%$).
