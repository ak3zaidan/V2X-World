# A Trust Management and Misbehaviour Detection Mechanism for Multi-Agent Systems and its Application to Intelligent Transportation Systems
Johannes Müller, Tobias Meuser, Ralf Steinmetz, Michael Buchholz · 2019 IEEE 15th International Conference on Control and Automation (ICCA) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icca.2019.8899968 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Reputation systems react slowly to sudden reliability changes (faults or takeover) in multi-agent cooperative information.
**Approach:** Subjective-logic mechanism attaching reliability to shared data, fusing multi-agent event reports, with trust revision to detect and isolate faulty and malicious agents.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation in an ITS application.
**Results:** Scales with MAS size; quickly detects and removes misbehaving agents.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Related-work gaps listed (homogeneous agents, resource use, isolating faulty nodes, centralisation)
- future work: transfer to other network topologies.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — fast isolation of misbehaving agents; not PKI-based.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
