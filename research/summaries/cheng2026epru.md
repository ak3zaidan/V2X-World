# EPRU: Efficient and Privacy-Aware Reputation Update Scheme With a Dual-Threshold Mechanism for Vehicular Platoons
Hongyuan Cheng, Xiaosong Guan, Yining Liu, Jiuru Wang et al. · IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tdsc.2026.3674335 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Authenticated but misleading data in platoons; periodic reputation updates are slow.
**Approach:** EPRU: real-time triggered reputation updates with ElGamal/homomorphic protection.
**Tools and scale:** Formal proofs and analysis.
**Results:** Resistance to forgery, replay, modification.
