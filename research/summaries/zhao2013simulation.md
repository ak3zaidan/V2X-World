# Simulation Framework for Vehicle Platooning and Car-following Behaviors Under Connected-vehicle Environment
Li Zhao, Jian Sun · Procedia - Social and Behavioral Sciences 2013 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sbspro.2013.08.105 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper studies the traffic assistance system consisting of different kinds of vehicles (manual, Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC) and Cooperative Adaptive Cruise Control (CACC) vehicle).
**Approach:** By using the application programming interface in microscopic-traffic simulation, the aim that constructing simulation framework of CACC platoon is achieved. Maneuvers like forming, adjusting, splitting, dismissing and joining in a platoon are implemented under the simulation platform.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** Results illustrate the lane capacity increased significantly when market penetration of CACC vehicles added, however platoon size have little impact on traffic capacity.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "These preliminary working will be a foundation for our future work in this area."
