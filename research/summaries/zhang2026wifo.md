# WiFo-M 2 : Empower Wireless Communications With Plug-and-Play Environment Sensing via Foundation Model
Haotian Zhang, Shijian Gao, Xiang Cheng · IEEE Transactions on Cognitive Communications and Networking 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tccn.2026.3717927 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The emerging convergence of next-generation wireless networks and agentic artificial intelligence (AI) is inspiring a new vision: embodied intelligent network entities utilize environmental sensing to refine their physical-layer (PHY) actions.
**Approach:** Despite a growing body of preliminary work, prevailing small and task-specific AI models require extensive manual design of data pre-processing, network architecture, and fine-tuning, leaving them tightly coupled to particular PHY actions, system configurations, and deployment scenarios. To address this, we propose a paradigm shift with WiFo-M2, a foundation model that enables environment sensing to be easily integrated into PHY actions, delivering universal performance gains.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Experiments show that WiFo-M2 improves the performance of a comprehensive suite of fundamental PHY actions, demonstrating strong generalization to unseen scenarios.
