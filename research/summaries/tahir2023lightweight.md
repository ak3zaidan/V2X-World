# Lightweight and Secure Multi-Factor Authentication Scheme in VANETs
H. Tahir, Khalid Mahmood, Muhammad Faizan Ayub et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2023.3286187 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The idea of a smart city and the utilization of vehicles are emerging rapidly.
**Approach:** This article presents the lightweight and secure multi-factor authenticated key agreement scheme for VANETs to ensure efficient and secure transmission of data via an open channel. Our presented scheme is secure, scalable, and lightweight compared to existing schemes due to its low computation and communication cost.
