# MDBV: Monitoring Data Batch Verification for Survivability of Internet of Vehicles
Jing-Wei Liu, Qingqing Li, Huijuan Cao, Rong Sun et al. · IEEE Access 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2018.2869543 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Survivability of IoV via quick verification of monitoring data.
**Approach:** Improved certificateless aggregate signature (MDBV).
**Tools and scale:** Random-oracle proof under CDH.
**Results:** Aggregate size roughly constant with vehicle count.
