# Enhancing the Reliability of Communication between Vehicle and Everything (V2X) Based on Deep Learning for Providing Efficient Road Traffic Information
Radwa Ahmed Osman, Sherine Nagy Saleh, Yasmine N. M. Saleh, Mazen Nabil Elagamy · Applied Sciences 2021 · https://doi.org/10.3390/app112311382 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Developing efficient communication between vehicles and everything (V2X) is a challenging task, mainly due to the characteristics of vehicular networks, which include rapid topology changes, large-scale sizes, and frequent link disconnections.
**Approach:** This article proposes a deep learning model to enhance V2X communication. Various channel conditions such as interference, channel noise, and path loss affect the communication between a vehicle (V) and everything (X).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Thus, the proposed model aims to determine the required optimum interference power to enhance connectivity, comply with the quality of service (QoS) constraints, and improve the communication link reliability. The proposed model fulfills the best QoS in terms of four metrics, namely, achievable data rate (Rb), packet delivery ratio (PDR), packet loss rate (PLR), and average end-to-end delay (E2E).
