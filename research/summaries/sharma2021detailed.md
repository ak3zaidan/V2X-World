# A detailed tutorial survey on VANETs: Emerging architectures, applications, security issues, and solutions
Sparsh Sharma, Sparsh Sharma, Ajay Kaul, Suhaib Ahmed, Surbhi Sharma, Surbhi Sharma · International Journal of Communication Systems 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1002/dac.4905 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad‐hoc network (VANET) is an emerging field of mobile ad‐hoc network (MANET) which facilitates intelligent transport system (ITS). It thus helps in enhancing the real‐time driving experience and road safety.
**Approach:** Due to association of VANET with life‐critical applications, there is a dire need to understand each and every aspect of it in terms of architecture, security, applications, routing, and so forth, before bringing it into implementation. Although there exist numerous studies on VANETs, they tend to cover only individual aspects of it.
**Tools and scale:** Finally, various simulators have been investigated, and comprehensive analysis has been done with respect to the feasibility of implementation of VANETs in these simulators.
**Results:** No quantitative results stated in the abstract.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- This first of its kind survey aims to provide a combined and thorough study of each aspect of VANET along with their advantages and limitations in a single solid document.
