# Detection and Isolation of False Data Injection Attack in Intelligent Transportation System via Robust State Observer
Xianhua Huang, Xinyu Wang · Processes 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/pr10071299 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** FDI attacks in connected ACC networks.
**Approach:** Robust state observer residual detection with adaptive threshold and observer banks for isolation.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Effective detection and isolation (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Precomputed thresholds limit detection
- adaptive threshold proposed.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — control layer.
**Novelty implications:** Adjacent.
