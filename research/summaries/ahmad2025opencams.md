# OpenCAMS: An Open-Source Connected and Automated Mobility Co-Simulation Platform for Advancing Next-Generation Intelligent Transportation Systems Research
Minhaj Uddin Ahmad, Akid Abrar, Sagar Dasgupta, Mizanur Rahman · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09186 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Siloed simulators cannot evaluate closed-loop mobility, sensing, decisions and connectivity, including cybersecurity.
**Approach:** OpenCAMS: time-synchronised bidirectional coupling of SUMO, CARLA and OMNeT++ (C-V2X) for safety, mobility and cybersecurity research.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO, CARLA, OMNeT++.
**Results:** Platform presented with use cases (no numbers extracted).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: 5G NR sidelink, ROS2 stacks, real-time GNSS spoofing models, MEC, real-world data streaming for DT validation.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — closed-loop co-simulation aimed at cybersecurity, but no PKI/SCMS and multi-process.
**Novelty implications:** Closed-loop cyber co-simulation platforms are emerging; PKI and revocation layers remain open.
