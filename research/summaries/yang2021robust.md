# A Robust CACC Scheme Against Cyberattacks Via Multiple Vehicle-to-Vehicle Networks
Tianci Yang, Carlos Murguia, Dragan Nešić, Chen Lv · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2023.3265369 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** CACC acceleration commands over a single V2V channel are attackable.
**Approach:** Transmit commands over multiple V2V networks; data fusion estimates true command and isolates compromised channels; robust H-infinity controller limits estimation error impact.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation; assumes at most q of N channels attacked.
**Results:** Simulation illustrates performance (no figures).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Using estimated data introduces uncertainty and decreases performance.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — multi-radio redundancy (e.g. DSRC plus C-V2X) as defence.
**Novelty implications:** Multi-channel redundancy for CACC exists.
