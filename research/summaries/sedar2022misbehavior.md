# Misbehavior Detection in Vehicular Networks: An Ensemble Learning Approach
R. Sedar, C. Kalalas, P. Dini, J. Alonso-Zarate, F. Vázquez-Gallego · Global Communications Conference 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/globecom48099.2022.10001264 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** MBD on unlabelled data.
**Approach:** Ensemble of clustering and RL.
**Tools and scale:** Open-source dataset (VeReMi extension).
**Results:** Most attacks detected; some curtailed by inaccurate clustering and erratic attackers; robust to mislabelled data; real-time capability explored.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Detection curtailed for certain misbehaviour types due to partly inaccurate clustering and erratic attacker activity.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
