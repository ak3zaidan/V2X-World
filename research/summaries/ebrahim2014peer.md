# Peer-to-Peer Network Simulators: an Analytical Review
Mansoor Ebrahim, Shujaat Khan, Syed Sheraz Ul Hasan Mohani · arXiv 2014 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.0400 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Simulators are the most dominant and eminent tool for analyzing and investigating different type of networks.
**Approach:** This Paper describes different types of existing P2P simulators as well as provides a survey and comparison of existing P2P simulators and extracting the best simulator among them.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** Generally P2P research is based on the principle of simulate first and then experiment in the real world and there is no reason that simulation results cannot be reproducible. A lack of standard documentation makes verification of results harder as well as due to such poor documentation implementation of well-known overlay algorithms was very difficult.
