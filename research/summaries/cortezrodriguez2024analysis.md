# Analysis of Two-Stage Rollout Designs with Clustering for Causal Inference under Network Interference
Mayleen Cortez-Rodriguez, Matthew Eichhorn, Christina Lee Yu · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.05119 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Rollout designs for interference have high variance.
**Approach:** Two-stage rollout with clustering; bias-variance analysis.
**Tools and scale:** Simulations.
**Results:** Bias grows with cut edges; variance depends on homophily and covariate balance.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Tension between clustering objectives.
**Overlap with our engine:** none (causal methodology only).
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
