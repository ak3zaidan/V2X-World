# Clustered Predictive Quality of Service for Connected Vehicles Using Federated Learning
Oscar Stenhammar, Gabor Fodor, Carlo Fischione · 2024 IEEE International Conference on Communications Workshops (ICC Workshops) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccworkshops59551.2024.10615431 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Single global pQoS models perform poorly across environments.
**Approach:** Road segments clustered by data similarity, each cluster model trained with federated learning.
**Tools and scale:** Numerical study.
**Results:** Clustered models outperform a single global model.
