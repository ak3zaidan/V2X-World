# A Hybrid Decision Tree and Random Forest Model for DDoS Attack Detection and Mitigation in the Internet of Vehicles with Enhanced Data Privacy
Piyush Agarwal, Shuchi Bhadula, Sachin Sharma · Recent Patents on Engineering 2026 · https://doi.org/10.2174/0118722121418032251125064501 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In the automotive industry, integrating connected and autonomous vehicles through the Internet of Vehicles and wireless communication has gained prominence. Secure communication and data privacy are the primary concerns in wireless communication.
**Approach:** The primary aim of this study is to design a secure and intelligent system for detecting and mitigating DDoS attacks in IoV.
**Tools and scale:** Another core goal is to ensure data privacy by implementing ElGamal encryption for all inter- vehicular communications.
**Results:** Experimental evaluation of the proposed hybrid model demonstrates superior performance in DDoS detection. The accuracy of the hybrid model is 99.81%, while those of logistic regression, kNN, and the Naive Bayes algorithm are 97.30%, 97.81%, and 97.10%, respectively.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future work includes restoration mechanisms, blockchain-based trust, and quantum computing for faster detection and post-quantum privacy.
