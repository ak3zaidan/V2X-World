# CARLA Connect: A Connected Autonomous Vehicle (CAV) Driving Simulator
Omkar Dokur, Srinivas Katkoori · 2022 IEEE International Symposium on Smart Electronic Systems (iSES) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ises54909.2022.00146 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** CARLA lacks connected vehicle features.
**Approach:** CARLA Connect: connectivity agent (V2V/V2I) and CV/CAV agents; HMI warnings.
**Tools and scale:** CARLA.
**Results:** Six V2V CV, three V2I CV and two V2V CAV algorithms validated visually.
