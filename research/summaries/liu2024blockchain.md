# Blockchain-Based Distributed Collaborative Computing for Vehicular Digital Twin Network
Lei Liu, Junqi Fu, Jie Feng, Guopeng Wang et al. · IEEE Network 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mnet.2023.3318996 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Being the main starting point for intelligent transportation systems, internet of vehicles has entered a rapid development period. This motivates a large number of novel vehicular applications with diversified and personalized demands.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a blockchain-based vehicular digital twin architecture for distributed collaborative computing. Then, we investigate the computation offloading in blockchain-based vehicular digital twin networks to demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed architecture.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Distributed collaborative computing provides a promising solution to meet these applications by on-demand resource management, but is faced with tremendous challenges in achieving trusted resource cooperation and accurate decision making.
