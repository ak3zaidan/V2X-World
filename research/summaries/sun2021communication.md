# A Communication Security Game on Switched Systems for Autonomous Vehicle Platoons
Guoxin Sun, Tansu Alpcan, Benjamin I. P. Rubinstein, Seyit Camtepe · 2021 60th IEEE Conference on Decision and Control (CDC) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cdc45484.2021.9683543 · relevance: medium · basis: full text

**Problem:** Message falsification by a compromised platoon member.
**Approach:** Switched CACC/ACC system with game-theoretic switching driven by an imperfect anomaly detector.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical with simulation.
**Results:** Mitigates attacks in short-term operation; minimum dwell time ensures string stability in benign conditions.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Cannot ensure string stability in an adversarial environment
- open problem whether a switching signal can achieve (probabilistic) string stability under communication attacks.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — none: control-level response, no PKI.
**Novelty implications:** Detection-driven fallback is known; linking to revocation timing is open.
