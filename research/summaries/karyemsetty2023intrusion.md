# Intrusion Detection System in Vehicular Network using Deep Learning Approach
Nagarjuna Karyemsetty, Thalakola Syamsundarao, S. Babu, D. S. Kumar, Jyothi Goddu, B. Samatha · 2023 5th International Conference on Smart Systems and Inventive Technology (ICSSIT) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icssit55814.2023.10060872 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** By exchanging safety-related messages, the Internet of Vehicles (IoV) technology can reduce traffic collisions.
**Approach:** With the help of the internet, vehicles can communicate and exchange information about location and speed with other cars and with roadside devices. False alarms, improper vehicle placement, and other forms of assault become commonplace in the automotive network.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The experimental findings demonstrate an accuracy of 99.57%, which is better than any other RNN- or CNN-based model currently available.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- To further enhance the model's efficacy and accuracy, it may be trained in the future on more datasets.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
