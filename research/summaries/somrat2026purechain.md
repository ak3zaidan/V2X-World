# A Purechain-Protected Ai-Based Misbehaviour Detection System for Iov
Md Mehedi Hasan Somrat, Esmot Ara Tuli, Mahbuba Iasmin Sumona, Jae-Min Lee et al. · 2026 International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Information and Communication (ICAIIC) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icaiic68212.2026.11454346 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Real-time MBD with tamper-proof logging.
**Approach:** LSTM detector with alerts logged on PureChain (PoA2 consensus).
**Tools and scale:** Experimental prototype; vehicular data streams.
**Results:** Detection accuracy above 96%; 1.26 s average blockchain latency at 14.56 TPS.
