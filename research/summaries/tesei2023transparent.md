# A transparent distributed ledger-based certificate revocation scheme for VANETs
Andrea Tesei, Domenico Lattuca, Marco Luise, Paolo Pagano et al. · Journal of Network and Computer Applications 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnca.2022.103569 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Existing revocation leaves misbehaving vehicles trusted for a long vulnerability window.
**Approach:** Distributed-ledger vehicular revocation scheme, privacy-aware and transparent.
**Tools and scale:** DLT implementation and evaluation against EU/US processing-time requirements.
**Results:** Vulnerability window below 1 second; meets standard message processing times.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — Explicitly targets the revocation vulnerability window (our "time to neutralise"), quantified.
**Novelty implications:** We cannot claim first quantification of revocation vulnerability window. Open: safety impact (crashes avoided) and SCMS-conformant MA pipeline in closed loop.
