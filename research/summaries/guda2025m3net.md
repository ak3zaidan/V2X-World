# M3Net: A Multi-Metric Mixture of Experts Network Digital Twin with Graph Neural Networks
Blessed Guda, Carlee Joe-Wong · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.09797 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** NDTs predict single metrics or use only simulated data.
**Approach:** M3Net: mixture-of-experts GNN built on RouteNet-Fermi for multiple metrics.
**Tools and scale:** Real-world network data.
**Results:** Delay MAPE from 20.06% to 17.39%; 66.47% and 78.7% accuracy for jitter and drops.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Balance of model complexity (4 vs 8 experts).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - surrogate.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
