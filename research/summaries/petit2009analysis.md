# Analysis of ECDSA Authentication Processing in VANETs
J. Petit · International Conference on New Technologies, Mobility and Security 2009 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ntms.2009.5384696 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Transport safety applications aim at avoiding vehicular accidents by using secure broadcast vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) communications. However, any security mechanism used for authenticating broadcast V2V messages comes with overhead in terms of computation and communications.
**Approach:** This paper provides an assessment of the processing overhead of ECDSA.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The IEEE1609.2 standard for vehicular ad hoc networks is based on the ECDSA algorithm for supporting the authentication mechanism.
