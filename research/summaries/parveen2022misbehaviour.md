# Misbehaviour Detection based on Least Square Twin Support Vector Machine in VANETs
H Summia Parveen, S.V. Evangelin Sonia · 2022 International Conference on Automation, Computing and Renewable Systems (ICACRS) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icacrs55517.2022.10029131 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Classifying VANET misbehaviour with ML.
**Approach:** Least Square Twin SVM used in a data-based trust model (message content) and a vehicle-based trust model (propagation behaviour); Dempster-Shafer fusion at a trust authority.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation dataset (unspecified in abstract).
**Results:** LSTSVM outperforms base classifiers (no numbers in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — local detection plus central trust aggregation resembles MA-side fusion.
**Novelty implications:** Central report fusion exists; linkage to SCMS revocation and safety outcome remains open.
