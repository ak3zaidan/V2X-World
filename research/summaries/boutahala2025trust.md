# Trust Verification in Connected Vehicles Using Unsupervised Variational Autoencoder
Ramzi Boutahala, H. Fouchal, Marwane Ayaida, Shi-Wen Mao · International Conference on Wireless Networks and Mobile Communications 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wincom65874.2025.11313388 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Connected and Automated Mobility (CCAM) is undergoing a paradigm shift, with safety and efficiency increasingly dependent on connectivity. Cooperative Intelligent Transport Systems (C-ITS) support this transformation by enabling the exchange of Cooperative Awareness Messages (CAMs) between vehicles and roadside infrastructure.
**Approach:** In this paper we propose a new authentication scheme based on deep learning.
**Tools and scale:** Through simulations using OMNeT++, SUMO, and Artery, our method achieved a $\text{48.9 \%}$ reduction in the volume of messages exchanged between vehicles, significantly decreasing communication channel overhead.
**Results:** Through simulations using OMNeT++, SUMO, and Artery, our method achieved a $\text{48.9 \%}$ reduction in the volume of messages exchanged between vehicles, significantly decreasing communication channel overhead.
