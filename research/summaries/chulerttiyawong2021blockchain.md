# A Blockchain Assisted Vehicular Pseudonym Issuance and Management System for Conditional Privacy Enhancement
Donpiti Chulerttiyawong, Abbas Jamalipour · IEEE Access 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2021.3112013 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Conditional privacy pseudonym issuance across jurisdictions.
**Approach:** Permissioned consortium blockchain with smart contracts for pseudonym issuance and management alongside PKI and RSUs.
**Tools and scale:** Veins small-scale simulation.
**Results:** Feasible in small-scale simulation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Small-scale simulation only.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — multi-jurisdiction pseudonym issuance relates to multi-provider SCMS runner-up.
**Novelty implications:** Multi-jurisdiction issuance is proposed; multi-provider SCMS interoperability measurement remains open.
