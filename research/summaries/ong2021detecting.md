# Detecting and Rectifying Vehicle Malicious Misbehavior for Intersection Movement Assist: A Sensor-Based Misbehavior Detection Study
B. Ong, Joshua Kolleda, Saleh R. Mousa, Scott Andrews, Dennis Fleming, Jim Marousek et al. · Transportation Research Record 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1177/03611981211051341 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Malicious vehicles in Intersection Movement Assist.
**Approach:** Minimum detectable error from field sensor/GPS profiles; sensor-based MBD in IMA.
**Tools and scale:** Field-test error profiles plus simulation.
**Results:** MDE grows laterally with distance, constant longitudinally; quantifies false positive/negative hazards.
