# Yes, CARLA CAN: Extending the CARLA Simulator for In-Vehicle Network Cybersecurity Experimentation
Luigi F. Marques da Luz, Vinicius L. Ventura, Divanilson R. Campelo · Anais Estendidos do XLIV Simpósio Brasileiro de Redes de Computadores e Sistemas Distribuídos (SBRC 2026) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.5753/sbrc_estendido.2026.23250 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Modern vehicles rely on in-vehicle networks (IVNs), but protocols such as CAN lack built-in security, exposing critical systems to cyber threats. Existing platforms often require real vehicles or hardware, limiting accessibility.
**Approach:** This work presents a software-based platform for automotive cybersecurity research and experimentation.
**Tools and scale:** CARLA with a virtual CAN bus; ECUs, attackers and IDS as independent nodes; no hardware.
**Results:** No quantitative results stated in the abstract.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Only CAN; plans to add automotive Ethernet (e.g. AVTP).
- Plans "automatic labeling for intrusion detection experiments".
- Plans classroom integration.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — In-vehicle network (CAN) security, not V2X/PKI; closed-loop attack-in-simulation idea is analogous but at a different layer.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves V2X-level closed-loop attacks and revocation untouched.
