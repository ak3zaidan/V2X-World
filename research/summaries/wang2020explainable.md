# Explainable Congestion Attack Prediction and Software-level Reinforcement in Intelligent Traffic Signal System
Xiaojin Wang, Yingxiao Xiang, Wenjia Niu, Endong Tong et al. · 2020 IEEE 26th International Conference on Parallel and Distributed Systems (ICPADS) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icpads51040.2020.00094 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With connected vehicle(CV) technology, the next-generation transportation system is stepping into its implementation phase via the deployment of Intelligent Traffic Signal System (I-SIG).
**Approach:** Since the congestion attack was firstly discovered in USDOT (U.S. Department of Transportation) sponsored I-SIG, deployed in three cities including New York, such realistic threat opens a new security issue.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: traffic signal attacks).
**Results:** We first adopt the Tree-regularized Gated Recurrent Unit (TGRU) to make explainable congestion attack prediction, in which 32-dimension features are defined to character a 8-phase intersection traffic. In massive experiments based on real-world intersection settings, we eventually distill 384 samples of congestion attacks to train a TGRU-based attack prediction model, and achieve an average 80% precision.
