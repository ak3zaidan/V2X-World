# Lightweight Kalman Spoofing Detection in Platoons of Vehicles
Dimitrios Kosmanos, Zisis-Rafail Tzoannos, Apostolos Xenakis, Costas Chaikalis · Electronics 2026 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics15010205 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** GPS/measurement spoofing in DSRC platoons.
**Approach:** Two-level Kalman-filter detector: residual heuristic with adaptive thresholds plus Mahalanobis consistency test at 95% confidence.
**Tools and scale:** DSRC platoon.
**Results:** No quantitative results given in the abstract.
