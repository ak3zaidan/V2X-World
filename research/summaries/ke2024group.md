# A Group-Vehicles Oriented Reputation Assessment Scheme for Edge VANETs
Changbo Ke, Fu Xiao, Yan Cao, Zhiqiu Huang · IEEE Transactions on Cloud Computing 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tcc.2024.3406509 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the development of the smart traffic, the traditional vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANETs) and Traffic Estimation and Prediction System (TrEPS) do not satisfy the growing safety requirement, due to the network delay, transmit price and privacy security.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a group-vehicles oriented reputation assessment scheme for edge VANETs. Firstly, based on edge computing, we build a reputation assessment framework for Group-Vehicles, to validate the correctness of message for other vehicles rapidly.
