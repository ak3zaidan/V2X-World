# A Novel Machine Learning-Based Trust Management Against Multiple Misbehaviors for Connected and Automated Vehicles
Qian-Kun Xu, Lei Zhang, Xiaojie Qin, Yi-Xuan Zhou · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2024.3422423 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Multiple misbehaviours in real traffic for CAV trust management.
**Approach:** Features (spatio-temporal logic, behavioural, traffic flow), fusion of beacons with map and detector data, multi-dimensional trust factors, per-minute and multi-minute ML trust prediction.
**Tools and scale:** Real traffic scenarios (data source not specified in abstract).
**Results:** XGBoost strong per-minute; DL better multi-minute.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — node-level trust over minutes (MA-like aggregation).
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
