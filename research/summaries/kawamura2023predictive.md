# Predictive QoS of wireless communication for autonomous driving vehicles by fine tuning
Kenichi Kawamura, Naoki Shibuya, Motoharu Sasaki, Takatsune Moriyama, Yasushi Takatori · 2023 IEEE 34th Annual International Symposium on Personal, Indoor and Mobile Radio Communications (PIMRC) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/pimrc56721.2023.10293947 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Predicting wireless quality degradation for autonomous vehicles.
**Approach:** NN throughput estimation with fine-tuning from large received-power and small throughput data.
**Tools and scale:** Two outdoor and one indoor use cases.
**Results:** Fine-tuning improves accuracy.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
