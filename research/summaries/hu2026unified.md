# A Unified Simulation Platform and Computation-Reuse Algorithm for Task Scheduling in Vehicle-Infrastructure Collaboration
Shihong Hu, Zhipeng Li, Zhihao Qu, Bin Tang, Baoliu Ye, Xiongxiong Xu · IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnsm.2025.3642265 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle-Infrastructure Collaboration (VIC) integrates vehicles and roadside infrastructure using advanced communication technologies, forming a crucial component of intelligent transportation systems (ITS).
**Approach:** In a VIC system, tasks generated by vehicles can either be processed locally or offloaded to nearby edge servers. Current research often focuses on optimizing task scheduling but overlooks the inherent spatiotemporal correlations among tasks, which can lead to redundant computations due to similar tasks producing identical results.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: SUMO; scale not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The results highlight the versatility of the CVSP, showing that the BB-CR algorithm reduces system costs and minimizes the probability of task loss compared to the baseline.
