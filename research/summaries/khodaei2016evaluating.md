# Evaluating on-demand pseudonym acquisition policies in vehicular communication systems
Mohammad Khodaei, Panos Papadimitratos · Proceedings of the First International Workshop on Internet of Vehicles and Vehicles of Internet 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1145/2938681.2938684 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** How frequently and for how long vehicles should obtain pseudonyms.
**Approach:** Three on-demand acquisition policies evaluated on a standard-compliant VPKI.
**Tools and scale:** KTH VPKI implementation; LuST and another large mobility dataset; modest VMs.
**Results:** VMs serve on-demand requests with very low delay; most privacy-protective policy has moderate overhead.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Investigate pseudonym utilisation with various refill intervals and lifetimes versus server workload and privacy.
- Analyse unlinkability based on pseudonym timing.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — VPKI load under pseudonym acquisition policies at city scale overlaps our provisioning/top-up evaluation.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first VPKI provisioning-load evaluation with real mobility; US butterfly-key SCMS with cellular transport remains distinct.
