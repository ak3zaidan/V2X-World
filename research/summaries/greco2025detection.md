# Detection of C-V2X Spoofing Attacks using Physical Layer Features and Graph Neural Networks
Danilo Greco, Muhammad Saad Sohail, Mario Marchese · 2025 IEEE International Conference on Cyber Security and Resilience (CSR) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/csr64739.2025.11130047 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Credential compromise means cryptography cannot assure content in C-V2X.
**Approach:** SDR-DL framework: position-change detector plus GNN on RSSI patterns.
**Tools and scale:** Python simulation, TensorFlow and PyTorch Geometric.
**Results:** High accuracy for modelled scenarios (no numbers).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Data requirements and computational complexity.
- future multi-feature fusion and explainable AI.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — PHY-feature spoofing detection.
**Novelty implications:** Simulation-only; real radio-layer ground truth evaluation open.
