# Fake Beacon: A Pseudonym Changing Scheme for Low Vehicle Density in VANETs
Junchao Wang, Yan Sun, Chris Phillips · 2023 IEEE 97th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2023-Spring) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2023-spring57618.2023.10199627 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Mix zones fail at low density.
**Approach:** RSU fake beacons imitating vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Traffic simulation with tracking adversary.
**Results:** Adversary trace recovery success drops over 50% versus traditional mix zones.
