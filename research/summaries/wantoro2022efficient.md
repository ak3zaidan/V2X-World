# Efficient and Privacy-Preserving Certificate Activation for V2X Pseudonym Certificate Revocation
Jan Wantoro, Masahiro Mambo · Journal of Sensor and Actuator Networks 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/jsan11030051 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Activation-code revocation (ACPC) uses network resources inefficiently between RSU and vehicles.
**Approach:** Efficient privacy-preserving activation code distribution with per-period code identities and openly cacheable codes.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Fewer distributed codes; smaller vulnerability window against revoked vehicles (stated qualitatively).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Determine optimal management and settings via simulations
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — SCMS activation-code revocation and vulnerability window, relevant to direction 1 design.
**Novelty implications:** Simulation of optimal settings explicitly open.
