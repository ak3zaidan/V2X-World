# Poster: Distributed Deep Learning-Based CQI Prediction for UAV-Assisted URLLC V2X Networks
Muhammed Mufeed M, Sneha S, Suresh Chavhan · Proceedings of the 7th International Workshop on Drone-Assisted Wireless Communications for 5G and Beyond 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3842435.3843961 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In 5G NR, downlink link adaptation mainly relies on the Channel Quality Indicator (CQI), which is a UE-reported metric that indicates the downlink channel quality as experienced by the receiver. Accurate CQI estimation is crucial for URLLC-V2X networks.
**Approach:** However, conventional CQI reporting is centralised and affected by feedback channel uncertainty, delays, and ageing.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** This proposed framework enables distributed CQI prediction at the Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) side, enabling proactive link adaptation with lower latency for URLLC V2X networks.
