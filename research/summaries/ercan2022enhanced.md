# An enhanced pseudonym certificates distribution mechanism for connected vehicles
Secil Ercan, Marwane Ayaida, N. Messai · International Journal of Communication Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1002/dac.5100 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** RSUs too costly for certificate download coverage.
**Approach:** Trusted road operator vehicles provide certificates; mathematical model with RSUs and ROVs.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** ROVs almost double communication probability for downloading certificates.
