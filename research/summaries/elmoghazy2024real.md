# A Real-World Testbed for V2X in Autonomous Vehicles: From Simulation to Actual Road Testing
Ammar Elmoghazy, Khalid Elgazzar, Sanaa Alwidian · 2024 IEEE 8th International Conference on Fog and Edge Computing (ICFEC) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icfec61590.2024.00012 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Traditional vehicle tests and simulation miss NLOS/occluded pedestrian situations.
**Approach:** Edge-computing testbed with F1Tenth scaled vehicle and RSU.
**Tools and scale:** F1Tenth, RSU edge device.
**Results:** End-to-end response time under 146 ms on an edge device.
