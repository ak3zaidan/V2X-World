# Efficient coverage planning for full-area C-ITS communications based on radio propagation simulation and measurement tools
Hagen Ußler, Christian Setzefand, Daniel Kanis, Paul Schwarzbach, Oliver Michler · ITS World 2021, Book of Abstracts, https://itsworldcongress.com/the-book-of-abstracts-download/ 2022 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.03282 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** RSU coverage planning for C-ITS.
**Approach:** 3D propagation simulation calibrated with CW and C-ITS measurements on a 3.5 km rural testbed.
**Tools and scale:** Propagation simulation, real measurements.
**Results:** Calibrated simulation allows RSU placement without measurement effort.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Validate calibration with larger database
- module-specific calibration
- consider ray tracing, beam steering, leaky cables.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — RSU placement (our RSUs relay CRLs).
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
