# A Framework Coupling VISSIM and OMNeT++ to Simulate Future Intelligent Transportation Systems
Tibor Petrov, Ilya Finkelberg, Nina Zarkhin, Peter Počta, Ľuboš Buzna, Ayelet Gal-Tzur et al. · Communications - Scientific letters of the University of Zilina 2021 · https://doi.org/10.26552/com.c.2021.2.c23-c29 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Assess relationship between communication reliability and transport service quality.
**Approach:** Real-time bidirectional coupling of VISSIM and OMNeT++; adaptive signal control from V2I data.
**Tools and scale:** VISSIM, OMNeT++; cross intersection.
**Results:** Communication errors increase with distance and co-channel interference; minimal near RSU (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: impact of imperfect communication on signal control and mitigations.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — communication-to-traffic control coupling.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
