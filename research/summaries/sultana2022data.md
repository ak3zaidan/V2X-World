# A Data-Centric and Dynamic-Range based Misbehavior Detection Approach for VANET
Rukhsar Sultana, Jyoti Grover, Meenakshi Tripathi · TENCON 2022 - 2022 IEEE Region 10 Conference (TENCON) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tencon55691.2022.9977588 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Fake alerts from credentialed insiders.
**Approach:** Data-centric consistency and plausibility checks; real-time labelled dataset of ten attacks in Jaipur road scenario; comparison with foreign scenario.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation in Jaipur and a foreign road network.
**Results:** Detects significant attacks; results differ between scenarios.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Results vary due to scenario differences.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — shows city-dependence of MBD results (relevant to our 50+ maps).
**Novelty implications:** Scenario-dependence of MBD shown; systematic multi-city evaluation open.
