# Message Linkable Group Signature With Information Binding and Efficient Revocation for Privacy- Preserving Announcement in VANETs
Lei Zhang, Jiangtao Li, Yafang Yang · IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tdsc.2024.3381436 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Fake announcements in VANETs vs privacy; Sybil and collusive endorsement.
**Approach:** Message-linkable group signature with information binding and efficient revocation, giving "strong trustworthiness" for announcements.
**Tools and scale:** Cryptographic construction; simulations for practicality.
**Results:** Claims first scheme with strong trustworthiness; practical per simulation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — alternative privacy credential (group signatures) with revocation, vs SCMS pseudonyms.
**Novelty implications:** Non-SCMS alternative; not a direct overlap.
