# ALI: Anonymous Lightweight Inter-Vehicle Broadcast Authentication With Encryption
Mir Ali Rezazadeh Baee, L. Simpson, Xavier Boyen, E. Foo, Josef Pieprzyk · IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tdsc.2022.3164436 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** No computationally efficient privacy-preserving broadcast authentication for latency-critical V2V in heavy traffic.
**Approach:** ALI: anonymous lightweight inter-vehicle broadcast authentication with beacon encryption.
**Tools and scale:** RELIC toolkit; Intel Core 2 Duo; 10^6 trials.
**Results:** Cryptographic overhead 149 bytes per message.
