# Open Source-based Over-The-Air 5G New Radio Sidelink Testbed
Melissa Elkadi, Doekseong Kim, Ejaz Ahmed, Moein Sadeghi, Anh Le, Paul Russell et al. · arXiv 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/milcom58377.2023.10356257 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Need open 5G NR sidelink prototype.
**Approach:** OAI-based NR sidelink on SDRs: PSBCH synchronisation and PSSCH data; RFSIM and OTA tests.
**Tools and scale:** OpenAirInterface5G, USRP B210/N310; 2-UE scenarios.
**Results:** PSBCH/PSSCH robust; RFSIM and OTA BLER compared.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- SCI1 hardcoded
- PSFCH/PSCCH and higher layers future work
- lab conditions vary results.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — NR sidelink implementation (real radio).
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
