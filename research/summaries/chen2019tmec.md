# TMEC: A Trust Management Based on Evidence Combination on Attack-Resistant and Collaborative Internet of Vehicles
Ji-Ming Chen, Ting-ting Li, John Panneerselvam · IEEE Access 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2018.2876153 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Detecting malicious relay nodes and unreliable multi-dimensional data in multi-hop IoV message dissemination.
**Approach:** TMEC trust management: Dempster-Shafer evidence combination of local and external evidence for data trust; direct trust from ETSI DENM interactions over short time intervals; indirect trust via collaborative-filtering recommendations (cosine similarity) to resist collusion; combined into global trust.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation experiments under several network scenarios; simulator, radio and scale not identifiable in the extracted sections. No PKI model.
**Results:** Reported to outperform existing trust models in precision and recall and to resist bad-mouth, selective-misbehaviour and time-dependent attacks, especially with large malicious proportions (no figures extracted).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Authors point to social IoV relationships and community-based services as future context
- no explicit limitations section.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — trust-based local misbehaviour detection on DENMs; no closed loop to revocation, no PKI.
**Novelty implications:** Trust/evidence-fusion detectors are well covered; does not address revocation latency or safety impact.
