# An Enhanced Hybrid Post-Quantum Digital Signature Scheme and Its Application
Shijun Yu · 2024 6th International Academic Exchange Conference on Science and Technology Innovation (IAECST) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iaecst64597.2024.11117218 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In order to meet the challenges brought by quantum computing, research on post-quantum cryptography has been carried out comprehensively.
**Approach:** This paper improves the post-quantum hybrid digital signature scheme used by the OQS project team. Subsequently, an enhanced scheme is constructed and implemented using the hybrid algorithm combiner proposed by Nina Bindel et al., solving the security issues.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** In July 2022, NIST announced that the Dilithium, Falcon, and SPHINCS+ algorithms were selected as candidate schemes for the standardization of post-quantum cryptographic digital signatures. The unforgeability and 2 -non-separability of the enhanced scheme are verified through formal analysis, and forward compatibility is achieved.
