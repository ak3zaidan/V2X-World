# Cloud based VANET Simulator (CVANETSIM)
Mohammad Mukhtaruzzaman, Mohammed Atiquzzaman · arXiv 2021 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.11147 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** No web-based VANET simulator with clustering module.
**Approach:** CVANETSIM: Java/MySQL/Tomcat discrete-event cloud simulator processing SUMO data with built-in clustering.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO input, web interface.
**Results:** Functional simulator (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Integrate more VANET protocols
- Android version.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
