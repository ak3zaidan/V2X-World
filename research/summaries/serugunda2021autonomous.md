# Autonomous Network Traffic Classifier Agent for Autonomic Network Management System
Claire Naiga Serugunda, Sisay Tadesse Arzo, Fabrizio Granelli, Riccardo Bassoli, Michael Devetsikiotis, Frank H.P. Fitzek · 2021 IEEE Global Communications Conference (GLOBECOM) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/globecom46510.2021.9685568 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** An autonomic network management system (ANMS) is expected to play a significant role in fifth and sixth-generation (5G and 6G) networks.
**Approach:** It enables the network to manage itself with minimum or no human intervention. Recently, an ANMS architecture called multi-agent-based network automation of the network management system (MANA-NMS) architecture was presented.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G.
**Results:** The results show that the Decision Tree NTCA has the highest mean classification accuracy, the least mean training latency, and the lowest mean classification latency.
