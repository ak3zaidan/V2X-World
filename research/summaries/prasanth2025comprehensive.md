# A Comprehensive Performance Study of Authentication Protocols for VANETs
Dumpala Prasanth, S. Christy · 2025 5th International Conference on Expert Clouds and Applications (ICOECA) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icoeca66273.2025.00051 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) are crucial for enabling intelligent transportation systems and providing secure and efficient communication among vehicles and infrastructure.
**Approach:** This paper presents a comprehensive performance analysis of authentication techniques, categorized into five key approaches: cryptography-based, signature-based, verification-based, emerging technology-driven, and lightweight algorithms. The findings aim to guide researchers and practitioners in selecting and designing robust authentication protocols for next-generation intelligent transportation systems.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** The results demonstrate the trade-offs among various techniques, such as the scalability of batch-assisted verification, the low latency of TESLA, and the privacy-preserving capabilities of Blockchain-based protocols. While lightweight techniques like PA-CRT and TESLA are ideal for resource-constrained environments, advanced approaches like 5G-SDN Hybrid Authentication provide superior scalability and low latency for high-density traffic scenarios.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "This study highlights the strengths and limitations of each approach, providing insights into their suitability for different VANET applications."
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
