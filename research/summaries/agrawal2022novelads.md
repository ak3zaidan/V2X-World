# NovelADS: A Novel Anomaly Detection System for Intra-Vehicular Networks
Kushagra Agrawal, Tejasvi Alladi, A. Agrawal, Vinay Chamola et al. · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2022.3146024 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Modern vehicular electronics is a complex system of multiple Electronic Control Units (ECUs) communicating to provide efficient vehicle functioning. These ECUs communicate using the well-known Controller Area Network (CAN) protocol.
**Approach:** This work presents a novel deep learning-based Intrusion Detection System incorporating thresholding and error reconstruction approaches. We also present reconstruction-error distribution plots to give a qualitative intuition about the proposed system’s ability to distinguish between genuine and anomalous sequences.
