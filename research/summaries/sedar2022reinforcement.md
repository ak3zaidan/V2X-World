# Reinforcement Learning Based Misbehavior Detection in Vehicular Networks
R. Sedar, C. Kalalas, F. Vázquez-Gallego, J. Alonso-Zarate · ICC 2022 - IEEE International Conference on Communications 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc45855.2022.9838796 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Detecting false V2X data to protect road safety.
**Approach:** RL-based detection at RSUs processing V2X broadcasts as time series.
**Tools and scale:** Open-source dataset (VeReMi extension).
**Results:** Recall 0.9970, F1 0.9845; vehicle-level accuracy 0.9882.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — RSU-side MBD.
**Novelty implications:** Saturated offline.
