# A survey on security challenges and malicious vehicle detection in vehicular ad hoc networks
Neha Roy, Y. Bevish Jinila · Contemporary Engineering Sciences 2015 · https://doi.org/10.12988/ces.2015.5121 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In Vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANETs), vehicles periodically broadcast messages for every 100 to 300 milli seconds.
**Approach:** Such messages are used to report a safety or non-safety application. Authentication on such safety messages assures the end users in the network.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** No quantitative results stated in the abstract.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, a malicious vehicle can trace the messages and can launch varied types of malicious activity which leads to the compromise of personal privacy.
- This paper presents the security challenges faced by the network and analyzes the malicious Sybil activity.
