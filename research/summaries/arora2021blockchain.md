# Blockchain Based Trust Model Using Tendermint in Vehicular Adhoc Networks
Sandeep Kumar Arora, Gulshan Kumar, Tai-hoon Kim · Applied Sciences 2021 · https://doi.org/10.3390/app11051998 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Blockchain is the consensus-based technology used to resolve conflicts in Byzantine environments. Vehicles validate the messages received from neighboring vehicles using the gradient boosting technique (GBT).
**Approach:** We proposed a blockchain-based trust management model for the vehicular adhoc network (VANET) based on Tendermint. Simulation results also show that the proposed system is 7.8% and 15.6% effective and efficient in terms of packet delivery ratio (PDR) and end-to-end delay (EED), respectively, to collect the trusted data between the vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset, blockchain (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** Simulation results also show that the proposed system is 7.8% and 15.6% effective and efficient in terms of packet delivery ratio (PDR) and end-to-end delay (EED), respectively, to collect the trusted data between the vehicles.
