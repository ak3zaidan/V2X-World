# Comparative Study of Simulators for Vehicular Networks
Rida Saghir, Thenuka Karunathilake, Anna Förster · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.00546 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Which VANET simulators give a good trade-off between resources and realism.
**Approach:** Identifies simulators and compares ns-3 and OMNeT++ (both with SUMO) on a city map with heavy flow and AODV.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3, OMNeT++, SUMO, AODV.
**Results:** Compared computation time, memory and PDR; results differ between simulators even with identical parameters.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Results differ across simulators with identical parameters (noted).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — simulator cross-comparison and performance, relevant to our cross-validation claims.
**Novelty implications:** Shows simulator disagreement is known; our matching to Veins/OpenCV2X must be argued carefully.
