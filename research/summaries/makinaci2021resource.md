# Resource Selection for C-V2X and Simulation Study for Performance Evaluation
Kemal Mert Makinaci, Tankut Acarman, Cagdas Yaman · 2021 IEEE 93rd Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2021-Spring) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2021-spring51267.2021.9448905 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-Hoc Network is an emerging technology and research area since it promises fast and lossless communication among vehicular nodes.
**Approach:** Vehicular to Everything (V2X) communication targets data sharing between the vulnerable road users and cars with mobile network assistance in the near future. Despite the nature of vehicular mobility and density, V2X requires transmitting massive data over a long range and being lossless.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting. The available text does not describe a V2X simulator, radio stack, traffic model or SCMS-style PKI in enough detail to report; scale not stated.
**Results:** Despite the nature of vehicular mobility and density, V2X requires transmitting massive data over a long range and being lossless. To evaluate and test the LTE deployment in a VANET, an NS3 simulator coupled with a SUMO simulator is built to enable scalable simulation of various traffic scenarios.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Vehicular to Everything (V2X) communication targets data sharing between the vulnerable road users and cars with mobile network assistance in the near future.
- LTE becomes the main infrastructure for V2X and called Cellular V2X (C-V2X), but it is still a developing technology necessitating further research efforts.
