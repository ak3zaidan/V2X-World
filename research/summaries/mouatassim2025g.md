# G-Net: A Novel Intrusion Detection Model for VANETs Based on Convolutional Neural Network
Khadija Mouatassim, Abdelfettah Mabrouk, Chaimae Ouchicha · International Conference on Innovative Research in Applied Science, Engineering and Technology (IRASET) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iraset64571.2025.11008324 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs) play a critical role in intelligent transportation systems by enabling seamless communication between vehicles and infrastructure.
**Approach:** These networks optimize traffic management, enhance road safety, and support the development of autonomous driving technologies. In this paper, we propose G-Net, a novel network intrusion detection model using convolutional neural networks CNNs for multi-class classification.
**Tools and scale:** The performance of the proposed intrusion detection model is evaluated on KDD-NSL dataset and compared with other existing works. Empirical results show that the proposed model achieved an accuracy of 98,57%, precision of 98,64%, recall of 98,57% and an F1-score of 98,58%, for 5-class classification using NSLKDD dataset.
**Results:** Empirical results show that the proposed model achieved an accuracy of 98,57%, precision of 98,64%, recall of 98,57% and an F1-score of 98,58%, for 5-class classification using NSLKDD dataset.
