# Collaborative Learning Based Sybil Attack Detection in Vehicular AD-HOC Networks (VANETS)
Sofia Azam, Maryum Bibi, Rabia Riaz, Sanam Shahla Rizvi et al. · Sensors 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s22186934 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Sybil attacks in VANETs.
**Approach:** Ensemble majority voting (hard/soft) over KNN, NB, DT, SVM, LR.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO + NS-2, three vehicle densities.
**Results:** 95% accuracy; soft voting better than hard.
