# Multi-Classification and Tree-Based Ensemble Network for the Intrusion Detection System in the Internet of Vehicles
Wanting Gou, Haodi Zhang, Ronghui Zhang · Sensors 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s23218788 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Internet of Vehicles(IoV) employs vehicle-to-everything (V2X) technology to establish intricate interconnections among the Internet, the IoT network, and the Vehicle Networks (IVNs), forming a complex vehicle communication network. However, the vehicle communication network is very vulnerable to attacks.
**Approach:** This paper proposes an adaptive tree-based ensemble network as the intrusion detection engine for the IDS in IoV. Finally, we evaluate the proposed IDS performance on various cyber-attacks from the in-vehicle and external networks in IoV by using the network intrusion detection dataset CICIDS2017 and the vehicle security dataset Car-Hacking.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The experimental results demonstrate remarkable performance, with an F1-score of 0.965 on the CICIDS2017 dataset and an F1-score of 0.9999 on the Car-Hacking dataset.
