# Towards a Simulation Framework for Edge-to-Cloud Orchestration in C-ITS
Phu H. Nguyen, Asmund Hugo, Karl Svantorp, Bjorn Magne Elnes · 2020 21st IEEE International Conference on Mobile Data Management (MDM) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mdm48529.2020.00077 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Scalability of an edge-cloud C-ITS message platform to millions of vehicles.
**Approach:** SUMO/Veins simulation framework with MQTT clients to load-test the C-ITS platform.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO, Veins, MQTT.
**Results:** Initial steps (scenarios, MQTT integration) only.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- These are fundamental first steps towards a full simulation framework.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — backend infrastructure load testing driven by traffic simulation, analogous to SCMS backend load; no PKI.
**Novelty implications:** Backend load testing via traffic simulation exists generically; SCMS-specific remains open.
