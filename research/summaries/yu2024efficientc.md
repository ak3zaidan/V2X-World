# Efficient Privacy-Preserving Multi-Location Task Allocation in Fog-Assisted Vehicular Crowdsourcing
Yantao Yu, Yun-Guo Guan, Xiaoping Xue, Jingxiao Ma et al. · ICC 2024 - IEEE International Conference on Communications 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc51166.2024.10623008 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Multi-location task allocation is one of the most crucial issues in vehicular crowdsourcing (VCS). To ensure service quality, the VCS service provider prefers to assign multi-location tasks to the workers whose future trajectories have high spatial proximity with the task locations.
**Approach:** To address these challenges, we propose a novel efficient privacy-preserving multi-location task allocation scheme in fog-assisted VCS. Specifically, we design a lightweight secure Euclidean distance computation protocol based on arithmetic secret sharing (ASS), which can compute Euclidean distance without revealing the two input locations.
**Tools and scale:** Our security analysis demonstrates the location privacy preservation of our scheme, and the experiment results on a real dataset also validate the efficiency of our scheme.
**Results:** Our security analysis demonstrates the location privacy preservation of our scheme, and the experiment results on a real dataset also validate the efficiency of our scheme.
