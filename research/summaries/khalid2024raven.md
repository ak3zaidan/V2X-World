# RAVEN: Robust Anonymous Vehicular End-to-End Encryption and Efficient Mutual Authentication for Post-Quantum Intelligent Transportation Systems
Haqi Khalid, S. Hashim, F. Hashim, Waleed Ameen Mahmoud Al-Jawher et al. · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2024.3416060 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ITS authentication and data transmission vulnerable to quantum computers.
**Approach:** RAVEN: lattice-based (discrete Gaussian) end-to-end encryption and mutual authentication protocol for vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** SVO logic, informal analysis, AVISPA formal check.
**Results:** Claimed efficient and secure; abstract gives no comparable numbers.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — PQ authentication for vehicles, but bespoke protocol, not 1609.2/SCMS, no network simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Bespoke lattice protocols are crowded; evaluating standard-conformant PQ SCMS at scale is not addressed.
