# LB-CLAA: An Anonymous and Efficient Postquantum Certificateless Aggregate Authentication Scheme for VANET
Jian-Hong Zhang, Miao Xue, Xinyan Cui · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2026.3709922 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Certificateless VANET authentication is quantum-vulnerable and PQ signatures are large.
**Approach:** LB-CLAA: split-signature design removing vehicle-side trapdoor sampling, commitment aggregation and hash forwarding. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Evaluation over realistic DSRC channels; 100 concurrent vehicles.
**Results:** E2E delay 201 ms for 100 vehicles; over 95% lower E2E latency than state-of-the-art schemes.

- Not stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — PQ signature bandwidth over DSRC.
**Novelty implications:** Minor; certificateless, not SCMS.
