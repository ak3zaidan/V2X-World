# Secure Control of Connected and Autonomous Electrified Vehicles Under Adversarial Cyber-Attacks
Shashank Dhananjay Vyas, Satadru Dey · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.23922 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Connected and Autonomous Electrified Vehicles (CAEV) is the solution to the future smart mobility having benefits of efficient traffic flow and cleaner environmental impact.
**Approach:** To alleviate this issue, we propose a secure control architecture of CAEV. Particularly, we design an additional control input using Reinforcement Learning (RL) to be applied to the vehicle powertrain along with the input commanded by the battery.
**Tools and scale:** We present simulation case studies to demonstrate the potential of the proposed approach in keeping the CAEV platoon operating safely without collisions by curbing the effect of adversarial attacks.
**Results:** We present simulation case studies to demonstrate the potential of the proposed approach in keeping the CAEV platoon operating safely without collisions by curbing the effect of adversarial attacks.
