# Towards a Realistic Simulation Framework for Vehicular Platooning Applications
Bruno Vieira, Ricardo Severino, Anis Koubaa, Eduardo Tovar · 22nd IEEE International Symposium on Real-Time Computing (ISORC 2019) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/isorc.2019.00028 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Platooning validation needs realistic simulation tools.
**Approach:** Gazebo + OMNeT++ over ROS framework for autonomous platoons and cooperative control.
**Tools and scale:** Gazebo, OMNeT++, ROS; CAM frequency experiments.
**Results:** Positive feedback between network parameters and control; PID steering more stable with higher CAM frequency.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Further validation of simulator-introduced delay, other network parameters and other platooning control models.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
