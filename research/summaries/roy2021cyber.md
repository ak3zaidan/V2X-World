# Cyber-Attack Detection in Socio-Technical Transportation Systems Exploiting Redundancies Between Physical and Social Data
Tanushree Roy, Sara Sattarzadeh, Satadru Dey · arXiv 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tsmc.2023.3328319 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cyber-physical-social connectivity is a key element in Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSs) due to the ever-increasing interaction between human users and technological systems.
**Approach:** Exploiting this socio-technical feature to our advantage, we propose a cyber-attack detection scheme for ITSs that focuses on cyber-attacks on freeway traffic infrastructure. The proposed scheme combines two parallel macroscopic traffic model-based Partial Differential Equation (PDE) filters whose output residuals are compared to make decision on attack occurrences.
