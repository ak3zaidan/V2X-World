# Deep Belief Network Based Intrusion Detection in Vehicle Ad Hoc Network
Hassan M. Al-Jawahry, M. G, T. Veena, Vankudoth Ramesh, Tabitha Indupalli · 2024 International Conference on Distributed Computing and Optimization Techniques (ICDCOT) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icdcot61034.2024.10515496 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANET) have garnered a great deal of attention and development in recent years.
**Approach:** These networks are essential to the development of fully and partially autonomous vehicles. But there are a lot of obstacles because of security risks that are either exclusive to VANET or inherent in ad hoc networks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The DBN model that was put into practice performs better than expected; it achieves 96.32% accuracy, 96.21% recall, 96.87% F1-Score, and 92.95% precision.
