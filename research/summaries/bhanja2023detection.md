# Detection of Sybil &amp;amp; DDoS Attacks in VANET using Intelligent Technique
Urmila Bhanja, Ankit Majhi, Smrutirekha Sahoo, Debapriya Parida · Research Square 2023 · https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-3187938/v1 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Sybil and DDoS detection in VANETs.
**Approach:** Fuzzy logic controllers combining certificate-based, distributed trust and reputation evaluations.
**Tools and scale:** 453 DDoS and 500 Sybil data samples; regression-based evaluation.
**Results:** Better accuracy than SVM, KNN, DT, ANN, KSVM; margin of error 0.023 (DDoS) and 0.022 (Sybil) at 95% CI.

- None stated in the full text.

**Novelty implications:** No impact.
