# Optimizing IoV cloud trust with adaptive blockchain and reinforcement learning
Francis Shamili S, Gopi R · Scientific Reports 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-00951-1 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the advent of the Internet of Vehicles (IoV), vehicles produce an unprecedented data flow, inspiring innovative data-sharing strategies within the IoV ecosystem.
**Approach:** To enhance security, efficacy, and driving experiences, the IoV facilitates real-time information interchange across vehicles, infrastructure, and cloud platforms, thereby transforming transportation. However, there are several concerns with the present IoV, including privacy breaches, security holes, and the possibility of malevolent vehicles uploading false information to the network, which might cause significant traffic jams.
