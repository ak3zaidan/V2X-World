# Near-Field Integrated Sensing, Computing and Semantic Communication in Digital Twin-Assisted Vehicular Networks
Yinchao Yang, Yahao Ding, Jiaxiang Wang, Zhaohui Yang, Chen Zhu, Zhaoyang Zhang et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2026.3683229 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** DT synchronisation in vehicular networks needs high rates, sensing and resource management.
**Approach:** Near-field ISCSC framework with MU-MIMO RSUs, mmWave radar, particle filtering, heuristic assignment and alternating optimisation.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Better semantic throughput and sensing precision than existing designs.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Incorporate MEC
- cooperative multi-RSU sensing
- multi-modal semantic communication
- DT computing latency modelling.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
