# A Simulation Platform for Truck Platooning Evaluation in an Interactive Traffic Environment
Jia Hu, Xuerun Yan, Guan Wang, Meiting Tu et al. · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2024.3388161 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Truck platooning is a promising technology in freight transport.
**Approach:** To fill the research gap, this paper proposes a next-generation simulation platform. The proposed platform bears the following features: i) Compatibility with various platooning decision makers, planners, controllers, vehicle types and platoon management strategies; ii) Capability of evaluating platoon performance on the lateral dimension; iii) Prototype platoon management system provided for FOs; iv) Capability of evaluating truck platoon management performance in terms of sustainability and economy; v) Capability of evaluating the impact of interactive background traffic on platoon performance. vi) Capability of evaluating the impact of truck platoon management on traffic mobility.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** The results reveal that existing platoon lane-change technologies should be upgraded to be compatible with high-traffic-demand scenarios.
