# An Effective Scheme for Location Privacy in VANETs
Xingjun Shi, Huibin Xu · Journal of Networks 2014 · https://doi.org/10.4304/jnw.9.8.2239-2244 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Location privacy is an important concern in VANETs(Vehicular Ad hoc Networks).
**Approach:** Anonymity is one of commonly adopted solution to protect location privacy. In this paper, we present a mechanism based on an ID-based cryptosystem in order to ensure vehicles’ anonymity by pseudonyms.
