# Optimizing Urban Traffic Efficiency and Safety via V2X: A Simulation Study Using the MOSAIC Platform
Sebastian-Ioan Alupoaei, Constantin-Florin Caruntu · Sensors 2025 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s25175418 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** V2X benefits under partial adoption and cascading incidents.
**Approach:** Eclipse MOSAIC + SUMO model of Iasi with single/dual incidents at 0/50/100% penetration.
**Tools and scale:** MOSAIC, SUMO.
**Results:** Full V2X reduces average time loss by 18% and peak density by more than 70%; partial adoption gives limited benefits.
