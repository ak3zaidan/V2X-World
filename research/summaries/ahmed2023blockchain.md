# Blockchain-Assisted Privacy-Preserving and Context-Aware Trust Management Framework for Secure Communications in VANETs
Waheeb Ahmed, Di Wu, Daniel Mukathe · Sensors 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s23125766 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) are used for improving traffic efficiency and road safety. However, VANETs are vulnerable to various attacks from malicious vehicles.
**Approach:** Although several solutions for trust management in VANETs have been proposed to address these issues of malicious vehicles, existing trust management schemes have two main issues. Secondly, existing trust management schemes are not designed to operate in various contexts of VANETs that occur frequently due to sudden variations in the network dynamics, making existing solutions impractical for VANETs.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset, blockchain (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** According to efficiency analysis and simulation results, the proposed framework outperforms the baseline schemes and demonstrates to be secure, effective, and robust for enhancing vehicular communication security.
