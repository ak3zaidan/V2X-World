# An Enhanced K Nearest Neighbor Classifier for Malicious Node Detection in VANET
Abhilash Sonker, R. K. Gupta · International Journal of Advanced Research in Science, Communication and Technology 2022 · https://doi.org/10.48175/ijarsct-3390 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Recently, wireless communication technologies have become a vital part of our lives.
**Approach:** The advancements made in communication technology, VANET systems is been introduced. In this paper, we proposed an enhanced k-Nearest Neighbor classifier that detected the malicious node in VANET.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Compared to the existing classifier, the proposed classifier achieves 20-25% improvement. By doing so, the communication overhead and delay metrics have been achieved and also helps to minimize the computational storage costs.
