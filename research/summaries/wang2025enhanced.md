# Enhanced V2R Authentication for VANETs Using Group Signatures and Dynamic Pseudonyms
Wei-Yu Wang, Zhao-Yang Han, Yaodong Zhu, T. Gadekallu et al. · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2025.3539895 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) facilitate real-time information exchange through Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) and Vehicle-to-Roadside (V2R) communications. While V2R communication plays a crucial role, it faces significant security challenges due to the transmission of sensitive data, leaving the system vulnerable to man-in-the-middle, replay, and ...
**Approach:** To overcome these issues, we propose a lightweight and efficient authentication scheme that enhances vehicle privacy and security through a combination of signatures, pseudonyms, batch verification, and flexible certificate management.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The evaluation results demonstrate that the proposed scheme ensures comprehensive security by providing two-way authentication and guaranteeing anonymity. Moreover, the scheme significantly reduces both communication and computational overhead, offering an efficient and secure solution for V2R communication in VANET.
