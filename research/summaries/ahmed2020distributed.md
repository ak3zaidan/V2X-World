# A distributed trust mechanism for malicious behaviors in VANETs
Ali Kamil Ahmed, Mohanad Najm Abdulwahed, Behnam Farzaneh · Indonesian Journal of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science 2020 · https://doi.org/10.11591/ijeecs.v19.i3.pp1147-1155 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs) are one of the most important types of networks which are widely used in recent years.
**Approach:** Along with all the benefits of Quality of Service (QoS) improvements, vulnerability analysis for this type of networks is an important issue. For instance, a Gray-hole attack decreases network performance.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: NS-2, ns-2
**Results:** Simulation results show that the proposed method can prevent Gray-hole attack efficiently with low overhead.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
