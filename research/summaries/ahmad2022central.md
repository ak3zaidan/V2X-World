# Central Aggregator Intrusion Detection System for Denial of Service Attacks
Sajjad Ahmad, Imran Raza, M. Hasan Jamal, Sirojiddin Djuraev, Soojung Hur, Imran Ashraf · Computers, materials & continua/Computers, materials & continua (Print) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.32604/cmc.2023.032694 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle-to-grid technology is an emerging field that allows unused power from Electric Vehicles (EVs) to be used by the smart grid through the central aggregator.
**Approach:** Since the central aggregator is connected to the smart grid through a wireless network, it is prone to cyber-attacks that can be detected and mitigated using an intrusion detection system. However, existing intrusion detection systems cannot be used in the vehicle-to-grid network because of the special requirements and characteristics of the vehicle-to-grid network.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The analysis shows that the proposed system has a higher throughput, lower jitter, and higher accuracy as compared to the existing schemes.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, existing intrusion detection systems cannot be used in the vehicle-to-grid network because of the special requirements and characteristics of the vehicle-to-grid network.
- EVs are registered with a Central Aggregator (CAG) to exchange authenticated messages, and malicious EVs are added to a blacklist for violating a set of predefined policies to limit their interaction with the CAG.
