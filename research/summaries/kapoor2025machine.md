# Machine and Deep Learning for VANET Security: A Survey of Intrusion Detection Models and Deployment Realities
Prachi Kapoor, Ajay Prasad · International Journal of Computer Networks And Applications 2025 · https://doi.org/10.22247/ijcna/2025/43 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** – Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) facilitate critical, real-time communication within intelligent transportation systems.
**Approach:** However, they are vulnerable to various cyberattacks due to their decentralized and dynamic characteristics. Artificial Intelligence (AI)-based Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS) demonstrate strong capabilities in identifying threats within these vehicular environments.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** No quantitative results stated in the abstract.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, they are vulnerable to various cyberattacks due to their decentralized and dynamic characteristics.

- Furthermore, the paper surveys current state-of-the-art AI-based IDS for VANETs, revealing a significant lack of evaluations focused on practical deployment aspects.
- Building on these findings, this paper provides a comprehensive discussion of the challenges associated with the implementation and deployment of these systems.
