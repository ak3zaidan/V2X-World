# Resilient Control Design for Vehicular Platooning in an Adversarial Environment
Soodeh Dadras, Sara Dadras, Chris Winstead · 2019 American Control Conference (ACC) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.23919/acc.2019.8814721 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we propose a mitigation scheme to prevent an attacker from causing collisions in a vehicular platoon under a gain modification attack.
**Approach:** A control algorithm, based on fractional-order calculus and using only local sensor information, is shown to significantly alleviate the impact of the attacker. The control is incorporated into the system when attacker(s) is (are) detected.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: platoon/CACC attacks).
**Results:** Simulations demonstrate that by applying our proposed control method, collisions are eliminated despite the attacker maliciously altering its gain such that system becomes unstable or shows oscillatory behavior.
