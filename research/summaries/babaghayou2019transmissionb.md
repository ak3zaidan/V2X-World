# Transmission Range Changing Effects on Location Privacy-Preserving Schemes in the Internet of Vehicles
Messaoud Babaghayou, Nabila Labraoui, Ado Adamou Abba Ari, Abdelhak Mourad Gueroui · International Journal of Strategic Information Technology and Applications 2019 · https://doi.org/10.4018/ijsita.2019100103 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The internet of vehicles (IoV) is getting a considerable amount of attention from different research parties.
**Approach:** IoV aims at enhancing the driving experience with its wide range of applications varying from safety, road management to entertainment; however, some of such applications bring severe security and privacy issues; identity exposing, and location tracking are good examples. By enabling vehicles to send their statuses to themselves via beacon messages, this creates an environmental awareness for safety purposes but also exposes them to the aforementioned attacks.
