# Literature Survey on Recent Progress in Inter-Vehicle Communication Simulations
Markus Hager, Jochen Seitz, Thomas Waas · Journal of Transportation Technologies 2015 · https://doi.org/10.4236/jtts.2015.53015 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** How IVC simulation practice changed 2009-2013.
**Approach:** Survey of 2013 IVC simulation publications.
**Tools and scale:** Literature analysis.
**Results:** 30% or less of publications present results too abstractly, mostly missing propagation or mobility details.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Open: when accurate channel emulation is needed versus simpler models
- mobility model degrees of freedom.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — simulation methodology rigour.
**Novelty implications:** Cite for reporting standards.
