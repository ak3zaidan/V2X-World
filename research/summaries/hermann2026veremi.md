# VeReMi NextGen: A Dataset for Evaluating Misbehavior Detection Systems in VANETs
Artur Hermann, Jan-Niklas Remmers, Dennis Eisermann, Benjamin Erb, Frank Kargl · IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference 2026 · https://doi.org/10.18725/oparu-60763 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** Existing MBD datasets lack attack diversity and splits.
**Approach:** VeReMi NextGen: Eclipse MOSAIC with InTAS, urban and highway, three driver profiles, 15 attacks, splits, public generator.
**Tools and scale:** Eclipse MOSAIC, InTAS.
**Results:** Attacks harder to detect than VeReMi Extension per state-of-the-art MDS.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- No RSSI in dataset.
- future VRUs.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — Simulation-generated labelled MBD benchmark with generator, close to our benchmark direction.
**Novelty implications:** New benchmark exists; per-unit counterfactual ground truth and PKI/radio joint labels remain open.
