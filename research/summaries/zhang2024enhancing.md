# On Enhancing Intersection Applications With Misbehavior Detection and Mitigation
Jiahao Zhang, Ziyi Liu, I. Ben Jemaa, Francesca Bassi, F. Nashashibi · 2024 IEEE 100th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2024-Fall) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2024-fall63153.2024.10757840 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** CPM attacks degrade intersection safety apps.
**Approach:** Architecture integrating MBD and mitigation with CPS; IMA application; safety metrics.
**Tools and scale:** Large-scale simulations (Artery/CARLA based framework).
**Results:** MBD filters malicious data and improves IMA robustness.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: trust-based MBD.
- multiple traffic scenarios.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — Quantifies safety-application impact of attacks and MBD in closed loop.
**Novelty implications:** Closed-loop MBD-to-safety exists for CPM/IMA; PKI revocation and SCMS timing differ.
