# Intrusion Detection System for Platooning Connected Autonomous Vehicles
Dimitrios Kosmanos, Apostolos Pappas, Francisco J. Aparicio-Navarro, Leandros Maglaras, Helge Janicke, Eerke Boiten et al. · 2019 4th South-East Europe Design Automation, Computer Engineering, Computer Networks and Social Media Conference (SEEDA-CECNSM) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/seeda-cecnsm.2019.8908528 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Spoofing and jamming against platooning CAVs.
**Approach:** ML IDS (RF, k-NN, OCSVM) with cross-layer data fusion.
**Tools and scale:** Platoon simulation; details not in abstract.
**Results:** Accuracy over 90% including unknown attacks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — cross-layer detection of jamming/spoofing.
**Novelty implications:** Cross-layer ML IDS for platoons claimed first in 2019; cannot claim first cross-layer detection.
