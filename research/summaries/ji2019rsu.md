Wen-Jiang Ji, Lei Zhu, Yi-Chuan Wang, Zheng Liu et al. · Journal of Intelligent & Fuzzy Systems 2019 · https://doi.org/10.3233/jifs-179286 · relevance: high · basis: abstract
