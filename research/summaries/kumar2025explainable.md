# Explainable Federated Framework for Enhanced Security and Privacy in Connected Vehicles Against Advanced Persistent Threats
G. K. Sudhina Kumar, Krishna Prakash, Balachandra Muniyal, Muttukrishnan Rajarajan · IEEE Open Journal of Vehicular Technology 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ojvt.2025.3576366 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The increasing adoption of autonomous and intelligent vehicles within ground transportation systems faces new security challenges. This shift from human-controlled operations opens up a broader attack surface for malicious players.
**Approach:** Furthermore, the absence of publicly available APT datasets in the vehicular domain complicates model development and validation, creating a significant gap in cybersecurity capabilities for this evolving vehicular domain. This research proposes a novel Federated Deep Neural Network (FDNN) framework with a privacy-preserving technique to address these concerns.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting (VANET/IoV/V2X). Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** For the mentioned datasets, the framework without privacy-preserving technique shows high APT detection accuracies of 97.32%, 96.81% and 98.06%, respectively. However, with the privacy-preserving technique, the framework shows 95.62%, 96.11% and 95.63% accuracies, respectively.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "The potential of the developed framework to detect the APTs in the cross-domain is discussed in future works."
**Overlap with our engine:** partial: a V2X authentication/privacy scheme; our engine implements the standard SCMS/IEEE 1609.2 path rather than this protocol, and the paper reports no closed-loop traffic, radio or revocation simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Rules out nothing specific in our candidate directions. It shows that lightweight V2X authentication protocols are widely proposed; we should not claim a new authentication scheme.
