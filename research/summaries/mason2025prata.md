# PRATA: A Framework to Enable Predictive QoS in Vehicular Networks via Artificial Intelligence
Federico Mason, Tommaso Zugno, Matteo Drago, Marco Giordani, Mate Boban, Michele Zorzi · IEEE Transactions on Communications 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tcomm.2025.3591175 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** PQoS solutions are evaluated offline, ignoring the feedback of adaptation on future QoS.
**Approach:** PRATA: 5G RAN end-to-end stack, automotive data generator and RL RAN-AI deciding segmentation level for teleoperated driving.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3 based 5G RAN simulation.
**Results:** RAN-AI almost doubles system performance vs baselines.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Most prior PQoS works offline and ignore feedback effect of service adaptation.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - closed-loop evaluation argument (adaptation affects future state), analogous to our closed-loop MBD argument but for QoS.
**Novelty implications:** The closed-loop vs offline argument is made for PQoS; for MBD/revocation it remains ours to make.
