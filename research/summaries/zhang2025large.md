# Large AI Model-Enabled Secure Communications in Low-Altitude Wireless Networks: Concepts, Perspectives and Case Study
Chuang Zhang, Geng Sun, Yijing Lin, Weijie Yuan, Sinem Coleri, Dusit Niyato · arXiv 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mcom.001.2500492 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Low-altitude wireless networks (LAWNs) have the potential to revolutionize communications by supporting a range of applications, including urban parcel delivery, aerial inspections and air taxis.
**Approach:** However, compared with traditional wireless networks, LAWNs face unique security challenges due to low-altitude operations, frequent mobility and reliance on unlicensed spectrum, making it more vulnerable to some malicious attacks. In this article, we investigate some large artificial intelligence model (LAM)-enabled solutions for secure communications in LAWNs.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: simulation.
**Results:** This approach improves reinforcement learning performance for secure communication tasks. Through a typical case study, simulation results validate the effectiveness of the proposed framework.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Specifically, we first explore the amplified security risks and important limitations of traditional AI methods in LAWNs.
- Finally, we outline future directions for integrating LAMs into secure LAWN applications.
