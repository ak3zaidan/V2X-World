# Real‐Time Emulation of Nonstationary Channels in Safety‐Relevant Vehicular Scenarios
Golsa Ghiaasi, Thomas Blazek, Mehdi Ashury, Rute Ramalho Santos, Christoph Mecklenbräuker · Wireless Communications and Mobile Computing 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2018/2423837 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Real-time emulation of nonstationary vehicular channels for modem testing.
**Approach:** Hardware-efficient real-time channel emulator architecture updating delay-Doppler models per stationarity region; benchmarks COTS and SDR 802.11p modems.
**Tools and scale:** LabVIEW, NI USRP-RIO 2953R; DRIVEWAY'09 nonstationary model and ETSI models.
**Results:** PER (temporal) and PEP (ensemble) compared for the same scenario; modem PER compared for nonstationary vs stationary ETSI models (no numbers in abstract).
