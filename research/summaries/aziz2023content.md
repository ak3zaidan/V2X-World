# Content-Aware Network Traffic Prediction Framework for Quality of Service-Aware Dynamic Network Resource Management
Waqar Ali Aziz, Iacovos I. Ioannou, Marios Lestas, Hassaan Khaliq Qureshi, Adnan Iqbal, Vasos Vassiliou · IEEE Access 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2023.3309002 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Next-generation mobile networks, such as Fifth-Generation (5G), and Sixth-Generation (6G) are envisioned to undergo an unprecedented transformation from connected things to connected intelligence with more stringent characteristics, i.e., low end-to-end latency, high bandwidth, reliable connectivity, etc.
**Approach:** Such networks will significantly increase network traffic in the distribution networks, causing the need for real-time automated decision-making, such as automated network resource allocation. The network resources be allocated dynamically based on the Quality of Service (QoS) requirements.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G.
**Results:** The results show that the fusion of RNN-BLSTM can predict the QoS-aware network traffic for over 13 hours with an average accuracy of 97.68%.
