# Secure and Lightweight Conditional Privacy-Preserving Authentication for Securing Traffic Emergency Messages in VANETs
Lu Wei, Jie Cui, Yan Xu, Jiujun Cheng et al. · IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tifs.2020.3040876 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Owing to the development of wireless communication technology and the increasing number of automobiles, vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) have become essential tools to secure traffic safety and enhance driving convenience. **Approach:** To solve the first issue, we propose a CPPA signature scheme based on elliptic curve cryptography, which can achieve message recovery and be reduced to elliptic curve discrete logarithm assumption, so that traffic emergency messages are secured with ultra-low communication overhead.

**Results:** Formal security proof and analysis show that our proposed scheme satisfies the security and privacy requirements of VANETs. Performance analysis demonstrates that our proposed scheme requires less storage size and has a lower transmission delay compared with related schemes.
