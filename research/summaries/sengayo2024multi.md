# Multi-environment automotive QOS prediction using Machine Learning for Vehicle-to-Anything (V2X) communications
Ndabuye Sengayo, Thomas Basikolo · ITU Journal on Future and Evolving Technologies 2024 · https://doi.org/10.52953/jfck2327 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Cross-MNO V2X QoS prediction.
**Approach:** LeMLP: LightGBM feature selection plus MLP; train on one MNO, test on another.
**Tools and scale:** Berlin V2X dataset.
**Results:** R^2 0.9447 with six features.
