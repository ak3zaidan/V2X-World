# Modeling and analyzing cyberattack effects on connected automated vehicular platoons
Pengcheng Wang, Xinkai Wu, Xiaozheng He · Transportation Research Part C: Emerging Technologies 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trc.2020.102625 · relevance: high · basis: abstract
