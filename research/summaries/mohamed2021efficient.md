# Efficient VANET safety message delivery and authenticity with privacy preservation
Taha M. Mohamed, Islam Z. Ahmed, Rowayda A. Sadek · PeerJ Computer Science 2021 · https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj-cs.519 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs) play an essential role in the development of the intelligent transportation system (ITS).
**Approach:** We propose an efficient message authentication system with a privacy preservation protocol. The proposed protocol consists of three main phases: the group registration phase, send/receive messages phase, and the leave/join phase.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** This protocol reduces the overall communication and computational overheads. The experimental results showed that the computational overhead in the registration phase was significantly reduced by 91.7%.
