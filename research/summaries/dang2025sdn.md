# SDN-Based False Data Detection With Its Mitigation and Machine Learning Robustness for In-Vehicle Networks
Long Dang, Thushari Hapuarachchi, Kaiqi Xiong, Yi Li · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06556 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** False data injection on in-vehicle CAN brake ECUs.
**Approach:** SDN-based detection with LSTM; adversarial robustness via DeepFool variant and adversarial training.
**Tools and scale:** 1,894-instance CAN dataset.
**Results:** LSTM detection optimised with RMSProp/Adagrad/Adam; adversarial training improves robustness to FGSM/BIM.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Develop more sophisticated attack models and better defences against adversarial attacks.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — in-vehicle network.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
