# V2X-LLM: Enhancing V2X Integration and Understanding in Connected Vehicle Corridors
Keshu Wu, Pei Li, Yang Zhou, Rui Gan, Junwei You, Yang Cheng et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02239 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Understanding and real-time analysis of BSM and SPaT data on connected-vehicle corridors is hard.
**Approach:** V2X-LLM: LLM layer on a CV data pipeline for scenario explanation, V2X data description, state prediction and navigation advisory.
**Tools and scale:** Real connected vehicle corridor (Park St, six RSU intersections, ~1.08 miles).
**Results:** Qualitative examples of explanations (numbers not extracted).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in extracted sections.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - LLM analysing V2X message data (BSM/SPaT), related to our LLM analysis agent; no security or simulation ground truth.
**Novelty implications:** LLM interpretation of V2X data exists; LLM root-cause analysis over cross-layer V2X security ground truth remains open.
