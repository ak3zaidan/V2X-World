# Communication-Efficient Conditional Privacy-Preserving Authentication Based on Ring Signature in VANETs
Ming Yang, Xinlin Zhang, Hang Liu, Chen-Hao Wang et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2025.3610447 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the privacy and security problems in mind, conditional privacy-preserving authentication (CPPA) schemes are introduced.
**Approach:** To solve these challenges, this paper constructs a communication-efficient CPPA (CE-CPPA) scheme based on ring signature in VANETs.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** However, existing efforts still have some issues regarding practicality, efficiency, and security. With the transformation of the signature format and employment of the non-interactive sum argument technique, a logarithmic-size signature is realized by CE-CPPA, improving the communication efficiency.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
