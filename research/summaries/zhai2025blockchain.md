# A Blockchain-based Federated Learning Framework with Knowledge Graphs for Smart Supply Chain Finance: Integrating Risk Pricing, Governance, and Secure Computation
Wen-Tian Zhai, Shidan Fu, Zebin Lu, Yue Chang · Proceedings of the 2025 6th International Conference on Big Data Economy and Information Management 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3800000.3800115 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Supply chain finance (SCF) faces significant challenges in multi-party data integration due to privacy constraints, data silos, and complex inter-enterprise relationships. Existing federated learning (FL) approaches often fail to model relational data and are vulnerable to non-IID data distributions.
**Approach:** To address these issues, this paper proposes FederatedChain-SCF, a novel framework that integrates blockchain, dynamic knowledge graphs (KGs), meta-learning, and secure multi-party computation (SMPC).
**Tools and scale:** Experiments on a real-world automotive SCF dataset show that our method achieves an AUC of 0.943, reduces the false negative rate by 31.5% compared to conventional FL, and maintains strong privacy (ε < 1.8) and communication efficiency (68% reduction).
