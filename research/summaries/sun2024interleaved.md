# Interleaved One-Shot SPS Performance under Smart DoS Attacks in C-V2X Networks
Zepei Sun, Randall Berry · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.00352 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Robustness of C-V2X SPS under smart DoS (jamming of reserved resources).
**Approach:** Evaluates interleaved one-shot transmissions within SPS under smart attackers.
**Tools and scale:** Python Monte Carlo C-V2X Mode 4 model.
**Results:** One-shot improves PDR and reduces IPG and AoI tails; effect diminishes at high density; attack interval matters modestly.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Effectiveness diminishes in high-density environments.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — C-V2X MAC attacks; our engine has LTE-V2X SPS and attacks in the loop.
**Novelty implications:** Smart SPS attacks are studied; combining them with verification load and safety outcome remains open.
