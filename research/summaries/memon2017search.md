# Search me if you can: Multiple mix zones with location privacy protection for mapping services
Imran Memon, Qasim Ali Arain, Muhammad Hammad Memon, Farman Ali Mangi et al. · International Journal of Communication Systems 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1002/dac.3312 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Summary The mobile vehicle is gaining popularity nowadays using map services like Google Maps and other mapping services.
**Approach:** However, map services users have to expose sensitive information like geographic locations (GPS coordinates) or address to personal privacy concerns as users share their locations and queries to obtain desired services. Existing mix zones location privacy protection methods are most general purposed and theoretical value while not applicable when applied to provide location privacy for map service users.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: pseudonym change/privacy).
**Results:** This method enables mobile vehicle users to query a route between 2 endpoints on the map, without revealing any confidential location and queries information.
