# A V2V Identity Authentication and Key Agreement Scheme Based on Identity-Based Cryptograph
Qiang Li · Future Internet 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/fi15010025 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cellular vehicle to everything (C-V2X) is a technology to achieve vehicle networking, which can improve traffic efficiency and traffic safety.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a V2V identity authentication and key agreement scheme based on identity-based cryptograph (IBC).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Cellular vehicle to everything (C-V2X) is a technology to achieve vehicle networking, which can improve traffic efficiency and traffic safety.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- The C-V2X equipment transmit secret data encrypted with the opposite equipment public key to the other equipment, they authenticate each other through a challenge response protocol based on identity-based cryptography, and they negotiate the working key used to encrypt the communication data.
