# Attacks on Self-Driving Cars and Their Countermeasures: A Survey
Abdullahi Chowdhury, Gour Karmakar, Joarder Kamruzzaman, Alireza Jolfaei et al. · IEEE Access 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2020.3037705 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Intelligent Traffic Systems (ITS) are currently evolving in the form of a cooperative ITS or connected vehicles. Both forms use the data communications between Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V), Vehicle-to-Infrastructure (V2I/I2V) and other on-road entities, and are accelerating the adoption of self-driving cars.
**Approach:** To bridge this research gap, in this paper, we analyze the attacks that already targeted self-driving cars and extensively present potential cyber-attacks and their impacts on those cars along with their vulnerabilities.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** As a result, self-driving cars are being compromised perceived as a serious threat. Therefore, analyzing the threats and attacks on self-driving cars and ITSs, and their corresponding countermeasures to reduce those threats and attacks are needed.
