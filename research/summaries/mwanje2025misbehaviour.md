# Misbehaviour Prediction in CAV Network using Aggregated Trust Analysis
Maria Drolence Mwanje, Omprakash Kaiwartya, Devki Nandan Jha, Ahmad M. Khasawneh, Ali Safaa Sadiq, Yue Cao · International Journal of Intelligent Transportation Systems Research 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s13177-025-00528-2 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** False position attacks.
**Approach:** Mamdani fuzzy aggregated trust on OBU with three features.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi Extension; DT, KNN, LR, SVM.
**Results:** Good for constant, random and eventual stop; weak on offset attacks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Feature overlap under random/constant offset.
- post-processing weights proposed.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — Offline.
**Novelty implications:** None.
