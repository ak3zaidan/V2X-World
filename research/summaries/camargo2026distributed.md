# A Distributed and Adaptive Failure Detector for Vehicular Networks based on Cooperative Awareness Messages
Edson  Tavares de Camargo, Antônio  Augusto Fröhlich · Unknown venue 2026 · https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7442010 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cooperative Intelligent Transportation Systems (C-ITS) rely on continuous coordination among vehicles, but operate over unreliable wireless channels and face node crashes and security threats.
**Approach:** A failure detector (FD) is a foundational service that identifies whether a node or communication link has crashed or stopped responding. In C-ITS, failure detectors are essential for supporting distributed applications because of the inherent need for coordination among different ITS stations.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed failure detector across different wireless propagation models and under message-content fault injection.
