# Clustered Vehicular Federated Learning: Process and Optimization
Afaf Taik, Zoubeir Mlika, Soumaya Cherkaoui · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2022.3149860 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Federated Learning (FL) is expected to play a prominent role for privacy-preserving machine learning (ML) in autonomous vehicles.
**Approach:** FL involves the collaborative training of a single ML model among edge devices on their distributed datasets while keeping data locally. While FL requires less communication compared to classical distributed learning, it remains hard to scale for large models.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: simulations.
**Results:** We show through simulations that the proposed process is capable of improving the learning accuracy in several non-independent and-identically-distributed (non-i.i.d) and unbalanced datasets distributions, under mobility constraints, in comparison to standard FL.
