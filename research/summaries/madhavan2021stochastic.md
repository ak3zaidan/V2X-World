# Stochastic Formulation of Causal Digital Twin: Kalman Filter Algorithm
PG Madhavan · arXiv 2021 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.05236 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** We provide some basic and sensible definitions of different types of digital twins and recommendations on when and how to use them.
**Approach:** Structural Vector Autoregressive Model (SVAR) for Causal estimation is recast as a state-space model.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The previous neural network algorithm and Kalman Smoother produced very similar results; however, Kalman Filter/Smoother may show better performance for noisy data from industrial IoT sources.
