# Secure Platooning Control for Connected Vehicles Subject to Hybrid Stochastic Cyber-Attacks
Xiu-Lan Song, Gaojian Zhou, De-Feng He, Hai-Ping Du, Yong-Fu Li · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2026.3652985 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle platooning systems have received widespread attention for their efficiency and safety.
**Approach:** However, cybersecurity concerns on cyber-attack threats, are becoming increasingly prominent in automotive community. Given the stochastic nature of cyber-attacks, a platoon may be suffered from multiple attacks in temporal and spatial perspectives.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Using the Lyapunov’s arguments, the conditions for achieving the stability performance are established, and the controller’s gains are dynamically adjusted to respond to the state variations of the vehicles under the cyber-attacks.
