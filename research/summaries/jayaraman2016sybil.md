# Sybil Attack Detection in Vehicular Networks
Bharat Jayaraman, Jinesh Kannimoola, Krishnashree Achuthan · Security and Privacy in Internet of Things (IoTs) 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1201/b19516-5 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil attacks forging multiple vehicle IDs.
**Approach:** Prevention method using cryptography, fixed RSUs and a central authority; formal Promela model verified with SPIN.
**Tools and scale:** SPIN model checker.
**Results:** Safety property verified.
