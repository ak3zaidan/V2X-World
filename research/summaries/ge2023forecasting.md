# Forecasting SDN End-to-End Latency Using Graph Neural Network
Zhun Ge, Jiacheng Hou, Amiya Nayak · 2023 International Conference on Information Networking (ICOIN) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icoin56518.2023.10048915 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** SDN end-to-end delay prediction.
**Approach:** STGCN vs MLR, XGBoost, RF.
**Tools and scale:** SDN dataset.
**Results:** RMSE 97.0%, 95.9%, 96.1% and 63.1% lower than baseline, MLR, XGBoost and RF.
