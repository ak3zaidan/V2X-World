# V2AIX: A Multi-Modal Real-World Dataset of ETSI ITS V2X Messages in Public Road Traffic
Guido Kueppers, Jean-Pierre Busch, Lennart Reiher, Lutz Eckstein · 2024 IEEE 27th International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/itsc58415.2024.10920150 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Lack of real-world datasets of ETSI ITS V2X messages.
**Approach:** V2AIX: multi-modal dataset of ETSI ITS-G5 messages captured in public road traffic (stationary and mobile), with context images.
**Tools and scale:** Real ITS-G5 captures in urban and highway settings.
**Results:** CAMs with seven unique vehicle-dimension pairs; matched to VW Group models (ID.3, Golf 8, etc.), showing that vehicle models can be identified from CAM content.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Some drivers may have deactivated V2X
- only VW Group vehicles equipped at the time.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — partial: real V2X message data; also evidence of linkability via static CAM fields (relevant to cross-layer linkability).
**Novelty implications:** Real-world linkability hint via vehicle dimensions is documented; no PKI ground truth.
