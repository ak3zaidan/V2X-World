# Physics-Based Misbehavior Detection System for V2X
 Communications
Alejandro Antonio Andrade Salazar, P. McDaniel, Ryan Sheatsley, J. Petit · SAE International Journal of Connected and Automated Vehicles 2022 · https://doi.org/10.4271/12-05-03-0020 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** V2X messages are authenticated but content not validated.
**Approach:** VCADS physics-based models constraining BSM fields, detecting anomalies by comparing a field with its derivation from orthogonal fields.
**Tools and scale:** Four real V2X field-testing datasets plus attack simulations.
**Results:** Detects about 85% to 95% of attacks perturbing one or more fields.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — physics-based BSM plausibility on real US data.
**Novelty implications:** Physics-based BSM checks with real data exist.
