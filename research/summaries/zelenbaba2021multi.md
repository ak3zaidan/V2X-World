# Multi-Node Vehicular Wireless Channels: Measurements, Large Vehicle Modeling, and Hardware-in-the-Loop Evaluation
Stefan Zelenbaba, Benjamin Rainer, Markus Hofer, David Loschenbrand, Anja Dakic, Laura Bernado et al. · IEEE Access 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2021.3100676 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Multi-node vehicular channels with large obstructing vehicles are poorly characterised.
**Approach:** First fully mobile multi-node channel sounder; measurements with a double-decker bus; GSCM parameterised for large obstructing vehicle; HIL validation with commercial modems via channel emulator.
**Tools and scale:** AIT multi-node sounder, GSCM from OpenStreetMap, channel emulator, commercial modems.
**Results:** Path loss, RMS delay spread and RMS Doppler spread deviate by less than 3.6 dB, 78 ns and 52 Hz for 80% of duration; PER from measured data within model bounds for 86% of duration; bus raises delay spread by >200 ns and Doppler spread by >100 Hz.
