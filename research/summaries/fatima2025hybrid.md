# Hybrid Machine Learning Framework for Intelligent Attack Detection in VANET Environments
Sabiya Fatima, Pratima Upadhyay, Prabhat Sharma, Sanjay Kumar Khadagade, Saket Bihari, Rovin Tiwari · 2025 International Conference on Data, Energy and Communication Networks (DECoN) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/decon67170.2025.11448093 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) are proving to be one of the main elements of the intelligent transportation system that allow vehicles and infrastructure to communicate with each other in real-time.
**Approach:** Nevertheless, due to dynamic topology and open wireless medium, they are very vulnerable to different security threats such as denial-of-service, Sybil, and black-hole attacks. In order to overcome these problems, this paper will propose Hybrid Machine Learning Framework that combines Random Forest (RF) and Deep Neural Network (DNN) to be used as intelligent and adaptive attack detection methods in VANET situations.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** This two-step system helps in improving accuracy of detecting, minimizes false positives, and avoids overloading computation which is unreasonable to operates in real time VANET. The experimental testing of the benchmark VANET datasets will show that the proposed hybrid RF-DNN framework achieve greater accuracy, recall, F1-score and resistance to changing patterns of attacks compared to the traditional case of use when single RF-ready models are employed.
