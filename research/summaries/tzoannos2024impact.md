# The Impact of Spoofing Attacks in Connected Autonomous Vehicles under Traffic Congestion Conditions
Zisis-Rafail Tzoannos, Dimitrios Kosmanos, A. Xenakis, C. Chaikalis · Telecom 2024 · https://doi.org/10.3390/telecom5030037 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In recent years, the Internet of Things (IoT) and the Internet of Vehicles (IoV) represent rapidly developing technologies. The majority of car manufacturing companies invest large amounts of money in the field of connected autonomous vehicles.
**Approach:** In essence, this paper presents an in-depth analysis of spoofing attack impacts under realistic road conditions, which may cause some traffic congestion. The novelty of this work has to do with simulation scenarios that take into consideration a set of cross-layer parameters, such as packet delivery ratio (PDR), acceleration, and speed.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** Finally, a statistical metric, coefficient of variation (CoV), which measures the consequences of a cyber-attack in a future crash, is estimated, showing a significant increase (12.1%) in a spoofing attack scenario.
