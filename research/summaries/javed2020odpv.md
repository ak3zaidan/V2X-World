# ODPV: An Efficient Protocol to Mitigate Data Integrity Attacks in Intelligent Transport Systems
Muhammad Awais Javed, Mohammad Zubair Khan, Usman Zafar, Muhammad Faisal Siddiqui, Rabiah Badar, Byung Moo Lee, Farhan Ahmad · IEEE Access 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2020.3004444 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Intelligent Transport Systems (ITS) require accurate information to be shared among vehicles and infrastructure nodes for applications including accident information or pre-crash warnings, to name a few. Due to its sensitive nature, ITS applications are vulnerable against data integrity attacks where nodes transmit false information that results in wrong decision making by the applications.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose an Outlier Detection, Prioritization and Verification (ODPV) protocol that efficiently isolates false data and improves traffic management decisions.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Extensive simulation results verify the effectiveness of the proposed protocol to isolate the outliers.
