# GDVAN: A New Greedy Behavior Attack Detection Algorithm for VANETs
Mohamed Nidhal Mejri, Jalel Ben‐Othman · IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tmc.2016.2577035 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANETs), whose main objective is to provide road safety and enhance the driving conditions, are exposed to several kinds of attacks such as Denial of Service (DoS) attacks which affect the availability of the underlying services for legitimate users. We focus especially on the greedy behavior which has been extensively addressed in the literature for Wireless LAN (WLAN) 
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a new detection approach called GDVAN (Greedy Detection for VANETs) for greedy behavior attacks in VANETs.
