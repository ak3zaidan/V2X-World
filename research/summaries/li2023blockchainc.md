# Blockchain-based Reputation Management Scheme for the Internet of Vehicles
Shijie Li, Xiaowu Liu, Wenshuo Ma, Kan Yu, Yangyang Liu · 2023 IEEE International Conference on Data Mining Workshops (ICDMW) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icdmw60847.2023.00137 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the development of 5G communication technology, the Internet of Vehicles (IoV) is ushering in a series of changes and improvements.
**Approach:** Facilitated by the exchange of information amongst vehicles, IoV stands poised to revolutionize road utilization and enhance the overall travel experience. Some researchers try to encourage vehicles to actively report honest information through vehicle reputation management, but they do not consider the intelligence and selfishness of vehicles, and these solutions cannot meet the high reliability and low latency requirements of IoV.
