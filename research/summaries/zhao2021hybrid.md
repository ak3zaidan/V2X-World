# A Hybrid-Equivalent Surface-Edge Current Model for Simulation of V2X Communication Antennas With Arbitrarily Shaped Contour
Huapeng Zhao, Xinhui Zhang, Jun Hu, Zhizhang David Chen, Ying-Chang Liang · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2020.3042336 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Existing equivalent antenna models for fast V2X simulation assume rectangular antenna contours.
**Approach:** Hybrid equivalent surface-edge current model (surface magnetic current, its image, edge current) solved from near fields, used to compute vehicle-mounted radiation and antenna coupling.
**Tools and scale:** Electromagnetic simulation plus measurements; antenna level only.
**Results:** Accurately models arbitrarily shaped antennas and significantly accelerates antenna-vehicle simulation (no numbers in abstract).
