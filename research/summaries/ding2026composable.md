Shenjia Ding, David Flynn, Paul Harvey, Takamichi Miyata, Sumiko Miyata · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18704 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Monolithic ML NDTs lack reusability.
**Approach:** Composable NDT from reusable subgraph unit twins and a route-level composer.
**Tools and scale:** Synthetic topologies, Topology Zoo, GNNet dataset.
**Results:** High in-distribution accuracy and stability out of distribution.
