# A Software-Defined Radio Testbed for Distributed LiDAR Point Cloud Sharing with IEEE 802.11p in V2V Networks
Mario Hernandez, Elijah Bryce, Peter Stubberud, Ebrahim Saberinia, Brendan Morris · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14523 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Bridging simulation and deployment for V2V LiDAR sharing.
**Approach:** ADALM-Pluto SDR 802.11p testbed with Docker/ROS/Matlab; IPFS/Filecoin storage model; channel study.
**Tools and scale:** Pluto SDRs.
**Results:** Mean BER 0.08 to 0.19 across modes; timing sync issues.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- 9-week implementation limited timing optimisation
- future: networking layer/timing sync, MAC for LiDAR volumes, RSU/edge architectures, storage optimisation.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
