# Modelling Safety of Connected and Autonomous Vehicles (CAVs) under Cyber-Attacks on Perception and Safety Monitors
Aria Kosari, Peter Popov, Rajkumar Roy · 2022 12th International Conference on Dependable Systems, Services and Technologies (DESSERT) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/dessert58054.2022.10018781 · relevance: medium · basis: full text

**Problem:** Safety assessment of CAVs under cyber-attacks on perception and safety monitors.
**Approach:** Probabilistic model extended with a generic cyber-attack that compromises components ("vulnerability modes").
**Tools and scale:** Analytical probabilistic model; no network simulator.
**Results:** Safety impact varies greatly across vulnerability modes; combined exploitation can be non-linear and dramatic even when single impacts are negligible.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Informal safety analysis used to identify vulnerability modes
- quantitative analysis suggested to guide designers.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — none: no V2X or PKI; related only to quantified risk ratings.
**Novelty implications:** Quantitative safety-under-attack modelling exists analytically; simulation-backed numbers remain open.
