# Segregation of Sybil Attack using Neighbouring Information in VANET
Anu Panchal, Dr. Dinesh Singh · IARJSET 2017 · https://doi.org/10.17148/iarjset.2017.4631 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Sybil with stolen IDs.
**Approach:** Neighbour-based trust (NBH).
**Tools and scale:** SUMO + NS2, 18 nodes, 1000x1000 m, AODV.
**Results:** Increased detection (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Very small scale (18 nodes).
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
