# Enhanced Machine Learning Framework for DDoS Attack Endurance in Vehicles Security for 5G Networks
Sharma Ji, Abhishek Kumar Mishra · International Conference on Communication, Security and Artificial Intelligence (ICCSAI) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccsai64074.2025.11063900 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Over the last few years, security has emerged as an issue of great concern in vehicular networks (VANETs).
**Approach:** Attacks classified as Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) and Denial of Service (DoS) are some major security threats. These attacks have the potential to destroy services by bombarding systems with excessive traffic.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** This study focuses on improving the security of vehicular networks by addressing threats arising from DoS and DDoS attacks. Together, these models achieve an impressive detection accuracy of 0.99, outperforming conventional methods.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- This research provides a robust solution to enhance vehicular network protection, ensuring more secure communication in connected vehicles, and establishes a foundation for future progress in safeguarding vehicular systems against cyber threats.
