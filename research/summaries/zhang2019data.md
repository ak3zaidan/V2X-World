# Data Security Sharing and Storage Based on a Consortium Blockchain in a Vehicular Ad-hoc Network
Xiaohong Zhang, Xiaofeng Chen · IEEE Access 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2018.2890736 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A vehicular ad-hoc network (VANET) can improve the flow of traffic to facilitate intelligent transportation and to provide convenient information services, where the goal is to provide self-organizing data transmission capabilities for vehicles on the road to enable applications, such as assisted vehicle driving and safety warnings.
**Approach:** To address these security challenges, we propose a data security sharing and storage system based on the consortium blockchain (DSSCB).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** A vehicular ad-hoc network (VANET) can improve the flow of traffic to facilitate intelligent transportation and to provide convenient information services, where the goal is to provide self-organizing data transmission capabilities for vehicles on the road to enable applications, such as assisted vehicle driving and safety warnings. Compared with the traditional blockchain system, the time required to confirm the data block was reduced by nearly six times and the transmission efficiency was improved by 83.33%.
