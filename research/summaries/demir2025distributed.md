# Distributed Federated Learning for Vehicular Network Security: Anomaly Detection Benefits and Multi-Domain Attack Threats
Utku Demir, Yalin E. Sagduyu, Tugba Erpek, Hossein Jafari, Sastry Kompella, Mengran Xue · WiseML@WiSec 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3733965.3733967 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** Centralised or local-only ML MBD is limited by scale and heterogeneity.
**Approach:** Distributed federated learning among one-hop neighbours; studies jamming and poisoning against DFL.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi Extension dataset.
**Results:** DFL improves accuracy over local-only; poisoning more harmful than jamming; joint attacks lower accuracy further.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: targeted poisoning, backdoor attacks, defence mechanisms.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Cross-domain attacks (jamming plus ML poisoning).
**Novelty implications:** Uses offline dataset; on-air DFL in a full stack remains open.
