# Nested Hash Access With Post Quantum Encryption for Mission-Critical IoT Communications
Dongyang Xu, Lei Liu, N. Zhang, Mian-Xiong Dong et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2023.3245360 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Secure ultrareliable low-latency communication (URLLC) has become a crucial requirement of mission-critical Internet of Things (IoT) applications and use cases, including automotive driving, remote surgery, and many others. However, it is still challenging to protect initial access of massive IoT devices over wireless channels, especially when malicious ...
**Approach:** We propose a nested hash access system with post-quantum encryption to solve this issue.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation results show the effectiveness of our proposed scheme despite attack.
**Results:** We show that the code information can be maintained at 128-bit or higher privacy level, depending on the length of repetition code.
