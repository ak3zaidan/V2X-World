# PIER: cyber-resilient risk assessment model for connected and autonomous vehicles
Seunghyun Park, Hyunhee Park · Wireless Networks 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s11276-022-03084-9 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract As more vehicles are being connected to the Internet and equipped with autonomous driving features, more robust safety and security measures are required for connected and autonomous vehicles (CAVs).
**Approach:** Therefore, threat analysis and risk assessment are essential to prepare against cybersecurity risks for CAVs. Although prior studies have measured the possibility of attack and damage from attack as risk assessment indices, they have not analyzed the expanding attack surface or risk assessment indices that rely upon real-time resilience.
