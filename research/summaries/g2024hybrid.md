# A hybrid CNN-GRU-based intrusion detection system for secure communication in vehicular adhoc network
K. G., P. E. · Information Security Journal 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1080/19393555.2024.2361244 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ABSTRACT Vehicular Ad hoc Network (VANET) is a component of the Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) which furnishes communication among vehicles.
**Approach:** It delivers comfort and safety information to passengers and vehicle drivers. Security plays a vital role during the transmission of data as the various distinct security attacks directly influence the safety of the passengers on the road.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The results show that the propounded model outperforms the other traditional detection mechanisms by 10.79% in terms of performance compared to other IDS.

- However, there are several demurrers in detecting abnormal activities efficiently as the traditional system faces imbalanced data problems.
