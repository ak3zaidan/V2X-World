# Enhanced Security Framework for Gray-Hole Attack Prevention in Vehicular Networks

**Problem:** The gray-hole attacks are certainly destructive to the integrity and reliability of vehicular ad-hoc networks where a malicious node will drop packets selectively while keeping the instance innocuous.
**Approach:** Commercial and Safety Applications Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks and IoT of Vehicles, ITS, and V2X VANETs nowadays are advancing fast in tune with the commercial and safety applications. Nonetheless, despite offering plenty of possible advantages, VANETs cowl an openly-distributed-dynamic nature that raises their exposure to different classes of attacks and threats, one of those being the gray-hole attack (GHA)_.
