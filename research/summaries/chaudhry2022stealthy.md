# Stealthy Data Corruption Attack Against Road Traffic Congestion Avoidance Applications
Aawista Chaudhry, Talal Halabi, Mohammad Zulkernine · 2022 52nd Annual IEEE/IFIP International Conference on Dependable Systems and Networks Workshops (DSN-W) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/dsn-w54100.2022.00011 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) leverage open and real-time sharing of traffic data to enable more efficient transportation.
**Approach:** Misbehaviour detectors have been extensively developed but remain siloed and lack consideration of advanced attacks amalgamating multiple misbehaviours. This paper presents a new approach to specifically designing stealthy data corruption attacks within ITS, and by extension in other data-reliant Cyber-Physical Systems.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The new stealthy attack achieves the intended long-term impact while improving evasion performance.
