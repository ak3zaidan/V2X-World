# Missing Feedback Loop And Disjointed Organizational Structure: Barriers To Quality Certification Of Vehicular Emissions
Om Parkash Kapoor, Dr. Anil Chandokh, Dr. Navdeep Sharma Dugala · International Journal of Environmental Science 2025 · https://doi.org/10.64252/bwc5ez98 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Furthermore, regulatory enforcement is observed to be reactive, contributing to inconsistent certification standards and limited corrective actions The absence of a structured feedback system prevents timely corrective and preventive actions (CAPA), while weak enforcement of SOPs results in procedural inconsistencies and poor data integrity.
**Approach:** This paper investigates the systematic causes and thereupon comes to identifying the absence of a (1) structured feedback loop and (ii) organization structure unclear of relative authority levels, roles & responsibilities and reporting lines, which are the means to manage across it. Through this study, we emphasize that formalized feedback loop-integrating real time PUC data, audit findings and stakeholder’s inputs- are essential to drive PUC towards continual improvement in emission certification.
