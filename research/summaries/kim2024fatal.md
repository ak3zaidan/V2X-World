# Fatal C-V2X denial-of-service attack degrading quality of service in a highway scenario
Kyungtae Kim, Dongyoon Kwon, Woo-Cheol Jin, Sinuk Choi, Jin‐Mo Kim, Ji-Woong Choi · Journal of Communications and Networks 2024 · https://doi.org/10.23919/jcn.2023.000066 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A denial-of-service (DoS) attack, which prevents other nodes from accessing resources, is one of the fatal security threats in the V2X field.
**Approach:** This paper analyzes the DoS attack on C-V2X networks from various viewpoints. First, we derive the conditions for the vulnerable vehicle that are expected to suffer fatal damage when subjected to the DoS attack.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: C-V2X
**Results:** We confirm that the DoS attack that attacks the vulnerable vehicle satisfying the derived conditions causes more damage than the conventional DoS attack provided in another study in terms of reliability, coverage, and timeliness (up to 2% reduction in packet delivery ratio, up to 30 m reduction in communication coverage, and 0.15-second increase in the lower 1% update delay).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- This paper provides insight into DoS attacks on C-V2X network, and future studies will cover the topic of attack detection and defense.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
