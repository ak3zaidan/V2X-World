# Cyber Security Challenges and Solutions for V2X Communications: A Survey
Aljawharah Alnasser, Hongjian Sun, Jing Jiang · Computer Networks 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.comnet.2018.12.018 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In recent years, vehicles became able to establish connections with other vehicles and infrastructure units that are located in the roadside. In the near future, the vehicular network will be expanded to include the communication between vehicles and any smart devices in the roadside which is called Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) communication.
**Approach:** In this paper, we present a comprehensive survey and taxonomy of the existing security solutions for V2X communication technology. Also, we present a threat analysis for V2X enabling technologies.
