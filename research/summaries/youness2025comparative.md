# Comparative Analysis of Recurrent Neural Network Architectures for Real-Time VANET Misbehavior Detection: Performance vs. Efficiency Trade-offs
Nayera Youness, Ahmad Mostafa, Mohammed A. Sobh, Ayman Mohammad Bahaa-Eldin et al. · International Conference on Computer and Applications (ICCA) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icca66035.2025.11430918 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Accuracy versus efficiency of RNN MBD.
**Approach:** Compares LSTM, GRU, Bi-LSTM on 20 attack types.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi Extension, 500k to 2M samples.
**Results:** Bi-LSTM 97.02% (500k), 15.8% slower to train than LSTM; GRU 89.03% on 2M after 282 min; inference under 41 ms.
