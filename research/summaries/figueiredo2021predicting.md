# Predicting Video Bitrate from Encrypted Streaming Traffic in SDN-based 5G Networks with ML
Diego Figueiredo, Rodrigo Dutra, Ilan Sousa et al. · 2021 IEEE Latin-American Conference on Communications (LATINCOM) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/latincom53176.2021.9647813 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Video streaming applications currently dominate mobile network traffic.
**Approach:** This work proposes a method to predict the video bitrate KQI from encrypted video streaming traffic. Furthermore, we evaluate the proposed method in two ML learning cases: offline and incremental, and discuss issues regarding the generalization capability of the ML algorithms.
