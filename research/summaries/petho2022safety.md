# Safety risk focused analysis of V2V communication especially considering cyberattack sensitive network performance and vehicle dynamics factors
Zsombor Pethő, Zsolt Szalay, Árpád Török · Vehicular Communications 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vehcom.2022.100514 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** CCAM safety depends on network performance under cyberattacks.
**Approach:** Framework linking safety risk of V2V applications to latency and PDR and vehicle dynamics; collision severity and message reception probability functions; safety risk index over warning intervals.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical/simulation framework.
**Results:** Safety risk index summed across warning intervals; function parameters should be represented in development.
