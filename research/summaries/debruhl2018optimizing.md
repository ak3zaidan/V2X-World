# Optimizing a MisInformation and MisBehavior (MIB) Attack Targeting Vehicle Platoons
Bruce DeBruhl, Patrick Tague · 2018 IEEE 88th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC-Fall) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtcfall.2018.8690803 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Platoon insider attacks combining misinformation and erratic driving.
**Approach:** MIB attack design; three optimal collision-inducing attacks.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Attacker can cause collision without being damaged.
