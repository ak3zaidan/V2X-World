# FLEXE: Investigating Federated Learning in Connected Autonomous Vehicle Simulations
W. Lobato, Joahannes B. D. da Costa, A. M. de Souza, D. Rosário, Christoph Sommer, L. Villas · IEEE Vehicular Technology Conference 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2022-fall57202.2022.10012905 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Due to the increased computational capacity of Connected and Autonomous Vehicles (CAVs) and worries about transferring private information, it is becoming more and more appealing to store data locally and move network computing to the edge.
**Approach:** This trend also extends to Machine Learning (ML) where Federated learning (FL) has emerged as an attractive solution for preserving privacy. Today, to evaluate the implemented vehicular FL mechanisms for ML training, researchers often disregard the impact of CAV mobility, network topology dynamics, or communication patterns, all of which have a large impact on the final system performance.
