# L-CPPA: Lattice-based conditional privacy-preserving authentication scheme for fog computing with 5G-enabled vehicular system
A. Almazroi, M. Alqarni, Mahmood A. Al-Shareeda, S. Manickam · PLoS ONE 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0292690 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** CPPA schemes vulnerable to quantum attacks in 5G fog vehicular systems.
**Approach:** L-CPPA: lattice CPPA with fog servers distributing keys via 5G-BS.
**Tools and scale:** NTL library; Intel i5-10700.
**Results:** Signing 694.161 ms, single verification 60.118 ms, batch verification 1348.218 ms; communication 7757 bytes.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - PQ authentication cost numbers relevant to verification-load modelling in direction 2.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on SCMS claims.
