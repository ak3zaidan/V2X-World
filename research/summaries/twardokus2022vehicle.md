# Vehicle-to-Nothing? Securing C-V2X Against Protocol-Aware DoS Attacks
Geoff Twardokus, Hanif Rahbari · IEEE Conference on Computer Communications 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/infocom48880.2022.9796667 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** C-V2X PHY/MAC are not resilient to protocol-aware attacks.
**Approach:** Two stealthy DoS exploits against LTE-V2X and NR-V2X scheduling; detection and mitigation.
**Tools and scale:** Hybrid testbed with USRPs and commercial LTE-V2X devices plus extensive simulations.
**Results:** Within seconds, target PDR reduced by 90% or channel throughput by 50%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - protocol-aware sidelink attacks (runner-up direction on NR sidelink attacks); no PKI or traffic-safety loop.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first protocol-aware C-V2X scheduling DoS; combining such attacks with verification load and SCMS remains open.
