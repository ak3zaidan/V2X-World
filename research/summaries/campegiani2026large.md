# Large Scale Actualisation of Post-Quantum Cryptography Migration
P. Campegiani, G. Di Clemente, Rodrigo Lopez Gonzalez, S. Migliaccio et al. · IST-Africa Conferences 2026 · https://doi.org/10.67725/ist-africa.2026.hiiq2631 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The advent of quantum computers poses a fundamental threat to current cryptography-based applications. As relevant guidelines are setting the deadlines for this migration just a few years in the future, it is due time to plan for effective mitigation and migration plans.
**Approach:** In this paper, we analyse how an innovative PKI service delivery model, called “PKI as a Service” (PKIaaS) could be well suited for successfully achieving a secure and effective post-quantum migration. Also, we investigate the performances of some relevant Post Quantum Cryptography (PQC) algorithms when implemented in a PKI setting.
**Tools and scale:** Non-vehicular setting. Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
