# Securing Cloud-Assisted Connected and Autonomous Vehicles: An In-Depth Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment
Al Tariq Sheik, Carsten Maple, Gregory Epiphaniou, Mehrdad Dianati · Sensors 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s24010241 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** TARA methods poorly capture cloud-assisted CAV threats.
**Approach:** STRIDE and DREAD applied to CCAV architectures; attack tree; defence taxonomy.
**Tools and scale:** Threat analysis.
**Results:** Quantified DREAD risks (method demonstration).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Need for future TARA work in the broader CCAV ecosystem
- reference architectures early-stage.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — quantified risk rating (runner-up ISO/SAE 21434), but expert-scored, not simulation-derived.
**Novelty implications:** DREAD-quantified TARA exists; simulation-grounded ratings remain open.
