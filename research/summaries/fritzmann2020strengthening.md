# Strengthening Post-Quantum Security for Automotive Systems
Tim Fritzmann, Jonas Vith, Johanna Sepulveda · 2020 23rd Euromicro Conference on Digital System Design (DSD) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/dsd51259.2020.00094 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** PQC on automotive microcontrollers and timing attacks on error-correcting codes.
**Approach:** ThreeBears implementation with stronger, timing-protected ECC.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
