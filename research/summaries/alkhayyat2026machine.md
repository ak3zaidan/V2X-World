# Machine Learning Benchmarking for V2X Link‑Quality Inference Across Communication Types
Mohammed Al-Khayyat, Mohammed Al-Hubaishi · n/a 2026 · https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-8663769/v1 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Transparent V2X link-quality assessment across communication types.
**Approach:** Consolidated TiHAN-V2X corpus (V2V, V2I, I2V, V2C) with a Vehicle Quality Index; ML benchmark with leave-one-scenario-out validation and SHAP.
**Tools and scale:** 17,283 records, twelve scenarios.
**Results:** Tree ensembles strongest; packet error dynamics, latency variability and SNR dominate.
