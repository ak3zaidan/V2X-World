# A Machine Learning-based Scheme for Enhancing the Detection of Position Falsification Attacks in Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks
Eslam Abdelkreem, Sherif Hussein, Ashraf Tammam · Scientific Reports 2024 · https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-4516995/v2 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** Weak detection for some position attack types.
**Approach:** RSSIConf feature based on RSSI confidence intervals; RF, KNN, XGB, MLP.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi.
**Results:** RF-FV2 near-perfect on CP; 99.9% on COP and RP.
