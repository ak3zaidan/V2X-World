# Trust-Based Admission and Departure Control for Cooperative Platooning
Farah-Emma Braiteh, Francesca Bassi, Rida Khatoun · International Conference on Control, Decision and Information Technologies 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/codit70676.2026.11631269 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Approach:** Subjective-logic trust with fuzzification for join/leave decisions and member MBD.
**Tools and scale:** PLEXE and SUMO.
**Results:** Effective identification of faulty or malicious vehicles.
