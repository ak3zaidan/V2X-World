# Efficient Authentication for Vehicular Digital Twin Communications
Jing Xu, Chao He, Tom H. Luan · 2021 IEEE 94th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2021-Fall) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2021-fall52928.2021.9625518 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Autonomous vehicles(AVs) have developed rapidly in recent years and derived many AV-related services. However, the stringent requirement of real-time sensing, computing, and communication may severely overload the AV, resulting in a poor passenger experience.
**Approach:** In this work, we leverage the digital twin(DT) concept to help the information fusion and computing for AV, propose a vehicular digital twin(VDT) framework. To tackle those issues, we give a concrete authentication protocol for secure communication between AV and DT.
