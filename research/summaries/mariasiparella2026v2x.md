# V2X FlexStack: A Flexible Open Source ETSI C-ITS Protocol Stack Implementation
Jordi Marias-i-Parella, Daniel Ulied, Marc Codina, Adrià Pons et al. · preprint 2026 · https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7116984 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Few reliable open-source ETSI C-ITS Release 2 stacks exist.
**Approach:** V2X FlexStack: Python (v2xflexstack) and Rust (rustflexstack) ETSI C-ITS Release 2 stack with SAP interfaces, LinkLayer abstraction, CA, DEN and VRU services. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Benchmarks on hosts and embedded platforms.
**Results:** Unsecured Python throughput substantially exceeds packet-processing requirements (rest truncated).

- Not stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — open-source ETSI stack in Rust; we implement ETSI GeoNetworking inside our engine.
**Novelty implications:** An open ETSI Rel-2 stack in Rust exists; our novelty is the integrated engine, not a stack per se.
