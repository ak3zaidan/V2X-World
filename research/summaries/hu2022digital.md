# Digital Twin-Assisted Real-Time Traffic Data Prediction Method for 5G-Enabled Internet of Vehicles
Chunhua Hu, Weicun Fan, Elan Zeng, Zhi Hang et al. · IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tii.2021.3083596 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The development of Internet of Vehicles (IoV) has produced a considerable amount of real-time traffic data. These traffic data constitute a kind of digital twin that connects the physical vehicles and their virtual representation via 5G communications.
**Approach:** Inspired by this drawback, in this article, a digital twin-assisted real-time traffic data prediction method is proposed by analyzing the traffic flow and velocity data monitored by IoV sensors and transmitted through 5G. At last, we conduct a set of experiments based on a traffic dataset collected by Nanjing city of China.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G, digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Reported results show the feasibility of our proposal in smart traffic flow and velocity prediction that call for a quick response and high accuracy.
