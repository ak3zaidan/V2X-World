# DeQoS Attack: Degrading Quality of Service in VANETs and Its Mitigation
Anjia Yang, Jian Weng, Nan Cheng, Jianbing Ni, Xiaodong Lin, Xuemin Shen · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2019.2905522 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we introduce a degradation-of-QoS (DeQoS) attack against vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs). Through DeQoS, the attacker can relay the authentication exchanges between roadside units (RSUs) and faraway vehicles to establish connections but will not relay the service afterwards, which wastes the limited connection resources of RSUs.
**Approach:** In this paper, we introduce a degradation-of-QoS (DeQoS) attack against vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs). Therefore, we design a new cross-layer relay-resistant authentication protocol by leveraging the distance-bounding technique.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** A series of simulations are conducted to verify the practicality of the attack using MATLAB. The experimental results demonstrate that the attacker can easily find exploitable vehicles and launch the DeQoS attack with an overwhelming probability (e.g., more than 0.98).
