# Decentralized prediction and reputation approach in vehicular networks
Maryam Najafi, Lyes Khoukhi, Marc Lemercier · Transactions on Emerging Telecommunications Technologies 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1002/ett.4456 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A vehicle ad‐hoc network (VANET) is an essential component of the intelligent transportation system.
**Approach:** In VANET, vehicle‐to‐vehicle communication plays a significant role in providing a secure network and safe exchange of information between nodes. However, VANET is prone to critical risks, threats, and attacks due to its unique characteristics and dynamic topology.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Based on this approach, vehicles can avoid interacting with unreliable vehicles and ignore incoming messages from them to reduce the effect of malicious nodes in the network. Our extensive simulations show the performance and accuracy of our approach.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, VANET is prone to critical risks, threats, and attacks due to its unique characteristics and dynamic topology.
- Also, the certainty rate remains between 91% and 99% regardless of the simulation scenario's duration.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
