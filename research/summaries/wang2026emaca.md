# EMACA: Efficient and Mobility-Aware V2E Authentication With Collaborative Agents in VANETs
Yun Wang, Ying Xia, Xue-Jiao Liu, Jia-Chi Chen · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2026.3702054 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As a representative of high-mobility Internet of Things (IoT) systems, vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) require time-critical vehicle-to-edge (V2E) interactions for real-time data exchange. Consequently, ensuring the reliability and responsiveness of V2E authentication is crucial for maintaining seamless network services.
**Approach:** To address this issue, we propose efficient and mobility-aware V2E authentication with collaborative agents (EMACA), an efficient and mobility-aware collaborative orchestration framework for V2E authentication, which autonomously optimizes the association between roadside units (RSUs) and mobile edge computing (MEC).
**Tools and scale:** Extensive simulation results demonstrate that EMACA significantly reduces average authentication latency by 9.19%–31.41% and decreases load standard deviation among MECs by 22.25%–65.60%.
**Results:** In this framework, each MEC hosts a collaborative AI agent, and a multiagent deep Q-network (MADQN)-based decision engine coordinates dynamic RSU-MEC association adjustments to reduce average authentication latency by balancing V2E authentication loads.
