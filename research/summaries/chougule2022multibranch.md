# Multibranch Reconstruction Error (MbRE) Intrusion Detection Architecture for Intelligent Edge-Based Policing in Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks
Amit Chougule, Varun Kohli, Vinay Chamola, Fei Richard Yu · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2022.3201548 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Edge anomaly detection for integrity, availability and authentication misbehaviour without training on attacks.
**Approach:** MbRE IDS: three CNN reconstruction models for frequency, pseudo-identity and motion branches; per-branch thresholds giving 8 binary behaviour codes; vehicle-level thresholds.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi-extension style data; emulation on Google Colab and Jetson Nano.
**Results:** Accuracy 100% (frequency), 98.5-100% (identity), 95.4-100% (motion).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — unsupervised MBD including pseudonym-identity behaviour.
**Novelty implications:** Unsupervised identity-aware MBD exists.
