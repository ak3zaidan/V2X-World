# Experimental Framework for Secure Post-Quantum TLS Client-Server Communication
Eva Kupcova, Jozef Simko, Matus Pleva, Milos Drutarovsky · 2024 International Symposium ELMAR 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/elmar62909.2024.10694092 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This article presents the development of an experimental framework for secure communication between client and server.
**Approach:** This framework aims to enable our further development in the field of post-quantum cryptography through the integration and testing of post-quantum algorithms, as well as the generation of corresponding certificates for our future sensor nodes. This framework has the potential to provide a platform for experimenting with new security technologies and contribute to the future development of cryptographic systems in the context of wireless communication, the Internet of Things, and sensor nodes.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** The framework is based on the TLS 1.3 protocol and utilizes post-quantum cryptography.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — post-quantum/hybrid authentication scheme; the engine implements PQ and hybrid signatures, but the abstract states no SCMS-scale or traffic-coupled evaluation.
**Novelty implications:** We cannot claim PQ authentication for vehicles is new; the end-to-end safety cost of PQ in a full SCMS remains open as far as this abstract shows.
