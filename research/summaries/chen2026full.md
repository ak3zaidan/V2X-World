# Full-Domain Coupler: A Wireless Native Neural Backbone for Channel Representation and Deduction
Zirui Chen, Ziqing Xing, Zhaoyang Zhang, Hongning Ruan et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22038 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Data representation is a fundamental issue in deep learning. However, as wireless data scales and deeply couples across many physical domains such as time, space, and frequency, existing wireless artificial intelligence (AI) technologies lack dedicated representation solutions.
**Approach:** To fill this gap, this paper proposes Coupler, a wireless native-AI neural backbone designed for representation learning of channel state information (CSI)--the pivotal data in wireless systems.
**Tools and scale:** Extensive experimental evaluations validate their significant performance gains and robust applicability even for real-world measured data, demonstrating the potential of Coupler as a promising basic architecture in the design of wireless foundation models.
