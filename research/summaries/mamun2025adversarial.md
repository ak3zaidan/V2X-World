# Adversarial Attacks on Machine Learning-Based IDS for V2X Networks: A CICIoV2024 Study
Q. Mamun, Mehedi Hasan, Tu Dac Ho, Zhen-Ni Pan, Shigeru Shimamoto · 2025 IEEE 101st Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2025-Spring) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2025-spring65109.2025.11174733 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the increasing adoption of Vehicle-toEverything (V2X) communication in next-generation intelligent transportation systems, ensuring cybersecurity in vehicular networks has become a critical challenge.
**Approach:** Machine Learning (ML)-based Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS) have been widely proposed to detect anomalous and malicious activities in V2X networks. However, these systems are vulnerable to adversarial attacks, where an attacker subtly manipulates input data to evade detection.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Our results demonstrate that adversarial attacks significantly degrade IDS performance, reducing detection accuracy by up to 40%, thereby exposing critical vulnerabilities in V2X cybersecurity frameworks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- With the increasing adoption of Vehicle-toEverything (V2X) communication in next-generation intelligent transportation systems, ensuring cybersecurity in vehicular networks has become a critical challenge.
- However, these systems are vulnerable to adversarial attacks, where an attacker subtly manipulates input data to evade detection.
- Future research directions include realworld deployment, federated learning-based IDS, and adversarially robust AI architectures for V2X security.
