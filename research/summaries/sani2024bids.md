# BIDS: Blockchain-Enabled Intrusion Detection System in Smart Cities
Meisam Sharifi Sani, Saeid Iranmanesh, Hamidreza Salarian, Raad Raad et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Magazine 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iotm.001.2300191 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Internet of Vehicles (IoV) plays a significant role in shaping smart cities by integrating vehicles, infrastructure, and information and communication technologies (ICT). IoV allows vehicles to connect and exchange information with each other and other smart devices contributing in shaping smart cities.
**Approach:** In order to address this issue, we propose a Blockchain-enabled intrusion detection system (BIDS) for the IoV network, in which vehicles share their mobility patterns with the traffic management system.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset, blockchain (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** Our simulations show that the BIDS method can detect up to 98% of malicious vehicles when only 5% of the vehicles are malicious, and up to 85% when 40% of the vehicles are malicious.
