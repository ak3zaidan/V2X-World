# Blockchain-based federated learning framework for malicious node detection in internet of vehicles (IoV) networks using fog and cloud computing
Srinivas Reddy Bandarapu, Muhammad Bilal, Pushpalika Chatterjee, Adnan Mustafa Cheema, Junaid Rashid, Jungeun Kim · Journal of King Saud University - Computer and Information Sciences 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s44443-025-00134-y · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Centralized ML IDS privacy and bottlenecks.
**Approach:** Blockchain-based FL with LSTM and NB.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi.
**Results:** LSTM accuracy 95%, precision 0.96, recall 0.97.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future use cases for multifactor authentication.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — offline.
**Novelty implications:** Saturated.
