# Public Key Infrastructure in the Post-Quantum Era
Fruzsina Bene, A. Kiss · International Symposium on Applied Computational Intelligence and Informatics 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/saci58269.2023.10158562 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In the last few years the threat of quantum computers has become bigger and bigger.
**Approach:** To this, we review in this paper the basics of classical PKI and quantum cryptography methods and the threat of quantum computing for PKI. We present quantum-safe solutions already in use and recommend new, well-performing solutions for a quantum-resistant PKI by comparison.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** According to the Cloud Security Alliance at around 2030 the power of quantum computers will increase so that the risk for exposure of traditionally encrypted data will be very high.

- None stated in the abstract.

**Novelty implications:** We cannot claim PQ authentication for vehicles is new; the end-to-end safety cost of PQ in a full SCMS remains open as far as this abstract shows.
