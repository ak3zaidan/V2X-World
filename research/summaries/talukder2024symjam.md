# SymJam: Symbiotic Jamming Attacks on NR-V2X
Munmun Talukder, Jiang Xie · GLOBECOM 2024 - 2024 IEEE Global Communications Conference 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/globecom52923.2024.10901590 · relevance: high · basis: abstract only

**Problem:** Exploiting NR-V2X unicast/groupcast and decentralised scheduling for stealthy jamming.
**Approach:** Two "symbiotic" jamming attacks coordinated via unicast against broadcast.
**Tools and scale:** Highway simulation, NR-V2X Mode 2.
**Results:** PRR reduced by 32.65%, PIR increased by 30.99%; impact nearly doubles in some windows and at higher density.

- None stated in the abstract.
