# Towards channel foundation models (CFMs): Motivations, methodologies and opportunities
Jun Jiang, Yuan Gao, Xin-Yi Wu, Shu-Gong Xu · venue unknown 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.13637 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Artificial intelligence (AI) has emerged as a pivotal enabler for next-generation wireless communication systems. However, conventional AI-based models encounter several limitations, such as heavy reliance on labeled data, limited generalization capability, and task-specific design.
**Approach:** To address these challenges, this paper introduces, for the first time, the concept of channel foundation models (CFMs)-a novel and unified framework designed to tackle a wide range of channel-related tasks through a pretrained, universal channel feature extractor. We further analyze the evolution of AI methodologies, from supervised learning and multi-task learning to self-supervised learning, emphasizing the distinct advantages of the latter in facilitating the development of CFMs.
