# AttackQA: Development and Adoption of a Dataset for Assisting Cybersecurity Operations using Fine-tuned and Open-Source LLMs
Varun Badrinath Krishna · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.01073 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Q&A support for SOC analysts.
**Approach:** AttackQA dataset of 25,335 Q&A pairs; RAG with fine-tuned open models.
**Tools and scale:** Llama 3 8B/70B/405B; SambaNova hardware.
**Results:** Fine-tuned open models beat GPT-4o in their RAG setting.
