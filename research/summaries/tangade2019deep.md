# A Deep Learning Based Driver Classification and Trust Computation in VANETs
Shrikant S. Tangade, S. Manvi, Stive Hassan · IEEE Vehicular Technology Conference 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtcfall.2019.8891462 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) are most promising technology for smart transportation systems to provide road safety. However, VANETs are more vulnerable to malicious nodes and susceptible to a number of security attacks.
**Approach:** In order to provide security against these attacks, most of the researchers have presented cryptography based schemes. Hence, many researchers have proposed trust management based schemes to address internal attacks.
**Tools and scale:** deep learning (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** Extensive simulation results are presented to validate effectiveness of proposed DL-DCTC scheme.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Addresses V2X attacks or misbehaviour/trust evaluation that our engine can execute and detect in closed loop; it does not model the SCMS, revocation or joint traffic/radio/PKI ground truth.
**Novelty implications:** Does not rule out our candidate directions; any claim about this specific attack/detection/trust mechanism must cite it. Left open: end-to-end detection-report-revocation evaluation with safety outcomes.
