# Implementation and Analysis of IEEE and ETSI Security Standards for Vehicular Communications
Bruno Fernandes, J. Rufino, Muhammad Alam, J. Ferreira · Journal on spesial topics in mobile networks and applications 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s11036-018-1019-x · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Performance impact of 1609.2 and ETSI security on safety communications.
**Approach:** API implementing 1609.2 and ETSI ITS security algorithms/protocols, integrated into an emulated PKI to measure coding/decoding delay.
**Tools and scale:** Emulated PKI.
**Results:** Delay of secure message processing measured (no numbers in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - implemented 1609.2 security with PKI emulation and measured latency impact on safety messages; not a full SCMS in city-scale simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Implementations of 1609.2 with latency measurement exist; validated full US SCMS against test vectors in closed loop remains ours.
