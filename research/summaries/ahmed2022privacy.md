# Privacy-preserving blockchain-based authentication and trust management in VANETs
Waheeb Ahmed, Di Wu, Daniel Mukathe · IET Networks 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1049/ntw2.12036 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) are characterized by high mobility of nodes and volatility, which make privacy, trust management, and security challenging issues in VANETs' design. In such networks, data can be exposed to a variety of attacks, the most dangerous is false information dissemination, which threatens the safety and efficiency of transportation systems.
**Approach:** False emergency messages can be injected by inside attackers to announce fake incidents such as traffic accidents, resulting in a false information attack. As the data in VANET is based on events, any trust mechanism must first identify the true events.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The efficiency of the proposed authentication scheme is validated through analysis while the trust model is validated through simulations.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- To address these security challenges, a blockchain‐based authentication scheme and trust management model are proposed for VANETs.
