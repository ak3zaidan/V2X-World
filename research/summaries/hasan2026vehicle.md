# In-Vehicle Digital Twin-Based Collision Warning Framework with Sybil Attack Detection
Mohammad Imtiaz Hasan, Abyad Enan, Jean Michel Tine, Araf Rahman et al. · arXiv (Cornell University) 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28625 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Real-time Sybil detection and mitigation within collision warning.
**Approach:** In-vehicle digital twin with TCN trajectory model and HNSW similarity search, integrated with collision risk assessment.
**Tools and scale:** Field experiments with two CVs in Clemson, SC; real Sybil BSM data; static and dynamic attacks.
**Results:** Accuracy 0.984, recall 1.00, F1 0.944; mean TET and TIT of near-collision events reduced by 88% and more (abstract truncated).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the full text.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — detection plus mitigation measured with safety surrogate metrics (TET/TIT), i.e. attack to safety outcome; direction 1 at vehicle level.
**Novelty implications:** We cannot claim first safety-metric evaluation of Sybil mitigation; system-level (MA/revocation) timing remains open.
