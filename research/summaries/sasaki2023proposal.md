# A proposal for a remote vehicle control emulator coordinated with CARLA, OMNeT++, and SUMO
Kengo Sasaki, Masaki Takanashi, Katsushi Sanda · 2023 IEEE 98th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2023-Fall) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2023-fall60731.2023.10333658 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** No emulator guarantees real-time performance for remote vehicle control evaluation.
**Approach:** Emulation architecture with OMNeT++, CARLA and SUMO, resolving tight coupling and real-packet injection.
**Tools and scale:** OMNeT++, CARLA, SUMO.
**Results:** Service performance (delay, throughput, trajectory, video quality) reflects wireless performance in two case studies.
