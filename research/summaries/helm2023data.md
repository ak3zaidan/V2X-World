# Data-efficient GNN models of communication networks using beta-distribution-based sample ranking
Max Helm, Benedikt Jaeger, Georg Carle · ITU Journal on Future and Evolving Technologies 2023 · https://doi.org/10.52953/fuqe7013 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ML models of networks need large, costly datasets.
**Approach:** Beta-distribution sample ranking to build minimal training sets for a GNN performance model.
**Tools and scale:** 100 small samples; tested on larger problems.
**Results:** MAPE 9.79% versus 37.82% baseline.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Limited to a dataset of 100 samples.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** Data-efficient surrogate training is known; relevant technique for our surrogate.
