# vNetRunner: Per-VNF Slice Modeling for 5G and Beyond Networks
Muhammad Sulaiman, Bo Sun, Mohammad A. Salahuddin, Raouf Boutaba, Aladdin Saleh · NOMS 2025-2025 IEEE Network Operations and Management Symposium 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/noms57970.2025.11073606 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Slice modelling for 5G MANO.
**Approach:** Per-VNF trained models composed into slice models.
**Tools and scale:** Open-source 5G testbed.
**Results:** Wasserstein distances 6 ms (delay), 0.554 Mbps (throughput); order of magnitude faster.
