# Efficient Blockchain-Based Pseudonym Authentication Scheme Supporting Revocation for 5G-Assisted Vehicular Fog Computing
Badiea Abdulkarem Mohammed, ‪Mahmood A. Al-Shareeda‬‏, Abeer Abdullah Alsadhan, Zeyad Ghaleb Al-Mekhlafi et al. · IEEE Access 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3372390 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Re-authentication cost across RSU regions in 5G fog.
**Approach:** Blockchain pseudonym authentication with bilinear pairings, no TA, fog-side revocation keys.
**Tools and scale:** Cost analysis on CYGWIN/Core i5.
**Results:** Lower computation, faster verification, lower energy than state of the art.
