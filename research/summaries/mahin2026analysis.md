# Analysis of Multi-Incident Scenarios on Expressway Safety and Traffic Performance: A SUMO-Based Microscopic Simulation Study
Hridoy  Deb Mahin · Unknown venue 2026 · https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6683180 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Traffic incidents are a major contributor to non-recurring congestion, travel delays, and secondary crashes on expressways.
**Approach:** While most prior studies focus on single-incident disruptions, the compound impacts of multiple, sequential incidents under mixed traffic conditions remain insufficiently explored. This study develops a microscopic simulation framework in Simulation of Urban Mobility (SUMO) to evaluate the safety and performance effects of multi-incident freeway scenarios across diverse lane-blockage configurations.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: SUMO; scale not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Results indicate that increasing CAV penetration generally improves system resilience by enhancing traffic flow efficiency and reducing critical safety conflicts.
