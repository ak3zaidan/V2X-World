# A Fuzzy-Based Context-Aware Misbehavior Detecting Scheme for Detecting Rogue Nodes in Vehicular Ad Hoc Network
Fuad A. Ghaleb, Faisal Saeed, Eman H. Alkhammash, Norah Saleh Alghamdi, Bander Ali Saleh Al-rimy · Sensors 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s22072810 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Context uncertainty and static thresholds hamper rogue node detection.
**Approach:** Fuzzy inference system evaluating vehicles; dynamic context reference; classification by deviation.
**Tools and scale:** Extensive experiments (dataset not extracted).
**Results:** 7.88% overall and 16.46% detection-rate improvement vs state of the art.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: ML to adapt detection threshold to context
- more representative context features.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — adaptive thresholds (direction 2).
**Novelty implications:** Adaptive thresholds widely proposed.
