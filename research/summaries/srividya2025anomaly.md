# Anomaly Detection in Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks (Vanets) Using a Hybrid Deep Learning Approach
K. Srividya, K. Hosmitha, K. N. Reddy, M. Jabbar · 2025 International Conference on Computer Science, Technology and Engineering (ICCSTE) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccste65902.2025.11138197 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Nowadays there is a rapid growth in the number of vehicles emphasizing the necessity for better Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) for Vehicular Ad-hoc networks (VANETs).
**Approach:** This interest towards VANETs increased the necessity to develop advanced machine learning and deep learning models to improve the capabilities of anomaly detection in connected vehicles. Existing models can only identify specific type of anomalies and they fail to detect anomaly in real-time data.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: SUMO
**Results:** The hybrid model with best results is CNN-GRU-Transformers with an accuracy of over $\mathbf{9 6 \%}$.
