# Foundation Model-Aided Channel-Adaptive Video Semantic Communication and Prototype Validation
Jiarun Ding, Peiwen Jiang, Chao-Kai Wen, Xiao Li, Shi Jin · IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/twc.2025.3600221 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The increasing demand for services such as live streaming and virtual reality places significant pressure on wireless communication systems.
**Approach:** Enhancing system performance or reducing bandwidth consumption is critical for delivering high-quality video experiences. Semantic communication, which focuses on the transmission of meaning, offers a promising solution.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: testbed.
**Results:** Simulations and over-the-air experiments confirm significant improvements in video quality and substantial reductions in bandwidth overhead compared to existing methods.
