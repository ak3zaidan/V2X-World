# Demo:Dynamic Suppression of Selfish Node Attack Motivation in the Process of VANET Communication
Bo-Wei Zhang, Xiao-Liang Wang, Ru Xie, Hua-Zheng Zhang, Frank Jiang · IEEE International Conference on Distributed Computing Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icdcs54860.2022.00137 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The selfish On-Board-Unit (OBU) attacks Vehicular Ad-Hoc Network (VANET) by various attacks for profit.
**Approach:** However, many existing methods are based on the principle of direct reciprocity for communication, and when an attack occurs, it is easy to crash in the case of large-scale networks. In order to reduce the number of attackers in the vehicle ad-hoc network and restrain the attack motivation of the OBUs, we propose an indirect reciprocal incentive mechanism based on reputation to encourage the OBUs in the VANET to help each other.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: blockchain
**Results:** In order to reduce the number of attackers in the vehicle ad-hoc network and restrain the attack motivation of the OBUs, we propose an indirect reciprocal incentive mechanism based on reputation to encourage the OBUs in the VANET to help each other. Finally, through a large number of simulation experiments, the performance of our proposed algorithm is obviously better than that of the baseline strategy, and is verified by the simulation results.
