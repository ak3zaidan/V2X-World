# Anomaly Detection in VANET Using Machine Learning-Based Data Analytics
S. C, S. S., S. S, S. Vr, R. M., N. T. · 2025 IEEE Pune Section International Conference (PuneCon) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/punecon67554.2025.11378696 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Anomaly detection in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) using machine learning-based data analytics is critical for safeguarding intelligent transportation systems from cyber threats and network irregularities.
**Approach:** This study reviews and synthesizes state-of-the-art approaches, highlighting the application of supervised algorithms (Random Forest, SVM, Logistic Regression), advanced deep learning models (CNN, GRU), and hybrid techniques that combine traditional and neural methods for both known and novel attack detection. Key findings indicate that ensemble and boosting algorithms (XGBoost, CatBoost, AdaBoost), coupled with robust feature engineering and data balancing, deliver superior detection accuracy often achieving F1-scores above 96% and real-time classification performance.
