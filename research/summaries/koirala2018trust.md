# Trust Management Based on Node Stay Time in VANET
Bonish Koirala, Shrikant S. Tangade, S. Manvi · International Conference on Advances in Computing, Communications and Informatics 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icacci.2018.8554563 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The scenario of security in VANET differs from the security in any other network.
**Approach:** This is mainly because VANET is dynamic by nature i.e. the movement and speed of the nodes are not constant. Some nodes in the VANET stay in the network for a longer time.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The proposed algorithm Trust Management Based on Node Stay Time (TMBNST) in VANET gives a better result than papers based on full cryptography.

- None stated in the abstract.
