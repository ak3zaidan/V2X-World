# A VeReMi-based Dataset for Predicting the Effect of Attacks in VANETs
Mohammed A. Abdelmaguid, Hossam S. Hassanein, Mohammad Zulkernine · Proceedings of the Int'l ACM Conference on Modeling Analysis and Simulation of Wireless and Mobile Systems 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3616388.3617548 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Datasets cannot evaluate proactive (predictive) security.
**Approach:** VeReMiAP from F2MD with CAMs, Fake Reporting Attacks and evaluation of their impact as road hazards.
**Tools and scale:** F2MD (Veins/SUMO).
**Results:** Temporal velocity and geospatial hazard analysis show attack ripple effects.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — dataset linking attacks to traffic impact (hazards).
**Novelty implications:** Attack-impact datasets exist; per-unit counterfactual ground truth does not.
