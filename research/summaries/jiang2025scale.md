# Scale What Counts, Mask What Matters: Evaluating Foundation Models for Zero-Shot Cross-Domain Wi-Fi Sensing
Cheng Jiang, Yi-He Yan, Yan-Xiang Wang, Chun-Tung Chou et al. · arXiv.org 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18792 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Wi-Fi sensing models fail across domains.
**Approach:** MAE pretraining on a large aggregated CSI corpus; disentangles data diversity versus model capacity.
**Tools and scale:** 1.3 million samples from 14 datasets, 4 devices, 2.4/5/6 GHz.
**Results:** Log-linear improvement on unseen domains with more pretraining data.

- Not stated in the abstract.
