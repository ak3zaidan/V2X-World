# LLM-Based Misbehavior Detection Architecture for Enhanced Traffic Safety in Connected Autonomous Vehicles
Yaqi Hu, Fei Wang, Dongdong Ye, Maoqiang Wu, Jiawen Kang, Rong Yu · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2025.3551327 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Fake traffic signs and forged motion information threaten CAVs.
**Approach:** LLM-selectable MBD architecture: adapter+LoRA gated fine-tuning for smaller LLMs, knowledge base plus prompt tuning for larger ones; distance-triggered sign checks and plausibility-triggered motion checks.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Fine-tuned LLMs outperform traditional methods.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — LLMs in MBD (runner-up LLM agent direction), but as detector not as analyst.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first LLM-based V2X misbehaviour detection.
