# Federated Learning for Digital Twin-Based Vehicular Networks: Architecture and Challenges
Latif U. Khan, Ehzaz Mustafa, Junaid Shuja, Faisal Rehman et al. · IEEE Wireless Communications 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mwc.012.2200373 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Emerging intelligent transportation applications, such as accident reporting, lane change assistance, collision avoidance, and infotainment, will be based on diverse requirements (e.g., latency, reliability, quality of physical experience). To fulfill such requirements, there is a significant need to deploy a digital twin-based intelligent transportation system.
**Approach:** Therefore, in this article, we present an overview of FL for the twin-based vehicular network. We also outline a few use cases of FL for the twin-based vehicular network.
