# Packet Inter-Reception Time Modeling for High-Density Platooning in Varying Surrounding Traffic Density

**Problem:** QoS prediction for ad-hoc platoon communication.
**Approach:** Empirical distribution fitting of 802.11p packet inter-reception time in varying traffic density.
**Tools and scale:** Simulated platoon data.
**Results:** Baseline model for future work.
