# Attacks on the Vehicle Ad-hoc Network from Cyberspace
Anas Alwasel, Shailendra Mishra, Mohammed Alshehri · International Journal of Advanced Computer Science and Applications 2023 · https://doi.org/10.14569/ijacsa.2023.0140729 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Detecting misbehaviour (fake BSMs) in VANETs.
**Approach:** Algorithm checking identity, location and speed; classifiers (Naive Bayes, J48, Random Tree, Bagging, LogitBoost) compared.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicle/attack simulation dataset; Weka-style classifiers.
**Results:** Random Tree about 99% accuracy; J48 99.75% at 7.75 s build; Bagging 98.98% at 15.15 s.
