# m4: A Learned Flow-level Network Simulator
Chenning Li, Anton A. Zabreyko, Arash Nasr-Esfahany, Kevin Zhao, Prateesh Goyal, Mohammad Alizadeh et al. · arXiv.org 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01770 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Flow-level simulators omit packet-level effects.
**Approach:** m4: learned flow-level simulator with spatial/temporal state-transition networks and dense intermediate supervision.
**Tools and scale:** Data-centre networks.
**Results:** Up to 104x speedup over packet-level; per-flow errors down 45.3% mean and 53.0% p90 vs flow-level simulation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Prior ML simulators use fixed-size global hidden state and handle graph data poorly (addressed).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - learned network simulator/world model (direction 3).
**Novelty implications:** No effect on V2X.
