# In-Vehicle Network Attack Based on CAN and UDS: Demonstration and Analysis
Shixuan Ren, Zhen Guo, Yuqiao Ning et al. · International Conference on Internet of Things, Automation and Artificial Intelligence (IoTAAI) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3653081.3653086 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the development of smart internet-connected vehicles, in-vehicle networks are facing increasing security threats.
**Approach:** This paper focuses on three common attack methods: CAN message injection, replay and Dos attack, and designs five attack experiments based on these three methods. This paper proposes an experimental method based on UDS (Unified Diagnostic Services) CAN message injection and DoS attack, which causes functional interference in new vehicles, and proposes protective measures to safeguard the security of in-vehicle CAN network based on the analysis of the experiments.
