# Decentralized Trust Model for Vehicle Ad-Hoc Networks (VANETs) with 5G Integration: A Blockchain-Based Approach for Enhanced Security and Privacy in Intelligent Transportation Systems
Rafe Alasem, R. Hasan, M. Mansour · World Electric Vehicle Journal 2026 · https://doi.org/10.3390/wevj17070375 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Centralised VANET trust models with 5G.
**Approach:** TrustChain-VANET: blockchain + IPFS, PQ algorithms, threshold/homomorphic encryption, geographic sharding, edge consensus. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Evaluation up to 5000 nodes.
**Results:** Authentication latency 90-120 ms vs 150-300 ms; 90% malicious node detection; 2000-2150 TPS.
