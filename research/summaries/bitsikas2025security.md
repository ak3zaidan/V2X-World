# Security Analysis of 5G NR Device-to-Device Sidelink Communications
Evangelos Bitsikas, Aanjhan Ranganathan · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16650 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Security of 5G NR V2X sidelink procedures.
**Approach:** Specification analysis of synchronisation, resource allocation, HARQ feedback and authorisation; plausible attacks and mitigations; reported to GSMA.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the extracted sections.
**Results:** Identifies attacks manipulating data integrity feedback and blocking resources, harming reliability and privacy.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Calls for security re-evaluation of NR V2X and future research building on it.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — partial: NR sidelink attacks (runner-up direction); not simulated with application-layer security load.
**Novelty implications:** First comprehensive NR sidelink security analysis is taken; open: quantitative in-simulation impact with 1609.2 verification.
