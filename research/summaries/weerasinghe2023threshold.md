# Threshold Cryptography-Based Secure Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) Communication in 5G-Enabled Intelligent Transportation Systems
Nuwan Weerasinghe, Muhammad Arslan Usman, Chaminda T. E. R. Hewage, Eckhard Pflüegel et al. · Future Internet 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/fi15050157 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Rapid trust between vehicles from different manufacturers/jurisdictions in 5G V2X.
**Approach:** Threshold-cryptography key exchange protocol.
**Tools and scale:** NS-3, commercial 5G network, public cloud testbed.
**Results:** Larger keys, higher thresholds and server verification reduce join time (as stated).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Use GPUs/parallel processing to reduce overhead
- SoC hardware
- apply to other scenarios like V2C
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Threshold cryptography for V2X trust; our engine has threshold authorities.
**Novelty implications:** Threshold crypto in V2X evaluated in NS-3; threshold SCMS authorities (FROST/CGGMP21) not covered.
