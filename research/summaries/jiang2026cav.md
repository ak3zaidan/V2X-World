# CAV control strategy based on integrating deep learning and Kalman filter in cyberattack scenarios
Wan Jiang · Conference on Smart Transportation and City Engineering 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3101237 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Connected and Autonomous Vehicle (CAV) is prone to suffer cyberattacks in Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) connections, which leads to failure in the CAV decision-making and control.
**Approach:** In order to ensure CAV’s reliable operation in cyberattack scenarios, this study proposes a CAV control strategy based on the integration of Deep Learning (DL) and Kalman filter. Finally, the proposed model outperforms LSTM and LSTM-CNN in prediction accuracy.
**Tools and scale:** And NGSIM dataset is accessed to conduct experiments in SUMO to simulate three types of cyberattack scenarios (delay, replay and random attack).
**Results:** Results show that after implementing the proposed CAV control strategy, the Value at Risk (VaR) of three cyberattack scenarios are respectively reduced by 1.99%, 7.08% and 28.25%.
