# Improving Memory Efficiency of NIST Post-Quantum Cryptography for Microcontrollers
Dominik Marchsreiter, Holger Kinkelin, Und Georg Carle · 2026 IEEE European Symposium on Security and Privacy Workshops (EuroS&PW) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/eurospw72509.2026.00051 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The quantum threat requires the automotive industry to transition to post-quantum cryptography (PQC). However, the newly standardized NIST PQC algorithms have a significantly larger memory footprint than their classical predecessors.
**Approach:** However, the newly standardized NIST PQC algorithms have a significantly larger memory footprint than their classical predecessors.
**Tools and scale:** In this work, we implement the NIST-standardized PQC algorithms ML-DSA, SLH-DSA, and ML-KEM on the AURIX TC4x microcontroller, which is the latest iteration of an industry-leading line of microcontrollers.
**Results:** In this work, we implement the NIST-standardized PQC algorithms ML-DSA, SLH-DSA, and ML-KEM on the AURIX TC4x microcontroller, which is the latest iteration of an industry-leading line of microcontrollers. Finally, we compare the stack consumption and execution time of our optimized implementations with those of the non-optimized and classical counterparts on the AURIX TC4x.
