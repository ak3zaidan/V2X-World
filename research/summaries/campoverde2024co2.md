# CO2 Emission and Fuel Consumption Effects in VANETs: a Simulation Analysis Using SUMO
Luis Miguel Samaniego Campoverde, Alex Ramiro Masaquiza Caiza · 2024 32nd Telecommunications Forum (TELFOR) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/telfor63250.2024.10819051 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, the Urban Mobility Simulation software (SUMO) is used to analyze vehicle emissions and fuel consumption.
**Approach:** The analysis is conducted in scenarios generated with OpenStreetMap (OSM) Web Wizard, using predefined configurations and varying vehicle characteristics, including different types of vehicles and driving styles. The results reveal that the use of quick configurations may introduce inaccuracies in the metrics, negatively impacting the precision of studies related to traffic and air pollution.
