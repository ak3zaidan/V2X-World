# UAV-Assisted Quantum Key Distribution for Secure Communications With Resource Limited Devices
Peng-Yong Kong · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2024.3375588 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** One-time-pad (OTP) can provide unconditionally secure communications between devices with limited resources, but requires continuous key distribution. Existing quantum key distribution (QKD) protocols produce a continuous secret key stream, but cannot be deployed at the devices that have no quantum channels.
**Approach:** In this paper, we first propose a scheme, called synchronized random measurement (SRM) to improve the secret key rate of a QKD protocol.
**Tools and scale:** Through simulations, we have confirmed the key quality and rate improvement of the proposed SRM.
**Results:** Through simulations, we have confirmed the key quality and rate improvement of the proposed SRM.
