# Location privacy preserving scheme based on dynamic pseudonym swap zone for Internet of Vehicles
Min Yang, Yong Feng, Xiaodong Fu, Qian Qian · International Journal of Distributed Sensor Networks 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1177/1550147719865508 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Pseudonym swap zones leak participant info to managers.
**Approach:** Dynamic pseudonym swap zones formed on demand with random partners and swap-plus-update.
**Tools and scale:** Analysis and simulation.
**Results:** High location privacy protection.
