# Millimeter Wave Vehicular Channel Emulation: A Framework for Balancing Complexity and Accuracy
Thomas Blazek, Erich Zöchmann, Christoph F. Mecklenbräuker · Sensors 2018 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s18113997 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** mmWave vehicular channel emulation must balance complexity and accuracy.
**Approach:** Sparse c-LASSO representation with Fourier or subband-projected Fourier bases; sequential estimator for consistent geometry.
**Tools and scale:** Synthetic and measured 60 GHz vehicular channels.
**Results:** Six multipath clusters optimal in accuracy-complexity (AIC) trade-off, with P=4 parallel subbands if affordable.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Optimal number of clusters may change by scenario.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
