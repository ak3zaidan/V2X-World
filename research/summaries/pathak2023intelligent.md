# Intelligent 6G Admission Control Leveraging LSTM-Based Request Forecasting
Priyanka Pathak, Susanna Schwarzmann, Riccardo Trivisonno, Marius Pesavento · ICC 2023 - IEEE International Conference on Communications 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc45041.2023.10278677 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** 5G mechanisms typically rely on resource over-provisioning or static reservations to satisfy the stringent demands of critical connections.
**Approach:** Consequently, 5G networks are inefficient in fulfilling application's low delay and high reliability requirements. Emerging 6G use cases will be even more demanding in terms of delay and reliability.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G, simulation.
**Results:** To achieve this goal, we propose an intelligent admission control (AC) scheme for a radio access node (AN). Our simulation-based evaluations show that the proposed approach improves a baseline approach – not using intelligence –as it is capable of providing reliability to a larger number of connections, while efficiently utilizing system's resources.
