# Blockchain-Enabled Trust Management With Location Privacy Preservation in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Yibing Li, Yangjie Cao, Y. Zhuang, Jie Li et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2024.3350694 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the advancement of intelligent transportation systems, location-based services (LBSs) have been widely applied in vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs). LBS utilizes mobile devices to gather vehicle location data, which is then processed using relevant technologies.
**Approach:** However, providing LBS brings critical security issues related to the exposure of vehicle positions, as well as privacy-preserving problems during the process of collecting location information in VANETs. We propose a distributed trust-based k anonymity scheme to address the aforementioned issues.
