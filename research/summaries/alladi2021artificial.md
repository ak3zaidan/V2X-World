# Artificial Intelligence (AI)-Empowered Intrusion Detection Architecture for the Internet of Vehicles
Tejasvi Alladi, Varun Kohli, Vinay Chamola, F. Yu et al. · IEEE wireless communications 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mwc.001.2000428 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Recent advances in the Internet of Things (IoT) and the adoption of IoT in vehicular networks have led to a new and promising paradigm called the Internet of Vehicles (IoV). However, the mode of communication in IoV being wireless in nature poses serious cybersecurity challenges.
**Approach:** Toward this end, we present an artificial intelligence (AI)-based intrusion detection architecture comprising Deep Learning Engines (DLEs) for identification and classification of the vehicular traffic in the IoV networks into potential cyberattack types.
**Tools and scale:** Extensive experimental results using popular evaluation metrics and average prediction time on a MEC testbed demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed scheme.
