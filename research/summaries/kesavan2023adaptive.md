# Adaptive neuro-fuzzy inference system and particle swarm optimization: A modern paradigm for securing VANETs
V. Kesavan, S. Murugavalli, P. Manoharan, Shitharth Selvarajan · IET Communications 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1049/cmu2.12692 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** VANET attack detection.
**Approach:** ANFIS + PSO (SC-SPVE).
**Tools and scale:** NS-2.
**Results:** Throughput 148.71 kbps, delay 23.60 ms, PDR 95.62%, accuracy 99.55%, detection time 6.76 ms.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — ML misbehaviour detection, network-layer.
**Novelty implications:** None.
