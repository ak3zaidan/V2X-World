# Conditional pseudonymity in vehicular ad hoc networks
Florian Schaub · OPen Access Repositorium der Universität Ulm (OPARU) (Ulm University) 2008 · https://doi.org/10.18725/oparu-1733 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Authorities holding pseudonym-identity mappings is a privacy risk.
**Approach:** Resolution information embedded in pseudonyms, resolvable only by multiple cooperating authorities; privacy-preserving issuance protocol.
**Tools and scale:** Thesis, protocol design.
**Results:** Issuers cannot create pseudonym-identity mappings.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Separation-of-duty resolution conceptually matches SCMS linkage authorities (LA1/LA2) we implement; prior art for split-authority resolution.
**Novelty implications:** Same topic area; does not provide the closed-loop, SCMS-complete, cross-layer evaluation our engine targets, so our claims stand but this work must be cited as related.
