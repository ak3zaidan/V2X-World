# Modeling Misbehavior Detection Timeliness in VANETs
Mateus Martinez de Lucena, Antonio Augusto Frohlich · 2022 IEEE 27th International Conference on Emerging Technologies and Factory Automation (ETFA) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/etfa52439.2022.9921605 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** MBD processing delays can let data expire before verification.
**Approach:** Cooperative MBD framework with Signal Temporal Logic property monitors for timeliness.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO Luxembourg scenario; threshold detector; varying density.
**Results:** High density increases expired data due to processing delay; accuracy reported per density.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Timeliness of verification/MBD vs safety, related to our "safety cost of security" direction.
**Novelty implications:** Shows timeliness issue for MBD; full-stack verification-load effect on safety still open.
