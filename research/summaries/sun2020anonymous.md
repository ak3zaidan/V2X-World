# Anonymous Identity Authentication Scheme for VANETs
Mei Sun, Juan Zhang, Yuyan Guo · Journal of Coastal Research 2020 · https://doi.org/10.2112/jcr-si104-037.1 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Journal of Coastal Research, Special Issue No. 104, pp. 203–209.
**Approach:** The network security of VANETs becomes the key to restrict the development of VANETs. In this paper, we propose an anonymous authentication scheme for VANETs, in which we avoid using certificates in the authentication process between the RSU and the vehicle.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** Coconut Creek (Florida), ISSN 0749-0208. And the RSU can update the local key to improve the security of the communication.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
