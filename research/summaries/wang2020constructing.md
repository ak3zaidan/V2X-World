# Constructing Optimal Sparse Decision Tree for Analying I-SIG System Attack
Xiaojin Wang, Yingxiao Xiang, Wenjia Niu, Endong Tong et al. · 2020 IEEE Intl Conf on Parallel &amp; Distributed Processing with Applications, Big Data &amp; Cloud Computing, Sustainable Computing &amp; Communications, Social Computing &amp; Networking (ISPA/BDCloud/SocialCom/SustainCom) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ispa-bdcloud-socialcom-sustaincom51426.2020.00066 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract only

**Problem:** Explaining consequences of the I-SIG congestion attack.
**Approach:** Optimal sparse decision tree over 29 features; 195 samples from VISSIM.
**Tools and scale:** VISSIM.
**Results:** 78.6% prediction accuracy with sparse features; claims first I-SIG attack dataset.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — partial: attack-consequence datasets; ours offers per-message ground truth at scale.
**Novelty implications:** A small I-SIG attack dataset exists; a large benchmark with counterfactuals remains open.
