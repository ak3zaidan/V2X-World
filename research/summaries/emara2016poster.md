# Poster: PREXT: Privacy extension for Veins VANET simulator
Karim Emara · 2016 IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference (VNC) 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc.2016.7835979 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Preserving location privacy is an important aspect in vehicular ad-hoc networks.
**Approach:** Although location privacy is thoroughly studied in the past decade, it is usually skipped in VANET simulators. In this paper, we propose a location privacy extension, PREXT, for Veins framework.
