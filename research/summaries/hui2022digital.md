# Digital Twin for Networking: A Data-driven Performance Modeling Perspective
Linbo Hui, Mowei Wang, Liang Zhang, Lu Lu, Yong Cui · IEEE Network 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mnet.119.2200080 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Digital twin networks need what-if performance evaluation; simulation and analytics are inefficient, inaccurate or inflexible.
**Approach:** Survey/position: requirements (fidelity, efficiency, flexibility), comparison of data-driven performance models, trends and opportunities.
**Tools and scale:** Survey.
**Results:** Finds conflicts between models' capacity for diverse inputs and limited production data.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Limited data from production networks constrains data-driven models.
- Opportunities identified in data collection, model construction and applications.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - what-if evaluation is the generic aim of our counterfactual reruns.
**Novelty implications:** What-if DTN modelling is an established framing; exact per-unit counterfactual ground truth is not discussed.
