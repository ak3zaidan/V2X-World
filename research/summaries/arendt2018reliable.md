# Reliable Communication over Arbitrarily Varying Channels under Block-Restricted Jamming
Christian Arendt, Janis Nötzel, Holger Boche · arXiv 2018 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.09753 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** We study reliable communication in uncoordinated vehicular communication from the perspective of Shannon theory.
**Approach:** Further we introduce the notion of block-restricted jamming and present a lower and an upper bound on the maximum error capacity of the corresponding restricted AVC.
