# Runtime Stealthy Perception Attacks against DNN-based Adaptive Cruise Control Systems
Xugui Zhou, Anqi Chen, Maxfield Kouzel, Haotian Ren et al. · Proceedings of the 20th ACM Asia Conference on Computer and Communications Security 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3708821.3710832 · relevance: medium · basis: full text

**Problem:** Security of DNN-based ACC against runtime stealthy camera perturbations.
**Approach:** Control-theoretic selection of critical attack times plus optimisation-based runtime patch generation.
**Tools and scale:** Production Level-2 ADAS control software in closed-loop simulation, public driving dataset, actual vehicle, driver and AEBS interventions.
**Results:** 142.9x higher hazard success rate and 82.6% higher evasion rate than baselines.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Highlights importance of driver intervention and basic safety mechanisms
- no explicit limitation list in extracted sections.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — none: perception, not V2X; but closed-loop safety measurement is methodologically similar.
**Novelty implications:** Nothing of our candidate directions is ruled out.
