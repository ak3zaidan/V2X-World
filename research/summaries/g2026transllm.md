# TransLLM-H: A Hybrid Federated–Decentralized Post-Quantum Framework for Semantic Trust in 6G Vehicular Networks
Kannan G, Neela Madheswari A · 2026 International Conference on Recent Advances in Electrical, Electronics, Ubiquitous Communication, and Computational Intelligence (RAEEUCCI) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/raeeucci67649.2026.11504703 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Byzantine-robust, quantum-resilient trust for 6G vehicular federated learning.
**Approach:** TransLLM-H: trust-weighted aggregation, PQ blockchain authentication, LLM-based semantic validation. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** 30 simulation runs.
**Results:** 52% lower aggregation latency, 31% better semantic trust accuracy, stable up to 50% malicious nodes.
