# Maturity of Vehicle Digital Twins: From Monitoring to Enabling Autonomous Driving
Robert Klar, Niklas Arvidsson, Vangelis Angelakis · 2024 IEEE 27th International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/itsc58415.2024.10920004 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Digital twinning of vehicles is an iconic application of digital twins, as the concept of twinning dates back to the twinning of NASA space vehicles.
**Approach:** Although digital twins (DTs) in the automotive industry have been recognized for their ability to improve efficiency in design and manufacturing, their potential to enhance land vehicle operation has yet to be fully explored. Most existing DT research on vehicle operations, aside from the existing body of work on autonomous guided vehicles (AGVs), focuses on electrified passenger cars.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting. The available text does not describe a V2X simulator, radio stack, traffic model or SCMS-style PKI in enough detail to report; scale not stated.
**Results:** The application of our DT maturity tool reveals that most DTs have reached level 3 and enable real-time monitoring. Additionally, DTs of level 5 already exist in closed environments, allowing for restricted autonomous operation.
