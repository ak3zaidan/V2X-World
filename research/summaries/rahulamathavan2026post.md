# Post-Quantum Access Control for Collaborative Perception in Vehicular Edge Networks
Yogachandran Rahulamathavan, Sangarapillai Lambotharan · 2026 IEEE 37th International Symposium on Personal, Indoor and Mobile Radio Communications (PIMRC) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/pimrc69400.2026.11711645 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Collaborative perception data needs PQ access control.
**Approach:** PQ-VECShare: RLWE CP-ABE with two-party computation outsourcing to RSU and MEC. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Experiments at 128-bit security.
