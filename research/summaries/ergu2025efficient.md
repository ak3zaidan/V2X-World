# Efficient Adversarial Attacks Against DRL-Based Resource Allocation in Intelligent O-RAN for V2X
Yared Abera Ergu, V. Nguyen, Ren-Hung Hwang, Ying-Dar Lin et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2024.3466511 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** DRL resource allocation in O-RAN near-RT RIC for V2X is vulnerable to adversarial attacks.
**Approach:** Observation-manipulation attack and policy infiltration via faked signal power.
**Tools and scale:** Simulated O-RAN V2X.
**Results:** Up to 40% decline in data rates, 77.74% reduction in packet delivery.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — O-RAN ML attacks.
**Novelty implications:** None.
