# Spatio-temporal change of wheat yield and its quantitative responses to compound drought-frost events - An example of the Huang-Huai-Hai Plain of China from 2001 to 2020.
Yan-Xia Zhao, Liu-Jun Xiao, Yining Tang, Xia Yao et al. · Science of the Total Environment 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scitotenv.2024.173531 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Extreme climate events such as frost and drought have great influence on wheat growth and yield.
**Approach:** In this study, wheat yield prediction model (SCYMvp) was developed by combining crop growth model (CGM), satellite images and meteorological variables.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** Wheat yield maps in the Huang-Huai-Hai Plain (HHHP) during 2001-2020 were generated using SCYMvp model. Wheat yield was negatively correlated with spring ECEs, and yield loss was between 4.6 and 49.8 kg/ha for each 1 d increase of spring ECEs.
