# Extensible Co-Simulation Framework for Supporting Cooperative Driving Automation Research
Defu Cui, Yuzhong Shen, Hong Yang, Zhitong Huang, Kyle Rush, Peter Huang et al. · Transportation Research Record: Journal of the Transportation Research Board 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1177/03611981221121263 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Existing simulators under-support sensing and communication functions needed to test AVs and cooperative driving automation.
**Approach:** Open-source co-simulation of Eclipse MOSAIC, SUMO and CARLA for AV/CAV testing in mixed traffic, extensible with network simulators for V2X.
**Tools and scale:** MOSAIC, SUMO, CARLA; tested on different network and traffic scales.
**Results:** Supports faster-than-real-time simulation for accelerated tests; shown extensible to V2X network simulators (no numbers in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — multi-tool traffic + AV + V2X co-simulation; no PKI/security.
**Novelty implications:** Co-simulation of traffic, driving and V2X is established; security ground truth remains our differentiator.
