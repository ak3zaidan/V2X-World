# Network Self-Configuration based on Fine-Tuned Small Language Models
Oscar G. Lira, Oscar M. Caicedo, Nelson L. S. Da Fonseca · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02861 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** LLM self-configuration is expensive and privacy-invasive.
**Approach:** SLM_netconfig fine-tuned small models with agents.
**Tools and scale:** Dataset from vendor docs.
**Results:** Higher syntactic and goal accuracy than LLM-NetCFG with lower latency.
