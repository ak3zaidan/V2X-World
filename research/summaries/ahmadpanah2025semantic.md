# Semantic-aware LLM orchestration for proactive resource management in predictive digital twin vehicular networks
Seyed Hossein Ahmadpanah · Computing 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s00607-025-01609-6 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Vehicular edge management is reactive and goal-static.
**Approach:** SP-LLM: LLM orchestrator using a predictive DT's forecasts, steered by natural-language commands.
**Tools and scale:** Simulations.
**Results:** Outperforms reactive MARL and LLM baselines in scalability and robustness.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Authors acknowledge shortcomings and suggest future directions (not extracted in detail).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - LLM operating on a vehicular DT via natural language.
**Novelty implications:** LLM orchestration of vehicular DTs exists for resource management; not for security analysis.
