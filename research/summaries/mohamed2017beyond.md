# Beyond passive detection of sybil attacks in VANET
Mohamed S. Mohamed, Prajjwol Dandekhya, Axel Krings · 2017 6th International Conference on Reliability, Infocom Technologies and Optimization (Trends and Future Directions) (ICRITO) 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icrito.2017.8342456 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Passive Sybil detection fails under dynamic transmit power.
**Approach:** Active Sybil detection with short detection packets.
**Tools and scale:** Field tests with Arada LocoMate OBUs.
**Results:** Capable of detection in dynamic power environments.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — PHY Sybil detection with real DSRC.
**Novelty implications:** None.
