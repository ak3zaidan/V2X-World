**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) represent a transformative approach to vehicular communication, offering applications in road safety, traffic management, and infotainment. The dynamic and open characteristics of VANETs make them vulnerable to numerous security challenges, including tampering with messages, unauthorized interception of communications, and Sybil attacks.
