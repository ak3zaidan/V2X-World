# Automated Digital Twin Generation for Network Testing: A Multi-Topology Validation
Shen-Jia Ding, David Flynn, Paul Harvey · ICC 2026 - IEEE International Conference on Communications 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc59461.2026.11587859 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Network testing relies on manual configuration and slow simulators.
**Approach:** AutoML-generated digital twin integrated with a network service controller.
**Tools and scale:** Multiple topologies.
**Results:** High accuracy and about 25,000 times faster than simulator-based testing.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — learned twin replacing a simulator for what-if testing (direction 3) in generic networks.
**Novelty implications:** Large speed-ups from learned twins are shown; V2X safety-relevant surrogate remains open.
