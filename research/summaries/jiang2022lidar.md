# LiDAR Aided Future Beam Prediction in Real-World Millimeter Wave V2I Communications
Shuai-Feng Jiang, Gouranga Charan, Ahmed Alkhateeb · IEEE Wireless Communications Letters 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/lwc.2022.3219409 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This letter presents the first large-scale real-world evaluation for using LiDAR data to guide the mmWave beam prediction task.
**Approach:** A machine learning (ML) model that leverages LiDAR sensory data to predict the current and future beams was developed. Based on the large-scale real-world dataset, DeepSense 6G, this model was evaluated in a vehicle-to-infrastructure communication scenario with highly-mobile vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: dataset, real-world; scale not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The experimental results show that the developed LiDAR-aided beam prediction and tracking model can predict the optimal beam in 95% of the cases and with around 90% reduction in the beam training overhead. The LiDAR-aided beam tracking achieves comparable accuracy performance to a baseline solution that has perfect knowledge of the previous optimal beams, without requiring any knowledge about the previous optimal beam information and without any need for beam calibration.
