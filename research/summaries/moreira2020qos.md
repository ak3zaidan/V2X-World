# QoS Predictability in V2X Communication with Machine Learning
D. C. Moreira, I. Guerreiro, Wan-Lu Sun, C. Cavalcante, D. A. Sousa · IEEE Vehicular Technology Conference 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2020-spring48590.2020.9129490 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Predicting whether C-V2X packets meet latency constraints.
**Approach:** Supervised learning and ARIMA predicting in-time packet delivery.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation of cellular V2X.
**Results:** QoS levels predicted 2 s ahead with 85% reliability.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Open questions: which data and features are available, prediction quality.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
