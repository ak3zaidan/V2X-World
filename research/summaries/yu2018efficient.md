# An Efficient SDN-Based DDoS Attack Detection and Rapid Response Platform in Vehicular Networks
Yao Yu, Lei Guo, Y. Liu, Jian Zheng et al. · IEEE Access 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2018.2854567 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the prosperity of wireless networks, vehicular networks (VNs) have been extensively studied in recent years. It is deployed to ensure road safety, enhance the driving experience, and reduce traffic congestion.
**Approach:** In this paper, we design a platform to efficiently detect and rapidly respond to the DDoS attack in VNs based on software-defined networking (SDN).
**Tools and scale:** By analyzing the simulation results, we verify that the detection scheme effectively reduces the time for starting attack detection and classification recognition and has a lower false alarm rate.
