# On Handling of Certificate Digest in V2X Communication
Takahito Yoshizawa, Bart Preneel · 2022 18th International Conference on Wireless and Mobile Computing, Networking and Communications (WiMob) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wimob55322.2022.9941683 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Is 1609.2/ETSI inline P2PCD useful?
**Approach:** Simulation of real-world highway traffic flow evaluating P2PCD.
**Tools and scale:** Highway traffic simulation.
**Results:** Benefit negligible on highways while adding processing burden.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Proposes restricting P2PCD to traffic environments where it brings benefit
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — Evaluates P2PCD, which our engine implements over the air.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first P2PCD evaluation; urban/PQ and safety analysis open.
