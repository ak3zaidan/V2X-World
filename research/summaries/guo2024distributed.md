# Distributed finite-time attack detection and estimation of intelligent connected vehicle platoon
Shenghui Guo, Li Chen, Darong Huang, Jiafeng Song · Journal of Control and Decision 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1080/23307706.2024.2381657 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper presents a distributed finite-time observer design approach for the problem of intelligent connected vehicles (ICVs) containing both attack signals and unknown disturbances. Firstly, an analysis is conducted on the two types of network attacks that ICVs may encounter, followed by the construction of a dynamic vehicle model and its transformation into a state space equation.
**Approach:** Secondly, an intermediate variable is introduced to design a distributed attack estimation observer, which both ensures accurate estimation of the vehicle safety state and attack signal within a limited time and overcomes the constraints of traditional observers. Furthermore, an adaptive threshold attack detection method is designed.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in abstract.
**Results:** Finally, the simulation results validate the efficiency of the design methods.
