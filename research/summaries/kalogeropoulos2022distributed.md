# A Distributed Model for Privacy Preserving V2I Communication with Strong Unframeability and Efficient Revocation
Panayiotis Kalogeropoulos, Dimitris Papanikas, Panayiotis Kotzanikolaou · Journal of Cybersecurity and Privacy 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/jcp2040040 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** Privacy-preserving V2I requires trusting a single authority.
**Approach:** Distributed trust between CA and signing authority; partially blind signatures; certificateless revocation; strong unframeability.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation of efficiency.
**Results:** Efficient for realistic scenarios (no numbers in extract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- V2I only.
- future V2V extension and reducing trust in signing authority.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Separation-of-duties privacy like SCMS LA/PCA split.
**Novelty implications:** Distributed-trust V2I scheme; SCMS-level comparison open.
