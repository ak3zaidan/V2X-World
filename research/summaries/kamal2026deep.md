# Deep learning-based multi-class misbehavior classification in fog-enabled vehicular ad hoc networks
Hadeer Kamal, Amira Y. Haikal, Mahmoud M. Saafan · Scientific Reports 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-66695-8 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Multi-class misbehaviour classification at fog servers.
**Approach:** LSTM/CNN hybrid architectures.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi Extension.
**Results:** High classification performance (figures in paper).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Increase type-17 messages; evaluate in large-scale fog deployments with full network simulation (SUMO with NS-3 or OMNeT++) covering RSU capacity, throughput, latency and channel congestion.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — explicitly calls for network-level evaluation, which our engine provides.
**Novelty implications:** Supports that MBD deployment costs in full simulation remain open.
