# Intrusion Detection System for Energy Efficient Cluster Based Vehicular Adhoc Networks
R. Lavanya, S. Kannan · Intelligent Automation and Soft Computing 2022 · https://doi.org/10.32604/iasc.2022.021467 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A vehicular Adhoc Network, a subfield of Mobile Adhoc Network is defined by its high mobility and by demonstrating dissimilar mobility patterns.
**Approach:** So, VANET clustering techniques are needed with the consideration of the mobility parameters amongst nearby nodes to construct stable clustering techniques. At the same time, security is also a major design issue in VANET which can be resolved by the Intrusion Detection Systems.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** In order to investigate the performance of the FLC-OFSVM model, an extensive set of simulations are carried out and the results are investigated in terms of several performance measures.
