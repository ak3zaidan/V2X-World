# Context Contamination in LLM Analysis of Network Security Logs: Poison with Passive Prompt Injection and Mitigation Evaluation
Rabimba Karanjai, Yang Lu, Hemanth Hegadehalli Madhavarao, Lei Xu et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14493 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Prompt injection via log fields into LLM SOC analysis.
**Approach:** LogInject framework and benchmark (12,847 entries, 2,569 adversarial); Context Stitching.
**Tools and scale:** Three production LLMs.
**Results:** Up to 88.2% attack success (83.4% average).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** Relevant risk for any LLM analysing V2X logs containing attacker-controlled fields.
