# LEO Satellite Communication Simulation Framework for Connected Vehicles
Jing Ma, Lei Zhong, Ryokichi Onishi · GLOBECOM 2023 - 2023 IEEE Global Communications Conference 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/globecom54140.2023.10437236 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Evaluating LEO satellite communication for connected vehicles is costly.
**Approach:** Integrated satellite mobility, vehicle mobility and network simulation; synthetic constellation over Japan.
**Tools and scale:** Integrated simulators.
**Results:** Performance comparable to Starlink in three scenarios.
