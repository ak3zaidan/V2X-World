# Transmission Range Adjustment Influence on Location Privacy-Preserving Schemes in VANETs
Messaoud Babaghayou, Nabila Labraoui · 2019 International Conference on Networking and Advanced Systems (ICNAS) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icnas.2019.8807823 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs) are in the rollout phase.
**Approach:** VANETs are mainly instantiated to mitigate the number of crashes and fatalities by enabling an intensive beaconing that contains the fine-grained location of each vehicle at the aim of creating environmental awareness for safety purposes. However, this frequent location information may be obtained by adversaries after overhearing the beacons; giving an unauthenticated entity(s) the ability to monitor the vehicles' whereabouts in a region of interest.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: location privacy).
**Results:** We made an evaluation of this feature's performances after integrating it into some of the well-known strategies, namely: SLOW and CAPS, against (1) the achieved location privacy using the traceability metric and (2) the network performances. The results show the beneficial usability of such a feature.
