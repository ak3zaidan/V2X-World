# Sybil Attack Detection in Urban VANETs Based on RSU Support
Hamid Hamed, Alireza Keshavarz-Haddad, Shapour Golbahar Haghighi · Electrical Engineering (ICEE), Iranian Conference on 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icee.2018.8472629 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil detection in urban VANETs.
**Approach:** RSU-supported scheme: two identities seen simultaneously at multiple RSUs suggest Sybil; observations at distinct RSUs indicate different vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Outperforms existing solutions in detection rate, false positives and requirements.

- None stated in abstract.
