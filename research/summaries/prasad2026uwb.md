# UWB-Assisted Cooperative Situational Awareness for Connected Vehicles: A Secure Proximity, Authentication, and Risk-awareness Framework
Rupesh Prasad Prasad · unknown venue 2026 · https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7485559 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Connected vehicles use situational awareness as these three sources mainly to build grounded on broadcast state messages, onboard sensors and infrastructure observations.
**Approach:** These are good sources but create a consequential gap: an authenticated asserting identity can be done by a signed message but does not tell us that the asserting identity is where it claims to be. In this paper, a secure collaborative situational-awareness is established, wherein the ultra-wideband (UWB) time-of-flight ranging is seen as a proximity evidence channel and not as a channel of truth on its own.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: ISO/SAE 21434 / TARA).
**Results:** The framework secures short-lived pseudonymous vehicle credentials to authenticated UWB ranging transcripts, range-checks range, range rate, map geometry, and calibrates kinematic state, converts the resulting consistency evidence into a risk score. Some of the threats include replay, relay, Sybil behavior, distance reduction, non-line-of-sight bias, jamming, compromised insiders, and privacy leakage, which are all covered in a threat model.
