# Temporal-Convolutional Adversarial Autoencoding With Channel-Wise Attention for Intrusion Detection in the Internet of Vehicles
A. Heidari, Abdulnasir Hossen, Rami Al-Hmouz, Majdi Mansouri · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2026.3676855 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Internet of Vehicles (IoV) lets cars talk to one another in smart ways by sharing data in real time between vehicles, infrastructure, and edge nodes.
**Approach:** But as more and more parts become linked, the danger of advanced attacks rises. At the same time, traditional Intrusion Detection Systems (IDSs) typically cannot keep up with IoV’s changing, low-latency, and resource-limited needs.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Our proposed VITA framework shows superior performance across several critical criteria, including an average 30% reduction in latency compared to state-of-the-art models. VITA achieves an average 8% improvement in detection accuracy, effectively identifying a broad spectrum of vehicular attacks, such as replay, Global Positioning System (GPS) spoofing, and Denial-of-Service (DoS) attacks.
