# Evaluating the impact of adversarial traffic patterns on vanet communication using veins simulation
Henry Agyapong · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14583 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Impact of adversarial traffic on VANET performance.
**Approach:** Flooding, false information and coordinated congestion scenarios at varying density.
**Tools and scale:** Veins with OMNeT++ and SUMO.
**Results:** PDR reduced by up to 96.55%; low-density flooding cuts throughput 27.89%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Study hybrid simultaneous attack strategies.
- Develop detection and countermeasures.
- Evaluate in large-scale and geographically diverse networks.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — attacks in network simulation, network metrics only.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
