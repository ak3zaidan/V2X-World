# ZSM-Based E2E Security Slice Management for DDoS Attack Protection in MEC-Enabled V2X Environments
Rodrigo Asensio-Garriga, Pol Alemany, Alejandro Molina Zarca, Roshan Sedar et al. · IEEE Open Journal of Vehicular Technology 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ojvt.2024.3375448 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Research on vehicle-to-everything (V2X) is attracting significant attention nowadays, driven by the recent advances in beyond-5G (B5G) networks and the multi-access edge computing (MEC) paradigm. However, the inherent heterogeneity of B5G combined with the security vulnerabilities of MEC infrastructure in dynamic V2X scenarios introduces unprecedented challenges.
**Approach:** We consider an entire 5G network, including vehicular user equipment, radio access networks, MEC, and core components, in the presence of DDoS targeting V2X services. This work is part of ETSI ZSM PoC “security SLA assurance in 5G network slices”.
**Tools and scale:** C-V2X/LTE-V2X/5G (mentioned in abstract)
