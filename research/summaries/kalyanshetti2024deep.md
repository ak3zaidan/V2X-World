# Deep Learning-Based Blockage Prediction For Intelligent Reflecting Surfaces-Aided V2X Networks
Ritika S. Kalyanshetti, Vaishnavi Kulkarni, Manasadevi Jagannath, A. P. Yashaswi et al. · 2024 International Conference on Wireless Communications Signal Processing and Networking (WiSPNET) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wispnet61464.2024.10532860 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Predicting blockage in IRS-assisted mmWave V2X links.
**Approach:** CNN, LSTM and GRU predictors.
**Tools and scale:** ViWi vision-aided wireless dataset.
**Results:** LSTM gave best average accuracy and lowest RMSE; few active IRS elements increase SNR and accuracy.
