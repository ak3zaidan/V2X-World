# Explainable Deep Learning-Enabled Malware Attack Detection for IoT-Enabled Intelligent Transportation Systems
Mohammad Wazid, Jaskaran Singh, Charvi Pandey, R. Simon Sherratt, Ashok Kumar Das, Debasis Giri, Youngho Park · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2025.3525505 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Internet of Things (IoT) has the potential to improve the complementary of communication, control, and information processing within the public transportation system. The IoT-enabled Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) ensures that automated transportation is networked and operated collaboratively.
**Approach:** We propose a secure deep learning-enabled malware attack detection for IoT-enabled ITS (in short, SDLMA-IITS).
