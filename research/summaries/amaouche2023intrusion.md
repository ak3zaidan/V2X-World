**Problem:** VANET Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks are becoming a very important topic for improving the quality of daily life in terms of comfort, safety, and minimizing the risk of accidents.
**Approach:** VANETs are connected to the internet, which means that they offer an increasing number of communication options, particularly vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I) and vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V), which makes the networks extremely sensitive to different forms of attacks. Intrusion detection systems (IDSs) play an important part in protecting VANETs by identifying malicious vehicles.
