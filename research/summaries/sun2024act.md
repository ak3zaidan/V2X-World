# To Act or Not to Act: An Adversarial Game for Securing Vehicle Platoons
Guoxin Sun, Tansu Alpcan, Benjamin I. P. Rubinstein, Seyit Camtepe · IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tifs.2023.3320610 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ML detectors evadable by intelligent adversaries in platoons.
**Approach:** Non-cooperative security game with imperfect information plus switched-system mitigation.
**Tools and scale:** Sophisticated simulator.
**Results:** Up to 87% reduction in distance disturbance vs baseline.
