# Machine Learning-Enabled Data Rate Prediction for 5G NSA Vehicle-to-Cloud Communications
Benjamin Sliwa, Hendrik Schippers, Christian Wietfeld · 2021 IEEE 4th 5G World Forum (5GWF) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/5gwf52925.2021.00059 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Client-based end-to-end data-rate prediction for 5G NSA vehicle-to-cloud.
**Approach:** First empirical analysis using conventional ML on locally acquirable measurements.
**Tools and scale:** Real 5G NSA drive measurements.
**Results:** Comparably accurate estimates despite massive data-rate fluctuations (numbers not extracted).
