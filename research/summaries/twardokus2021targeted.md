# Targeted Discreditation Attack against Trust Management in Connected Vehicles
Geoff Twardokus, Jaime Ponicki, S. Baker, Peter Carenzo et al. · ICC 2021 - IEEE International Conference on Communications 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc42927.2021.9500246 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Misbehaviour detection counting signature verification failures can be abused.
**Approach:** Stealthy reactive jammer corrupting victim's signatures to discredit it; open-source USRP testbed.
**Tools and scale:** USRP-based testbed with IEEE 1609.2.
**Results:** Victim discredited in prominent MBD schemes with two minutes of jamming.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Mitigation is challenging
- discussed testbed extensibility
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — Cross-layer attack (radio jamming) against 1609.2 misbehaviour detection, i.e. MBD-to-revocation false positives; relevant to direction 1 and NR attacks runner-up.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first framing/discreditation attack against MBD; open: its effect on SCMS MA decisions and safety in closed loop.
