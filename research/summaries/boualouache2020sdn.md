# SDN-based Misbehavior Detection System for Vehicular Networks
Abdelwahab Boualouache, Ridha Soua, Thomas Engel · 2020 IEEE 91st Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2020-Spring) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2020-spring48590.2020.9128604 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Static MDSs do not adapt to context.
**Approach:** SDN-based context-aware MDS tuning security parameters; Sybil-resistant and privacy-standard compliant.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Higher detection and lower false positives than static MDS.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — context-dependent detection thresholds (direction 2).
**Novelty implications:** Adaptive thresholds known.
