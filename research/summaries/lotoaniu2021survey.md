# A Survey of Attacks Over Controller Area Networks and Potential Countermeasures
Sione Talia'uli Fatai Loto'aniu · Tuwhera (Auckland University of Technology) 2021 · https://hdl.handle.net/10292/14137 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Automotive manufacturers have a specialized internal communication network, called vehicle bus which connects the Electronic Control Units (ECU) in a motor vehicle through a single or multiple bus architecture.
**Approach:** The CAN bus protocol is designed to be lightweight, robust, fast and secure. A CAN simulator is used for software development is then mounted and tested for functionality.
**Tools and scale:** A CAN simulator is used for software development is then mounted and tested for functionality.
**Results:** The research findings show that the inbuilt security functions are effective on a limited range of attacks.
