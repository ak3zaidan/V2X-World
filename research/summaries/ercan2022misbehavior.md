# Misbehavior Detection for Position Falsification Attacks in VANETs Using Machine Learning
Secil Ercan, Marwane Ayaida, Nadhir Messai · IEEE Access 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2021.3136706 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Position falsification detection uses the same features centrally.
**Approach:** Three new sender-position features with kNN, RF and ensemble learning, distributed IDS.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi-type data.
**Results:** Improved detection (numbers in full text).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future work: add a reaction mechanism (notifications, passive or active revocation).
- Future work: other attack types (DoS, DDoS, Sybil, black hole).
- Future work: multi-class detection of attack type.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — distributed MBD; their missing reaction/revocation is what direction 1 adds.
**Novelty implications:** Explicit call for reaction mechanisms supports direction 1's gap.
