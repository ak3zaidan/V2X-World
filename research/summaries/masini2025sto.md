# Sto-CAV: A Distributed Simulation Platform for Connected and Automated Vehicles Running With Heterogeneous Technologies and Devices in the Loop
Barbara M. Masini, Mirko Mirabella, Leonardo Donnini, Muhammad Ali Umair, Marco Meucci, Mauro Aresti et al. · IEEE Access 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2025.3572358 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular application testing is costly and logistically hard.
**Approach:** Sto-CAV distributed remote-access platform with heterogeneous RATs (ITS-G5, LTE-V2X, VLC) and devices in the loop.
**Tools and scale:** Geographically distributed virtual and physical components.
**Results:** Average latencies suitable for quasi-real-time applications.
