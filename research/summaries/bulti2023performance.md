# On the performance of multiple association with mobility support in 5G ultra‐dense networks: Realistic network simulation
Dinkisa A. Bulti, Yihenew Wondie · IET Communications 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1049/cmu2.12576 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract A user equipment (UE) in ultra‐dense networks (UDN) has a high chance of being in close proximity to many cells.
**Approach:** multiple association (MA) can be used to serve users with high data rate demands in such a deployment scenario. However, because of the small footprint of the low power node (LPN), mobile users may experience highly frequent cell re‐association issues.
**Tools and scale:** Non-vehicular or unspecified setting. The available text does not describe a V2X simulator, radio stack, traffic model or SCMS-style PKI in enough detail to report; scale not stated.
**Results:** This approach goes with mobility management in long term evolution advanced (LTE‐A)/fifth generation (5G) and can easily be integrated with the existing system. In the realistic network evaluation, up to 65.7% decrease in re‐association frequency was obtained for the UEs average speed of 90 Km/h with cluster size .
