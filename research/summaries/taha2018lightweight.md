# Lightweight Group Authentication with Dynamic Vehicle-Clustering for 5G-Based V2X Communications
Sanaa Taha, Xuemin Shen · Global Communications Conference 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/glocom.2018.8647864 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** However, achieving such low latency with high mobility in vehicular networks is a challenge.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a lightweight group authentication scheme for 5G- based V2X communications using three different vehicles' roles in each cluster: an ordinary vehicle, Cluster Head (CH), and gateway vehicle. To increase cluster connectivity time, our clustering algorithm is developed based on three metrics: cluster's relative velocity, relative position, and received signal strength.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** The security analysis and performance evaluation show that our proposed scheme thwarts inside and outside authentication attacks, and achieves up to 50% less computation overhead than those achieved in the existing schemes.
