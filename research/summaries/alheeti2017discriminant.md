# Using discriminant analysis to detect intrusions in external communication for self-driving vehicles
Khattab M. Ali Alheeti, Anna Gruebler, Klaus Dieter McDonald-Maier · Digital Communications and Networks 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcan.2017.03.001 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Security systems are a necessity for the deployment of smart vehicles in our society. Security in vehicular ad hoc networks is crucial to the reliable exchange of information and control data.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose an intelligent Intrusion Detection System (IDS) to protect the external communication of self-driving and semi self-driving vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** We perform simulations using Network Simulator 2 to demonstrate that the IDS achieves a low rate of false alarms and high accuracy in detection.
