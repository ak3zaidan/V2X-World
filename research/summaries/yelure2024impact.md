# Impact and Analysis of Attacks on Routing Protocols in Vehicular Ad hoc Network (VANET): Assessing Security Threats
Bhushan S. Yelure, Arun M. Patokar, Siddheshwar Vilas Patil, Rajesh Mawale, Sangita B. Nemade, Varsha P. Gaikwad · IEIE Transactions on Smart Processing and Computing 2024 · https://doi.org/10.5573/ieiespc.2024.13.3.294 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-hoc networks have gained substantial interest from researchers in recent times.
**Approach:** They offer the potential to deploy different applications utilized by intelligent transportation systems, which could improve traffic control and ensure road safety. The vehicle has a significant position within a Vehicular Ad hoc network system.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** In the absence of an attack, the AODV protocol demonstrates an improved collision ratio and packet delivery.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- In comparison to a Sybil attack, other forms of attacks, such as black-hole, gray-hole, and rushing attacks, have a greater influence on the performance of AODV.
