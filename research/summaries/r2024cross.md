**Problem:** As secure communication over these networks is crucial, this overview looks at recent advances in quantum cryptography in addition to a range of conventional cryptographic techniques, such as symmetric and asymmetric encryption.
**Approach:** This paper presents a thorough overview of both classical and quantum cryptography in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs), Which are becoming an increasingly important components of smart transportation system. We analyze these methods in terms of security, effectiveness, and usability for VANET scenarios through review of the entirety of available literature.
