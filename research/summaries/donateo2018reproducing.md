# Reproducing Real World Emission Tests with a Traffic Simulator
Teresa Donateo, Mattia Giovinazzi, Antonio Tamborrino · SAE Technical Paper Series 2018 · https://doi.org/10.4271/2018-37-0001 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** &lt;div class="section abstract"&gt;&lt;div class="htmlview paragraph"&gt;This investigation analyzes the feasibility of using a traffic simulator, and in particular the open source software SUMO (Simulation of Urban Mobility) to reproduce speed profiles acquired under Real Driving Emission (RDE) tests.
**Approach:** The first step of the investigation describes the experimental tests performed in Lecce to obtain RDE cycles with a Class3b vehicle. Several tests are executed with the same vehicle over the same route with the same driver.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting. The available text does not describe a V2X simulator, radio stack, traffic model or SCMS-style PKI in enough detail to report; scale not stated.
**Results:** The first step of the investigation describes the experimental tests performed in Lecce to obtain RDE cycles with a Class3b vehicle. The experimental tests also revealed the possibility to correlate CO&lt;sub&gt;2&lt;/sub&gt; emissions with either the specification of the cycle (speed and acceleration of the vehicle) or the engine working points (load and speed).
