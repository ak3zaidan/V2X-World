# Towards Next Generation of Pedestrian and Connected Vehicle In-the-loop Research: A Digital Twin Co-Simulation Framework
Zijin Wang, Ou Zheng, Liangding Li, Mohamed Abdel-Aty, Carolina Cruz-Neira, Zubayer Islam · arXiv 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tiv.2023.3250353 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Digital Twin is an emerging technology that replicates real-world entities into a digital space.
**Approach:** It has attracted increasing attention in the transportation field and many researchers are exploring its future applications in the development of Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) technologies. Connected vehicles (CVs) and pedestrians are among the major traffic participants in ITS.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: Carla, real-world, Sumo, testbed; scale not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** No quantitative results stated in the abstract.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- The proposed framework is expected to provide guidance to the future Digital Twin research, and the architecture we build can serve as the testbed for further research and development of ITS applications on CV and pedestrians.
