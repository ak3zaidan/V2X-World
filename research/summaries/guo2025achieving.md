# Achieving Multi-Attribute Superiority and Sybil Attack Detection in IoV: A Heuristic-Based Dynamic RSU Deployment Scheme
Hongzhi Guo, Xinhan Wu, Zishuo Yin, Bomin Mao, Yi-Jie Xun, Jiajia Liu et al. · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2024.3509980 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** RSU deployment ignoring Sybil detection constraints.
**Approach:** PSO-meme heuristic deployment (PJHDA) and multi-objective adjustment (HRMA3) accounting for Sybil detection.
**Tools and scale:** Numerical evaluation.
**Results:** Better multi-attribute performance, coverage and load balancing, higher Sybil detection rates than benchmarks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — RSU placement vs security.
**Novelty implications:** RSU placement for security exists.
