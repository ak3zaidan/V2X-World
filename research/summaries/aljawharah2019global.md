# Global Roaming Trust-based Model for V2X Communications
Alnasser Aljawharah, Sun Hongjian · arXiv 2019 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.12381 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Smart cities need to connect physical devices as a network to improve the efficiency of city operations and services.
**Approach:** In this paper, a global roaming trust-based security model is proposed for V2X communications. In addition, various experiments are conducted with different percentage of malicious nodes to measure the performance of the proposed model.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation results show that the proposed model improves False Negative Rate (FNR) by 33.5% in comparison with the existing method.
**Results:** Smart cities need to connect physical devices as a network to improve the efficiency of city operations and services. Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) is one of the key components in smart cities, due to its capability of supporting communications between vehicles to improve the driving experience.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "Whilst Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) communications are essential, cyber-security poses a significant challenge in V2X communications."
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — addresses misbehaviour/attack detection (direction 1 area), but per the abstract without an integrated SCMS backend, closed-loop revocation, per-message joint ground truth or counterfactual reruns.
**Novelty implications:** Does not rule out any candidate direction on the evidence of the abstract. Leaves open quantifying this in a closed loop with real SCMS revocation and traffic safety outcomes.
