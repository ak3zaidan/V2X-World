# Conclave - Secure and Robust Cooperative Perception for Connected Autonomous Vehicle Using Authenticated Consensus and Trust Scoring
Edward Andert, Francis Mendoza, Hans Walter Behrens, Aviral Shrivastava · Proceedings of the 61st ACM/IEEE Design Automation Conference 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3649329.3658491 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** Cooperative perception security and reliability.
**Approach:** CONClave: authentication, consensus and trust scoring pipeline.
**Tools and scale:** Cooperative perception tests.
**Results:** Detects 96.7% of 300 extrinsic-error tests; faster than TruPercept.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: all cooperative driving scenarios including path plans.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Authenticated cooperative perception.
**Novelty implications:** None.
