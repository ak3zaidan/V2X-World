# Vehicle Platooning Based on Second-Order Pinning Consensus Control and Its Cyber Attack Detection
Ikumi Kidokoro, Koichi Kobayashi, Yuh Yamashita · 2025 IEEE International Conference on Consumer Electronics (ICCE) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icce63647.2025.10929809 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we propose a vehicle platooning method based on pinning consensus control and its cyber attack detection.
**Approach:** The consensus problem, in which the state of each agent is matched through communications, is a well-known control problem. Pinning control is a method of adding external control inputs to pre-specified agents called pinning agents.
