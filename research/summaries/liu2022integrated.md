# Integrated Simulation Platform for Internet of Vehicles
Xing Liu, Wei Yu, Cheng Qian, David Griffith, Nada Golmie · ICC 2022 - IEEE International Conference on Communications 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc45855.2022.9838333 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** IoT simulators simulate only subsets (network or mobility).
**Approach:** Framework integrating 3D object motion with OMNeT++ using ray-traced received power.
**Tools and scale:** OMNeT++, ray tracing; two IoV scenarios.
**Results:** Accurately simulates physical and communication aspects (qualitative).

- Not stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — integrated mobility + ray-traced radio.
