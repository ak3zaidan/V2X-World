# The Key to Intelligent Transportation Systems: Identity and Credential Management for Secure and Privacy-Preserving Vehicular Communication Systems
Mohammad Khodaei · KTH Publication Database DiVA (KTH Royal Institute of Technology) 2020 · https://openalex.org/W3035417242 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Communication (VC) systems can greatly enhance road safety and transportation efficiency and enable a variety of applications providing traffic efficiency, environmental hazards, road conditions and infotainment.
**Approach:** Vehicles are equipped with sensors and radars to sense their surroundings and external environment, as well as with an internal Controller Area Network (CAN) bus. Hence, vehicles are becoming part of a large-scale network, the so-called Internet of Vehicles (IoV).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: location privacy).
**Results:** Towards that, we provide a secure and privacy-preserving VPKI design that improves upon existing proposals in terms of security and privacy protection and efficiency. Our results confirm the efficiency, scalability and robustness of our VPKI.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- We point out the remaining challenges to be addressed in order to build a central building block of secure and privacy-preserving VC systems, a Vehicular Public-Key Infrastructure (VPKI).
