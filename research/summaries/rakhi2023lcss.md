# LCSS Based Sybil Attack Detection and Avoidance in Clustered Vehicular Networks
S. Rakhi, K. R. Shobha · IEEE Access 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2023.3294469 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil detection in clustered VANETs incl. power control.
**Approach:** Cluster head compares RSS with LCSS similarity; change point detection for power control.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Improved detection rate, delay and false positives.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- LCSS fails under power control (addressed by CPD).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
