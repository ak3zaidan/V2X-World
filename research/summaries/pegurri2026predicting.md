# Predicting Networks Before They Happen: Experimentation on a Real-Time V2X Digital Twin
Roberto Pegurri, Habu Shintaro, Francesco Linsalata, Wang Kui, Tao Yu, Eugenio Moro et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16559 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** NDTs struggle to combine high-fidelity physics with real-time constraints.
**Approach:** Real-time V2X DT coupling Tokyo Mobility DT (live sensing, trajectory forecasting) with VaN3Twin ray tracing.
**Tools and scale:** Proof of concept in Tokyo; 60 GHz links.
**Results:** RSSI max average error 1.01 dB; LoS transitions forecast within 250 ms end-to-end latency.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: real-time prediction of higher-layer network metrics using full-stack simulation.
- Model complexity constrains feasible prediction horizon.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - V2X DT, PHY-focused.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on security directions.
