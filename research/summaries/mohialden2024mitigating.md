Yasmin Makki Mohialden, Nadia Mahmood Hussien, Samira Abdul-Kader Hussain · CyberSystem journal. 2024 · https://doi.org/10.57238/csj.9tjh0036 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As of now, maintaining the security and dependability of V2X communication remains an open issue due to the complexity and vulnerability of wireless networking technologies for Autonomous Vehicles (AVs). This study is conducted to address the research gap.
