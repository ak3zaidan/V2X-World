# Per Link Data-Driven Network Replication Towards Self-Adaptive Digital Twins
Samir Si-Mohammed, Fabrice Theoleyre · 2025 International Conference on Modeling, Analysis and Simulation of Wireless and Mobile Systems (MSWiM) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mswim67937.2025.11308908 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Wireless models fall short in real environments without costly measurements.
**Approach:** Per-link PDR prediction models with dynamic adaptive model selection.
**Tools and scale:** Real small-scale deployment; public dataset.
**Results:** High short- and mid-term accuracy; per-link average-PDR calibration insufficient.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Evaluation limited to a small-scale testbed.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
