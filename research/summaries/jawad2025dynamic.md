# Dynamic key revocation and hybrid cryptographic approaches for secure authentication in the social internet of vehicles
Muhammad N. Jawad, ‪Mahmood A. Al-Shareeda‬‏, Omar Yawez Mustafa Mustafa, Mohammed Amin Almaiah et al. · International Journal of Innovative Research and Scientific Studies 2025 · https://doi.org/10.53894/ijirss.v8i2.5956 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** SIoV authentication suffers replay, session hijack and lacks quick key revocation.
**Approach:** ZKP plus AES-GCM authentication with dynamic session-key revocation and fog handoff.
**Tools and scale:** ROR security proof; performance evaluation.
**Results:** 58% lower authentication latency; 45% and 72% better communication and computation efficiency.

- None explicitly stated in extracted text.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — session-key revocation, not certificate revocation.
