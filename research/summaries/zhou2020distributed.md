# Distributed collaborative intrusion detection system for vehicular Ad Hoc networks based on invariant
Man Zhou, Lansheng Han, Hongwei Lu, Cai Fu · Computer Networks 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.comnet.2020.107174 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The characteristics of high mobility and rapid topology change of the Vehicle Ad Hoc Network (VANET) makes it vulnerable to various malicious attacks. The adversary utilizes the instability of the communication link induced by the frequent changes of topology structure to undermine the reliability and timeliness of vehicular communication, which raises serious security threats.
**Approach:** In this paper, a distributed collaborative intrusion detection system based on invariant called DCDIV is proposed to identify betray attacks in VANET.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The simulation results demonstrate that the DCDIV has higher detection rate, lower false alarm rate, and faster attack detection rate compared with existing methods, and ensures system security during the detection process.
