# Control of Vehicular Platoons: Stochastic Robustness Against Jamming Attacks
Hamed Rezaee, Thomas Parisini, Marios M. Polycarpou · IFAC-PapersOnLine 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ifacol.2020.12.1528 · relevance: low · basis: abstract only

**Problem:** Platoon control under stochastic jamming of communication and radar.
**Approach:** Constant time-gap controller with almost-sure regulation guarantees.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Robust to a wide range of jamming attacks under conditions.
