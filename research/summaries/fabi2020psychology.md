# A psychology-inspired trust model for emergency message transmission on the Internet of Vehicles (IoV)
A. Fabi, Sabu M. Thampi · International Journal of Computer Applications 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1080/1206212x.2020.1814557 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The future transportation systems demand an intelligent traffic system that can be achieved by connecting the vehicles to the Internet of Things (IoT) which in turn forms an Internet of Vehicles (IoV) network.
**Approach:** The capabilities of the IoV network need to be leveraged to intelligently deal with the current traffic situations. As there are many vehicles connected in the network, it is essential to monitor, track, manage, and communicate the connected device fleet in the IoV network.
