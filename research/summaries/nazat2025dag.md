# DAG-AD-NAVs: Novel Attacks and Defenses on Decentralized Networks of Autonomous Vehicles With Directed Acyclic Graph Structures
Sazid Nazat, Walaa Alayed, Mustafa Abdallah · IEEE Access 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2025.3631751 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Autonomous vehicles (AVs) provide major enhancements to transportation networks, but securing decentralized AV networks against attacks remains a critical challenge.
**Approach:** This paper introduces a novel framework for AV connectivity using Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) structures. We propose four different attack scenarios on DAG connectivity framework of AVs (including insider attacks like Attack-A and Attack-B, and isolation attacks like Attack-C and Attack-D).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The goal of these attacks is to demonstrate the vulnerabilities of these DAG-based AV networks. Our defense strategies reduce anomalous AVs by an average of 5.8x across attack scenarios, including 10x for binary tree, 3x for partial mesh, and 4.5x for attack Graph-A.
