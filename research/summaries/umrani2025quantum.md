# Quantum secure threshold password authenticated key exchange scheme for VANET
Alia Umrani, Paolo Palmieri · Cybersecurity 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1186/s42400-025-00489-9 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Single-server PAKE in VANETs is exposed to offline dictionary attacks.
**Approach:** Threshold PAKE combining threshold PKE (DMLWE) and AKC; password shares across n decryption servers.
**Tools and scale:** Standard-model proofs; parameter analysis.
**Results:** Two communication rounds; negligible decryption failure.

- Not stated in extracted text.
