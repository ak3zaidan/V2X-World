# Simulation Framework of Misbehavior Detection and Mitigation for Collective Perception Services
Jiahao Zhang, Ines Ben Jemaa, Fawzi Nashashibi · 2024 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iv55156.2024.10588853 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** Lack of platforms to evaluate CPM misbehaviour detection and mitigation.
**Approach:** Framework combining Artery, CARLA and intersection assistance app; attacks on CPM; MBD as mitigation; datasets.
**Tools and scale:** Artery, CARLA.
**Results:** Shows feasibility of CPM attacks and MBD impact on application resilience; provides reporting and perception datasets.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: subjective logic for omission and ghost attacks.
- other realistic trajectories.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — Closed-loop attack, detection and mitigation with application-level safety impact and misbehaviour reporting datasets.
**Novelty implications:** Closed-loop CPM MBD exists; PKI revocation reaction and SCMS-level time-to-neutralise remain.
