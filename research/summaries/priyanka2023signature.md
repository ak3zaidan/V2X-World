# Signature Based V2X Communication and Authentications Using Resourceful Signcryption and Optimised Ecc
j Priyanka, Ramakrishnan M · venue unknown 2023 · https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1397170/v1 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract Vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) serve as the backbone of the intelligent transportation system, connecting vehicles together (ITS). VANET is becoming more and more popular as the number of vehicles that are capable of interacting with one another grows.
**Approach:** In this work the secure communication has been designed with the Optimised ECC as well as Signcryption with duple pseudonyms generator.
**Tools and scale:** The work has been analysis with the other efficient protocol over VANET simulation environment with the performance metrics of beacon overhead, authentication delay and Unknown Neighbouring ratio.
**Results:** In VANET the communication has been done by V2V, V2I and V2X, since wireless networks are available, maintaining communication protection and privacy has become a major challenge for VANETs.
