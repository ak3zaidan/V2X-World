# $L^{2}S^{2}C^{2}$: Lattice-based Lightweight Scheme for Secure Communication in Controller Area Networks
Harsha Vasudev, Alessandro Brighente, Mauro Conti · IEEE Wireless Communications and Networking Conference 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wcnc61545.2025.10978117 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Controller Area Network (CAN) is a standard bus-based communication protocol for reliable Cyber-Physical Systems. For instance, in cars, around 100 Electronic Control Units (ECUs) are connected via CAN.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose $L^{2}S^{2}C^{2}$, a novel post-quantum authentication protocol that leverages Physically Unclonable Functions (PUF) and Lattice-based Cryptography (LBC).
