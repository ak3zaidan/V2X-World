# Detecting Subtle Cyberattacks on Adaptive Cruise Control Vehicles: A Machine Learning Approach
Tianyi Li, Mingfeng Shang, Shian Wang, Raphael Stern · IEEE Open Journal of Intelligent Transportation Systems 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ojits.2024.3522969 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Subtle attacks on ACC vehicles cause congestion, fuel and crash risks while evading detection.
**Approach:** Traffic modelling of control-command manipulation, data poisoning and DoS; micro and macro impact analysis; 1D-CNN GAN anomaly detector on trajectories.
**Tools and scale:** Car-following simulation; numerical results.
**Results:** Outperforms contemporary neural models in detecting all three stealthy attack types.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- New attack forms may emerge
- detector limited to fixed input time-series length (e.g. 2 s).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — attack impact on traffic flow and detection.
**Novelty implications:** Stealthy attack micro/macro impact studied.
