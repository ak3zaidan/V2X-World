# Securing Software-Defined Vehicular Network Architecture against DDoS attack
Houda Amari, Wassef Louati, Lyes Khoukhi, Lamia Hadrich Belguith · 2021 IEEE 46th Conference on Local Computer Networks (LCN) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/lcn52139.2021.9524953 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In the recent decades, Intelligent Transport Systems (ITS) attracted researchers’ great attention.
**Approach:** ITS plays a very important role in making citizens’ lives easier in term of mobility, safety, quality of life and security. Vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs) became an inseparable component of ITS.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** We propose a new VANET architecture called FCSDVN-ML, in which we combine three emerging paradigms: Software-Defined Network (SDN), Fog Computing (FC) and Machine Learning (ML) to improve security in VANETs.
