# FedFMSL: Federated Learning of Foundation Models With Sparsely Activated LoRA
Panlong Wu, Kangshuo Li, Ting Wang, Yanjie Dong, Victor C. M. Leung, Fangxin Wang · IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tmc.2024.3454634 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Foundation models (FMs) have shown great success in natural language processing, computer vision, and multimodal tasks.
**Approach:** FMs have a large number of model parameters, thus requiring a substantial amount of data to help optimize the model during the training. Federated learning has revolutionized machine learning by enabling collaborative learning from decentralized data while still preserving clients’ data privacy.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** We employ extensive experiments to verify the effectiveness of FedFMSL, results show that FedFMSL outperforms other SOTA baselines by up to 59.19% in default settings while tuning less than 0.3% parameters of the foundation model.
