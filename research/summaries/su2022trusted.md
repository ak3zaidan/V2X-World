# Trusted Blockchain‐Based Signcryption Protocol and Data Management for Authentication and Authorization in VANETs
Jinqi Su, Runtao Ren, Yinghao Li, Raymond Y. K. Lau et al. · Wireless Communications and Mobile Computing 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2022/9572992 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANETs) are the industrial cornerstone of intelligent transportation system (ITS), which are widely used in traffic management, automatic driving, and road optimization. With the expansion of the scale of the mobile ad hoc networks (MANETs) and smart vehicles (SV), VANETs will produce a large amount of data.
**Approach:** In order to solve the cybersecurity risks of large‐scale deployment of VANET, this paper proposes a trusted blockchain‐based signcryption protocol and data management (TB‐SCDM) for authentication and authorization (A&amp;A) in VANETs. In the existing attack model, TB‐SCDM can ensure the confidentiality and undeniability of information, as well as can effectively resist 51% attacks, eclipse attacks and double‐spending attacks, etc.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset, blockchain (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** In the existing attack model, TB‐SCDM can ensure the confidentiality and undeniability of information, as well as can effectively resist 51% attacks, eclipse attacks and double‐spending attacks, etc.
