# Detecting RSU Misbehavior in Vehicular Edge Computing
Nalam Venkata Abhishek, Teng Joon Lim, Biplab Sikdar, Ben Liang · 2019 IEEE/CIC International Conference on Communications in China (ICCC) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccchina.2019.8855952 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Compromised RSUs add delay by corrupting or dropping packets.
**Approach:** Vehicles send feedback on each RSU to a central server; aggregated trust vs threshold; detection of false-feedback vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Effective detection; impact of adversary parameters shown.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — RSU misbehaviour detection (our engine has RSU relay).
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
