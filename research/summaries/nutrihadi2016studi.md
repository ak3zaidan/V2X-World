# Studi Kinerja VANET Scenario Generators: SUMO dan VanetMobisim untuk Implementasi Routing Protocol AODV menggunakan Network Simulator 2 (NS-2)
Firdaus Nutrihadi · Jurnal Teknik ITS 2016 · https://doi.org/10.12962/j23373539.v5i1.14307 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Mobility generator choice for VANET simulation.
**Approach:** Compares SUMO and VanetMobiSim with AODV on NS-2 (grid and Surabaya map).
**Tools and scale:** NS-2, SUMO, VanetMobiSim.
**Results:** SUMO-AODV better in all three scenarios.
