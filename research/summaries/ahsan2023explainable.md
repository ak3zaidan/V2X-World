# An Explainable Ensemble-based Intrusion Detection System for Software-Defined Vehicle Ad-hoc Networks
Shakil Ibne Ahsan, Phil Legg, S M Iftekharul Alam · Cyber Security and Applications 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.csa.2025.100090 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Improving IDS reliability and explainability for VANETs.
**Approach:** Ensemble (RF and CatBoost, logistic regression meta-learner) with SHAP feature importance.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi dataset.
**Results:** Improved accuracy and fewer misclassifications vs prior works; compared to reported 98.5-99.35% baselines.
