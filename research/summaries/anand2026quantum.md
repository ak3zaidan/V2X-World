**Approach:** For future-proofing digital certificates against quantum attacks, this paper proposes a hybrid PKI model that harmoniously merges Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) and Quantum Key Distribution (QKD). We investigate viable PQC algorithms, including lattice-based and hash-based cryptography, in combination with QKD for secured key exchange.
