# Blockchain-Based Secure Trust Management Scheme for Internet of Vehicles Over Cyber-Physical System
Venkata Chunduri, Mahmood Alsaadi, Sachin Gupta, Tariq Ahamed Ahanger, Adapa Gopi, Faisal Yousef Alghayadh et al. · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2024.3485481 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The importance of vehicular networking technology has grown significantly with advancements in autonomous driving and intelligent transportation systems over cyber-physical systems (CPS).
**Approach:** Due to its open-access environment, vehicular networking presents significant security problems in assuring message reliability and vehicle trustworthiness. To overcome this problem, this study proposes a framework for managing trust in cars for safe transportation in cyber-physical systems (T-CPS).
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: blockchain
**Results:** The experimental results show clear enhancements in scalability and resilience using the proposed algorithm.
