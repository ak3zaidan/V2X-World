# Identity and Aggregate Signature-Based Authentication Protocol for IoD Deployment Military Drone
S. Jan, Habib Ullah Khan · IEEE Access 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2021.3110804 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the rapid miniaturization in sensor technology, ruddervator, arduino, and multi-rotor system, drone technology has fascinated researchers in the field of network security. It is of critical significance given the advancement in modern strategic narratives.
**Approach:** This has special relevance to drone-related operations.
**Tools and scale:** The security of the proposed designs is formally verified using a random oracle model (ROM), a real-or-random (ROR) model, and by informally using pragmatic illustration and mathematical lemmas.
**Results:** Therefore, this research illustrates the designing of two separate protocols based on the aggregate signature, identity, pairing cryptography, and Computational Diffie-Hellman Problem (CDHP) to guarantee data integrity, authorization, and confidentiality among drones and ... The results show that these protocols are verifiably protected in the ROM and ROR model using the CDHP.
