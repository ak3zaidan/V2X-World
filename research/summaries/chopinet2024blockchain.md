# Blockchain-based Jamming Detection in Vehicular Networks
Nathan Chopinet, Léo Mendiboure, Virginie Deniau, Anthony Fleury, Jonathan Villain, Christophe Gransart · International Conference on Blockchain Computing and Applications (BCCA) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/bcca62388.2024.10844392 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cooperative Intelligent Transportation Systems (CITS) represent a major step forward in the transport sector.
**Approach:** However, the development of new communication systems, particularly in a wireless connection, is also necessarily associated with new security risks. Among the various attacks, the presence of a jammer disrupting the reception of radio signals could seriously disrupt the reliability and integrity of exchanges, and constitutes a major threat.
