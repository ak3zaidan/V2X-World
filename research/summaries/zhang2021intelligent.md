# Intelligent Drone-assisted Anonymous Authentication and Key Agreement for 5G/B5G Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks
Jing Zhang, Jie Cui, Hong Zhong et al. · IEEE Transactions on Network Science and Engineering 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnse.2020.3029784 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Drones (or unmanned aerial vehicles) can play many assistant roles in the complex communication network, and can be used as an air relay node to support ground communications.
**Approach:** In this paper, considering the emergency of vehicular ad-hoc networks, we design an assistant communication scheme based on the intelligent drone to help vehicles securely communicate with each other under adversary but actual conditions. To effectively assist vehicle communication, and ensure that vehicle privacy is not compromised, we propose an intelligent drone-assisted anonymous authentication, and key agreement for 5G/B5G vehicular ad-hoc networks.
