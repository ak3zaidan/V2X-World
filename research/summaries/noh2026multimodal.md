# Multimodal Large Language Model-guided Constrained Optimization for RAN Intelligent Control
Hyeonho Noh · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06122 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Artificial intelligence (AI)-based radio access network (RAN) controllers are commonly designed for predefined operating scenarios and optimization tasks, limiting their adaptability when network conditions and operator requirements change after deployment.
**Approach:** This paper proposes multimodal large language model (MLLM)-guided constrained optimization for RAN intelligent control (MLLM-coRIC), a requirement-adaptive hierarchical Open RAN (O-RAN) framework for joint resource and power allocation. MLLM-coRIC jointly exploits the operator's natural-language specification and radio-frequency (RF)-derived network context as multimodal inputs, enabling a single deployed framework to address different optimization problems without redesigning task-specific algorithms or retraining control policies.
