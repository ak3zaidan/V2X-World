# Security Measures with Enhanced Behavior Processing and Footprint Algorithm against Sybil and Bogus Attacks in Vehicular Ad Hoc Network
Krzysztof Stępień, Aneta Poniszewska-Marańda · Sensors 2021 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s21103538 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Sybil and bogus attacks.
**Approach:** B&STL&T algorithm improved into B&SEBP&F (behaviour processing and footprints).
**Tools and scale:** Multiple scenarios with varying attacker-victim ratios.
**Results:** Detection of all attackers reduced by about 30% versus previous work (as reported).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Tabulates limitations of other trust schemes.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
