# Certificateless Dual-Layer Anonymous Privacy-Preserving Authentication Scheme for the IoV Environment
Yang Yang, Hai-Yang Yu, Yi-Long Ren, Ming Chen et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2026.3710779 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Internet of Vehicles (IoV) enables vehicles and roadside infrastructure to exchange safety-related information to improve traffic efficiency and driving safety. However, the highly dynamic topology and open wireless channels impose stringent requirements on authentication efficiency, scalability, and privacy preservation.
**Approach:** To address these challenges, we propose certificateless dual-layer anonymous privacy-preserving authentication scheme (CL-DAPA), an efficient certificateless anonymous privacy-preserving authentication scheme for the IoV.
**Tools and scale:** CL-DAPA adopts a dual-layer anonymity architecture composed of a static anonymous identity layer and a bidirectionally self-evolving pseudonym layer implemented via a double-seed mechanism, enabling autonomous pseudonym generation with conditional traceability.
**Results:** The Internet of Vehicles (IoV) enables vehicles and roadside infrastructure to exchange safety-related information to improve traffic efficiency and driving safety. The performance and security analyses demonstrate that CL-DAPA provides superior efficiency and privacy protection compared to existing CPPA schemes, especially in environments with high communication volumes and resource constraints.
