# Identifying Treatment and Spillover Effects with Control-Based and Forecast-Based Counterfactuals
Viviana Celli, Augusto Cerqua, Guido Pellegrini · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20156 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Spillovers and interference pose fundamental challenges for causal inference, as treatment assigned to one unit may affect the outcome of others, violating the no-interference assumption underlying most empirical strategies.
