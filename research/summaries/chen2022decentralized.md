# A Decentralized Trust Management System for Intelligent Transportation Environments
Xiao Chen, Jie Ding, Zhenyu Lu · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2020.3013279 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Commercialized 5G technology will provide reliable and efficient connectivity of motor vehicles that could support the dissemination of information under an intelligent transportation system. However, such service still suffers from risks or threats due to malicious content producers.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a novel prototype of the decentralized trust management system (DTMS) based on blockchain technologies.
