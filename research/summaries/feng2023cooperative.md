# Cooperative Adaptive Cruise Control for Vehicles Under False Data Injection Attacks
Zhongwei Feng, Keyun Qin, Xiao-Hang Jiao, Fei-Fei Du, Dongsheng Li · Industrial Cyber-Physical Systems 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icps58381.2023.10128066 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** FDI on CACC.
**Approach:** MPC-based CACC with intrusion detection that switches to ACC upon detection.
**Tools and scale:** Comparative simulation.
**Results:** Detection mechanism effective.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — detection-triggered fallback (CACC to ACC), a local reaction.
**Novelty implications:** Fallback-on-detection known.
