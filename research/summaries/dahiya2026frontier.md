# Are Frontier LLMs Ready for Cybersecurity? Evidence for Vertical Foundation Models from Dual-Mode Vulnerability Benchmarks
Vivek Dahiya, Sunny Nehra, Vipul Dholariya, Bhavik Shangari et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23243 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Are frontier LLMs ready for cybersecurity.
**Approach:** Dual-mode benchmark: white-box vulnerability detection and black-box web testing (118 vulnerabilities).
**Tools and scale:** Six frontier and two specialised models.
**Results:** 10-50% false positives; 4-8% black-box coverage (10-19% with tools); methodology raises per-family detection above 50%.
