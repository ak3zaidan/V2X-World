# DMTAS-VB: Dynamic Model Update-Based Trust Assessment Strategy for VANETs Considering Blockchain
Yufeng Li, Yawen Xie, Qi Liu, Jiangtao Li · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2025.3527119 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As one of the most critical aspects of mobile ad hoc networks, vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) have attracted increasing attention with the growing demand for safety in transportation systems.
**Approach:** Trust assessment is crucial in VANETs, as it can effectively identify and mitigate the impact of malicious vehicles, thereby ensuring the reliability and security of network communication. However, the models in most existing trust schemes cannot be dynamically updated over time, failing to adapt to the high dynamics of VANETs.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: blockchain
**Results:** The innovative three-chain structure (MainBC, MesBC, and RepBC) is adopted to achieve functional separation, and core identity authentication, high-frequency message exchange, and reputation management are handled independently. Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed scheme outperforms comparative approaches in detection performance under various conditions (vehicle number, vehicle speed, proportion of malicious vehicles) and different attack modes (simple attack, Zigzag attack, bad-mouth attack, ballot attack, and collusion attacks).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, the models in most existing trust schemes cannot be dynamically updated over time, failing to adapt to the high dynamics of VANETs.
- Moreover, they lack protection for data privacy, increasing the risk of information tampering and leakage.
