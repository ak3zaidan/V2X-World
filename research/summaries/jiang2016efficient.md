# An Efficient Anonymous Batch Authentication Scheme Based on HMAC for VANETs
Shunrong Jiang, Xiaoyan Zhu, Liangmin Wang · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2016.2517603 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** CRL checking before verification costs communication, storage and time and leaks privacy.
**Approach:** ABAH: RSU-managed domains, pseudonyms with identity-based signature batch authentication, HMAC replacing CRL checks.
**Tools and scale:** Security and performance analysis.
**Results:** Lower verification delay than CRL-based authentication (no numbers).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - alternative to CRL-based revocation checking, which our engine models in the 1609.2/SCMS form.
**Novelty implications:** CRL-check cost is a known problem; quantifying it in a full SCMS closed loop is not done here.
