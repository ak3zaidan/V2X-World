# MTH-IDS: A Multi-Tiered Hybrid Intrusion Detection System for Internet of Vehicles
Li Yang, Abdallah Moubayed, Abdallah Shami · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2021.3084796 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Modern vehicles, including connected vehicles and autonomous vehicles, nowadays involve many electronic control units connected through intra-vehicle networks to implement various functionalities and perform actions. Modern vehicles are also connected to external networks through vehicle-to-everything technologies, enabling their communications with other vehicles, infrastructures, and smart devices.
**Approach:** In this paper, the vulnerabilities of intra-vehicle and external networks are discussed, and a multi-tiered hybrid IDS that incorporates a signature-based IDS and an anomaly-based IDS is proposed to detect both known and unknown attacks on vehicular networks.
**Tools and scale:** Modern vehicles, including connected vehicles and autonomous vehicles, nowadays involve many electronic control units connected through intra-vehicle networks to implement various functionalities and perform actions.
**Results:** However, the improving functionality and connectivity of modern vehicles also increase their vulnerabilities to cyber-attacks targeting both intra-vehicle and external networks due to the large attack surfaces.
