# OSS-GPT: An LLM-Powered Intent-Driven Operations Support System for 6G Networks
Abdelkader Mekrache, Adlen Ksentini, Christos Verikoukis · 2025 IEEE 11th International Conference on Network Softwarization (NetSoft) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/netsoft64993.2025.11080632 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** —With the high demands of 6G networking services in terms of Quality of Service (QoS), managing these networks requires intelligent next-generation Operations Support Systems (OSSs). According to standardization bodies such as ETSI and 3GPP, OSS must support end-to-end, cross-domain management across all 6G domains.
**Approach:** To address these challenges, we propose enabling natural language interaction with OSS by leveraging Large Language Models (LLMs). We employ multi-agent LLMs with a hierarchical planning mechanism, creating a chatbot-like system that processes natural language inputs effectively.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 6G, LLM, GPT, real-world. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Real-world experiments conducted at EURECOM’s OSS demonstrated that the proposed approach can efﬁciently manage all 6G domains using natural language.
