# Blockchain Based Machine Learning Model for Secure Data Transfer and Route Preservation in UAV Integrated VANET Systems
Divya Sree A, Kapil Sharma · Journal of Machine and Computing 2025 · https://doi.org/10.53759/7669/jmc202505182 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The rise of driver assistance and automotive telecommunication systems shows great potential for adaptive transport solutions using vehicular ad hoc networks (VANET).
**Approach:** Generally, the two main issues in vehicle ad hoc networks that malicious attackers can greatly affect are privacy and safety. Preventing the spread of harmful messages among vehicles is crucial to protecting the private properties of automobiles from potential threats.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** According to simulation results, the suggested approach increases event spoofing identification precision by 96.21%.
