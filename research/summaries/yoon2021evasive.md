# An Evasive Scheduling Enhancement Against Packet Dropping Attacks in C-V2X Communication
Youngjoon Yoon, Hyogon Kim · IEEE Communications Letters 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/lcomm.2020.3030811 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Predictability of C-V2X Mode 4 SB-SPS enables targeted packet dropping.
**Approach:** Show attack makes victim a long-lasting "ghost vehicle"; propose randomising used resource locations.
**Tools and scale:** Analysis and simulation.
**Results:** Attack can push victim into higher crash risk than with no C-V2X; randomisation effective at some cost.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Cost aspect of the enhancement discussed.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — SB-SPS targeted collision attack on LTE-V2X with safety framing; our engine's LTE-V2X MAC can host this.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first SB-SPS targeted jamming; joint effect with security and MBD open.
