# Post-quantum cryptography enhanced IP address management framework for secure smart transportation networks
Ying Chen, Mary Jane Samonte · International Conference on Intelligent Transportation and Smart Cities 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3119161 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** V2X IP address management relies on quantum-vulnerable ECC.
**Approach:** PQC-enhanced IPAM with ML-DSA in a cloud/RSU/vehicle hierarchy and PQC cryptographically generated IPv6 addresses bound to Dilithium keys. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Experimental evaluation.
**Results:** RSU verification 0.32 +/- 0.04 ms vs 1.15 +/- 0.08 ms for ECDSA; total handshake 42.5 +/- 3.8 ms.
