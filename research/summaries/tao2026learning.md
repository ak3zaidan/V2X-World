Yihang Tao, Senkang Hu, Haonan An, Zhengru Fang et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19596 · relevance: medium · basis: full text

**Problem:** Threshold-based consensus CP defences can be bypassed by attacks optimising timing and target regions.
**Approach:** MVIG attack: mutual view information graphs, spectral graph analysis and temporal modelling.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the extracted sections.
**Results:** Significantly outperforms existing attacks with real-time performance.

- effectiveness bounded by benign CAVs' joint coverage
