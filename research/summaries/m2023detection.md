# Detection of intrusions in clustered vehicle networks using invasive weed optimization using a deep wavelet neural networks
M V B Murali Krishna M, Christo Ananth, N Krishnaraj · Measurement Sensors 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.measen.2023.100807 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A VANET is a kind of IMS, or intelligent transportation system, that connects moving vehicles. Guaranteeing timely and accurate information sharing between vehicles and infrastructure helps make roads safer and more efficient.
**Approach:** Wireless connections are used for data transmission in VANET, making security a critical design challenge. In this aspect, this study presents an effective invasive weed optimization algorithm based energy efficient clustering with deep wavelet neural network based intrusion detection (IWOEEC-DWNN) technique for VANET.
