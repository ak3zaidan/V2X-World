# Evaluating V2V Security on an SDR Testbed
Geoff Twardokus, Hanif Rahbari · Conference on Computer Communications Workshops 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/infocomwkshps51825.2021.9484435 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Exposing strengths and weaknesses of IEEE 1609.2-based V2V security.
**Approach:** V2Verifier: open-source SDR testbed supporting DSRC and C-V2X with commercial OBUs; jamming and replay attacks.
**Tools and scale:** SDRs and commercial OBUs; 1609.2.
**Results:** Quantifies jamming and replay threats; 1609.2 mitigates replay.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - 1609.2 security over DSRC and C-V2X with attacks; hardware testbed, no SCMS backend or city-scale traffic.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first open 1609.2 attack evaluation across DSRC and C-V2X; city-scale closed loop with SCMS remains ours.
