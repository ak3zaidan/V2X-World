# Assessing the Impact of Cyber Attacks on Traffic Flow with Mixed Autonomous and Human-Driven Vehicles
Christian Wellens-Miles, Siôn Parkinson, Mauro Vallati · 2026 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iv66570.2026.11623970 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Impact of cyber attacks on mixed AV/HDV traffic flow.
**Approach:** Simulates Sybil, spoofing on V2V/V2I, vehicle sabotage and RSU attacks; averages multiple runs.
**Tools and scale:** Eclipse MOSAIC Berlin scenario.
**Results:** Some attacks cause complete road blockages at peak hours.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — city-scale attacks inside the simulation with traffic-flow outcome (part of direction 1/2), though without PKI or revocation.
**Novelty implications:** City-scale attack-to-traffic impact in MOSAIC exists; adding SCMS revocation and per-message ground truth remains open.
