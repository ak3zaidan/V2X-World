# Trustworthy and Privacy- Preserving Artificial Intelligence for Secure Vehicular Networks
Dilip Nannaware, Rajesh Balaji, Aryan Nannaware · JOURNAL OF EMERGING TRENDS AND NOVEL RESEARCH 2026 · https://doi.org/10.56975/jetnr.v4i8.235561 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Autonomous and connected vehicles are moving from controlled trials into limited real-world deployment, making the security of AI-driven vehicular systems a practical engineering concern. Vehicles now exchange sensor data, coordinate maneuvers, and update onboard models over Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) links, creating a broad attack surface.
**Approach:** This paper reviews four defense directions: robust learning under adversarial and Byzantine threat models, privacy-preserving computation through Differential Privacy, Homomorphic Encryption, and Secure Multi-Party Computation, decentralized trust and identity management using blockchain and post-quantum cryptography, and cross-layer security designs for 5G/6G vehicular networks.
