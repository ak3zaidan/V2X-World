# A Real-Time Sybil Attack Detection for Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETS) Using Spatio-Temporal Learning and XGBoost
Seyed Salar Sefati, Astik Kumar Samal, Octavian Fratu, Simona Viorica Halunga · International Symposium on Wireless Personal Multimedia Communications (WPMC) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wpmc67460.2025.11351272 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Real-time Sybil detection.
**Approach:** Time-evolving graph model with spatio-temporal beacon features, similarity clustering and XGBoost.
**Tools and scale:** OMNeT++-generated labelled dataset.
**Results:** Over 96% accuracy and under 4% FPR across densities.
