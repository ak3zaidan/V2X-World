# Post-Quantum Impacts on V2X Certificates – Already at The End of The Road
Takahito Yoshizawa, Bart Preneel A · IEEE Vehicular Technology Conference 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2023-spring57618.2023.10199793 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** IEEE 1609.2/ETSI certificate formats are not forward compatible with PQC.
**Approach:** Analysis showing PQC certificates can cause DDoS-like effects on legacy and PQC-ready vehicles and reduce channel capacity and range; proposes certificate definition revisions.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical.
**Results:** Stalemate risk for PQC deployment; larger messages reduce capacity and effective range.
