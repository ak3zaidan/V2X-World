# Secure Platooning of Autonomous Vehicles Under Attacked GPS Data
Xingkang He, Ehsan Hashemi, Karl H. Johansson · arXiv.org 2020 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.12975 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Securing platooning when one unknown vehicle's GPS is arbitrarily manipulated.
**Approach:** Two detectors using relative (camera/radar) measurements and local innovations; saturated-innovation observer; distributed consensus controller.
**Tools and scale:** Numerical simulations.
**Results:** Estimation and platooning errors asymptotically bounded under conditions; effectiveness shown.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Bounded uncertainties and a single attacked vehicle assumed.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — control layer.
**Novelty implications:** Adjacent.
