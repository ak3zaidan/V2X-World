# MobiTrust: Trust-Aware Pricing Scheme for Edge-Based Mobile Sensor-Cloud for Vehicular IoT
D. Rajavel, Aishwariya Chakraborty, S. Misra · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2022.3179595 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this work, we propose MobiTrust, a game theoretic pricing scheme, to address the problem of ensuring trust in the edge-enabled mobile sensor-cloud (MSC) architecture for vehicular Internet-of-Things (IoT).
**Approach:** In the edge-enabled MSC, a sensor-cloud service provider (SCSP) utilises multiple sensor-equipped vehicular IoT-based devices belonging to different owners and multiple edge devices deployed by different edge infrastructure providers (IPs) to provision Sensors-as-a-Service (Se-aaS). Since these entities are revenue-driven and they depend on each other for successful service provisioning, there is a need to establish trust among them to ensure the sustainability of MSC.

**Results:** Results of extensive simulations show that MobiTrust increases profits for the stakeholders and QoS by 9% and 11%, respectively, compared to benchmarks.
