# Hybrid and Multifaceted Context-Aware Misbehavior Detection Model for Vehicular Ad Hoc Network
Fuad A. Ghaleb, Mohd Aizaini Maarof, Anazida Zainal, Bander Ali Saleh Al-Rimy et al. · IEEE Access 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2019.2950805 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** MBD cannot cope with dynamic context; accuracy falls and false alarms rise.
**Approach:** HCA-MDS: data-centric and behavioural features, Kalman and Hampel filter context reference, online statistical detection.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Improved accuracy and false alarms (details in full text).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Extension with AI-based classifiers on top of statistical outputs (future work).
- Learning attack patterns from classifier outputs (ongoing).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — context-aware MBD thresholds.
**Novelty implications:** Minor.
