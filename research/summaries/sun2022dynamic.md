# Dynamic Digital Twin and Distributed Incentives for Resource Allocation in Aerial-Assisted Internet of Vehicles
Wen Sun, Peng Wang, Ning Xu, Gaozu Wang, Yan Zhang · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2021.3058213 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Internet of Vehicles (IoV), when empowered by aerial communications, provides vehicles with seamless connections and proximate computing services.
**Approach:** The unpredictable network dynamics of aerial-assisted IoV pose challenges to the resource allocation. In this article, dynamic digital twin (DT) of aerial-assisted IoV is established to capture the time-varying resource supply and demands, so that unified resource scheduling and allocation can be performed.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Thanks to ADMM, the incentive mechanism can be run at multiple RSUs in parallel to reduce delay and relieve the computational burden of UAVs. Simulation results show that the proposed scheme can improve the satisfaction of vehicles and the energy efficiency at the same time.
