# PaTAVTT: A Hardware-in-the-Loop Scaled Platform for Testing Autonomous Vehicle Trajectory Tracking
Zhigang Xu, Mingliang Wang, Fengzhi Zhang, Sheng Jin et al. · Journal of Advanced Transportation 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2017/9203251 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the advent of autonomous vehicles, in particular its adaptability to harsh conditions, the research and development of autonomous vehicles attract significant attention by not only academia but also practitioners.
**Approach:** Due to the high risk, high cost, and difficulty to test autonomous vehicles under harsh conditions, the hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) scaled platform has been proposed as it is a safe, inexpensive, and effective test method. This paper uses a case of the development process of tracking control for high-speed U-turn to build the tracking control function.
