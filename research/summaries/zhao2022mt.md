# MT-FlowFormer: A Semi-Supervised Flow Transformer for Encrypted Traffic Classification
Rui-Jie Zhao, Xian-Wen Deng, Zhicong Yan, Jun Ma, Zhi Xue, Yi-Jun Wang · Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3534678.3539314 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the increasing demand for the protection of personal network meta-data, encrypted networks have grown in popularity, so do the challenge of monitoring and analyzing encrypted network traffic.
**Approach:** Currently, some deep learning-based methods have been proposed to leverage statistical features for encrypted traffic classification, which are barely affected by encryption techniques. However, these works still suffer from two main intrinsic limitations: (1) the feature extraction process lacks a mechanism to take into account correlations between flows in the flow sequence; and (2) a large volume of manually-labeled data is required for training an effective deep classifier.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Experimental results on two real-world traffic datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art methods with a large margin.
