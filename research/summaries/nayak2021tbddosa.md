# TBDDoSA-MD: Trust-Based DDoS Misbehave Detection Approach in Software-defined Vehicular Network (SDVN)
Rajendra Prasad Nayak, Srinivas Sethi, Sourav Kumar Bhoi, Kshira Sagar Sahoo, Noor Zaman Jhanjhi, Thamer Tabbakh et al. · Computers, materials & continua/Computers, materials & continua (Print) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.32604/cmc.2021.018930 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** DDoS/jamming misbehaviour in SDVN.
**Approach:** Trust-based detection from direct and indirect trust; discard malicious messages.
**Tools and scale:** Veins (OMNeT++ + SUMO); vs TBF.
**Results:** Detection accuracy >90%, detection time as low as 30 s.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — reports detection time.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
