# Network Digital Twins: A Systematic Review
Roberto Verdecchia, Leonardo Scommegna, Benedetta Picano, Marco Becattini et al. · IEEE Access 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3453034 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Network management is becoming more complex due to various factors. The growth of IoT increases the number of nodes to control.
**Approach:** To address this gap, in this paper, we present a systematic review of the Network Digital Twin literature.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: federated, digital twin, field. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** From the analysis of 138 primary studies, various insights emerge. Networking Digital Twin is a particularly recent concept that has been explored in the literature since 2017 and is experiencing a steady increase to this day.
