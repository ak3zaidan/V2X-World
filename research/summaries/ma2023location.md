# Location Privacy Threats and Protections in 6G Vehicular Networks: A Comprehensive Review
Baihe Ma, Xu Wang, Lin Xiaojie, Jiang, Yanna, Caijun Sun, Zhe Wang et al. · arXiv (Cornell University) 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.04503 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Location privacy in 6G vehicular networks.
**Approach:** Survey of localization techniques and LPPMs (user-side, server-side, interface-based); new cross-layer tracking challenge.
**Tools and scale:** Survey.
**Results:** Interface-based LPPMs under-studied; adversaries can combine multi-layer tracking data.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Need multi-layered privacy protection and dynamic adaptation to cross-layer attacks.
