# QuantumShield V2X: A Continuous-Variable Quantum Key Distribution Framework for Eavesdropping-Resistant V2X Communications
Collins Izuchukwu Okafor, Love Allen Chijioke Ahakonye, Dong-Seong Kim, Jae Min Lee · 2025 16th International Conference on Information and Communication Technology Convergence (ICTC) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ictc66702.2025.11388951 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Eavesdropping on V2X key exchange, with quantum computers threatening public-key key exchange.
**Approach:** CV-QKD (Gaussian-modulated coherent states) transceivers in OBUs and RSUs to establish symmetric keys with eavesdropper detection. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Simulation of CV-QKD under V2X channel conditions; no traffic simulator named in the abstract.
