# PPA6-IoV: A Six-Step Privacy-Preserving Authentication Protocol for the Internet of Vehicles
S. Ibrahim, Hakem Beitollahi · IEEE Access 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3459948 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Today, vehicular communication can significantly improve traffic safety and road efficiency and as well as reduce the environmental pollution.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a novel six-step authentication protocol, which is called PPA6-IoV, between vehicles such that the privacy of both initiator vehicle and receipt vehicle is preserved.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** The Crossware ARM simulator along with NS2 simulator is used to evaluate and compare the performance of PPA6-IoV with previous state-of-the-art techniques. Simulation results show that PPA6-IoV on average has 39% and 91% less communication and computational costs, respectively compared to previous state-of-the-art techniques.

- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
