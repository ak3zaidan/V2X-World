# V2X Misbehavior and Collective Perception Service: Considerations for Standardization
Mohammad Raashid Ansari, Jean-Philippe Monteuuis, Jonathan Petit, Cong Chen · IEEE Conference on Standards for Communications and Networking 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cscn53733.2021.9686156 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Standards lack misbehaviour specifications for Collective Perception.
**Approach:** Threat assessment of CPS per ETSI TR 103 562 and TS 103 324; attack trees rated by reproducibility, impact and stealthiness; proposed defences and standardisation inputs.
**Tools and scale:** Threat analysis.
**Results:** Most attacks highly reproducible; three high-impact; low stealthiness since attackers expose certificates; defences need redundant data from honest neighbours or sensors.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Open challenges for standardisation and research to achieve secure CPS (discussed).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — CPM MBD standardisation; our engine may not cover CPM.
**Novelty implications:** CPM MBD gaps already identified by authors.
