# Is Machine Learning Ready for Traffic Engineering Optimization?
Guillermo Bernardez, Jose Suarez-Varela, Albert Lopez, Bo Wu, Shihan Xiao, Xiangle Cheng, Pere Barlet-Ros, Albert Cabellos-Aparicio · 2021 IEEE 29th International Conference on Network Protocols (ICNP) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icnp52444.2021.9651930 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Traffic Engineering (TE) is a basic building block of the Internet.
**Approach:** In this paper, we analyze whether modern Machine Learning (ML) methods are ready to be used for TE optimization. We address this open question through a comparative analysis between the state of the art in ML and the state of the art in TE.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Our experimental results show that the proposed MARL+GNN solution achieves equivalent performance to DEFO in a wide variety of network scenarios including three real-world network topologies. At the same time, we show that MARL+GNN can achieve significant reductions in execution time (from the scale of minutes with DEFO to a few seconds with our solution).
