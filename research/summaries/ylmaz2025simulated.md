# Simulated versus Field Test LTE V2X Per Performance in Congested Highway Vehicular Scenarios
Mustafa Harun Yılmaz · 2025 IEEE 101st Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2025-Spring) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2025-spring65109.2025.11174725 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Is LTE-V2X simulation consistent with USDOT field tests?
**Approach:** Compares PER from USDOT 2021-2022 LTE-V2X field tests with a simulation tool for static devices at 100 ms TTI, 600 ms TTI and congestion control ON.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation tool; USDOT field data; up to 1,500 devices simulated.
**Results:** Simulation within 95% confidence intervals for 600 ms TTI and CC cases; sometimes outside for 100 ms TTI; extended to 1,500 devices with CC and 252 devices at 100 ms.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Simulation sometimes falls outside field confidence bars at 100 ms TTI.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — validation of LTE-V2X simulation against US field data; same kind of validation our LTE-V2X stack needs.
**Novelty implications:** Field-validated LTE-V2X simulation exists; we can use the USDOT data for our own validation.
