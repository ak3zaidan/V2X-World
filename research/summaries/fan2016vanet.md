# VANET Routing Replay Attack Detection Research Based on SVM
Qing Gang Fan, Li Wang, Yan Ning Cai, Yong Qiang Li et al. · MATEC Web of Conferences 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1051/matecconf/20166305020 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In the process of establishing routing in VANET, because of the interference of routing request (RREQ) and routing response (RREP), the nodes in the network are busy with looking for routing or establishing routing. The impact on the network performance is extremely serious, such as increasing a lot of network overhead, consuming valuable bandwidth resources.
**Approach:** In this paper, the influence of routing replay attack in VANET is studied.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in abstract.
**Results:** The feasibility of VANET routing replay attack IDS based on SVM is verified through simulation experiment.
