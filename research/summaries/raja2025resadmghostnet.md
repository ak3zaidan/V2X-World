# ResADM‐GhostNet With Spider Wasp Optimizer: A Real‐Time DDoS Detection Framework for Vehicular Networks
M. Raja, G. Jayandhi, M. Sathesh et al. · International Journal of Communication Systems 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1002/dac.70243 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ABSTRACTVANETs are being integrated into society to ensure communication inside the networks is secured and protected.
**Approach:** This paper presents a ResADM‐GhostNet model with Spider Wasp Optimizer (SWO) for DDoS detection in vehicular networks. The proposed framework gained high efficiency in RT DDoS detection in vehicular networks compared to other models.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Feature extraction is performed on the traffic and spatiotemporal data patterns obtained from the network traffic and the environment where the network is deployed through packet‐level, flow‐based, and spatiotemporal features to improve the detection rate. The suggested technique attained an accuracy of 98.74%, a specificity of 98.91%, and a precision of 97.58% for 80% of the training data split.
