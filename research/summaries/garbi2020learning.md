# Learning Queuing Networks by Recurrent Neural Networks
Giulio Garbi, Emilio Incerto, Mirco Tribastone · arXiv 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3358960.3379134 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Building analytical performance models needs mathematical expertise.
**Approach:** Encode queuing network fluid ODEs into an interpretable RNN trained on measurements for what-if analysis.
**Tools and scale:** Synthetic models and a real load-balancing system.
**Results:** Max discrepancy below 10% under unseen configurations.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: mixed multi-class and layered QNs, neural ODEs, residual networks, active learning for sampling.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - interpretable learned what-if models.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
