# Empirical Analysis of Client-Based Network Quality Prediction in Vehicular Multi-MNO Networks
Benjamin Sliwa, Christian Wietfeld · 2019 IEEE 90th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2019-Fall) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtcfall.2019.8891392 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Exploiting multiple MNOs for vehicular data transfer with anticipatory networking.
**Approach:** Real measurement campaign and online data-rate prediction with several ML models and aggregation strategies.
**Tools and scale:** Public cellular networks, multiple scenarios.
**Results:** Multi-MNO gives significant benefits even with one dominant MNO; MNO-specific models required.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Feature impact depends strongly on network configuration, requiring per-MNO models.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
