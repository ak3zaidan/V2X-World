# Quantum Key Distribution for V2I communications with software-defined networking
A. Stavdas, E. Kosmatos, C. Maple, Emilio Hugues-Salas et al. · IET Quantum Communication 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1049/qtc2.12070 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract The evolution of Connected and Autonomous Vehicles (CAVs) promises improvements in our travel experience and the potential to enhance road safety and reduce environmental impact. This will be utilising highly diverse traffic environments that enable several advanced mobility applications.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a method for the integration of QKD in V2I networks to enable secure data communication.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Quantum Key Distribution is used in the end‐to‐end path of vehicle‐to‐infrastructure (V2I) networks. We have investigated the security performance of QKD in a V2I network over an urban environment.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — LLM agents for network/security operations; our engine has an LLM agent that configures and analyses runs, but this work is not V2X and not tied to a deterministic simulator with ground truth.
**Novelty implications:** LLM agents for SOC/NetOps/RCA are established; cross-layer V2X root-cause analysis against simulator ground truth is not covered here.
