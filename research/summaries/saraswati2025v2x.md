# Towards V2X-Enabled Vulnerable Road Users Protection: Baseline Performance Evaluation of a Fundamental Kinematic Model for CAM-Based Trajectory Prediction at Road Intersections
Ni Putu Amanda Saraswati, Ngurah Indra ER, G. Sukadarmika, W. G. Ariastina, Jean-Marie Bonnin · 2025 International Conference on Smart-Green Technology in Electrical and Information Systems (ICSGTEIS) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icsgteis68532.2025.11284362 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Enhancing the safety of Vulnerable Road Users (VRUs) at urban intersections is a critical challenge for Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS).
**Approach:** This paper presents a baseline performance evaluation of a fundamental kinematic model for Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) trajectory prediction. Using a co-simulation framework of SUMO, OMNeT++, and Artery, we evaluate a linear regression model's performance across two Vehicle-to-Pedestrian (V2P) scenarios with varying pedestrian dynamics (walking vs. running) and two prediction horizons (1s and 2 s).
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: Artery, SUMO; scale not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** We also find that a higher data frequency from a more dynamic road user is insufficient to improve prediction accuracy.
