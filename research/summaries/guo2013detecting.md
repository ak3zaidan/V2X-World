# Detecting blackhole and greyhole attacks in vehicular Delay Tolerant Networks
Yinghui Guo, Sebastian Schildt, Lars Christian Wolf · 2013 Fifth International Conference on Communication Systems and Networks (COMSNETS) 2013 · https://doi.org/10.1109/comsnets.2013.6465569 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Blackhole/greyhole attacks in vehicular DTNs.
**Approach:** MDS where nodes exchange encounter records securely to assess trustworthiness.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation with several DTN routing protocols.
**Results:** High detection rate and low false positives across greyhole drop probabilities, low energy use.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — routing misbehaviour in DTNs.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
