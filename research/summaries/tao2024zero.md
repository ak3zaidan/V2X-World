# Zero-Knowledge Proof of Distinct Identity: a Standard-compatible Sybil-resistant Pseudonym Extension for C-ITS
Ye Tao, Hong-Yi Wu, Ehsan Javanmardi, Manabu Tsukada et al. · 2024 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iv55156.2024.10588511 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Pseudonym unlinkability enables Sybil attacks.
**Approach:** zk-PoDI: zk-SNARK proof that a vehicle does not own another local pseudonym, standard-compatible.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Low latency, adjustable difficulty, moderate computation, negligible communication.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Implementation and evaluation in a realistic city-scale simulation environment left for future work
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Sybil resistance for pseudonyms; city-scale evaluation explicitly open.
**Novelty implications:** City-scale simulation of Sybil-resistant pseudonyms is open.
