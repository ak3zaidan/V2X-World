# Jamming detection on 802.11p under multi-channel operation in vehicular networks
Huong Nguyen-Minh, Abderrahim Benslimane, Abderrezak Rachedi · IEEE International Conference on Wireless and Mobile Computing, Networking and Communications (WiMob) 2015 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wimob.2015.7348039 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Multichannel operation has been suggested for vehicular networks in order to support simultaneously safety and non-safety applications. Without awareness of lower layers, safety messages may be generated out of the control channel interval that leads to synchronous contention at the beginning of control channel interval (CCHI).
**Approach:** In this work, we proposed a detection method that can distinguish between packet lost due to normal collisions and due to jamming attacks. An analytical model is also introduced and validated in order to study the efficiency and the precision of the proposed detection method.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** Obtained numerical results from analytical model and network simulation show that our detection method has a high detection probability under reactive jamming attacks.
