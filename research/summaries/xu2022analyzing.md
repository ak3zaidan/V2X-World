# Analyzing the inconsistency in driving patterns between manual and autonomous modes under complex driving scenarios with a VR-enabled simulation platform
Zheng Xu, Yihai Fang, Nan Zheng, Hai L. Vu · Journal of Intelligent and Connected Vehicles 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1108/jicv-05-2022-0017 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Purpose With the aid of naturalistic simulations, this paper aims to investigate human behavior during manual and autonomous driving modes in complex scenarios.
**Approach:** Practical implications This paper attempts to fill the existing research gap in preparing close-to-reality tools for AV experience and further understanding human behavior during high-level autonomous driving. Social implications This work aims to systematically analyze the inconsistency in driving patterns between manual and autopilot modes in various driving scenarios (i.e. multiple scenes and various traffic conditions) to facilitate user acceptance of AV technology.
