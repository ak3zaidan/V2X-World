# Analyzing the Traffic of MANETs using Graph Neural Networks
Taha Tekdogan · 2022 IEEE International Conference on Machine Learning and Applied Network Technologies (ICMLANT) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icmlant56191.2022.9996518 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have been taking role in many areas, thanks to their expressive power on graph-structured data.
**Approach:** On the other hand, Mobile Ad-Hoc Networks (MANETs) are gaining attention as network technologies have been taken to the 5G level. However, there is no study that evaluates the efficiency of GNNs on MANETs.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G, dataset.
**Results:** SAG model showed 82.1% accuracy on average in the experiments.
