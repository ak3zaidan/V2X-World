# Towards a Novel Trust-Based Multicast Routing for VANETs
Hui Xia, San-shun Zhang, Ben-xia Li, Li Li, Xiang-guo Cheng · Security and Communication Networks 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2018/7608198 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) is an important application area of the Cyber-Physical System (CPS). To further promote effective communication between vehicles, vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) have been widely used in the ITS.
**Approach:** In order to address the above-mentioned issues, we propose a novel trust-based multicast routing protocol (TMR) to defend against multiple attacks and improve the routing efficiency.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Comprehensive simulation experiments show that our new protocol can effectively improve the transmission rate of data packets at the expense of a slight increase in end-to-end delay and control overhead.
