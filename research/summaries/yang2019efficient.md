# An efficient blockchain‐based batch verification scheme for vehicular ad hoc networks
Yi Yang, De-Biao He, Hua-Qun Wang, Lu Zhou · Transactions on Emerging Telecommunications Technologies 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1002/ett.3857 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As an important branch of the Internet of Things (IoT), Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) have attracted wide attention from industrial and academic. Due to an opening wireless channel, the security and efficiency of VANETs have also become serious.
**Approach:** To solve the above problems, we propose an efficient blockchain‐based batch verification scheme for VANETs using the Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC).
