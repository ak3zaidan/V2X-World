# Federated Generative Learning for Digital Twin Network Modeling
Dongzi Jin, Yingyu Li, Yong Xiao · 2024 IEEE 99th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2024-Spring) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2024-spring62846.2024.10683000 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** High-fidelity digital twin network modelling is costly; federated generative models suffer mode collapse under heterogeneous data.
**Approach:** Federated generative model with multiple generators (FMG-DTN) and a loss encouraging category diversity.
**Tools and scale:** Real-world traffic dataset; complexity analysis.
**Results:** Lower communication overhead than a state-of-the-art method; high-fidelity synthetic data.
