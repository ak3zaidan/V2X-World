**Problem:** Intelligent transportation systems (ITS) have been attracting the attention of industry and academia alike for addressing issues raised by the 2030 agenda for sustainable development goals (SDG) approved by the United Nations.
**Approach:** However, the diversity and dynamics of present-day transportation scenarios are already very complex, turning the management of ITS into a virtually impossible task for conventional traffic control centers. Recently, the digital twin (DT) paradigm has been presented as a modern architectural concept to tackle complex problems, such as the ones faced by ITS.
