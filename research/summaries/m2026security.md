# Security in the 6G-Enabled Internet of Vehicles: An Extended Review
Bhagyashri M · JOURNAL OF ADVANCE AND FUTURE RESEARCH 2026 · https://doi.org/10.56975/jaafr.v4i6.511249 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The transition from a fifth-generation Internet of Vehicles to a sixth-generation deployment target reshapes the security problem rather than just renaming it.
**Approach:** It groups the surveyed work around four organising questions: what does the 6G-IoV stack actually look like, how is each layer being attacked, what learning and cryptographic mechanisms are proposed against those attacks, and how trustworthy are the published results once the threat model is held to a stricter standard.
**Tools and scale:** Five themes recur across the reviewed papers: shallow or opaque threat modelling, single-dataset evaluation regimes, immature post-quantum readiness, an almost complete absence of safety-coupled performance metrics, and the lack of red-team studies that would expose the distance between in-distribution accuracy and adversarial robustness.
