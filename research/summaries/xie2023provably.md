# Provably Secure and Anonymous V2I and V2V Authentication Protocol for VANETs
Qian-Na Xie, Zixuan Ding, Panpan Zheng · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2023.3253710 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs) enable the connection and information exchange between vehicles and transportation infrastructure (V2I), vehicle and vehicle (V2V) to improve the safety and efficiency of the transportation system. **Approach:** We use Physical Unclonable Function (PUF) and biological key to avoid RSU captured attacks and OBU intrusion attacks, design a feature embedding strategy with dynamic pseudo-identity to recover the malicious vehicle’s identity by the Trusted Authority (TA).
**Tools and scale:** In the application test simulating the actual scenario, our protocol is more efficient because of lower overhead and batch authentication strategy.
**Results:** In the application test simulating the actual scenario, our protocol is more efficient because of lower overhead and batch authentication strategy. The comparative analysis and security proof results show that our protocol is more superior to others.
