# Adaptive Hybrid Machine Learning Model for Real-Time Intrusion and Anomaly Detection in Vanet Environments
Vaibhav Thorat, M. Tomar · 2026 International Conference on Computing Theory and Wireless Communications (ICCTWC) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icctwc68241.2026.11583380 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper presents, an adaptive hybrid machine learning model of AdaBoost and Support Vector Machine(SVM) is provided and applied in detecting intrusion and an anomaly in the data of Vehicular Ad Hoc Network (VANET) in real-time.
**Approach:** The VANET security presents serious issues due to the topology being highly dynamic, the frequent communication and due to the fact that it is prone to several types of cyber-attacks. The hybrid method also clears better detection accuracy, minimizes false positive probabilities and improves the stability of the system relative to the conventional standalone designs.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** It has an adaptive mechanism with dynamically changing model parameters in response to changing conditions in the network, to achieve an efficient real-time performance.
