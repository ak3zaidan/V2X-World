# Anomaly Detection Frameworks and Trends in Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks (VANETs)
K. N. Reddy, K. Hosmitha, K. Srividya et al. · 2025 International Conference on Intelligent Communication Networks and Computational Techniques (ICICNCT) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icicnct66124.2025.11233000 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Intelligent Transportation Systems play a key role in many crucial operations.
**Approach:** The recent developments in anomaly detection for VANETs are examined in this review, with an emphasis on hybrid model approaches that combine rule-based, machine learning, and deep learning methodologies. This paper also investigates the use of GenVRAM and other synthetic datasets, hierarchical detection architectures, and the efficiency of merging models at various network layers.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Particularly in situations with limited data availability and high mobility, traditional rule-based or single layer machine learning (ML) techniques have demonstrated limited efficacy in detecting such intricate, dynamic threats. For improved VANET security, the paper contrasts current methods, identifies important issues like latency and data scarcity, and suggests future directions like federated anomaly detection and adaptive learning.
