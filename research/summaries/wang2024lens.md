# Lens: A Foundation Model for Network Traffic in Cybersecurity
Qineng Wang, Chen Qian, Xiaochang Li, Ziyu Yao, Hua-Jie Shao · arXiv.org 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03646 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Traffic foundation models ignore network knowledge and struggle with new classes.
**Approach:** Lens: knowledge-guided mask span prediction pretraining; classification reframed as generation with context-aware finetuning.
**Tools and scale:** Benchmark traffic datasets; JSD/TVD for generation.
**Results:** Outperforms baselines on 8 of 12 classification tasks, average accuracy 96.33%; better generation fidelity.
