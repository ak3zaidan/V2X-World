# A lightweight and secure online/offline cross-domain authentication scheme for VANET systems in Industrial IoT
Haqi Khalid, S. Hashim, S. M. S. Ahmad, F. Hashim et al. · PeerJ Computer Science 2021 · https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj-cs.714 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** However, in various domains, such as industrial wireless scenarios, small cell domains, and vehicular ad hoc networks, an efficient and stable authentication algorithm is required (VANET).
**Approach:** Thus, to solve the above issues, we propose an online/offline lightweight authentication scheme for the VANET cross-domain system in IIoT to improve the security and efficiency of the VANET. The proposed scheme utilizes an efficient AES-RSA algorithm to achieve integrity and confidentiality of the message.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** Hence, it is vulnerable to specific attacks (e.g., replay attacks, modification attacks, man-in-the-middle attacks, and insider attacks), making the system inefficient. The proposed work includes two different significant goals to achieve first, then secure message on which the data is transmitted and efficiency in a cryptographic manner.
