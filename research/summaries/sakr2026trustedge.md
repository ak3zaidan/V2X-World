# TrustEdge-V2X: Deployment-Aware Edge Intelligence for V2X/IoV Intrusion and Misbehavior Detection
Hesham A. Sakr, Mina Shenouda, Nadeem Sarwar, Ibrahim Elewah et al. · Computers 2026 · https://doi.org/10.3390/computers15090577 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** MBD models evaluated on accuracy but not edge deployability.
**Approach:** TrustEdge-V2X: deployment-aware framework with EdgeScore ranking, robustness, XAI, repeated runs.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi NextGen, CICIoT2023, HCRL CarHacking; eight models, six scenarios.
**Results:** LightGBM EdgeScore 0.9383, F1 0.9878, MCC 0.9758, 0.0094 ms per sample; accuracy-best model differs from EdgeScore-best in five cases.
