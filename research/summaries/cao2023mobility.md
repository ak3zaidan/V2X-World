# Mobility-Aware Multiobjective Task Offloading for Vehicular Edge Computing in Digital Twin Environment
Bin Cao, Ziming Li, Xin Liu, Zhihan Lv et al. · IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jsac.2023.3310100 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In vehicular edge computing (VEC), vehicle users (VUs) can offload their computation-intensive tasks to edge server (ES) that provides additional computation resources.
**Approach:** To solve the above model, an improved CMA-ES algorithm based on the guiding point (GP-CMA-ES) is proposed. Therefore, a convergence-related variable grouping strategy based on the relationship detection between variables and objectives is proposed.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** In real-word, the mobility of VUs cannot be ignored and the downlink delay in receiving process results from ES is related to the mobility of VUs.
