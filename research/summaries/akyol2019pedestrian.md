# Pedestrian-Friendly Traffic Signal Control Using SUMO
Görkem Akyol, Mehmet Ali Silgu, Hilmi Berk Çelikoğlu · EPiC Series in Computing 2019 · https://doi.org/10.29007/c6k6 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The center of the Kadıköy area in Istanbul is extremely crowded due to overlap of the terminals for the subway and the marine transit lines.
**Approach:** When the Kadıköy-Kartal subway’s terminal is added in the middle of the Kadıköy in 2013 without the analysis on crowd dynamics, vehicular and pedestrian traffic have become much more complicated to be efficiently managed. When the pedestrians have a crossing gap, most of them make the decision of crossing without considering the signal phase.
