# Model-Based Deep Reinforcement Learning Framework for Channel Access in Wireless Networks
Jong In Park, J. Chae, K. Choi · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2023.3325575 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this article, we propose a model-based reinforcement learning (RL) algorithm for wireless channel access. The model-based RL is a relatively new RL paradigm that integrates the concept of the world model into the agent.
**Approach:** In this article, we focus on developing a sophisticated world model based on the partially observable Markov decision process (POMDP). For establishing the wireless channel access problem, we introduce two separate environments, one of which describes the channel occupancy dynamics and the other governs data traffic arrival patterns.
