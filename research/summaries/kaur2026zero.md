# A Zero-Trust Edge-Assisted PKI Architecture for Scalable and Real-Time Security in Internet of Vehicles
Kirtpreet Kaur, Krishnendu Rarhi · 2026 International Conference on System, Computation, Automation and Networking (ICSCAN) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icscan66520.2026.11588376 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The rapid growth of IoVs poses substantial security challenges for scalable authentication, certificate management, and real-time revocation. Traditional PKI systems struggle with excessive latency, centralized bottlenecks, and inefficient Certificate Revocation List (CRL) methods.
**Approach:** This work introduces EAZT-PKI (Edge-Assisted Zero-Trust PKI), a novel architecture utilizing Roadside Units (RSUs) for localized certificate validation and a federated learning (FL) model for dynamic trust score computation without raw data sharing.
**Tools and scale:** Implemented using SUMO, Python-based RSU services, OpenSSL, and Flower, experimental results demonstrate reduced verification delay, near real-time revocation, and improved scalability compared to conventional approaches.
