# CTR-M: A Certificate-Trust-Reliability Messaging Framework with Timed Operational Semantics for Secure Autonomous V2X Communication
Aditi Jha, Ravi Shankar Pandey · 2026 International Conference on Artificial Intelligence, Computer, Data Sciences and Applications (ACDSA) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/acdsa67686.2026.11468019 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular communication networks must ensure that the messages exchanged between vehicles need to be authentic, timely, and reliable. The existing V2X security schemes emphasize cryptographic validation but they rarely focus on how delay, behavioral trust, and wireless conditions collectively influence reliability.
**Approach:** To address these limitations, we propose the Certificate-Trust-Reliability Messaging (CTR-M) framework, where interactions between vehicles are modelled as a time behavior - driven dependent communication process.
