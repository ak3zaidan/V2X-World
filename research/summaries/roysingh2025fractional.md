# Fractional Collisions: A Framework for Risk Estimation of Counterfactual Conflicts using Autonomous Driving Behavior Simulations
Sreeja Roy-Singh, Sarvesh Kolekar, Daniel P. Bonny et al. · CVPR 2025 - Workshop on Data-Driven Autonomous Driving Simulation (DDADS) 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07540 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** We present a methodology for estimating collision risk from counterfactual simulated scenarios built on sensor data from automated driving systems (ADS) or naturalistic driving databases.
**Approach:** The framework's utility is also demonstrated on 250k miles of proprietary, open-loop sensor data collected on ADS test vehicles, re-simulated with an arbitrary ADS software release.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Our methodology predicted fractional collisions within 1% of ground truth collisions. Our ADS reduced naturalistic collisions by 4x and fractional collision risk by ~62%.
