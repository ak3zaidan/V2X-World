# Secure Lattice-Based Aggregate Signature Scheme for Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Sunil Prajapat, Deepika Gautam, Pankaj Kumar, Srinivas Jangirala, Ashok Kumar Das, Youngho Park et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2024.3383967 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Fast authentication and identity privacy in VANETs.
**Approach:** Lattice identity-based aggregate signature with batch verification and pseudo-identities; cloud storage.
**Tools and scale:** Analysis.
**Results:** Lower storage overhead, higher authentication efficiency.
