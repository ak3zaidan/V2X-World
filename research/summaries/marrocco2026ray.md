# In Ray Tracing We Trust? on the Usage of Ray Tracing for Vehicular Network Simulations
Simone Marrocco, Leonardo Maccari, Michele Segata · IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc69225.2026.11629156 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The evolution of GPUs has made the use of ray tracing techniques for network simulations feasible despite its computational demand.
**Approach:** Ray tracing is an appealing technique for cooperative driving simulations as well because vehicular scenarios are complex by nature and the proper estimation of communication performance is crucial. One recent yet already well-known ray tracing tool is Sionna which, thanks to the support by NVIDIA, has been growing fast and is already being widely adopted by the community.
