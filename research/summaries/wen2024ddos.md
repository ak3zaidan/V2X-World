# DDoS Attack Detection Based on Differential Privacy Graph Data Protection in IoV
Qianqian Wen, Zhong Li · Proceedings of the 2024 3rd International Conference on Algorithms, Data Mining, and Information Technology 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3701100.3701159 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With IoV’s rapid growth and widespread use, securing it has become a key focus in scholarly research.
**Approach:** In vehicle networking, DDoS attacks are prevalent and impactful. Existing deep learning methods for DDoS detection in IoV often yield superior results.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Existing deep learning methods for DDoS detection in IoV often yield superior results. However, these methods typically analyze individual flows, neglecting flow relationships and resulting in poor robustness.
