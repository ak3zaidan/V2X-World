# AVoD: Advanced Verify-on-Demand for Efficient Authentication against DoS Attacks in V2X Communication
Taehyoung Ko, Cheongmin Ji, Manpyo Hong · Security and Communication Networks 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2021/2890132 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** SCMS verification cost enables DoS in congestion.
**Approach:** Advanced verify-on-demand: classify similar messages and verify only the first per group per time unit.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Reduced verification time for large message volumes.
