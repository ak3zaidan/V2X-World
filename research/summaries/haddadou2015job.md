# A Job Market Signaling Scheme for Incentive and Trust Management in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Nadia Haddadou, Abderrezak Rachedi, Yacine Ghamri-Doudane · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2015 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2014.2360883 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Malicious and selfish nodes in VANET safety applications.
**Approach:** DTM2 distributed trust model based on job-market signalling with credits and sending costs.
**Tools and scale:** Theoretical and simulation analysis, urban and highway, 3600 s and 10800 s runs.
**Results:** Excludes 100% of malicious nodes without false positives; good reception with selfish nodes.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Nodes have limited reputation information beyond direct neighbours (noted).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — exclusion of malicious nodes and time to exclusion, relevant to direction 1's time-to-neutralise.
**Novelty implications:** Time-to-exclusion metrics exist for trust models; SCMS revocation timing is distinct.
