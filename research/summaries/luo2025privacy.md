# Privacy-Preserving Traffic Flow Prediction Based on a Spatiotemporal Hybrid Attention Network
Zhenyan Luo, Qiaoshi Lu, Zhaolin Li, Yifan Shu et al. · 2025 IEEE International Conference on Blockchain Technology and Information Security (ICBCTIS) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icbctis66509.2025.11387618 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With rapid urbanization, traffic flow prediction has become a crucial technology in intelligent transportation systems, alleviating congestion, enhancing traffic efficiency, and supporting decision-making in traffic management and scheduling. However, traffic data often contain sensitive personal mobility information, posing critical challenges for privacy protection and data security.
**Approach:** To address these challenges, this paper proposes a privacy-aware Spatiotemporal Hybrid Attention Network (STHAN), which integrates graph neural networks, Transformer, and LSTM architectures to jointly capture complex spatiotemporal dependencies.
**Tools and scale:** Experiments conducted on two public datasets demonstrate that STHAN achieves superior short- and long-term forecasting accuracy and robustness, while maintaining a security-preserving design that reduces risks of privacy breaches in intelligent transportation systems.
**Results:** Experiments conducted on two public datasets demonstrate that STHAN achieves superior short- and long-term forecasting accuracy and robustness, while maintaining a security-preserving design that reduces risks of privacy breaches in intelligent transportation systems.
