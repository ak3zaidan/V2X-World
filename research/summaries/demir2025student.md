# Student Research Track: Twin the Drive: An HGBR-based Machine Learning Model as a Vehicular Digital Twin for Risk Detection
Cansu Demir, Narges Mehran, Alexander Meschtscherjakov · Adjunct Proceedings of the 17th International Conference on Automotive User Interfaces and Interactive Vehicular Applications 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3744335.3762326 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Interpretable vehicular DTs for risk detection.
**Approach:** Gradient-boosted regression detecting unsafe headway, harsh braking and TTC.
**Tools and scale:** NGSIM.
**Results:** Approximates headway and braking; under-predicts TTC risks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Simple features cannot model complex interactions for TTC.
**Overlap with our engine:** none - though TTC/harsh braking are safety metrics we also compute.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
