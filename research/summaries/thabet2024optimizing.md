# Optimizing Dijkstra's Algorithm for Managing Urban Traffic Using Simulation of Urban Mobility (Sumo) Software
Amenah Sufyan Mhmood Thabet, Ahmet Zengin · Annals of Mathematics and Physics 2024 · https://doi.org/10.17352/amp.000124 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Among the challenges of urbanization is traffic management as a measure of growth and progress.
**Approach:** Recent population growth has resulted in a significant increase in vehicles, causing traffic jams that are challenging for the existing transportation networks. This congestion affects other services, including public transit, airports, road maintenance, and pollution caused by emissions of CO2 and other gases.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: SUMO; scale not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** This paper focuses on an improved Dijkstra algorithm based on traffic congestion levels to address the above problems. Improved Dijkstra algorithm can provide (a) real data collected from the map via OpenStreetMap, (b) Add four features to SUMO(Simulation of Urban Mobility) simulator software (time period, rush-hour, number of vehicles, and routing algorithm), (c) it could know congestion level for roads (d) rerouting vehicles to avoid traffic congestion.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
