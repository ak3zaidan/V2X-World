# Evaluating the Dedicated Short-range Communication for Connected Vehicles against Network Security Attacks
Tu Le, Ingy Elsayed-Aly, Weizhao Jin, Seunghan Ryu, Guy Verrier, Tamjid Al Rahat et al. · International Conference on Vehicle Technology and Intelligent Transport Systems 2020 · https://doi.org/10.5220/0009355500370044 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** : According to the National Highway Trafﬁc Safety Administration, there are more than 5 million road crashes every year in the U.S.
**Approach:** More than 90 people die in car crashes every day. Even though the number of people sur-viving crashes has increased signiﬁcantly thanks to safety features, such as airbags and anti-lock brakes, many people experience permanent injuries.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: DSRC
**Results:** Our results show that DSRC is vulnerable to these dangerous attacks and such attacks can be easily implemented by adversaries without signiﬁcant resources. Based on our evaluation, we also discuss potential countermeasures to better improve the security and safety of DSRC and connected vehicles.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- With the rapid development of autonomous driving technology, vehicles in the near future will be able to operate completely without human drivers, increasing the need of reliable connected vehicle technologies.
