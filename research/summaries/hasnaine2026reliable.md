# Reliable mmWave LoS/NLoS Classification via Large Wireless Model Embeddings
Quazi Rian Hasnaine, Eslam Hasan, Mohamed I. Ismail, Rhana Elsayed et al. · SoutheastCon 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/southeastcon63549.2026.11476542 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The evolution toward 6 G networks necessitates ultra-reliable operation in millimeter-wave (mmWave) and subterahertz bands, where propagation is fundamentally constrained by high path loss and susceptibility to blockage. Consequently, accurate prediction of Line-of-Sight (LoS) and Non-Line-of-Sight (NLoS) is critical for effective beam management and link ...
**Approach:** This paper proposes a real-world LoS/NLoS prediction framework using the DeepSense 6 G dataset and investigates the benefits of transformer-based channel embeddings generated by the large wireless model (LWM).
**Tools and scale:** These results demonstrate the importance of foundation-model-based representations and real-world data for reliable link-state prediction in emerging 6G networks.
**Results:** Our proposed LWM-based prediction model outperforms state-of-the-art models by more than $9 \%, 15 \%$, and 12% in LoS, NLoS, and average scenarios, respectively, in terms of accuracy. These results demonstrate the importance of foundation-model-based representations and real-world data for reliable link-state prediction in emerging 6G networks.
