# Integrating Generative AI with Network Digital Twin for 6G: An Edge-Cloud Collaborative Approach
Wanqing Guan, Pei-Chun Li, Haijun Zhang, Yuan Wu · IEEE Communications Magazine 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mcom.001.2500002 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The sixth-generation (6G) of wireless systems aim to support the emerging Internet of Everything (IoE) applications and provide Artificial Intelligence-Generated Content (AIGC) services to diverse mobile devices. The strict communication requirements of various IoE applications and the personalized user requirements of mobile AIGC services pose great challenges to the 6G network management.
**Approach:** In this article, a hybrid deployment architecture of a joint DT for 6G network is introduced, integrating with the generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) models which are trained and deployed in an edge-cloud collaborative way.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 6G, digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Creating a digital twin (DT) to represent the wireless networks can facilitate the achievement of zero-touch and autonomous wireless network management with accurate modeling of the physical network and proactive analysis of its behavior.
