# Eviction of Misbehaving and Faulty Nodes in Vehicular Networks
M. Raya, Panos Papadimitratos, I. Aad, D. Jungels et al. · IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications 2007 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jsac.2007.071006 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Certificate revocation alone is too slow to evict misbehaving or faulty nodes when infrastructure is sparse.
**Approach:** Framework of protocols for local identification and containment of misbehaving nodes (neighbour-based) and their eventual eviction via the CA. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Analysis and simulation (details in full text).
