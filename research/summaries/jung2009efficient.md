# An Efficient Anonymous Authentication Protocol Based on Multiple Anonymous Certificates in VANET
Chae-Duk Jung, Chul Sur, Youngho Park, Kyung-Hyune Rhee · The KIPS Transactions PartC 2009 · https://doi.org/10.3745/kipstc.2009.16c.5.589 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Overhead of anonymous certificate issuance by TA/RSU (Korean paper, English abstract).
**Approach:** RSU issues multiple short-time anonymous certificates after one mutual authentication.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation (RSU service rate, vehicle computation).
**Results:** More efficient than prior best protocol; maintains unlinkability even with multiple compromised RSUs.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — RSU-issued certificates, unlike SCMS.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on our novelty claims; background only.
