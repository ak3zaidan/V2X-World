# An Efficient Certificateless Signature verification scheme using Bilinear pairing for VANETs
Md Badhesha, ArunSekar Rajasekaran, Gulfishan Firdose Ahmed, T. Prabakaran et al. · 2025 Seventh International Conference on Research in Computational Intelligence and Communication Networks (ICRCICN) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icrcicn68210.2025.11364948 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) demand se cure, efficient, and privacy-preserving authentication mechanisms to support reliable vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I) and vehicle tovehicle (V2V) communication. Existing schemes often suffer from high communication overhead, scalability issues, or inadequate protection against active adversaries.
**Approach:** To overcome these limitations, this paper proposes a lightweight bilinear pairingbased authentication protocol that enables secure registration, pseudonym generation, and mutual authentication between vehicles and Roadside Units (RSUs).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** The new scheme reduces communication overhead with compact cryptographic operations and guarantees a strong level of privacy preservation through the use of identity-based keys and dynamic pseudonyms.
