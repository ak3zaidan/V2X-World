# BOppCL: Blockchain-Enabled Opportunistic Federated Learning Applied in Intelligent Transportation Systems
Qiong Li, Wennan Wang, Yizhao Zhu, Zuo-Bin Ying · Electronics 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics13010136 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Privacy and trust in opportunistic federated learning for ITS.
**Approach:** Blockchain-enabled opportunistic FL with incentives, secure gradient distribution, and BFT inside DPoS.
**Tools and scale:** MNIST, SVHN, CIFAR-10 experiments.
**Results:** Convergence and accuracy comparable to traditional OppCL.
