# An Efficient Data-Driven Traffic Prediction Framework for Network Digital Twin
Haihan Nan, Ruidong Li, Xiaoyan Zhu, Jianfeng Ma et al. · IEEE Network 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mnet.2023.3335952 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Network digital twin, as a fundamental enabling technology in emerging network scenarios and applications, establishes a connection between the physical and virtual networks through real-time representation and synchronization of physical entities. The value of network digital twins lies in their ability to accurately model future network states and behaviors, facilitating numerous downstream network tasks such as network optimization and management.
**Approach:** This article first examines the general forms and characteristics of various network traffic based on specific datasets. Subsequently, we introduce a multi-module data-driven framework for network traffic prediction to effectively cater to diverse data types and downstream task requirements.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** A case study of cellular traffic prediction based on traffic characteristics and requirements is proposed to demonstrate the practicability of our framework.
