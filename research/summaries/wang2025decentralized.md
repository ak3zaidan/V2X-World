# A Decentralized Oracle Network Constructed From Weighted Schnorr Multisignature
Zhi-Wei Wang · IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnsm.2025.3539615 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** However, purely relying on the cryptographic scheme cannot fully identify each participating node’s reputation; thus, we design three on-chain contracts for recording and identifying the oracle nodes’ reputation and realizing the reword mechanism.
**Approach:** Thus, we present a weighted Schnorr multisignature to meet this requirement, which is proven to be unforgeable. We implement our system over the Ethereum platform and analyze its on-chain and off-chain costs.
