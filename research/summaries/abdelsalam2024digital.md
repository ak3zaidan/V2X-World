# A Digital Twin prototype for traffic sign recognition of a learning-enabled autonomous vehicle
Mohamed AbdElSalam, Loai Ali, Saddek Bensalem, Weicheng He, Panagiotis Katsaros, Nikolaos Kekatos et al. · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.09097 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we present a novel digital twin prototype for a learning-enabled self-driving vehicle.
**Approach:** The primary objective of this digital twin is to perform traffic sign recognition and lane keeping. The digital twin architecture relies on co-simulation and uses the Functional Mock-up Interface and SystemC Transaction Level Modeling standards.
