# Enhancing Vehicular Communication Systems: Predicting Signal-to-Noise Ratio Using Machine Learning Models
Almas Begum, Rajendra Thilahar C, Monikaa R, Gopinath M et al. · 2024 International Conference on Electronic Systems and Intelligent Computing (ICESIC) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icesic61777.2024.10846759 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle communication systems facilitate both vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) and vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I) connections, playing a crucial role in Intelligent Transportation Systems. Accurate estimation of signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) is necessary for modern communication systems to be reliable and efficacious.
**Approach:** This study aims to identify which machine learning model achieves the highest accuracy in predicting SNR. The Random Forest model outperformed all other models we tested with an MSE of 0.0058, which translates to an RMSE of 0.0762 and an R2 score over the roof.9997 Yes, they performed well also but Random Forest outperformed Gradient Boosting and Linear Regression in all the metrics.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: testbed. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** The models were built using a comprehensive dataset containing wireless measurements from the iV2V industrial testbeds. The iV2I+ dataset records data from an industrial site where it acquires around 16~h of the autonomous cleaning robot connected to a private cellular network, meanwhile, the iV2V has accumulated about 10 hours in side-link situations between three Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs). The models were analyzed based on performance measures including R2 Score, Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE), and Mean Squared Error (MSE).
