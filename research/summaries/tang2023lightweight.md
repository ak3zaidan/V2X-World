# Lightweight Reputation Management for Multi-Role Internet of Vehicles
Chaogang Tang, Huaming Wu, Shuo Xiao · IEEE Internet of Things Magazine 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iotm.001.2200230 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the rapid development of the Internet of Vehicles (IoV), smart vehicles can fulfill multiple roles in either the information-centric IoV or the task-oriented IoV.
**Approach:** However, malicious vehicles may undermine the trustiness of vehicles towards each other, and further damage these IoV networks. Given the multiple roles undertaken by vehicles in IoV networks, we aim to design a lightweight reputation-based mechanism for a hybrid IoV network in this article.
