# Analysis of Machine Learning Algorithms for DDoS Attack Detection in Connected Cars Environment
Ali El Attar, Ayoub Wehby, Fadlallah Chbib, Hassane Aissaoui Mehrez, Ahmad Fadlallah, Joel Hachem et al. · 2023 Eighth International Conference On Mobile And Secure Services (MobiSecServ) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mobisecserv58080.2023.10329152 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cyberattacks against the Internet of Vehicles (IoV) will continue to evolve as the industry continues to adopt new and advanced connected technologies.
**Approach:** These advances should result in a complex ecosystem that integrates different technologies (5G, 6G, Cloud, IoT, etc.) and presents a large attack surface. Denial of service (DoS) attacks are among the most dangerous attacks against connected vehicles, where an attacker overwhelm the network with random generated messages.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G
**Results:** The obtained results demonstrate that XGBoost, Random Forest, and SVM algorithms have a very high detection precision.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, there is no comprehensive comparative analysis of their efficiency in the IoV context.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
