# An ML-Based Authentication for Privacy Preservation in a Distributed Edge-Enabled Internet of Vehicles
Mian Ahmad Jan, Sohail Abbas, Houbing Song, Rahim Khan · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2024.3483275 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In the Internet of Vehicles (IoV), privacy preservation is a major challenge due to the mobility of vehicles and their resource-constrained nature.
**Approach:** Thus, lightweight but reliable authentication schemes need to be designed to combat these attacks. To addresses these challenges, we propose an ML-based authentication scheme that trains and classifies the vehicles at the edge servers in a distributed manner, preserves the privacy of communicating entities and minimizes the bandwidth consumption and delay experienced by the vehicles.
