# Agile calibration process of full-stack simulation frameworks for V2X communications
Ioannis Mavromatis, Andrea Tassi, Robert J. Piechocki, Andrew Nix · 2017 IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference (VNC) 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc.2017.8275604 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Simulations can be trusted only when they agree with real trials, which are imperfect.
**Approach:** Procedure to macroscopically calibrate a full-stack network simulator (Veins) against trial data, from ideal-like scenarios to full-stack optimisation.
**Tools and scale:** Veins; real 802.11p trial transceivers; multiple MCS.
**Results:** Calibrated Veins tracks trials; at 64-QAM 2/3 and 3/4 Veins outperforms trial at 550 m but is significantly worse at 700 m.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future research avenues discussed for calibration (further full-stack optimisation).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — simulator calibration against real trials, relevant to our validation claims.
**Novelty implications:** Calibration of Veins against trials is prior art.
