# An effective misbehavior detection model using artificial neural network for vehicular ad hoc network applications
Fuad A. Ghaleb, Anazida Zainal, Murad A. Rassam, Fathey Mohammed · 2017 IEEE Conference on Application, Information and Network Security (AINS) 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ains.2017.8270417 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Mobility misbehaviour in VANETs harms safety and efficiency; ML little studied for MBD.
**Approach:** Four-phase MBD model (acquisition, sharing, analysis, decision) with new features and a feed-forward/back-propagation ANN classifier.
**Tools and scale:** NGSIM real-world traffic dataset with injected attacker data.
**Results:** Significant improvement over a baseline model (no numbers in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - ML MBD offline.
**Novelty implications:** No impact on closed-loop claims.
