# A Differentiated Identity Authentication Protocol for VANETs Based on Safety Level
Yi Yu, Xiaoyan Huang, Ke Zhang, Fan Wu et al. · International Conference on Speech Technology and Human-Computer Dialogue 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icct56141.2022.10072685 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs), authentication is a crucial security service for both inter-vehicle and vehicle-roadside unit communications. Most of traditional protocols do not consider the impact of vehicular safety on authentication efficiency, thus implementing the same authentication process for all vehicles, which leads to large overhead and inflexible authentication.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a differentiated identity authentication protocol based on vehicular safety level to reduce the authentication overhead and increase flexibility.
**Tools and scale:** Most of traditional protocols do not consider the impact of vehicular safety on authentication efficiency, thus implementing the same authentication process for all vehicles, which leads to large overhead and inflexible authentication.
**Results:** Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed protocol can not only effectively improve the flexibility of vehicular authentication, but also have advantages in reducing computation delay and communication overhead.
