# Attack Impact and Misbehavior Detection in Vehicular Platoons
Konstantinos Kalogiannis, Mohammad Khodaei, Weaam Mostafa Nemr Mohamed Bayaa, Panos Papadimitratos · Proceedings of the 15th ACM Conference on Security and Privacy in Wireless and Mobile Networks 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3507657.3528552 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Insiders with valid credentials falsify kinematics; state-of-the-art MBD fails during platoon manoeuvres.
**Approach:** Systematic attacks on platoon controllers (falsification, jamming, collusion, two novel manoeuvre attacks); fixes for middle-join/leave privilege escalation; MDS over the platoon lifetime; extensible cyber-physical simulation framework.
**Tools and scale:** Cyber-physical platoon simulation (Plexe/Veins based); VPKI context from the KTH group.
**Results:** MDS F1 of 87% across platoon lifetime including manoeuvres.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Framework can be extended to other driving automation functions.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — credentialed insider attacks executed in closed-loop co-simulation with MBD and a VPKI context, overlapping our attack-in-simulation capability.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first closed-loop insider attack plus MBD in platoon co-simulation; MA-driven revocation and time-to-neutralise not covered.
