# Secure and Efficient Privacy-Preserving Authentication Scheme for 5G Software Defined Vehicular Networks
Jiaqi Huang, Y. Qian, R. Hu · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2020.2996574 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular networks provide various applications for vehicles to improve road safety and traffic efficiency as well as infotainment.
**Approach:** Many solutions have been proposed in the past decade, but most of them either rely too much on the ideal tamper-proofed devices or not being efficient enough for scenarios with high vehicle density. Noting the great potential of 5G technology, in this paper we explore a new architecture of 5G software defined vehicular network and propose a secure and efficient privacy preserving authentication scheme for vehicular networks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** Meanwhile, in recent years, great progresses have been made in both the Long-Term Evolution (LTE) and the fifth-generation (5G) wireless network based vehicular networks. The 5G enabled vehicular networks are envisioned to support higher data transmission rate and a larger number of connected devices than the 802.11p and LTE based networks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "However, how to efficiently secure vehicular networks with privacy-preserving remains a big challenge."
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
