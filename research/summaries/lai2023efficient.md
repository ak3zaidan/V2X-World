# Efficient Group Authentication and Key Agreement Scheme for Vehicular Digital Twin
Chengzhe Lai, Meng Li, Guanjie Li, Dong Zheng · 2023 IEEE/CIC International Conference on Communications in China (ICCC) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccc57788.2023.10233652 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As a novel paradigm, vehicular digital twin (VDT) aims to improve the passengers’ driving experience by using digital twins deployed in the cloud to communicate with each other.
**Approach:** However, it is vulnerable to encounter malicious attacker which can compromise the sharing data between digital twins and further impacts the safe driving of vehicle in the physical world. In this paper, we propose an efficient group authentication and key agreement method for vehicular digital twin, named GVDT.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** In particular, we design a new lightweight ring cooperative authentication only needs one round to achieve implementing mutual authentication of members. The analysis results demonstrate that the proposed scheme not only satisfies the security requirements of VDT, but also reduces the computation and communication costs efficiently.
