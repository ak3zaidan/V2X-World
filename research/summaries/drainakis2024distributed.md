# Distributed Predictive QoS in Automotive Environments Under Concept Drift
Georgios Drainakis, Panagiotis Pantazopoulos, Konstantinos V. Katsaros, Vasilis Sourlas, Angelos Amditis, Dimitra I. Kaklamani · 2024 IFIP Networking Conference (IFIP Networking) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.23919/ifipnetworking62109.2024.10619805 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Concept drift in vehicular federated pQoS.
**Approach:** DareFL drift detection and scheduled retraining preserving FL privacy; releases a high-fidelity vehicular network simulator.
**Tools and scale:** Own released simulator; two drift scenarios.
**Results:** Comparable accuracy with up to 70% network resource saving.
