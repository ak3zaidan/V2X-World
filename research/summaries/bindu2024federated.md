# Federated Learning Framework for Intrusion Detection System in Internet of Vehicles with Memory-Augmented Deep Autoencoder
G. Hima Bindu, Deepthi Reddy Dasari · Research Square 2024 · https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-5007599/v1 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract Intrusion detection systems (IDS) are crucial for maintaining the security and integrity of Internet of Vehicles (IoV) configurations.
**Approach:** However, traditional IDS systems face issues such as scalability, flexibility in changing IoV settings, and privacy concerns due to centralized data collection. The increasing number of networked cars in the IoV poses significant security concerns, including identifying and mitigating cyberattacks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Experimental results show that on the CSE-CIC-IDS-2018 dataset, FLF-MADAE has the highest accuracy level of 99.12% and an F1 score of 99.21%; for the car hacking dataset, MADAE has the highest accuracy level of 99.24% and an F1 score of 98.77%.

- However, traditional IDS systems face issues such as scalability, flexibility in changing IoV settings, and privacy concerns due to centralized data collection.
- However, autoencoders can generalize and reconstruct anomalies, potentially causing them to go undetected.
