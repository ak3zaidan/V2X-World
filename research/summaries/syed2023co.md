# A Co-Simulation Study to Assess the Impacts of Connected and Autonomous Vehicles on Traffic Flow Stability during Hurricane Evacuation
Zaheen E Muktadi Syed, Samiul Hasan · arXiv 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.06267 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Hurricane evacuation has become a major problem for the coastal residents of the United States.
**Approach:** Devastating hurricanes have threatened the lives and infrastructure of coastal communities and caused billions of dollars in damage. There is a need for better traffic management strategies to improve the safety and mobility of evacuation traffic.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: real-world, SUMO; scale not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** However, in a co-simulation study, to evaluate the effect of CAVs in the same evacuation traffic scenario, results indicate that with 25 percentage of CAVs the number of potential collisions decrease up to 42.5 percentage.
