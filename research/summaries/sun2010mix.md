# Mix-zones Deployment for Location Privacy Preservation in Vehicular Communications
Yipin Sun, Xiangyu Su, Baokang Zhao, Jinshu Su · 2010 10th IEEE International Conference on Computer and Information Technology 2010 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cit.2010.472 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Location privacy is a main concern in vehicular communications where vehicles have to broadcast traffic routine information frequently.
**Approach:** A promising approach to prevent a vehicle from been tracked suggests vehicle to change pseudonyms in regions called mix-zones, where the adversary cannot eavesdrop the vehicular communication. However, the deployment problem of mix-zones has not been well addressed in previously reported works.
