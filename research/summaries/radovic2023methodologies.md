# Methodologies for Future Vehicular Digital Twins
Danilo Radovic, Markus Hofer, Faruk Pasic, Enrico M. Vitucci, Aleksei Fedorov, Thomas Zemen · arXiv 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.09902 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The role of wireless communications in various domains of intelligent transportation systems is significant; it is evident that dependable message exchange between nodes (cars, bikes, pedestrians, infrastructure, etc.) has to be guaranteed to fulfill the stringent requirements for future transportation systems.
**Approach:** A precise site-specific digital twin is seen as a key enabler for the cost-effective development and validation of future vehicular communication systems. Furthermore, achieving a realistic digital twin for dependable wireless communications requires accurate measurement, modeling, and emulation of wireless communication channels.
