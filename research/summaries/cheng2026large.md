# Large Wireless Foundation Models: Stronger over Bigger
Xiang Cheng, Boxun Liu, Xuanyu Liu, Xuesong Cai · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10963 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** AI-communication integration is widely regarded as a core enabling technology for 6G. Most existing AI-based physical-layer designs rely on task-specific models that are separately tailored to individual modules, resulting in poor generalization.
**Approach:** Therefore, we introduce the concept of large wireless foundation models (LWFMs) and present a novel framework for empowering the physical layer with foundation models under wireless constraints. Specifically, we propose two paradigms for realizing LWFMs, including leveraging existing general-purpose foundation models and building novel wireless foundation models.
