# Risk-Aware Joint Communication and Control Resource Allocation for Secure Vehicle Platoons
Zhiwei Lin, Mingrui Zheng, Zebin Huang, Shuaiyu Zhou · IEEE Wireless Communications Letters 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/lwc.2025.3637860 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Platoon control, communication and security (authentication delay, attacks) are optimised separately.
**Approach:** Risk-aware joint control-communication-security framework solved by DR-PDA (LQR control, SCA surrogates, Wasserstein-CVaR robustness). (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Numerical simulation.
**Results:** Low total cost, reduced leakage and attack success, small optimality gap; removing modelled authentication delay lowers cost but increases leakage.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — explicitly models the latency-security trade-off of authentication delay on control, related to direction 2.
**Novelty implications:** Safety/control cost of authentication delay has been modelled analytically for platoons; open: measuring it in a full-stack simulator with realistic PKI.
