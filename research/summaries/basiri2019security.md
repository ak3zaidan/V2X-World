# Security of Vehicle Platooning: A Game-Theoretic Approach
Mohammad Hossein Basiri, Mohammad Pirani, Nasser L. Azad, Sebastian Fischmeister · IEEE Access 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2019.2961002 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, conclusion/future-work sections)

**Problem:** In this paper, we study the security of a vehicle platoon exposed to cyber attacks using a game-theoretic approach.
**Approach:** The platoon topologies under investigation are directed (called predecessor following) or undirected (bidirectional) weighted graphs. The edge weights specify the quality of the communication links between the vehicles in both the unidirectional/bidirectional data transfer environments.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: platoon/CACC attacks).
**Results:** The simulation and experimental results conducted on a vehicle platoon setup using Robotic Operating System (ROS) demonstrate the effectiveness of our analyses.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
