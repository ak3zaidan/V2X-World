# A Comprehensive Survey on the Applications of Blockchain for Securing Vehicular Networks
Tejasvi Alladi, Vinay Chamola, Nishad Sahu, Vishnu Venkatesh et al. · IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/comst.2022.3160925 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular networks promise features such as traffic management, route scheduling, data exchange, entertainment, and much more. With any large-scale technological integration comes the challenge of providing security.
**Approach:** This study catalogues some of the notable efforts in this direction over the last few years. We analyze around 75 blockchain-based security schemes for vehicular networks from an application, security, and blockchain perspective.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting (VANET/IoV/V2X). Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** The abstract gives no specific quantitative results.
