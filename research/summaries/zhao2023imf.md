# IMF-PR: An Improved Morton-Filter-Based Pseudonym-Revocation Scheme in VANETs
Cong Zhao, Jiayu Qi, Tianhan Gao, Xinyang Deng · Sensors 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s23084066 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** CRL generation, storage and transmission costs.
**Approach:** Improved Morton-filter CRL with distributed management.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Transmission delay 10.3% and 74.9% lower than C2RL and C3RL; query throughput 25% and 68.8% higher.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: anonymous authentication for V2I and V2V.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — CRL distribution efficiency; our engine uses SCMS linkage-seed CRLs.
**Novelty implications:** Compressed CRL structures exist.
