# SVEA: an experimental testbed for evaluating V2X use-cases
Frank J. Jiang, Mustafa Al-Janabi, Tobias Bolin, Karl H. Johansson, Jonas Martensson · 2022 IEEE 25th International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/itsc55140.2022.9922544 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** V2X use-case evaluation is mostly simulation-only.
**Approach:** Open-source SVEA 1/10-scale testbed with communication stack; platooning, speed regulation from roadside camera, remote driving.
**Tools and scale:** Small-scale vehicles, real networks.
**Results:** Three use-cases implemented.
