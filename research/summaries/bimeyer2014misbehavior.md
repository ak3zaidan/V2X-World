# Misbehavior detection and attacker identification in vehicular ad hoc networks
N. Bißmeyer · Technischen Universität Darmstadt 2014 · https://doi.org/10.26083/tuprints-00004257 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Detect misbehaviour and identify responsible attackers or faulty nodes to exclude them.
**Approach:** Dissertation proposing location-based evaluation methods and frameworks; tested in outdoor field operational tests and trials with test vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation and real field operational tests.
**Results:** Shows processing of fake info affects safety and efficiency; field tests gave new insights on MBD and attacker identification.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Most existing solutions only tested in simulation (motivating real tests).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — MBD and attacker identification for exclusion; real-data validation.
**Novelty implications:** Field-tested MBD exists; closed-loop revocation timing still open.
