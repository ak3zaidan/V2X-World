# Distributed online Data Anomaly Detection for connected vehicles
N. Negi, Ons Jelassi, H. Chaouchi, S. Clémençon · Digital Signal Processing and Signal Processing Education Workshop 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icaiic48513.2020.9065280 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Wireless connectivity evolution increased the volume of acquired available data in different Internet of Things based industries. Data quality and processing time are the most challenging issues for successful data analytic algorithms to produce efficient business intelligence.
**Approach:** In this article we tackle these two points and propose a distributed framework for data anomaly detection. In this article, we use LSTM model and apply distributed learning approach to train the model.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset, deep learning (mentioned in abstract)
