# Measurements and Modeling of Millimeter-Wave Vehicle-to-Vehicle Propagation With Vehicle Obstructions
Xichen Liu, Shuning Liu, Wangyang Zhou, Lin Yang, Guangrong Yue · IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/twc.2025.3540968 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Gap in mmWave V2V propagation measurements with vehicle obstructions.
**Approach:** Measurement campaign with large/small obstructing vehicles in urban street and underground parking; deterministic and stochastic path loss models; small-scale fading analysis.
**Tools and scale:** mmWave channel sounding; real data.
**Results:** Stochastic path-loss model for LOS and OLOS; analysis of RMS delay spread, K-factor and correlations (no numbers in abstract).
