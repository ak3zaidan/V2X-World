# MetaMind: General and Cognitive World Models in Multi-Agent Systems by Meta-Theory of Mind
Ling-Yi Wang, Rashed Shelim, Walid Saad, Naren Ramakrishna · arXiv.org 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00808 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A major challenge for world models in multi-agent systems is to understand interdependent agent dynamics, predict interactive multi-agent trajectories, and plan over long horizons with collective awareness, without centralized supervision or explicit communication.
**Approach:** In this paper, MetaMind, a general and cognitive world model for multi-agent systems that leverages a novel meta-theory of mind (Meta-ToM) framework, is proposed. Through MetaMind, each agent learns not only to predict and plan over its own beliefs, but also to inversely reason goals and beliefs from its own behavior trajectories.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: simulation.
**Results:** Extended simulation results on diverse multi-agent tasks demonstrate that MetaMind can achieve superior task performance and outperform baselines in few-shot multi-agent generalization.
