# <scp>VehiGAN</scp> : Generative Adversarial Networks for Adversarially Robust V2X Misbehavior Detection Systems
Md Hasan Shahriar, Mohammad Raashid Ansari, Jean-Philippe Monteuuis, Md Shahedul Haque, Cong Chen, Jonathan Petit et al. · ACM Transactions on Cyber-Physical Systems 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3745787 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ML MBDS generalise poorly and are vulnerable to adversarial ML.
**Approach:** Threat model for stealthy misbehaviour masked by adversarial attacks; ensemble of WGANs (VehiGAN) with physics-guided preprocessing.
**Tools and scale:** VASP V2X attack simulation tool generating a dataset with 35 misbehaviours.
**Results:** Outperforms baseline in 20 of 35 misbehaviours; excels on multi-field advanced misbehaviours.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract (truncated).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — uses VASP (attack simulator) and adversarial MBD.
**Novelty implications:** Adversarial-robust MBD exists.
