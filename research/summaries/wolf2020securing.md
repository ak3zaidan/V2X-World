# Securing CACC: Strategies for Mitigating Data Injection Attacks
Michael Wolf, Alexander Willecke, Johannes Müller, Keno Garlichs, Thomas Griebel, L. Wolf et al. · IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc51378.2020.9318396 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Protecting CACC controllers from injected false data.
**Approach:** Categorisation of mitigation strategies; proposes a controller with parameter-dependent reaction to an MDS-signalled suspiciousness value.
**Tools and scale:** Platoon simulation study.
**Results:** Improves safety and efficiency under attack but reveals a fundamental safety-efficiency trade-off.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Fundamental trade-off between safety and efficiency of the mitigation.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — explicitly couples MDS output to vehicle control and quantifies safety/efficiency trade-off, overlapping directions 1 and 2 at the local (not PKI) level.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first to show MBD-response safety/efficiency trade-off; infrastructure revocation in the loop remains open.
