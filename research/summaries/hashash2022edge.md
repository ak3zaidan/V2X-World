# Edge Continual Learning for Dynamic Digital Twins over Wireless Networks
Omar Hashash, Christina Chaccour, Walid Saad · arXiv 2022 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.04795 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Digital twins (DTs) constitute a critical link between the real-world and the metaverse.
**Approach:** In this paper, a novel edge continual learning framework is proposed to accurately model the evolving affinity between a physical twin (PT) and its corresponding cyber twin (CT) while maintaining their utmost synchronization. As the de-synchronization time continues to increase over sequential episodes, an elastic weight consolidation (EWC) technique that regularizes the DT history is proposed to limit de-synchronization time.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** Ultimately, the proposed framework achieves a simultaneously accurate and synchronous CT model that is robust to catastrophic forgetting. Simulation results show that the proposed solution can achieve an accuracy of 90 % while guaranteeing a minimal desynchronization time.
