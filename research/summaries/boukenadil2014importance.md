# Importance of Realistic Mobility Models for Vanet Network Simulation
Bahidja Boukenadil · International journal of Computer Networks &amp; Communications 2014 · https://doi.org/10.5121/ijcnc.2014.6513 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** VANET protocol evaluation depends on realistic mobility.
**Approach:** Compares MOVE and CityMob mobility generators for routing protocol simulation.
**Tools and scale:** NS-2-style routing simulation; MOVE (SUMO-based) and CityMob.
**Results:** Protocol performance is greatly affected by the mobility model (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Plan to compare other mobility models in future work.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — mobility realism argument only.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
