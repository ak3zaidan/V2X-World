# Autonomous Incident Resolution at Hyperscale: An Agentic AI Architecture for Network Operations
Arun Malik · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09122 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Hyperscale incident response cannot keep pace.
**Approach:** Multi-agent architecture with skills, runbooks, progressive autonomy, rollback.
**Tools and scale:** Production at a major cloud provider.
**Results:** Autonomous resolution above 90% for common incident categories.
