# A survey and taxonomy of distributed certificate authorities in mobile ad hoc networks
Mohammad Masdari, S. Jabbehdari, M. Ahmadi, S. Hashemi et al. · EURASIP Journal on Wireless Communications and Networking 2011 · https://doi.org/10.1186/1687-1499-2011-112 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Certificate authorities (CAs) are the main components of PKI that enable us for providing basic security services in wired networks and Internet.
**Approach:** But, we cannot use centralized CAs, in mobile ad hoc networks (MANETs). So, many efforts have been made to adapt CA to the special characteristics of MANETs and new concepts such as distributed CAs (DCAs) have been proposed that distribute the functionality of CA between MANET nodes.
