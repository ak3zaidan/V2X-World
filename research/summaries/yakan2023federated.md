# Federated Learning for V2X Misbehavior Detection System in 5G Edge Networks
Hadi Yakan, Ilhem Fajjari, Nadjib Aitsaadi, Cédric Adjih · International Conference on Modeling, Analysis and Simulation of Wireless and Mobile Systems 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3616388.3617533 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Protecting 5G V2N application servers from V2X attacks.
**Approach:** Federated learning MBD inside the 5G core/edge.
**Tools and scale:** Extensive emulations vs centralized and distributed approaches.
**Results:** Accuracy 98.4%, precision 99.3%, detection rate 96.9%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — network-side MBD over cellular.
**Novelty implications:** Edge/core MBD exists.
