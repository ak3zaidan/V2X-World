# Privacy-Preserving Digital Twin Framework for Autonomous and Connected Vehicles
Rahanatu Suleiman, Akshita Maradapu Vera Venkata Sai · 2026 IEEE 23rd Consumer Communications &amp;amp; Networking Conference (CCNC) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ccnc65079.2026.11366505 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Digital Twins (VDTs) are emerging as key components in Autonomous and Connected Vehicles (ACVs), enabling real-time monitoring and threat detection. However, in decentralized environments, collaborative learning among VDTs raises significant privacy concerns such as data leakage, re-identification risks and compromised VDTs.
**Approach:** This paper presents a decentralized Federated Learning (FL) framework with Differential Privacy (DP) to enable secure and privacy-preserving threat detection across VDTs using a Bi-directional Long Short-Term Memory Autoencoder (Bi-LSTM AE) within an FL setting. We incorporate DP into the FL process to further enhance privacy and assess its impact across different clients.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: federated, digital twin, real-world. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Without DP, the model achieved an F1-score of about 90% and AUC of 95%, performing better than similar methods. Although, recall remains high, there is a trade-off in precision and accuracy resulting in a global F1-score of 77%.
