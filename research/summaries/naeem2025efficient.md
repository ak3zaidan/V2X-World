# Efficient Intrusion Detection with Improved Attention-Based CNN-BiGRU Architecture for Intelligent Vehicular Networks
Amna Naeem, Muazzam Ali Khan, Muhammad Hanif, Tamara Kokenovna Zhukabayeva, Jawad Elsayed Ahmad, Muhammad Shahbaz Khan · IEEE Vehicular Technology Conference 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2025-fall65116.2025.11310092 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Intrusion detection in vehicular networks.
**Approach:** Inception, skip connection, BiGRU and attention.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi-like dataset.
**Results:** F1 up to 99.93% (type 4); multi-class 91.21% accuracy.
