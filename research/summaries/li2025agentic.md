# Agentic Link Construction for Environment and Intent Aware 6G Communication
Zhao-Yang Li, Shang-Zhuo Xie, Qian-Qian Yang · IEEE International Symposium on Personal, Indoor and Mobile Radio Communications 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/pimrc69400.2026.11711765 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The emergence of sixth-generation (6G) networks heralds an intelligent communication ecosystem driven by the rapid proliferation of intelligent services and increasingly complex communication scenarios.
**Approach:** However, current physical-layer designs-typically following modular and isolated optimization paradigms-fail to achieve global end-to-end optimality due to neglected inter-module dependencies. Although large language models (LLMs) have recently been applied to communication tasks such as beam prediction and resource allocation, existing studies remain limited to single-task or single-modality scenarios and lack the ability to jointly reason over communication states and user intents for personalized strategy adaptation.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: dataset.
**Results:** Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed model significantly outperforms conventional planning-based algorithms under challenging channel conditions, achieving robust, efficient, and personalized end-to-end communication strategies.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- To address these limitations, this paper proposes a novel multimodal communication decision-making model for link construction leveraging reinforcement learning on pretrained LLMs.
