# Highway mobility and vehicular ad-hoc networks in ns-3
Hadi Arbabi, Michele C. Weigle · Proceedings of the 2010 Winter Simulation Conference 2010 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wsc.2010.5678993 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The study of vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs) requires efficient and accurate simulation tools.
**Approach:** As the mobility of vehicles and driver behavior can be affected by network messages, these tools must include a vehicle mobility model integrated with a quality network simulator. We present the first implementation of a well-known vehicle mobility model to ns-3, the next generation of the popular ns-2 networking simulator.
