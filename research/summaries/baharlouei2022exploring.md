# Exploring Realistic VANET Simulations for Anomaly Detection of DDoS Attacks
Hamideh Baharlouei, Adetokunbo Makanju, Nur Zincir-Heywood · 2022 IEEE 95th Vehicular Technology Conference: (VTC2022-Spring) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2022-spring54318.2022.9860624 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Simulation realism factors change security results.
**Approach:** Re-run VANET DDoS attack and anomaly detection while varying grid pattern, communication settings and mobility pattern.
**Tools and scale:** VANET simulation (Veins type).
**Results:** Considering these factors leads to different results.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future research directions arise from their observations on simulation realism.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — demonstrates that simulator configuration changes security conclusions, the core motivation for our counterfactual reruns and sensitivity analysis.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first to show simulation settings flip security results; per-unit counterfactual ground truth remains open.
