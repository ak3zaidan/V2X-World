# A Copula-Based Attack Prediction Model for Vehicle-to-Grid Networks
Toffa Zidane Nonvignon, Amar Bensaber Boucif, Mesfioui Mhamed · Applied Sciences 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3390/app12083830 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) networks are a part of the Smart Grid networks. Their primary goal is to recharge electric vehicles.
**Approach:** In this paper, a model based on copulas to detect intrusion cases in V2G networks is proposed.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** To achieve this model, a database is generated first from three scenarios using tools including MiniV2G, Wireshark, and CICflowMeter.

- None stated in the abstract.
