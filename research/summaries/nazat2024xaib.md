# XAI-based Feature Ensemble for Enhanced Anomaly Detection in Autonomous Driving Systems
Sazid Nazat, Mustafa Abdallah · arXiv (Cornell University) 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.15405 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The rapid advancement of autonomous vehicle (AV) technology has introduced significant challenges in ensuring transportation security and reliability.
**Approach:** Traditional AI models for anomaly detection in AVs are often opaque, posing difficulties in understanding and trusting their decision making processes. This paper proposes a novel feature ensemble framework that integrates multiple Explainable AI (XAI) methods: SHAP, LIME, and DALEX with various AI models to enhance both anomaly detection and interpretability.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: VEREMI, VeReMi
**Results:** Our feature ensemble technique demonstrates improved accuracy, robustness, and transparency of AI models, contributing to safer and more trustworthy autonomous driving systems.
