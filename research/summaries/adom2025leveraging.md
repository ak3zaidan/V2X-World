# Leveraging Functional Encryption and Deep Learning for Privacy-Preserving Traffic Forecasting
Isaac Adom, Mohammmad Iqbal Hossain, Hassan Mahmoud, Ahmad Alsharif et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13267 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Over the past few years, traffic congestion has continuously plagued the nation's transportation system creating several negative impacts including longer travel times, increased pollution rates, and higher collision risks.
**Approach:** To address these challenges, we propose in this paper a secure, privacy-preserving location reporting and traffic forecasting system that guarantees privacy protection of driver data while maintaining high traffic forecasting accuracy. With extensive evaluation on real datasets, we demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed scheme with less than 10% mean absolute error for a 60-minute forecasting horizon, all while protecting driver privacy.
