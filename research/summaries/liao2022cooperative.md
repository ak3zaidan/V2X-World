# Cooperative Ramp Merging Design and Field Implementation: A Digital Twin Approach Based on Vehicle-to-Cloud Communication
Xishun Liao, Ziran Wang, Xuanpeng Zhao, Kyungtae Han, Prashant Tiwari, Matthew J. Barth et al. · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2020.3045123 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Ramp merging lacks coordination; most cooperative merging uses DSRC.
**Approach:** Vehicle-to-cloud digital twin: on-board devices upload via 4G/LTE, server builds DTs of vehicles and drivers and returns advisory information.
**Tools and scale:** Real field implementation in Riverside, California, three passenger vehicles.
**Results:** Improves safety and environmental sustainability vs no advisory, with acceptable communication delay (no numbers in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none - cooperative driving application via cloud DT; no security or simulator.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on our claims.
