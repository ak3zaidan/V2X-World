# Blockchain-based reputation model for vehicle platooning with common global goal
Hanaa Nafea, Awais Qasim, Attiya Hussain, Ilyas Fakhir · Multiagent and Grid Systems 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1177/15741702251338060 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle platooning is an active area of research in which vehicles travel closely together to improve fuel efficiency and safety, and relies heavily on the trustworthiness and cooperation of the participating vehicles.
**Approach:** Traditional methods of managing trust in such systems face significant challenges, including vulnerability to malicious behavior and lack of transparency. Any such attack on the platoon from outside or inside will be disastrous as human lives are involved.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: blockchain
**Results:** No quantitative results stated in the abstract.

- Traditional methods of managing trust in such systems face significant challenges, including vulnerability to malicious behavior and lack of transparency.
- In this research we propose a blockchain-based reputation model for vehicle platooning, designed to address these challenges and enhance the overall reliability and efficiency of platooning systems.
