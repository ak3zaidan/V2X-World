# Trajectory tracking and recovery attacks in VANET systems
Kun Wang, Lei Wang, Minhao Cui · International Journal of Communication Systems 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1002/dac.3797 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Attackers could recover vehicle trajectories.
**Approach:** Matrix-completion trajectory tracking and recovery attack (TTRA) and minimal RSU placement via set cover heuristic.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Recovers locations with tolerable deviation (no numbers in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.

**Novelty implications:** Trajectory recovery from sparse samples exists; linkability using PKI and radio together open.
