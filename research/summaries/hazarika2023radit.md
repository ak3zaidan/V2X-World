# RADiT: Resource Allocation in Digital Twin-Driven UAV-Aided Internet of Vehicle Networks
Bishmita Hazarika, Keshav Singh, Chih-Peng Li, Anke Schmeink, Kim Fung Tsang · IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jsac.2023.3310048 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Digital twin (DT) has emerged as a promising technology for improving resource allocation decisions in Internet of Vehicles (IoV) networks.
**Approach:** In this paper, we consider an IoV network where mobile edge computing (MEC) servers are deployed at the roadside units (RSUs). The IoV network provides ubiquitous connections even in areas uncovered by RSUs with the assistance of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) which can act as a relay between RSUs and task vehicles.
