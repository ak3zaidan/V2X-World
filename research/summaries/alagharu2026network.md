# Network World Models as Environments for Algorithm Design on Complex Systems
Rishab Alagharu, Hongji Pu, Zeeshan Memon, Xinyuan Song, Yuntong Hu, Liang Zhao · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01048 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Scoring candidate algorithms on complex networks by simulation is costly.
**Approach:** Action-conditioned Network World Model of diffusion dynamics as fast evaluator in an LLM coding-agent design loop with counterfactual probes.
**Tools and scale:** Eight tasks, five diffusion models (IC, LT, CLT, SIR, SIS), networks 10^3-10^5 nodes.
**Results:** Designed algorithms match or exceed best baseline in 138 of 141 settings; up to 14.5x faster rollouts than Monte Carlo.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in extracted sections.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - world model with counterfactual probes as evaluator (direction 3), epidemic/influence networks not V2X.
**Novelty implications:** World-model-as-evaluator with counterfactual probes exists in network science; V2X version open.
