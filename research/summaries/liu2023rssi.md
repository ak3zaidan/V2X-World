# RSSI-Based Sybil Attack Detection Under Fading Channel in VANET
Beiyuan Liu, Jiaxiu Cai, Jiajia Liu · ICC 2023 - IEEE International Conference on Communications 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc45041.2023.10279536 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** RSSI-based Sybil detection fails under fading and high mobility.
**Approach:** ML distance estimation plus mean-shift clustering for Sybil detection.
**Tools and scale:** Numerical simulation.
**Results:** High accuracy with proper detection time and search radius.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — PHY-based Sybil detection.
**Novelty implications:** None beyond existing RSSI approaches.
