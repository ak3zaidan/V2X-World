# Secure platooning control of automated vehicles under cyber attacks
Eman Mousavinejad, Ljubo Vlacic · ISA Transactions 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isatra.2022.02.046 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Secure platooning under cyber attacks with uncertain dynamics.
**Approach:** Ellipsoidal set-membership local estimators via convex optimisation and scalable control protocol.
**Tools and scale:** Comparative simulation studies.
**Results:** Tracking errors remain near zero under different attack strategies.
