Johan Janson Olstam · Transport Simulation 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1201/9780429093258-3 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In recent years, the transport simulation of large road networks has become far more rapid and detailed, and many exciting developments in this field have emerged.
**Approach:** Within this volume, the authors describe the simulation of automobile, pedestrian, and rail traffic coupled to new applications, such as the embedding of traffic simulation into driving simulators, to give a more realistic environment of driver behavior surrounding the subject vehicle.
