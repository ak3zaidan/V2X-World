# FS-Net: A Flow Sequence Network For Encrypted Traffic Classification
Chang Liu, Long-Tao He, G. Xiong, Zigang Cao, Zhen Li · IEEE Conference on Computer Communications 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/infocom.2019.8737507 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With more attention paid to user privacy and communication security, the volume of encrypted traffic rises sharply, which brings a huge challenge to traditional rule-based traffic classification methods.
**Approach:** Combining machine learning algorithms and manual-design features has become the mainstream methods to solve this problem. However, these features depend on professional experience heavily, which needs lots of human effort.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: dataset, real-world; scale not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Our comprehensive experiments on the real-world dataset covering 18 applications indicate that FS-Net achieves an excellent performance (99.14% TPR, 0.05% FPR and 0.9906 FTF) and outperforms the state-of-the-art methods.
