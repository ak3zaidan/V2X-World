# Geometry Based Channel Models with Cross- and Autocorrelation for Vehicular Network Simulations
Christian Nelson, Nikita Lyamin, Alexey Vinel, Carl Gustafson, Fredrik Tufvesson · 2018 IEEE 87th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC Spring) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtcspring.2018.8417740 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** V2V simulations lack measured auto- and cross-correlation of fading.
**Approach:** Geometry-based measured highway multilink channel model (LOS/OLOS) in OMNeT++/Plexe.
**Tools and scale:** OMNeT++, Plexe.
**Results:** Autocorrelation matches measurements; 10th percentile data age rises from 0.1 s to 1.5 s when autocorrelation is considered.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — correlated fading affecting application-level information age.
**Novelty implications:** Our PHY should consider correlated shadowing for safety metrics.
