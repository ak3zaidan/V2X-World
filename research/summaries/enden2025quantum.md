K. L. van der Enden, R. Kirschner, M. Krumtunger, A. Wilms · venue unknown 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.19070 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A future quantum internet brings promising applications related to security, privacy and enabling distributed quantum computing. Integration of these concepts into the future trends of the automotive sector is of considerable interest, as it enables both the development of practical quantum internet use cases and the adoption of innovative technologies in the automotive sector.
**Approach:** In this work we analyze cross-platform megatrends in both the quantum internet and the automotive industry, identifying mutually beneficial regions of interest. For the long-term ($\geq10$ years) we develop a comprehensive list of use cases for the quantum internet within the automotive sector.
