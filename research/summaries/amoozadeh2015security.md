# Security vulnerabilities of connected vehicle streams and their impact on cooperative driving
Mani Amoozadeh, Arun Raghuramu, Chen-nee Chuah, Dipak Ghosal et al. · IEEE Communications Magazine 2015 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mcom.2015.7120028 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Autonomous vehicles capable of navigating unpredictable real-world environments with little human feedback are a reality today.
**Approach:** Such systems rely heavily on onboard sensors such as cameras, radar/LIDAR, and GPS as well as capabilities such as 3G/4G connectivity and V2V/V2I communication to make real-time maneuvering decisions. Autonomous vehicle control imposes very strict requirements on the security of the communication channels used by the vehicle to exchange information as well as the control logic that performs complex driving tasks such as adapting vehicle velocity or changing lanes.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: adjacent V2X security).
**Results:** Our simulation results show that an insider attack can cause significant instability in the CACC vehicle stream. We also illustrate how different countermeasures, such as downgrading to ACC mode, could potentially be used to improve the security and safety of the connected vehicle streams.
