# EVCCS: Realistic Simulation Framework for Electric Vehicle Commute and Charge
Sushil Poudel, Mahmoud Abouyoussef, Muhammad Ismail · 2022 IEEE Vehicle Power and Propulsion Conference (VPPC) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vppc55846.2022.10003312 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the growing number of electric vehicles (EVs) on the roads, it is necessary to understand EV charging patterns within a network of public charging stations.
**Approach:** This is needed for optimal allocation of chargers and coordination of charging requests. However, existing datasets do not reflect the charging patterns of EVs in a network of charging stations.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting. The available text does not describe a V2X simulator, radio stack, traffic model or SCMS-style PKI in enough detail to report; scale not stated.
**Results:** As a case study, the EVCCS is used to simulate the commute and charging of 54 EVs in the city of Cookeville, TN, USA, for one year.
