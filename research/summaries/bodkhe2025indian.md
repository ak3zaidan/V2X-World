# Indian SUMO traffic scenario-based misbehaviour detection dataset for connected vehicles
Umesh Bodkhe, Sudeep Tanwar · Multimodal Transportation 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.multra.2024.100148 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** No Indian MBD datasets.
**Approach:** AhmST SUMO scenario (Ahmedabad) and dataset with position, heading and speed injections; ML evaluation.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO-based generation.
**Results:** Optimised model with improved accuracy.
