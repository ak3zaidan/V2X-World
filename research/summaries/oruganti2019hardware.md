# Hardware-in-loop based Automotive Embedded Systems Cybersecurity Evaluation Testbed
Pradeep Sharma Oruganti, Matt Appel, Qadeer Ahmed · Proceedings of the ACM Workshop on Automotive Cybersecurity 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3309171.3309173 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper explains the work-in-progress on a vehicle safety and security evaluation platform.
**Approach:** Since the testing of cyber-attacks on an actual may be costly or dangerous, the platform enables us to evaluate the threat and the risk associated with cyber-attacks in a safe virtual environment. The goal is to integrate vehicle and powertrain models, mobility and network simulators to actual hardware running the control algorithms using CAN communication.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: adjacent V2X security).
**Results:** In the current paper, the framework and ideology behind is testbed is described and current progress is shown. A simple GPS spoofing attack on a virtual test vehicle is done and some initial results are discussed.
