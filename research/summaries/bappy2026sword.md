# SWORD: A Secure LoW-Latency Offline-First Authentication and Data Sharing Scheme for Resource Constrained Distributed Networks
Faisal Haque Bappy, Tahrim Hossain, Raiful Hasan, Kamrul Hasan et al. · ICC 2026 - IEEE International Conference on Communications 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc59461.2026.11588138 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** While many resource-constrained networks, such as Internet of Things (IoT) and Internet of Vehicles (IoV), are inherently distributed, the majority still rely on central servers for fast authentication and data sharing. Blockchain-based solutions offer decentralized alternatives but often struggle to meet the stringent latency requirements of real-time applications.
**Approach:** To address this, we introduce SWORD, a novel offline-first authentication and data-sharing scheme designed specifically for resource-constrained networks. Additionally, we provide a comprehensive security analysis, demonstrating that SWORD is resilient against spoofing, impersonation, replay, and man-in-the-middle attacks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** Our experimental results show that SWORD outperforms traditional blockchain-based solutions while offering similar resource efficiency and authentication latency to central-server-based solutions.
