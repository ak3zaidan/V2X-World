# Blockchain-Assisted Trust Management Scheme for Securing VANETs
Waheeb Ahmed, Di Wu, Daniel Mukathie · KSII Transactions on Internet and Information Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.3837/tiis.2022.02.013 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The main goal of VANETs is to improve the safety of all road users.
**Approach:** Therefore, the accuracy and trustworthiness of messages transmitted in VANETs are essential, given that life may rely on them. VANETs are provided with basic security services through the use of public key infrastructure-based authentication.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: blockchain
**Results:** The results of the simulation indicate that the proposed scheme outperforms the compared schemes and is highly resistant to bogus message attacks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, the trust of users is still an open issue in VANETs.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
