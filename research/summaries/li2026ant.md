# ANT: A Multi-Granularity Network Traffic Dataset and Benchmark for Agents Behavior Auditing
Fan Li, Xiangyu Gao, Zixuan Liu, Tong Li et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06514 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Auditing LLM agent behaviour from network traffic.
**Approach:** ANT dataset: 3,114 episodes, 276,417 flows, 40,049 primitive segments.
**Tools and scale:** 13 baselines.
**Results:** Existing methods recover uneven signals; struggle with risk identification.
