# An Intelligent CNN-LSTM Framework for Real-Time Attack Detection in Smart City Vehicular Networks
Sonika Bhardwaj, Ramesh Saha · 2025 7th International Conference on Information Systems and Computer Networks (ISCON) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iscon65210.2025.11341600 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The increasing connectivity of Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) in smart city infrastructures are exposes by the growing number of security threats.
**Approach:** The paper proposes the lightweight-hybrid deep learning (DL) based IDS that combines Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) with the Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) in order to detect the network anomalies and cyber-attacks in real time. The propose model influences the spatial feature extraction ability of the CNN and temporal sequence modeling that enhances the detection accuracy while maintaining low computational footprint, make it suitable for the deployment on edge-enabled vehicular nodes.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The results are demonstrate that the model achieves high accuracy of 96.8% and low latency of 18 ms that outperform the conventional models, like, SVM, Random Forest, and standalone CNN or LSTM in terms of precision, recall, and resource efficiency.
