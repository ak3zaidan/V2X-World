# Hybrid pseudonym change strategy for location privacy in VANET: protecting location privacy in VANET
Leila Benarous, Benamar Kadri · Int. J. Inf. Priv. Secur. Integr. 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1504/ijipsi.2020.111455 · relevance: high · basis: abstract
