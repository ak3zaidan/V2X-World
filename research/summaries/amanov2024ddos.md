# DDoS Attack Detection in SDN-Based Instrumented Ad Hoc Networks
Anuarbek Amanov, D.S. Isakov · Q A Iasaýı atyndaǵy Halyqaralyq qazaq-túrіk ýnıversıtetіnіń habarlary (fızıka matematıka ınformatıka serııasy) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.47526/2024-1/2524-0080.07 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The physical characteristics of today's urban intersection structures and the traffic flows caused by unplanned road intersections lead to many negative effects such as time/cash loss, stress, increased fuel consumption, and more.
**Approach:** When Software Defined Networking emerged as a new technology, it brought many advantages such as high availability, scalability, and performance, but also introduced new security vulnerabilities targeted by attackers. In the simulation study conducted within the scope of this work, an SDN (Software Defined Networking)-based DDoS (Distributed Denial of Service) attack was carried out, and changes in data before and after the attack were examined.
