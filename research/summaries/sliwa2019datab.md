# Towards Data-Driven Simulation of End-to-End Network Performance Indicators
Benjamin Sliwa, Christian Wietfeld · 2019 IEEE 90th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2019-Fall) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtcfall.2019.8891513 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** System-level simulations differ from real end-to-end indicators and real tests cannot be repeated under identical conditions.
**Approach:** Data-driven simulation combining ML prediction models and GPR error models of end-to-end indicators.
**Tools and scale:** Real measurements; opportunistic vehicular data transfer case study.
**Results:** Fraction of system-level simulation computation time with significantly better match to real world.
