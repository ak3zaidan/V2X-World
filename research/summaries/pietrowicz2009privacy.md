Stanley Pietrowicz, Richard Ferrer, Robert G. White, Eric van den Berg et al. · CCNC 2009 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ccnc.2009.4784924 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Privacy and scalability of combinatorial certificate scheme from VII PoC for 200M vehicles.
**Approach:** Analysis of trade-offs between privacy, bad-actor removal, and innocent-vehicle impact from shared certificate revocation.
**Tools and scale:** US VII PoC; nationwide analysis.
