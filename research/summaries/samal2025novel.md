# A Novel Lightweight Privacy-Preservation Authentication Protocol for VANET Environment
K. Samal, Shanta Kumari Sunanda, Debasish Jena · 2025 International Conference on Advancements in Power, Communication and Intelligent Systems (APCI) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/apci65531.2025.11136928 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The rapid advancement of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) has significantly contributed to the emergence of smart cities, which aim to enhance the quality of urban life. Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs) play a crucial role in this transformation by enabling improved road safety, traffic management, and passenger comfort.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a lightweight and privacy-preserving authentication protocol for secure communication between vehicles and Roadside Units (RSUs). To protect user privacy, we incorporate a blind signature-based strategy that ensures anonymity and unlinkability during interactions with TA and RSU.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting (VANET/IoV/V2X). Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** Performance evaluation demonstrates that our approach offers lower computational and communication overhead than existing solutions.
