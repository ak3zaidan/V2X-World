# Position-Based Machine Learning Propagation Loss Model Enabling Fast Digital Twins of Wireless Networks in ns-3
Eduardo Nuno Almeida, Helder Fontes, Rui Campos, Manuel Ricardo · Proceedings of the 2023 Workshop on ns-3 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3592149.3592150 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Fast, precise wireless DTs in ns-3.
**Approach:** P-MLPL: position- and direction-aware ML path loss (XGBoost/SVR) plus stochastic fast fading from testbed traces.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3; testbed traces.
**Results:** Median propagation-loss error 2.5 dB (0.5x Friis/log-distance error); throughput error up to 2.5 Mbit/s.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Validated for the single considered scenario; future work in conclusion.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - data-driven channel models.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
