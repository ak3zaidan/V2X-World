# Large Foundation Model Empowered Radio Frequency Fingerprinting for Wireless Networks
Kaiqiang Li, Jingping Qiao, Xiwei Li et al. · International Conference on Speech Technology and Human-Computer Dialogue 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icct67417.2025.11374241 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Radio frequency fingerprinting (RFF) is a crucial technology for identifying wireless devices, playing an essential role in network security.
**Approach:** In this paper, we introduce large foundation models (LFM), the most advanced deep learning model, into RFF and propose a hybrid CNN and Transformer model termed RFF-CAT to solve the performance challenge. To solve the data noise challenge, we design a Multi-Packet Fusion (MPF) strategy to enhance RFF accuracy.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: deep learning, CNN.
**Results:** Recently, deep learning models have shown promising results in RFF but still face substantial challenges, including data noise and performance limitations. This approach dynamically adjusts the weights assigned to each data packet, reducing the influence of packets with high interference and enhancing inference accuracy.
