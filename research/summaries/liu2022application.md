# Application of Lightweight Digital Twin System in Intelligent Transportation
Qian Liu, Xiaoqing Qi, Shanshan Liu, Xiuju Cheng, Xin Ke, Feng Wang · IEEE Journal of Radio Frequency Identification 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jrfid.2022.3212169 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper discusses the latest trends in the development of intelligent transportation systems, and the importance of digital twin technology in solving transportation problems.
**Approach:** A lightweight digital twin system based on roadside sensing device, C-V2X network, multi-sensor fusion and global cooperative algorithm is proposed. Its software cost is low, but it can accurately reproduce, predict and control the microscopic operation process of traffic participants.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Finally, the typical applications of the system are given for reference, hope to provide a path to improve the popularity of digital twin technology, and effectively improve the traffic environment.
