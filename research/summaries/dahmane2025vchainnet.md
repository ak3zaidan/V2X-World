# vChainNet: Accurate and Scalable End-to-End Slice Modeling for 5G and Beyond Networks
Hadj Ahmed Chikh Dahmane, Sherif Mostafa, Moustafa Youssef, Muhammad Sulaiman, Raouf Boutaba · 2025 12th International Conference on Wireless Networks and Mobile Communications (WINCOM) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wincom65874.2025.11313372 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Traditional models miss 5G VNF/slice behaviour.
**Approach:** vChainNet: per-VNF seq2seq models composed for slice delay; distribution-based loss.
**Tools and scale:** 5G slice data.
**Results:** Up to 10.86% accuracy gain and up to 53.33x speedup; 95% fewer parameters.

- None stated.
