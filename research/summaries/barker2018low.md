# A Low-Cost Hardware-in-the-Loop Agent-Based Simulation Testbed for Autonomous Vehicles
Ray Barker, Aaron Hurst, Ridge Shrubsall, Ghulam Mubashar Hassan, Tim French · 2018 IEEE/ASME International Conference on Advanced Intelligent Mechatronics (AIM) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/aim.2018.8452376 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** We have developed a flexible and low-cost hardware testbed for autonomous vehicle research and education.
**Approach:** The testbed provides the ability to autonomously control multiple small vehicles within a 1200x900 mm environment and is entirely open source with regard to both hardware and software, making it easily reproducible. An agent-based approach is used for vehicle control, meaning that different agent models can be applied to each vehicle as desired for simulation.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting. The available text does not describe a V2X simulator, radio stack, traffic model or SCMS-style PKI in enough detail to report; scale not stated.
**Results:** The testbed provides the ability to autonomously control multiple small vehicles within a 1200x900 mm environment and is entirely open source with regard to both hardware and software, making it easily reproducible.
