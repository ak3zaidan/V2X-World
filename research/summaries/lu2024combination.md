# The Combination and Application of Vehicle-Connected Vehicle Intelligence and Digital Twin Technology
Zhongxu Lu · Highlights in Science, Engineering and Technology 2024 · https://doi.org/10.54097/6h176694 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** LTE-V2X car networking technology has garnered significant attention from research institutions worldwide.
**Approach:** This technology has undergone two active phases of technological evolution, with onboard operating systems gradually enhancing functionality to support intelligent control service platforms and integrated businesses, while automotive electronic systems facilitate advancements in autonomous driving, and 5G technology sees increasing application. Key technologies revolve around the integration of intelligence and networking in smart cities.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: real world; scale not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** As information related to people, vehicles, and roads grows increasingly vast, and the surrounding environment becomes more complex, timely, effective, and accurate processing of this data is essential to ensure traffic safety, enhance traffic flow, and improve the driving experience and comfort for both drivers and passengers.
