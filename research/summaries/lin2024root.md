# Root Cause Analysis In Microservice Using Neural Granger Causal Discovery
Cheng-Ming Lin, Ching Chang, Wei-Yao Wang et al. · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01140 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In recent years, microservices have gained widespread adoption in IT operations due to their scalability, maintenance, and flexibility.
**Approach:** To address these challenges, we propose RUN, a novel approach for root cause analysis using neural Granger causal discovery with contrastive learning. RUN enhances the backbone encoder by integrating contextual information from time series, and leverages a time series forecasting model to conduct neural Granger causal discovery.
