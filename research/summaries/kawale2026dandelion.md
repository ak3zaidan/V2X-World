# Dandelion-Optimized Residual Attention CNN for Identification of Attacks and Security Issues in VANETs
Raghunath M. Kawale, Ritesh V. Patil, Lalit Vasantrao Patil et al. · 2026 International Conference on ICT and Photonics (ICTP) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ictp67998.2026.11485200 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As there is a surge in connected cars, protecting Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) from cyber-attacks is crucial to make the communication safe and dependable.
**Approach:** To increase the models’ rate of convergence and also improve detection we see in the Dandelion Optimizer (DO) which we use to adjust the model’s weights. Also, we present that the DSDPRACNN-DO model is very much a promising solution to the issue of VANET attacks.
**Tools and scale:** To this end, the Dual Sampling Dilated Pre-activation Residual Attention Convolution Neural Network with Dandelion Optimizer (DSDPRACNN-DO) starts by gathering attack data from the VeReMi extension dataset.
**Results:** Further, refining deep complex learning model parameters to enhance accuracy and convergence is still challenging. The Fuzzy Min-Max Neural Network (FMMNN) pre-processes data for handling uncertainties then feature selection with the Walk-Spread Algorithm (WSA) to achieve exploration-exploitation balance.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "Further, refining deep complex learning model parameters to enhance accuracy and convergence is still challenging."
- "The challenges call for an efficient, privacy-preserving detection system suitable for VANET environments."
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — addresses misbehaviour/attack detection (direction 1 area); datasets/twins/surrogates (direction 3 area); pseudonym privacy/linkability (runner-up area), but per the abstract without an integrated SCMS backend, closed-loop revocation, per-message joint ground truth or counterfactual reruns.
**Novelty implications:** Does not rule out any candidate direction on the evidence of the abstract. Leaves open quantifying this in a closed loop with real SCMS revocation and traffic safety outcomes.
