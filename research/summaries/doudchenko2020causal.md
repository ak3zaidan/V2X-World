# Causal inference with bipartite designs: A generalized propensity score approach
Nick Doudchenko, Minzhengxiong Zhang, Yao Zhao et al. · arXiv 2020 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.02108 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Bipartite experiments, in which one set of units receives a treatment while outcomes are measured on another set, have recently garnered attention for their ability to capture interference across two distinct populations, such as buyers and sellers in online marketplaces.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a unified framework for causal inference in bipartite designs that leverages generalized propensity scores (GPS) to estimate exposure-response functions. We further introduce practical modeling and weighting strategies, along with double deconfounding methods, that integrate the GPS into both the outcome model and the assignment mechanism.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: GPS.
**Results:** Under standard unconfoundedness assumptions, we show that our GPS-based estimators are unbiased and derive theoretical bounds on their variance. Through extensive simulations, we demonstrate that these approaches achieve substantial bias reduction compared to naive methods.
