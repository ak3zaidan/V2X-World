# Enhancing Security and Anonymity in the Internet of Vehicles Using Group Signatures
Yungui Chen, Dingyun Sui, Liwei Tian, Zixin Bei · Proceedings of the 2024 International Conference on Intelligent Driving and Smart Transportation 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3704657.3704695 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the rapid development of Internet of Vehicles technology, ensuring the privacy and security of vehicle communications becomes a critical challenge.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a novel anonymous communication scheme based on group signatures to address privacy protection and secure authentication issues in vehicular ad-hoc networks. Through theoretical analysis and experimental simulations, we demonstrate the superiority of our scheme in terms of computational efficiency, communication overhead, and storage requirements, all while maintaining strong resistance against various potential attacks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** Our approach ensures the privacy of vehicle identities while achieving efficient message authentication and traceability.
