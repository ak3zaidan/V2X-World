# Ford Highway Driving RTK Dataset: 30,000 km of North American Highways
Sarah E. Houts, Nahid Pervez, Umair Ibrahim, Gaurav Pandey, Tyler G. R. Reid · Proceedings of the 33rd International Technical Meeting of the Satellite Division of The Institute of Navigation (ION GNSS+ 2020) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.33012/2020.17686 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** There is a growing need for vehicle positioning information to support Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS), Connectivity (V2X), and Autonomous Driving (AD) features.
**Approach:** These range from a need for road determination ($<$5 meters), lane determination ($<$1.5 meters), and determining where the vehicle is within the lane ($<$0.3 meters). This paper presents the Ford Highway Driving RTK (Ford-HDR) dataset.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: dataset; scale not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** No quantitative results stated in the abstract.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- This dataset is being released into the public domain to spark further research in the community.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — addresses V2X simulation/datasets (direction 3 area), but per the abstract without an integrated SCMS backend, closed-loop revocation, per-message joint ground truth or counterfactual reruns.
**Novelty implications:** Does not rule out any candidate direction on the evidence of the abstract. Leaves open quantifying this in a closed loop with real SCMS revocation and traffic safety outcomes.
