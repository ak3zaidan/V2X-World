# A Low-Latency Post-Quantum Group Authentication Framework for VANETs
Ashutosh Kumar, M. Malhotra, A. Anjali, A. Yadav · IEEE Networking Letters 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/lnet.2026.3666683 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Low-latency PQ group authentication for VANETs.
**Approach:** Kyber group key distribution, Dilithium message authentication, ZKPs, Hyperledger Besu RSU reputation.
**Tools and scale:** OMNeT++ and SUMO.
**Results:** Up to 52.7% lower communication overhead; group key update latency 1.2 ms in dense traffic.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — PQ authentication evaluated in OMNeT++/SUMO.
**Novelty implications:** None.
