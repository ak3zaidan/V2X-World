# Simulation-Driven Vehicular Traffic Data Augmentation: Extending Sensor Coverage Through Virtual Sensing
Davide Andrea Guastella, Eladio Montero Porras, Evangelos Pournaras, Gianluca Bontempi · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13993 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Urban traffic management relies on sensor networks whose spatial coverage is limited by deployment costs and privacy regulations.
**Approach:** Machine learning models trained on such sparse data cannot generalize to unmonitored locations and must be retrained whenever the sensor infrastructure changes. We propose a simulation-based methodology that addresses this problem by generating augmented traffic count datasets in which each physical sensor is replaced by a virtual sensor placed at a surrogate location in the road network.
