# On the impact of sybil attacks in cooperative driving scenarios
Felipe Boeira, Marinho P. Barcellos, Edison P. de Freitas, Alexey Vinel et al. · 2017 IFIP Networking Conference (IFIP Networking) and Workshops 2017 · https://doi.org/10.23919/ifipnetworking.2017.8264890 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Platooning employs a set of technologies to manage how a group of vehicles operates, including radar, GPS and Inter-Vehicular Communication (IVC).
**Approach:** It uses broadcasted information such as acceleration, position and velocity to operate vehicle members of the platoon. Cooperation among vehicles allows platoons to reduce fuel consumption and risks associated with driver mistakes.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: attacks on CAV control).
**Results:** Cooperation among vehicles allows platoons to reduce fuel consumption and risks associated with driver mistakes. Simulation results show that this attack may impact the longitudinal control and compromise the entire platoon control.
