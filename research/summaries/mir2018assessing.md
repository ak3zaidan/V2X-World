# Assessing the Impact of Realistic Simulation Environment on Vehicular Communications
Zeeshan Hameed Mir · 2018 Fifth HCT Information Technology Trends (ITT) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ctit.2018.8649487 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Over the last few decades, simulations have emerged as the primary tool in vehicular ad hoc network (VANET) research.
**Approach:** The unique characteristics of Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) communications require more detailed and flexible simulation approaches. For example, incorporating V2V channel models which take into account site-specific and different types of obstructions, vehicular movement patterns and their impact on the system-level performance metrics.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** However, most of the simulation tools use simplified techniques at the physical (PHY) layer to achieve an optimum mix of accuracy and computational complexity. Secondly, to augment the network simulator with the results collected from representative V2V channel model to further improve on the accuracy of simulation-based studies.
