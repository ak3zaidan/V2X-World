# Context-Aware Intelligent Traffic Light Control through Secure Messaging
Mükremin Özkul, Ilir Capuni, Elton Domnori · Journal of Advanced Transportation 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1155/2018/4251701 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we propose STCM, a context-aware secure traffic control model to manage competing traffic flows at a given intersection by using secure messages with real-time traffic information.
**Approach:** The vehicle is modeled as a virtual sensor which reports the traffic state, such as its speed and location, to a traffic light controller through a secure and computationally lightweight protocol. During the reporting process, a vehicle’s identity and location are kept anonymous to any other vehicle in the system.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: traffic signal attacks).
**Results:** The simulation results demonstrate that the system significantly reduces the waiting time of the vehicles in both light and heavy traffic flows compared to the pretimed signal control and the adaptive Webster’s method. Simulation results also yield effective robustness against impersonating attacks from malicious vehicles.
