# Two Phase Methodology for the Detection of Malicious Vehicles from Vehicular Ad hoc Network
Shubham Soni, Nitika Kapoor · 2022 4th International Conference on Advances in Computing, Communication Control and Networking (ICAC3N) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icac3n56670.2022.10074385 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The vehicular ad hoc network is the decentralized form of network in which malevolent user can join the network which can trigger a variety of active and passive attacks.
**Approach:** The denial of service is an active type of intrusion that is triggered by the malicious nodes present in the network. This research work introduces a new two-phase methodology for detecting and isolating the attack.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** NS2 is applied to stimulate proposed technique and certain metrics are considered to compute the results.
