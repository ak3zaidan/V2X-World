# Computation Offloading and Service Caching for Intelligent Transportation Systems With Digital Twin
Xiaolong Xu, Zhongjian Liu, Muhammad Bilal, S. Vimal, Houbing Song · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2022.3190669 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Mobile edge computing (MEC) provides a novel computing paradigm to satisfy the increasing computation requirements of mobile applications.
**Approach:** In MEC-enabled intelligent transportation systems (ITS), the latency-sensitive computing tasks are offloaded to RSUs for execution, reducing the transmission latency compared with the cloud solutions. However, the repetitive executions of the same tasks whose outputs are dependent on the inputs lead to the extra system latency, an alternative is to cache the required services on RSUs in advance.
