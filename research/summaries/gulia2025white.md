# White-Box Modeling of V2X Link Performance Using Stabilized Symbolic Regression
Rahul Gulia, Feyisayo Favour Popoola, Ashish Sheikh · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.19809 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** DNN BLER models lack interpretability and are costly.
**Approach:** Stabilised symbolic regression deriving compact BLER expressions from V2X simulation data (SNR, velocity, MCS, DMRS, LOS/NLOS).
**Tools and scale:** Realistic V2X link-level simulation data; GP with population 20,000, 300 generations.
**Results:** 158-node expression; R^2 0.8684, MSE 2.08e-2.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in extracted sections.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - PHY abstraction for V2X simulators.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
