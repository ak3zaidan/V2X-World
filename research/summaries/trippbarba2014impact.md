# Impact of packet error modeling in VANET simulations
Carolina Tripp-Barba, Luis Urquiza-Aguiar, José Estrada, José Alfonso Aguilar-Calderón, Aníbal Zaldívar-Colado, Mónica Aguilar Igartua · 2014 IEEE 6th International Conference on Adaptive Science &amp; Technology (ICAST) 2014 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icastech.2014.7068133 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Impact of packet error modelling in VANET simulation.
**Approach:** Compares basic and realistic packet error models over three densities and three channel capacities in multi-hop scenarios.
**Tools and scale:** Network simulator; urban multi-hop.
**Results:** Basic model matches realistic one at low/medium capacity when configured properly; significant differences at 27 Mbps even with low load.
