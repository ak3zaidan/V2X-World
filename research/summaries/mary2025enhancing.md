# Enhancing Adversary Identification in UAV Networking using Piggybacking AODV Protocol
P. Mary, K. Manivannan, L. Karuppasamy, S. Nelson et al. · 2025 5th International Conference on Intelligent Technologies (CONIT) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/conit65521.2025.11166878 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In today’s world, Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) are becoming increasingly common in wireless networks. With this rise comes the urgent need for strong security measures to protect against potential threats.
**Approach:** Through rigorous analysis and extensive simulation experiments, we show that Piggybacking AODV is effective and feasible.
**Tools and scale:** Through rigorous analysis and extensive simulation experiments, we show that Piggybacking AODV is effective and feasible.
**Results:** Our approach cleverly adds detection mechanisms to AODV routing messages, reducing overhead and seamlessly integrating with existing network protocols. With the least amount of communication overhead and detection times, improve both the detection and message delivery rates.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- While there are existing studies on detecting and dealing with attacks, one challenge remains: how to proactively identify compromised UAV nodes.
