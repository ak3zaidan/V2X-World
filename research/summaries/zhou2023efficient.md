# An Efficient Identity Authentication Scheme With Dynamic Anonymity for VANETs
Yanwei Zhou, Lei Cao, Zirui Qiao, Z. Xia et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2023.3236699 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Nowadays, as an essential technique for intelligent transportation, vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) has significantly improved people’s travel experience, providing richer, and smarter services for vehicles while ensuring driver safety. **Approach:** In order to further solve the above problems, we propose a novel privacy-preserving identity authentication protocol based on the certificateless aggregate signature scheme, allowing the user to generate a fuzzy identity to hide her real identity, and the private key can be kept unchanged even if the corresponding pseudonym is updated.
**Tools and scale:** We prove that our protocol satisfies unforgeability in the random oracle based on a classic complexity assumption.
**Results:** Furthermore, to achieve efficiency in computation, bilinear mapping is avoided in our proposed scheme. Finally, the security and efficiency analyses demonstrate that our construction enjoys more security features and better performance compared with the existing schemes.
