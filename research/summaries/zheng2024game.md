# Game-Theoretic Adversarial Interaction-Based Critical Scenario Generation for Autonomous Vehicles
Xiaokun Zheng, Hua-Wei Liang, Jian Wang, Han-Qi Wang · Machines 2024 · https://doi.org/10.3390/machines12080538 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Ensuring safety and efficiency in the rapidly advancing autonomous vehicle (AV) industry presents a significant engineering challenge.
**Approach:** Comprehensive performance evaluations and critical scenario testing are essential for identifying situations that AVs cannot handle. Thus, generating critical scenarios is a key problem in AV testing system design.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Simulation results show that the adversarial scenarios generated by this method significantly increase incident rates by 158% to 1313% compared to natural driving scenarios, while ride comfort and driving efficiency decrease, and risk significantly increases. These findings provide critical insights for model improvement and demonstrate the proposed method’s suitability for assessing AV performance in dynamic traffic environments.
