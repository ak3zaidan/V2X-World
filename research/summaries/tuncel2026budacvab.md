# BUDACVAB: Blockchain utilized driver authentication considering various brands for VANETS
Huseyin Tuncel, Kubra Kalkan · Peer-to-Peer Networking and Applications 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s12083-026-02254-2 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** To address storage and performance challenges, Correlation-based Feature Selection (CFS) is applied to reduce the size of raw driving data before storing it on the blockchain.
**Approach:** This study proposes a blockchain-based authentication framework that enables secure and interoperable driver authentication across heterogeneous vehicle manufacturers. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed framework improves authentication performance, achieving up to 99% accuracy using the KNN algorithm, while also increasing blockchain throughput in terms of Transactions Per Second (TPS).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** This approach significantly decreases storage requirements while improving system efficiency. The proposed framework provides a scalable, secure, and efficient solution for cross-manufacturer authentication in VANET environments.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "However, these approaches suffer from several limitations, including high computational cost, storage constraints, and reduced performance when processing raw driving data."
- "This limitation remains a critical challenge that needs to be addressed to ensure real-world applicability."
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
