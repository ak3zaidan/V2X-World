# Against the Monolithic Wireless World Model: Why NextG Needs Composable and Agentic Intelligence
Aladin Djuhera, Farhan Ahmed, Vlad C. Andrei, Swanand Ravindra Kadhe, Alecio Binotto, Haris Gacanin, Holger Boche · ICML 2026 Workshop on AI and ML for NextG 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16689 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** AI-native 6G visions increasingly invoke wireless foundation models, large multimodal models, and wireless world models as the natural endpoint of AI-native networking, drawing an analogy to recent developments in large language models (LLMs).
**Approach:** We argue that this analogy is structurally incomplete. The success of LLMs is based on a broad, reusable, and largely self-contained tokenized data substrate, whereas the wireless domain lacks an equivalent data foundation.
