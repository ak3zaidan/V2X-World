# A Life-long Learning Intrusion Detection System for 6G-Enabled IoV
Abdelaziz Amara korba, Souad Sebaa, Malik Mabrouki, Yacine Ghamri-Doudane et al. · International Wireless Communications and Mobile Computing Conference (IWCMC) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iwcmc61514.2024.10592500 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The introduction of 6G technology into the Internet of Vehicles (IoV) promises to revolutionize connectivity with ultra-high data rates and seamless network coverage. However, this technological leap also brings significant challenges, particularly for the dynamic and diverse IoV landscape, which must meet the rigorous reliability and security requirements of 6G networks.
**Approach:** This paper presents a novel intrusion detection system leveraging the paradigm of life-long (or continual) learning. To the best of our knowledge, this study is the first to synergize class-incremental learning with federated learning specifically for cyber attack detection.
**Tools and scale:** C-V2X/LTE-V2X/5G, federated learning (mentioned in abstract)
