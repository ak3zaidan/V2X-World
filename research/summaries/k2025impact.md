# The impact of realistic radio propagation modeling on the system level performance of Vehicular Adhoc Network simulation
Sundari K, A Senthil Thilak · 2025 International Conference on Vehicular Technology and Transportation Systems (ICVTTS) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icvtts67119.2025.11296589 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANETs) are essential in modern transportation, enabling communication among vehicles and infrastructure. However, VANET simulations often lack realism, especially in modeling radio propagation.
**Approach:** This paper explores the importance of realistic radio channel modeling in Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS). We review existing V2V channel models used in simulations and analyze the 3GPP V2V model, focusing on its key channel metrics.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: real-world. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** By comparing simulation models with real-world conditions, we reveal critical gaps that affect evaluation accuracy.
