# Latency Prediction for Delay-sensitive V2X Applications in Mobile Cloud/Edge Computing Systems
Wenhan Zhang, Mingjie Feng, Marwan Krunz, Haris Volos · GLOBECOM 2020 - 2020 IEEE Global Communications Conference 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/globecom42002.2020.9348104 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Estimating offloading latency for delay-sensitive V2X on MEC.
**Approach:** Decompose latency into trend (LSTM with k-medoids) and noise (Epanechnikov kernel plus moving average).
**Tools and scale:** Latency measurements collected while driving.
**Results:** Prediction error reduced to half a standard deviation of raw data.
