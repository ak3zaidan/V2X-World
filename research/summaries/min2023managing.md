# Managing and Optimizing 5G & Beyond Network Resources for Multi-Task Digital Twin Applications in Industry 4.0
Ziran Min, Shuang Zhou, Zhuang-Wei Kang, Shashank Shekhar, C. Mahmoudi, S. Gokhale, A. Gokhale · IEEE International Symposium on Real-Time Distributed Computing 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/isorc58943.2023.00039 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Industry 4.0 is leading factories to undergo a significant transformation, where automation is achieved through the use of modern smart technologies, such as 5G & beyond $(5 \mathrm{G}+)$ network and digital twins.
**Approach:** Yet, many Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) applications, including smart factories and robotic repair, present challenges in delivering dedicated and real-time network services between the physical world entities and their digital twins due to the different network requirements of each sub tasks of the applications. Although 5G+ networks can provide high-speed, low-latency, and reliable network services, managing and optimizing the network resources in real-time remains complex and time-consuming.
