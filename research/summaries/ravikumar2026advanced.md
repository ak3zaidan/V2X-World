# Advanced Intrusion Detection System for Position Falsification Attacks in VANETs Using Ensemble Learning and KNN-KFSC Models
Ch. Ravikumar, Sireesha Vikkurty, Vankudoth Ramesh, M. Gayathri et al. · Journal of Engineering Science and Technology Review 2026 · https://doi.org/10.25103/jestr.194.05 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Position falsification IDS.
**Approach:** KNN with kinematic feature selection (KNN-KFSC) and federated learning versus XGBoost, RF, DT.
**Tools and scale:** Resampled VeReMi (benign 60,000; ~30,000 per attack).
**Results:** XGBoost up to 97%; KNN-KFSC 99%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Extend datasets to emerging attacks; improve kinematic features; hybrid DL models; trials in real VANETs on latency and data variability.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
