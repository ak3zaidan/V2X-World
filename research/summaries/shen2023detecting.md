# Detecting Data Spoofing in Connected Vehicle based Intelligent Traffic Signal Control using Infrastructure-Side Sensors and Traffic Invariants
Junjie Shen, Ziwen Wan, Yunpeng Luo, Yiheng Feng et al. · 2023 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iv55152.2023.10186689 · relevance: high · basis: abstract only

**Problem:** Detecting data spoofing against CV-based signal control.
**Approach:** Trust estimation of connected vehicles from infrastructure-side sensors and traffic invariants.
**Tools and scale:** Industrial-grade traffic simulator (named in paper); two congestion attacks.
**Results:** >=95% true positive rate with <7% false positives; robust to sensor noise.
