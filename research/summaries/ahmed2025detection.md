# Detection, Identification, and Mitigation of False Data Injection Attacks in Vehicle Platooning
Najeebuddin Ahmed, Amir Ameli, Hassan Naser · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2024.3456080 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle platooning has gained significant attention due to its potential to enhance road safety, fuel efficiency, and traffic flow.
**Approach:** However, the reliance on interconnected communication technology in platooning necessitates robust cybersecurity measures. This paper introduces frameworks for detecting and identifying cyber-attacks, specifically False Data Injection Attacks (FDIAs), aimed at securing vehicle platoons.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: platoon/CACC attacks).
**Results:** The simulation results highlight the accuracy of attack detection, particularly under stealthy attacks, and the successful identification of compromised vehicles.
