# Distributed Deception Attack Detection in Platoon-Based Connected Vehicle Systems
Zhiyang Ju, Hui Zhang, Ying Tan · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2020.2980137 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sensor deception attacks on platoon position/distance can cause collisions.
**Approach:** Distributed Kalman filter with modified GLR detection and estimation of piecewise-constant attacks. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** CarSim simulation.
**Results:** Effective versus standard chi-square detector.
