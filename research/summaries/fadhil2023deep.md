# Using Deep-Learning for 5G End-to-End Delay Estimation Based on Gaussian Mixture Models
Diyar Fadhil, Rodolfo Oliveira · Information 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/info14120648 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Estimating 5G end-to-end delay.
**Approach:** GMM components via EM used as labels for a DNN estimator.
**Tools and scale:** Simulated 5G scenarios.
**Results:** DLEGMM more accurate than EM-GMM but needs more computation.
