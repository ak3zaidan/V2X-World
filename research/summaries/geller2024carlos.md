# CARLOS: An Open, Modular, and Scalable Simulation Framework for the Development and Testing of Software for C-ITS
Christian Geller, Benedikt Haas, Amarin Kloeker, Jona Hermens, Bastian Lampe, Till Beemelmanns et al. · arXiv 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iv55156.2024.10588502 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** C-ITS software needs automated, scalable simulation-based development and testing.
**Approach:** CARLOS: containerised microservice framework on CARLA and ROS for prototyping, data-driven development and automated testing.
**Tools and scale:** CARLA, ROS, Docker, CI.
**Results:** Benefits over native CARLA in scalability and maintainability (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Potential for orchestrating distributed large-scale simulations to be explored.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — AD software testing framework; no V2X security.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
