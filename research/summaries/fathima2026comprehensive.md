# A Comprehensive Review of Intrusion Detection Systems for Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks: Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Emerging Challenges
Syed Simrin Fathima, Jimmy Singla · 2026 7th International Conference on Inventive Research in Computing Applications (ICIRCA) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icirca69024.2026.11570298 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The significance of VANET to Intelligent transport automation lies in their ability to communicate real time between the vehicle and the infrastructure; however, the dynamics of VANETs make them highly susceptible to attacks from cybercriminals; hence, the necessity for strong Intrusion Detection System (IDS).
**Approach:** The objective of this research paper is to conduct a thorough review of the various IDS methodologies that employ ML, DL and hybrid approaches.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The findings from the study reveal that IDSs that employ DL models have the highest accuracy and effectiveness in detecting intrusions compared to all other IDS architectures; nonetheless, DL models face challenges related to scalability, complexity, resource usage and lack of transparency.
