# CPP-CLAS: Efficient and Conditional Privacy-Preserving Certificateless Aggregate Signature Scheme for VANETs
Yu-Lei Chen, Jianhua Chen · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2021.3121552 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the popularity of vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs), the secure communications between the vehicle and vehicle as well as between the vehicle and infrastructure have attracted people’s great attention. Vehicle privacy protection (including vehicle identity privacy and location privacy), high mobility, density, and limited bandwidth are also a problem ...
**Approach:** Based on the aforementioned analysis, we design a fully aggregated conditional privacy-preserving CLAS scheme (CPP-CLAS) for VANETs.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Also, with the use of CLAS technology, the computation and communication cost of the scheme are significantly reduced. Performance and security analyses show that the CPP-CLAS scheme is more efficient and secure than other existing related schemes.
