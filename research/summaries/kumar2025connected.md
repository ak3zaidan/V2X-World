# Connected Vehicles Secure Data Sharing using Secure and Differential Privacy computation on multi-party
K. T. kumar, Rallabandi Venkata Santoshi Saraswati Swetha Nagini, A. Shivaprasad, R. Maheswari et al. · 2025 International Conference on Computational Innovations and Engineering Sustainability (ICCIES) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccies63851.2025.11032978 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** To ensure effective and secure transportation systems, networked automobiles need to share enormous amounts of data that safeguard users' privacy. Data leakage issues, large computing expense, and lack of differential privacy guarantees are some of the challenges with existing methods.
**Approach:** The Privacy-Preserving Vehicular Data Exchange Framework (PPVDEF) to address the above issues.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The proposed Method achieves the data privacy by 97.6%, accuracy by 98.5% and scalability by 96.3%.
