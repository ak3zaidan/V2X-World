# On Effectiveness of Graph Neural Network Architectures for Network Digital Twins (NDTs)
Iulisloi Zacarias, Oussama Ben Taarit, Admela Jukan · 2025 IEEE Conference on Network Function Virtualization and Software-Defined Networking (NFV-SDN) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/nfv-sdn66355.2025.11349484 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Which GNN architectures suit NDTs?
**Approach:** AI-NDT with multi-layer knowledge graph; four GNN architectures compared.
**Tools and scale:** RIPE Atlas measurements.
**Results:** GraphTransformer performs best.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Other architectures may suit shorter training time.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
