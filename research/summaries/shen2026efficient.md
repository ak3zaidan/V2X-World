# An Efficient, Identifiable and Abortable Multi-Party Signature Scheme for VANETs
Gang Shen, Zhenhua Han, Weizhi Meng, Mingwu Zhang · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2026.3664438 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs) are networks based on short-range wireless communication technology, mainly used for direct communication between vehicles and interaction with roadside infrastructures. The emergence of VANETs has improved the efficiency and safety of vehicle travel.
**Approach:** To address this issue, we propose an efficient, identifiable, and abortable multi-party signature scheme for VANETs. Specifically, we utilize the property of zero-knowledge proofs to design a method that can efficiently identify malicious vehicles during the signature process, and define a strategy to quickly locate false proofs.
