# Simulator and on-road testing of truck platooning: a systematic review
Telmo Costa Botelho, Sérgio Pedro Duarte, Marta Campos Ferreira, Sara Ferreira, António Lobo · European Transport Research Review 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1186/s12544-024-00705-6 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** State of simulator and on-road truck platooning testing.
**Approach:** PRISMA systematic review of 30 articles (Web of Science, Scopus).
**Tools and scale:** Review.
**Results:** Typical formation: three trucks at 80 km/h with 10 m gaps; simulator studies focus on human factors, on-road on technology.
