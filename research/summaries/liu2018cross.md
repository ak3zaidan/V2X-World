# A Cross-Layer MAC Aware Pseudonym (MAP) Scheme for the VANET
Zi-Shan Liu, Lin Zhang, Wei Ni, I. Collings · Global Communications Conference 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/glocom.2018.8647808 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** MAC-layer transmission patterns link old and new pseudonyms even with synchronous changes.
**Approach:** MAC semantic linking attack and MAC-aware pseudonym (MAP) scheme with time-slotted access changing slot pattern with pseudonym.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation; metrics pseudonym age, anonymity set, time-to-confusion, PDR.
**Results:** MAP improves location privacy and transmission efficiency.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
