# MINT-V2X: A Mobility-Integrated Network Trajectory Dataset for Predictive Resource Management
Abdullah Anjum, Abdolazim Rezaei, Mehdi Sookhak · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22654 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) communication systems are based on datasets that not only contain vehicle trajectory data but also wireless network parameters with a realistic level of fidelity, enabling the creation of prediction and optimization models. There is a very critical research infrastructure gap today, and publicly available datasets are likely to be limited to one of the two: mobility or network parameters, and rarely provide a single, integrated view that combines both.
**Approach:** This paper introduces MINT-V2X, a comprehensive dataset generated by coupling SUMO traffic dynamics with OMNeT++/Simu5G network simulation. We demonstrate strict algorithmic consistency through network metric correlations (CQI-SINR: 0.993; SINR-PDR: 0.946).
**Tools and scale:** SUMO + OMNeT++/Simu5G (LTE/C-V2X); 1,386 vehicles, 15 RSUs, 3 h urban traffic, 100 ms granularity; 14 validation tests from 3GPP Rel-14, ETSI and Shannon capacity.
**Results:** The validation framework is composed of 14 standardized tests based on 3GPP Release 14 (C-V2X), ETSI standards and Shannon capacity theory. The resulting dataset contains 9.87 million synchronized data points from 1,386 vehicles from 15 roadside units (RSUs) during 3 hours of urban traffic simulation. Finally, we demonstrate the value of the dataset by conducting an RSU load prediction case study, showing that using trajectory data yields better predictive performance than network-history-only baselines.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Documented short-range deviations, e.g. "RSRP upper-bound exceedance".
- Simulation-only data (no field validation stated).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Synchronized per-vehicle mobility + PHY metrics with ground-truth RSU association from a coupled simulator, released for reproduction; no security/PKI or misbehaviour labels, no counterfactual pairs.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim the first joint trajectory–network V2X dataset; joint traffic+radio+PKI per-message ground truth and counterfactual labels remain open.
