**Problem:** Since the emergence of wireless communication networks, a plethora of research papers focus their attention on the quality aspects of wireless links.
**Approach:** The analysis of the rich body of existing literature on link quality estimation using models developed from data traces indicates that the techniques used for modeling link quality estimation are becoming increasingly sophisticated. A number of recent estimators leverage Machine Learning (ML) techniques that require a sophisticated design and development process, each of which has a great potential to significantly affect the overall model performance.
