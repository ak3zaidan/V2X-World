# Privacy-Preserving Public Key Infrastructure for Vehicular Networks
Pavlo Gaiduk, Kumar Rajeev Ranjan, Thomas Basmer, Florian Tschorsch · 2020 IEEE 45th Conference on Local Computer Networks (LCN) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/lcn48667.2020.9314787 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Policy-based privacy in PKIs is insufficient.
**Approach:** Zero-knowledge-proof PKI with anonymous reputation-based blacklisting.
**Tools and scale:** Benchmarks on resource-constrained on-board connectivity unit.
**Results:** About 67 kB payload and 35 minutes computation per day to authenticate.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
