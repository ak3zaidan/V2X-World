# LLM4CoT: Large Language Models Orchestrating Chain-of-Task Optimization via Meta-Adapter in 6G Heterogeneous Networks
Han Ji, Cheng-Xiang Wang, Zhou-Hao Li et al. · IEEE Transactions on Network Science and Engineering 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnse.2026.3693704 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The sixth-generation (6G) wireless communication is evolving from traditional terrestrial homogeneous architectures toward integrated space-air-ground-sea heterogeneous networks (HetNets).
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a large language model (LLM)-empowered chain-of-task (CoT) optimization framework, termed LLM4CoT, to tackle the cross-layer multi-task optimization, including link quality estimation, access point (AP) selection, and resource allocation. In addition, we design a unified MetaAdapter structure that captures intertask causal dependencies while mitigating error propagation across tasks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** In such HetNets, network optimization must jointly address crosslayer multitask coordination spanning the physical, link, and network layers, as optimizing each layer independently often fails to achieve globally optimal performance. Experimental results demonstrate that LLM4CoT with MetaAdapter achieves network sumrate comparable to conventional joint optimization algorithms, and outperforms stateoftheart lightweight machine learning baselines in both sumrate and generalization.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "However, existing studies have largely focused on multitask optimization within individual layers, leaving crosslayer multitask coordination an open challenge."
