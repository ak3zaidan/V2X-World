# Clustering of Geographical Segments for Predictive Quality of Service of Connected Vehicles
Oscar Stenhammar, Saeed Razavikia, Gábor Fodor, Carlo Fischione · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2025.3580989 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Concept drift in pQoS for connected vehicles.
**Approach:** Road segment clustering with per-cluster federated models; joint optimisation of training and cluster count via proximal alternating minimisation with convergence guarantees.
**Tools and scale:** Real-world data.
**Results:** Clustered model outperforms baselines (truncated).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Trade-off between cluster count, accuracy and overhead.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
