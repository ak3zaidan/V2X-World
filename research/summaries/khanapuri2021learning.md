# Learning Based Vehicle Platooning Threat Detection, Identification and Mitigation
Eshaan M. Khanapuri, Veera Venkata Tarun Kartik Chintalapati, Rajnikant Sharma, Ryan Gerdes · venue not stated 2021 · https://doi.org/10.36227/techrxiv.14079644 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The security of cyber-physical systems, such as vehicle platoons, is critical to ensuring their proper operation and acceptance to society. In platooning, vehicles follow one another according to an agreed upon control law that determines vehicle separation.
**Approach:** In this paper we focus on detecting, identifying and mitigating so called destabilizing attacks that could cause vehicle collisions. Results indicate that with noise upto 30% in range/relative speed data we achieve an accuracy upto 96.3%.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting (VANET/IoV/V2X). Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** Realistic simulator CARLA and MATLAB simulation results validate the effectiveness of our proposed approaches
