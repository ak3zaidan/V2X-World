# Rephrase and Contrast: Fine-Tuning Language Models for Enhanced Understanding of Communication and Computer Networks
Liujianfu Wang, Yuyang Du, Jingqi Lin, Kexin Chen et al. · 2025 International Conference on Computing, Networking and Communications (ICNC) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icnc64010.2025.10993716 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Lightweight LLMs understand networking poorly.
**Approach:** Rephrase and Contrast fine-tuning with GPT-assisted QA mining.
**Tools and scale:** Networking problem set.
**Results:** 63.73% accuracy improvement over base model.
