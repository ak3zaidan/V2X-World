# Method for Determining Node Position in Vehicle Ad-Hoc Network Based on Generator Simulation Urban Mobility (SUMO) Optimization
Wahyudi Ramadhan, Andani, Syafruddin Syarif · 2024 11th International Conference on Electrical Engineering, Computer Science and Informatics (EECSI) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/eecsi63442.2024.10776399 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The development of vehicle ad hoc network (VANET) technology is increasingly developing; this can be achieved because road traffic simulators and network communications are complex and always changing, and a hybrid framework is often required.
**Approach:** One hybrid approach is by simulating vehicles in a network, combining Urban Mobility Simulation (SUMO) with OMNET++, which is widely used in discrete simulations, and traffic modeling, which is also used to detect nodes that have experienced losses and are prone to errors. In addition, traffic modeling contributes to traffic recovery, which is very important for developing complex frameworks.
