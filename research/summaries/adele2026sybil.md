# Sybil Attack Detection in VANETs: A Forgetting-Aware Approach Using Stochastic Gradient Descent with Exponential Weight Decay
Gideon  Oluwasegun Adele, Anirudh Paranjothi, Johnson  P. Thomas · SSRN Electronic Journal 2026 · https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7265298 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Stale beacons bias Sybil detection.
**Approach:** SGD with exponential weight decay (forgetting) favouring recent nodes.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Higher accuracy/precision/recall/F1 and lower FPR and log-loss than baselines.
