# Toward Generative 6G Simulation: An Experimental Multi-Agent LLM and ns-3 Integration
Farhad Rezazadeh, Amir Ashtari Gargari, Sandra Lagén, Houbing Song, Dusit Niyato, Lingjia Liu · 2025 IEEE International Mediterranean Conference on Communications and Networking (MeditCom) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/meditcom64437.2025.11104374 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Full-stack 6G simulation needs automated scenario generation and analysis.
**Approach:** LangChain multi-agent system (simulation generation with CoT and RAG, test designer, test executor, result interpretation) producing ns-3 scripts from natural language and interpreting results.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3; Streamlit, Pinecone, LangChain/LangGraph, OpenAI models; 5G scenarios.
**Results:** Generates and interprets simulations with actionable feedback (quantitative results not extracted).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in extracted sections.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - LLM agents configuring, running and interpreting network simulations, overlapping our LLM agent feature.
**Novelty implications:** LLM-driven simulation generation and interpretation is published; novelty must rest on V2X security/PKI analysis and ground-truth-checked reasoning.
