# A Digital Twin based Framework to Enable “What-If” Analysis in BGP Optimization
Marco Polverini, Ilaria Germini, Antonio Cianfrani, Francesco G. Lavacca et al. · NOMS 2023-2023 IEEE/IFIP Network Operations and Management Symposium 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/noms56928.2023.10154393 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Nowadays, inter domain routing optimization is performed based on the so called “Tweak and Pray” approach, which consists in performing changes in the configuration of the BGP protocol without knowing in advance the consequences of such a modification. This is due to the lack of cooperation among Network Operators in the configuration of the BGP to optimize the inter domain routing.
**Approach:** In this paper we propose a novel framework based on the Digital Twin technology to enable the execution of “what-if” analysis in the context of Traffic Engineering performed by tuning BGP parameters.
