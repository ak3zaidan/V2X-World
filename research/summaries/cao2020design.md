# Design and implementation of a wideband channel emulation platform for 5G mmWave vehicular communication
Jue Cao, Fai Tila, Andrew Nix · IET Communications 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1049/iet-com.2019.1016 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Evaluating 5G mmWave systems at high velocity.
**Approach:** Real-time HIL wideband channel emulator combining up to eight 160 MHz sub-channels.
**Tools and scale:** 60 GHz modems; AWGN, Ricean, Rayleigh channels.
**Results:** Negligible throughput loss to 293.9 km/h (K>=10 dB); connectivity to 587.8 km/h; Rayleigh limit 51.4 km/h.
