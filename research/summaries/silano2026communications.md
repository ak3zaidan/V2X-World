# Communications-Aware NMPC for Multi-Rotor Aerial Relay Networks Under Jamming Interference
Giuseppe Silano, Daniel Bonilla Licea, Davide Liuzza et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, 2026 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2026.3738530 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Multi-Rotor Aerial Vehicles (MRAVs) are increasingly used in communication-dependent missions where connectivity loss directly compromises task execution.
**Approach:** This paper presents a modular communications-aware control approach combining a high-level $\max$-$\min$ trajectory generator with an actuator-level Nonlinear Model Predictive Controller (NMPC).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Results show a near two-order-of-magnitude increase in minimum end-to-end capacity, markedly reducing outage events, with moderate average-capacity gains.
