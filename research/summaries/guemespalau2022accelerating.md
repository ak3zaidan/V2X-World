# Accelerating Deep Reinforcement Learning for Digital Twin Network Optimization with Evolutionary Strategies
Carlos Güemes-Palau, Paul Almasan, Shihan Xiao, Xiangle Cheng et al. · arXiv 2022 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.00360 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Approach:** Evolutionary strategies to train DRL routing agents.
**Tools and scale:** NSFNET and GEANT2.
