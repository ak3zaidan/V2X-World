# Identity-Based Authentication in VANETs: A Review
Ahmad Manasrah, Qussai M. Yaseen, Hussain Al-Aqrabi, Lu Liu · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2025.3528932 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Robust authentication protocols are essential to safeguard vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) and vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I) communications, given the vulnerability of the open wireless communication medium in VANETs to interception and manipulation.
**Approach:** While researchers are actively developing authentication and privacy schemes to secure message exchanges, existing solutions have not fully satisfied all the security and privacy requirements, thereby fueling ongoing research in this field. This paper presents a comprehensive overview of VANETs, covering their key components, prevalent attack types, and the security and privacy prerequisites essential for effective authentication and privacy mechanisms.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** The primary objective is to empower the research community to design and implement efficient and secure authentication schemes, minimizing communication and computational overhead while ensuring resilience against a multitude of potential attacks.
