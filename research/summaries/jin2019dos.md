# DoS-resilient cooperative beacon verification for vehicular communication systems
Hongyu Jin, Panagiotis Papadimitratos · Ad hoc networks 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adhoc.2018.10.003 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Signature verification overload and clogging DoS on OBUs.
**Approach:** Cooperative beacon verification adding symmetric-key authentication on top of pseudonymous PKI.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Low waiting times for beacons even under DoS.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial: engine models verification load and DoS; cooperative symmetric layer differs.
**Novelty implications:** Verification-DoS mitigation exists; safety impact in closed loop remains open.
