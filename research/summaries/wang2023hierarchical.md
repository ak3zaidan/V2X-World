# Hierarchical Graph Neural Networks for Causal Discovery and Root Cause Localization
Dongjie Wang, Zhengzhang Chen, Jingchao Ni et al. · arXiv 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.01987 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we propose REASON, a novel framework that enables the automatic discovery of both intra-level (i.e., within-network) and inter-level (i.e., across-network) causal relationships for root cause localization.
**Approach:** The Topological Causal Discovery component aims to model the fault propagation in order to trace back to the root causes. To achieve this, we propose novel hierarchical graph neural networks to construct interdependent causal networks by modeling both intra-level and inter-level non-linear causal relations.
