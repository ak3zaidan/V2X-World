# IP-CHOCK Reference Detection and Prevention of Denial of Service (DoS) Attacks in Vehicular Ad-Hoc Network
Karan Verma · Research Anthology on Combating Denial-of-Service Attacks 2021 · https://doi.org/10.4018/978-1-7998-5348-0.ch030 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-Hoc Network (VANET) is a subset of Mobile Ad-Hoc Network (MANET) and it is considered as a substantial component of Intelligent Transportation System (ITS).
**Approach:** DoS attacks on VANET are varying and may be overwhelmed by VANET protocols, such as TCP or UDP flooding attacks. Different secure communications models can be used to detect and prevent IP spoofing DoS attacks, by which the attacks are committed by fraudulent and malicious nodes.
