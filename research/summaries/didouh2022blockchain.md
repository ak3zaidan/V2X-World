# Blockchain-Based Collaborative Certificate Revocation Systems Using Clustering
Ahmed Didouh, Houda Labiod, Yassin El Hillali, Atika Rivenq · IEEE Access 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2022.3160171 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Centralized revocation struggles to revoke in real time as fleets grow.
**Approach:** Decentralized collaborative revocation: dynamically formed blockchain communities (clustering) revoke malicious vehicles; analytical models of real-time revocation; Sybil and fake-position attacks.
**Tools and scale:** Real V2X hardware (three vehicles and one RSU) plus large-scale simulation.
**Results:** Exposure and detection times meet real-time requirements; feasibility at scale in simulation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: proof of concept as a real traffic management server.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — measures exposure/detection time for revocation of misbehaving vehicles, overlapping direction 1 (time to neutralise), though via blockchain, not SCMS.
**Novelty implications:** Revocation-time metrics exist for decentralized schemes; the standard SCMS MA/CRL pipeline and its safety effect remain open.
