# Towards Synthetic Data Generation of VANET Attacks for Efficient Testing
Thomas Rosenstatter, Kateryna Melnyk · IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iv55152.2023.10186685 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Simulation for VANET attack datasets is costly.
**Approach:** Attack generation framework training deep generative models on simulated or real attacks; ETSI-compliant CAM/DENM simulator.
**Tools and scale:** Standard-compliant CAM/DENM generator.
**Results:** Feasibility of deep-learning attack generation shown.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Early-stage.
- outline.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Learned surrogate for attack data, near our learned world model direction.
**Novelty implications:** Learned attack generators exist; counterfactual ground truth not addressed.
