# Measurement and Prediction of Transit System Performance Using Probe Data Generated through DSRC and non-DSRC Technologies
Gregory L. Newmark ·  2024 · https://doi.org/10.31979/mti.2024.2008 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Transit performance from DSRC and GTFS-RT probe data.
**Approach:** History of DSRC transit uses; segment time-point imputation from GTFS-RT.
**Tools and scale:** Several days of GTFS-RT data.
**Results:** DSRC never embedded in transit operations.
