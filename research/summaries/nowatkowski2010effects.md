# The effects of limited lifetime pseudonyms on certificate revocation list size in VANETS
Michael Nowatkowski, Jennie Wolfgang, Chris McManus, H. Owen · SoutheastCon 2010 · https://doi.org/10.1109/secon.2010.5453849 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** CRL size may grow quickly with pseudonym policies and revocation triggers.
**Approach:** Quantifies parameters determining CRL size; proposes a 'valid after' field in the 1609.2 certificate.
**Tools and scale:** Analysis.
**Results:** Large CRL sizes identified (no numbers in abstract).

- None stated.
