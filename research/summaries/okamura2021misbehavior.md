# Misbehavior Detection Method by Time Series Change of Vehicle Position in Vehicle-to-Everything Communication
Toshiki Okamura, Kenya Sato · Journal of Transportation Technologies 2021 · https://doi.org/10.4236/jtts.2021.112018 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Position faking.
**Approach:** Detection from time-series change of vehicle positions.
**Tools and scale:** Four misbehaviour models.
**Results:** Higher F-measure than related method for random positions.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Suited to time-varying position attacks.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
