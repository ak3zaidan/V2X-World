# LUCOOP: Leibniz University Cooperative Perception and Urban Navigation Dataset
Jeldrik Axmann, Rozhin Moftizadeh, Jingyao Su, Benjamin Tennstedt, Qianqian Zou, Yunshuang Yuan et al. · 2023 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iv55152.2023.10186693 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Recently published datasets have been increasingly comprehensive with respect to their variety of simultaneously used sensors, traffic scenarios, environmental conditions, and provided annotations.
**Approach:** However, these datasets typically only consider data collected by one independent vehicle. Hence, there is currently a lack of comprehensive, real-world, multi-vehicle datasets fostering research on cooperative applications such as object detection, urban navigation, or multi-agent SLAM.
