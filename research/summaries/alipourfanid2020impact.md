Amir Alipour-Fanid, Monireh Dabaghchian, Kai Zeng · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2020.3030251 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Approach:** Jamming and fading integrated into CACC state equations; time-domain mean string stability analysis; collision probability estimation. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Analytical + Monte Carlo.
