# Improving Unlinkability in C-ITS: A Methodology For Optimal Obfuscation
Yevhen Zolotavkin, Yurii Baryshev, Vitalii Lukichov, Jannik Mähn et al. · Proceedings of the 9th International Conference on Information Systems Security and Privacy 2023 · https://doi.org/10.5220/0011786900003405 · relevance: medium · basis: full text

**Problem:** High-assurance unlinkability for CAM-based C-ITS.
**Approach:** Hidden Markov Model of two cars communicating with an RSU; optimal obfuscation of observable states maximising entropy under distortion constraint.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical/information-theoretic.
**Results:** Proves at most two observable states per hidden state are optimal.
