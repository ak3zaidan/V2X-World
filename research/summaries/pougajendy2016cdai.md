# CDAI: a novel collaborative detection approach for impersonation attacks in vehicular ad-hoc networks
Jayashree Pougajendy, A. Parthiban · Secur. Commun. Networks 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1002/sec.1716 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Ensuring security in vehicular ad‐hoc networks remains a challenging task because of the high mobility of vehicles and the wireless communication environment.
**Approach:** The wireless environment, which is un‐trusted, paves a way for the attackers to perform various attacks at the cost of time, money, safety and human lives. One such attack that needs attention is the impersonation attack because of its ability to disrupt the proper functioning of the network.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: PKI
**Results:** From the simulation experiments, it is evident that CDAI achieves a detection rate of 97.85% and a false positive rate of 0.015%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Ensuring security in vehicular ad‐hoc networks remains a challenging task because of the high mobility of vehicles and the wireless communication environment.
- Existing defense mechanisms viz., public key infrastructure (PKI) methods incur infrastructural overheads, position based verification methods face limitations such as inadaptability to certain environments (one way roads, heavy traffic conditions), high storage, and communication overheads.
- In this paper, collaborative detection approach for impersonation attacks (CDAI), a position‐based verification scheme based on received signal strength, is proposed to overcome all the existing limitations.
