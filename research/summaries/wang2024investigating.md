# Investigating the Effects of Human–Machine Interface on Cooperative Driving Using a Multi-Driver Co-Simulation Platform
Zijin Wang, Mohamed Abdel-Aty, Lishengsa Yue, Jiahao Zhu, Ou Zheng, Mohamed H. Zaki · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Vehicles 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tiv.2023.3296678 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Effects of HMI design on cooperative speed-adaptation driving.
**Approach:** Multi-driver co-simulation platform; three HMIs tested in driving simulator.
**Tools and scale:** Multi-driver driving simulator.
**Results:** Delta-t HMI worsened speed adaptation; Delta-v graphic HMI improved performance and was preferred.
