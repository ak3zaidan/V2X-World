# Small-Scale Testbed for Evaluating C-V2X Applications on 5G Cellular Networks
Kaj Munhoz Arfvidsson, Kleio Fragkedaki, Frank J. Jiang, Vandana Narri, Hans-Cristian Lindh, Karl H. Johansson et al. · arXiv 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iv55156.2024.10588559 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Hard to prototype C-V2X applications with real 5G networks and hardware in a controlled setting.
**Approach:** Small-scale testbed with 1/10 vehicles on a 5G network; shared situational awareness test case.
**Tools and scale:** Real 5G network, small-scale vehicles, ITS agents.
**Results:** Shows effect of extra network users and handovers on the application (no numbers extracted).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Implement full 5G shared-situational-awareness and intelligent intersection applications
- investigate features limiting handovers.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
