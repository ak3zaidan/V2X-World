# Misbehavior detection and efficient revocation within VANET
Hamssa Hasrouny, Abed Ellatif Samhat, Carole Bassil, Anis Laouiti · Journal of Information Security and Applications 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jisa.2019.03.001 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** How to keep only trustworthy participants and revoke misbehaving ones efficiently.
**Approach:** Certificate revocation framework triggered by in-vehicle MDSs and an infrastructure Misbehaviour Authority; periodic geographic CRLs listing revoked vehicles in an area; PKI with group formation and trust evaluation.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation scenarios and risk analysis.
**Results:** Advantages of the revocation framework shown (no figures in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — MDS-to-MA-to-CRL revocation pipeline with CRL distribution, which our engine implements end to end (with real SCMS linkage-based CRLs).
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim the first MDS-triggered revocation framework with CRL distribution; what remains is quantified time-to-neutralise and safety impact with standard SCMS mechanisms.
