# Building a Graph-based Deep Learning network model from captured traffic traces
Carlos Güemes-Palau, M. Ferriol-Galmés, A. Cabellos-Aparicio, P. Barlet-Ros · arXiv.org 2023 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11889 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** ML network models trained on simulated data inherit simulator pitfalls.
**Approach:** GNN with new packet-sequence encoding and improved message passing trained on captured traces (GNNet Challenge 2023).
**Tools and scale:** Captured real traffic traces.
**Results:** Test MAPE 27.831% vs baseline barely over 100%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Simulated scenarios fail to capture all real complexities.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - network surrogate from real traces.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
