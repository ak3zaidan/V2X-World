# Detecting Injection Attacks on Cooperative Adaptive Cruise Control
Marco Iorio, Fulvio Risso, Riccardo Sisto, Alberto Buttiglieri, Massimo Reineri · 2019 IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference (VNC) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc48660.2019.9062798 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Injection attacks deceive CACC controllers.
**Approach:** Realistic simulation security analysis of smooth falsification attacks; MBD using correlation between motion parameters of single and consecutive vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Realistic platoon simulation (Plexe-type).
**Results:** Slight smooth falsifications cause numerous crashes; detector detects all simulated attacks and prevents safety-critical situations.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — attack-induced crashes and detection-in-loop for platoons.
**Novelty implications:** Closed-loop local detection preventing crashes in CACC exists; not via PKI revocation.
