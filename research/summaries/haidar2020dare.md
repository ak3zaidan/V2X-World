# DARE: A Reports Dataset for Global Misbehavior Authority Evaluation in C-ITS
Farah Haidar, Joseph Kamel, Ines Ben Jemaa, Arnaud Kaiser, Brigitte Lonc, Pascal Urien · 2020 IEEE 91st Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2020-Spring) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2020-spring48590.2020.9128687 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The cloud (global) Misbehaviour Authority component lacks evaluation data.
**Approach:** DARE dataset of misbehaviour reports derived from local embedded detection, across road topologies, attacker penetration rates and attack scenarios.
**Tools and scale:** F2MD-based simulation (VEINS/SUMO).
**Results:** Dataset released for global MA development and evaluation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — misbehaviour report stream for global MA evaluation, overlapping direction 1 (MA decision design).
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first dataset for global MA evaluation; open: MA decisions coupled to actual revocation (CRL/linkage) and resulting safety, in closed loop.
