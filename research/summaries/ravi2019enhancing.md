# Enhancing Vehicular Anonymity in ITS: A New Scheme for Mix Zones and Their Placement
Nirupama Ravi, C. Mani Krishna, Israel Koren · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2019.2936529 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Intelligent transportation systems (ITS) achieve improved throughput and safety using periodic vehicle-to-vehicle and vehicle-to-infrastructure wireless communication.
**Approach:** Vehicles use pseudonyms which are frequently exchanged to protect against eavesdroppers using such messages for tracking. Such exchange takes place in mix zones where all wireless transmission is forbidden in order to prevent matching the new pseudonym of a vehicle with its previous one.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: pseudonym change/privacy).
**Results:** Mix zones are not free: in addition to infrastructure cost, they impose a cost in terms of reduced vehicular throughput and disruption to vehicular communication. Second, we introduce a heuristic to place mix zones appropriately so that the gain in privacy is balanced against the cost in reduced throughput.
