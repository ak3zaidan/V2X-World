# A trust management framework for vehicular ad hoc networks
Rezvi Shahariar, Chris Phillips · International Journal of Security, Privacy and Trust Management 2024 · https://doi.org/10.5121/ijsptm.2023.12102 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) enable road users and public infrastructure to share information that improves the operation of roads and driver experience. However, these are vulnerable to poorly behaved authorized users.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a new Tamper-Proof Device (TPD) based trust management framework for controlling trust at the sender side vehicle that regulates driver behaviour. If a conflict arises, the Road-Side Unit (RSU) decides, using the weighted voting system, whether the originator is to be believed, or not.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in abstract.
**Results:** The framework is evaluated against a centralized reputation approach and the results demonstrate that it outperforms the latter.
