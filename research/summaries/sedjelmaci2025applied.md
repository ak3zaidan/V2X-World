# Applied differential comparative study of VANET simulators: TrAD protocol study using veins and VNS VANET simulators in both real and standard city maps
Amina Sedjelmaci, Hayet Benosman, Ahmed Abdul Rahuman · Indonesian Journal of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science 2025 · https://doi.org/10.11591/ijeecs.v40.i3.pp1357-1367 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Do simulators agree when evaluating the same protocol?
**Approach:** TrAD protocol in Veins (OMNeT++) and VNS (NS-3) on Manhattan grid and Tlemcen.
**Tools and scale:** Veins, VNS.
**Results:** VNS consistently more optimistic; real map gives 10-15% lower PRR; discrepancies larger for delay in complex environments.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Only two simulators, one protocol, mainly V2V, one real city.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — simulator disagreement, relevant to our cross-validation claim.
**Novelty implications:** Simulator discrepancy is documented; our claim of matching Veins must be shown quantitatively.
