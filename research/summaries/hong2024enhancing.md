# Enhancing Black Hole Attack Detection in VANETs: A Hybrid Approach Integrating DBSCAN Clustering with Decision Trees
Seung-Phil Hong · Scalable Computing Practice and Experience 2024 · https://doi.org/10.12694/scpe.v25i6.3270 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Ensuring the security of communication is crucial in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) to protect the integrity of information sharing among cars. To implement VANET communication as an answer for the different uses, secure communication is necessary.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a robust hybrid approach for the detection of black hole attacks in VANETs, leveraging the synergy between DBSCAN (Density-Based Spatial Clustering of Applications with Noise) clustering and Decision Trees. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed hybrid approach, providing a promising solution for bolstering the security of VANETs against emerging threats.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting (VANET/IoV/V2X). Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** Here in result 73.89% improvement is received in Packet Drop Rate using DBSCAN, also minor improvement over Throughput and Average end to end delay and major improvement in terms of Network Routing Load.
