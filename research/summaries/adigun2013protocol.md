# Protocol of change pseudonyms for VANETs
Adetundji Adigun, Boucif Amar Bensaber, Ismail Biskri · 38th Annual IEEE Conference on Local Computer Networks - Workshops 2013 · https://doi.org/10.1109/lcnw.2013.6758514 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Traceability via pseudonyms.
**Approach:** Periodic change protocol (CA-issued or self-generated) with lifetime t = RSU spacing / mean speed.
**Tools and scale:** Bandwidth evaluation.
**Results:** Bandwidth analysis by speed.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Evaluate bit error rate and highway scenario; design a dissemination routing protocol.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
