# Adaptive Federated Reinforcement Learning With Temporal Hybrid Deep Model for Consumer Internet of Vehicles Intrusion Detection
Hamad Naeem, Amjad Alsirhani, F. Alserhani, Maha M. Althobaiti, E. Alabdulkreem · IEEE transactions on consumer electronics 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tce.2025.3634753 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Consumer devices that are integrated into the Internet of Vehicles (IoV) systems are becoming more susceptible to the sophisticated intrusion attacks, which are a major threat to user safety and the overall integrity of the system.
**Approach:** Federated Learning (FL) has been a prominent technique for collaborative training models while maintaining the confidentiality of sensitive information. In addition, conventional FL-based IDS approaches have problems to handle non-identical IID (IID) data distributions across clients, and usually use a fixed aggregation method like FedAvg and FedProx, which has poor adaptability to changeable client behaviors.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Iterative aggregation greatly improves intrusion detection performance, with detection accuracies of 99.85% and 95.18% on the benchmark datasets CICIOV2024 and CICEVSE2024, respectively.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- To tackle these limitations, this research proposes an adaptive federated learning framework by preprocessing IoVs network traffic through standardization and IID (independent and identical distributed) distribution of data among clients.
