# A POST-QUANTUM BLOCKCHAIN FRAMEWORK FOR CONNECTED SMART VEHICLES
Md Shkhakhanur Shkhagor, Evgeniy Anikeev, Andrey Akimenko · INTELLECTUAL INFORMATION SYSTEMS: FROM THEORY TO PRACTICE – 2026 : Proceedings of the International Scientific and Practical Conference, Voronezh, July 02, 2026 2026 · https://doi.org/10.58168/intinfsys2026_88-95 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Unified security architecture for connected vehicles.
**Approach:** OmniGuard V2X prototype: blockchain audit, ZK privacy, decentralised identity, PQ key establishment, federated learning, encrypted forensic black box.
**Tools and scale:** Prototype; small scale.
**Results:** Feasibility only; protocol overhead, large-scale detection accuracy and FL robustness not reported.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- No protection against Sybil attacks; open DID registration.
- Majority-controlled validators can record false data.
- Larger fleets, diverse network conditions and repeated trials needed.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — prototype architecture.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
