# Digital Twin-Driven Vehicular Task Offloading and IRS Configuration in the Internet of Vehicles
Xiaoming Yuan, Jiahui Chen, Ning Zhang, Jianbing Ni et al. · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2022.3204585 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Digital mymargin Twin (DT) and Intelligent Reflective Surface (IRS), the most two promising technologies of 6G make the Internet of Vehicles (IoV) more adaptive. However, future autonomous driving needs powerful networking resources and high-quality wireless communications to guarantee the Quality of Service (QoS).
**Approach:** In this work, we propose a Digital Twin-Driven Vehicular Task Offloading and IRS Configuration Framework (DTVIF) to efficiently monitor, learn, and manage the IoV. Specifically, we adopt Mobile Edge Computing (MEC) and IRS to provide augmented computing capacities for vehicles and improve transmission performance when vehicles communicate to MEC servers.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 6G, reinforcement learning, digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** DT is employed to achieve real-time data collection and digital representation of physical operating environments of IoV to better support decisions making. The simulations demonstrate that the proposed algorithm can effectively reduce the processing latency of task offloading and reduce the average energy consumption in DTVIF.
