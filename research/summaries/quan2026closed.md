# Closed-Loop Hybrid Digital Twin Platform for Connected and Automated Vehicle Validation
Kanglong Quan, Zhebing Xia, Linfeng Jiang, Hao Yu, Ziheng Qiao, Dapeng Dong et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iwcmc69287.2026.11580074 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Simulation-based CAV testing lacks integration with real vehicles.
**Approach:** Hybrid DT coupling CARLA-SUMO co-simulation with a real vehicle via low-latency V2X link and CAN middleware.
**Tools and scale:** CARLA, SUMO, physical test site.
**Results:** Stable synchronisation and low-latency closed-loop control.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: lower latency, multiple physical vehicles, V2X-aware evaluation via communication simulation, closed-loop AEB and lane-change validation.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
