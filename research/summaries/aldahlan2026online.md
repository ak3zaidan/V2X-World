# Online-Tuned Fuzzy Pre-Filtering with an Attention BiLSTM for Misbehavior Detection in Vehicular Named Data Networking
Bassma Aldahlan · Sensors 2026 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s26134179 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Position falsification in VNDN; static thresholds drift.
**Approach:** Online-tuned fuzzy pre-filter plus attention BiLSTM.
**Tools and scale:** Synthetic VNDN benchmark and live OMNeT++/Veins/SUMO LuST testbed.
**Results:** Macro F1 0.964; ROC-AUC 0.984; 0.44 ms per sample.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — runtime MBD in a live simulator.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
