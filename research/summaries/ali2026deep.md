# Deep Learning-Driven and Zero-Knowledge Proof-Enhanced Task Offloading for Privacy-Preserving Vehicular Networks
Muhammad Ali, Tariq Qayyum, Zouheir Trabelsi, A. Tariq · 2026 IEEE International Conference on Communications Workshops (ICC Workshops) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccworkshops63917.2026.11586275 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In vehicular networks, dynamic topology and limited on-board resources make it difficult for vehicles to execute computationally intensive tasks locally. Machine learning–based task offloading has emerged as a promising solution to optimize resource utilization and network performance.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a deep learning–driven, privacy-preserving task offloading framework for vehicular networks.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves up to 30% reduction in task latency and 25% improvement in offloading success rate compared to traditional heuristic approaches, while maintaining strong privacy guarantees and low verification overhead.
**Results:** Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves up to 30% reduction in task latency and 25% improvement in offloading success rate compared to traditional heuristic approaches, while maintaining strong privacy guarantees and low verification overhead.
