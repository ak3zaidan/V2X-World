# The Unified Butterfly Effect: Efficient Security Credential Management System for Vehicular Communications
Marcos A. Simplicio, Eduardo Lopes Cominetti, Harsh Kupwade Patil, Jefferson E. Ricardini, Marcos Vinicius M. Silva · 2018 IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference (VNC) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc.2018.8628369 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** SCMS butterfly key expansion requires two key pairs.
**Approach:** Unified butterfly key expansion using a single key pair for signing and encryption.
**Tools and scale:** Analysis.
**Results:** Processing and bandwidth for provisioning reduced by 10% to 50% for all entities with no security loss.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong - modifies the SCMS butterfly key provisioning our engine implements (unified butterfly keys are part of later IEEE 1609.2.1).
**Novelty implications:** Butterfly-key variants are established; our contribution can only be executing/validating them in simulation.
