# Predicting transmission success with Support Vector Machine in VANETs
Mamoudou Sangare, Soumya Banerjee, Paul Muhlethaler, Samia Bouzefrane · 2018 IFIP/IEEE International Conference on Performance Evaluation and Modeling in Wired and Wireless Networks (PEMWN) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.23919/pemwn.2018.8548826 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Estimating reception probability for vehicle-RSU transmissions.
**Approach:** SVM trained on outputs of an analytical Matern-process CSMA model for 1D 802.11p VANETs.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical model, no simulator.
**Results:** Numerical predictions (truncated).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Uses analytical model instead of real measurements.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
