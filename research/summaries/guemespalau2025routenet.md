# RouteNet-Gauss: Hardware-Enhanced Network Modeling with Machine Learning
Carlos Güemes-Palau, Miquel Ferriol-Galmés, Jordi Paillisse-Vilanova, Albert López-Brescó, Pere Barlet-Ros, Albert Cabellos-Aparicio · IEEE Transactions on Networking. 34 (2026) 3840-3853 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ton.2026.3668972 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** DES is costly and may be inaccurate.
**Approach:** RouteNet-Gauss: testbed used as hardware accelerator for training data plus a modular GNN; temporal aggregated estimation (TAPE).
**Tools and scale:** Physical testbed network; TensorFlow.
**Results:** Prediction error reduced up to 95% and 488x inference speedup vs DES; generalises to networks up to 10x larger.
