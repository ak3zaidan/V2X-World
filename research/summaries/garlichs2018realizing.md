# Realizing Collective Perception in the Artery Simulation Framework
Keno Garlichs, Martin Wegner, Lars C. Wolf · 2018 IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference (VNC) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc.2018.8628412 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Collective Perception (CP) service is being standardised at ETSI and needs simulation support.
**Approach:** Describes advances of a CP service implementation in the Artery framework and lists open standardisation questions.
**Tools and scale:** Artery (OMNeT++/Veins/Vanetza, SUMO), ITS-G5.
**Results:** Qualitative; presents open research questions for CPM standardisation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Open questions on CPM generation rules and standardisation are discussed (abstract).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — ETSI stack simulation with sensor-sharing service; no security/PKI.
**Novelty implications:** CPM simulation in Artery exists; not a security contribution.
