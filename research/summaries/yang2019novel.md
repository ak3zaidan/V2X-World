# A Novel Classifier Exploiting Mobility Behaviors for Sybil Detection in Connected Vehicle Systems
Zhe Yang, Kuan Zhang, Lei Lei, Kan Zheng · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2018.2872456 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil attackers disguised as multiple vehicles in connected vehicle systems.
**Approach:** Central-server learning on mobility features (NB, DT, SVM), base-station location certificates with subjective logic, edge-betweenness community detection for collusion.
**Tools and scale:** Real-world vehicle trajectory dataset.
**Results:** Effective resistance to Sybil attackers (no figures in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — centralized (MA-like) Sybil detection.
**Novelty implications:** Central mobility-based Sybil detection exists.
