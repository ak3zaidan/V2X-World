# Independent Mix Zone for Location Privacy in Vehicular Networks
Nan Guo, Linya Ma, Tianhan Gao · IEEE Access 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2018.2800907 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The sensitive information of vehicles is related to location in vehicular networks.
**Approach:** Pseudonym change is an effective way to protect the location privacy of vehicles, which is to establish a specific area called mix zone, where at least$k$number of vehicles change pseudonyms together to obtain$k$anonymity. However, it depends on the number of collaborative ones in spatiotemporal context.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: pseudonym change/privacy).
**Results:** It shows that indMZ can ensure$k$anonymity with$k/2$average cost of extended beacon message and be independent on any trusted third party.
