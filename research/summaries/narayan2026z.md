# Z-IDS: Zero-Day Intrusion Detection in IoV Using Generative Adversarial Networks
Kurmala Gowri Raghavendra Narayan, T. Balaji, R. S. R. Singh, Vanga Odelu · IEEE Access 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2026.3682762 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the rapid growth of Internet of Vehicles (IoV) technology, the attack surface has expanded significantly in recent years.
**Approach:** Traditional intrusion detection systems, such as signature-based and rule-based approaches, are inadequate for IoV environments due to their limitations in detecting zero-day attacks. To address this challenge, machine learning techniques have been widely explored in the literature for zero-day attack detection.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** However, the highly imbalanced nature of IoV datasets often leads to biased performance, favoring majority attack classes and reducing detection accuracy for minority classes. The model achieves an accuracy of 99.72% with GAN-augmented data and 99.99% in zero-day attack scenarios.
