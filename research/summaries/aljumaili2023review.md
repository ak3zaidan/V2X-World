# A Review on Secure Authentication Protocols in IOV: Algorithms, Protocols, and Comparisons

**Problem:** The Internet of Vehicles (IoV) is an emerging technological advancement with the potential to significantly transform the automotive industry and transportation networks. Its primary objective is to facilitate communication among vehicles, infrastructure, and a wide range of networked devices.
**Approach:** The primary focus lies on security because to the diverse range of security and privacy risks that have the potential to jeopardize the well-being, privacy, and lives of individuals within vehicles. The development of communication protocols for autonomous cars presents novel issues in the exploration and enhancement of IoV networks' performance.
