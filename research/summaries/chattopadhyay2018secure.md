# Secure Estimation in V2X Networks with Injection and Packet Drop Attacks
Arpan Chattopadhyay, U. Mitra, E. Ström · International Symposium on Wireless Communication Systems 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iswcs.2018.8491221 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle-to-anything (V2X) communications are essential for facilitating cooperative intelligent transport system (C- ITS) components such as traffic safety and traffic efficiency applications.
**Approach:** Integral to proper functioning of C- ITS systems is sensing and telemetery. To this end, this paper examines how to ensure security in sensing systems for V2X networks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The attack is achieved by two mechanisms: false data injection (FDI) and garbage packet injection.
