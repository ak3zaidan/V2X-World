# Acceptability Evaluation of Inter-driver Interaction via a Vehicle Agent Using Vehicle-to-Vehicle Communication on a Driving Simulator
Toshiyuki Hagiya, Kazunari Nawa · Journal of Information Processing 2021 · https://doi.org/10.2197/ipsjjip.29.667 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Misunderstandings between drivers; exchange intentions via V2V agent.
**Approach:** Wizard-of-Oz driving simulator study with five scenarios.
**Tools and scale:** Driving simulator.
**Results:** Concept approved; four hypothesised acceptability factors.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Limited scenarios
- different participants across experiments
- real roads differ.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
