# Security Enhancement of PKI-Based OTA Update Mechanisms in Software-Defined Vehicles (SDVs)
Bansi Pambhar, J. N., Shrikant S. Tangade · 2026 IEEE Madhya Pradesh Section Conference (MPCON) 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mpcon69668.2026.11508546 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Modern Software-Defined Vehicles (SDVs) are relying on Over-the-Air (OTA) updates to deliver security patches, performance improvements, and feature enhancements throughout the vehicle lifecycle. Although Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) is mostly used to secure OTA updates through digital signatures and certificate verification, its depend entirely on secure key management.
**Approach:** This paper presents a structured evaluation framework to analyze the security strength of the PKI-based OTA update mechanism.
**Tools and scale:** A detailed simulation of the entire OTA workflow is implemented, including key generation, X.509 certificate validation, digital signature verification, and vehicle integrity checks.
**Results:** Modern Software-Defined Vehicles (SDVs) are relying on Over-the-Air (OTA) updates to deliver security patches, performance improvements, and feature enhancements throughout the vehicle lifecycle. In addition, an attacker model is introduced to demonstrate how arbitrary software installation becomes possible if the OTA private key is compromised.
