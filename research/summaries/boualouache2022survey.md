# A Survey on Machine Learning-based Misbehavior Detection Systems for 5G and Beyond Vehicular Networks
Abdelwahab Boualouache, Thomas Engel · IEEE Communications Surveys &amp; Tutorials 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/comst.2023.3236448 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** No systematic analysis of ML-based misbehaviour detection systems (MDSs) for 5G-and-beyond V2X.
**Approach:** Survey classifying ML-based MDSs from security and ML perspectives, with lessons learned, recommendations for development, validation and deployment, and open research and standardisation issues.
**Tools and scale:** Survey; covers 802.11p/ITS-G5, LTE-V2X and NR-V2X context.
**Results:** Taxonomy and recommendations (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Developing effective ML-based MDSs is still an open issue.
- Open research and standardisation issues highlighted (e.g., validation and deployment of MDSs).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - surveys the MBD space our engine runs inside the simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Useful for open questions; does not cover closed-loop MBD to revocation evaluation.
