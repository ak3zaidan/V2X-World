# Malicious node identification routing and protection mechanism for VANET against various attack
Er. Ritika Saini, Harish Kundra · INTERNATIONAL JOURNAL OF COMPUTERS & TECHNOLOGY 2020 · https://doi.org/10.24297/ijct.v20i.8852 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the help of road side unit vehicles communicate among themselves.
**Approach:** The objective of this paper to secure the communication among the vehicles and the road side unit. To avoid these types of attacks, our propose technique include feature like key management system to prevent the communication among the vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** This network helps us to improve the safety and efficiency of the occupants during travelling in vehicles.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — addresses misbehaviour/attack detection (direction 1 area), but per the abstract without an integrated SCMS backend, closed-loop revocation, per-message joint ground truth or counterfactual reruns.
**Novelty implications:** Does not rule out any candidate direction on the evidence of the abstract. Leaves open quantifying this in a closed loop with real SCMS revocation and traffic safety outcomes.
