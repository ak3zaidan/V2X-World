# Controller-Targeted DDoS Attack Detection and Mitigation in Software-Defined Internet of Vehicles (SD-IoV)
Behaylu Tadele Alemu, Alemu Jorgi Muhammed · International Conference on Information and Communication Technology for Development for Africa (ICT4DA) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ict4da59526.2023.10302231 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Software-defined networking (SDN) is an emerging technology that decouples the control plane from the data plane, offering flexibility, central control, and programmability.
**Approach:** It is beneficial for Internet of Vehicles (IoV) networks, which integrate Internet of Things (loT) and Vehicular Ad Hoc Network (VANET) to implement Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS). By utilizing an SDN controller, IoV networks can benefit from centralized control and improved manageability.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: SUMO
**Results:** By utilizing an SDN controller, IoV networks can benefit from centralized control and improved manageability. To achieve this, a detection module using entropy and flow rate to identify attack traffic behavior, and a mitigation module by analyzing payload lengths is designed.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- The integration of SDN and IoV, namely, the Software-Defined Internet of Vehicles (SD- IoV) has been a promising solution for future communication.
- However, the central controller exposes a single point of failure vulnerable to Distributed Denial of Service (DDo$S$) attacks.
