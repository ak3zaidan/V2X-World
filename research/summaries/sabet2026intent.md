# Intent-First Aerial V2V for Tactical Coordination and Separation: Protocol and Performance Under Density and Disturbance
Mehrnaz Sabet · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20595 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Dense low-altitude aerial operations require more than pre-flight route coordination and last-resort collision avoidance.
**Approach:** Once aircraft are airborne, disturbances can emerge on timescales shorter than strategic reauthorization can absorb, while collision avoidance is too late and disruptive to serve as routine traffic management. Although tactical separation is recognized as the intermediate layer, realizing it at scale requires a deployable neighborhood communication mechanism that provides fresh, trusted information for local coordination.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Results show that V2V reduces stale-belief divergence, preserves observability through cooperative perception, rejects invalid tactical messages, suppresses false local inference, and structures shared-resource coordination.
