# Augmented Reality-Assisted Digital Twin for Fault Detection in Electric Vehicle Wiring Systems
Rajalakshmi M., Sheik Mastan S A R, Karthik C., Bibhuti Bhusan Dash, Utpal Chandra De, Sudhansu Shekhar Patra · 2025 International Conference on Intelligent Computing, Information and Control Systems (ICOIICS) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icoiics67115.2025.11390350 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Due to complex wiring harnesses, electric vehicles (EVs) might suffer from performance degradation, increased maintenance costs, and safety risks with faulty cables.
**Approach:** On the other hand, traditional methods of fault detection take much time to think about and cost a lot, and usually require an expert to solve them. In this work, we propose a new system- a real-time fault detection system for EV wiring harnesses-where an Augmented Reality (AR) system is integrated with AR visualization, Unity Digital Twin simulation, ESP32-based embedded programming, and Firebase for real-time synchronization.
