# An Analytical Study of Routing Attacks in Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs)
Priyanka Sirola, Amit Joshi, Kamlesh Chandra Purohit · venue not stated 2014 · https://www.ijcse.net/docs/IJCSE14-03-04-064.pdf · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-hoc Network (VANET) is a rising & most challenging research area to provide Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) services to the end users. The implementation of routing protocols in VANET is an exigent task as of its high mobility & frequent link disruption topology.
**Approach:** At present, to provide efficient communication in vehicular networks several routing protocols have been designed, but the networks are vulnerable to several threats in the presence of malicious nodes. In this paper, we investigate the several security issues on network layer in VANET.
