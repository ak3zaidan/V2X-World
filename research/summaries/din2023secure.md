# Secure and Privacy-Preserving Trust Management System for Trustworthy Communications in Intelligent Transportation Systems
Ikram Ud Din, Kamran Ahmad Awan, Ahmad Almogren · IEEE Access 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2023.3290911 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As Intelligent Cyber-Physical Transportation Systems (ICPTS) become increasingly complex and interconnected, the quest for secure and robust communication between diverse components and entities emerges as a significant challenge. This paper presents an innovative Context-Aware Cognitive Memory Trust Management System (CACMTM) tailored for ICPTS.
**Approach:** The proposed approach integrates a cognitive memory-based trust management method designed explicitly for IoT in the metaverse, leveraging past experiences and adapting to the evolving behaviors of IoT entities for enhanced trust evaluation. The proposed modular CACMTM architecture consists of four main modules: Trust Evaluation, Trust Decision, Trust Update, and Knowledge.
