# Multi-Level Location Privacy Protection Based on Differential Privacy Strategy in VANETs

**Problem:** Location-based service (LBS) has been widely used and brings convenience to people's lives.
**Approach:** In order to obtain the desired services, a user must report its current location information to the LBS provider. If this information falls into the hands of malicious adversaries, users may even face serious threats.
