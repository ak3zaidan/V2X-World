# A Secure and Cooperative Departure Protocol for Connected Automated Platoons
Farah-Emma Braiteh, Davy Tse, Ounas Yhia, Francesca Bassi et al. · 2025 12th IFIP International Conference on New Technologies, Mobility and Security (NTMS) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ntms65597.2025.11076683 · relevance: medium · basis: full text

**Problem:** Leave messages in platoon protocols can be abused to destabilise the platoon.
**Approach:** Secure cooperative departure protocol with data consistency checks and a limit of three departure attempts in 5 s, after which the vehicle is classified as misbehaving.
**Tools and scale:** Plexe simulator.
**Results:** Without countermeasures attacks disrupt formation; with checks the misbehaving leaver is detected and the platoon split or reformed.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None explicit in extracted sections.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — partial: in-simulation misbehaviour detection with local reaction; no PKI reporting or revocation.
**Novelty implications:** Nothing of our candidate directions is ruled out.
