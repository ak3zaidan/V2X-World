# Quality and Trust Indicators of Digital Road Infrastructure Data Are Essential to Improve Its Usability: An Intelligent Speed Assist (ISA) Study
J. van de Sluis, Daniel Altgassen, P. M. Schackmann · International Conference on Vehicle Technology and Intelligent Transport Systems 2025 · https://doi.org/10.5220/0013470100003941 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** : The promise of a Digital Road Infrastructure (DRI) is to improve both road and vehicle safety.
**Approach:** ADAS/ADS with DRI support, can help vehicles in overcoming certain sensor limitations, handle more complex operational situations and offer additional situational awareness.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** : The promise of a Digital Road Infrastructure (DRI) is to improve both road and vehicle safety. Trust and quality indicators are calculated for data coming from camera, map and DRI, which are used to verify and compare theses sources and make improved ISA speed limit decisions.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "ADAS/ADS with DRI support, can help vehicles in overcoming certain sensor limitations, handle more complex operational situations and offer additional situational awareness."
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — addresses misbehaviour/attack detection (direction 1 area), but per the abstract without an integrated SCMS backend, closed-loop revocation, per-message joint ground truth or counterfactual reruns.
**Novelty implications:** Does not rule out any candidate direction on the evidence of the abstract. Leaves open quantifying this in a closed loop with real SCMS revocation and traffic safety outcomes.
