# Providing Distributed Certificate Authority Service in Mobile Ad Hoc Networks
Y. Dong, H.W. Go, A.F. Sui, V.O.K. Li et al. · First International Conference on Security and Privacy for Emerging Areas in Communications Networks (SECURECOMM'05) 2005 · https://doi.org/10.1109/securecomm.2005.57 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, we propose an architecture for providing distributed Certificate Authority (CA) service in Mobile Ad Hoc Networks (MANET), based on threshold cryptography.
**Approach:** We have two major contributions: 1) we make use of the cluster structure to provide CA service, and design a scheme for locating CA server nodes in MANET; 2) we provide a proactive secret share update protocol, which periodically updates CA secret shares with low system overhead.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** Compared with existing approaches, our CA architecture provides faster CA services to user nodes at reduced system overhead.
