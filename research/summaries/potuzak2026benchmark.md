# Benchmark of road network division methods for distributed or parallel road traffic simulation
Tomas Potuzak · The Journal of Supercomputing 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s11227-026-08542-1 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract The division of road network into sub-networks prior to the simulation (i.e., static division) is a common part of distributed or parallel road traffic simulation.
**Approach:** Its quality has significant impact of the speed of the entire simulation, but the mutual comparison of the division methods is difficult, as their descriptions contain inconsistent tests. Hence, in this paper, a benchmark for static road network division methods is presented.
