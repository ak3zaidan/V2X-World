# SixPack: Abusing ABS to avoid Misbehavior detection in VANETs
Francesco Pollicino, Dario Stabili, Giampaolo Bella, Mirco Marchetti · 2021 IEEE 93rd Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2021-Spring) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2021-spring51267.2021.9448656 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Evading state-of-the-art anomaly detectors.
**Approach:** SixPack insider attack faking sudden braking with ABS activation, creating a fake vehicle then rejoining.
**Tools and scale:** F2MD on LuST and LuSTMini.
**Results:** Generates high false-positive rates preventing consistent detection.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — evasion attacks against F2MD-style detectors.
**Novelty implications:** Detector evasion via plausible manoeuvres exists.
