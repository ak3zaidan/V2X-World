# Tracking Based Mix-Zone Location Privacy Evaluation in VANET
Lei Hou, Nianmin Yao, Zhimao Lu, Furui Zhan et al. · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2021.3109065 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Mix-Zone is one of the most effective real-time location privacy preserving techniques over road networks.
**Approach:** By breaking the continuity of location exposure and changing pseudonyms, this strategy can effectively keep users from tracking attacks. Existing Mix-Zone evaluation mechanisms are mainly divided into two categories – Calculation and vehicle tracking.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: pseudonym change/privacy).
**Results:** In this paper, we propose two categories of Mix-Zone tracking methods based on the basic BP and the customized artificial neural networks, where the former is used as verification and based on the result analysis of which we designed the latter, which greatly improves the tracking result, revealing the privacy preserving level of the Mix-Zone more reasonably.
