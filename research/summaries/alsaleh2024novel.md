# A Novel Intrusion Detection Model of Unknown Attacks Using Convolutional Neural Networks
Abdullah Alsaleh · Computer Systems Science and Engineering 2024 · https://doi.org/10.32604/csse.2023.043107 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the increasing number of connected devices in the Internet of Things (IoT) era, the number of intrusions is also increasing.
**Approach:** An intrusion detection system (IDS) is a secondary intelligent system for monitoring, detecting and alerting against malicious activity. IDS is important in developing advanced security models.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Experimental results using the AWID dataset, which is one of the most common open intrusion detection datasets, revealed a higher detection accuracy (94%) compared to SVM and random forest methods.
