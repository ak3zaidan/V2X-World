# Digital Twin in Vehicular Communications: Challenges and Opportunities
Jun-Liang Ye, Yu-Na Jiang, Zi-Wei Chen, Zi-Jing He et al. · Future Internet 2026 · https://doi.org/10.3390/fi18090479 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Digital twins (DTs) are increasingly studied together with vehicular communications, but the literature spans different twinned entities, synchronization assumptions, computing placements, and levels of experimental evidence, which makes results difficult to compare. This survey provides a communication-centric review of DT-enabled vehicular systems.
**Approach:** We first introduce an operational definition for a vehicular DT and distinguish a synchronized DT from a static simulator or digital model. We then organize the field according to twin granularity, physical–virtual synchronization, vehicle–edge–cloud placement, enabling V2X technologies, and measurable evaluation dimensions.
