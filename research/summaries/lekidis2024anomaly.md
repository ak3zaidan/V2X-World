# Anomaly detection mechanisms for in-vehicle and V2X systems
Alexios Lekidis · Proceedings of the 19th International Conference on Availability, Reliability and Security 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3664476.3671012 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Modern V2X systems have an increasing number of interfaces that allow remote connectivity but also include the risk of exposure to cyber threats.
**Approach:** Hence, the attack surface for such threats is constantly increasing, and combined with privacy issues that may arise through the presence of sensitive user data in the V2X ecosystem, this necessitates the requirement for novel security mechanisms. However, the existing mechanisms to ensure protection against such threats face major hurdles, such as 1) the lack of in-vehicle addressing schemes, 2) the abundance of V2X interfaces, and 3) the manufacturer-specific architecture of each vehicle comprised of various heterogeneous systems.
