# MonGNN: A neuroevolutionary-based solution for 5G network slices monitoring
Anouar Rkhami, Yassine Hadjadj-Aoul, Gerardo Rubino, Abdelkader Outtagarts · 2021 IEEE 46th Conference on Local Computer Networks (LCN) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/lcn52139.2021.9524880 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Monitoring the status of network slices is a priority for network operators to ensure that SLAs are not violated.
**Approach:** To overcome the limitations of direct slices’ monitoring, network tomography (NT) is seen as a promising solution. NT-based solutions require constraining monitoring traffic to follow specific paths, which we can achieve by using segment-based routing (SR).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** In this paper, we focus on the complexity of that task and propose MonGNN, a standalone solution based on Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) and genetic algorithms to find a trade-off between the quality of monitors’ placement and the cost to achieve it. Simulation results show the efficiency of our approach compared to existing methods.
