# Security Concept with Distributed Trust-Levels for Autonomous Cooperative Vehicle Networks
T. Madl · 2021 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iv48863.2021.9576024 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The newly proposed cooperative intelligent transportation system (cITS) is a big step towards completely autonomous driving.
**Approach:** It is a key requirement for vehicles to exchange crucial information. Only with exchanged data, such as hazard warnings or route planning each vehicle will have enough information to find its way without a driver.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: PKI
**Results:** No quantitative results stated in the abstract.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, this data has to be authentic and trustworthy, since it will directly influence the behavior of every vehicle inside such a network.
