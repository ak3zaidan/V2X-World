# Idle Nodes Detection and Reactivation-Aware-Buffer Management in Delay-Tolerant Network Using IEMATA and SPKO
Ajay Prasad, Sandeep Gurung, Kalpana Sharma · IEEE Access 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2026.3700836 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In Delay Tolerant Networks (DTNs), buffer management is crucial for optimized message delivery by focusing on message storage and prioritization. However, existing studies do not determine the state of a node, leading to buffer space wastage.
**Approach:** Therefore, this paper presents a buffer management framework based on Idle Node Detection and Reactivation in DTN.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** The proposed model achieves a low buffer allocation time of 2165 ms.
