# Deep-Ensemble-Learning-Based GPS Spoofing Detection for Cellular-Connected UAVs
Yong-Chao Dang, Chafika Benzaïd, Bin Yang, T. Taleb, Yu-Long Shen · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2022.3195320 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) are an emerging technology in the 5G-and-beyond systems with the promise of assisting cellular communications and supporting IoT deployment in remote and density areas. Safe and secure navigation is essential for UAV remote and autonomous deployment.
**Approach:** In this article, we propose a novel deep-ensemble-learning-based, mobile-network-assisted UAV monitoring and tracking system for cellular-connected UAV spoofing detection.
**Tools and scale:** Indeed, the opensource simulator can use commercial software-defined radio tools to generate fake global positioning system (GPS) signals and spoof the UAV GPS receiver to calculate wrong locations, deviating from the planned trajectory.
**Results:** The experimental results show the effectiveness of our method in detecting GPS spoofing, achieving above 97% accuracy rate under two BSs, while it can still achieve at least 83% accuracy under only one BS.
