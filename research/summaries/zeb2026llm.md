# LLM-Orchestrated Digital Twin for Smart Optical Network Operations
Sanwal Zeb, Imran Chowdhury Dipto, M. Masood, J. Pedro et al. · International Conference on Transparent Optical Networks 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icton71309.2026.11704977 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The growing complexity of optical network planning demands automated and accessible solutions beyond expert-driven workflows. Although digital twins enable accurate physical-layer modeling and optimization, their use is limited by technical interfaces.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a large language model (LLM)-orchestrated framework that enables intent-driven interaction with optical network digital twins (DTs) using natural language.
**Tools and scale:** The framework is evaluated on a domainspecific benchmark covering multi-step network operations.
**Results:** It achieves a Domain-Grounded Accuracy of 89.22% with average latency of 15.11 seconds, while maintaining low computational overhead and minimal hallucination. These results demonstrate that compact, locally deployed LLMs, combined with structured tool integration, provide a practical alternative to fine-tuned or cloud-based solutions for smart optical network operations.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — LLM agents for network/security operations; our engine has an LLM agent that configures and analyses runs, but this work is not V2X and not tied to a deterministic simulator with ground truth.
**Novelty implications:** LLM agents for SOC/NetOps/RCA are established; cross-layer V2X root-cause analysis against simulator ground truth is not covered here.
