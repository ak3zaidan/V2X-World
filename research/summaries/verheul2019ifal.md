# IFAL: Issue First Activate Later Certificates for V2X
Eric R. Verheul, Christopher Hicks, Flavio D. Garcia · IEEE European Symposium on Security and Privacy 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/eurosp.2019.00029 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper presents IFAL, a provably secure and privacy conscious scheme for Vehicle-to-Vehicle and Vehicle-to-Infrastructure (V2X) communication. Issue First Activate Later (IFAL) is a practical and secure improvement to the leading European candidate for V2X (ETSI) and one that also merits over the leading US standard.
**Approach:** This paper presents IFAL, a provably secure and privacy conscious scheme for Vehicle-to-Vehicle and Vehicle-to-Infrastructure (V2X) communication.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** We also present a new formalisation of V2X security and privacy which we apply to IFAL to show that it is a provably secure and privacy conscious V2X scheme.
