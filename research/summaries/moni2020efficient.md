# An Efficient RSU Authentication Scheme based on Merkle Hash Tree for VANETs
S. Moni, D. Manivannan · ICC 2020 - 2020 IEEE International Conference on Communications (ICC) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc40277.2020.9149028 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Efficient vehicle authentication of RSUs.
**Approach:** Merkle hash tree based RSU authentication.
**Tools and scale:** Security analysis; comparison with ECDSA+ECDH aggregation.
**Results:** Lower computation and communication overhead than ECDSA+ECDH aggregation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none: RSU certificates in engine follow 1609.2.
**Novelty implications:** No implications.
