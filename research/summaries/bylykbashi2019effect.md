# Effect of security and trustworthiness for a fuzzy cluster management system in VANETs
Kevin Bylykbashi, Donald Elmazi, Keita Matsuo, Makoto Ikeda, Leonard Barolli · Cognitive Systems Research 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogsys.2019.01.008 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Recently, smart cities and Internet of Things (IoT) applications, such as Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs) and Opportunistic networks have been deeply investigated. However, these kinds of wireless networks have security problems.
**Approach:** In this work, we present a Fuzzy Cluster Management System (FCMS) for VANETs. We present and compare two fuzzy-based system models: FCMS1 and FCMS2 for clustering of vehicles in VANETs.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** We evaluate both systems by simulations. The simulation results show that vehicles with the same VRSVC and with high VDC, VS and VT values have higher possibility to remain in the cluster.
