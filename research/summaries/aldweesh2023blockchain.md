# A Blockchain-Based Data Authentication Algorithm for Secure Information Sharing in Internet of Vehicles
Amjad Yousef Aldweesh · World Electric Vehicle Journal 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/wevj14080223 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Secure communication between connected electric vehicles is critical for realizing the full potential of the Internet of Vehicles. However, the authentication and security of the information shared between vehicles remains a major challenge.
**Approach:** In this work, we propose a blockchain-based data authentication algorithm to enable secure information sharing between electric vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed algorithm achieves high authentication success rates with acceptable latency and computation overhead.
