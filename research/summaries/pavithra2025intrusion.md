# Intrusion Detection Using SA-BiLSTM and Enhanced Deep RL Routing with Modular Homomorphic Encryption for Secure Data Transmission in VANET
T. Pavithra, B. Nagabhushana · Optical Memory and Neural Networks 2025 · https://doi.org/10.3103/s1060992x24601052 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract Vehicular Ad Hoc Network (VANET) has become a revolutionary and creative technology that serves as an essential part of Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS).
**Approach:** However, due to their wireless nature and complex operating environment, VANETs are vulnerable to a range of malicious user assaults. It is critical to identify intrusions in the VANET system in order to provide reliable and secure communication among all of the system’s vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** In this proposed model, consider if any incident happens on the road, vehicles in that sector are grouped by utilizing the Improved K harmonic means clustering algorithm (IKHM), and the CH is determined according to its minimal distance and highest energy using the Greater Cane Rat Algorithm (GCRA) optimization. The proposed model’s performance parameters are examined, and the results show that, for 500 vehicle nodes, the outcomes are 82.2% PDR, 13.65J energy usage, 20.3% routing overhead, 18.7 mbps throughput, and 11.22 delay.
