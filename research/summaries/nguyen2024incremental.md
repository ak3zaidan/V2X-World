# Incremental Learning for Enhancing Misbehavior Detection in Multi-Access Vehicular Networks
Lan-Huong Nguyen, Van-Linh Nguyen, Ren-Hung Hwang · 2024 IEEE 100th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2024-Fall) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2024-fall63153.2024.10757773 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Pre-trained MBD models do not adapt.
**Approach:** Incremental learning for MBD engines in vehicles or RSUs.
**Tools and scale:** VTC 2024-Fall; simulation.
**Results:** Up to 12% higher accuracy and 45% faster continuous processing than state of the art.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — online MBD.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
