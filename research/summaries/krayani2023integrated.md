# Integrated Sensing and Communication for Joint GPS Spoofing and Jamming Detection in Vehicular V2X Networks
Ali Krayani, Gabriele Barabino, Lucio Marcenaro, Carlo Regazzoni · IEEE Wireless Communications and Networking Conference 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wcnc55385.2023.10118852 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communication is expected to be a prominent component of the sixth generation (6G) to accomplish intelligent transportation systems (ITS). Autonomous vehicles relying only on onboard sensors cannot bypass the limitations of safety and reliability.
**Approach:** Thus, integrated sensing and communication is proposed as an effective way to achieve high situational- and self-awareness levels, enabling V2X to perceive the physical world and adjust its behaviour to emergencies. This paper proposes a method to jointly detect GPS spoofing and jamming attacks in a V2X network.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset, C-V2X/LTE-V2X/5G (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** Thus, integrated sensing and communication is proposed as an effective way to achieve high situational- and self-awareness levels, enabling V2X to perceive the physical world and adjust its behaviour to emergencies.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Autonomous vehicles relying only on onboard sensors cannot bypass the limitations of safety and reliability.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Addresses V2X attacks or misbehaviour/trust evaluation that our engine can execute and detect in closed loop; it does not model the SCMS, revocation or joint traffic/radio/PKI ground truth.
**Novelty implications:** Does not rule out our candidate directions; any claim about this specific attack/detection/trust mechanism must cite it. Left open: end-to-end detection-report-revocation evaluation with safety outcomes.
