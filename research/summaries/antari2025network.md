# Network Traffic Classification Using Machine Learning, Transformer, and Large Language Models
Ahmad Antari, Yazan Abo-Aisheh, Jehad Shamasneh, Huthaifa I. Ashqar · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02141 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Traffic classification with ML, Transformers and LLMs.
**Approach:** Comparison incl. GPT-4o and Gemini.
**Tools and scale:** 30,959 observations, 19 features from Arbor devices.
**Results:** Transformer 98.95%, XGBoost 97.56%; LLM few-shot promising.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Misclassification in IPSec and Backup.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
