# V2X-Radar: A Multi-modal Dataset with 4D Radar for Cooperative Perception
Lei Yang, Xinyu Zhang, Jun Li, Chen Wang, Jiaqi Ma, Zhiying Song et al. · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10962 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Modern autonomous vehicle perception systems often struggle with occlusions and limited perception range.
**Approach:** Previous studies have demonstrated the effectiveness of cooperative perception in extending the perception range and overcoming occlusions, thereby enhancing the safety of autonomous driving. In recent years, a series of cooperative perception datasets have emerged; however, these datasets primarily focus on cameras and LiDAR, neglecting 4D Radar, a sensor used in single-vehicle autonomous driving to provide robust perception in adverse weather conditions.
