# Intelligent Connected Vehicle Intrusion Detection and Mitigation: An Analysis of Explainable AI
Ravula Vishnukumar, Adla Padma, Mangayarkarasi Ramaiah · 2024 International Conference on Emerging Techniques in Computational Intelligence (ICETCI) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icetci62771.2024.10704143 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** IoT and AI created a Transportation Management System, resulting in the Internet of Vehicles.
**Approach:** Intelligent vehicles are combined with contemporary communication technologies (5G) to achieve automated driving and adequate mobility. IoV faces security issues in the next five (5) areas: data safety, V2X communication safety, platform safety, Intermediate Commercial Vehicles (ICV) safety, and intelligent device safety.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G
**Results:** Numerous types of AI models have been created to reduce the outcome infiltration risks on ICVs.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
