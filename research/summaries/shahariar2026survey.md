# A Survey of Security Threats and Trust Management in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Rezvi Shahariar, Chris Phillips · Transactions on Engineering and Computing Sciences 2026 · https://doi.org/10.14738/tmlai.1303.18943 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper presents a survey of state-of-the-art trust models for Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs).
**Approach:** Trust management plays an essential role in isolating malicious insider attacks in VANETs which traditional security approaches fail to thwart. To this end, many trust models are presented; some of them only address trust management, while others address security and privacy aspects besides trust management.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** This paper first reviews, classifies, and summarizes state-of-the-art trust models, and then compares their achievements.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- We then conclude the survey by suggesting future work for sender-side evaluation of trust in VANETs.
- Additionally, the challenges (real-time constraints and efficiency) are emphasized whilst considering the deployment of a trust model in VANETs.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — same area (V2X/vehicular security or networking) with a narrower, mechanism-level focus.
**Novelty implications:** Does not remove any of our claims beyond its specific mechanism; we cannot claim to be first on that mechanism. The joint closed-loop traffic/radio/PKI evaluation with counterfactual ground truth remains open as far as this abstract shows.
