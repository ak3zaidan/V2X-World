# SD2PA: a fully safe driving and privacy-preserving authentication scheme for VANETs
S. Alfadhli, Song-Feng Lu, Abdulaziz Fatani, Haider Al-Fedhly et al. · Hum. centric Comput. Inf. Sci. 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1186/s13673-020-00241-x · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Privacy and security are the main concerns for meeting the safety aims of the VANET system.
**Approach:** In this paper, we analyse recent VANET schemes that utilise a group authentication technique and found important vulnerabilities in terms of driving safety. To defeat these problems, we propose a lightweight scheme, SD2PA, based on a general hash function for VANET.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** These systems also suffer from vulnerabilities in terms of management efficiency and computational complexity. Moreover, the vehicle authentication is only done once by the VANET system administrator during the vehicle’s moving, so the authentication redundancy for the entire system is reduced and system management efficiency is enhanced.

- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
