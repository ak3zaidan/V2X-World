# Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) Message Content Plausibility Check for Platoons through Low-Power Beaconing
Hyogon Kim, Taeho Kim · Sensors 2019 · https://doi.org/10.3390/s19245493 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** WAVE and C-V2X standards lack message content plausibility checks; remote attackers are dangerous.
**Approach:** Discusses sensor fusion, behaviour and communication-constraint checks; proposes low-power "whisper" beacons of rotating IDs to verify physical proximity.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation for autonomous platooning.
**Results:** Low-power beacons filter remote false-position attacks.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Tx power too low may break function
- mobility affects attack distance
- future: explore efficacy of single-hop whispering further.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — communication-constraint plausibility.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
