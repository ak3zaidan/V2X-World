# A Survey on GenAI-Driven Digital Twins: Toward Intelligent 6G Networks and Metaverse Systems
Faisal Naeem, Mansoor Ali, Georges Kaddoum, Yasir Faheem et al. · IEEE Open Journal of the Communications Society 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ojcoms.2025.3641307 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sixth-Generation (6G) networks aim to deliver unprecedented network performance by facilitating intelligent, ultra-low-latency, and massively connected applications that seamlessly integrate the physical and digital domains through context-aware operation. These applications work across physical and digital environments.
**Approach:** This paper examines how a range of generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) models can be used alongside DTs to strengthen resource allocation and improve security in 6G networks. This paper concludes by drawing attention to emerging conceptual frameworks for DT–GenAI integration.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 6G, digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** Within this broader shift, digital twins (DTs) have demonstrated notable improvements in overall network performance by creating high-fidelity digital counterparts of physical 6G systems. Their effectiveness depends a great deal on access to reliable and sufficiently rich data, and the inherent complexity of 6G environments often makes accurate modeling and efficient resource coordination challenging. It also sets out a GenAI-enabled DT framework for various 6G-enabling applications, highlighting the potential roles of different GenAI models in supporting semantic communications, the metaverse, integrated sensing and communication (ISAC), AI-generated content (AIGC), and reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RIS).
