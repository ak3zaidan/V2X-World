# Decentralized Routing and Radio Resource Allocation in Wireless Ad Hoc Networks via Graph Reinforcement Learning
Xiaochen Zhang, Haitao Zhao, Jun Xiong, Xiaoran Liu, Hang Yin, Xuanhan Zhou, Jibo Wei · IEEE Transactions on Cognitive Communications and Networking 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tccn.2024.3360517 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In wireless Ad Hoc networks, optimal routing is closely coupled with radio resource allocation.
**Approach:** This paper adopts a cross-layer approach and improves the performance of multi-hop transmission by joint routing and power control. A hop-by-hop optimization framework based on deep reinforcement learning (DRL) is proposed for efficient and decentralized decisions on relay nodes and transmit power.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Numerical results show that our proposed method outweighs both heuristic benchmarks and state-of-the-art DRL counterparts in terms of both throughput and path connection probability.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Particularly, information exchange among nodes during optimization strictly adheres to practical limitations on their sensing ability, fitting the distributed configuration of Ad Hoc networks.
