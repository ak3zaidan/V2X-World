# A comparative analysis of random forest and isolation forest intrusion detection systems
Sara Amaouche, Azidine Guezzaz, Said Benkirane, Mourade Azrour · CRC Press eBooks 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1201/9781003587552-20 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This study proposes a new security approach for vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) to address their vulnerability to security threats.
**Approach:** VANETs are essential to modern transportation, as they facilitate communication between vehicles and infrastructure for a variety of applications. However, their dynamic and decentralized nature exposes them to risks such as malicious attacks and unauthorized access.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** To reduce these threats, we present a method combining machine learning models and advanced pre-processing techniques. The results demonstrate the robustness of both approaches, with RF excelling in identifying known attack patterns and IF effectively detecting anomalies.
