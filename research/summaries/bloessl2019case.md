# A Case for Good Defaults: Pitfalls in VANET Physical Layer Simulations
Bastian Bloessl, Aisling OrDriscoll · 2019 Wireless Days (WD) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wd.2019.8734227 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Researchers misparameterise PHY models in VANET simulators.
**Approach:** Identifies suboptimal default PHY parameter values in common frameworks, shows impact, and reviews papers using them.
**Tools and scale:** Common VANET simulators (Veins etc.).
**Results:** Majority of reviewed papers copy defaults or omit PHY parameters.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Argues for reasonable defaults as much as functional correctness.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — default-parameter pitfalls; directly relevant to our 300 configurable settings and sensitivity work.
**Novelty implications:** We cannot claim discovering default-parameter sensitivity; we can build on it with systematic sensitivity across PKI parameters.
