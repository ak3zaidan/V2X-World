# A Trust Management Framework for Connected Autonomous Vehicles Using Interaction Provenance
Mohammad Aminul Hoque, Ragib Hasan · ICC 2022 - IEEE International Conference on Communications 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc45855.2022.9838476 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Connected autonomous vehicles (CAVs) have fostered the development of intelligent transportation systems that support critical safety information sharing with minimum latency and making driving decisions autonomously.
**Approach:** However, the CAV environment is vulnerable to different external and internal attacks. Authorized but malicious entities which provide wrong information impose challenges in preventing internal attacks.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Simulation results show that the framework is effective and can be integrated with the CAV stack with minimal computation and communication overhead.

- However, the CAV environment is vulnerable to different external and internal attacks.
- Authorized but malicious entities which provide wrong information impose challenges in preventing internal attacks.
