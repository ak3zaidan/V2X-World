# A Survey of the Social Internet of Vehicles: Secure Data Issues, Solutions, and Federated Learning
Ling Xing, Pengcheng Zhao, Jian-Ping Gao, Honghai Wu et al. · IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Magazine 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mits.2022.3190036 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The Social Internet of Vehicles (SIoV) is constructed so that the vehicle, as an entity, generates social awareness, which leads to the social behavior of the vehicle. The SIoV has multidimensional, heterogeneous, massive, real-time dynamic data, so its data security is a major concern.
**Approach:** The structure of the SIoV and the characteristics of security primitives are studied in this review, which defines the social community by the vehicle.
