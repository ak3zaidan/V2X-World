# What-if Analysis Framework for Digital Twins in 6G Wireless Network Management
Elif Ak, Berk Canberk, Vishal Sharma, Octavia A. Dobre, Trung Q. Duong · 2024 International Wireless Communications and Mobile Computing (IWCMC) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iwcmc61514.2024.10592526 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** 6G network management needs what-if analysis within a DTN.
**Approach:** DTN with ns-3 physical-twin layer, ML/RL service layer for carrier-sense threshold and power control, and CTGAN synthetic what-if scenarios.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3; CTGAN.
**Results:** Framework efficient; scenario-maker step and twinning interval matter (no numbers).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - ns-3 based what-if analysis; generative not exact counterfactual.
**Novelty implications:** What-if DTN with simulators exists; exact same-seed counterfactuals are not used.
