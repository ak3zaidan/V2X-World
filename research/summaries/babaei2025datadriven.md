# Towards a Data‐Driven Digital Twin AI‐Based Architecture for Self‐Driving Vehicles
Parinaz Babaei, Nosrat Riahinia, Omid Mahdi Ebadati E, Ali Azimi · IET Intelligent Transport Systems 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1049/itr2.70017 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Mapping AI roles across autonomous vehicle platform layers.
**Approach:** Bibliometric review of 628 Scopus papers (2014-2024), top 185 assessed; proposes data-driven DT AI architecture.
**Tools and scale:** Text mining of literature.
**Results:** CNNs dominate perception/control/localisation; deep RL dominates planning/decision-making.
