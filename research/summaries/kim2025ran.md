# RAN Twin-aided Handover for 6G Networks
Yekaterina Kim, I. Orikumhi, Nakyung Lee, Sunwoo Kim · 2025 IEEE 102nd Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2025-Fall) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2025-fall65116.2025.11310160 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Handover interruption and failures in dense mmWave 6G.
**Approach:** RAN Twin combining multimodal sensing and history to produce candidate cells and early sync, compatible with 3GPP Rel-18.
**Tools and scale:** System simulation.
**Results:** Better latency and HO rate than baseline 3GPP HO.
