# Structural Generalization for Microservice Routing Using Graph Neural Networks
Chenrui Hu, Ziyu Cheng, Di Wu, Yuxiao Wang, Feng Liu, Zhimin Qiu · 2025 3rd International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Automation Control (AIAC) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/aiac68175.2025.11332534 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper focuses on intelligent routing in microservice systems and proposes an end-to-end optimization framework based on graph neural networks.
**Approach:** The goal is to improve routing decision efficiency and overall system performance under complex topologies. The method models invocation relationships among microservices as a graph.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** To improve the model’s ability to assess path quality, an edge-aware attention mechanism is introduced. Experimental results show that the proposed method outperforms existing mainstream strategies across multiple key metrics.
