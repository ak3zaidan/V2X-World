# Blockchain-Based Lightweight Certificate Authority for Efficient Privacy-Preserving Location-Based Service in Vehicular Social Networks
Huajie Shen, Jun Zhou, Zhenfu Cao, Xiaolei Dong, Kim-Kwang Raymond Choo · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2020.2974874 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Blockchain LBS in vehicular social networks lacks authentication or needs an online CA.
**Approach:** Lightweight threshold CA (threshold proxy signature by a coalition of CAs in a consortium blockchain) and privacy-preserving LBS protocol PPVC.
**Tools and scale:** Security analysis and performance evaluation.
**Results:** Effective and efficient (no numbers).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - threshold CA for vehicles, related to our threshold authorities (FROST/CGGMP21/Umbrella) but non-SCMS.
**Novelty implications:** Threshold CAs for vehicular settings exist; threshold SCMS authorities with deployment-faithful protocols remain to be checked.
