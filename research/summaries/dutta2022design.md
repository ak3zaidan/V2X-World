# Design and Analysis of Secure Distributed Estimator for Vehicular Platooning in Adversarial Environment
Raj Gautam Dutta, Yaodan Hu, Feng Yu, Teng Zhang et al. · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2020.3036376 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Platooning of connected vehicles is a solution geared toward improving traffic throughput, highway safety, driving comfort, and fuel efficiency.
**Approach:** These vehicles are equipped with Cooperative Adaptive Cruise Controller (CACC) that integrates information from dedicated short-range communication (DSRC) radio and sensors for safe navigation. The possibility of malicious attacks such as Denial of Service (DoS) or False Data Injection (FDI) on sensor data or control inputs tends to affect reliability, and jeopardize the safety of connected vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: platoon/CACC attacks).
**Results:** While under attack, our RDSE outperforms the DKF and we provide a theoretical bound on state estimation error generated by RDSE during an attack.
