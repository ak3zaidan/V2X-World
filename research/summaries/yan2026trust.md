**Problem:** To enhance the security of Internet of Vehicles (IoV) communications, this paper proposes a fuzzy logic-based trust evaluation model for IoV.
**Approach:** This model evaluates the reliability of information propagated by nodes by monitoring the interaction behaviors of multiple vehicles.

**Results:** This model aims to identify false event messages and improve data accuracy, thereby strengthening the information security of the Internet of Vehicles.
