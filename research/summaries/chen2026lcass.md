# LcASS: Efficient Lattice-Based Certificateless Aggregation Signature Scheme With Blockchain for VANETs
Yu-Lei Chen, Dong-Wei Guo, Jianhua Chen · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2026.3717576 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Quantum threats, OBU limits and privacy/traceability balance in VANETs.
**Approach:** LcASS: RLWE certificateless aggregate signatures with dual-blockchain pseudonym management and mandatory revocation. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** QROM proof; performance comparison.
**Results:** Up to 82.5% faster signing, 77.2% faster verification, 36.3% lower overhead; batch of 100 in 134.02 ms.
