# Deep Multimodal Learning for Real-Time DDoS Attacks Detection in Internet of Vehicles
Mohamed Ababsa, Soheyb Ribouh, Abdelhamid Malki, Lyes Khoukhi · International Conference on Communications 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc52391.2025.11160909 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Real-time DDoS detection.
**Approach:** Multimodal LSTM/GRU with attention plus MLP fusion; F2MD-generated synthetic data.
**Tools and scale:** F2MD with 10%, 30%, 50% attacker densities.
**Results:** Average accuracy 96.63%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- VeReMi extension limitations motivated new data.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
