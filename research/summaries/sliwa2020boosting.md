# Boosting Vehicle-to-Cloud Communication by Machine Learning-Enabled Context Prediction
Benjamin Sliwa, Robert Falkenberg, Thomas Liebig, Nico Piatkowski, Christian Wietfeld · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2019.2930109 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Machine-type vehicle-to-cloud traffic stresses limited cellular resources.
**Approach:** Client-side opportunistic transmission scheme using measured and ML-predicted channel state for delay-tolerant uploads.
**Tools and scale:** Field evaluation in public LTE networks.
**Results:** Mean data rate up 194%, average power consumption down up to 54%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the extracted sections.
**Overlap with our engine:** none - cellular uplink optimisation (our engine carries pseudonym top-ups over cellular but does not optimise timing).
