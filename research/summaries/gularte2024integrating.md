# Integrating Cybersecurity in V2X: A Review of Simulation Environments
Kevin Herman Muraro Gularte, João Paulo Javidi da Costa, José Alfredo Ruiz Vargas, Antonio Santos da Silva, Giovanni Almeida Santos, Yuming Wang et al. · IEEE Access 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2024.3504404 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Survey gaps on V2X simulators for cybersecurity.
**Approach:** Review of SUMO, NS-3, OMNeT++, Veins, CARLA, CarSim; market and publication trends; security simulation parameters for jamming, spoofing, DoS, eavesdropping.
**Tools and scale:** Survey.
**Results:** Advocates co-simulation integration and AI-driven approaches.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Identifies integration of co-simulation and AI-driven approaches as research opportunities.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — maps the simulator landscape our engine competes in.
**Novelty implications:** Confirms no surveyed tool integrates full SCMS; useful for positioning.
