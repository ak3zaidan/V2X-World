# DoTime: A Synthetic Benchmark Generator for Interventional and Counterfactual Time Series
Dennis Thumm, Billy Tim Anthony, Ying Chen · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27263 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Few interventional/counterfactual time-series benchmarks.
**Approach:** DoTime generator of temporal SCMs with interventions and counterfactual sampling.
**Tools and scale:** PyPI package; four frozen suites.
**Results:** Suitable as prior for a causal foundation model.

- Not stated in the abstract.
