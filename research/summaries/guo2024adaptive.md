**Problem:** The Internet of Vehicles (IoV) is a large-scale interactive network that operates in a dynamic and changeable environment, encompassing diverse types of private information.
**Approach:** In recent years, safeguarding vehicle location privacy in IoV has been a topic of concern. However, the independent location privacy protection mechanisms cannot consistently meet the rigorous security requirements of various IoV scenarios, which will pose a significant threat to the location privacy of IoV users.
