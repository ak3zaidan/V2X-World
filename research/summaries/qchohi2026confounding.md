# Confounding-Valid Conformal Inference for Counterfactual KPIs in Wireless Networks
Abdessamed Qchohi, Jessica Moysen Cortes, Matteo Zecchin · n/a 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05073 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Logged telemetry may omit controller variables, causing hidden confounding in counterfactual KPI inference.
**Approach:** CV-CCI: combines confounded observational telemetry with scarce randomised data via GESPI to keep finite-sample coverage.
**Tools and scale:** Two RAN control tasks: MAC scheduling and handover; code public.
**Results:** Maintains prescribed coverage across confounding levels with informative sets (numbers not extracted).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Randomised telemetry is scarce because it disrupts operation.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - counterfactual KPI inference in wireless (direction 3); our engine can generate both randomised and confounded data plus exact counterfactual truth.
**Novelty implications:** Conformal counterfactual inference in RAN is established; a V2X benchmark with exact counterfactual ground truth to test such methods is not.
