# A Survey on Blockchain Intelligence-Based Trust Management for Internet of Vehicles
Zhi-Peng Wang, R. M. Noor, L. M. Kiah et al. · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2026.3715918 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Internet of Vehicles (IoV) integrates conventional automotive systems with advanced communication, sensing, and computing technologies to enhance traffic safety and transportation efficiency through vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communication.
**Approach:** In this article, we investigate the applications of BI in TM for the IoV. Subsequently, a future-oriented TMS architecture is proposed, along with its internal components’ collaborative workflow and deployment analysis.
