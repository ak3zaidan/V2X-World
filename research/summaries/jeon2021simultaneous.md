# Simultaneous Cyber-Attack Detection and Radar Sensor Health Monitoring in Connected ACC Vehicles
Woongsun Jeon, Zhenming Xie, Ali Zemouche, Rajesh Rajamani · IEEE Sensors Journal 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jsen.2020.3011698 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper focuses on the detection of cyber-attacks on a wireless communication channel and simultaneous radar sensor health monitoring for a connected vehicle. A semi-autonomous adaptive cruise control (SA-ACC) vehicle is considered which has wireless communication with its immediately preceding vehicle in the same lane.
**Approach:** The wireless connectivity enables the vehicle to operate at small time-gap distances without creating string instability. However, the reliability of the wireless connectivity is critical for ensuring safe vehicle operation.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The performance of the observer is first extensively evaluated in simulations.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- The presence of two unknown inputs related to both sensor failure and cyber-attack seemingly poses a difficult estimation challenge which is addressed in this paper.
- The fundamental estimation algorithm developed herein can be extended in the future to enable cyber-attack detection in more complex connected vehicle architectures.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Attacks on cooperative driving / platoon control with traffic consequences; overlaps our closed-loop attack-to-safety evaluation, without a PKI/revocation loop.
**Novelty implications:** Does not rule out any candidate direction; it does not model SCMS certificates, linkage-based revocation, per-message joint ground truth or counterfactual reruns.
