# A Novel Approach to Sybil Attack Detection in VANETs Using Verifiable Delay Functions and Hierarchical Fog-Cloud Architecture
Habiba Hadri, Mourad Ouadou, Khalid Minaoui · Journal of Cybersecurity and Privacy 2026 · https://doi.org/10.3390/jcp6020059 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Sybil detection with privacy.
**Approach:** Verifiable delay functions plus fog-cloud hierarchy, suspicion scores, ML hybrid.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation; linkability analysis.
**Results:** Detection above 97.8%, FPR below 2.3%; pseudonym linkability reduced from about 12-15% to below 4%; RSU overhead 75 kbps per 100 vehicles.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the full text.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — quantifies pseudonym linkability (cross-layer linkability runner-up).
**Novelty implications:** Linkability metrics under pseudonym rotation are measured; radio-layer identifiers are not.
