# A Self-Authentication and Deniable Efficient Group Key Agreement Protocol for VANET
Mu Han, Lei Hua, Shi-Dian Ma · KSII Transactions on Internet and Information Systems 2016 · https://doi.org/10.3837/tiis.2017.07.021 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the rapid development of vehicular ad hoc Network (VANET), it is gaining significant popularity and receiving increasing attentions from academics and industry in security and efficiency. To address security and efficiency issues, a self-authentication and deniable efficient group key agreement protocol is proposed in this paper.
**Approach:** This scheme establishes a group between road-side unit (RSU) and vehicles by using self-authentication without certification authority, and enhances certification efficiency by using group key (GK) transmission method.
