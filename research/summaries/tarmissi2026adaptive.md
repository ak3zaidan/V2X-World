# Adaptive Modeling of Positional Falsification Malicious Behavior Using Type‐2 Fuzzy‐Based Aggregated Trust Analysis
Khaled Tarmissi, Amal Alshardan, Nuha Alruwais et al. · Transactions on Emerging Telecommunications Technologies 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1002/ett.70429 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ABSTRACT Internet of Vehicles (IoV) involves a number of connected vehicles that can continuously share Basic Safety Messages (BSM).
**Approach:** To address these limitations, this paper proposes TrustFRF (Trust‐aware Fuzzy Random Forest), an adaptive vehicular trust‐based detection model that continuously assesses vehicle trust based on its position consistency, motion patterns, and past‐time interactions, without relying on predefined attack patterns. The proposed model is evaluated using the publicly available “VANET malicious node dataset.” Experimental results suggest that TrustFRF achieves a notable detection accuracy of 99.40% with a low inference latency of 0.53 s for real‐time edge implementation.
