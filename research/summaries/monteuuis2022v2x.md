# V2X Misbehavior in Maneuver Sharing and Coordination Service: Considerations for Standardization
Jean-Philippe Monteuuis, Jonathan Petit, Mohammad Raashid Ansari, Cong Chen, Seung Yang · IEEE Conference on Standards for Communications and Networking 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cscn57023.2022.10051093 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** Standards lack misbehaviour specifications for Maneuver Sharing and Coordination Service (MSCS).
**Approach:** Threat assessment of MSC messages with a reproducibility/impact/stealthiness matrix; inputs for standards.
**Tools and scale:** Qualitative analysis; Qualcomm authors.
**Results:** Identifies attacks such as conflicting maneuver negotiations leading to collisions; no numeric results.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Notes attack trees become too large for this many attacks.
- proposes standards inputs, not detectors.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Misbehaviour risk rating; our engine could quantify impact ratings.
**Novelty implications:** Qualitative ratings exist; quantified simulation-based ratings for MSCS remain open.
