# HRCred: Revocation-Consistent Hardware-Rooted Credentials for Cross-Domain Industrial Cyber–Physical Systems
Hao-Zhe Zhou, Hang Lei, Mao-Lin Yang · Electronics 2026 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics15174004 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Mobile industrial devices increasingly cross administrative domains. A maintenance terminal certified in one factory can be dispatched to another, while autonomous vehicles cross fog domains while reaching cloud digital twins.
**Approach:** Here, we present HRCred, which converts hardware identities rooted in physical unclonable functions (PUFs) into domain-bound, epoch-bound, and threshold-issued short-lived pseudonymous credentials.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** On constrained hardware, the device side costs 3.6 mJ (18.8 ms authentication and 5.4 ms verification, on par with the lightest single-gateway token) while resisting up to t−1 compromised issuers, cutting cross-domain linkage AUC to 0.55, and keeping all 12,000 measured post-revocation acceptances below the analytical bound.
