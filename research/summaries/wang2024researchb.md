# Research on Digital Twin Vehicle Stability Monitoring System Based on Side Slip Angle
Jianlong Wang, Chuanwei Zhang, Zhi Yang, Meng Dang et al. · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2023.3296268 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Focusing on the low efficiency of the current active safety control method with intelligent networked vehicles, which cannot warn potential dangers in advance, a digital twin vehicle stability monitoring system based on side slip angle is proposed.
**Approach:** By analyzing the practical significance of digital twin technology in automobile field, the framework of vehicle stability monitoring system is proposed, which includes vehicle physical system, virtual vehicle model system, vehicle twin data platform and comprehensive monitoring system.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** The PSOLSTM (Particle Swarm Optimization Long and Short-Term Memory) algorithm relied on the improved LSTM (Long and Short-Term Memory) is designed in the cause of construct automobile side slip angle state estimator model, which has better accuracy and followability. The experiments show that the maximum estimation error of automobile side slip angle is only 0.634deg under four different working conditions.
