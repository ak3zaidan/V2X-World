# Design and Application of Intelligent Transportation Multi-Source Data Collaboration Framework Based on Digital Twins
Xihou Zhang, Dingding Han, Xiaobo Zhang, Leheng Fang · Applied Sciences 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/app13031923 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The increasing urban traffic problems have made the transportation system require a large amount of data.
**Approach:** Aiming at the current problems of data types redundancy and low coordination rate of intelligent transportation systems (ITS), this paper proposes an improved digital twin architecture applicable to ITS. For various collaboration methods, this paper specifically describes the collaboration methods and scopes, and designs the framework and interfaces for data mapping.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Based on the improved digital twin architecture, a framework for dynamic and static data collaboration in ITS is constructed.
