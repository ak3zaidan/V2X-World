# Beacon-based trust management for location privacy enhancement VANETs
Yu-Chih Wei, Yi-Ming Chen, Hwai-Ling Shan · Asia-Pacific Network Operations and Management Symposium 2011 · https://doi.org/10.1109/apnoms.2011.6077002 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Trust management ignores location privacy.
**Approach:** Secure broadcast authentication plus beacon-based trust management fusing event and vehicle trust with Dempster-Shafer.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation under alteration and DoS attacks with fixed and random silent period pseudonym schemes.
**Results:** Highly resilient under both FSP and RSP schemes.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — trust management interacting with pseudonym change schemes.
**Novelty implications:** Interaction of MBD with pseudonym change studied early.
