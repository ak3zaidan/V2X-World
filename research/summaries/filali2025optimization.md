# Optimization of big data transfers among data center networks using deep reinforcement learning with graph neural networks
Imen Filali, Ridha Ejbali, Sarah A. Alzakari, Amel Ali Alhussan · PeerJ Computer Science 2025 · https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj-cs.3212 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The big data era is an emerging paradigm that has gained a lot of interest in the last few years from industry, academia, and governments around the world.
**Approach:** Cloud computing infrastructure often operates over multiple distributed data centers around the globe, following a pay-as-you-go pricing model. Enabling fast data transfer across these data centers, with low monetary cost and without link congestion, is not a trivial task.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Results show that our method consistently optimizes big data centers among data centers.
