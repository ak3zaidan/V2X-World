# A Deep Learning Approach for Distributed QoS Prediction in Beyond 5G Networks
Lina Magoula, Nikolaos Koursioumpas, Sokratis Barmpounakis, Panagiotis Kontopoulos et al. · 2022 IEEE 33rd Annual International Symposium on Personal, Indoor and Mobile Radio Communications (PIMRC) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/pimrc54779.2022.9977782 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Beyond 5G networks bring a new era in system automation, by introducing new and demanding, in terms of Quality of Service (QoS), use cases and applications. Predicting the QoS for end users in a timely manner and enabling service adaptation methods to react in advance in case of QoS degradation is of high importance, especially for safety-critical applications such as in vehicular communications.
**Approach:** This study proposes a distributed QoS prediction scheme based on the well-known Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) architecture to account for the natural high correlation of samples closely located in time.
