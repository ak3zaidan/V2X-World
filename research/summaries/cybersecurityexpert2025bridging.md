# Bridging Identity Assurance Gaps: Integrating FIDO2 and Certificate-Based Authentication for Phishing-Resistant, Scalable Enterprise Security
Cybersecurity Expert, Florida, USA, Badal Bhushan · International journal of data science and machine learning. 2025 · https://doi.org/10.55640/ijdsml-05-02-02 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Identity protection is an essential component of enterprise security in the current era.
**Approach:** This article provides an in-depth examination of two modern and popular authentication protocols, namely FIDO2/WebAuthn and Certificate-Based Authentication (CBA).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** FIDO2 facilitates passwordless authentication with the assistance of cryptographic credentials securely bound to a person's device, offering improved usability and phishing resistance. A structured framework is provided to enable organizations to achieve identity assurance at scale and support evolving technology and regulatory demands.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- CBA, rooted in public key infrastructure (PKI), remains a necessary requirement in compliance-focused environments and is crucial for safeguarding human and machine identities.
