# Improving Security in the Internet of Vehicles: A Blockchain-Based Data Sharing Scheme
Lian-Hai Wang, Chenxi Guan · Electronics 2024 · https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics13040714 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** To ensure the aggregation of a high-quality global model during the data-sharing process in the Internet of Vehicles (IoV), current approaches primarily utilize gradient detection to mitigate malicious or low-quality parameter updates. However, deploying gradient detection in plain text neglects adequate privacy protection for vehicular data.
**Approach:** This paper proposes the IoV-BDSS, a novel data-sharing scheme that integrates blockchain and hybrid privacy technologies to protect private data in gradient detection.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** It also exhibits resilience against 30% poisoning attacks, with a test error rate remaining below 0.16. Furthermore, our scheme incurs a lower computational overhead and faster inference speed, markedly reducing experimental costs by approximately 26% compared to similar methods, rendering it suitable for highly dynamic IoV systems with unstable communication.
