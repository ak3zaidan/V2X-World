# Detection of Rational Byzantine Roadside Units Mounting Black Hole Attack in Q-Learning Based Routing Algorithm in VANET
Piyush Rai, Bodhisatwa Mazumdar, Subhra Mazumdar · venue not stated 2026 · https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7624532 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Black hole attacks during the Q-learning training phase of VANET routing represent a largely unexplored yet critical vulnerability.
**Approach:** In this paper, we investigate the threat by modeling compromised Roadside Units (RSUs) that strategically maximize attack gain.
**Tools and scale:** We prove the protocol’s safety, liveness, and soundness under a partially synchronous model and report simulations on grids up to 100 x 100 that are consistent with the analysis.
