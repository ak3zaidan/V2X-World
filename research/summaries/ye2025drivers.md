# How do the drivers react to different C-V2X-based communication conditions in dilemma zones? A driving simulator study
Shi Ye, Tiantian Chen, Oscar Oviedo-Trespalacios, Yasir Ali, Taeho Oh, Inhi Kim · Accident Analysis &amp; Prevention 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aap.2025.108208 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** How drivers react to C-V2X communication conditions in dilemma zones.
**Approach:** Unity + VISSIM co-simulation driving simulator; four conditions; 62 drivers.
**Tools and scale:** Driving simulator.
**Results:** Communication loss makes drivers more likely to proceed; perfect and interrupted communication reduce it; heterogeneity by driving history.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — human reaction to communication loss, a safety outcome mechanism.
**Novelty implications:** Human-driver response data could parameterise our driver models.
