# The Best of Both Worlds: Hybrid Data-Driven and Model-Based Vehicular Network Simulation
Benjamin Sliwa, Manuel Patchou, Christian Wietfeld · GLOBECOM 2020 - 2020 IEEE Global Communications Conference 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/globecom42002.2020.9348150 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Real measurements are slow and uncontrollable; system-level simulation deviates from reality.
**Approach:** Hybrid simulation: model-based mobility, multi-dimensional REMs and DDNS.
**Tools and scale:** Compared against real measurements and ns-3; opportunistic vehicular data transfer.
**Results:** Better match to reality and ~300x higher computational efficiency than ns-3.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future work: optimise prediction via cooperative methods.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - hybrid learned vehicular simulation.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on security.
