# A Framework for the Systematic Assessment of Anomaly Detectors in Time-Sensitive Automotive Networks
Philipp Meyer, Timo Häckel, Teresa Lübeck et al. · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.01324 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Connected cars are susceptible to cyberattacks.
**Approach:** In this paper, we present an assessment framework that allows for reproducible, comparable, and rapid evaluation of detection algorithms.
**Tools and scale:** It is based on a simulation toolchain, which contributes configurable topologies, traffic streams, anomalies, attacks, and detectors.
**Results:** We demonstrate the assessment of NADSs in a comprehensive in-vehicular network with its communication flows, on which we model traffic anomalies.
