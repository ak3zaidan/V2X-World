# Privacy-Preserving Intrusion Detection in Software-defined VANET using Federated Learning with BERT
Shakil Ibne Ahsan, Phil Legg, S M Iftekharul Alam · arXiv (Cornell University) 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.07343 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The absence of robust security protocols renders the VANET (Vehicle ad-hoc Networks) network open to cyber threats by compromising passengers and road safety.
**Approach:** Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS) are widely employed to detect network security threats. With vehicles' high mobility on the road and diverse environments, VANETs devise ever-changing network topologies, lack privacy and security, and have limited bandwidth efficiency.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The FL-BERT technique has yielded promising results, opening avenues for further investigation in this particular area of research. We reached the result of our approaches by comparing existing research works and found that FL-BERT is more effective for privacy and security concerns.

- With vehicles' high mobility on the road and diverse environments, VANETs devise ever-changing network topologies, lack privacy and security, and have limited bandwidth efficiency.
