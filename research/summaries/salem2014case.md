# The Case for Dynamic Key Distribution for PKI-Based VANETS
Ahmed H. Salem, Ayman Abdel-Hamid, Mohamad Abou El-Nasr · International journal of Computer Networks & Communications 2014 · https://doi.org/10.5121/ijcnc.2014.6105 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** TPD stores many keys in PKI-based VANETs.
**Approach:** Dynamic key distribution via nearest RSU to a CA cloud; reduced-message revocation.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical and network simulation (Manhattan model).
**Results:** Effects of speed, density, region and RSU count on revocation messages.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future work noted.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - on-demand key provisioning via infrastructure.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
