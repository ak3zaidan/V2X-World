# A certificateless aggregate signature scheme for security and privacy protection in VANET
E. Cahyadi, Tzu-Wei Su, Chou-Chen Yang, M. Hwang · Int. J. Distributed Sens. Networks 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1177/15501329221080658 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In the vehicular ad hoc network, moving vehicles can keep communicating with each other by entering or leaving the network at any time to establish a new connection. However, since many users transmit a substantial number of messages, it may cause reception delays and affect the entire system.
**Approach:** A certificateless aggregate signature scheme can provide a signature compression that keeps the verification cost low.
**Tools and scale:** The scheme is proven to be secure against the Type-1 ( A 1 ) and Type-2 ( A 2 ) adversaries in the random oracle model under the computational Diffie–Hellman problem assumption.
**Results:** The scheme is proven to be secure against the Type-1 ( A 1 ) and Type-2 ( A 2 ) adversaries in the random oracle model under the computational Diffie–Hellman problem assumption. Then, the performance evaluation demonstrates that our proposed scheme is more suitable for deployment in vehicular ad hoc network environments.
