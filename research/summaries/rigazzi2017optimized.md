# Optimized Certificate Revocation List Distribution for Secure V2X Communications
Giovanni Rigazzi, Andrea Tassi, Robert J. Piechocki, Theo Tryfonas, Andrew Nix · IEEE Vehicular Technology Conference 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtcfall.2017.8288287 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Pseudonym PKI CRL distribution limits scalability.
**Approach:** Compressed CRLs via optimised Bloom filters with configurable false positives.
**Tools and scale:** Large-scale urban network simulations.
**Results:** Significant overhead reduction without increased revocation complexity.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Bloom-filter false positives.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - CRL compression and distribution in urban simulation.
**Novelty implications:** No effect on SCMS-specific claims.
