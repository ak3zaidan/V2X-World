# Securing Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks Against Blackhole Attacks Using an Enhanced Trust Management Framework
Mukhtar Abubakar, Armaya’u Zango Umar, Abubakar Aminu Mu’azu · UMYU Scientifica 2026 · https://doi.org/10.56919/usci.2652.037 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks (VANETs) are essential for Intelligent Transportation Systems, enabling vehicle-to-vehicle and vehicle-to-infrastructure communication to improve road safety and traffic efficiency.
**Approach:** This study proposes an Enhanced Trust Management Framework (ETMF) that integrates neighbour monitoring, local and global trust evaluation, and collaborative decision-making between vehicles and Road Side Units (RSUs) to mitigate blackhole attacks.
**Tools and scale:** ETMF was implemented and evaluated using OMNeT++ 5.6.2, SUMO 1.8.0, and VEINS 5.2 in a realistic Abuja road network scenario comprising 50 vehicles, 5 RSUs, and 0–40 malicious nodes over a 600-second simulation period.
**Results:** Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks (VANETs) are essential for Intelligent Transportation Systems, enabling vehicle-to-vehicle and vehicle-to-infrastructure communication to improve road safety and traffic efficiency. Results showed that ETMF reduced end-to-end delay by up to 25%, improved anomaly detection by up to 8%, and increased detection rates by 2.06%–8.33% compared with FIDS-HMM under blackhole attack conditions.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future work will focus on improving convergence behaviour and event detection performance in larger and more densely populated vehicular networks.
