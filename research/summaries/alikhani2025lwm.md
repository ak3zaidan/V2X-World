# LWM: A Pre-trained Wireless Foundation Model for Universal Feature Extraction
Sadjad Alikhani, Gouranga Charan, Ahmed Alkhateeb · 2025 IEEE International Conference on Machine Learning for Communication and Networking (ICMLCN) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icmlcn64995.2025.11140266 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Need for task-agnostic wireless channel representations.
**Approach:** LWM: transformer foundation model pretrained self-supervised on large wireless channel datasets.
**Tools and scale:** Large-scale channel datasets.
**Results:** Consistent classification improvements over raw channels, especially with limited data.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - wireless foundation model (adjacent to direction 3 world model).
**Novelty implications:** Wireless channel foundation models exist; cross-layer V2X world models do not here.
