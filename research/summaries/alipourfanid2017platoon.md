# Platoon Stability and Safety Analysis of Cooperative Adaptive Cruise Control under Wireless Rician Fading Channels and Jamming Attacks
Amir Alipour-Fanid, Monireh Dabaghchian, Kai Zeng · arXiv 2017 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.08476 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, introduction, evaluation and conclusion excerpts)

**Problem:** Jamming and Rician fading on CACC stability.
**Approach:** State-dependent communication reliability in string dynamics; attacker locations; reachable states.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical and simulation.
**Results:** Higher jamming requires higher transmit power to stabilise.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Notes jamming impact on stability was largely open.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Radio-to-control safety coupling, like our joint ground truth.
**Novelty implications:** None.
