# D-CIPHER: Dynamic Collaborative Intelligent Multi-Agent System with Planner and Heterogeneous Executors for Offensive Security
Meet Udeshi, Minghao Shao, Haoran Xi et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.10931 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Large Language Models (LLMs) have been used in cybersecurity such as autonomous security analysis or penetration testing.
**Approach:** This approach was inadequate for complex CTF tasks. Inspired by real-world CTF competitions, where teams of experts collaborate, we introduce the D-CIPHER LLM multi-agent framework for collaborative CTF solving.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Additionally, D-CIPHER incorporates an Auto-prompter agent to improve problem-solving by auto-generating a highly relevant initial prompt. D-CIPHER achieves state-of-the-art performance on three benchmarks: 22.0% on NYU CTF Bench, 22.5% on Cybench, and 44.0% on HackTheBox, which is 2.5% to 8.5% better than previous work.
