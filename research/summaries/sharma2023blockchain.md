# Blockchain-Based Trust Management Scheme Over the Spatiotemporal Features of Software-Defined Vehicle Network
Aditi Sharma, U. Amaleshwari, R. Shanmugapriya et al. · 2023 International Conference on New Frontiers in Communication, Automation, Management and Security (ICCAMS) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccams60113.2023.10526191 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle ad hoc networks (VANETs) have a lot of promise for enhancing traffic flow and addressing driving safety, however, they have not yet fully matured.
**Approach:** A trust evaluation and management method is developed based on the software-defined vehicle network (SDVN) architecture to address these issues and enhance the network performance of vehicular communication. Message consistency and security are ensured in the SDVN network by the proposed trust management mechanism, which also speeds up consensus and improves trust management’s overall effectiveness, according to experimental analysis.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The trust evaluation method considers vehicles’ spatiotemporal properties to control and standardize their behavior, improving the precision of malicious behavior detection.
