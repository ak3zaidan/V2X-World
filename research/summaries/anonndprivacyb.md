# Privacy-Preserving Prediction of Electric Vehicle Charge Events
, Robert Marlin · thesis  · https://doi.org/10.5204/thesis.eprints.261417 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Electric vehicles are expected to dominate global transport by 2050, creating major challenges for energy forecasting, infrastructure planning, and data privacy.
**Approach:** This research introduces a privacy-preserving, decentralised framework that predicts when and where EVs will charge without exposing sensitive user information. Using hierarchical federated learning with advanced privacy and optimisation techniques, the system achieves accurate, scalable spatio-temporal forecasting across distributed EV networks.
