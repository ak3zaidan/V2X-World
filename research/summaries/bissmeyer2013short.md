# Short paper: Experimental analysis of misbehavior detection and prevention in VANETs
Norbert Bissmeyer, Klaus Henrik Schroder, Jonathan Petit, Sebastian Mauthofer, Kpatcha M. Bayarou · 2013 IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference 2013 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vnc.2013.6737612 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Internal attackers inject authenticated fake messages.
**Approach:** Categorisation of internal attackers; real-vehicle implementation showing malware-induced false warnings; countermeasures.
**Tools and scale:** Real vehicles.
**Results:** Attack feasible due to insufficiently specified standards; countermeasures detect and block it.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — real-vehicle demonstration of insider attack and blocking.
**Novelty implications:** Real-vehicle insider attacks demonstrated early.
