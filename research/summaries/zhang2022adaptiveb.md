# Adaptive radial basis function sliding mode control for platoons under DoS attacks
Xiaofei Zhang, Haiping Du, Zhijuan Jia, Chi Cui, Yanyan Yang · IET Intelligent Transport Systems 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1049/itr2.12289 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract This paper proposes an adaptive sliding mode control based on an auxiliary distance sensor for nonlinear platoons over vehicular ad‐hoc networks under Denial‐of‐Services (DoS) attacks and external disturbances.
**Approach:** DoS attacks can cause packet dropping for the wireless network and will lead to the collision or degradation to adaptive cruise control of platoons. To solve this problem, an adaptive Radial Basis Function (RBF) sliding mode control method is proposed for the attacked platoons.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Then the adaptive sliding mode controllers are designed to achieve the control goals: the spacing error converges to zero, and the platoon maintains the string stability. Numerical examples are applied to show the effectiveness of the proposed method.
