# Exploring the Causality of End-to-End Autonomous Driving
Jiankun Li, Hao Li, Jiangjiang Liu et al. · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.06546 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Deep learning-based models are widely deployed in autonomous driving areas, especially the increasingly noticed end-to-end solutions.
**Approach:** In this work, we propose a comprehensive approach to explore and analyze the causality of end-to-end autonomous driving. Then, we quantitatively assess the factors influencing model decisions by visualizing and statistically analyzing the response of key model inputs.
