# Ghost Riders: Sybil Attacks on Crowdsourced Mobile Mapping Services
Gang Wang, Bolun Wang, Tianyi Wang, A. Nika et al. · IEEE/ACM Transactions on Networking 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnet.2018.2818073 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Crowdsourced maps (Waze) lack location authentication.
**Approach:** Demonstrated software Sybil devices causing false congestion/accidents and rerouting, and tracking users; defence via co-location edges forming proximity graphs. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Real Waze experiments and large-scale simulations.
**Results:** A single Sybil device with limited resources can cause havoc; co-location graphs dramatically reduce attack impact.
