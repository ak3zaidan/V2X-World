# Recommendation-Based Trust Model for Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X)
Aljawharah Alnasser, Hongjian Sun, Jing Jiang · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2019.2950083 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** An intelligent transportation system (ITS) is one of the main systems which have been developed to achieve safe traffic and efficient transportation. It enables the vehicles to establish connections with other road entities and infrastructure units using vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communications.
**Approach:** In this article, a recommendation-based trust model for V2X communications is proposed to defend against internal attacks. In addition, we conduct various experiments with different percentage of malicious nodes to measure the performance of the proposed model.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Our model improves the packet dropping rate (PDR) with 36% when the percentage of malicious nodes is around 87.5%.
