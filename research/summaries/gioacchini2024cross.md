# Cross-Network Embeddings Transfer for Traffic Analysis
Luca Gioacchini, Marco Mellia, Luca Vassio, Idilio Drago, Giulia Milan, Zied Ben Houidi, Dario Rossi · IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tnsm.2023.3329442 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Artificial Intelligence (AI) approaches have emerged as powerful tools to improve traffic analysis for network monitoring and management.
**Approach:** However, the lack of large labeled datasets and the ever-changing networking scenarios make a fundamental difference compared to other domains where AI is thriving. We believe the ability to transfer the specific knowledge acquired in one network (or dataset) to a different network (or dataset) would be fundamental to speed up the adoption of AI-based solutions for traffic analysis and other networking applications (e.g., cybersecurity).
