# An Distributed CA System : Identity authentication system in transnational railway transportation based on blockchain
Xiaofeng Zhan, Xuelin Cheng, Wei Guo, Ke-Ting Yin et al. · 2021 International Conference on Computer Information Science and Artificial Intelligence (CISAI) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cisai54367.2021.00198 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Aiming at the issue of identity authentication among the subjects, this paper proposes a distributed CA scheme based on blockchain to realize mutual recognition of transnational identities, and ensure the sharing of transnational document data and the protection of privacy and security.
**Approach:** The blockchain-based transnational railway transportation data exchange system combines the immutable and traceable characteristics of blockchain technology, and uses blockchain smart contracts and consensus mechanisms to propose a specific technical route for the sharing and exchange of document data based on blockchain.
