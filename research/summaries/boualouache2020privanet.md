# PRIVANET: An Efficient Pseudonym Changing and Management Framework for Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks
Abdelwahab Boualouache, Sidi-Mohammed Senouci, Samira Moussaoui · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2019.2924856 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Efficient pseudonym changing and management remains open.
**Approach:** PRIVANET: grid of location privacy zones at roadside infrastructure, reputation incentives, user-centric privacy, hybrid pseudonym and CRL distribution, IP/MAC derived from pseudonym, placement model.
**Tools and scale:** IEEE T-ITS; Veins (OMNeT++/SUMO).
**Results:** Privacy protection and VLPZ placement evaluated.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the full text.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — derives IP/MAC from pseudonym (cross-layer identifiers) and distributes CRLs.
**Novelty implications:** Co-changing network identifiers with pseudonyms is proposed; measuring residual cross-layer linkability is open.
