# Cloud-based Sybil Attack Detection Scheme for Connected Vehicles
Anika Anwar, Talal Halabi, Mohammad Zulkernine · 2019 3rd Cyber Security in Networking Conference (CSNet) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/csnet47905.2019.9108923 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Sybil attacks on connected vehicles without a central authority.
**Approach:** Cloud-based authorisation unit with symmetric crypto and real-time location tracking, plus trust evaluation.
**Tools and scale:** Experiment and security analysis.
**Results:** Efficacy in detection rate, complexity and requirements (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — centralized detection.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
