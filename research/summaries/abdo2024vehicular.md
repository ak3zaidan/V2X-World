# VEhicular Secure Network Open Simulator (VESNOS): A Cyber-security oriented co-simulation platform for connected and automated driving
Ahmed Abdo, Guoyuan Wu, Nael Abu-Ghazaleh · Proceedings of the 38th ACM SIGSIM Conference on Principles of Advanced Discrete Simulation 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3615979.3656054 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Traffic simulators lack real connected-vehicle features and US security protocols.
**Approach:** VESNOS: co-simulation of SUMO, OMNeT++ and an SCMS implementation for connected and automated driving.
**Tools and scale:** SUMO, OMNeT++, SCMS (open-source components); network-level evaluation.
**Results:** Platform contribution (details in paper).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
