# SP-TRUST: a trust management model for speed trust in vehicular networks
Ilhem Souissi, Nadia ben Azzouna, Rihab Abidi, Tahar Berradia, Lamjed Ben Said · International Journal of Computer Applications 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1080/1206212x.2022.2108562 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** ABSTRACT Information security mechanisms are crucial for Vehicular Ad-hoc NETworks (VANET) applications in order to preserve their robustness.
**Approach:** The efficiency of these applications relies on the reliability of the used information, especially the vehicle-related information such as location and speed. Trust management models are crucial to evaluate the quality of the used information.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The results of the experimental studies proved that the model performs well in detecting different behaviors of malicious vehicles in different scenarios, especially when the percentage of malicious vehicles is lower than 50%.
