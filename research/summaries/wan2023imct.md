# ImCT: A Feasible Scheme for Deploying Implicit Certificates with Certificate Transparency in IoT

**Approach:** To inherit the advantage of implicit certificates and make CT more friendly to resource-restricted IoT systems, this paper proposes ImCT, the first feasible and efficient scheme for deploying ECQV implicit certificates with CT in IoT.
