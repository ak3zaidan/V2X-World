# V-PASS: Sybil-Resistant Pseudonym Self-Provisioning for V2X
He-Xuan Yu, M. Barat, Shaoyu Li, M. Shahriar et al. · Wireless Network Security 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3765613.3811692 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** SCMS/CCMS batch issuance of short-lived pseudonyms implies trillion-scale annual certificate management, storage and constant cellular connectivity.
**Approach:** V-PASS: vehicles derive unlinkable pseudonyms locally from one enrolment credential; a Secure Element enforces one pseudonym per window with hardware attestation (ex-ante Sybil resistance); split trust SE/ECU; stateless receiver verification. (summarised from the abstract only)
