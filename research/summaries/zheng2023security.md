# A Security-Enhanced Pairing-Free Certificateless Aggregate Signature for Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks
Hang-Cheng Zheng, Min Luo, Yunru Zhang, Cong Peng et al. · IEEE Systems Journal 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jsyst.2022.3220869 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As self-organizing, highly heterogeneous and multi-interactive networks, vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs) are rapidly improving the efficiency of modern traffic. This promising technology is significant for supporting current intelligent transportation by cooperative data sharing and processing.
**Approach:** In this article, we propose an efficient, privacy-preserving, and pairing-free CLAS scheme with enhanced security.
**Tools and scale:** We prove that it is existentially unforgeable against adaptive chosen message attacks in the random oracle model.
**Results:** A certificateless aggregate signature (CLAS) can not only ensure data security, but also reduce communication costs, which is suitable for a resource-constrained environment.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Considering requirements for secure interactions and the limitation of resources in VANETs, protecting data authenticity while enhancing computational efficiency has become the main challenge.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — Bespoke VANET/IoV authentication or privacy scheme (CPPA/certificateless/batch/blockchain style), evaluated by analysis or small simulation; not IEEE 1609.2/SCMS and no closed-loop safety evaluation.
**Novelty implications:** Adds to the large body of standalone authentication schemes; does not touch end-to-end SCMS simulation or safety impact of security.
