# A Unified Attack Detection Strategy for Multi-Agent Systems over Transient and Steady Stages
Jinming Gao, Yijing Wang, Wentao Zhang et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03496 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper proposes a unified detection strategy against three kinds of attacks for multi-agent systems (MASs) which is applicable to both transient and steady stages.
**Approach:** For attacks on the communication layer, a watermarking-based detection scheme with KullbackLeibler (KL) divergence is designed. For hybrid attacks, based on the above detection mechanisms, a general framework resorting to trusted agents is presented, which requires weaker graph conditions and less information transmission.
