# Jamming Attack on DSRC Communication Caused by a C-V2X Sidelink Device
Breno Sousa, Naercio Magaia, Sara Silva, Hieu Nguyen, Yong Liang Guan · 2025 IEEE 101st Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2025-Spring) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtc2025-spring65109.2025.11174370 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** DSRC and C-V2X co-channel coexistence; jamming by sidelink device.
**Approach:** Experiments with CBR traffic, LOS and NLOS.
**Tools and scale:** Real DSRC and C-V2X devices.
**Results:** Attenuation up to 24 dB (256 B) and 35 dB (1,399 B).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Cross-technology interference; our engine has both stacks.
**Novelty implications:** Measured co-channel jamming exists.
