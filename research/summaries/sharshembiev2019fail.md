# Fail-Safe Mechanism Using Entropy Based Misbehavior Classification and Detection in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Kumar Sharshembiev, Seong-Moo Yoo, Elbasher Elmahdi, Yong-Kab Kim, Geun-Ho Jeong · 2019 International Conference on Internet of Things (iThings) and IEEE Green Computing and Communications (GreenCom) and IEEE Cyber, Physical and Social Computing (CPSCom) and IEEE Smart Data (SmartData) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ithings/greencom/cpscom/smartdata.2019.00042 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Unintentional misbehaviour turning multi-hop routing into flooding.
**Approach:** Selective flow sampling and entropy change for real-time classification; fail-safe algorithm.
**Tools and scale:** Veins with weighted p-persistence routing in a US city area.
**Results:** Real-time detection and classification (no figures in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Targeted attacks out of scope.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — faults vs attacks distinction.
**Novelty implications:** Little bearing.
