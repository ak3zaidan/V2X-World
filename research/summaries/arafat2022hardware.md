# Hardware-in-the-Loop Simulation for Assessing Red-Light Violation Warning Application With Semi-Actuated Signal Control in a Connected Vehicle Environment
Mahmoud Arafat, Mohammed Hadi, Thodsapon Hunsanon, Tao Wang · Transportation Research Record: Journal of the Transportation Research Board 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1177/03611981221140371 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Safety/mobility impacts of red-light violation warning under semi-actuated signals.
**Approach:** HIL simulation evaluating RLVW with and without assured green period (AGP).
**Tools and scale:** HIL signal controller, traffic simulation.
**Results:** RLVW without AGP has limited benefit; with AGP and 100% RLVW, red-light running reduced by about 92%; AGP increases stops and delay.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Optimal AGP setting balancing mobility and safety needs further investigation.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — V2X application safety impact via simulation.
**Novelty implications:** No impact on security claims.
