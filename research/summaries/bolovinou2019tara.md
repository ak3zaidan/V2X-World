# TARA+: Controllability-aware Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment for L3 Automated Driving Systems
Anastasia Bolovinou, Ugur-Ilker Atmaca, Al Tariq Sheik, Obaid Ur-Rehman et al. · 2019 IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium (IV) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ivs.2019.8813999 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In this paper, a novel model for the cyber-security analysis of Level 3 (L3) Automated Driving (AD) systems is proposed by integrating aspects of functional safety.
**Approach:** The model is built based on the state-of-the-art framework for cyber security analysis, known as Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment (TARA), which quantifies the likelihood and the impact of attack and combines them in order to derive an attack risk value. The novelty lies in the bespoke integration of the impact calculation, which incorporates the notion of controllability of an attack by the AD system and/or by the driver.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: ISO/SAE 21434 / TARA).
**Results:** The proposed model is applied for the Urban Chauffeur and the Highway Chauffeur AD system functions, providing insights into the security risk in a wide area of distinct operational design domains as defined by SAE J3016.
