# A Novel Model based on Group Controlled Observation for DDOS Attack Detection and Prevention in VANET
Sindhu Grover, Pooja Mittal · Indian Journal of Science and Technology 2016 · https://doi.org/10.17485/ijst/2016/v9i36/97141 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Objectives: To identify the Distributed Denial Of service (DDOS) attack in the network and generate a preventive path while performing the V2V communication.
**Approach:** The objective of work is to reduce the communication delay and communication loss. Methods/Statistical Analysis: In this work a group controlled method is provided for vehicular network to observe the attack within the group.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Findings: In Vehicular Ad Hoc Network (VANET), the large node traffic exist which results heavy network communication. Application/ Improvements: The work is defined specifically for vehicular network and to identify the DDOS attack.
