# Energy Aware Emergency Packets Dissemination and Effective Trusted RSU Distribution in Vehicular Adhoc Networks
K. Al-sharhanee, N. F. Abdulsattar, Ali Hamzah Najim, A. Alkhayyat, Fatima H. Alsalamy · 2023 6th International Conference on Engineering Technology and its Applications (IICETA) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iiceta57613.2023.10351346 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In Vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANETs), the vehicles suffers from improper traffic monitoring and ineffective trust management system due to this delay and overhead is highly increased during communication in VANETs.
**Approach:** In order to overcome the earlier research demerits in this article Energy Aware Emergency Packets Dissemination and Effective Trusted RSU Distribution (EAETR) approach is performed in the VANETs environment which is the combination of effective trusted RSU distribution and emergency packets dissemination. Through these methods the trustworthiness of the vehicles are increased so that the network is free from the disturbance of the external environment and simultaneously using the emergency packets dissemination the confidential and high priority packets get separated from the normal communication channel so that the network congestion is reduced.
