# Federated learning-based scheme for detecting passive mobile attackers in 5G vehicular edge computing
Abdelwahab Boualouache, Thomas Engel · Annals of Telecommunications 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s12243-021-00871-x · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Detecting passive attacks is always considered difficult in vehicular networks. Passive attackers can eavesdrop on the wireless medium to collect beacons.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a novel federated learning-based scheme for detecting passive mobile attackers in 5G vehicular edge computing. We then build an efficient machine learning (ML) model to detect tracking attacks based only on the receiving beacons.
**Tools and scale:** Passive attackers can eavesdrop on the wireless medium to collect beacons.
**Results:** Indeed, only 20 received beacons are required to achieve 95% accuracy. The obtained results are also validated through simulations.
