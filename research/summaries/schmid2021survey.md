# A Survey on Client Throughput Prediction Algorithms in Wired and Wireless Networks
Josef Schmid, Alfred Höss, Björn W. Schuller · ACM Computing Surveys 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1145/3477204 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Network communication has become a part of everyday life, and the interconnection among devices and people will increase even more in the future. Nevertheless, prediction of Quality of Service parameters, particularly throughput, is quite a challenging task.
**Approach:** In this survey, we provide an extensive insight into the literature on Transmission Control Protocol throughput prediction. We assessed more than 35 approaches spanning from equation-based over various time smoothing to modern learning and location smoothing methods.
