# TriP: Misbehavior Detection for Dynamic Platoons using Trust
Keno Garlichs, Alexander Willecke, Martin Wegner, Lars C. Wolf · 2019 IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Conference (ITSC) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/itsc.2019.8917188 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Dynamic platoons must cooperate with unknown, possibly malicious partners.
**Approach:** TriP trust model rating members by divergence between reported and actual behaviour, with countermeasures.
**Tools and scale:** Evaluation against literature attacks (platoon simulation).
**Results:** Detects all attacks and prevents harm via countermeasures.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — detection plus in-loop countermeasure in platoons.
**Novelty implications:** Local closed-loop mitigation in platoons exists; infrastructure revocation not addressed.
