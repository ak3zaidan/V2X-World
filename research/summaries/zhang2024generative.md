# Generative AI Agents with Large Language Model for Satellite Networks via a Mixture of Experts Transmission
Ruichen Zhang, Hongyang Du, Yinqiu Liu et al. · arXiv 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jsac.2024.3459037 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In response to the needs of 6G global communications, satellite communication networks have emerged as a key solution.
**Approach:** To solve these problems, this paper develops generative artificial intelligence (AI) agents for model formulation and then applies a mixture of experts (MoE) approach to design transmission strategies. Afterward, by integrating the expertise of multiple specialized components, we propose an MoE-proximal policy optimization (PPO) approach to solve the formulated problem.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The simulation results validate the accuracy and effectiveness of employing a generative agent for problem formulation.
