# MPC-CSAS: Multi-Party Computation for Real-Time Privacy-Preserving Speed Advisory Systems
Ming-Ming Liu, Long Cheng, Yingqi Gu, Ying Wang et al. · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2021.3052840 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Consensus-based speed advisory systems need privacy but existing methods need many iterations.
**Approach:** MPC-CSAS: multi-party computation for privacy-preserving speed advisory, any cost function, one iteration.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation of a typical CSAS.
**Results:** Good performance in one iteration without extra infrastructure.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — Privacy-preserving cooperative control, not PKI.
**Novelty implications:** None.
