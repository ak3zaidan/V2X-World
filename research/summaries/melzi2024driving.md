# Driving simulator: Analysing the impact of mechanical latency on the perception of lateral dynamics
Stefano Melzi, Giorgio Previati · Journal of Vibration and Control 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1177/10775463241246906 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Mechanical latency is the time elapsed between the driver’s input and the subsequent movement of a driving simulator.
**Approach:** Large latencies may overlap with the time constants of a vehicle, thus altering its dynamics. As latency introduces inconsistencies between the driver’s input and the vehicle’s response, it may also result in motion sickness.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting. The available text does not describe a V2X simulator, radio stack, traffic model or SCMS-style PKI in enough detail to report; scale not stated.
**Results:** Tests were performed with an innovative cable-driven simulator moving over a 6 × 6 metre platform. The results showed that 20% of the population can perceive latency variations below 40 ms.
