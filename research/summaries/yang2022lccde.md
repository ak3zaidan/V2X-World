# LCCDE: A Decision-Based Ensemble Framework for Intrusion Detection in The Internet of Vehicles
Li Yang, Abdallah Shami, Gary Stevens et al. · arXiv 2022 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.03399 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Modern vehicles, including autonomous vehicles and connected vehicles, have adopted an increasing variety of functionalities through connections and communications with other vehicles, smart devices, and infrastructures.
**Approach:** To protect IoV systems against cyber threats, Intrusion Detection Systems (IDSs) that can identify malicious cyber-attacks have been developed using Machine Learning (ML) approaches. To accurately detect various types of attacks in IoV networks, we propose a novel ensemble IDS framework named Leader Class and Confidence Decision Ensemble (LCCDE).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Experiments on two public IoV security datasets (Car-Hacking and CICIDS2017 datasets) demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed LCCDE for intrusion detection on both intra-vehicle and external networks.
