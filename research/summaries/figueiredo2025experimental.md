# Experimental dataset of video and radar detection for cooperative perception in urban environment
Andreia Figueiredo, João Amaral, Marcos Mendes, Rodrigo Rosmaninho, Duarte Dias, Pedro Rito et al. · Data in Brief 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2025.112091 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cooperative perception is an emerging concept in intelligent transportation systems that enhances situational awareness by allowing vehicles and infrastructure nodes to share sensor information.
**Approach:** By extending the sensing range beyond the line of sight of a single agent, cooperative perception enables safer and more informed decision-making in complex traffic situations. To support research in this area, especially from the perspective of infrastructure-based sensing, high-quality datasets are essential.
