# Towards Reliable and Secure Post-Quantum Co-Processors based on RISC-V
Tim Fritzmann, Uzair Sharif, Daniel Müller-Gritschneder, C. Reinbrecht et al. · Design, Automation and Test in Europe 2019 · https://doi.org/10.23919/date.2019.8715173 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Increasingly complex and powerful Systems-on-Chips (SoCs), connected through a 5G network, form the basis of the Internet-of-Things (IoT). These technologies will drive the digitalization in all domains, e.g. industry automation, automotive, avionics, and healthcare.
**Approach:** To this end, we present three contributions.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** A major requirement for all above domains is the long-term (10 to 30 years) secure communication between the SoCs and the cloud over public 5G networks. We show that our coprocessor achieves high reliability and security capabilities while preserving good performance.
