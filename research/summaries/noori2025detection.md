# Detection and reporting of spectrum misbehaviour in connected vehicle networks
Hamed Zamani Noori · cIRcle (University of British Columbia) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.14288/1.0451064 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** No mechanism exists to detect interference and congestion in the V2X band.
**Approach:** Three-layer architecture (detection, reporting, central authority): 802.11p PLCP/PMD and C-V2X SB-SPS based detectors; reporting adapted from IEEE 1609 SCMS misbehaviour reporting; proof-of-concept deployment.
**Tools and scale:** UBC thesis; DSRC, ITS-G5, C-V2X; PoC demonstration.
**Results:** Validated PoC (details not in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — reuses SCMS misbehaviour-reporting path for spectrum misbehaviour; our engine has DSRC/C-V2X MAC and SCMS MA.
**Novelty implications:** Spectrum-misbehaviour reporting through SCMS is proposed; its interaction with the MA decision loop in simulation remains open.
