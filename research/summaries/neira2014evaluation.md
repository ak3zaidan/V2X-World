# Evaluation of V2X antenna performance using a multipath simulation tool
Edith Condo Neira, Ulf Carlberg, Jan Carlsson, Kristian Karlsson, Erik G. Strom · The 8th European Conference on Antennas and Propagation (EuCAP 2014) 2014 · https://doi.org/10.1109/eucap.2014.6902335 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Choosing V2X antenna placement on vehicles at 5.9 GHz.
**Approach:** Statistical multipath simulation method to evaluate vehicle-mounted antennas in different traffic environments.
**Tools and scale:** Multipath simulation tool plus measurements; ITS-G5.
**Results:** Radiation patterns covering the whole azimuth plane are important; method fast and effective.
