# Realistic Corner Case Generation for Autonomous Vehicles with Multimodal Large Language Model
Qiujing Lu, Meng Ma, Ximiao Dai et al. · arXiv 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.00243 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** To guarantee the safety and reliability of autonomous vehicle (AV) systems, corner cases play a crucial role in exploring the system's behavior under rare and challenging conditions within simulation environments.
**Approach:** To tackle this challenge, we present AutoScenario, a multimodal Large Language Model (LLM)-based framework for realistic corner case generation.
