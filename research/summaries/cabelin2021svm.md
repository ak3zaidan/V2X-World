# SVM-based Detection of False Data Injection in Intelligent Transportation System
Joe Diether Cabelin, Paul Vincent Alpano, Jhoanna Rhodette Pedrasa · International Conference on Information Networking 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icoin50884.2021.9333942 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** FDI attacks in VANETs.
**Approach:** Per-vehicle SVM IDS classifying packets as trusted or malicious.
**Tools and scale:** MATLAB + NS-3 co-simulation; highway.
**Results:** High accuracy, low false positives, improved highway congestion (no numbers in abstract).
