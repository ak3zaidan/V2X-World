# An Extensible and Effective Anonymous Batch Authentication Scheme for Smart Vehicular Networks
Jing Zhang, Hong Zhong, Jie Cui, Yan Xu, Lu Liu · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2020.2970092 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Traditional pseudonym schemes preload system keys and have large CRLs.
**Approach:** Anonymous batch authentication where CRL size depends only on revoked vehicles via two hashed seeds per vehicle.
**Tools and scale:** Security analysis.
**Results:** Reduced CRL scale and rapid identity revocation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - seed-based compact revocation resembles linkage-seed CRLs of SCMS.
**Novelty implications:** Seed-based compact CRLs are known; not novel for us.
