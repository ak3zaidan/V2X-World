# Estimating Causal Effects of HIV Prevention Interventions with Interference in Network-based Studies among People Who Inject Drugs
TingFang Lee, Ashley L. Buchanan, Natallia V. Katenka et al. · arXiv 2021 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.04865 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Evaluating causal effects in the presence of interference is challenging in network-based studies of hard-to-reach populations.
**Approach:** Like many such populations, people who inject drugs (PWID) are embedded in social networks and often exert influence on others in their network.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** We analyzed data from the Transmission Reduction Intervention Project, which ascertained a network of PWID and their contacts in Athens, Greece, from 2013 to 2015. The results suggest that HIV risk behavior can be mitigated by exposure to a community alert when an increased risk of HIV is detected in the network.
