# Misbehavior Detection in Connected Vehicle: Pre-Bayesian Majority Game Framework
Adil Attiaoui, Mouna Elmachkour, Abdellatif Kobbane, Marwane Ayaida · Proceedings of the 11th International Conference on Vehicle Technology and Intelligent Transport Systems 2025 · https://doi.org/10.5220/0013359100003941 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Trust in ephemeral VANETs.
**Approach:** Pre-Bayesian plus majority game framework.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation with varying malicious proportions.
**Results:** Robust up to 40% malicious nodes; convergence delays.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Convergence delay in highly adversarial settings.
- future integrations.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — Game theory.
**Novelty implications:** None.
