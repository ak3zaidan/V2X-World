# A Secure Trust-aware Cross-layer Routing Protocol for Vehicular Ad hoc Networks
S. Baccari, Mohamed Hadded, Haifa Touati, Paul Muhlethaler · Journal of Cyber Security and Mobility 2021 · https://doi.org/10.13052/jcsm2245-1439.1023 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** TRPM cross-layer routing vulnerable to black/grey hole and MAC DoS.
**Approach:** Trust-based TRPM.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation.
**Results:** Improves PDR and channel access rate under malicious nodes.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Extend to other attacks, in particular packet transfer delay attacks
**Overlap with our engine:** none — Routing trust.
**Novelty implications:** None.
