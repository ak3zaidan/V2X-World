# Anomaly Detection for In-Vehicle Communication Using Transformers
Victor Cobilean, Harindra S. Mavikumbure, Chathurika S. Wickramasinghe, Benny J. Varghese et al. · IECON 2023- 49th Annual Conference of the IEEE Industrial Electronics Society 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iecon51785.2023.10311788 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the advancements of modern vehicle infrastructures, vehicles are increasingly relying on the signals received from a vast number of sensors and electronic components. Wireless technologies enable communication between vehicles and infrastructure, but it also increase the vulnerability surface.
**Approach:** To address these challenges, this paper proposes a transformer neural network-based intrusion detection system (CAN-Former IDS) that predicts anomalous behavior within the CAN protocol communication. In this paper, we will simultaneously analyze both the sequence of IDs and the message payload values.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset, deep learning (mentioned in abstract)
