# SAMM: Situation Awareness with Machine Learning for Misbehavior Detection in VANET

**Problem:** ML misbehaviour detectors have limited detection capability.
**Approach:** SAMM: situation awareness used to predict neighbour trust and re-evaluate ML outputs.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi dataset; several ML models.
**Results:** Improves accuracy on VeReMi (figures not in abstract excerpt).
