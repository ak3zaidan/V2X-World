# A Decentralized Reputation Management System for Securing the Internet of Vehicles
R. Engoulou, Martine Bellaiche, Talal Halabi, S. Pierre · International Conference on Computing, Networking and Communications 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/iccnc.2019.8685551 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The evolution of the Internet of Vehicles (IoV) paradigm has recently attracted a lot of researchers and industries. Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANET) is the networking model that lies at the heart of this technology.
**Approach:** To secure the interconnected vehicular system, many cryptography techniques, communication protocols, and certification and reputation-based security approaches were proposed. Nonetheless, some limitations are still present, preventing the practical implementation of such approaches.
**Tools and scale:** Car-Hacking/CAN dataset (mentioned in abstract)
