# Anti-Quantum Lattice-Based Ring Signature Scheme and Applications in VANETs
Chunhong Jiao, Xinyin Xiang · Entropy 2021 · https://doi.org/10.3390/e23101364 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Ring signatures for VANET anonymity need PQ security and efficiency.
**Approach:** Trapdoor-free lattice ring signature (SIS) applied to VANETs.
**Tools and scale:** Simulation of efficiency.
**Results:** More efficient signing/verification than existing lattice ring signatures.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Existing lattice ring signatures have large parameters
- reconstructing PKC on PQ problems is the main future direction
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — PQ anonymous signatures for VANETs; not SCMS.
**Novelty implications:** None for our directions.
