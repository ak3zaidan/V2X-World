# Platooning in Connected Vehicles: A Review of Current Solutions, Standardization Activities, Cybersecurity, and Research Opportunities
Farah-Emma Braiteh, Francesca Bassi, Rida Khatoun · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Vehicles 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tiv.2024.3447916 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Platooning lacks a complete, secure service description across research, standards and testbeds.
**Approach:** Review of CACC/platooning protocols, ISO standards, C-ITS PKI, trust model and Misbehaviour Authority, testbeds, threats and IDS/mitigation per platoon phase.
**Tools and scale:** Survey.
**Results:** Finds no structured description of platooning as a complete service; proposes the essential functional elements; notes that most works assume secure communication.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- A complete and secure platooning service is missing
- assumption of secure C-ITS communication does not reflect real conditions
- robustness under varied environmental and communication conditions needs study
- industrial readiness and cross-OEM/country interoperability unresolved.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — survey, though it discusses PKI and MA roles.
**Novelty implications:** Confirms gap: platooning plus PKI/MA end to end is not studied.
