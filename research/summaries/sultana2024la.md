# LA-DETECTS: Local and Adaptive Data-Centric Misbehavior Detection Framework for Vehicular Technology Security
Rukhsar Sultana, Jyoti Grover, Meenakshi Tripathi, Prinkle Sharma · IEEE Open Journal of Vehicular Technology 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ojvt.2024.3513152 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Static MBD lacks adaptability to DoS, replay and Sybil variants.
**Approach:** LA-DETECTS: per-vehicle plausibility and consistency checks with dynamically computed parameters and confidence intervals; weighted average to reduce false positives.
**Tools and scale:** VeReMi extension.
**Results:** Outperforms benchmarks on false info, DoS, disruptive and Sybil variants (no figures in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — adaptive local checks.
**Novelty implications:** Adaptive thresholds known.
