# Have we reached satisfactory methodologies to approach the subjectivity in driver-in-the-loop simulators? A systematic review
Cádmo Augusto Rodrigues Dias, Jánes Landre Júnior · SIMULATION 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1177/00375497251341905 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Driver-in-the-loop (DIL) simulation has become crucial in the automotive industry, providing a controlled setting for assessing vehicle performance, driver characteristics, and scenario configurations.
**Approach:** However, the incorporation of human factors introduces subjectivity into simulation outcomes. This systematic review examines the interplay between objective metrics (OM) and subjective assessments (SA) in DIL vehicle dynamic simulator research.
