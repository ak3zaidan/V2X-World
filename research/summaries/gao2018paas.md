# PAAS: PMIPv6 Access Authentication Scheme Based on Identity-Based Signature in VANETs
Tianhan Gao, Xinyang Deng, Yingbo Wang, Xiang-Jie Kong · IEEE Access 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2018.2841008 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** However, the lack of security considerations limits the rapid growth of PMIPv6.
**Approach:** Motivated by this, a novel authentication scheme based on identity-based signature for PMIPv6 is proposed. The formal security proof under SVO logic and the performance analysis are presented to demonstrate the robustness and efficiency of our proposed scheme.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** The authentication signaling can be finely integrated into the mobility management procedure of PMIPv6, which equips our scheme with high authentication efficiency.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — a standalone authentication/crypto scheme evaluated in isolation (per the abstract); no closed-loop traffic, radio and SCMS co-simulation.
**Novelty implications:** Leaves open how such schemes behave inside a full SCMS with realistic traffic and radio load; it does not rule out any candidate direction.
