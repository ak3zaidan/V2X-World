# A Performance Analysis of Tree-Based and Deep Learning Models for Predicting Quality of Service in Vehicle-to-Network Communication
Luis A. Correia, A. L. Ottoni, M. Novo · 2025 Workshop on Communication Networks and Power Systems (WCNPS) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wcnps69127.2025.11295923 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Short and long-term data rate prediction for V2N.
**Approach:** Tree-based and DL models compared.
**Tools and scale:** Public dataset.
**Results:** RF best short-term (to 5 s); significant degradation to 20 s.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Performance degradation at longer horizons to be addressed in future work.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
