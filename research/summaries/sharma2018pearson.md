# Pearson Correlation Analysis to Detect Misbehavior in VANET
Prinkle Sharma, Jonathan Petit, Hong Liu · 2018 IEEE 88th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC-Fall) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtcfall.2018.8690964 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Location forging attacks on OBUs.
**Approach:** Pearson correlation matrix of kinematic fields to detect forging in real time without training.
**Tools and scale:** Real datasets from Wyoming CV Pilot and UMTRI.
**Results:** Needs 4-7 s of history depending on road type to be fully effective.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Requires 4-7 s of history.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — real US CV pilot data for MBD; detection latency stated.
**Novelty implications:** Real US BSM data MBD exists; detection latency numbers usable for time-to-neutralise modelling.
