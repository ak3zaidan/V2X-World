# BGEM: Bipartite Graph-based Extension Modeling in Graph Neural Network for QoS Prediction
Yingwei Luo, Yugen Du, Shuo Shi, Xia Chen, Guoxing Tang · 2025 International Joint Conference on Neural Networks (IJCNN) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ijcnn64981.2025.11228938 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** With the advancement of cloud computing and the proliferation of internet services, Quality of Service (QoS) has emerged as a critical metric for service caller, necessitating increasingly accurate predictive capabilities.
**Approach:** While existing neural network methods have made significant progress, they have often overlooked the implicit complex interactions manifest in the distribution of QoS values. To overcome this deficiency, we introduce a Bipartite Graph-based Extension Modeling (BGEM) method with user-service interaction and a custom GCN module named Hidden Relationship Perception(HRP-GCN).
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Extensive experiments conducted on real-world datasets demonstrate that our proposed BGEM method outperforms current state-of-the-art approaches in QoS prediction, particularly under low-density conditions, where it still maintains superior performance.
