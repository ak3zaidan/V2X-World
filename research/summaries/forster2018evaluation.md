# An Evaluation of Pseudonym Changes for Vehicular Networks in Large-Scale, Realistic Traffic Scenarios
David Förster, Hans Löhr, Anne Grätz, J. Petit et al. · IEEE transactions on intelligent transportation systems (Print) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2017.2775519 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** How effective are pseudonym change strategies in large realistic traffic?
**Approach:** Evaluates four pseudonym change strategies and parameters against a limited-coverage tracking attacker.
**Tools and scale:** Large-scale realistic traffic simulation, urban and highway scenarios.
**Results:** Urban: satisfactory privacy but high change frequency required; highway: high tracking success even with short intervals, especially in low traffic; proposes urban change intervals higher than foreseen.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Privacy protection in uniform traffic conditions remains a challenge
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Pseudonym change privacy at scale in realistic traffic; our engine provisions pseudonyms but this covers linkability evaluation.
**Novelty implications:** Large-scale pseudonym-change tracking evaluation done; cross-layer (radio + PKI) linkability remains.
