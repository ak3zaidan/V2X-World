# Agent-Based Secure Route Switching and QoS Management in VANETs Using the Wiedemann Car-Following Prediction Model
M. Saleh · International Journal of Electrical and Electronic Engineering &amp; Telecommunications 2025 · https://doi.org/10.18178/ijeetc.14.6.341-353 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Security and QoS in VANET multipath routing.
**Approach:** Agent-based route switching with Wiedemann car-following connectivity prediction and DH+SAS key agreement over Wi-Fi Direct.
**Tools and scale:** Heterogeneous VANET simulation, MITM attacks.
**Results:** Secure variants improve PDR under attack with modest delay increase; LDD strategy best.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future work: ML-based prediction, RSU-edge collaboration.
- Future work: real-world trace validation.
- Future work: dynamic trust and cryptographic adaptation under evolving attacks.

**Novelty implications:** No impact.
