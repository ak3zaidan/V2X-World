**Approach:** The proposed scheme employs a fuzzy extractor combined with identity-based passwords to achieve three factor authentication during login, and further incorporates Physical Unclonable Functions (PUF) and Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC) into the authentication process to enhance security.
**Tools and scale:** Vehicular setting (VANET/IoV/V2X). Not detailed in the catalogued abstract (no integrated traffic, radio and SCMS simulation identified); see the paper for setup and scale.
**Results:** Once authenticated, vehicles can broadcast messages for a certain period without repeated authentication with Roadside Units (RSU) or the Trusted Authority (TA), thus improving communication efficiency.
