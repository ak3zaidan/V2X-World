# Integrated coupling of road traffic and network simulation for realistic emulation of connected vehicle applications
Jian Sun, Yuwei Yang, Keping Li · SIMULATION 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1177/0037549716634189 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** CV evaluation needs run-time interaction of traffic, network and application simulators.
**Approach:** HLA-based federation integrating VISSIM, NS2 and application federates; time synchronisation and track consistency strategies.
**Tools and scale:** VISSIM, NS2, HLA RTI; intersection speed guidance and collision warning case studies.
**Results:** Validity verified on two case studies (qualitative).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — distributed traffic-network-application coupling (we are single-process).
**Novelty implications:** No impact on security claims.
