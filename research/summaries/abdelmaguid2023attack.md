# Attack Endgame: Proactive Security Approach for Predicting Attack Consequences in VANET
Mohammed A. Abdelmaguid, Hossam S. Hassanein, Mohammad Zulkernine · International Conference on Communications 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc45041.2023.10279188 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Approach:** Attack life cycle; fake-accident attack on traffic warnings in F2MD; RNN/LSTM to predict attack endgame.
**Tools and scale:** F2MD (Veins/SUMO).
