# Certificateless and Lightweight Authentication Scheme for Vehicular Communication Networks
W. Hathal, H. Cruickshank, Zhi-Li Sun, C. Maple · IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tvt.2020.3042431 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Reducing the number of road accidents is a key agenda item for governments across the world. This has led to an increase in the amount of attention given to Vehicular Communication Systems (VCS), which are seen as an important technology that can offer significant improvements in road safety.
**Approach:** To combat this issue, we propose a certificateless and lightweight authentication scheme to provide means of secure communications for VCS.
**Tools and scale:** According to the security analysis and extensive simulation of our scheme, the results show that it can withstands various types of attacks.
**Results:** Moreover, we employ TESLA as the underlying broadcast authentication protocol to achieve the required security goals for safety message broadcasting.
