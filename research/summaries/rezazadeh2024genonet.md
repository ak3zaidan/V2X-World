# GenOnet: Generative Open xG Network Simulation with Multi-Agent LLM and ns-3
Farhad Rezazadeh, Amir Ashtari Gargari, Sandra Lagén, Josep Mangues, Dusit Niyato, Lingjia Liu · 2024 3rd International Conference on 6G Networking (6GNet) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/6gnet63182.2024.10765766 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Open 6G development needs advanced, multidisciplinary simulation.
**Approach:** GenOnet: multi-agent LLM (GPT-based) integrated with ns-3 to generate, debug, execute and interpret Open 5G simulations; O-RAN and 3GPP-aware.
**Tools and scale:** ns-3 (C++ and Python); demo.
**Results:** Demo; no quantitative results in abstract.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - LLM agents that configure, run and interpret network simulations, like our LLM agent; cellular O-RAN, not V2X security.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first LLM agent driving a network simulator. Open: LLM agent for V2X cross-layer security analysis with ground truth.
