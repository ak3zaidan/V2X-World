# Measuring location privacy in V2X communication systems with accumulated information
Zhendong Ma, Frank Kargl, Michael Weber · 2009 IEEE 6th International Conference on Mobile Adhoc and Sensor Systems 2009 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mobhoc.2009.5336983 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle-to-vehicle/vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2X) communication systems are envisioned to greatly improve road safety, traffic efficiency, and driver convenience.
**Approach:** However, many V2X applications rely on continuous and detailed location information, which raises location privacy concerns. A multitude of privacy-protection mechanisms have been proposed in recent years.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: location privacy).
**Results:** However, few efforts have been made to develop privacy metrics, which can provide a rigorous way to assess the privacy risk, evaluate the effectiveness of a given mechanism, and exploit the full possibilities of protection methods in V2X systems. Therefore, in this paper we present a trip-based location privacy metric for measuring user location privacy in V2X systems.
