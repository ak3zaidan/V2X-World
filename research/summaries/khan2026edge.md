# Edge-Aware Digital Twin for Real-Time Driver Behavior and Route Anomaly Prediction Under 5G-V2X Constraints
Ajmal Khan, Misha Urooj Khan, Ahmad Suleman, Zeeshan Kaleem · IEEE Internet of Things Journal 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/jiot.2026.3701088 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Connected vehicles operating under 5G NR-V2X require reliable anomaly detection that remains robust under latency variation, packet loss, and heterogeneous sensing conditions. This article presents an edge-aware vehicle-to-everything (V2X)-integrated digital twin (DT) framework for real-time driver behavior and route anomaly (DBRA24) prediction.
**Approach:** The proposed architecture combines temporal vehicle dynamics modeling, communication-context encoding, probabilistic calibration, and adaptive execution orchestration.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: NR-V2X, 5G, digital twin. (From the abstract only.)
**Results:** The framework is evaluated on three heterogeneous datasets: DBRA24, V2X-VANET cooperative driving dataset (V2X-CDD), and vehicle telemetry for driver behavior analysis (VT-DBA). Results show that the full architecture achieves PR-AUC of 0.989 on DBRA24, 0.985 on V2X-CDD, and 0.857 on VT-DBA, with calibration error (ECE) reduced to 0.012, 0.014, and 0.008, respectively. The proposed DT provides a practical solution for latency-aware anomaly reasoning in 5G-V2X-enabled intelligent transportation systems.
