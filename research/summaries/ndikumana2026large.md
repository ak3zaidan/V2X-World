# Large Language Model Assisted Intent-Based Satellite-Integrated Access and Backhaul FWA for Rural Areas
Anselme Ndikumana, Kim Khoa Nguyen, Adel Larabi et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21272 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Rural areas exhibit low population density and highly variable connectivity needs shaped by both household usage and field operations such as planting, harvesting, and mining.
**Approach:** To address these underexplored challenges, we propose an AI driven Intent Aware Satellite Integrated Access and Backhaul (IAB) approach to connect rural areas. In our proposal, a large language model (LLM) translates users' intents into explicit network requirements.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** During intensive outdoor activities, household fixed networks may remain underutilized, resulting in inefficient resource use and unnecessary energy consumption. The simulation results show that our approach significantly reduces energy consumption while maximizing energy efficiency.
