# WiFo-E: A Scalable Wireless Foundation Model for End-to-End FDD Precoding in Communication Networks
Weibo Wen, Shijian Gao, Haotian Zhang, Xiang Cheng et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09186 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Accurate precoding in massive multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) frequency-division duplexing (FDD) systems relies on efficient channel state information (CSI) acquisition. **Approach:** To overcome this limitation, we introduce WiFo-E, a wireless foundation model designed for scalable end-to-end precoding.
**Tools and scale:** Extensive simulations demonstrate that WiFo-E outperforms conventional per-configuration training and shows strong generalization to unseen system configurations, providing a flexible and efficient foundation for adaptive massive MIMO precoding.
