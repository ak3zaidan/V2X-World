# Trust Management in Vehicular Ad hoc NETwork
Ryma Abassi · Advances in Information Security, Privacy, and Ethics 2018 · https://doi.org/10.4018/978-1-5225-5736-4.ch003 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** A vehicular ad hoc network (VANET) is a self-organized network that can be formed by connecting vehicles equipped with on-board units.
**Approach:** Two types of communications are provided in VANET: vehicular-to-vehicular and vehicular-to-infrastructure. In the first communication type, vehicles communicate directly, whereas in V2I, vehicles communicate through routers called road side units (RSU).
