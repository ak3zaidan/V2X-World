# i2Nav-Robot: A Large-Scale Indoor-Outdoor Robot Dataset for Multi-Sensor Fusion Navigation
Hailiang Tang, Tisheng Zhang, Liqiang Wang, Xin Ding, Man Yuan, Zhiyu Xiang et al. · arXiv 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.11485 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Accurate and reliable navigation is crucial for autonomous unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs).
**Approach:** However, current UGV datasets fall short in meeting the demands for advancing navigation techniques due to limitations in sensor configuration, time synchronization, ground truth, and scenario diversity. Hence, we present i2Nav-Robot, a large-scale dataset designed for multi-sensor fusion navigation in indoor-outdoor environments.
