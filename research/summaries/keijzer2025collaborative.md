# Collaborative Vehicle Platoons With Guaranteed Safety Against Cyber-Attacks
Twan Keijzer, Paula Chanfreut, José María Maestre, Riccardo Maria Giorgio Ferrari · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2024.3503370 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cyber-attacks on platoon communication threaten safety.
**Approach:** Topology-switching coalitional MPC with reduced-order unknown input observer to detect, isolate and disable attacked links; robustness to undetected attacks.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical guarantees and simulation.
**Results:** Relaxed string stability and guaranteed crash safety.
