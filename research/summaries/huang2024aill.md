# AILL-IDS: An Automatic Incremental Lifetime Learning Intrusion Detection System for Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Yunfan Huang, Maode Ma · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2024.3510584 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) play a critical role in enabling communication among intelligent vehicles, yet their dynamic and decentralized nature makes them highly vulnerable to cyber-attacks. Traditional Intrusion Detection Systems (IDSs) provide limited defense against these evolving threats, as they rely on static rules or machine learning (ML) models that lack the capacity for real-time updates.
**Approach:** The Incremental Lifetime Learning IDS (ILL-IDS) was introduced to address this limitation by enabling adaptive learning of new attack types. In response, this study proposes the Automatic Incremental Lifetime Learning IDS (AILL-IDS), a novel IDS framework that significantly reduces the need for labeled data through incremental semi-supervised learning.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in abstract.
**Results:** Experimental results demonstrate that AILL-IDS achieves a high detection rate of 0.97 and an average F1 score of 0.90, using only 5.5% labeled data, thereby offering an efficient and scalable solution for securing VANETs against emerging cyber threats.
