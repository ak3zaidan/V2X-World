# LLM-Based Telemetry Repair and Fault Detection in V2X Networks with Digital Twin Guidance
Bishmita Hazarika, Keshav Singh, Berk Canberk, Trung Q. Duong · GLOBECOM 2025 - 2025 IEEE Global Communications Conference 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/globecom59602.2025.11432308 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Wireless disruptions create telemetry gaps that degrade V2X analytics and fault detection.
**Approach:** LLM with retrieval-augmented generation and DT-based environmental priors to repair telemetry; federated continual learning for fault classification.
**Tools and scale:** Real driving datasets with simulated wireless impairments.
**Results:** Improved reconstruction fidelity, reduced multi-step gap degradation, stable classifiers (no numbers in abstract).
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Not stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — LLM applied to V2X network fault analysis, related to our LLM analysis agent direction.
**Novelty implications:** LLMs for V2X telemetry/fault analysis exist; LLM-driven cross-layer root-cause analysis over PKI+radio+traffic ground truth remains open.
