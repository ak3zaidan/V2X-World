# Fuzzy Elliptic Curve Cryptography-Based Privacy-Preserving Message Authentication and PROMETHEE Trust Management Scheme for Fog-Enabled VANETs
Govindaraju Kaladi, N. Sreenath · IETE Journal of Research 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1080/03772063.2025.2531951 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs) support intelligent transportation but face challenges like authentication attacks and high communication delays.
**Approach:** This paper proposes a Fuzzy Elliptic Curve Cryptography-based Privacy-Preserving Message Authentication and PROMETHEE-based Trust Management (FECCPTM) scheme for fog-enabled VANETs. The approach enhances node and message authentication while ensuring data privacy in fog-enabled environments.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: ECC
**Results:** Simulation results show that FECCPTM improves packet delivery rate by 19.82%, detection rate by 21.28%, reduces delay by 17.96%, and lowers control packet transmission by 19.62% compared to baseline methods. These improvements confirm FECCPTM as a lightweight, privacy-preserving solution for secure message and node authentication in dynamic vehicular environments.
