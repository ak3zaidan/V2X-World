# Building Trust on the Move: A Cryptographic Framework for Secure Data Exchange in Intelligent Vehicular Networks
Susan Zehra, Stephan Olariu, Samy El-Tawab · ACM Symposium on Solid Modeling and Applications 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/sm69703.2026.11614082 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** As vehicles evolve into mobile computers, future transportation systems will depend on secure data sharing and collaborative computation between cars and roadside infrastructure. Ensuring security, privacy, and accountability in such dynamic networks is challenging because vehicles continuously join, leave, and relay data under intermittent connectivity.
**Approach:** This paper introduces VERA-VANET (Verifiable Encrypted Routing and Attestation), a cryptographic protocol that makes vehicular communication both secure and mathematically verifiable.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** VERA-VANET combines asymmetric encryption, aggregate signatures, and zero-knowledge attestations, and integrates with IEEE 802.11p / C-V2X using trusted hardware for secure key storage.
