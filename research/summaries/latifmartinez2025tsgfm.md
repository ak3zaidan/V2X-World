# TSGFM - Towards a Graph Foundation Model for Time Series Analysis in Network Monitoring
Hamid Latif-Martínez, Pedro Casas, José Suárez-Varela, Albert Cabellos-Aparicio et al. · 2025 9th Network Traffic Measurement and Analysis Conference (TMA) 2025 · https://doi.org/10.23919/tma66427.2025.11096996 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Zero-shot analysis of network monitoring time series.
**Approach:** TSGFM spatiotemporal GNN foundation model pretrained on multi-domain series.
**Tools and scale:** Five datasets, seven zero-shot scenarios, five baselines.
**Results:** Best in six of seven scenarios; at least 18% better on network monitoring.
