# Digital Twin-Driven Asymmetric Driving Aggressiveness Assessment for Connected Autonomous Vehicles
Wen Hu, Zejian Deng, Yang Wu, Bangji Zhang, Penghao Li, Dongpu Cao · 2023 IEEE 3rd International Conference on Digital Twins and Parallel Intelligence (DTPI) 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1109/dtpi59677.2023.10365324 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Accurately evaluating the driving aggressiveness could greatly improve the driving efficiency for connected autonomous vehicles (CAV) in the mixed traffic.
**Approach:** Current aggressiveness evaluation methods merely focus on the collision probability, inadequately assessing hazard levels due to symmetry between interactive vehicles. Meanwhile, existing methods operate on a microcosmic scale, guiding motion planning within short time.
