# On the Efficiency of Pairing-Based Authentication for Connected Vehicles: Time is Not on Our Side!
Mir Ali Rezazadeh Baee, L. Simpson, Xavier Boyen, E. Foo et al. · IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tifs.2021.3087359 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Are pairing-based short signatures efficient enough for VANETs?
**Approach:** Implements and evaluates four pairing-based short signature schemes over BLS and BN curves.
**Tools and scale:** Theoretical and experimental analysis.
**Results:** Pairing-based schemes too slow for VANET timing ("time is not on our side").

- None stated.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Verification-latency feasibility of signature schemes, relevant to verification policy.
**Novelty implications:** None.
