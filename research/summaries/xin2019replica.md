# Replica attack detection method for vehicular ad hoc networks with sequential trajectory segment
Yan Xin, Xia Feng · Int. J. Distributed Sens. Networks 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1177/1550147719827500 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In vehicular ad hoc networks, attackers can disguise as replicas of legitimate vehicles by cracking or colluding and then use the identity replicas in a malicious way.
**Approach:** Not only the generation of replicas itself poses an aggressive behavior, but also the replicas can enable other insider attacks, such as denial of service, information interception, and replay attack. To solve this issue, researchers have presented many solutions in wireless sensor network or in mobile ad hoc networks.

**Results:** No quantitative results stated in the abstract.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- However, majority of current schemes are not good at dealing with conspiracy replicas or lack of considering peculiar characteristics of high mobility of vehicles.
