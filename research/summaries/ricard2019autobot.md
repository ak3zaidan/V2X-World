# Autobot: An Emulation Environment For Cellular Vehicular Communications
Quentin Ricard, Philippe Owezarski · 2019 IEEE/ACM 23rd International Symposium on Distributed Simulation and Real Time Applications (DS-RT) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/ds-rt47707.2019.8958691 · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** Real-time emulation of cellular vehicular communications.
**Approach:** Autobot using NetEm with latency distributions from measurements.
**Tools and scale:** NetEm; server and laptop setups.
**Results:** Up to 140 vehicles at 10 Hz (server) and 60 at 8 Hz (laptop); latency emulated fairly accurately.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- NetEm delay variation limits
- extend to infotainment and OTA updates
- fluctuating bandwidth from dataset.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
