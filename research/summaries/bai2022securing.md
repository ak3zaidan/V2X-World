# Securing Signal-free Intersections against Strategic Jamming Attacks: A Macroscopic Approach
Yumeng Bai, Saurabh Amin, Xudong Wang, Li Jin · 2022 IEEE 61st Conference on Decision and Control (CDC) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/cdc51059.2022.9992788 · relevance: medium · basis: full text

**Problem:** Security-by-design of signal-free CAV intersections against strategic jamming.
**Approach:** Macroscopic fluid model; jamming raises V2I latency, triggering safety mode and lowering saturation rate; attacker-operator security game.
**Tools and scale:** Analytical fluid queue model with numerical example; no packet-level simulator.
**Results:** Derives unique Nash equilibria and regime diagrams for investment in nominal discharge versus recovery capability depending on attack and recovery costs.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None explicit in extracted sections.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — partial: links V2X jamming to traffic delay; ours does this at microscopic and packet level.
**Novelty implications:** Game-theoretic jamming-vs-recovery investment at intersections is done analytically; microscopic validation remains open.
