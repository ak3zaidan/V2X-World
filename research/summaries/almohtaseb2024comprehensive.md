# A Comprehensive Review of VANET Attacks: Predictive Models, Vulnerability Management, and Defense Selection
Abeer Al-Mohtaseb, Ali Qasim Hanoon, Ghassan Samara, Essam Al Daoud, Omar Khair Alla Alidmat, Radwan M. Batyha et al. · International Arab Conference on Information Technology (ACIT) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/acit62805.2024.10877115 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad Hoc Network (VANET) is a critical network in the Internet of Things (IoT) era, as it plays a key role in enhancing road safety by broadcasting alerts between vehicles to prevent accidents and save lives.
**Approach:** However, due to VANET's open structure, limited infrastructure, and various security vulnerabilities, messages can be intercepted, forged, or disrupted by malicious attacks. This paper proposes an "operation room" framework designed to enhance VANET security by addressing these threats.
