# Detection and identification of attacks in Vehicular Ad-Hoc NETwork
Khaoula Jeffane, Khalil Ibrahimi · 2016 International Conference on Wireless Networks and Mobile Communications (WINCOM) 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wincom.2016.7777191 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-hoc NETwork (VANET) is a particular type of the Mobile Ad-hoc NETworks (MANET) and is developed to provide communications in a group of vehicles in range of each other and between vehicles and fixed equipments (Road Side Unit) within a communication range, usually called equipment of the road. This network is very sensible to safety problem.
**Approach:** In this work, a new mechanism is proposed to study the safety problem in VANET networks. Our proposed solution is used to detect and to identify DoS attacks by using the values of packet delivery ratio (PDR) metric.
**Tools and scale:** IEEE 802.11p/DSRC (mentioned in abstract)
**Results:** Simulation results show the acceptable performance.
