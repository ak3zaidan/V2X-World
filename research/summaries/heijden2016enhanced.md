# Enhanced Position Verification for VANETs Using Subjective Logic
R. V. D. Heijden, Ala’a M. Al-Momani, Frank Kargl, O. Abu-Sharkh · IEEE Vehicular Technology Conference 2016 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtcfall.2016.788100 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** 1609.2 ensures integrity but not correctness of position contents.
**Approach:** Two enhanced position verification mechanisms fused with subjective logic.
**Tools and scale:** VEINS; varied traffic density, attacker types and fractions.
**Results:** As effective as existing approaches without tailoring; thresholds 400 m and 350 m chosen.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Future: integrate additional mechanisms and trust; study impact of intrusion response (they only detect and do not change neighbour tables).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - MBD inside Veins simulation; explicitly leaves intrusion response (closed loop) open.
**Novelty implications:** Supports novelty of closed-loop response evaluation (authors list it as future work).
