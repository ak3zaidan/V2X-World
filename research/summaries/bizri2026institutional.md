# Institutional Approaches to Post-Quantum Cryptography: A Comparative Analysis of Migration Frameworks
Mustafa El Bizri, Ahmad M. El-Hajj, Layth Sliman, A. M. Haidar · IEEE Access 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2025.3650465 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The advancement of quantum computing is increasingly recognized as a significant threat to classical public-key cryptographic systems, triggering a global shift toward Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC).
**Approach:** This article presents a topical survey and comparative analysis of the transition strategies adopted by leading cybersecurity agencies—including National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), Agence Nationale de la Sécurité des Systémes d’Information (ANSSI), Bundesamt für Sicherheit in der Informationstechnik (BSI), Cryptography Research and Evaluation Committees (CRYPTREC), and European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA). To enhance analytical depth, the study introduces the Institutional–Sectoral PQC Migration Convergence Framework, linking institutional pillars to sectoral readiness for reproducible comparative assessment.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** These insights support the development of secure, interoperable, and future-proof cryptographic infrastructures in the quantum era, operationalized via the proposed 10-step migration playbook.
