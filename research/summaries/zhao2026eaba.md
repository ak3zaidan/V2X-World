# EABA: Edge-Assisted Batch Authentication for Vehicular Cooperative Perception
Pin-Can Zhao, Xin-Rui Zhang, Yi-Li Tang, F. Yu · ICC 2026 - IEEE International Conference on Communications 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icc59461.2026.11586978 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cooperative perception enables connected and autonomous vehicles (CAVs) to enhance environmental awareness by sharing sensor data with neighboring vehicles. However, authenticating high-frequency perception messages poses significant challenges due to stringent latency requirements and increasing vehicle density.
**Approach:** This paper presents an Edge-Assisted Batch Authentication (EABA) framework that leverages roadside units (RSUs) as trusted edge authenticators to overcome these limitations.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** A comprehensive security analysis demonstrates the scheme’s ability to preserve message authenticity, vehicle privacy, and batch-level verifiability while defending against common vehicular network attacks. Performance evaluation reveals that EABA significantly reduces authentication overhead, achieving the latency and throughput requirements necessary for real-time cooperative perception.
