# A Framework to Systematically Analyse the Trustworthiness of Nodes for Securing IoV Interactions
Indu Bhardwaj, S. Khara, Priestly B. Shan · Scalable Computing : Practice and Experience 2020 · https://doi.org/10.12694/scpe.v21i3.1743 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Trust plays essential role in any securing communications between Vehicles in IOV.
**Approach:** This motivated us to design a trust model for IoV communication. In this paper, we initially review literature on IoV and Trust and present a hybrid trust model that separates the malicious and trusted nodes to secure the interaction of vehicle in IOV.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** Result depicts that PDR of trusted node is 0.63 that is much higher than the PDR of malicious node that is 0.15.
