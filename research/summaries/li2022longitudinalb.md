# Longitudinal speed tracking control for a electric connected vehicle with actuator saturation subject to a replay attack
Xiang Li, Xiangyang Xu, Lu Chen et al. · Research Square 2022 · https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1497118/v1 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract In this paper, a delay-tolerable and anti-windup control synthesis technique is proposed.
**Approach:** Based on above problems, a nominal controller satisfying energy-to-peak performance considering the attack-induced delays is designed. Finally, the effectiveness and superiority of the proposed method based on Matlab Simulink and hardware-in-the-loop (HiL) test platform are verified.
