# Digital Twin-Aided Learning to Enable Robust Beamforming: Limited Feedback Meets Deep Generative Models
Ying Li, Kai Li, Lei Cheng, Qingjiang Shi, Zhi-Quan Luo · 2021 IEEE 22nd International Workshop on Signal Processing Advances in Wireless Communications (SPAWC) 2021 · https://doi.org/10.1109/spawc51858.2021.9593108 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In massive multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) systems, robust beamforming is a key technology that alleviates multi-user interference under channel estimation errors.
**Approach:** Recently, stochastic weighted minimum mean square error (WMMSE) algorithm was proposed to achieve robust risk-neutral beamforming, assuming the knowledge of channel probability distribution. However, this information is difficult to acquire especially in the fifth generation new radio (5G NR) frequency division duplex (FDD) systems with limited feedback.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: 5G, NR.
**Results:** Numerical results are presented to show the excellent performance of the proposed algorithm in terms of both channel generation and robust beamforming.
