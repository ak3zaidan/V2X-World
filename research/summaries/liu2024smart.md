# A Smart Data-Driven Multi-Level Synchronous Digital Twin Model for Vehicle-Assisted Driving
Jianhang Liu, Yuxiang Wang, Wenwen Gong, Hanwen Liu, Yanwei Xu, Huaizhen Kou · IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tce.2023.3341227 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Recently, the method of non-intelligent vehicles, acting as information consumers, receiving integrated data shared to the cloud by mobile edge nodes and RSUs has garnered significant interest for its potential to enhance road safety.
**Approach:** The method can assist the customers in achieving advanced driver assistance system functionalities. Nonetheless, challenges such as resource constraints at RSUs, long communication distances between the cloud and vehicles, and frequent data interactions make it difficult to meet the strict latency requirements in highly mobile and complex traffic environments.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** We make use of multi-level synchronous deduction along with high-level proactive updating to reduce update latency significantly so that it can meet the timeliness requirements for cyber-physical services in differentiated scenario.
