# Lattice-Based Lightweight Quantum Resistant Scheme in 5G-Enabled Vehicular Networks
Z. Al-Mekhlafi, Mahmood A. Al-Shareeda, S. Manickam, B. Mohammed, Amjad Qtaish · Mathematics 2023 · https://doi.org/10.3390/math11020399 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Quantum-vulnerable, heavy, RSU-dependent authentication in 5G vehicular networks.
**Approach:** Lattice scheme using matrix multiplication, no RSU-aided joining.
**Tools and scale:** Security and performance analysis.
**Results:** Favourable performance.
