# Dynamic Mix-Zone for Location Privacy in Vehicular Networks
Bidi Ying, Dimitrios Makrakis, Hussein T. Mouftah · IEEE Communications Letters 2013 · https://doi.org/10.1109/lcomm.2013.070113.122816 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Location privacy is an important issue in vehicular networks since knowledge of a vehicle's location can result in leakage of sensitive information.
**Approach:** In this paper, we propose a method, named Dynamic Mix-zone for Location Privacy (DMLP). With DMLP, a vehicle' s mix-zone is dynamically formed at the time the vehicle requests it.
