# An Anti-Jamming Game in VANET Platoon with Reinforcement Learning
Yexian Fan, Xingyu Xiao, Wei Feng · 2018 IEEE International Conference on Consumer Electronics-Taiwan (ICCE-TW) 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icce-china.2018.8448435 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper proposes a game strategy of anti-jamming power control-based Dyna-Q reinforcement learning to counter the smart jamming attacks suffered by the periodic beacons in Vehicular ad-hoc networks(VANETs) platoon.
**Approach:** Simulation results indicate that without knowing the radio channel model of the jammer, the proposed algorithm can not only realize the vehicle maximize efficiency and improve the signal-to-interference-plus-noise(SINR) of radio channel but also attain better convergence effects compared with a Q-learning based scheme.
