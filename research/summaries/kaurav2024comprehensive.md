# A comprehensive verification of the header format and bandwidth utilization to detect distributed denial of service attack in vehicular ad hoc network
Arun Singh Kaurav, Kandala Kalyana Srinivas · International Journal of Power Electronics and Drive Systems/International Journal of Electrical and Computer Engineering 2024 · https://doi.org/10.11591/ijece.v14i6.pp6538-6550 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular ad hoc network (VANET) is a promising technology for controlling traffic on roads.
**Approach:** In order to defend against distributed denial of service DDoS attacks, we propose a comprehensive verification header format bandwidth detection (CVHB) in VANET.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The result of CVHB is showing improvement as compared to multilayer distributed self-organizing maps (MSOM) in VANET.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- "The most important challenge in VANET is its security from malicious vehicles."
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — addresses misbehaviour/attack detection (direction 1 area), but per the abstract without an integrated SCMS backend, closed-loop revocation, per-message joint ground truth or counterfactual reruns.
**Novelty implications:** Does not rule out any candidate direction on the evidence of the abstract. Leaves open quantifying this in a closed loop with real SCMS revocation and traffic safety outcomes.
