# Quantum resistant blockchain and deep learning revolutionize secure communications for autonomous vehicles
Reyadh Alluhaibi · Scientific Reports 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-28938-y · relevance: high · basis: full text

**Problem:** AV networks face quantum threats to cryptography.
**Approach:** Lattice-based PQ communication, blockchain data sharing with zk-SNARKs, CNN/RNN anomaly detection.
**Tools and scale:** Experiments largely on synthetic data.
**Results:** Detection accuracy 95.2%, precision 94.1%, recall 93.5%; blockchain 150 tx/s, 1.5 s block validation.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Computational cost.
- Reliance on synthetic data; needs large-scale real-world validation.
- Future work: efficiency, real vehicular data, integration with legacy ITS.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — blockchain + DL framework.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
