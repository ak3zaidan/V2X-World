# Deep Learning-Based QoE Prediction for Streaming Services in Mobile Networks
Huang Gan, Ozgur Ercetin, Hakan Gokcesu, Gokhan Kalem · 2022 18th International Conference on Wireless and Mobile Computing, Networking and Communications (WiMob) 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1109/wimob55322.2022.9941672 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Video streaming accounts for the most of the global Internet traffic and providing a high user Quality of Experience (QoE) is considered an essential target for mobile network operators (MNOs).
**Approach:** QoE strongly depends on network Quality of Service (QoS) parameters. In this work, we use real-world network traces obtained from a major cellular operator in Turkey to establish a mapping from network side parameters to the user QoE.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Results:** The experimental results demonstrate that the proposed model-aided deep learning model can guarantee higher prediction accuracy compared to predictions only relying on mathematical models. We also demonstrate that even though a trained model cannot be directly transferred from one geographical area to another, they significantly reduce the volume of required training when used for prediction in a new area.
