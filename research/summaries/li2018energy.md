# Energy-Efficient Location Privacy Preserving in Vehicular Networks Using Social Intimate Fogs
Gaolei Li, Qiaolun Zhang, Jianhua Li, Jun Wu et al. · IEEE Access 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2018.2859344 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Although the ways to protect vehicular location privacy have been actively studied in recent years, the locations of vehicles are frequently submitted for authentication during accessing location-based services (LBS), which makes it easier for attackers to launch attacks by threaten the location privacy of vehicles.
**Approach:** Moreover, the rapid deployment of electric vehicles requires location privacy-preserving to be more energy-efficient. In this paper, we proposed a novel energy-efficient location privacy preserving (ELPP) scheme in vehicular networks using social intimate fogs (SIFs).
