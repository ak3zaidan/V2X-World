Hongjuan Li, Hui Kang, Jiahui Li, Geng Sun et al. · arXiv 2026 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.17817 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** The rapid expansion of low-altitude economy Internet of Things (LAE-IoT) networks has created unprecedented security challenges due to dynamic three-dimensional mobility patterns, distributed autonomous operations, and severe resource constraints.
