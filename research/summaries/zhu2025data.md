# Data-driven prescribed performance trajectory tracking for nonholonomic vehicle under FDI attacks
Liqiu Zhu, Shengyue Zhou, Yue Hong, An-Yang Lu · Journal of Control and Decision 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1080/23307706.2025.2546565 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** This paper investigates the issue of prescribed performance trajectory tracking for nonholonomic vehicle subject to unknown factors under false data injection (FDI) attacks.
**Approach:** The model-free adaptive control (MFAC) scheme is extended to address the tracking problem of an unknown system with multidimensional states under prescribed performance. First, to drive the vehicle to follow the reference trajectory with specified dynamic and static performance, prescribed performance functions with exponential convergence properties are introduced to impose constraints on the distance error and orientation error.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: attacks on CAV control).
**Results:** Subsequently, an error converter is designed to map the error constraints into the real number domain, thereby improving vehicle robustness against FDI attacks. Finally, simulation results demonstrate that the proposed method enables the vehicle to track the reference trajectory with the prescribed performance under the designed expected velocity.
