# An Approach To Detect The Wormhole Attack In Vehicular Adhoc Networks
Harbir Kaur, Sanjay Batish, Arvind Kakaria · International Journal of Smart Sensor and Adhoc Network 2012 · https://doi.org/10.47893/ijssan.2012.1143 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** In VANET there is no centralized infrastructure due to which it is vulnerable to various security attacks . One of such attack is wormhole attack, it enables an attacker to capture packets at one location and tunnels them to another location making a wormhole in-between the legitimate nodes of the network.
**Approach:** In this paper we proposes a method in which we use decision packets to detect the wormhole nodes in the network and for maintaining the integrity of the packets we compute hash value of each packet.
