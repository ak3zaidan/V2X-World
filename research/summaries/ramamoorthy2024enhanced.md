# An Enhanced Location-Aided Ant Colony Routing for Secure Communication in Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
Raghu Ramamoorthy · Human-Centric Intelligent Systems 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s44230-023-00059-7 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** Seamless secure data transmission in VANETs.
**Approach:** ELAACR: location-aided group-signature key management plus ant colony routing.
**Tools and scale:** NS 2.35, MOVE, SUMO 0.22; Hosur-Bengaluru highway.
**Results:** Throughput 640 Kbps, PLR 0.018%, PDR about 98%.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Designed for highways; urban signals and non-uniform speeds need refinement.
**Overlap with our engine:** none.
**Novelty implications:** No effect.
