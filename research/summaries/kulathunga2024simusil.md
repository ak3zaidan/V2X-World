# SimuSIL: A Software-in-the-Loop Simulator for Cooperative Driving in Intelligent Transportation Systems
Ranga Kulathunga, Osanda De Silva, Dilshani Abeywickrama, Tharindu Vithanage, Dileeka Dias · 2024 Moratuwa Engineering Research Conference (MERCon) 2024 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mercon63886.2024.10688793 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Realistic vehicle dynamics in discrete-step V2X simulation.
**Approach:** SimuSIL: Veins extended with Webots ECU models.
**Tools and scale:** Veins, SUMO, OMNeT++, Webots.
**Results:** Captures packet-loss anomalies in car following; eco-driving fuel and CO2 shown.

- Not stated.
