# A certificateless aggregate signature scheme for healthcare wireless sensor network
Pankaj Kumar, S. Kumari, V. Sharma, A. Sangaiah et al. · Sustainable Computing: Informatics and Systems 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.suscom.2017.09.002 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Abstract Healthcare industry is one of the areas where wireless sensor network provides a lot of opportunities. Online data sharing in healthcare industry not only increases the efficiency but also reduces the time constraints.
**Approach:** In the healthcare wireless sensor network, patient’s report is available online for health professionals without any delay after patient’s checkup.
**Tools and scale:** We construct a certificateless aggregate signature scheme and prove the security of constructed scheme by using widely-accepted Random Oracle Model under the computationally hard Diffie-Hellman assumption.
