# Resilient Federated Learning for Vehicular Networks: A Digital Twin and Blockchain-Empowered Approach
Jian Li, Chuntao Zheng, Ziyao Chen · Future Internet 2025 · https://doi.org/10.3390/fi17110505 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Federated learning (FL) is a foundational technology for enabling collaborative intelligence in vehicular edge computing (VEC).
**Approach:** However, the volatile network topology caused by high vehicle mobility and the profound security risks of model poisoning attacks severely undermine its practical deployment. This paper introduces DTB-FL, a novel framework that synergistically integrates digital twin (DT) and blockchain technologies to establish a secure and efficient learning paradigm.
**Tools and scale:** Mentioned in abstract: simulations.
**Results:** Extensive simulations demonstrate that DTB-FL accelerates model convergence by 43% compared to FedAvg and maintains 75% accuracy under poisoning attacks even when 40% of participants are malicious—a scenario where baseline FL methods degrade to below 40% accuracy.
