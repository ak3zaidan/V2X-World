# A traceable and attribute–updatable data sharing scheme with privacy preserving based on attribute tree for cloud–assisted VANETs
Han Luo, Chang Liu, Menglong Qi, Chengzhi Yu et al. · Journal of King Saud University: Computer and Information Sciences 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s44443-026-00769-5 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Cloud-assisted Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks (VANETs) facilitate efficient data sharing by leveraging cloud for storing critical traffic information. However, ensuring secure, privacy preserving, and accountable data access with dynamic attribute updates in such environments remains challenging.
**Approach:** Therefore, this paper proposes a Traceable and Attribute-Updatable Data Sharing Scheme (TAUDS) with privacy preserving based on attribute tree for cloud-assisted VANETs. We design an attribute update mechanism via dynamic leaf node adjustments on the attribute tree.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract; appears to be analytical/cryptographic or review work.
**Results:** It first performs an attribute-based binary search to narrow the suspect user set, then conducts identity-based binary localization to precisely identify malicious users, which reduces tracing overhead. For privacy protection, TAUDS employs symmetric encryption to keep user identities confidential, and uses a linear secret sharing scheme to achieve policy hiding.
