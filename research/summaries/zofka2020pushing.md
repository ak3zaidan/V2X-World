# Pushing ROS towards the Dark Side: A ROS-based Co-Simulation Architecture for Mixed-Reality Test Systems for Autonomous Vehicles
Marc Rene Zofka, Lars Tottel, Maximilian Zipfl, Marc Heinrich et al. · 2020 IEEE International Conference on Multisensor Fusion and Integration for Intelligent Systems (MFI) 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1109/mfi49285.2020.9235238 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Validation and verification of autonomous vehicles is still an unsolved problem.
**Approach:** However, these models must be linked together for an extensive and uniform mapping of real world traffic domain for HAD performance assessment.Hereby, we propose the concept of a co-simulation architecture built upon the Robot Operating System (ROS) for both coupling and for integration of different domain expert models, immersion and stimulation of real pedestrians as well as AD systems into a common test system.
**Tools and scale:** Mentions: simulation (details not in abstract).
**Results:** We demonstrate the applicability of the ROS powered co-simulation by coupling behavior models in our mixed reality environment.
