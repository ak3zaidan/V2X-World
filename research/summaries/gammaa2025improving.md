# Improving VANET Simulation Channel Model in an Urban Environment via Calibration Using Real-World Communication Data
Ahmed Gammaa, Seyedmehdi Khaleghian, T. Tran, Mina Sartipi · arXiv.org 2025 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.07954 · relevance: high · basis: full text (extracted sections)

**Problem:** VANET simulators must be calibrated to reproduce real DSRC packet loss.
**Approach:** Genetic-algorithm calibration of Veins channel propagation and PHY parameters against field data.
**Tools and scale:** OMNeT++/Veins; DSRC urban testbed in downtown Chattanooga, TN.
**Results:** Calibrated model reproduces packet delivery patterns significantly better than Veins defaults.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Recommendations for future research in conclusion (not extracted).
**Overlap with our engine:** partial - calibration of a Veins DSRC model to real data; our engine matches Veins PHY/MAC, so this informs our validation.
**Novelty implications:** Calibrated DSRC simulation exists; no effect on security claims.
