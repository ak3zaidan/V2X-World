# Crypto-Agile FPGA Architecture with Single-Cycle Switching for OFDM-Based Vehicular Networks
Mahmoud Elomda, Ahmed A. Ibrahim, M. Abdelaziz · Signals 2026 · https://doi.org/10.3390/signals7020038 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Crypto agility inside OFDM V2X baseband.
**Approach:** Kintex-7 FPGA with context-aware cipher pre-selection and single-cycle switching (AES-128 synthesised; ASCON, Keccak in RTL simulation). (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** FPGA.
**Results:** Sub-microsecond crypto latency with moderate resources.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Only AES-128 synthesised; ASCON and Keccak validated in RTL simulation only.
**Overlap with our engine:** none — hardware.
**Novelty implications:** No impact.
