# Privacy-Preserving Authentication Based on Pseudonyms and Secret Sharing for VANET
Ye Xu, Feng-Ying Li, Bin Cao · Computers, Communications and IT Applications Conference 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/comcomap46287.2019.9018809 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** CPPA with frame-attack prevention and efficient revocation.
**Approach:** CPPT: hash-chain pseudonyms with Shamir (t,n) secret sharing; CRL proportional to revoked vehicles.
**Tools and scale:** Simulations.
**Results:** Outperforms ECPP, DCS, Hybrid, EMAP in revocation, update and authentication overhead.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- None stated in the abstract.
**Overlap with our engine:** partial — Revocation overhead independent of pseudonym count (as SCMS linkage achieves).
**Novelty implications:** None.
