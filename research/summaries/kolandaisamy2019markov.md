# Markov Chain Based Ant Colony Approach for Mitigating DDoS Attacks Using Integrated Vehicle Mode Analysis in VANET
Raenu Kolandaisamy, Rafidah Md Noor, Muhammad Reza Z’aba, Ismail Ahmedy et al. · IEEE International Conference on Energy, Systems and Information Processing (ICESIP) 2019 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icesip46348.2019.8938253 · relevance: medium · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANET) remains receiving increasing attention in arrears to the multiplicity of services that they existent. VANET is mostly measured to present security associated traffic and infotainment services.
**Approach:** A novel scheme attack detection using vehicle mode analysis in Exploratory Based Ant Colony Approach (EBACA) for VANET is proposed. By getting a series of messages from a vehicle, the receiving vehicles are presented with an adequate amount of data that permits a significant analysis.
