# A Decentralized, Secure, and Reliable Vehicle Platoon Formation With Privacy Protection for Autonomous Vehicles
Rabia Khan, Amjad Mehmood, Houbing Song, Carsten Maple · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1109/tits.2025.3537765 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Vehicle platooning enables vehicles to drive cooperatively on highways and motorways.
**Approach:** This is to weigh numerous benefits such as lesser fuel consumption, safe drive and better road utilization. The main research focus of vehicle platoons is secure and dynamic platoon formation and ensuring privacy of vehicular data.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the available text unless given above (topic: location privacy).
**Results:** Platoon formation time has been compared with other benchmarks and shows better results. While the performance against DDoS and impersonation attacks in comparison to the benchmarks is also improved.
