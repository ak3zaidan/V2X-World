# Lattice-Based Revocable Certificateless Signature
Ying-Hao Hung, Yuh-Min Tseng, Sen-Shan Huang · Symmetry 2017 · https://doi.org/10.3390/sym9100242 · relevance: low · basis: abstract

**Problem:** Certificateless signatures (CLS) are noticeable because they may resolve the key escrow problem in ID-based signatures and break away the management problem regarding certificate in conventional signatures.
**Approach:** However, no study on addressing the revocation problem in the existing lattice-based CLS schemes is presented. In this paper, we focus on the revocation issue and present the first revocable CLS (RCLS) scheme over lattices.
**Tools and scale:** Not stated in the abstract beyond the above; no traffic simulator, radio stack or SCMS-scale deployment is described.
**Results:** For post-quantum cryptography, lattice-based cryptography is significant due to its efficiency and security.
