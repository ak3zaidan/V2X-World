# EmuLab of Security Credential Management System (SCMS) for Vehicular Communications
Robert D. Mushrall, Matthew D. Furtado, Hong Liu · IEEE Vehicular Technology Conference 2018 · https://doi.org/10.1109/vtcfall.2018.8690778 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** SCMS needs a research platform; CRL distribution and PQ butterfly keys remain open.
**Approach:** SCMS Emulation Lab with functional SCMS framework; experiments on pseudonym certificates and self-healing after root CA compromise.
**Tools and scale:** Emulation lab (UMass Dartmouth).
**Results:** Not stated in the abstract.
**Limitations and future work (as stated by the authors):**
- Open challenges: efficient CRL distribution
- butterfly key expansion not post-quantum
**Overlap with our engine:** strong — A functional SCMS emulation framework for research, directly overlapping our SCMS backend.
**Novelty implications:** Cannot claim first SCMS research emulator; differentiator is traffic/radio coupling, validation and scale.
