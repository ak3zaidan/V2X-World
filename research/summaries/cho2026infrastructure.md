# Infrastructure-Free V2V Key Repair for Threshold BLS Signatures in Autonomous Vehicle Platoons
Sangwoo Cho, Woosang Im, Gihyeon Shin, Young-Sik Kim · International Conference on Ubiquitous and Future Networks 2026 · https://doi.org/10.1109/icufn69619.2026.11628640 · relevance: high · basis: abstract

**Problem:** RSU-based repair of lost threshold BLS shares in platoons lets the RSU reconstruct the master key.
**Approach:** V2V key repair by topology-aware local polynomial interpolation where helpers send repair tokens, not shares; information-theoretic privacy. (summarised from the abstract only)
**Tools and scale:** Monte Carlo, 5,000 trials, 5.9 GHz V2X channel model.
**Results:** 16.2x lower repair latency, 5.67x lower overhead, over 96% repair success under 30% churn and 30% loss.
