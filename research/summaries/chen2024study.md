# A Study on the Situation of Connected Car Patent Portfolios
Abel C. H. Chen, C. T. Chang · arXiv (Cornell University) 2024 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12810 · relevance: high · basis: full text (abstract, conclusion/future-work sections)

**Problem:** In recent years, the countries of the world have drafted the specifications of connected cars; for instance, the Security Credential Management System (SCMS) has been proposed by United States Department of Transportation (USDOT), and the Cooperative Intelligent Transportation System (C-ITS) Credential Management System (CCMS) has been proposed by European Union (EU).
**Approach:** Therefore, several companies have developed the technology and productions of connected cars based on specifications, and connected car patent portfolios have been proactively performed. Therefore, this study uses Patent Search System (PSS) to find and analyze the contents of patents for obtaining the innovation reports of connected cars according to patents.
