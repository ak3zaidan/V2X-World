Expectation checks across the settings catalogue¶

What the engine should do when a setting moves, written down before the run with its theory or source, and checked against same-seed runs. Two kinds of row:

  • Single-setting rows (crates/v2xw-engine/tests/expectations/*.yaml, 274 rows over 129 settings). Each row moves one published setting to an end of its recommended range, or to a literal value. It names a fingerprint metric, a direction, and optionally a magnitude band (changed / base) and a minimum relative change. It runs on the family scenarios/sensitivity/catalogue.yaml routes the setting to.
  • Interaction checks (crates/v2xw-engine/tests/expectations/interactions/*.yaml, 47 checks). Each is a 2 × 2 on one family, with all four runs on the same seed: the base, factor A alone, condition B alone, and both. A's effect without B (A − base) is compared with its effect under B (AB − B), in the metric's own units (Montgomery, Design and Analysis of Experiments 9e, 5.2). The verdict is dampens, amplifies, holds (same sign) or reverses, and A's own direction is checked too. Examples: density × DCC, message rate × signature size, data rate × frame size, demand × signal control, trucks × NLOSv blockage.

Run it:

  • v2xw sensitivity --expect [--only PREFIXES] [--cache FILE] [--markdown FILE] [--json]. It prints one PASS / FAIL / DEAD / REFUSED line per row, failures first, and exits non-zero on any failure. It plans only the perturbations the table names. That is about 7 minutes from an empty cache on two threads, and about 1 minute with the cache.
  • cargo test -p v2xw-engine --test sensitivity_expectations, the fast part:
  • every interaction's four scenarios pass the loader;
  • every row is planned;
  • the interaction judge goes red on a wrong expectation;
  • one interaction runs end to end.
  • -- --ignored runs the whole table.

Reading the results¶

A row that fails or is dead is either a family that does not exercise the setting (too little load, too few vehicles, a car-only fleet) or an open modelling question. Each row's source gives the theory the expectation comes from, and a corrected expectation keeps the measured value and the reason in its source. The rows that do not pass are listed under Failing at the end.
