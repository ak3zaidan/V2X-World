Configuration reference¶

Every setting a researcher or engineer varies to compare V2X protocols, with its scenario path, default, accepted range and source. Every row is a scenario field: the loader validates it, and the engine publishes it with a unit, label, explanation and status (settings.describe / crate::scenario::publish). The Studio settings window and the agent read it from there. A value outside its range, or a combination where the setting would have no effect, is refused when the scenario loads, with the field named in the error.

The ranges are the validate::BOUNDS rows, and the closed value sets are the validate::CHOICES rows.

How this compares with the reference simulators¶

The knobs below are the ones Veins (omnetpp.ini for Mac1609_4, PhyLayer80211p, Decider80211p, SimpleObstacleShadowing), Artery (Middleware, DccEntity, GeoNetworking), OpenCV2X (LteMacVUeMode4, LtePhyVUeMode4), ns-3 WAVE and NR-V2X (WaveHelper, YansWifiPhy, LogDistancePropagationLossModel, NakagamiPropagationLossModel, BsmApplication, nr-v2x SPS attributes) and SUMO (vType accel, decel, tau, minGap, lcCooperative) expose. A knob those tools expose that this engine does not is listed under Not configurable yet with its reason.

802.11p medium access (radio.mac)¶

This applies at radio.tiers.mac: medium or high, and is refused at abstract, which models no access. Broadcast OCB never doubles the window and never retries (EN 302 663 Annex C.4.2), so CWmax and a retry limit would change nothing and are not offered.

Further traffic fields: free-speed factor and spread, start-up reaction, queue start-up delay, per-class drivers (truck, bus, motorcycle, bicycle), junction gaps and turning speed, kerbside stops, OSM signal cycle, all-red and pedestrian timing, and GNSS receiver settings (actors.vehicles.gnss). config-inventory-traffic.md lists them with their defaults, ranges and sources, together with every parameter left hard-coded and the reason.

The IDM delta exponent and the other model internals are parameters of the chosen model: actors.vehicles.driving.car_following.params, published from the model's card. actors.vehicles.drivers is refused with mobility.provider: sumo, since SUMO's vType holds those drivers.

Not configurable yet¶

Knob Why it is not offered
802.11p 20 MHz channels The PHY, the error model and the sensitivity tables are 10 MHz, and 802.11p is refused on a wider channel. A 20 MHz PHY needs its own timing and PER curves.
CWmax, retry limit (802.11p) Broadcast OCB has no ACK. CW never doubles and nothing is retried (EN 302 663 Annex C.4.2), so these would change nothing.
CWmin/AIFSN for be/bk Safety traffic uses vo/vi. If one of these categories is moved to be/bk, it runs the standard's values.
NR numerology other than the profile's Sub-channel and slot structure come from the profile's transcribed link-level curves (Lusvarghi 2024 at µ = 1).
Sidelink mode 1 (network-scheduled) No gNB/eNB scheduler is modelled.
Sub-channel size and count Fixed by the profile (J3161/1, EN 303 613, EN 303 798), because the BLER curves were computed for those pools.
LIMERIC as such The ETSI adaptive approach is TS 102 687's LIMERIC-derived algorithm, and its α, β and target are above.
Signal timing plan per junction Fixed-time plans come from the map. Coordination, cycle length, all-red and pedestrian timing are configurable for every junction at once (world.signals), not per junction.

Tests¶

crates/v2xw-engine/tests/researcher_config.rs runs a baseline and the setting side by side on the same seed for each area:

  • PHY: 27 Mbit/s and a 15 dB noise figure lower delivery.
  • MAC: CWmin 1023 multiplies the access delay.
  • Channel: exponent 3.8, and Rayleigh fading, decode less than free space with no fading.
  • DCC: ETSI α = 0.5 gates frames, and DCC off records no DCC state.
  • Sidelink: random selection changes collisions.
  • Traffic: timid drivers are slower.
  • Messages: 5 Hz halves the frames.
  • Run: the warm-up drops early samples.
  • Loader: it refuses the meaningless settings.

crates/v2xw-engine/tests/network_audit_config.rs does the same for the network fields: sidelink noise figure and CBR threshold, the reactive DCC table, CBF timers, SPaT, MAP and CPM intervals, the verification queue depth, and the loader's refusals. Every number found in the network crates, exposed or kept and why, is listed in config-inventory-network.md.

v2xw sensitivity --catalogue sweeps every published numeric and choice setting at once. It runs each one at both ends of its recommended range, or at each of its choices, against a same-seed base on a small family of scenarios (scenarios/sensitivity/catalogue.yaml). It then lists the settings that move no metric, the effects that contradict crates/v2xw-engine/tests/expectations/*.yaml, and the values every family refuses. The result is docs/reference/sensitivity-catalogue.md, and crates/v2xw-engine/tests/sensitivity_catalogue.rs is its fast CI subset.
